## Game notes (optional, suggestions only)
*(Design suggestions only; not lore.)*
- Niman "Advanced" difficulty: Legends calls Niman "simple, easily learned", with mastery taking 10+ years (Drallig). A low entry cost and a long mastery curve would fit both.
- Vaapad gating: one rule could replace `maceLiving`, `maceTrustEstablished` and the 2696 trigger. Clone Wars: Windu's personal permission (or Bulq, unsanctioned, with a corruption risk). NJO to Legacy: the recovered records (*The Jedi Path*, the Great Holocron).
- Juyo gating by era: Old Republic, teacher's judgment ("not to the reckless"); post-Ruusan, a hand-picked flag (Vaunk); Clone Wars, Drallig-style refusal. The `jediCouncilPermission` flag could stay for Juyo only.
- Makashi: v3's unlock trigger "Precision Footwork" already fits Legends; only the descriptive "minimal footwork" needs changing.
- Saberstaff profile: wide coverage and surprise against untrained foes, with penalties to power and penetration actions and a self-injury risk without training. A bonus applies only to thrown attacks.
- Tutaminis: blade-catching could be a rare master feat, not a general high-proficiency effect.
- The NJO style descriptions are partly game-derived (*Jedi Outcast*, *Jedi Academy*); fine as stances.
- Tradition tags: "Jedi origin, also Sith" for Soresu, Ataru, Niman and Sokan would keep Sith characters from being blocked from forms Legends shows them using.

## Decisions needed
1. Vaapad before Windu [conflict]: created in the late Republic ([Form VII/Legends §Vaapad](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VII/Legends)), or an older name used in the New Sith Wars era (*Book of Sith*, *Path of Destruction*; reconciled by *Insider* 92), or a typing error ([BTS] Karpyshyn 2006)? Best fit: the old name existed, and Windu's technique is late-Republic.
2. Niman origin [conflict]: Kashi Mer Royale Macheteros → Legions of Lettow → Jedi ([Form VI/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VI/Legends); [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Form VI: Niman](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends)) or "developed from Jar'Kai" ([Lightsaber combat/Legends §Early developments](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends))? Best fit: Kashi Mer (more sources).
3. Niman weaknesses [conflict]: "little weakness" ([Lightsaber combat/Legends §Form VI: Niman](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends)) or relaxed bladework, nearly hopeless against Makashi ([Form VI/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VI/Legends))? Best fit: keep the named weaknesses (cited, more detailed).
4. Niman difficulty [conflict]: "simple, easily learned/easily mastered" ([Form VI/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VI/Legends)) or "at least ten years" to master (Drallig, [Form VI/Legends §Applications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VI/Legends))? Best fit: easy to learn, long to master.
5. Restore Ventress as a Makashi user (skilled, not master) per [Form II/Legends §Applications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_II/Legends)? Best fit: yes.
6. Change Makashi "minimal footwork" to "footwork-centered, minimal energy" per [Form II/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_II/Legends)? Best fit: yes.
7. Vaapad mastery: "only Windu" (v3), or Billaba and Vos as masters too, with Windu its last master ([Form VII/Legends §Vaapad](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VII/Legends); [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Developments during the Clone Wars](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends))? Best fit: the latter; drop the unsourced Yoda quote.
8. Clone Wars Vaapad teachers: v3's Windu/Bulq/Billaba, or Windu (permission only) plus Bulq (unsanctioned) ([Form VII/Legends §Applications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VII/Legends))? Best fit: drop Billaba as a teacher.
9. Replace the `maceLiving` and `maceTrustEstablished` flags and the 2696 trigger with a teacher-or-records gate that matches v3's corrected note? Best fit: yes.
10. Trispzest lock: winged species only, or fliers and levitators (Maw, [Trispzest §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trispzest))? Best fit: fliers and levitators.
11. Saberstaff "more damage": remove it, or limit it to thrown attacks ([Double-bladed lightsaber/Legends §Specifications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Double-bladed_lightsaber/Legends))? Best fit: remove it, with a thrown-only bonus at most.
12. Revan as a Niman user: keep it on one uncited LC sentence, or drop it ([Lightsaber combat/Legends §Form VI: Niman](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends))? Best fit: keep it as [unclear], or drop it.
13. Telekinetic combat peak: replace Luke Skywalker with Darth Traya ([Telekinesis/Legends §Telekinetic lightsaber combat](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends))? Best fit: yes.
14. Dun Möch "dishonor by the Jedi Order": reword to "Sith doctrine; Jedi use rare and debated" ([Dun Möch/Legends §Usage](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dun_M%C3%B6ch/Legends))? Best fit: yes.
15. Tradition column: "Jedi origin; also Sith" for Soresu, Ataru, Niman and Sokan (Part 1 A44)? Best fit: yes.
16. Missing items: add Juyo-Kos (SWTOR era), the reverse Shien grip, mounted combat, the Imperial Knight styles and named moves, as forms, techniques or lore notes? Best fit: Juyo-Kos and reverse grip as variants; the rest as techniques.
