# SWRPG Galaxy Timeline, 25–44 ABY (Part 1 of 4): The Yuuzhan Vong Invasion to the Fall of Coruscant (25–27 ABY)

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 24, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends), as cited per entry. Rulings of 24 Nov 2026 applied (lore-rulings-2026-11-24c.md; D-484).

## Summary
This four-part file rebuilds the "YUUZHAN VONG WAR", "POST-VONG / DARK NEST CRISIS" and "SECOND GALACTIC CIVIL WAR" blocks of `SW_Legends_Galaxy_Timeline.md`, and the 43–44 ABY *Fate of the Jedi* row, as one sourced row per event. It picks up where `SW_Timeline_4_25ABY` ends (the eve of the invasion) and stops before *Crucible* (45 ABY). Part 1 (this file) gives the heads of state for the whole span and covers the Yuuzhan Vong War from the first attacks in 25 ABY to the fall of Coruscant in 27 ABY. Part 2 covers 27–34 ABY (from the fall of Coruscant to the end of the war, the founding of the Galactic Alliance, Zonama Sekot and the recovery years). Part 3 covers 35–41 ABY (the Dark Nest Crisis, the Swarm War and the Second Galactic Civil War). Part 4 covers 42–44 ABY (*Fate of the Jedi*: Abeloth and the Lost Tribe of the Sith) and holds the back sections for the whole set. Key facts: the war ran 25–29 ABY and killed over 365 trillion people; Coruscant fell in 27 ABY and was retaken in 29 ABY; the New Republic became the Galactic Alliance in 28 ABY; the Second Galactic Civil War ran 40–41 ABY; the Lost Tribe emergence ran 41–44 ABY.

## YUUZHAN VONG WAR — 25–29 ABY
## ══════════════════════════════════

### Overview
The Yuuzhan Vong, a warlike nomadic species from another galaxy, invaded at a point code-named Vector Prime in 25 ABY after decades of scouting, and quickly took much of the Outer Rim [Yuuzhan Vong War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong_War). Their technology was entirely biological, they could not be sensed in the Force [carried over from the old timeline, not re-checked], and they worshipped their gods through pain; they were led by Supreme Overlord Shimrra Jamaane and the priest caste, but Shimrra was secretly the puppet of the Shamed One Onimi [Shimrra Jamaane](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shimrra_Jamaane) [Onimi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Onimi). Chief of State Borsk Fey'lya's New Republic was ill-prepared, at first refused to admit the threat, and could not stop the drive toward the Core [Yuuzhan Vong War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong_War) [Borsk Fey'lya](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Borsk_Fey%27lya). The Imperial Remnant fought beside the Republic at Ithor in 25 ABY, though its Moffs then chose to sit out the war [Battle of Ithor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Ithor) [Imperial Remnant/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Remnant/Legends). The Hapan navy lost three-quarters of its ships at Fondor in 25 ABY [Battle of Fondor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Fondor). Luke Skywalker's New Jedi Order was hunted (the Jedi Praxeum on Yavin 4 was destroyed in 26 ABY) and the Republic refused it aid [Invasion of Yavin 4](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Invasion_of_Yavin_4). Coruscant fell in 27 ABY, two years into the war, and was reshaped by its conquerors; the New Republic fragmented and its government moved to Dac [Battle of Coruscant (Yuuzhan Vong War)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Coruscant_(Yuuzhan_Vong_War)) [27 ABY/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/27_ABY/Legends). In 28 ABY Bastion was attacked and the Remnant joined the fight; the Chiss joined it only late [Chiss Ascendancy/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chiss_Ascendancy/Legends). The trap at Ebaq 9 in 28 ABY turned the war; Cal Omas reformed the Republic into the Galactic Federation of Free Alliances (Galactic Alliance). The living planet Zonama Sekot arrived over Coruscant in 29 ABY and the war ended with the deaths of Shimrra and Onimi, the surrender of Warmaster Nas Choka and the Sekot Accords [Yuuzhan Vong War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong_War). Over 365 trillion people died [Yuuzhan Vong War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong_War).
