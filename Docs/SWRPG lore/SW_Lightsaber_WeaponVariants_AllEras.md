# Lightsaber and Blade Variants (All Legends Eras)

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 21, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
Covers the standard lightsaber and its hilt and blade variants (saberstaff, shoto, crossguard, curved hilt, pike, lightwhip, dual-phase, training saber, lightfoil and others), plus pre-saber and saber-capable weapons such as Force-imbued swords, protosabers, Sith swords and electrostaffs. Eras run from the Je'daii (c. 24,500 BBY) to the NJO and SWTOR's war against Zakuul. Key facts: protosabers were cabled to a power pack, which ruled out saber throws and telekinetic saber combat. Saberstaffs were a Sith invention the Jedi frowned on. Shotos counter lightwhips. Curved hilts trade some techniques for precision and overhand power.

## Entries

### Baseline: the standard lightsaber
- **Type:** weapon
- **Era:** All lightsaber eras
- **Key facts:**

- **Hilt:** straight, about 20–30 cm, usually self-built and customized. [Lightsaber/Legends §Hilt variations](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber/Legends)
- **Blade:**
  - Massless; releases no energy except light until it touches something solid.
  - Cuts almost anything, with speed depending on density. Cauterizes, so wounds rarely bleed much. [Lightsaber/Legends §Cutting power](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber/Legends)
  - Feels "solid" in dense material because of electromagnetic resistance. Rarely, some solids pass the field and short the arc.
  - Repels most force fields, blaster bolts and other blades. [Lightsaber/Legends §Cutting power](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber/Legends)
- **Water:** Most sabers short out when submerged. In rain they steam but keep working. [Lightsaber/Legends §Lightsaber-resistant materials](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber/Legends)

- **Related:** CombatTechniques (resistant materials)
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Double-bladed lightsaber (saberstaff)
- **Type:** weapon
- **Era:** From Exar Kun (3996 BBY) to the NJO
- **Key facts:**

- **Origin:**
  - Invented by Dark Lords of the old Sith Empire, and likely influenced by the Zabrak zhaboka. [Double-bladed lightsaber/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Double-bladed_lightsaber/Legends)
  - First recorded use: Exar Kun (Sith holocron schematics; he modified his Jedi saber), [Lightsaber/Legends §Hilt variations](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber/Legends) [Double-bladed lightsaber/Legends §Specifications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Double-bladed_lightsaber/Legends). He revealed the second blade in the Duel in the Senate, 3996 BBY. [Duel in the Senate](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Duel_in_the_Senate)
- **Builds:**
  - Original single mechanism with two emitters.
  - Later single-mechanism designs with an enlarged hilt.
  - Most common: two sabers joined at the pommel. [Double-bladed lightsaber/Legends §Specifications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Double-bladed_lightsaber/Legends)
  - Paired sabers with locking mechanisms can split for Jar'Kai mid-fight. Cord-linked versions are flailed (more unpredictable, less control). [Lightsaber/Legends §Hilt variations](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber/Legends)
- **Weaknesses:**
  - Needs special training, or the wielder may bisect or impale themselves.
  - Power or penetration styles are hard to apply.
  - The large hilt is a target, and the sun djem contact mark is a known counter. A two-mechanism build keeps one blade if the other is cut. [Double-bladed lightsaber/Legends §Specifications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Double-bladed_lightsaber/Legends)
  - Most duelists pick single blades, which gives saberstaff users a surprise edge. [Double-bladed lightsaber/Legends §Specifications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Double-bladed_lightsaber/Legends)
- **Jedi attitude:**
  - The Council "traditionally frowned on" it as a weapon designed to kill more effectively. No rule forbade it, but the Jedi discouraged it. [Double-bladed lightsaber/Legends §Use during the Sith Wars](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Double-bladed_lightsaber/Legends)
  - Popular with Sith in the Jedi Civil War; Bastila Shan used one. [Double-bladed lightsaber/Legends §Use during the Sith Wars](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Double-bladed_lightsaber/Legends)
  - Kas'im favored it. Bane trained Zannah with it. Few Jedi used it (Sarro Xaj). [Double-bladed lightsaber/Legends §Use during the Sith Wars](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Double-bladed_lightsaber/Legends)
  - By 32 BBY, combat use was practically unheard of. [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Post-Ruusan developments](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends)
  - In the NJO, double-bladed training became standard under Katarn's katas. [Battlemaster/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battlemaster/Legends)

- **Related:** Forms Part 2 (Jar'Kai); CombatTechniques (sun djem)
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Shoto (short saber / lightdagger)
- **Type:** weapon
- **Era:** All eras
- **Key facts:**

- **Main role:** an off-hand Jar'Kai blade that counters the opponent's weapon while the main blade strikes back. Masters can land many attacks fast. It also **counters lightwhips**. [Shoto lightsaber/Legends §Purpose and usage](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shoto_lightsaber/Legends)
- A shorter blade means a less intense gyroscopic effect, so it is easier to handle. [Lightsaber/Legends §Blade variations](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber/Legends)
- **Primary weapon for small users:** Yoda, Vandar Tokare, Picaroon C. Boodle. [Lightsaber/Legends §Blade variations](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber/Legends)
- **Game source (labelled):** *Saga Edition Core Rulebook* says it was crafted to be thrown effectively. [Shoto lightsaber/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shoto_lightsaber/Legends)
- **Guard shoto ("lightsaber tonfa"):** a 90° side handle with the blade along the forearm, for blocking. Phrik casing recommended. [Lightsaber/Legends §Hilt variations](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber/Legends)

- **Related:** Forms Part 2 (Jar'Kai); Lightwhip
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Crossguard / forked / vented
- **Type:** weapon
- **Era:** Clone Wars (forked; Nightsisters); Galidraan (pre-44 BBY); SWTOR war against Zakuul (vented)
- **Key facts:**

- **Purpose:** Metal quillons or extra short emitters protect the hand, catch and block blades, and shunt power from the main blade. Preferred by Makashi users. [Crossguard lightsaber/Legends §Design](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Crossguard_lightsaber/Legends)
- **Forked:** one secondary blade at 45° (Roblio Darté, Clone Wars). [Crossguard lightsaber/Legends §Forked lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Crossguard_lightsaber/Legends)
- **Physical crossguard:** a Jedi at Galidraan (pre-44 BBY). [Crossguard lightsaber/Legends §Physical crossguard](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Crossguard_lightsaber/Legends)
- **Vented:** three blades; vented dualsaber has four.
  - Dazh Ranos used it in the war against Zakuul. (SWTOR: *Knights of the Eternal Throne*, 2016; SWTOR is fully Legends) [Crossguard lightsaber/Legends §Vented lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Crossguard_lightsaber/Legends)
  - Nightsisters (Clone Wars, *Jedi Alliance*). [Crossguard lightsaber/Legends §Vented lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Crossguard_lightsaber/Legends) [Crossguard lightsaber/Legends §Behind the scenes](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Crossguard_lightsaber/Legends)

- **Related:** Form II (Makashi)
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Curved hilt (dueling saber)
- **Type:** weapon
- **Era:** Prime of Form II to the Clone Wars (Dooku, Ventress); Bane c. 1000 BBY
- **Key facts:**

- **Function:**
  - Precise moves and flexibility in blade-on-blade combat; more power on overhand strikes.
  - Shifts the attack angle slightly to confuse an opponent (Bane vs Farfalla).
  - Held more in the palm. Harder to build (crystal alignment). [Curved-hilt lightsaber/Legends §History and use](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Curved-hilt_lightsaber/Legends)
- Some techniques get harder and many get more effective. It suits Makashi but works with Form V (Bane with Kas'im's gift; Anakin with Ventress's saber). [Curved-hilt lightsaber/Legends §History and use](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Curved-hilt_lightsaber/Legends)
- Dooku's curved hilt was also dual-phase. [Dual-phase lightsaber/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dual-phase_lightsaber/Legends)

- **Related:** Form II; Form V; Duels: Tython 990 BBY
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Lightsaber pike
- **Type:** weapon
- **Era:** Clone Wars (Temple Guards); SWTOR (Knights of Zakuul)
- **Key facts:**

- **Type 1 (polearm):**
  - Long phrik (or other saber-resistant) shaft, up to about 2 m, with a shorter, thicker blade activated by a switch down the shaft.
  - Nearly body-length; keeps enemies at a distance. [Lightsaber pike/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_pike/Legends) [Lightsaber/Legends §Hilt variations](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber/Legends)
  - Similar to the Morgukai cortosis staff. [Lightsaber pike/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_pike/Legends)
- **Type 2 (double):** fights "nearly identical" to a saberstaff. Jedi Temple Guards (Clone Wars) used yellow blades. [Lightsaber pike/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_pike/Legends)
- Considered the domain of exotic-weapon specialists. [Lightsaber pike/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_pike/Legends)
- Knights of Zakuul used pikes (SWTOR: *Knights of the Fallen Empire*). [Lightsaber pike/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_pike/Legends)

- **Related:** Training (Temple Guards); CombatTechniques (phrik)
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Lightwhip
- **Type:** weapon
- **Era:** New Sith Wars (Githany) to 40 ABY (Lumiya)
- **Key facts:**

- **Build:** several small crystals; no cell barriers, so the blade is flexible and often several meters long. Multi-tassel versions exist (Lumiya). [Lightwhip/Legends §Specifications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightwhip/Legends)
  - Lumiya added solid tassels to offset its weaknesses and built parts of the whip from beskar. [Lightwhip/Legends §Specifications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightwhip/Legends) [Beskar/Legends §A Mandalorian resource](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Beskar/Legends)
- **Strengths:**
  - Rare, so duelists are unfamiliar with it.
  - Long reach.
  - Bends around parries.
  - Multiple tassels overwhelm defenses. [Lightwhip/Legends §Specifications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightwhip/Legends)
- **Weaknesses:**
  - Hard to control unless already a whip master; can maim its user.
  - Weaker blade that can't always cut durasteel.
  - Must swing wide to build momentum; poor at parrying; vulnerable to speed and brute force.
  - Can short out violently when struck hard. [Lightwhip/Legends §Specifications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightwhip/Legends)
- **Counter:** shoto. [Shoto lightsaber/Legends §Purpose and usage](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shoto_lightsaber/Legends)
- **Users:** Githany, Lumiya, Silri. [Lightsaber/Legends §Blade variations](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber/Legends)

- **Related:** Shoto; Duels: Roqoo Depot 40 ABY
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Dual-phase
- **Type:** weapon
- **Era:** c. 400 BBY (Halcyon); Jedi Civil War; Clone Wars to the NJO
- **Key facts:**

- Switches instantly between preset lengths for surprise. Some have blade-width adjustment or switchable crystals. [Dual-phase lightsaber/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dual-phase_lightsaber/Legends)
- Keiran Halcyon (c. 400 BBY) built one that went from 1.3 m to 3 m. Most date from the Jedi Civil War; they went out of style and returned in the Clone Wars. [Dual-phase lightsaber/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dual-phase_lightsaber/Legends)
- **Users:** Dooku (curved hilt), Vader (straight hilt), Gantoris, Corran Horn. Considered antiquated by the Yuuzhan Vong War (Ganner Rhysode mocked them). [Dual-phase lightsaber/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dual-phase_lightsaber/Legends)
- In modern times the secondary setting was more often shorter, for precision cutting. [Lightsaber/Legends §Blade variations](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber/Legends)

- **Related:** Curved hilt
- **Uncertainty:** none

## Game notes (optional, suggestions only)
*(Design suggestions only.)*
- A per-variant modifier table could cover reach, parry, two-handed power, surprise, self-injury risk when untrained, and throwable.

- A double-bladed weapon could expose a "hilt target" weakness to sun djem.
