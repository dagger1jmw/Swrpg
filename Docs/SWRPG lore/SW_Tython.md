## Summary
Tython is a Deep Core world so tied to the Force that it reacts to imbalance with Force storms and groundquakes, and it's unsafe for those who can't sense the Force. The Je'daii formed there, and the Jedi Order was founded there, but a cataclysm in the Force Wars left it uninhabitable. The survivors left, and the Deep Core erased the lanes, so the planet was lost and the savage Flesh Raiders spread across it. After the Sacking of Coruscant (3653 BBY), Satele Shan rediscovered Tython, and the Order moved its headquarters there and built a new Temple. It stays lost until about 3651 BBY and is then the Order's seat. In the Cold War, the Flesh Raiders, Dark Jedi and an illegal Twi'lek settlement trouble it. The Sith briefly take the Temple in 3637, and the Order largely abandons the planet after 3636.

## Entries

### Tython (the planet)
- **Type:** location (planet; planet-level nexus, balanced)
- **Era:** ancient (Je'daii) onward
- **Key facts:**
  - **Location:** the fifth planet of the Tython system in the Deep Core, grid L-10 [Tython, 2014 Wookieepedia snapshot](https://webarchive.library.unt.edu/unt/csid/20140911225948/http://starwars.wikia.com/wiki/Tython). A terrestrial world with a breathable atmosphere [same](https://webarchive.library.unt.edu/unt/csid/20140911225948/http://starwars.wikia.com/wiki/Tython).
  - **Moons:** Ashla and Bogan. The Je'daii based their philosophy of balance between the light and dark sides on them [same](https://webarchive.library.unt.edu/unt/csid/20140911225948/http://starwars.wikia.com/wiki/Tython). The first settlers recognised the light and dark aspects of the Force by watching the two moons. Bogan was later a place of exile for Je'daii who had strayed too far to the dark, and Ashla for those who had strayed too far to the light [Bogan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bogan).
  - **Its bond with the Force:** Tython's environment is remarkably sensitive to the Force. Powerful disturbances, such as a being very strong in the light or the dark, can cause immense Force storms and groundquakes [Tython, 2014 snapshot](https://webarchive.library.unt.edu/unt/csid/20140911225948/http://starwars.wikia.com/wiki/Tython). The Je'daii saw the Force as three aspects of a whole: Ashla (light), Bogan (dark) and Bendu (balance). Tython itself reacted violently to imbalance between light and dark [Je'daii Order, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/Je%27daii_Order).
  - **Unsafe for non-Force-users:** the Je'daii soon realised Tython was unsafe for those who couldn't sense the Force, so the other Tythans settled the other worlds of the system [Tython, 2014 snapshot](https://webarchive.library.unt.edu/unt/csid/20140911225948/http://starwars.wikia.com/wiki/Tython).
- **Ruling (Jared, Nov 22, 2026):** a planet-level nexus, leaning balanced; too dangerous for non-Force-users to inhabit; too much conflict causes Force storms.
- **Uncertainty:** none.

### The Je'daii, the Force Wars, and the loss of Tython
- **Type:** history
- **Era:** c. 36,453 BBY to c. 25,000 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - Eight Tho Yor ships brought Force-sensitive pilgrims to Tython, where a ninth, far larger Tho Yor waited above a stone pinnacle, and the Je'daii Order formed there [Je'daii Order, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/Je%27daii_Order).
  - **The Force Wars:** a ten-year series of conflicts on Tython, more than seven centuries before the Republic's founding [Force Wars](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_Wars). After the Rakatan threat was defeated, the Je'daii split between followers of the Ashla and the Bogan. The Ashla Je'daii founded the Jedi Order under a High Council [Force Wars](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_Wars).
  - **The cataclysm:** in 25,783 BBY a cataclysm destroyed Tython's biosphere and made it uninhabitable. The surviving Ashla Je'daii fled to the Rim and settled on Ossus 730 years later [Force Wars](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_Wars).
  - **Lost:** the Jedi left for Ossus, and Tython was lost as the Deep Core erased the hyperlanes. The savage Flesh Raiders evolved on the empty world [Tython, 2014 snapshot](https://webarchive.library.unt.edu/unt/csid/20140911225948/http://starwars.wikia.com/wiki/Tython).
- **Related:** SW_Characters_01_DawnOfTheJedi; SW_Force_Traditions_AllEras.
- **Uncertainty:** none.

### Rediscovery and the Order's return (3653–3647 BBY)
- **Type:** history
- **Era:** 3653–3647 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - After the Coruscant Temple was destroyed, Satele Shan set out to find the lost world [SWTOR codex, "Rediscovering Tython"](https://swtor.jedipedia.net/en/plc/ancient-jedi-statue). Guided by the Force, she explored the Deep Core and came back with astrogation charts [SWTOR codex, "Rediscovering Tython" (French text)](https://torcommunity.com/database/codex/NxwVc1A/rediscovering+tython/).
  - The Order judged Coruscant no longer suitable for its headquarters and moved to Tython, rediscovered "just a year earlier" by Jedi Knight Satele Shan [Flesh Raider uprising, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/flesh_raider_uprising).
  - After Satele Shan's rediscovery, the Order moved its headquarters to its ancestral homeworld, replacing the Coruscant Temple destroyed at the end of the Great Galactic War [Kaleth, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/Kaleth).
  - SWTOR codex: lives were lost to the troubled landscape, the Flesh Raiders and forces unknown, but the Council soon set up a new temple, determined to rebuild Tython [SWTOR codex, via swtorista](https://swtorista.com/articles/swtor-tython-coruscant-jedi-old-republic-codex-lore-entries/).
  - **Temple construction finished in 3647 BBY** (SW_Planets_Travel_OldRepublic, Part 1).
- **Related:** batch 20 (the Order seat relocating 3653–3650, Tython from 3650); SW_Force_Nexus_Vergence; Satele Shan (the roster).
- **Uncertainty:** the year of rediscovery [conflict]. The game uses 3651 (batch 20, with Satele made Grand Master after the rediscovery). The Flesh Raider uprising page puts the rediscovery a year before the move. Both fit a rediscovery around 3652–3651.

### Places

- **Era:** recorded from the Jedi reoccupation (c. 3651 BBY) onward
- **Key facts:**

  - **Kalikori village:** see the next entry.
- **Related:** SW_Jedi_Daily_Life_Training_OldRepublic.

### Kalikori village and the Twi'lek Pilgrims
- **Type:** location and group
- **Era:** founded around the time of the Jedi resettlement; Cold War
- **Key facts:**
  - The village is named for an old Twi'lek word for "beginning". It's a settlement in the Tythonian hills, founded by Twi'lek Pilgrims fleeing persecution [SWTOR codex, via swtorista](https://swtorista.com/articles/swtor-tython-coruscant-jedi-old-republic-codex-lore-entries/).
  - Wookieepedia calls it "an illegal settlement of Twi'lek Pilgrims" in 3643 BBY [Raid on the Flesh Raider weapons cache](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Raid_on_the_Flesh_Raider_weapons_cache).
  - As the village grew, with fertile fields and its own irrigation, the Pilgrims found they were expanding into Flesh Raider territory, and they've fought the creatures off ever since without outside help [SWTOR codex, Tython locations](https://swtor.jedipedia.net/en/cdx/tython).
  - The village is led by Matriarchs (Sumari during the war with the Flesh Raiders) and suffered greatly from Flesh Raider attacks in the Cold War [Flesh Raider uprising, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/flesh_raider_uprising). In 3643 the villagers betrayed Orgus Din and his Padawan after the Dark Jedi Bengel Morr promised to protect them from the Flesh Raiders [same](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/flesh_raider_uprising).
- **Ruling (Jared, Nov 22, 2026):** the settlement sits awkwardly with Tython being unsafe for non-Force-users, and Jared considers it a break from the lore. The narrator shouldn't treat Tython as open to ordinary settlement. The "illegal settlement" wording, and the village's constant losses to the Flesh Raiders and the landscape, fit that reading.
- **Uncertainty:** none.

### The Flesh Raiders
- **Type:** species (native, savage)
- **Era:** after the Jedi left (c. 25,000 BBY) onward
- **Key facts:**
  - The savage Flesh Raiders evolved on the empty world after the Jedi left [Tython, 2014 snapshot](https://webarchive.library.unt.edu/unt/csid/20140911225948/http://starwars.wikia.com/wiki/Tython).
  - **The 3643 uprising:** in the Cold War they attacked the planet's other inhabitants, the Jedi and the Twi'lek Pilgrims, in greater numbers [Raid on the Flesh Raider weapons cache](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Raid_on_the_Flesh_Raider_weapons_cache). By 3643 BBY the Dark Jedi Callef and Bengel Morr had followed the Order to Tython, hidden in its unexplored regions, and started organising the Flesh Raiders [Flesh Raider uprising, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/flesh_raider_uprising).
- **Related:** SW_Species_OldRepublic (if listed); the roster (Bengel Morr, Orgus Din).
- **Uncertainty:** their origin beyond "evolved on the empty world" isn't covered in Legends sources fetched here.
