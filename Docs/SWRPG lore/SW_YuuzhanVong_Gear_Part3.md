# Yuuzhan Vong Personal Biotechnology (Gear) — Part 3: Gaps, Game Notes, Decisions, Sources

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 21, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
Part 3 holds the closing sections for the Yuuzhan Vong gear topic: what could not be found, what was checked and excluded, game statistics from sourcebooks (labelled), game-design suggestions, the decisions the developer needs to make, the full source list, the checklist, and topics set aside for later. Parts 1 and 2 hold the lore.

## Not found
1. **A general rule on whether gear rejects or attacks non-Vong.** Sources show cases only: a captured amphistaff stays loyal to its captor [Amphistaff §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Amphistaff); feral polyps attacked until Jacen's Vongsense calmed them [Darth Caedus §Loss and rediscovery](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Caedus); Heartstriker answered the Vong language [Heartstriker](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Heartstriker); other amphistaffs refused to fight the Scepter of Power's user [Scepter of Power](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scepter_of_Power). No page states a rule for gear meeting a stranger.
2. **Explicit caste restrictions on weapons.** Rank-locked items exist (tsaisi, command cloak, grafted armour), but no rule bars a caste from carrying weapons, and non-warriors did carry them (Part 2, H).
3. **Prices, licences, black-market data.** The only law found is the post-war ban on thud bugs and razor bugs. The Black market/Legends page has no Vong material.
4. **Peace Brigade armed with Vong weapons.** Only their unmasker use and their role as auxiliaries were found.
5. **Telekinesis on an amphistaff or bug, and the mind trick by name.** Only the general rule ("most direct powers fail") and examples on other items (senalak, masquer, objects) were found.
6. **A non-Vong using a tizowyrm.** Only Vong users found.
7. **The function of the limpin implant.** The page gives none.
8. **Years for some events:** Tsavong Lah's death at Ebaq 9; the exact year of the Second Battle of Borleias (it followed the fall of Coruscant, 27 ABY [Battle of Borleias (Yuuzhan Vong War; Second) §Prelude](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Borleias_%28Yuuzhan_Vong_War%3B_Second%29)); the year of Jacen's seedship ordeal.
9. **How Mara sensed masquered infiltrators** despite the Vong's absence from the Force [Mara Jade Skywalker §Escalation](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mara_Jade_Skywalker).
10. **Training time for outsiders.** No source gives how long it takes a non-Vong to learn to use a villip, amphistaff or bug; Jariah Syn's teacher is named but the duration isn't [Jariah Syn](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jariah_Syn).
11. **Darth Wyyrlok (III)'s page** added nothing on Vong gear beyond destroying Krayt's armour (taken from [Darth Krayt §Resurrection](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Krayt)).

### Suggestions (design ideas, not lore)
- **Bonding flag on living weapons:** an amphistaff or tsaisi starts "bonded" to its captor; an outsider needs a bonding event (Vongsense, the Vong language, a fresh capture at the polyp) or the staff stays inert or hostile. Supported by Heartstriker, Jacen, and the capture rule.
- **Lightsaber vs vonduun armour:** make armour negate saber slashes, but let thrusts or called shots at armpit gills, a bare face or the throat bypass it (the Belkadan and Ebaq 9 kills).
- **Force targeting rule:** direct-target powers (mind trick, sense, choke, push on the body) fail on Vong and biots. Environmental and object telekinesis, lightning, Force net and Dark Transfer work. Lightning destroys vonduun armour.
- **Stun immunity:** Vong ignore stun settings and spray-stick shocks; snap bugs barely affect them.
- **Era availability:** war era: capture-only for outsiders. Post-war: thud and razor bugs contraband, armour copies in Jedi training halls. Legacy era: rare, through Vong exiles (Chonyo-type teachers), feral Wayland biots and One Sith labs.
- **Pain as cost:** most biots (masquer, cloaker, fighting claw, implants) hurt the wearer; the gnullith is the noted exception. A per-use strain cost fits.
- **Implant risk:** escalation implants can be rejected (Shamed status); Steng's Talons rarely survive implantation; Yorik-Kul slowly kills (Krayt).

## Decisions needed
1. **Lightsaber vs vonduun armour:** resists sabers ([Vonduun Skerr Kyrric §Advantages](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vonduun_Skerr_Kyrric), [Vonduun crab/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vonduun_crab/Legends), [Lightsaber/Legends §Lightsaber-resistant materials](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber/Legends)) vs saber kills on armoured Vong (stab to the heart [Duel on Belkadan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Duel_on_Belkadan); through the throat [Jaina Solo Fel §Sword of the Jedi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jaina_Solo_Fel)). Best fit: resists slashing; thrusts and weak points get through.
2. **Amphistaff uncuttable vs Luke "slashing" the Scepter of Power** ([Amphistaff §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Amphistaff) vs [Scepter of Power](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scepter_of_Power)). Best fit: a wound, not a severed staff.
3. **Jacen's Helska IV disguise:** cloaker plus gnullith ([Darth Caedus §The first losses; the first victories](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Caedus); [Ooglith cloaker §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ooglith_cloaker)) vs masquer ([Jaina Solo Fel §Early strikes](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jaina_Solo_Fel)). Best fit: cloaker (two sources; underwater use).
4. **Vacuum protection:** cloaker is the vacuum suit ([Ooglith cloaker §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ooglith_cloaker)) vs masquers protecting hostages from vacuum ([Jaina Solo Fel §Trickster goddess](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jaina_Solo_Fel)). Best fit: cloakers; treat the Trickster case as an exception.
5. **Jacen's slave seed:** fell off the hook ([Hook of bone](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hook_of_bone)) vs stabbed in and tied to a dhuryam ([Darth Caedus §Loss and rediscovery](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Caedus)). Best fit: Jacen page (detailed); possibly two attempts [inferred].
6. **Embrace of Pain after 40 ABY:** "last known" Embrace used on Ben in 40 ABY vs Legacy-era Embraces (Krayt, One Sith, Maladi) ([Embrace of Pain §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Embrace_of_Pain)). Best fit: Legacy Embraces exist; "last known" meant known to the Jedi.
7. **Yammosks and the Force:** yammosks sense the Force ([Yammosk §Abilities](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yammosk); [Vergere §Life with the Yuuzhan Vong](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vergere)) vs voxyn unique among Vong creations ([Voxyn §Resilient Jedi hunters](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Voxyn)). Best fit: yammosks sense it (two sources).
8. **Krayt's armour:** wore vonduun crab armour ([Vonduun Skerr Kyrric §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vonduun_Skerr_Kyrric)) vs an exoskeleton grown from his implants ([Darth Krayt §The rise of Darth Krayt (c. 25 ABY–130 ABY)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Krayt)). Best fit: implant exoskeleton (the character's own page).
9. **Dark Transfer:** works on Vong ([Yuuzhan Vong §Biology and appearance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong)) vs Maladi's bioform resisting it ([Darth Maladi §Division](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Maladi)). Best fit: a specially engineered exception.
10. **Surge coral and the Force:** slaves "vanish in the Force" ([Yorik-Kul](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yorik-Kul)) vs Jacen gaining Vongsense through a slave seed ([Darth Caedus §Loss and rediscovery](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Caedus)). Best fit: both; the Jedi's discipline makes the difference [inferred].
11. **Whipsting vs finger-spear:** one item or two ([Whipsting](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Whipsting); [Finger-spear](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Finger-spear)). Best fit: treat as variants of one shaper-hand weapon.
12. **Fighting claw page** has few or no citations ([Fighting claw](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fighting_claw)). Keep as [last resort] or drop.
13. **Tokhlor tallow 1.6 kg** has no source ([Tokhlor tallow](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tokhlor_tallow)). Use as a game stat or drop.
14. **Mind tricks on Vong:** no specific source; apply the general "direct powers fail" rule ([Yuuzhan Vong §Biology and appearance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong))?
