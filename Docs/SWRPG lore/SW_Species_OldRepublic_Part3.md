# Playable Species of the Old Republic Era (c. 3681–3600 BBY), Part 3 of 3: comparison, conflicts and decisions

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 21, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
Part 3 holds the species comparison table, a short dated timeline of species-relevant events, the conflicts between the Era 09 character files and the species pages, and the closing sections (Not found, Canon only, Not used, Game notes, Decisions needed, Sources, checklist). Of 45 species profiled, 35 are playable-ready and 10 are thin. Most numeric lifespans are game stats; only a few species have lifespan prose.

### Notable ageing findings
- **Long-lived (prose sources):**
  - Hutt: adult at 200, lives over 1,000 [Hutt/Legends §Life cycle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hutt/Legends). Karagga was over 800 [Karagga §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Karagga).
  - Wookiee: several centuries, prime around 200 [Wookiee/Legends §Life cycle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wookiee/Legends). Bowdaar was about 177 in 3661 BBY [inferred].
  - Yoda's species: Yoda died at 900 [Yoda's species/Legends §Known members](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yoda%27s_species/Legends).
  - Epicanthix: up to 130 (novel) [Epicanthix](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Epicanthix).
- **Fast maturity:** Chiss match a 20-year-old Human at 10 [Chiss/Legends §Biology and appearance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chiss/Legends).
- **Age-linked decline:**
  - Trandoshans stop regenerating at about 54 (game stat) [Trandoshan/Legends §Biology and appearance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trandoshan/Legends).
  - Sullustan eyes fail after 30 [Sullustan/Legends §Biology and appearance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sullustan/Legends).
  - Elderly Purebloods mottle and their tendrils wither [Sith (species)/Legends §Hybridization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_%28species%29/Legends).
  - Chagrians lose taste by adulthood [Chagrian/Legends §Biology and appearance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chagrian/Legends).
- **Force and longevity:**
  - Forceful Humans can reach 200 [Human/Legends §Life cycle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Human/Legends).
  - Yoda's species' average is unknown because all known members were long-lived Jedi [Yoda's species/Legends §Biology and appearance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yoda%27s_species/Legends).
- **Unnatural longevity (not species traits):**
  - Scourge, made immortal by the Emperor [Scourge (Sith)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scourge_%28Sith%29).
  - Khem Val, held in stasis for centuries [Khem Val §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Khem_Val).

## Changes from the previous file
No previous species file exists. As instructed, this section lists every species fact in the Era 09 character files (SW_Characters_09_GGW_ColdWar_Index, Part1, Part2, Part3a, Part3b) that conflicts with a species or homeworld page, then the checks that found no conflict.

### Conflicts
1. **Yuun (Gand):** the character file gives birth as 3670 BBY, from the Yuun (Gand) infobox [Yuun (Gand)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yuun_%28Gand%29). The Gand planet page says Yuun was born there in 3668 BBY [Gand (planet)/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gand_%28planet%29/Legends). [conflict]
2. **Xalek (Kaleesh):** the character file gives birth as 3665 BBY on Kalee [Xivhkalrainik](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Xivhkalrainik). The Kalee page says Xivhkalrainik was born there in 3664 BBY [Kalee/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kalee/Legends). [conflict]
3. **Exal Kressh:** the character file lists her as a "Sith hybrid", from her infobox [Exal Kressh](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Exal_Kressh). The Sith species page names her as a hybrid showing only red skin, yellow eyes and Force-sensitivity [Sith (species)/Legends §Hybridization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_%28species%29/Legends), but its Cold War section calls her "a Pureblood and apprentice to the Emperor" [Sith (species)/Legends §Cold War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_%28species%29/Legends). [conflict] The species page disagrees with itself; the character file follows her own page.
4. **Broonmark (Talz):** the character file has a Talz serving the Republic and then the Empire in the Cold War [Broonmark](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Broonmark). The Talz page agrees that Cold War Talz colonised Hoth and served the Republic, yet also says Imperial scouts "found" the Talz around 15 BBY, as a species "not generally known to exist" [Talz/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Talz/Legends). The Alzoc III page has a Talz-world Senator near the end of the Cold War [Alzoc III/Legends §Pre-Imperial era](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Alzoc_III/Legends). [conflict] This is inside the species page; the character file sides with the Cold War material.
5. **Ula Vii (Epicanthix):** the character file has an Epicanthix Imperial agent c. 3643 BBY [Ula Vii](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ula_Vii). The Epicanthix page backs this [Epicanthix §Characteristics](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Epicanthix), but its History section dates Imperial scouts reaching the Epicanthix to after Palpatine came to power [Epicanthix §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Epicanthix). [conflict, mild] Possibly a second contact; [unclear].
