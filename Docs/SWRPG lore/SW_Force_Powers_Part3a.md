## Summary

- **Forbidden or lost Jedi techniques:**
  - *Morichro* (known by 24,500 BBY, forbidden by 1000 BBY, revived by Yaddle).
  - Retaining identity after death (lost; rediscovered by Qui-Gon Jinn; also preserved among the Ysanna).
  - Sabla-Mandibu's "Forbidden Force Techniques" list. Only Electric Judgment and Deadly Sight are confirmed on it; both are covered in Part 2b (§D, §F).
- **Rare perception:** shatterpoints (Mace Windu, Kreia, Caedus, Jaina); Aing-Tii flow-walking, time-drifting and fighting-sight; Vongsense, the only way to sense the Yuuzhan Vong.
- **Orders with their own techniques:** Fallanassi (White Current illusions that fool technology; Force immersion); Baran Do (*hassat-durr* / *ayna-seff*); Witches of Dathomir (spells); Matukai (body-channeling); Zeison Sha (telekinesis); Jensaarai (concealment, cortosis armor); Theran Listeners (Force-listening); Keetael (tracking, farsight); Luka Sene (Field Detection); Disciples of Twilight (Revelation, Shadow Vision); Kathol Ascendants (Ta-Ree spells such as Shadowstrike).
- **Species traits:**
  - Miraluka: Force sight.
  - Kiffar: psychometry in 1 of 100.
  - Iktotchi: precognition that weakens off their moon.
  - Anzati: *daen nosi*.
  - Tasar crystals grant minor powers.
- **Other Legends powers not covered in Parts 1–2** (Force deflection, Force cloak, Sever Force, wall of light, Force healing of others, teleportation, Dimension Shift and others) are summarised in §T11.

## Entries (T1–T11)

Each main entry covers: (1) names and classification, (2) effect and limits, (3) users and feats, (4) era, (5) how it is learned, (6) costs, dangers and whether it is a dark act, (7) counters, (8) related powers, (9) game stats, then uncertainty.

### T1. Shatterpoint perception
1. **Names and classification:** shatterpoints; Kreia calls them "fractures". Sense; neither light nor dark. [Shatterpoint/Legends][shat]
2. **What it does:** with immense focus the user sees the "fault lines" in events or objects, and finds the single "strike" that changes the outcome.
   - **On objects:** the Force is surged into the weak point to shatter even materials thought near-indestructible.
   - **Perception varies:** Windu only uses "sight" for lack of a better word, while another user sees the lines as "blazing mindfire" (*Legacy — War* 6).
   [Shatterpoint §Description][shat]
3. **Users and feats:**
   - **Mace Windu** saw Dooku as a shatterpoint able to end the war but let him live out of attachment (*Shatterpoint*, 22 BBY).
   - **Kreia's** philosophy turned on "fractures" and the "critical point" (3951 BBY, KOTOR II).
   - **Jacen Solo** broke Centerpoint guards' blasters by pressing on their weak points.
   - **As Darth Caedus,** he shattered the Mandalorian Protector Roegr's beskar armor with a light tap (Second Galactic Civil War, 40–41 ABY).
   - **Luke** taught it to **Jaina**, who "effortlessly" shattered a disc of pure Mandalorian iron (41 ABY, *Invincible*).
   [Shatterpoint §History][shat]
4. **Era:** rare in all eras.
5. **How it is learned:** innate talent (Windu, Kreia); Master instruction (Luke to Jaina).
6. **Costs and dangers:** Kreia's warning that well-meant acts can cause harm. Not a dark act; Caedus's use was simply a dark-sider using it.
7. **Counters:** the Yuuzhan Vong page lists shatterpoint among powers with partial effect on the Vong; details are [unclear]. [Yuuzhan Vong][vong]
8. **Related:** precognition and Force vision (Part 1b §S8); Vaapad (lightsaber forms; outside the Force-powers files).
9. **Game stats:** none.
- **Uncertainty:** who sees "mindfire" in *Legacy — War* 6 was not identified, so it is [unclear]. The exact Vong interaction is also [unclear].

### T2. *Morichro*
1. **Names and classification:** *morichro*. Wookieepedia files it as Control and as light-side, yet it was *forbidden* to Knights "without exceptions": **[conflict]**, light-filed vs forbidden. [Morichro/Legends][mor]
2. **What it does:** slows bodily functions, one's own or another's, into mild catatonia, long-term suspended animation, or death.
   - **Self-use:** survive without food, water or air for over a year. It does not protect against vacuum.
   - **On others:** it subdues them. If the target is not monitored they may stay unconscious for good or die of dehydration or starvation. An untrained user may accidentally stop a heart.
   [Morichro §Description][mor]
3. **Users and feats:**
   - **Grandmaster Awdrysta Pina** stopped Arden Lyn's heart during the First Great Schism (c. 24,500 BBY). She was revived by the Kashi Mer talisman in 4 BBY, possibly helped by the Palawa trance (§T8).
   - **Yaddle** was "one of its chief practitioners" in the Order's last years.
   [Morichro §History][mor]
4. **Era:**
   - Known by 24,500 BBY.
   - Unchecked before c. 5000 BBY.
   - The Council classed it as forbidden and limited its teaching to "a select few" by 1000 BBY.
   - It was then forgotten. Yaddle recovered it from three holocrons that held almost nothing on it. The Council let her teach some students but then banned its use, because abuse "could easily prove fatal".
5. **How it is learned:** holocrons; a Master (Yaddle).
6. **Costs and dangers:** it can kill. Not inherently dark, since it is a Control power; killing or neglect of a target makes the use dark.
7. **Counters:** the Palawa trance and the Kashi Mer talisman (Arden Lyn); standard counters.
8. **Related:** hibernation trance (Part 1a §C8); Force Stasis (Part 2a §B).
9. **Game stats:** none found.
- **Uncertainty:** the source of the 1000 BBY "forbidden" classification is a reference tag that was not resolved, so it is cited only to the page.

### T3. Force ghost (retaining identity after death)
1. **Names and classification:** Force ghost / Force spirit; preserving identity in the pneuma. Filed under Control.
   - **[conflict]:** in the ROTS novelization and comic, Qui-Gon says only light-siders can learn it. Wookieepedia's behind-the-scenes note says this "is not, in fact, true", because Sith spirits exist.
   [Force ghost/Legends][ghost]
2. **What it does:** the user keeps their identity after death.
   - **Interaction:** a ghost can interact only with other noncorporeal beings unless it manifests, as a featureless grey mass or a full image.
   - **Powers:** it can use some Force powers, not all.
   - **Travel:** it can travel instantly to familiar places where the Force is strong enough to act as a beacon (*Hero's Guide*).
   - **Duration:** it fades over time unless bound by the dark side to a place or object through essence transfer (Part 2b §E). Light-side ghosts sometimes manifested for decades (*Legacy* 11).
   [Force ghost §Description][ghost]
3. **Users and feats:**
   - **Qui-Gon Jinn** rediscovered it with a Shaman of the Whills and taught **Yoda** and **Obi-Wan**.
   - **Yoda** taught **Qu Rahn**.
   - **The Dark Woman** learned it.
   - **Arca Jeth** and ancient Sith Lords knew it.
   - **Plagueis** knew a Sith could keep his spirit but was not interested.
   - **Sith spirits** bound by malice: Valley of the Dark Lords; the Exar Kun and Ulic duel.
   - **Ergast's Force walk** binds ghosts as power sources (SWTOR, Part 2b §E).
   [Force ghost §History][ghost]
4. **Era:** known in the ancient Republic; lost "over the millennia"; preserved by the **Ysanna** of Ossus; rediscovered c. 32–19 BBY.
5. **How it is learned:** taught (Qui-Gon's line). Yet the page also says it "did not have to be learned" and could come from denying the Force's will at death or from a selfless sacrifice (**[conflict]**). Asli Krimsan's holocron describes "sublimating one's organic cells".
6. **Costs and dangers:** for Jedi, none; it is acceptance of death. Dark-side binding of the spirit is dark (essence transfer, Part 2b §E). Sith "found little peace" in it.
7. **Counters:** time (fading); Force walk binding (Part 2b §E).
8. **Related:** Oneness (Part 1b §U4); essence transfer (Part 2b §E).
9. **Game stats:** (game stat: *Hero's Guide* travel rule with a familiar destination and a Force beacon.)
- **Uncertainty:** Morgan Katarn appearing as a ghost at a nexus is behind-the-scenes speculation, so it is not used.

### T4. Aing-Tii techniques: flow-walking, time-drifting, fighting-sight, ship-jumping, fold space
1. **Names and classification:** flow-walking and fighting-sight are Sense; time-drifting is uncategorised. The Aing-Tii "did not believe in the light and dark". Neither. [Flow-walking][flow]; [Aing-Tii/Legends][aing]
2. **What they do and their limits:**
   - **Flow-walking** views the past and future at a location the user knows. The page says it could even alter the past, and "had very few limitations". The walker must stay anchored or "lose oneself to the flow", and leaves a blur. [Flow-walking §Uses][flow]
   - **Time-drifting** extends flow-walking. If the drifter is seen, the visited person's actions can change. [Time-drifting][drift]
   - **Fighting-sight** is highly accurate battle precognition. [Fighting-sight][fight]
   - **Ship-jumping:** the Aing-Tii use the Force with their technology to move ships instantly through space (*Jedi vs. Sith*). [Aing-Tii §Technology][aing]
   - **Fold space** (Transmigrate): bends space to move an object, "or possibly oneself", instantly. Tadar'Ro taught Luke and Ben during their visit in 43.5 ABY; Abeloth also used it; Luke and Leia used it inside the Monolith (45 ABY). A use called "Aura of Freedom" let the user and nearby allies resist being moved by the Force. Not Aing-Tii-only. [Fold space §intro and page text (no sections)][fold]
3. **Users and feats:**
   - **Jacen Solo**, on his five-year journey through other Force traditions, saw the *Tachyon Flier* crash and left an imprint to guide Leia (35 ABY).
   - **Jacen** flow-walked to the 19 BBY Temple raid to see Anakin fall, and into two High Council meetings (40 ABY).
   - **Jacen** learned fighting-sight there too.
4. **Era:** Aing-Tii, Kathol Rift; reached by outsiders c. 30–35 ABY.
5. **How it is learned:** from the Aing-Tii monks. They believe even non-Force-sensitives can manipulate the Force.
6. **Costs and dangers:** losing oneself in the flow; changing the timeline. Not a dark act, but Jacen used what he saw on his road to becoming Caedus (DS3 §Jacen Solo / Darth Caedus).
7. **Counters:** none recorded.
8. **Related:** precognition and Force vision (Part 1b §S8); instinctive astrogation (Part 1b §S12); teleportation and Dimension Shift (§T11); Force storm (wormhole), a dark-side power whose wormholes also moved objects and people across vast distances (Part 2b §F).
9. **Game stats:** none.
- **Uncertainty:** **[conflict]** / behind the scenes: in *Fate of the Jedi: Omen*, Cilghal reads Jysella Horn's battle precognition as flow-walking. The Fighting-sight page notes she had no knowledge of fighting-sight.

### T5. Vongsense
1. **Names and classification:** Vongsense. Sense; neither light nor dark. [Vongsense][vs]
2. **What it does:** senses the Yuuzhan Vong telepathically. They are otherwise invisible in the Force.
3. **Users and feats:**
   - **Anakin Solo** discovered it after attuning a lambent crystal for his lightsaber (25–27 ABY).
   - **Tahiri Veila**, after vivisection by Mezhan Kwaad.
   - **Jacen Solo**, after Vergere's ordeal.
   - **Finn Galfridian** (cause unexplained).
   - **Karness Muur** (in Celeste Morne), **Allana Solo** and the **One Sith**, without the usual link.
4. **Era:** Yuuzhan Vong War onward (25 ABY+).
5. **How it is learned:** it usually needs a physical or mental bond with Vong biotechnology.
6. **Costs and dangers:** some Jedi must temporarily drop their connection to the Force to use it. Not a dark act.
7. **Counters:** none recorded.
8. **Related:** Force sense (Part 1b §S1).
9. **Game stats:** none.
- **Uncertainty:** none.

### T6. Order traditions

#### T6a. Fallanassi and the White Current
1. **Classification:** an order, mostly female, that calls the Force the "White Current". Pacifist; neither light nor dark. [Fallanassi/Legends][fal]; [White Current/Legends][wc]
2. **Techniques:**
   - **Illusions** that outsiders and "all technological instruments" believe.
   - **Force immersion:** the immersed see reality and sense beings, even Force- or tech-cloaked ones, and their numbers, by their ripples in the Current.
   - **No telekinesis or physical manipulation** at all.
   - **Force scribe:** an upper-tier technique that writes words on objects that never fade and can be seen, read, altered or erased only by someone trained in the White Current. [Force scribe §intro][fscr]
   - **Dopplegänger** (Force shadow) is filed among Fallanassi powers (§T11). [Dopplegänger][dop]
3. **Feats:** they hid the H'kig temple on J't'p'tan as a smoking ruin. At Luke's request, Wialu created an illusion of battle cruisers that turned the **Battle of N'zoth (17 ABY)**.
4. **Era:** from Lucazec (Ialtra). They refused to serve Palpatine and fled after Talsava betrayed them.
5. **Learning:** within the order; rarely taught to men.
6. **Costs and dangers:** none; they use the Current only to hide.
7. **Counters:** immersion in the Current sees through the illusions.
8. **Related:** Force illusion (Part 2a §B); Force stealth (Part 1a §C11).
9. **Game stats:** none.

#### T6b. Witches of Dathomir
1. **Classification:** clans descended from the exiled Jedi **Allya** (c. 600 BBY). Every clan except the Nightsisters holds "never concede to evil". [Witches of Dathomir][wod]
2. **Techniques:** Force use is framed as chanted **spells**. Examples: the Spell of Interpretation (Comprehend Speech, Part 1b §S7); rancor-taming (Allya).
3. **Feats:** they repelled the Jedi at the *Chu'unthor* crash, killing 14 (340 BBY). Yoda negotiated with Rell. Luke taught Teneniel Djo Force stealth (8 ABY).
4. **Era:** c. 600 BBY onward.
5. **Learning:** within the clan; the *Book of Law*.
6. **Costs and dangers:**
   - Dark-side use ("night spells") is punished by exile.
   - **Using anger to fuel an otherwise permitted power is also punished.** This is an intent-based rule: the same spell is acceptable or not depending on emotion.
   - Nightsister dark magic: see the DS files; Sith sorcery is in Part 2b §G.
7. **Counters:** standard counters.
8. **Related:** Nightsister techniques: Heartshadow, their name for Darksight (Part 1b §S8b), and the **Revitalization of the Whuffa**, which revitalizes the body and, with enough will, regrows a limb ([Revitalization of the Whuffa §intro][whuf]).
9. **Game stats:** none.

#### T6c. Baran Do (*hassat-durr*, *ayna-seff*)
1. **Classification:** a Kel Dor sage tradition. Non-militant: no lightsabers, staffs only. Neither light nor dark. [Baran Do][bd]; [Hassat-durr][hd]; [Ayna-seff][ay]
2. **Techniques:**
   - **Weather sense:** amplified senses warn of dangerous weather.
   - ***Hassat-durr*** ("lightning rod") techniques fill the body with electromagnetic radiation. They can disable prosthetics and implants, jam shock shackles, boost comlink range, hide mental patterns, and even release Force lightning "for utility or combat".
   - ***Ayna-seff*** ("dead brain") makes brain activity undetectable and changes how animals perceive the user.
   - **Hidden Ones** faked their deaths with Force stealth.
3. **Feats:** Koro Ziil taught *ayna-seff* to **Jacen Solo** (34 ABY), who took 3 days; **Luke** took 2.
4. **Era:** "thousands of years" before the Kel Dor joined the Republic. Eclipsed by Jedi recruitment and forgotten, they survived the Purge and contacted the New Jedi Order.
5. **Learning:** sages. Practice device: a durasteel sphere held steady over a charged plate.
6. **Costs and dangers:** **Deadly if imperfect:** a student's radiation "was a magnet for lightning", and the user could be struck and killed. Not a dark act; the Baran Do's lightning is utility lightning (**[conflict]** with Force lightning as a dark power; see Part 2b §D, which also indexes hassat-durr).
7. **Counters:** none recorded.
8. **Related:** Force stealth; Force lightning and Electric Judgment (Part 2b §D).
9. **Game stats:** none.

#### T6d. Matukai
- **Classification:** body-channeling adepts. Neither light nor dark. [Matukai/Legends][mat]
- **Techniques:**
  - Force-strengthened bodies; tireless exertion for hours.
  - Holding one stance for "hours or even days".
  - Force speed (Part 1a §C4).
  - Able to awaken Force-sensitivity in beings not naturally gifted.
- **Feats and era:**
  - Founded before the Old Sith Wars by a woman from Karvoss II.
  - Jedi delegation under Bodo Baas (580 BBY).
  - Thame Cerulian never wanted to fight one again.
  - Hunted by Inquisitors.
- **Learning:** wandering groups of 3–5, never more than 50–60 members; apprentice and adept ranks. Weapon: the *wan-shen*.
- **Dangers and counters:** none recorded.

#### T6e. Zeison Sha
- **Classification:** survivalist adepts of Yanibar. Neither light nor dark, but **Dark Zeison Sha** existed. [Zeison Sha][zs]
- **Techniques:** telekinesis "in many ways superior" to the Jedi's, near-instinctual at high levels; the discblade.
- **Era:** founded by Jedi-linked refugees during the New Sith Wars and abandoned when their Jedi died. A Jedi approach failed shortly before 580 BBY.
- **Learning:** at home; no separation from parents.
- **Dangers:** pride and resentment led some to the dark side.
- Telekinesis mechanics: see Part 2a §A.

#### T6f. Jensaarai
- **Classification:** "followers of the hidden truth". A Jedi–Sith blend founded by Nikkos Tyris in the Clone Wars from a corrupting Sith manuscript. Mixed side (**[conflict]**: Sith-derived but largely free of the dark side under the Saarai-kaar). [Jensaarai/Legends][jen]
- **Techniques:**
  - Constant concealment from Vader and from the New Jedi Order (Part 1a §C11).
  - Lightsabers, but more emphasis on animal-styled **armor of spun cortosis fibers** that protects against lightsabers.
  - **Ballistakinesis:** telekinesis that hurls a handful of small objects at lethal speed; inaccurate, but it blankets an area, so evasion is "possible, but difficult" (*Jedi Academy Training Manual*, 2009). [Ballistakinesis §intro][bal]
- **Feats:** survivors hid during the Jedi attack. Later they made peace with the New Republic after Corran Horn's duel (*I, Jedi*, 18 ABY [inferred]).
- **Dangers:** the manuscript corrupted Tyris (see DS2).

#### T6g. Theran Listeners (Theran Force-listening)
- **Classification:** a cult on Nam Chorios that speaks with the Force-sensitive spook-crystals. Healing and foresight. [Theran Listener][thl]; [Theran Force-listening][tfl]
- **Technique:** "hear through the Force": understand other languages and listen to distant speakers.
- **Users:** Qui-Gon and Obi-Wan; Jacen (after the Yuuzhan Vong War); Jaina. Ahsoka Tano is also cited, from *The Clone Wars*.
- **Goal:** keeping the droch plague confined.

#### T6h. Keetael
- **Classification:** a Draethos warrior and hunter tradition of a few hundred adepts. It **uses both light and dark** aspects without following either: side **[conflict]** / neither. [Keetael][kee]
- **Techniques:** Force tracking; meditative farsight to "even interstellar" distances (Part 1b §S9, §S11).
- **Learning:** *Ube-tel* training hunts; a silver disc on completion.
- **Dangers:** pure dark leads warriors to become "vengeful and selfish"; pure light can "cost a victory".

#### T6i. Luka Sene
- **Classification:** Miraluka academic order on Alpheridies. Light-leaning; guardians against the dark side. [Luka Sene][luka]
- **Technique:** **Field Detection**: senses energy fields, comms devices, sensors and shield generators, with their direction. [Field Detection][fd]
- **Dangers:** the talented risk the dark side. When Acaadi turned Sith, Jirany Sha was sent after him.

#### T6j. Disciples of Twilight
- **Classification:** order on Dyspeth, founded c. 900 BBY by "Twilight", rumoured to be an ex-Jedi. Protectors of the weak; still undeclared in 137 ABY. [Disciples of Twilight][dot]
- **Techniques:**
  - **Revelation:** enemies outlined by a shimmering light. [Revelation][rev]
  - **Shadow Vision:** brief low-light vision. It does not beat total concealment. [Shadow Vision][shv]
- **Source:** *Legacy Era Campaign Guide*.

#### T6k. Ysanna and Altisian Jedi
- **Ysanna:**
  - Ossus tribe descended from Jedi trapped by the Cron supernova.
  - They hid from Gnost-Dural's colony.
  - They preserved the secret of surviving death (§T3).
  - They met Luke and Kam Solusar (10–11 ABY [inferred]); Jem and Rayf were trained.
  - No other unique named techniques found. [Ysanna/Legends][ysn]; [Force ghost §History][ghost]
- **Altisian Jedi:**
  - A Jedi splinter under Djinn Altis: multiple apprentices, families, and members who were not Force-sensitive.
  - No unique techniques found. [Altisian Jedi][alt]

#### T6l. Kathol Ascendants and Ta-Ree spells (incl. Shadowstrike)
- **Classification:** Ta-Ree is a manifestation of the Force found only in the Kathol sector. The Kathol Rift disaster twisted space and the Force there, letting many natives use this "spirit energy". **Ascendants** (*gosho-ree*) are those highly skilled in Ta-Ree magic, often shamans or chieftains; any sentient could use some Ta-Ree energy, but Ascendants could use multiple spells. No side is given. [Ta-Ree §intro][taree]; [Ascendant (Kathol) §intro][asc]
- **Limits:** usable only inside the Kathol sector "under most circumstances". When the Kathol were freed, they made sure Ta-Ree energy would never be usable again, though some kept it. [Ta-Ree §intro][taree]
- **Spells (Wookieepedia's Ta-Ree spells category, 48 pages):** examples include Psychokinesis, Sense life, Block pain, Lessen injury, Pyrospray, Generate lightning, Energy blade, Duplicate, Cloak presence and **Shadowstrike**. [Category:Ta-Ree spells][tarc]
- **Shadowstrike:** lets an Ascendant land blows on a target without touching them. A target who is not Force-sensitive cannot dodge the blows. It requires the Psychokinesis and Sense life spells. [Shadowstrike §intro][shs]
- **Lessen injury:** reduces how severe an injury is; it requires Block pain. [Lessen injury §intro][lsi]
- **Era and sources:** *Endgame* (West End Games, Oct 1996) for both spells; Ta-Ree also appears in *The Essential Chronology* (2000), *The Complete Star Wars Encyclopedia* (2008) and the *Jedi Academy Training Manual* (2009). The Kathol Outback setting is the early New Republic era [inferred]. [Shadowstrike §Sources][shs]; [Ta-Ree §Sources][taree]
- **Dangers and counters:** none recorded.
- **Related:** Aing-Tii of the Kathol Rift (§T4); telekinesis (Part 2a §A); Ta-Ree spells parallel ordinary Force powers but are a separate, sector-bound tradition.

## Game notes (suggestions only)
These are suggestions for the developer, not lore claims.
- **Order techniques as unlockable "schools"** tied to a teacher: Baran Do for *ayna-seff*; the Aing-Tii for flow-walking; the Fallanassi for immersion and illusion. Each has the costs Legends itself gives:
  - lightning-magnet risk for *hassat-durr*;
  - getting lost in the flow;
  - no telekinesis for the Fallanassi.
- **Forbidden techniques** (*morichro*, Electric Judgment, Deadly Sight) could need a Master's permission or holocron access, with a Council reputation penalty.
- **Species traits:**
  - Miraluka start with Force sight.
  - Kiffar have a 1% psychometry roll.
  - Iktotchi precognition takes a penalty off their home moon.
