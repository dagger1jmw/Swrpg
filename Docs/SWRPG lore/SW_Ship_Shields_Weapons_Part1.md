## Summary
Legends shields come in two kinds: **ray shields** stop energy fire (lasers, turbolasers) and **particle shields** stop physical objects (torpedoes, missiles, debris); warships run both. Shields soak energy and bleed it into heat sinks; they can be broken by **concentrated fire on one spot**, their **projectors burn out** under load (crews can swap them in minutes on big ships), and on Star Destroyers the **domes on the tower** are both shield generators and targets. Recharge speed is a design trait (the 7500s BBY shield breakthrough made shields "quicker to regenerate"; Mon Calamari cruisers replenish faster because they carry three times the usual number of generators), but **no Legends source gives a recharge time for a capital ship's main shields**. Weapons: turbolasers are two-stage laser cannons with three times a laser cannon's bolt power, firing every two seconds; ion cannons pass ray shields and disable systems for minutes; proton torpedoes and concussion missiles ignore ray shields and are stopped only by particle shields or hull.

### 4. What overwhelms shields

| Method | What the lore says | Citation |
|---|---|---|
| Concentrated fire on one spot | Starship shields handle massive punishment but can be disabled by concentrating fire on a specific location | [Deflector shield/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Deflector_shield/Legends) [unclear] |
| Battery fire control | Capital-ship energy weapons are grouped in batteries by type and hull position (e.g. an ISD's port-dorsal heavy turbolasers form one battery); targeting computers are shared within a battery so gunners can concentrate on one target (EGW) | [Space warfare/Legends, "Weaponry"](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Space_warfare/Legends) |
| Coordinated volleys | Turbolasers are banked and linked by computerised fire control to fire coordinated, sustained volleys (EGWT). | Massed simultaneous torpedoes | Massed fighter torpedo fire damages capital ships if launches are timed to hit roughly simultaneously (*X-Wing: Rogue Squadron*; *Starfighters of Adumar*).
