## Summary
Part 4 covers 3–4 ABY: Hoth, Dagobah and Bespin (3 ABY); *Shadows of the Empire* and the second Death Star plans (3.5 ABY); Jabba's palace, Yoda's death and Endor (4 ABY, 39:3 GrS); then the Empire's fragmentation, the Bakura Incident, the Nagai–Tof War, the Alliance of Free Planets and the Declaration of a New Republic one month after Endor (39:4 GrS). ### Overview
Hoth was an Imperial victory and one of the Rebellion's worst ground defeats, but the leadership escaped [Battle of Hoth/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Hoth/Legends). The Alliance then moved from Golrath Station to Haven Base on Arbra, and after Endor to Bright Tree Village [3 ABY/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/3_ABY/Legends); [4 ABY/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/4_ABY/Legends). Endor (4 ABY, 39:3 GrS) killed Palpatine and Vader, destroyed the second Death Star and the *Executor*, and began the Empire's fragmentation and the Imperial Civil War [Battle of Endor/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Endor/Legends). The Alliance became the transitional Alliance of Free Planets and, a month later, the New Republic [Alliance of Free Planets](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Alliance_of_Free_Planets); [Declaration of a New Republic/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Declaration_of_a_New_Republic/Legends).
