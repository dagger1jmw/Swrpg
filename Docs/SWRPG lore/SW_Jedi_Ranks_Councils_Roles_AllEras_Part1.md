# Star Wars Legends: Jedi Ranks, Councils and Roles Across All Eras (Part 1: to 1000 BBY)

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 21, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
Part 1 of 2, covering the Je'daii (from c. 36,453 BBY) to the Ruusan Reformations (1000 BBY), plus two topics that span all eras: leadership titles (with the full sourced Grand Master list) and how Jedi became Masters. The key facts are the nine-Temple-Master Je'daii Council; the early Order's loose councils and Jedi Assembly, when Knights had full legal autonomy; the High Council's disputed origin (Force Wars to after 3997 BBY); the regional council hierarchy (Taris under Dantooine under the High Council) and the Order's collapse to about a hundred Jedi by 3952 BBY; and the New Sith Wars' Jedi Lords and Grand Council, disbanded at Ruusan. The cross-era overview table at the start covers both parts. Part 2 (SW_Jedi_Ranks_Councils_Roles_AllEras_Part2.md) covers 1000 BBY to 138 ABY.

### Je'daii Council and ranks (Je'daii era)
- **Type:** institution and rank
- **Era:** Tython, from c. 36,453 BBY (Tho Yor Arrival) to the Force Wars (c. 25,793 BBY); Temple Masters listed c. 25,793 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - The Je'daii were governed by the Je'daii Council, also called the "Masters' Council" or "Council of Masters". It was made up of the nine Temple Masters, each heading one of the nine Temples. [Je'daii Order §Structure](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Je'daii_Order)
  - The number of Temple Masters could differ from nine: the Selkath couple Calleh and Naro jointly held Mahara Kesh. [Je'daii Order §Structure](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Je'daii_Order)
  - The Council met by holoconference or in person at Kaleth. It could exile Je'daii who strayed too far toward either side of the Force to the moons Ashla or Bogan. [Je'daii Order §Structure](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Je'daii_Order)
  - Rank ladder (training detail is in the Training files): Padawan, Journeyer, Ranger, Master, Temple Master. The Council deployed Rangers across the Tython system on missions requested by the Council or the Settled Worlds. Rangers wore a "Ranger star" and chose a Hunter-class starfighter or a Peacemaker-class cruiser. [Je'daii Order §Structure](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Je'daii_Order)
  - **Master rank:** the Council bestowed it on Je'daii whose understanding of the Ashla and the Bogan warranted it. Some Masters taught at the Temples; others withdrew into seclusion and only appeared in times of danger. [Je'daii Master](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Je'daii_Master); [Je'daii Order §Structure](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Je'daii_Order)
  - Temple Masters (c. 25,793 BBY): Ketu (Akar Kesh), Quan-Jang (Anil Kesh), Jake Fenn (Bodhi), Naro and Calleh (Mahara Kesh), Kora Ryo (Kaleth), Ruhr (Padawan Kesh), Lha-Mi (Stav Kesh), Miarta Sek (Qigong Kesh), Tem Madog (Vur Tepe). [Je'daii Temple Master §The Temple Masters](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Je'daii_Temple_Master)
  - Other Council members listed: Telaat (Akar Kesh), Nordia Gral (Padawan Kesh, its first Temple Master by 36,019 BBY) and Vor'Dana (Stav Kesh). [Je'daii Council](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Je'daii_Council); [Je'daii Order §Early history](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Je'daii_Order)
- **Related:** SW_Jedi_Training_AllEras_Part1.md (Journeyer and Great Journey); SW_Force_Traditions_AllEras.md
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Early Jedi Order governance (to c. 4000 BBY)
- **Type:** institution
- **Era:** Force Wars (c. 25,793 BBY) to c. 4000 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - "From the Jedi Order's founding, a council of Masters governed its members." The founders Rajivari and Garon Jard held week-long councils to grow the Order's wisdom and defend it from the dark side. [Jedi Council/Legends §Councils of the Old Jedi Order](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Council/Legends)
  - The early council was "structureless and fluid" and had authority over recruiting and training new Padawans. [Jedi High Council/Legends §Early history](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council/Legends)
  - As the Order grew, trusted Masters also met in a **Jedi Assembly**, created mainly to watch renegade Jedi and counter dark-side splinter groups. It urged Masters to take stewardship of new systems. [Jedi Council/Legends §Councils of the Old Jedi Order](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Council/Legends)
  - The Jedi joined the Republic as peacekeepers 730 years after the Order's founding. These early Knights had no central leadership and no academy; a Knight was made by their own Master after a series of tasks. [Jedi Knight/Legends §Overview and history](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Knight/Legends)
  - **Relationship to government:** at first informal, "governed by honor and tradition rather than law". Knights had "absolute autonomy"; even senior Masters could not legally veto a Jedi's chosen course; no law bound the Order to serve the state. [Jedi Order/Legends §Relations with the Galactic Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order/Legends)
  - The High Council had 12 members "as early as the Great Hyperspace War" (5000 BBY). [Jedi Order/Legends §Leadership](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order/Legends) Another source says it may then have been only an "inner circle" within the assembly, with no fixed meeting place, meeting on Ossus, Chandrila, Alderaan and elsewhere. [Jedi High Council/Legends intro and §Early history](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council/Legends)
  - In the Pius Dea era, most Jedi refused to fight the government; some fought as "Knights errant" without the High Council's permission, and others joined a splinter group, the Order of the Terrible Glare. [Jedi Order/Legends §The beginning of the Sith](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order/Legends)
  - Grand Masters: Awdrysta Pina (by 24,500 BBY) and Biel Ductavis (appointed 10,966 BBY). [Grand Master/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grand_Master/Legends)
  - Jedi Shadows were sent after the Great Hyperspace War to destroy Sith artifacts. [Jedi Shadow §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Shadow)
  - The first Lore Keepers served on Tython and tended the "Fount of Rajivari", the first Jedi Archives. [Lore Keeper §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lore_Keeper)

### Great Sith War era leadership (c. 4000–3986 BBY)
- **Type:** institution and leadership
- **Era:** c. 4000–3986 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - As of 3997 BBY the High Council had been "formally created" and was making decisions by the time of Exar Kun's fall. [Jedi High Council/Legends §Early history](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council/Legends)
  - In this period the High Council was "largely ceremonial" with "limited direct authority over individual Jedi" (*KOTOR Campaign Guide*). [Jedi High Council/Legends §Early history](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council/Legends)
  - In the early Old Sith Wars, groups of Masters were stationed on Ossus and later at the Coruscant Temple. [Jedi Council/Legends §Councils of the Old Jedi Order](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Council/Legends)
  - The Dantooine Enclave was presided over by Vodo-Siosk Baas until Exar Kun killed him; afterwards a council governed it. [Dantooine Jedi Enclave Council](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dantooine_Jedi_Enclave_Council)
  - After the war, Nomi Sunrider was recognized as overall leader of the Order at the Conclave at Exis Station and held the title "Councilor" on Coruscant. [Jedi High Council/Legends §Early history](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council/Legends) Her Grand Master period is listed from 3986 BBY. [Grand Master/Legends §List of Grand Masters](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grand_Master/Legends)
  - The Knight article says the High Council was founded after the Great Sith War, when the Republic gave the Knights a spire on Coruscant. [Jedi Knight/Legends §Overview and history](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Knight/Legends)
  - Arca Jeth was appointed Watchman of the Onderon system. [Jedi Watchman/Legends §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Watchman/Legends)
  - "Thousands of Jedi" gathered at the Deneba conclave after Koros Major, and thousands raised the Wall of Light against Exar Kun. [Nomi Sunrider](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nomi_Sunrider)

### Mandalorian Wars to First Jedi Purge (c. 3976–3950 BBY)
- **Type:** institutions and events
- **Era:** c. 3976–3950 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Regional councils:** academies far from Coruscant had smaller councils for direct oversight, acting "as watchmen for a large region of space"; High Council members sometimes sat on them. [Jedi High Council/Legends §Organization and politics](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council/Legends)
  - Councils of the Almas, Taris and Telos academies are named. [Jedi High Council/Legends §Organization and politics](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council/Legends)
  - **Dantooine Enclave Council:** four Masters, described as a subset of the High Council because Vrook Lamar and Vandar Tokare sat on both. It ran the Enclave and supervised other academy councils such as Taris. [Dantooine Jedi Enclave Council](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dantooine_Jedi_Enclave_Council)
    - Members at the Great Hunt: Lamar, Bala Nisi, Qual, Aleco Stusea.
    - Mandalorian Wars: Lamar, Zhar Lestin, Nisi, Tokare.
    - Jedi Civil War: Dorak replaced Nisi.
    - It was dissolved when Malak bombarded the Enclave. [Jedi Council/Legends §Dantooine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Council/Legends); [Dantooine Jedi Enclave Council §Members of the Council](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dantooine_Jedi_Enclave_Council)
  - **Tarisian Council (3964 BBY):** Masters Lucien Draay, Feln, Q'Anilia, Raana Tey and Xamar. It answered to Dantooine, which answered to the High Council. It was secretly overseen by the Covenant; its graduating Padawans were murdered by their Masters. [Jedi Council/Legends §Taris](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Council/Legends) The High Council recalled it to Coruscant, dissolved it and closed the Taris academy. [Jedi High Council/Legends §Early history](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council/Legends)
  - **Jedi Covenant:** a secret group founded after the Great Sith War by Krynda Draay. Even the Enclave Council and High Council did not know of it. [Jedi Covenant](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Covenant) Haazen had Lucien Draay appointed to the High Council. [Jedi High Council/Legends §Early history](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council/Legends)
  - **Neutrality and the Revanchists:** the High Council told all Jedi to stay out of the Mandalorian Wars. [Jedi High Council/Legends §Early history](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council/Legends) Revan's Jedi acted against the Council's wishes. The Council later approved Draay's motion to recall or even detain the Revanchists, but finally allowed them into the war. [Revanchist](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Revanchist)
  - **Trial of Meetra Surik (3959 BBY):** the High Council (Kavar, Vrook Lamar, Lonna Vash, Atris, Zez-Kai Ell) exiled her. She was the only Jedi to come back and face the Council after the war. She was told to surrender her lightsaber. [Trial of Meetra Surik](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trial_of_Meetra_Surik) The vote to excommunicate was unanimous. [Jedi High Council/Legends §Early history](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council/Legends) Zez-Kai Ell left the Order the same day. [Trial of Meetra Surik](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trial_of_Meetra_Surik)
  - **Collapse:** after the Jedi Civil War the Order numbered "less than one-hundred". The surviving Council disbanded the Order, its Masters going into hiding, and the Coruscant Temple was abandoned. [Jedi Order/Legends §First Jedi Purge and aftermath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order/Legends)
  - The Conclave at Katarr (3952 BBY) gathered the "last known hundred" Jedi; Darth Nihilus killed all of them. [Conclave at Katarr](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Conclave_at_Katarr)
  - The Watchmen had been extinct for decades by the Triumvirate's rule. [Jedi Watchman/Legends §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Watchman/Legends)
  - The surviving Masters Lamar, Kavar and Zez-Kai Ell, meeting as a High Council in the rebuilt Dantooine Enclave, tried to cut Surik off from the Force and were killed by Kreia. [Jedi Council/Legends §Dantooine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Council/Legends)
  - **Rebuilding:** Surik re-established the High Council with her companions. [Jedi Order/Legends §First Jedi Purge and aftermath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order/Legends) Mical "was believed" to have been made a Master and given a Council seat. [Jedi High Council/Legends §Early history](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council/Legends)
  - Known High Council members c. 3963–3951 BBY: Atris, Kavar, Zez-Kai Ell, Vrook Lamar, Lonna Vash, Vandar Tokare; Dorjander Kace (until 3963 BBY); Lucien Draay (3963 BBY); Mical (from 3951 BBY). [Jedi High Council/Legends §Known members](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council/Legends)

### Great Galactic War to the New Sith Wars (3600–1000 BBY)
- **Type:** institutions and leadership
- **Era:** 3653 BBY to 1000 BBY
- **Key facts (beyond the developer's 3700–3600 BBY rank and High Council file):**
  - After the Great Galactic War, Council members were rarely seen together, for security and because of their mobile duties. Past disasters (Katarr, the Sacking of Coruscant) led the Order to set wartime succession protocols: Jedi summoned to replace fallen members often served only until stability returned (SWTOR, *TOR Encyclopedia*). [Jedi High Council/Legends §Early history](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council/Legends)
  - **Barsen'thor** ("Warden of the Order", from Cerean) was a ceremonial title given only to exceptional Jedi; by the Cold War it had been given twice before Yuon Par's apprentice received it. [Barsen'thor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Barsen'thor) That Jedi took Syo Bakarn's vacant Council seat. [Jedi Consular §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Consular)
  - **Grand Masters:** Zym (until 3653 BBY, killed after the fall of the Temple), then Satele Shan (c. 3651–3636 BBY), "the youngest known" Grand Master. [Grand Master/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grand_Master/Legends); [Jedi High Council/Legends §Known members](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council/Legends)
  - Tasiele Shan was exiled to an Outer Rim world by the Republic and the Order for challenging the Order's views on attachment and marriage. [Exile (punishment)/Legends §Examples of exile](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Exile_%28punishment%29/Legends) A Jedi named Allya was exiled to Dathomir in 600 BBY. [Exile (punishment)/Legends §Examples of exile](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Exile_%28punishment%29/Legends)
  - **New Sith Wars, Jedi Lords:** with the Republic too weak to defend systems outside the Core, Jedi who freed whole systems became "barons or kings" there, built castles and passed their titles to their children. [Jedi Lord §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Lord)
  - Even the Senate had Jedi Supreme Chancellors, and the High Council competed directly with the Jedi Grand Council. [Jedi Lord §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Lord)
  - The Order "actively ruled the Republic as a quasi-military dictatorship" in this period. [Jedi Order/Legends §Leadership](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order/Legends)
  - **Jedi Grand Council:** the governing body of the Jedi Lords, outside the High Council's jurisdiction. [Jedi Grand Council §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Grand_Council)
  - **Army of Light:**
    - The Grand Council's last meeting pledged unanimous support to Lord Hoth's Army of Light and put all feudal holdings and vassals under his command. [Jedi Grand Council §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Grand_Council)
    - Hoth, the Temple's battlemaster, was "Seneschal of the Army" as recognized by the Grand Council. [Army of Light/Legends §Organization and structure](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Army_of_Light/Legends)
    - The Army was made of Seven Legions of Light, each led by one of the greatest Lords (e.g. Newar Forrth, Third Legion), with Knights, esquires and servants from the baronies. [Army of Light/Legends §Organization and structure](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Army_of_Light/Legends)
  - Skere Kaan, the High Council's chosen champion, broke from the Order and founded the Brotherhood of Darkness in 1010 BBY. [Army of Light/Legends §Prelude](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Army_of_Light/Legends)
  - Self-declaring the rank of Master was "not uncommon" in the Old and New Sith Wars, as the Order was not centrally controlled. [Jedi Master/Legends §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Master/Legends)

### Leadership titles and Grand Masters
- **Type:** ranks and titles
- **Era:** all eras
- **Key facts:**
  - **Grand Master:**
    - The recognized head of the whole Order, appointed by the High Council with a unanimous vote and given only to the greatest Masters. It was "not unheard of" for several Masters to hold it at once, "forming elite guilds before the Ruusan Reformation" (*The Jedi Path*). [Grand Master/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grand_Master/Legends)
    - It was normally held for life or until given up. The Grand Master spoke for the Council to politicians and military leaders and broke ties on the Council (SWTOR, *TOR Encyclopedia*). [Grand Master/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grand_Master/Legends)
  - **Master of the Order:**
    - The elected leader of the High Council. The sitting members choose by unanimous vote, and it was often the same person as the Grand Master. [Master of the Order/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Master_of_the_Order/Legends)

### Rank above Knight: becoming a Master
- **Type:** rank
- **Era:** all eras (era differences noted)
- **Key facts:**
  - The highest formal rank in the Order. Masters were the smallest share of the membership, and the rank was given to "very few" Knights in each generation. [Jedi Master/Legends §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Master/Legends)
  - **Old Republic route (*The Jedi Path*):** the commonest path was training several Padawans to Knighthood, typically one after another, with all passing the Trials. Alternatives were a modified version of the Trials of Knighthood, or noted service to the Republic. Each could win the High Council's acknowledgment and promotion. [Jedi Master/Legends §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Master/Legends)
  - The High Council might raise a Knight who trained a Padawan to Knighthood, "though this was not always the case": some were promoted in old age, and some never. [Jedi Knight/Legends §Service to the Order](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Knight/Legends)
  - **Self-declaration:** strongly discouraged and rare in the Golden Age, but "not uncommon" in the Old and New Sith Wars and in times of disbandment. Examples are Jorus C'baoth and his clone Joruus. [Jedi Master/Legends §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Master/Legends); [Jedi Order/Legends §Jedi ranks](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order/Legends)
  - **No formal rank between Knight and Master** was found. The nearest are:
    - a Knight sitting on the Council during the transition [Jedi Knight/Legends §Service to the Order](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Knight/Legends);
    - the "Old Guard" under consideration for Council seats [Jedi Master/Legends §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Master/Legends);
    - honorifics such as Warrior or Sage Master [Jedi Sentinel/Legends §Philosophy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Sentinel/Legends).
  - **Master privileges:** a few Temple chambers were sealed to non-Masters; the Holocron Vaults and the Treasury were open only to High Council members. [Jedi Master/Legends §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Master/Legends)
  - **Je'daii:** the Council bestowed Master on Rangers. [Je'daii Master](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Je'daii_Master)
  - **New Jedi Order:** Luke Skywalker had to promote himself because no Master was left to do it. [Jedi Master/Legends §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Master/Legends) He became the reborn Order's first Master after the cloned Emperor's defeat. [New Jedi Order §Last of the Old, First of the New](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/New_Jedi_Order) He promoted students by their mastery of the Force; as the Order grew, most Knights became Masters for "extraordinary deeds" for the New Republic, for example Kyle Katarn after defeating Desann. [Jedi Master/Legends §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Master/Legends)
  - **Je'daii Temple Master:** a rank above Master (see Je'daii entry). [Je'daii Temple Master](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Je'daii_Temple_Master)
- **Related:** SW_Jedi_Training_AllEras_Part1.md and Part2 (knighting); SW_Jedi_Order_Ranks_Council_OldRepublic.md
- **Uncertainty:** [conflict] How many Padawans: training "several" Padawans in a row was the usual path [Jedi Master/Legends §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Master/Legends) vs training "a Padawan" successfully to Knighthood [Jedi Order/Legends §Jedi ranks](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order/Legends); [Jedi Knight/Legends §Service to the Order](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Knight/Legends). Both cite *The Jedi Path*; "a Padawan" appears in two articles.
