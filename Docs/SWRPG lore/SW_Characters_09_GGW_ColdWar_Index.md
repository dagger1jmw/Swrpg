# Era 09 Characters: Great Galactic War, Cold War and early Galactic War (c. 3681–3636 BBY), Index

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 21, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited in each part. Draft for review.

**Set:** this Index (master index, open questions, inclusion rules, topic-file conflicts); SW_Characters_09_GGW_ColdWar_Part1.md (Jedi and light-side users, 39 characters); SW_Characters_09_GGW_ColdWar_Part2.md (Sith and other dark-side users, 56); SW_Characters_09_GGW_ColdWar_Part3a.md (Republic non-Force figures, 39); SW_Characters_09_GGW_ColdWar_Part3b.md (Imperial non-Force figures, Mandalorians, underworld and others, 49). Group 3 was split into 3a and 3b to keep each file under ~70 KB; since that makes more than three parts, this Index was added.

## Summary
This set is the source for the game's canon NPC roster for Era 09 (3681–3636 BBY; default start 3661 BBY). Every fact is cited to a Legends Wookieepedia page (SWTOR and TCW 2008 sources count). Each character has a status for 3661 BBY and for 3653 BBY, where "not stated" is a valid answer. The old file's numeric game stats (attributes, Force scores, form ratings, tiers) are design estimates; the sources say nothing about any of them (see the tally below). Headline corrections: Vindican was a Pureblood who died in 3681 BBY; Ngani Zho was not killed by Malgus; Dar'Nala was a Togruta; Darth Nyriss belongs to Era 08; the Dread Masters were jailed on Belsavis from 3668 BBY to c. 3641 BBY; Malgus was not killed at Ilum; and several old death years were off by one to three years (Baras 3640 BBY, Jadus's faked death 3643 BBY, Marr c. 3636 BBY).

Missing developer files: SW_Force_Powers_* and the developer's SW_Jedi_Training / Sith_Training files are **not on the box**, so they could not be cross-checked. The other listed topic files were used to find names: GGW Part1–3, Power Structures, Jedi Ranks, Korriban, Jedi Temple Life, Coruscant, Mandalorians, Nar Shaddaa, Republic Military, Imperial Military Intelligence, Dromund Kaas and Lightsaber_*.

## Entries

### Inclusion criteria
- **Included:** (1) every character in the old file; (2) every named person in the topic files with a role between 3681 BBY and 3636 BBY; (3) the companions and key NPCs of the eight SWTOR class stories; (4) named office-holders (Chancellors, Supreme Commanders, Dark Councilors, ministers, Mandalores, Hutt bosses) found while checking (1)–(3).
- **Class protagonists** (Hero of Tython, Barsen'thor, Emperor's Wrath, Darth Nox, Cipher Nine, Grand Champion, Voidhound, Meteor) appear by title only, with no species, sex or birth year, because these are player-chosen.
- **Placement:** by the developer's three groups. Force-users who served as soldiers or spies (Ardun Kothe, Warren Sedoru) are in Part1; fallen Jedi who became Sith (Sajar, Parkanas Tark, Ako Domi) are listed where their page puts their main role.
- **Old-file items with no source** are marked in each part's "Changes" section.

### Answers to the four open questions
1. **When and how did Darth Vindican die?** In 3681 BBY on the Korriban orbital station. Kao Cen Darach wounded him and his apprentice Malgus executed him [Darth Vindican §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Vindican); (infobox) [Darth Vindican](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Vindican). He was a Pureblood, not a Human [Darth Vindican](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Vindican). See Part2 › Darth Vindican.
2. **Did Malgus kill Ngani Zho, and when did Zho die?** No. Zho vanished in 3653 BBY. Ten years later he was killed in the Vesla system by Sith troopers while defending Teff'ith; the infobox gives c. 3643 BBY aboard the Sun Razer [Ngani Zho §Cold War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ngani_Zho); [Ngani Zho](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ngani_Zho). See Part1 › Ngani Zho.
3. **How old was Aric Jorgan in 3661 BBY?** Born 3673 BBY (infobox) [Aric Jorgan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aric_Jorgan), so about 12 [inferred]. His page text gives only his birthplace, Rendili [Aric Jorgan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aric_Jorgan). See Part3a › companions table.
4. **Who was Orgus Din: species, homeworld, birth?** A male Human from Rishi, known as the "Plain Jedi". He fought in the Minos Cluster, trained Fortris Gall, Bengel Morr and the Hero of Tython, sat on the Council on Tython, and was killed by Darth Angral in 3643 BBY aboard the Oppressor [Orgus Din](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Orgus_Din); [Orgus Din §Hunting the Republic superweapons](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Orgus_Din). **No birth year exists in any source.** See Part1 › Orgus Din.
