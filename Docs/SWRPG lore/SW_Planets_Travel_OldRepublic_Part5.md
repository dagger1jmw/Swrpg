# Part C — Travel

## C1. Technology
- **Hyperdrive basics.** A ship can jump only when it is reasonably clear of a major body's gravity well, so it uses sublight engines to climb out first. An automatic failsafe cuts the hyperdrive when a gravity well lies in the path. Engineers exploit this failsafe to build artificial gravity wells that pull ships out of hyperspace (interdiction) [Hyperdrive/Legends §Safety mechanisms](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hyperdrive/Legends). Inertial dampers, null quantum field generators, shields and stasis fields protect ship and crew in transit [Hyperdrive/Legends §Safety mechanisms](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hyperdrive/Legends).
- **Detectable signatures.** Entering and leaving hyperspace produces wake rotation and Cronau radiation. The signature is used to track fleet movements and by planetary customs [Hyperdrive/Legends §Entering hyperspace](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hyperdrive/Legends). (General Legends; not dated to the era.)
- **Navicomputers and astrogation.** Calculating a jump from a known position to a place never travelled to before can take the navicomputer a few hours [Astrogation/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Astrogation/Legends). By the Mandalorian Wars (c. 3976 BBY, before this window) navicomputers could store millions of routes, making beacons redundant [Hyperspace beacon/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hyperspace_beacon/Legends).
- **Beacons.** The Republic Spacelane Bureau maintained and patrolled beacons. Pirates and Hutts sometimes reprogrammed them to send ships into deep space for ambush, and old beacons were rumoured to guide Hutt slave ships [Hyperspace beacon/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hyperspace_beacon/Legends). (Pre-window background; whether beacons still mattered in 3661 is [unclear].)
- **Routes age.** Galactic rotation makes mapped routes obsolete within decades [Hyperspace route/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hyperspace_route/Legends). In-era examples: the Tython lanes erased by the Deep Core [Tython/Legends §Rediscovery](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tython/Legends) and the Kyyr supernova damage to the Ootmian Pabol (c. 4000 BBY) [Ootmian Pabol/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ootmian_Pabol/Legends).
- **Interdiction.** Zabrak engineers developed interdiction technology in the Mandalorian Wars. New hyperdrive sensors and multiphase null field units soon made those interdictors useless [Interdiction field/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Interdiction_field/Legends). No interdictor ship is attested for 3681–3600 BBY [unclear]; see Not found.
- **Mines.** In the Galactic War (3641 BBY onward), Mandalorian mercenaries hired by the Empire laid a minefield to blockade the Hydian Way and steer Republic convoys into an ambush [Hydian Way/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hydian_Way/Legends). A Republic attempt to mine the Bimmiel asteroid field (Kanz sector) was stopped by Imperial forces (3639 BBY) [Bimmiel asteroid field](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bimmiel_asteroid_field); [3639 BBY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/3639_BBY). No mine use is recorded for 3661 [unclear].
- **Natural hazards.** Asteroid fields and nebulae can greatly lengthen trips between nearby worlds [Hyperspace/Legends §Hyperspace travel times](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hyperspace/Legends). The Maelstrom Nebula forces sublight travel on the Relgim Run [Maelstrom Nebula/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Maelstrom_Nebula/Legends). A nebula has cut off Ruhnuk since 3663 BBY (Part A, Ruhnuk). The Deep Core's gravity wells make it slow to cross [Hyperspace/Legends §Hyperspace travel times](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hyperspace/Legends).
- **Gree hypergates.** Instant transport between paired gates (Asation, Oricon; Grand Hypergates to Gree and its colonies). Galactic War era, c. 3639–3638 BBY [Hypergate](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hypergate); [Grand Hypergate](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grand_Hypergate).

## C5. Documents, customs and crossing between Republic and Imperial space
- **In 3661 (war):** no travel-permit or border-crossing rule is on record for wartime [unclear]. The smuggler Voidhound was flagged for an ID error c. 3643 BBY; Diplomatic Spaceport 27-B incident 3640 BBY [Customs Enforcement Division §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Customs_Enforcement_Division).

## C6. Travel-time model (summary)
- **What the lore supports:**
  - Travel follows charted lanes; trunk routes are listed in Part B.
  - Speed scales with hyperdrive class as a multiplier of a base time [Hyperdrive/Legends §Classes](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hyperdrive/Legends).
  - Plotting an uncharted jump takes hours [Astrogation/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Astrogation/Legends).
  - Hazards (nebulae, Deep Core, asteroid fields) lengthen or block trips [Hyperspace/Legends §Hyperspace travel times](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hyperspace/Legends).
  - Ships must climb out of gravity wells before jumping, and gravity wells can pull them out [Hyperdrive/Legends §Safety mechanisms](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hyperdrive/Legends).
  - Blockades can close a whole route segment for a year.
  - Gated worlds (Part A field 6) have no known route.
- **Game stats only:** every specific class number for era ships except the *Ascendant Spear*. Those come from the KOTOR Campaign Guide (2008) and are labelled (game stat: …).
- **Gaps:** base trip times between any two named worlds in the era; hours or days per grid square; any era-specific speed rating for common freighters or Imperial capital ships; wartime border documents. A travel system needs a designer-set base table here; flagged in Decisions needed.
