# Lightsaber Forms (All Legends Eras), Part 2: Other Forms, Principles and Styles

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 21, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
Covers the non-numbered forms and principles: Sokan, Tràkata, Jar'Kai, Dun Möch, Form Zero, Su ma, Lus-ma, Trispzest, Juyo-Kos, mounted and telekinetic saber combat, the New Jedi Order's Strong/Medium/Fast styles and three rings, and the Imperial Knights' Praetoria styles. Eras run from Sokan's creation in the Great Hyperspace War (5000 BBY) to the Legacy era. Key facts: Tràkata and Dun Möch rely on deception and were avoided or debated among Jedi. The NJO styles were stopgaps before the classic forms were rediscovered.

## Entries

### Sokan (principle, not a true form)
- **Type:** combat principle
- **Era:** Created 5000 BBY (Great Hyperspace War); applied to 19 BBY
- **Key facts:**

- **Creation:** Developed by ancient Jedi Knights during the Great Hyperspace War (5000 BBY). [Sokan §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sokan) [Great Hyperspace War/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Hyperspace_War/Legends)
- **Core idea:** Use the environment for tactical advantage.
  - Tumble, jump and move to strike vital areas.
  - Maneuver foes into vulnerable terrain.
  - High mobility with "no openings" in defense. [Sokan §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sokan)
- **Eras:** Before the Clone Wars, users blended in Ataru's kinetic elements. Study of it was available inside the Temple. [Sokan §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sokan) [Lightsaber combat/Legends §The Unorthodox and Hybrid Fighting Methods](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends)
- **Best on:** Home ground or familiar terrain. [Sokan §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sokan)
- **Moves:** High Ground Defense and Unhindered Charge (ignore terrain and obstacles). [Sokan §Known Moves and Maneuvers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sokan)
- **Applications:**
  - Maul used an "unorthodox" Sokan: he used the Theed laser gates to split Qui-Gon from Obi-Wan (32 BBY).
  - Yoda used a three-point leap around the hangar against Dooku (22 BBY).
  - Kenobi used elements of it on Mustafar by taking the high ground; whether he had formal training is [unclear]. Afterwards Vader used saber throws instead of chasing opponents who held the high ground. [Sokan §Applications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sokan)
- **Light/dark lean:** None stated.

- **Related:** Form IV (Ataru); Duels: Naboo 32 BBY, Mustafar 19 BBY
- **Uncertainty:** [unclear] Kenobi used elements of it on Mustafar by taking the high ground; whether he had formal training is .…

### Tràkata
- **Type:** lightsaber technique/form
- **Era:** Old Republic (no date) to the Yuuzhan Vong War
- **Key facts:**

- **Creation:** A Jedi Consular "in the days of the Old Republic", to exploit the saber's on/off ability. [Tràkata/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tràkata/Legends)
- **Strengths:**
  - Feints made by switching the blade off and on.
  - Combined with Jar'Kai, it was "extremely effective even against multiple opponents". [Tràkata/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tràkata/Legends)
  - Deactivating the blade gave "greater control over the Force" by removing a distraction (Saga Edition Core Rulebook). [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Tràkata](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends)
- **Moves:**
  - Pass the Blade: switch off, slip past the block, then reignite into the foe.
  - Unbalancing Block: catch the blade, then switch off so the foe stumbles.
  - Flash Slash: ignite only for the swing. [Tràkata/Legends §Known moves and maneuvers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tràkata/Legends) [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Form-specific Maneuvers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends)
- **Light/dark lean:** Built on deception. Jedi avoided it because they disliked relying on deception. Sith struggled with it because their power came from passion, not cool tactics. [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Tràkata](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends)
- **Users:**
  - Kao Cen Darach ("this or a similar technique", vs Vindican and Malgus, Great Galactic War) [unclear].
  - Mara Jade Skywalker (NJO).
  - Xizor (vs Jax Pavan's lightwhip).
  - Corran Horn (killed Shedao Shai on Ithor, Yuuzhan Vong War).
  - Tahiri Veila (vs Ben Skywalker). [Tràkata/Legends §Applications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tràkata/Legends)

- **Related:** Jar'Kai; SW_Lightsaber_CombatTechniques_AllEras.md
- **Uncertainty:** [unclear] Kao Cen Darach ("this or a similar technique", vs Vindican and Malgus, Great Galactic War) .

### Jar'Kai (dual-blade combat)
- **Type:** lightsaber form (dual-blade)
- **Era:** Pre-saber Atrisian origin; Jedi, Sith and others in all eras to the Legacy era
- **Key facts:**

- **Origin:**
  - The Yovshin Swordsmen of the Atrisian Empire created it as a sword style, named for the Atrisian city where the original Jar'Kai dueling sabers were forged. The Jedi later adopted it. [Jar'Kai/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jar%27Kai/Legends) [Yovshin Swordsmen](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yovshin_Swordsmen)
  - Niman (Kashi Mer) was a separate dual-blade root. [Jar'Kai/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jar%27Kai/Legends)
  - "Jar'Kai" also became a blanket term ("the Jar'Kai tactic") for using two blades at all, with or without training. [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Jar'Kai (dual-blade) combat](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends)
- **Strengths:**
  - Keeps up a strong offense: attack with one blade while the other guards, or both attack in a "wave-front".
  - Easier to hold off several foes.
  - Works as a counter to other multi-blade fighters.
  - Easy to graft onto other forms (Ventress + Makashi; Windu and Billaba + Vaapad). [Jar'Kai/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jar%27Kai/Legends)
- **Weaknesses:**
  - No two-handed blows, and weaker blocks, so a strong single-hilt fighter can batter through (Anakin vs Ventress, Yavin 4).
  - Lightsaber gyroscopic effects make two blades harder to control, so special training is needed. Without it the second blade is a hindrance.
  - Users may neglect the Force (Raskta Lsu).
  - Users may come to depend on the second blade (Ventress, Ahsoka). [Jar'Kai/Legends §Weaknesses](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jar%27Kai/Legends)
  - Maul found it "predictable". [Jar'Kai/Legends §Weaknesses](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jar%27Kai/Legends)
- **Moves:** Rising Whirlwind and Twin Strike (a dual power swing). [Jar'Kai/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jar%27Kai/Legends)
- **Weapons:**
  - Classic Niman dual-blade drills use a shoto in the off-hand (Bulq, Aayla Secura); Maris Brood used two guard shotos.
  - Off-hand items can also be electrostaffs, lightsaber-resistant swords, knives or a lightwhip.
  - More than two blades by anatomy: Skwelli used six; Grievous used four; Pong Krell used two double-bladed sabers. [Jar'Kai/Legends §Weapon Specifications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jar%27Kai/Legends)
- **Variant — dual-saber Ataru:** Kas'im kept a secret two-saber Form IV. He discouraged students from Jar'Kai, calling it flawed. [Jar'Kai/Legends §Alternative Uses](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jar%27Kai/Legends) [Kas'im](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kas%27im)
- **Opponents' views:** Dooku thought dual-blade styles inferior. [Jar'Kai/Legends §Practitioners](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jar%27Kai/Legends)
- **Practitioners by era (selection):**
  - Ancient: Awdrysta Pina (Jedi katanas), Kavar, Bastila Shan, Vandar Tokare, Fortris Gall.
  - Jedi Civil War: many Dark Jedi under Revan and Malak; Traya's Sith.
  - New Sith Wars: the Dark Underlord (two Sith swords), Raskta Lsu, Newar Forrth.
  - Clone Wars: Grievous, Ventress, Bulq, Saato, Keelyvine Reus, Pong Krell, Ahsoka Tano, Barriss Offee.
  - Purge: Maris Brood, Starkiller.
  - New Reborn (14 ABY): Alora.
  - NJO: Luke Skywalker (shoto, vs Lumiya at Roqoo Depot, 40 ABY) and Lomi Plo.
  - Legacy: Darth Krayt, Wyyrlok III, Darth Reave. [Jar'Kai/Legends §Practitioners](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jar%27Kai/Legends) [Jar'Kai/Legends §Alternative Uses](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jar%27Kai/Legends)
- **Light/dark lean:** None stated. The Jedi Council frowned on the related saberstaff (see WeaponVariants). [inferred]

- **Related:** Form VI (Niman); SW_Lightsaber_WeaponVariants_AllEras.md (shoto, saberstaff); SubgroupStyles (Yovshin Swordsmen)
- **Uncertainty:** [inferred] Light/dark lean: None stated. The Jedi Council frowned on the related saberstaff (see WeaponVariants).

### Dun Möch (Sith psychological technique)
- **Type:** Sith technique
- **Era:** Old Republic to the Clone Wars (examples c. 3951 BBY to 19 BBY)
- **Key facts:**

- **What:** Distraction and doubt, usually by taunting, during saber combat. Mental attacks strip the opponent's Force concentration and erode their will. It is "the Force as a form of psychological warfare". [Dun Möch/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dun_Möch/Legends)
- **Requirement:** Great patience, rare among the Brotherhood-era Sith. [Dun Möch/Legends §Usage](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dun_Möch/Legends)
  - Bane vs Sirak at the Korriban academy: he drew the fight out, withheld the killing blow, and let his rage build before a disarming strike. Kas'im praised it as a lesson to all. [Dun Möch/Legends §Usage](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dun_Möch/Legends) [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Dun Möch](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends)
- **Extended (strategic) use:**
  - Desann faked Jan Ors's death.
  - Traya fed false intelligence that drew Nihilus to his death.
  - Sidious's Clone Wars is called an "extreme" Dun Möch. [Dun Möch/Legends §Usage](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dun_Möch/Legends)
- **By Jedi:** Possible but debated: Surik vs Sion on Malachor V ("theorized") and Kenobi taunting Ventress on Teth ("may not have actually been Dun Möch"). [Dun Möch/Legends §Usage](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dun_Möch/Legends) [unclear]
- **Light/dark lean:** Dark (a Sith doctrine), though it is said a Jedi could use it in some situations. [Dun Möch/Legends §Usage](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dun_Möch/Legends)
- Fohargh used Dun Möch to humiliate Bane. [Kas'im §The Brotherhood of Darkness](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kas%27im)

- **Related:** Duels: Bane vs Sirak c. 1000 BBY; SW_Lightsaber_CombatTechniques_AllEras.md

### Form Zero (ethical principle)
- **Type:** ethical principle
- **Era:** Post-Ruusan Order (Vaunk) to the NJO
- **Key facts:**

- **What:** Knowing when not to draw the saber and to use another skill (for example the mind trick). Skarch Vaunk mentions it in *The Jedi Path*; Yoda supported it. [Form Zero/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_Zero/Legends)
- **NJO:** Kyle Katarn taught that an unlit saber was often enough to make potential enemies cooperate. [Form Zero/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_Zero/Legends)
- **Fay** never raised a saber and fought only with the Force if needed. [Form Zero/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_Zero/Legends)

- **Related:** Training
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Su ma (rotation moves)
- **Type:** move family
- **Era:** All eras (no dates given)
- **Key facts:**

- Rotation in combat in three kinds: jung su ma (spin), ton su ma (somersault), en su ma (cartwheel). These are the three axes of rotation. [Form IV/Legends §Applications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_IV/Legends) [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Su ma](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends)
- Related basic moves: jung (180° turn), jung ma (360° spin to build momentum), shun (one-handed 360° spin), sai (leap over a leg attack). [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Moves and Maneuvers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends)

- **Related:** Form IV; CombatTechniques (basic moves)
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Trispzest (aerial)
- **Type:** lightsaber form (aerial)
- **Era:** Imperial era to the NJO era (Kharys; Maw at Ruusan)
- **Key facts:**

- **Creation:** Kharys, Majestrix of Skye (an airborne S'kytri and Vader-trained Force-sensitive). It combines Form VII with S'kytri aerial dueling. The name means "heart palpitation". [Trispzest §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trispzest) [Form VII/Legends §Practitioners/Juyo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VII/Legends)
- **Tactics:** It favors sai cha (decapitation) against grounded "walkers". The whirlwind attack (hover and spin) is effective against several foes. [Trispzest §Techniques](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trispzest)
- **Weakness:** Open to attacks from directly below. Kyle Katarn wounded Maw that way at Ruusan's Valley Tower. [Trispzest §Techniques](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trispzest)
- **Users:** Kharys, Nazish, Maw (a legless Dark Jedi who levitated). [Trispzest §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trispzest)
- **Kharys's fate:** Luke beat her within months of starting his training. [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Developments during the Great Jedi Purge](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends)

- **Related:** Form VII; CombatTechniques (sai cha)
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Mounted lightsaber combat
- **Type:** combat method
- **Era:** All eras (no dates given)
- **Key facts:**

- One-handed (the other hand steers). Low lunges, diagonal slashes and arcs use the height advantage. The user must also protect the mount. Study was available in the Temple. ### Telekinetic lightsaber combat
- **Type:** combat method
- **Era:** Old Republic (Traya, c. 3951 BBY) to the NJO era
- **Key facts:**

### New Jedi Order styles (Strong / Medium / Fast)
- **Type:** lightsaber styles
- **Era:** NJO, from c. 13/14 ABY
- **Key facts:**

- **Origin:** Created after the NJO had to "start from scratch". Kyle Katarn, Battlemaster from c. 13/14 ABY, introduced them with katas for single, dual and double-bladed sabers. [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Redevelopment](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends) [Battlemaster/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battlemaster/Legends)
- **Sources are video games:** *Jedi Outcast* (2002) and *Jedi Academy* (2003), so the style descriptions are partly derived from **game mechanics** (labelled game-derived). [Fast style §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fast_style)
- **Strong:**
  - Slow, powerful swings that batter through blocks and usually win blade locks.
  - Devastating when there is an opening.
  - Weak against blasters (too much blade travel) and open to counters. [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Strong Style](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends)
- **Medium:**
  - Balance of speed and power; usually taught first, like Shii-Cho.
  - A respectable fallback, but skilled gunslingers (Boba Fett) or duelists (Luke) break it fast. [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Medium Style](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends)
  - It replaced Niman as the NJO's "jack-of-all-trades" until the forms were rediscovered. [Form VI/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VI/Legends)
- **Fast ("inner ring"):**
  - Quick strikes chained almost endlessly.
  - The best blaster defense; strong against slower foes, especially with Force speed.
  - Weak blows are easily parried; short range; a much stronger foe can hammer through. [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Fast Style](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends) [Fast style §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fast_style)
  - Stance: blade held across the body. [Fast style §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fast_style)
- **Three rings of defense** (Kam Solusar, NJO; source *I, Jedi*):
  - Outer: big diagonal sweeps, four diagonal guards.
  - Middle: right-angle guards, good for blast deflection.
  - Inner: a single navel guard; parries rather than blocks, then a counter to the chest or abdomen. [Lightsaber combat/Legends §The Three Rings of Defense](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends) [Battlemaster/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battlemaster/Legends)

- **Related:** Training (Kam Solusar, Kyle Katarn); CombatTechniques (three rings)
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Imperial Knight styles (Legacy era)
- **Type:** lightsaber styles (team)
- **Era:** Legacy era (Fel Empire)
- **Key facts:**

- **Praetoria Vonil:** offensive; move fast and strike hard. [Praetoria Vonil](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Praetoria_Vonil)
- **Praetoria Ishu:** defensive; protect allies so they can find openings. [Praetoria Ishu](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Praetoria_Ishu)
- Both are unique to the Imperial Knights, stress teamwork, and were practiced in addition to the classical forms (*Legacy Era Campaign Guide*). ## Game notes (optional, suggestions only)
*(Design suggestions only.)*
- Sokan could be a terrain modifier that rewards high ground, chokepoints and separating enemies. The Mustafar and Theed anchors show how it could work.
- Tràkata could be a feint action with a high success rate but a deception flag (Jedi conduct penalty?).
- Dun Möch could be a social/morale attack that lowers the target's Force-power accuracy, with a dark-side flag.
- The NJO styles could be stances (speed vs power vs blaster defense) available before forms are rediscovered.

## Decisions needed
1. NJO Strong/Medium/Fast: catalog them as forms, or as stances layered on forms? The sources call them the "three forms of the New Jedi Order" ([Lightsaber combat/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends)), which fits forms.
2. Dun Möch by Jedi: the sources leave Jedi use "theorized" or doubtful. Give it a dark-side cost when a Jedi uses it?
3. Tràkata for Jedi: the sources say Jedi "refrained" from it, yet Mara Jade and Corran Horn used it. Allow it with a conduct note?
4. Trispzest: restrict it to fliers or levitators (S'kytri, Maw), which fits all sources?
5. Include thin-source entries (Lus-ma, Juyo-Kos, Su ma) as playable, or as lore only?
6. Untrained dual-wielding ("the Jar'Kai tactic", [Lightsaber combat/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends)): give it a separate penalty profile from trained Jar'Kai?
