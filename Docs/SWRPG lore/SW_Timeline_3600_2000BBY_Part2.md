# SWRPG Galaxy Timeline, c. 3,600–2,001 BBY (Part 2 of 2): Changes, conflicts and decisions

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 24, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
Part 2 holds the checking sections for the Entries in Part 1: an era index, what this file changes in the earlier files, what was not found, what was not used, the developer's decisions, the source list and the submission checks. Headline findings: the Sith wars ended "before 3600 BBY" per *The Essential Guide to Warfare* (tighter than the "prior to 3522 BBY" the draft map uses); the Temple expansion has two dates (3,519 BBY and c. 3,500 BBY); one Wookieepedia page puts the last Alsakan Conflict in 3,000 BBY (unreferenced, against 3,017 everywhere else); Rori's colonisation has two dates (2,032 and c. 2,000 BBY); and no Wookieepedia year page exists for 3,621–3,523 BBY.

## Game notes (optional, suggestions only)
- The span is a sandbox with few fixed points. Hard anchors a narrator must keep: the Sith wars over by 3,600 BBY; Desolous killed at Yaga Minor sometime after c. 3,522 BBY; Hapes closed from c. 3,100 BBY; 3,017 BBY's Corellian-imposed peace; the Mining Guild on Ruusan by 2,200 BBY; the Sith believed long extinct by 2,000 BBY.
- Darth Desolous fits the existing PP-11 "narrator-directed decline" ruling: he is the one named Sith threat after the Empire, and the Jedi trap at Yaga Minor is a ready-made set piece.
- The 3,017 BBY war can serve as a Corellian "great power" moment: a pirate/privateer campaign that ends with a sword on the Senate floor.

## Decisions needed
1. **When did the Sith wars end?** Three statements, which overlap rather than clash on the date: "before 3600 BBY" (*The Essential Guide to Warfare*, via [Coruscant/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant/Legends); also "Sith Wars of the 3600s" in [Sith Wars/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Wars/Legends) and "the Galactic War, which concluded in the 3600s BBY" in [Alsakan Conflicts/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Alsakan_Conflicts/Legends)); "prior to 3522 BBY" (only an upper bound from Wookieepedia's arithmetic, [Third Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Galactic_War)); and the New Sith Wars page's "sixteen hundred years prior" to 2,000 BBY ([New Sith Wars §Schism](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/New_Sith_Wars)). Proposal for review: adopt "ended before 3,600 BBY", which fits all three and the third Galactic War's last dated battle (c. 3,622 BBY).
2. **How did the Sith Empire end?** [conflict] (a) The Republic and Jedi won and "the Sith Empire of old was finally defeated", with no Eternal Empire or third war in between ([Jedi Order/Legends §Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order/Legends), cites *SWTOR*); (b) it "vanished at some point following the third Galactic War" ([New Sith Wars §Schism](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/New_Sith_Wars), cites *The New Essential Chronology*); (c) it "fell like its predecessor" and the Republic absorbed its territory (unreferenced, [Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_(Post%E2%80%93Great_Hyperspace_War))). Version (b) fits the SWTOR sequence; (a) ignores the Eternal Empire and third war. The existing PP-11 ruling (narrator-directed decline, no invented date) already fits (b); confirm it stands, and whether (a)'s "Republic victory" may be used as flavour.
3. **Darth Desolous's timing.** Only "after c. 3,522 BBY" is sourced (Wookieepedia's arithmetic from the *TFU* databank). An unreferenced line puts his faction "over a century after the Eternal Empire's invasion" ([Galactic Republic/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_Republic/Legends)). Choose whether to pin the conflict to a narrow window (for example, his adult life, c. 3,490–3,400 BBY [inferred]) or leave it open after 3,522 BBY.
4. **Seventeenth Alsakan Conflict: 3,017 BBY vs 3,000 BBY.** [conflict] 3,017 BBY with a Corellian victory ([3017 BBY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/3017_BBY); [Seventeenth Alsakan Conflict](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Seventeenth_Alsakan_Conflict); *The Essential Atlas*, *The Essential Guide to Warfare*) against an unreferenced "3000 BBY ... final defeat of Alsakan" ([Galactic Republic/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_Republic/Legends)). 3,017 fits every referenced source; recommend 3,017 BBY.
5. **Hapes sealing its borders.** [conflict] (a) c. 3,100 BBY by a later "Isolationist" Queen Mother, who also orders *Star Home* built ([3100 BBY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/3100_BBY), cites *The Essential Atlas*; [Hapan §Origins](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hapan)); (b) by the first Queen Mother soon after c. 4,030 BBY, around *Star Home*'s completion, with isolation of "almost 3000 years" (unreferenced, [Hapes Consortium/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hapes_Consortium/Legends)); (c) *Star Home* itself built "shortly after the end of the Mandalorian Wars" (c. 3,960 BBY) ([Star Home](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Star_Home)). (a) is the only dated, referenced version. Decide the sealing date (recommend c. 3,100 BBY) and whether *Star Home* is c. 3,960 or c. 3,100 BBY.
6. **Jedi Temple rebuilding dates.** [conflict] 3,519 BBY ([Coruscant/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant/Legends), cites *The Complete Star Wars Encyclopedia*) vs "major additions beginning circa 3500 BBY" ([Jedi Temple/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Temple/Legends), cites *Complete Locations*) vs 3,500 BBY ([Inter-Sith Wars period](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Inter-Sith_Wars_period)). Also the Archives' small chambers: rebuilt "with the Temple" ([Jedi Archives/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Archives/Legends)) vs 2,510 BBY ([2510 BBY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/2510_BBY)). These can be read as successive stages; decide whether to treat them so.
7. **Iktotchi first contact.** c. 3,500 BBY ([3500 BBY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/3500_BBY)) vs "between 4000 BBY to 3500 BBY" ([Iktotchi/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Iktotchi/Legends), based on an Iktotchi in a c. 3,963 BBY comic). The developer has ruled E.3500.iktotchon narrator colour only; confirm the date used in narration.
