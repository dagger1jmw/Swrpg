# Star Wars Legends: Coruscant and the Jedi Temple, c. 3700–3653 BBY, Part 1: The Jedi Temple and other Jedi sites

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 21, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
- **Building history:** the Temple grew from a small meditative enclave on land granted after the Great Hyperspace War. After Ossus fell in 3996 BBY the Four Masters' Ziggurat expanded it, and by 3653 BBY it had four Council spires. [Jedi Temple §The Old Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Temple/Legends); [High Council Tower §Old Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/High_Council_Tower/Legends)
- **Era-attested detail** is sparse and comes mostly from the Sacking:
  - the Temple Court and Processional Way;
  - the main gate and entrance hall with mezzanines;
  - the Great Hall with hooded statues;
  - the defence-grid mainframe;
  - security recorders;
  - intact lower levels reached through The Works;
  - towers and wings that collapsed.

  [Sacking of Coruscant §Assault on the Jedi Temple](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sacking_of_Coruscant); [Sacking of Coruscant §Security breaches](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sacking_of_Coruscant)
- **Later layouts:** the familiar room-by-room layout (five spires, the large Archives, the Halls of Healing with bacta wards, the Room of a Thousand Fountains) belongs to the Temple rebuilt from c. 3500 BBY and is kept separate.
- **Ruins and other sites:** this part also covers the ruins after 3653 BBY and the other Jedi sites of the era (Tython, Ossus, Dantooine, Ilum, Genarius, Balmorra).

### The Jedi Temple on Coruscant: history to 3653 BBY
- **Type:** location (Jedi headquarters)
- **Era:** c. 5000 BBY to 3653 BBY (destroyed); ruins until reconstruction
- **Key facts:**
  - **Land grant:**
    - After the Great Hyperspace War, the Republic granted the Jedi land over the sacred spire, a Force nexus holy to the native Coruscanti. [Jedi Temple §The Old Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Temple/Legends); [Sacred spire §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sacred_spire/Legends)
    - The Senate hoped for a fortress, but the Order built only a small meditative enclave. [Jedi Temple §The Old Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Temple/Legends); [Coruscant §Wars with the Sith](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant/Legends)
    - The Temple district dates its founding to c. 5000 BBY. [Temple district](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Temple_district/Legends)
  - **The Temple proper:**
    - After the devastation of Ossus (3996 BBY) the Order moved its academy to the spire. The Four Masters began the Temple Ziggurat, which grew for about a thousand years and reused tiles and stained glass from Ossus. [Jedi Temple §The Old Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Temple/Legends); [Jedi Archives §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Archives/Legends); [Four Masters §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Four_Masters/Legends)
    - Several pages date the Four Masters' founding to "circa 4000 BBY". [First Knowledge Quarter §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/First_Knowledge_Quarter/Legends); [Great Hall (Jedi Temple) §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Hall_(Jedi_Temple)/Legends); [Processional Way §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Processional_Way/Legends)
  - **Council towers:**
    - After the four Jedi Council spires were built, the High Council moved permanently to the High Council Chamber in its tower. [Jedi Temple §The Old Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Temple/Legends)
    - As of 3653 BBY the High Council occupied one of **four** spires atop the Temple Ziggurat. [High Council Tower §Old Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/High_Council_Tower/Legends)
  - **Archives:**
    - The Order closed some satellite facilities and moved all artifacts from Exis Station to the Temple archives. [Jedi Temple §The Old Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Temple/Legends)
    - In this era the library/Archives was a set of small, cramped chambers several storeys above the main entrance. The large Archives of later eras were not built until c. 2500 BBY. [Jedi Archives §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Archives/Legends)
  - **Earlier abandonment:** the Temple was briefly abandoned during the First Jedi Purge (3954–3951 BBY) and then reoccupied. [Jedi Temple §The Old Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Temple/Legends); [Jedi Archives §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Archives/Legends)
  - **3681–3653 BBY:** the Temple "stood as a beacon" and supported the Republic through the Great Galactic War. [Jedi Temple §The Old Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Temple/Legends)
  - **3653 BBY:**
    - Malgus's raiding party killed the Temple's main line of defence to shut off Galactic City's security network.
    - Bombs planted inside went off as the fleet fired from orbit.
    - The exterior crumbled and the four towers fell onto the roof, "however, much of the interior remained intact".
    - Entire floors of archives, holocrons, combat manuals and star charts were lost.

    [Jedi Temple §The Old Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Temple/Legends)
- **Related:** the Sacking in SW_Great_Galactic_War_Part3.md; Temple attested features (below)

### Who lived and worked at the Temple, 3681–3653 BBY
- **Type:** people
- **Era:** 3681–3653 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Grand Master Zym** led the Council in agreeing to fight for the Senate in 3681 BBY. He survived the Sacking (he was on Alderaan) and was later killed in the Temple ruins. [Zym](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zym); [Jedi Temple §The Old Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Temple/Legends)
  - **Satele Shan:**
    - As a Padawan, after Korriban (3681 BBY), the Council debriefed her and let her sit in on a strategy meeting.
    - She watched the Jedi contingent leave the Temple for Minos (3680 BBY).
    - In 3671 BBY her meditation at the Temple was interrupted by cheering from Senate Plaza.

    [Satele Shan §Fighting the Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satele_Shan)
  - **Gnost-Dural:** after years at the front, the High Council granted his petitions to return to the Temple Archives on Coruscant for research. [unclear date] [Gnost-Dural §Great Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gnost-Dural)
  - **Ven Zallow:**
    - A Master who trained Aryn Leneer to Knighthood during the war.
    - In 3653 BBY he led the defence, with six Knights, in the entrance hall and was killed by Malgus.
    - His astromech T7-O1 survived.

    [Ven Zallow §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ven_Zallow); [Sacking of Coruscant §Assault on the Jedi Temple](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sacking_of_Coruscant); [Sacking of Coruscant §Final day](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sacking_of_Coruscant)
  - **Others killed in the defence:**
    - Jedi Knights Rolend and Yanila Zavros, a married Togruta couple. [Sacking of Coruscant §Assault on the Jedi Temple](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sacking_of_Coruscant)
    - An unnamed Zabrak Padawan, killed by Malgus. [Sacking of Coruscant §Assault on the Jedi Temple](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sacking_of_Coruscant)
  - **Bengel Morr:** Orgus Din's Nautolan apprentice, inside the Temple during the attack. He was presumed dead but escaped and later fell to the dark side. [Orgus Din §Great Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Orgus_Din); [Mission to Coruscant (Desolator crisis) §Prelude](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mission_to_Coruscant_(Desolator_crisis))
  - **Kellian Jarro:** a Master who killed over 100 Mandalorians in the streets of Galactic City as the Temple burned. [Kellian Jarro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kellian_Jarro)
  - **Republic soldiers** guarded the building in 3653 BBY "while most Jedi were away either fighting the Sith or participating in the peace talks". [Sacking of Coruscant §Assault on the Jedi Temple](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sacking_of_Coruscant)
  - **Jedi based there:** no source gives a number. In 3653 BBY "dozens of Jedi" fought the Sith in the entrance hall. Six High Council members died in the Sacking, and most Knights and Padawans inside the Temple were killed. [conflict] Jedi High Council/Legends says "half of the Council was killed, including Grand Master Zym"; resolved (lore batch 13): six died in the Sacking, and Zym died afterwards in the ruins. [Sacking of Coruscant §Assault on the Jedi Temple](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sacking_of_Coruscant); [Sacking of Coruscant](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sacking_of_Coruscant)

### After 3653 BBY: the ruins (post-window, labelled)
- **Type:** location state
- **Era:** 3653 BBY onward (later dates are outside the user's window)
- **Key facts:**
  - **3653 BBY:**
    - The Senate voted to postpone rebuilding indefinitely for lack of funds. Interim Chancellor Paran Am-Ris told Zym, who promised that the Temple would one day be rebuilt. [Jedi Temple §The Old Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Temple/Legends)
    - The Order moved to Tython. [Temple district §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Temple_district/Legends); [Jedi Temple (Tython) §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Temple_(Tython)/Legends)
  - **3653 BBY, in the ruins:**
    - Aryn Leneer went to the ruins to mourn Zallow and fought Malgus there. [Sacking of Coruscant §Final day](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sacking_of_Coruscant); [Duel in the ruins of the Jedi Temple](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Duel_in_the_ruins_of_the_Jedi_Temple)
    - Zym and Commander Gin Lesl were lured there, where the bounty hunter Braden killed them both. [Jedi Temple §The Old Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Temple/Legends); [Gin Lesl §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gin_Lesl)
  - **Cold War (from 3653 BBY):** Jedi who stayed on Coruscant formed a Jedi delegation in the Senate, and "others tried to protect the ruins of the Jedi Temple and protect any of its remaining treasures". [Jedi Order §The Cold War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order/Legends)
  - **Archives:** the surviving Council had Gnost-Dural rebuild the lost knowledge from Tython. [Jedi Archives §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Archives/Legends)
  - **3643 BBY (SWTOR, post-window):**
    - The ruins were "Imperial-occupied", reachable fastest through The Works.
    - A Jedi Knight and Padawan Kira Carsen fought Imperial saboteurs to "the remains of the High Council Chamber". There they killed Lord Tarnis and disabled the Planet Prison superweapon.

    [Duel in the Jedi Temple ruins §Prelude](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Duel_in_the_Jedi_Temple_ruins); [Duel in the Jedi Temple ruins §The duel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Duel_in_the_Jedi_Temple_ruins)
  - **Rebuilding:**
    - The Temple was rebuilt and expanded from c. 3500 BBY, with the academy returning from Tython. [Jedi Temple §Temple Ziggurat](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Temple/Legends)
    - Rebuilt "before 3600 BBY" with expansions in 3519 BBY. [Coruscant §Wars with the Sith](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant/Legends)
    - Reoccupied "no later than 1000 BBY". [Temple district §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Temple_district/Legends)
    - Spires and the High Council Tower rebuilt in 1019 BBY. [High Council Tower §Old Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/High_Council_Tower/Legends); [Tower of First Knowledge §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tower_of_First_Knowledge/Legends)

## Decisions needed
1. **Temple founding date:** c. 5000 BBY (land grant and enclave) vs c. 4000 BBY (Four Masters' Temple after Ossus). Best fit: both; the enclave c. 5000 BBY, the Temple proper from c. 3996 BBY.
2. **Era layout:** use only attested features vs borrow later (c. 1000–19 BBY) layouts. Best fit: attested core plus later rooms marked "campaign invention".
3. **Number of spires in 3653 BBY:** four Council spires (High Council Tower page; "four towers falling") vs five towers (later layout). Best fit: four for this era.
4. **Extent of destruction:** "destroyed" or "nearly destroying" vs "much of the interior remained intact" with intact lower levels. Best fit: exterior and towers ruined, interior partly intact.
5. **High Council Chamber:** the tower "collapsed" in 3653 BBY vs "the remains of the High Council Chamber" in the ruins in 3643 BBY. Best fit: the tower fell, but the chamber's remains survived inside.
6. **Rebuilding date:** "several years" (Archives), before 3600 BBY with 3519 BBY expansions (Coruscant), c. 3500 BBY (Temple), or no later than 1000 BBY (Temple district). Best fit: c. 3519–3500 BBY.
7. **Tower of First Knowledge rebuilt:** 1019 BBY (its own page) vs "northeastern wing" with the tower completed in 1000 BBY (First Knowledge quarter page). Best fit: 1019 BBY, northwest.
8. **Tython:** rediscovered after the Sacking by Satele Shan vs "recently rediscovered" before the migration. Best fit: after the Sacking. **Confirmed (lore batch 13):** the Order was "homeless for a short period" after the Sacking, then resettled Tython (Jedi Order/Legends §The Cold War).
9. **Genarius:** Jedi Enclave purged in the Great Galactic War vs system discovered in 232 BBY (same page). Best fit: keep the war-era Enclave and flag it as a source contradiction.
10. **Republic Executive Building:** facade blackened in 3653 BBY (Senate Building page) vs built much later under Palpatine (its own page). Best fit: it did not exist; read it as another government annex.
11. **Temple Guard in the window:** inferred from "established thousands of years prior" vs no attestation at the Sacking. Best fit: include them as a minor presence, tagged [inferred].
12. **Yinta Lake:** the brief listed it as a Coruscant spaceport, but it is on Vulta. Best fit: drop it from Coruscant and use Liston and the Coruscant Spaceport.
