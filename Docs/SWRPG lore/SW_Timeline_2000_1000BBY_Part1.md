# SWRPG Galaxy Timeline, 2,000–1,000 BBY (Part 1 of 3): The Fourth Great Schism to the Republic Dark Age

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 24, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
This three-part file rebuilds the "NEW SITH WARS — 2,000–1,000 BBY" block of `SW_Legends_Galaxy_Timeline.md` as one sourced row per event, covering the whole Draggulch Period: the Fourth Great Schism and Darth Ruin, the Dark Underlord, the 1,500 BBY Republic victories, Mizra, Darth Rivan, the Jedi Chancellors, the Sictis Wars, the Republic Dark Age (Jedi Lords, the Sith civil war, both *Charge Matrica* contests, Mandalore the Uniter, Kerra Holt and Operation Influx), the Light and Darkness War, all seven battles of Ruusan, Darth Bane's path, the thought bomb, the Rule of Two and the Ruusan Reformations. Part 1 (this file) covers 2,000–1,011 BBY. Part 2 covers 1,010–1,000 BBY and the undated rows. Part 3 holds "Changes from the previous file", Not found, Canon only, Not used, Game notes, Decisions needed, Sources and the checklist. The key facts: the schism and Ruin's founding of the New Sith are dated c. 2,000 BBY, but Ruin's death is undated beyond "c. 2,000 BBY"; Wookieepedia gives two dates for the Army of Light (1,010 BBY and "around 1,002 BBY", ruled 1,010) and two headquarters for the Brotherhood (Korriban and Roon, ruled Korriban); and four Ruusan battle dates disagree between pages (Part 3, Decisions needed).

## NEW SITH WARS — 2,000–1,000 BBY
## ════════════════════════════

### Overview
The New Sith Wars filled the Draggulch Period, 2,000–1,000 BBY [Draggulch Period](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Draggulch_Period). The Jedi Master Phanius left the Order on good terms, secretly sought forbidden teachings, united the surviving Sith clans and returned as Darth Ruin; fifty Jedi Knights deserted to him in the Fourth Great Schism, and the New Sith carved out an empire in the galaxy's north-east [New Sith Wars §Schism](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/New_Sith_Wars) [New Sith Wars §Sith ascendancy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/New_Sith_Wars). The Republic held at Gap Nine, Corphelion and King's Galquek c. 1,500 BBY but was routed at Mizra in 1,466 BBY; after that came economic collapse, plague, shrinking borders and four centuries of Jedi Chancellors [New Sith Wars §Civilization decays](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/New_Sith_Wars). The Sictis Wars (1,250–1,230 BBY) ended with Belia Darzu's assassination [Sictis Wars](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sictis_Wars). In the last century, the Republic Dark Age, the Republic barely functioned, Jedi Lords ruled systems as barons, and the Sith fell into civil war among warlords [Republic Dark Age](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Dark_Age) [New Sith Wars §Dark Age](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/New_Sith_Wars). Skere Kaan ended that civil war in 1,010 BBY by founding the Brotherhood of Darkness; Lord Hoth's Army of Light fought it through the Light and Darkness War to the Ruusan campaign (1,002–1,000 BBY), which ended with Kaan's thought bomb, Darth Bane's survival and the Ruusan Reformations [Light and Darkness War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Light_and_Darkness_War) [Ruusan campaign](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ruusan_campaign) [Ruusan Reformations/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ruusan_Reformations/Legends).

#### 1,100–1,011 BBY — The Republic Dark Age

The last century of the war is called the Republic Dark Age: the Republic could no longer afford its communications network, dropping settlements outside the Core off the grid, and the Candorian plague killed up to two-thirds of some population centres [Republic Dark Age](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Dark_Age). The Sith, more numerous than ever, fought each other instead of finishing the Republic [New Sith Wars §Dark Age](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/New_Sith_Wars). Jedi who defended abandoned worlds became Jedi Lords, holding castles and manors and passing titles to their heirs; their Jedi Grand Council rivalled the Jedi High Council [Jedi Lord](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Lord) [New Sith Wars §Dark Age](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/New_Sith_Wars). Much of the Republic's former territory was held by Sith warlords such as Odion and Daiman, and the Republic made no attempt to win it back [New Sith Wars §Dark Age](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/New_Sith_Wars).
