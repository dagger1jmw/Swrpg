# Star Wars Legends: Jedi Ranks, Councils and Roles Across All Eras (Part 2: 1000 BBY–138 ABY)

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 21, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
Part 2 of 2, covering 1000 BBY to c. 138 ABY: the four Coruscant Councils of the late Republic and their seats, the branches (Guardian, Consular, Sentinel) and specialist roles, the Order's relationship to each government, Clone Wars generalship, expulsions and resignations, the Dark Times, New Jedi Order and Galactic Alliance governance, the Legacy era, and Order size. The key facts are a 12-seat High Council (5 lifetime, 4 long-term, 3 limited-term) beside three 5-seat Councils, all dissolved in 19 BBY; Jedi as Clone Wars generals who could refuse the Chancellor only with "substantial justification"; and the New Jedi Order's move from Luke Skywalker's informal leadership through a Masters' Council and a joint Alliance High Council (28 ABY) to a 12-seat NJO High Council (36 ABY). The cross-era table, leadership titles, the Grand Master list and Master rank are in Part 1 (SW_Jedi_Ranks_Councils_Roles_AllEras_Part1.md).

  - All Coruscant Councils appointed their own members by unanimous vote, and each had a designated leader. All four were dissolved in 19 BBY. [Jedi Council/Legends §Councils of the Old Jedi Order](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Council/Legends)
  - Members of the three smaller Councils often sat on another Council at the same time, or had retired from the High Council and kept their seat. [Council of First Knowledge/Legends §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Council_of_First_Knowledge/Legends); [Council of Reconciliation/Legends §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Council_of_Reconciliation/Legends)
  - **Knights on the High Council:**
    - Membership was usually restricted to Masters, but a Knight could sit "should certain insight be needed" or during the transition before formal elevation. [Jedi Order/Legends §Leadership](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order/Legends)
    - Ki-Adi-Mundi is the example given; this was "a simple technicality". [Jedi Knight/Legends §Service to the Order](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Knight/Legends)
  - **Chancellor's appointee:** Palpatine appointed Knight Anakin Skywalker in 19 BBY, legal under the Enhanced Security and Enforcement Act. The Council accepted him in order to gain a spy but refused to make him a Master. [Jedi High Council/Legends §Organization and politics](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council/Legends); [Jedi Order/Legends §Leadership](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order/Legends)
  - **Decision-making:** the Council was said to meditate together until it reached "a single mind". By late in the Clone Wars it argued fiercely and voted, and rarely meditated together (*Revenge of the Sith* novelization). [Jedi High Council/Legends §Organization and politics](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council/Legends)
  - **Wartime use of the Council:** the Council used Anakin to spy on the Chancellor; Obi-Wan Kenobi was outvoted. [Jedi High Council/Legends §The end of the Council](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council/Legends)
  - **Composition:** in the Golden Age it was largely scholars (Jocasta Nu, Thame Cerulian, Omo Bouri, Tor Difusal), unlike the military leaders of wartime Councils. [Jedi High Council/Legends §Near the Republic's end](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council/Legends)
  - **"Old Guard":** Masters such as Qui-Gon Jinn who were under consideration for a Council seat. [Jedi Master/Legends §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Master/Legends)
  - **Satellite academies:** the Almas Council was made up of all the Masters who taught at that academy and took orders from the High Council (31 BBY). [Jedi Council/Legends §Almas](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Council/Legends)

### Clone Wars generalship (22–19 BBY)
- **Type:** ranks
- **Era:** 22–19 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - All active Knights became Jedi Generals in the Grand Army. [Jedi Knight/Legends §Overview and history](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Knight/Legends) Most Knights and Masters were made generals, though some refused at the "Jedi Schism". [Jedi General/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_General/Legends)
  - The Order was "technically part of the Judicial Department" and was reassigned as officers in the Grand Army and Navy. [Jedi General/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_General/Legends)
  - The planned order of battle (ORBAT):
    - 10 High Jedi Generals (systems armies);
    - 20 Senior Jedi Generals (sector armies);
    - 80 generals commanding corps;

### Dark Times (19 BBY–4 ABY)
- **Type:** institutions (absence)
- **Era:** 19 BBY to 4 ABY
- **Key facts:**
  - All four Councils were dissolved in 19 BBY. [Jedi Council/Legends §Councils of the Old Jedi Order](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Council/Legends)
  - Only three High Council members survived, in exile: Shaak Ti (died on Felucia), Obi-Wan Kenobi (died on the Death Star) and Yoda (died on Dagobah). [Jedi High Council/Legends §The end of the Council](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council/Legends)
  - The title Master of the Order "fell out of use for several decades". [Master of the Order/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Master_of_the_Order/Legends)
  - No Knights were promoted under the Empire. [Jedi Knight/Legends §Service to the Order](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Knight/Legends)
  - Fewer than one hundred Jedi escaped, "approximately one percent of the entire Order". [Order 66/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Order_66/Legends) Masters were believed more likely than Knights to survive. Named surviving Masters: Kenobi, Yoda, Shaak Ti, Echuu Shen-Jon, Qu Rahn, Rahm Kota, K'Kruhk, T'ra Saa, Tholme, A'Sharad Hett and Quinlan Vos. [Great Jedi Purge/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Jedi_Purge/Legends)

[Great Jedi Purge/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Jedi_Purge/Legends); [Conclave on Kessel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Conclave_on_Kessel)
  - The Coruscant resistance group Whiplash smuggled Jedi off-world; its leader was Thi Xon Yimmon as of 18 BBY. [Whiplash](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Whiplash)

### New Jedi Order and Galactic Alliance governance (4 ABY–c. 127 ABY)
- **Type:** institutions
- **Era:** 4 ABY to c. 127 ABY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Luke alone:**
    - Luke founded the Yavin 4 academy with New Republic permission; the first class had 12 pupils. [New Jedi Order §Jedi Praxeum (11–18 ABY)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/New_Jedi_Order)
    - At first the NJO was "very informal", with Jedi choosing their own missions. [Jedi High Council/Legends §Reestablishment](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council/Legends)
  - **Masters' Council:**
    - Formed during the Yuuzhan Vong War to unify the Jedi response [New Jedi Order §Yuuzhan Vong War (25–29 ABY)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/New_Jedi_Order).
    - Described "as of 35 ABY" as an informal 12-member body [Jedi High Council (New Jedi Order)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council_%28New_Jedi_Order%29).
    - It dealt "with strictly Jedi matters" and was autonomous from the Alliance; how far it controlled Knights was "a matter of debate". It met on Coruscant or Ossus. [Jedi Council/Legends §Jedi High Council (New Jedi Order)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Council/Legends)
  - **Galactic Alliance High Council (28 ABY):**
    - Created by Chief of State Cal Omas on Mon Calamari: 6 Jedi and 6 Alliance leaders, the ruling council of the Alliance until after the war. [High Council (Galactic Alliance) §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/High_Council_%28Galactic_Alliance%29)
    - Jedi members: Luke Skywalker, Cilghal, Kenth Hamner, Kyp Durron, Tresina Lobi and Knight Saba Sebatyne. Ayddar Nylykerka had temporary status. [High Council (Galactic Alliance) §Yuuzhan Vong War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/High_Council_%28Galactic_Alliance%29); [High Council (Galactic Alliance) §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/High_Council_%28Galactic_Alliance%29)
  - **NJO High Council (from 36 ABY):**
    - After the Swarm War, Luke declared himself Grand Master at a conclave on Ossus and formalized the Masters' Council into a High Council in the Coruscant chamber. [Jedi High Council (New Jedi Order) §Masters' Council](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council_%28New_Jedi_Order%29); [Jedi High Council (New Jedi Order) §Jedi High Council](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council_%28New_Jedi_Order%29)
    - All Jedi were to "answer to a reformed Jedi High Council and accept missions at its pleasure". [Jedi High Council/Legends §Reestablishment](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council/Legends)
  - **Seats:**
    - 12 Masters, each appointed by unanimous vote of the current members. Seats could stay empty for years (Mara Skywalker's and Tresina Lobi's after 40 ABY); only nine sat after Hamner's death in 44 ABY. [Jedi High Council (New Jedi Order) §Organization and politics](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council_%28New_Jedi_Order%29)
    - A secretary was elected in the early years, and any available Master could be called in. [Jedi High Council (New Jedi Order) §Organization and politics](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council_%28New_Jedi_Order%29)
  - **Members (from 35 ABY):** Cilghal, Kyp Durron, Kenth Hamner (to 44 ABY), Corran Horn, Kyle Katarn, Tresina Lobi (to 40 ABY), Octa Ramis, Saba Sebatyne, Luke Skywalker, Mara Jade Skywalker (to 40 ABY), Kam Solusar, Tionne Solusar. Later Barratk'l (43 ABY) and Jaina Solo Fel (44 ABY). [Jedi High Council (New Jedi Order) §Members](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council_%28New_Jedi_Order%29)
  - **Weakness:** in the Second Galactic Civil War, members deferred to Luke, and the Council was "largely ineffective". It went into exile on Endor's moon and joined the coalition against Caedus. [Jedi High Council/Legends §Reestablishment](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council/Legends); [Jedi High Council (New Jedi Order) §Second Galactic Civil War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council_%28New_Jedi_Order%29)
  - **Acting Grand Masters:** Kenth Hamner (43.5–44 ABY, agreed by Luke and Daala), then Saba Sebatyne, elected unanimously. [Grand Master/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grand_Master/Legends)
- **Related:** Relationship to government; SW_Jedi_Training_AllEras_Part2.md

  - [conflict] When the Masters' Council began: during the Yuuzhan Vong War (25–29 ABY) [New Jedi Order §Yuuzhan Vong War (25–29 ABY)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/New_Jedi_Order) vs "as of 35 ABY" [Jedi High Council (New Jedi Order)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council_%28New_Jedi_Order%29); [Jedi Council/Legends §Jedi High Council (New Jedi Order)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Council/Legends). - **Era:** c. 127–138 ABY

[Jedi High Council (New Jedi Order) §Members](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council_%28New_Jedi_Order%29)

## Game notes (optional, suggestions only)
- **Era defaults for Council size (suggestion):**

| Era | Suggested default |
|---|---|
| Late Republic | 12 + 5 + 5 + 5 Coruscant Councils |
| Dark Times | none |
| NJO | Masters' Council or High Council of 12 with vacancies allowed (9 seated in 44 ABY) |
| Legacy after 130 ABY | 3-seat council in hiding |

- **NPC roles (suggestion):** draw an NPC Jedi's role from the Specialist roles table filtered by era. For example, no Service Corps after 19 BBY, Jedi Generals only in war eras, and the Sentinel title in the NJO only near the Legacy era.
- **A player Jedi's path to the Council (suggestion):**
  1. Gain Master rank (Part 1).
  2. Be noted as "Old Guard" (under consideration).
  3. Join a limited-term seat during a crisis or as a Temple body's voice.
  4. Move to a long-term seat, then a lifetime seat on a vacancy.

  In the Clone Wars a Chancellor's appointment is possible, but it does not grant Master rank. In the NJO, a seat opens by unanimous vote, but vacancies can last years.
- **Expulsion (suggestion):** a Council of Reconciliation hearing with testimony (Republic era); outcomes of exile, stripped rank, or reassignment to the Service Corps.
