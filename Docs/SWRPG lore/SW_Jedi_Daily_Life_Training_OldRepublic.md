# Jedi Order Daily Life and Training (c. 3,700–3,600 BBY, with general Legends material)

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched 21 November 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
This file covers how the Jedi Order trained and lived in the Old Republic window of about 3,700–3,600 BBY: the Great Galactic War (3681–3653 BBY), the Cold War on Tython (3653–3641 BBY), the Galactic War (3641–3636 BBY) and the scattered years up to the Ossus colony (to 3628 BBY). It covers Initiate and Padawan training, classes and instructors, Temple duties, the Service Corps (including the Agricultural Corps) and missions. The detailed daily timetable (morning Force studies, midday classes, afternoon combat, five meditations a day) comes from *The Jedi Path*, which describes the later post-Ruusan Order, so it is labelled **general** throughout. The era-specific evidence comes mainly from SWTOR, and it shows a looser, wartime pattern. Learners were sent to Tython to finish training and take the Trials. Some were recruited as adolescents or adults. Padawans used electroblade practice swords until they built a lightsaber at the ancient Forge, and the Council could send a Padawan on a mission alone.

**How to read the citations:** I read every page from starwars.fandom.com through its MediaWiki API, because the normal page view was blocked by a Cloudflare check. No mirror was used. When the statement on a Wookieepedia page carries a footnote, the footnoted source is given in parentheses, for example "(ref: *The Jedi Path*)". SWTOR missions and codex entries are named as the wiki names them, for example "(ref: SWTOR Jedi Knight mission 'Attack of the Flesh Raiders')". The year 3643 BBY for the Tython starting missions is the wiki's own dating ("TOR dates" note) and is used as given.

**How to read "general" and "era":** "Era" means a cited source ties the fact to 3,700–3,600 BBY. "General" means the source describes the Order without tying it to this window. Most general facts come from *The Jedi Path* (2010), an in-universe manual that describes the Order around the end of the Republic. "General (pre-Ruusan)" marks general facts that the page says applied to the older Order before the Ruusan Reformation (c. 1000 BBY), which takes in this window, but without naming it.

**About [conflict] and [gray]:** When sources disagree, every version is listed with its citation, with a note on which fits best. The decision is left to the developer. [gray] marks a fact from a gray-area source (published 2014 to early 2016, not SWTOR, not pre-2014 Legends). It is kept only with the backing stated. One [gray] item is used, from the *Force and Destiny Core Rulebook* (30 July 2015). *Savage Spirits* (2 June 2016, classed as Canon by Wookieepedia) and *Nexus of Power* (17 March 2016) are excluded, and their facts are listed under "Not used". D-465 (Jared): *Nexus of Power* (FFG) is not Legends. Lines sourced only to it are under Not used; where another Legends source backs a fact, the fact stays and only the *Nexus of Power* citation is removed.

### Daily schedule of an Initiate (general)
- **Later-era default (lore batch 13, ruling 1):** adopted as the default for 3681–3653 BBY, though attested only for the post-Ruusan Order. Wartime colour: many Initiates and Padawans are away with Masters at the front, so the Temple feels emptier than in peacetime [inferred].
- **Type:** routine
- **Era:** general (post-Ruusan Coruscant academy). No source gives a timetable for 3,700–3,600 BBY (see "Not found").
- **Key facts:**
  - The Coruscant academy set out an Initiate's day, and lessons grew harder as a student moved up through the system (general; ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_training/Legends) ("Training in the Old Jedi Order")
  - **Morning:** Force studies were required in the morning, teaching the Force and its three applications, "Control, Sense, and Alter" (general; ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_training/Legends) ("Training in the Old Jedi Order"). The Initiate page also calls these "Morning rituals" of Force studies (general; ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Initiate/Legends) ("Instruction")
  - **Midday:** classroom teaching. Non-Jedi instructors taught political strategy, galactic law, the sciences and languages (general; ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_training/Legends) ("Training in the Old Jedi Order"). The Initiate page names midday political science and the histories of "a thousand worlds and cultures" (general; ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Initiate/Legends) ("Instruction")
  - **Midday meal, then afternoon:** after the midday meal, clans did required physical training (general; ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Initiate/Legends) ("Instruction"). Afternoons were for physical activity, including lightsaber training in Forms I to VI with training lightsabers (general; ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_training/Legends) ("Training in the Old Jedi Order")
  - **Meditation:** five required meditation sessions were spread through the day, along with communal meals (general; ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_training/Legends) ("Training in the Old Jedi Order"). The Meditation page says the Council of First Knowledge created the five-sessions-a-day regimen, and instructors urged Initiates and Padawans to meditate whenever they had a spare moment (general). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Meditation/Legends) ("Jedi Order")
  - **Meals:** each clan's instructor led the children to meals in the refectories (general; ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Initiate/Legends) ("Youngling Clans"). On Coruscant the refectories were Jedi-run dining halls with kitchens, eating areas and gardens on several levels, and some served only certain meals (general; the page's history is dated to c. 1000 BBY onward, ref: *Star Wars: Complete Locations* (2005)). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Temple_refectory/Legends) (intro; "History")
  - **Personal upkeep:** Initiates wore tan or gray tunics and trousers with brown boots and a belt from the time they could walk. They kept their own uniform in order with daily cleaning, using the Temple laundry only as an extra (general; ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Initiate/Legends) ("Dress")
  - **Things to memorise:** students had to know the Temple layout by heart, and the names and histories of the Jedi memorialised there (general; ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Initiate/Legends) ("Instruction")
- **Related:** Jedi Initiate; Clans; Jedi instructors; Padawan schedule
- **Uncertainty:** The timetable describes the Coruscant academy of the late Republic. Whether Tython in the Cold War kept the same day is not stated [unclear]. The SWTOR Tython Temple had training grounds around it (see "Jedi Temple (Tython)"), but no source gives its timetable.

### Jedi Initiate (training stage)
- **Type:** rank / training stage
- **Era:** general; era examples c. 3661 BBY and 3643 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - An Initiate was a Force-sensitive child in early training at a Jedi academy, from joining a clan until passing the Initiate Trials and being chosen as a Padawan. In peacetime Initiates rarely left their academy (general; ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Initiate/Legends) (intro)
  - Every new class began with the Jedi Code and the Three Pillars: "the Force, Knowledge, and Self-Discipline". Each Jedi received a copy of *The Jedi Path* (general; ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Initiate/Legends) ("Instruction")
  - Force study began with Control. Students had to prove themselves in their other classes before they were allowed lightsaber training. Advanced meditation came with the Third Pillar, then the six levels of lightsaber combat, then the Initiate Trials (general; ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Initiate/Legends) ("Instruction")
  - The Trials could be retaken, but an Initiate could not stay an extra year at the academy: "One had to progress, or leave" (general; ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Initiate/Legends) ("Instruction")
  - In the Order's early years, and after reforms or purges, the Initiate stage did not exist or was not the main source of Knights. Masters took on anyone with Force affinity (general; ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Initiate/Legends) ("Overview")
  - **Era** `unverified` (not covered by SW_Jedi_Temple_Life_GGW_Part1; queued for a check, lore batch 13): a Kiffar orphan born c. 3661 BBY was handed to the Coruscant Temple as an infant and trained there (ref: *Fatal Alliance*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shigar_Konshi) ("Jedi training")
  - **Era:** a learner found by the Order trained under "several Jedi Masters" during the Cold War. As a "Jedi Initiate" the learner was sent to Tython in 3643 BBY to learn from an official teacher and take the Jedi Trials (ref: SWTOR Jedi Knight mission "Attack of the Flesh Raiders"; TOR dates). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hero_of_Tython) ("Early life")
  - **Era:** a child prodigy was trained "under a number of Jedi instructors" during the Cold War as an Initiate, then sent to Tython by 3643 BBY to learn from an official mentor and complete the Jedi Trials (ref: SWTOR Jedi Consular mission "The Path of a Jedi"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/The_Barsen%27thor) ("Early Jedi training"; "Arrival")
- **Related:** Daily schedule; Clans; Initiate Trials; Padawan
- **Uncertainty:** In both 3643 BBY examples the wiki calls the learner an "Initiate" who goes to Tython for the "Jedi Trials". It is [unclear] whether these were Initiate Trials or Knighthood Trials (the same point is noted in SW_Jedi_Order_Ranks_Council_OldRepublic.md).

### Clans (general)
- **Later-era default (lore batch 13, ruling 1):** adopted as the default for 3681–3653 BBY, though attested only for the post-Ruusan Order. Clans of about ten, with fosterers.
- **Type:** institution (Initiate group)
- **Era:** general (post-Ruusan); not attested in this window
- **Key facts:**
  - Fosterers placed infants into clans using the Force. A clan lived and studied together for a little over a decade, under a trained instructor who led early exercises and meals (general; ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Initiate/Legends) ("Youngling Clans")
  - Clan-mates were meant to see each other as family (general; ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Initiate/Legends) ("Instruction")
  - Clans formed the classes of the Coruscant academy. A child joined at about age three, or the equivalent maturity for other species (general). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clan_%28Jedi%29/Legends) (intro; "Overview")
- **Related:** Jedi Initiate; Daily schedule
- **Uncertainty:** Clan size [conflict]. Version A: "about ten Initiates" (ref: *The Jedi Path*) [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Initiate/Legends) ("Youngling Clans"). Version B: "ten to twenty" (no footnote on that line) [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clan_%28Jedi%29/Legends) (intro). Version A carries a footnote, and B's range includes it. Both are general. No source shows clans in 3,700–3,600 BBY.

### Initiate Trials
- **Type:** test
- **Era:** general; some details marked pre-Ruusan
- **Key facts:**
  - Later form: three tests. These were knowledge of the Jedi Code, observed meditation and duelling technique, and fluency in using the Force. Those not chosen as Padawans were placed in the Service Corps by the Reassignment Council, or could leave the Order (general; ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Initiate/Legends) ("Instruction")
  - The Code was reviewed almost daily in meditation or class, and an Initiate who did not memorise it would leave the Order (general; ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Initiate_Trials/Legends) ("Overview")
  - **General (pre-Ruusan):** in the old Order a Master might have two or more students and test them together. With looser age rules, students often took the test in their teens or twenties (ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Initiate_Trials/Legends) ("Overview")
  - **General (pre-Ruusan):** the second test was originally building a lightsaber with crystals provided by the Master. After Ruusan it became a test of self-discipline (ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Initiate_Trials/Legends) ("Overview")
  - The third test varied. One example is the Dantooine Council requiring Initiates to face the dark side, for example Revan's task to find and redeem the fallen apprentice Juhani (ref: *Knights of the Old Republic*; c. 3956 BBY, before this window). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Initiate_Trials/Legends) ("Overview")
- **Related:** Jedi Initiate; Lightsaber construction; Service Corps
- **Uncertainty:** Which form of the Initiate Trials was used in 3,700–3,600 BBY is not stated (see "Not found").

### Recruitment and age of entry
- **Type:** practice
- **Era:** general, with era examples 3681–3643 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - General (post-Ruusan): the Acquisition Division brought Force-sensitive children to the Temple, using mandatory blood tests on Republic worlds (general; ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Acquisition_Division/Legends) (intro)
  - General: Jedi asked parents' permission by standard protocol. Taking a child against the parents' wishes happened only when the Jedi judged it necessary for safety (ref: *HoloNet News*; *Power of the Jedi Sourcebook*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Initiate/Legends) ("Affiliation")
  - **Era:** Aryn Leneer, a Human orphan on Balmorra, was found by Master Ven Zallow at an early age and taken to the Coruscant Temple. Zallow "took over Leneer's training immediately" (ref: *Deceived*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aryn_Leneer) ("Early life")
  - **Era:** the Zabrak Hallen was found in her adolescent years and taken to the Tython Temple to begin training as a Padawan during the Cold War (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hallen_%28Jedi%29) ("Biography")
  - **Era:** Master Bela Kiwiiks took the eighteen-year-old Kira Carsen, a Sith-trained runaway, as her Padawan and returned with her to Tython (ref: SWTOR Jedi Knight mission "The Defector"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bela_Kiwiiks) ("Biography"). Carsen was born in 3663 BBY [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kira_Carsen) (intro), and was two years into the apprenticeship in 3643 BBY (ref: TOR dates). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kira_Carsen) ("Life as a Jedi")
  - **Era:** during the Galactic War the Jedi considered Nadia Grell "too mature to become a Padawan" (ref: *TOR Encyclopedia*). She was later taken as the Barsen'thor's Padawan at her late father's request, relayed by Master Syo Bakarn (ref: SWTOR Jedi Consular mission "Fearless"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nadia_Grell)
- **Related:** Jedi Initiate; Padawan
- **Uncertainty:** none on the individual facts. Kira Carsen's start year of c. 3645 BBY is [inferred] from her birth year and the "two years into her apprenticeship" line.

### Padawan training
- **Type:** rank / training stage
- **Era:** general; era specifics 3681–3636 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - A Padawan went everywhere with the Master unless the Master judged it unwise, learning by experience. The pair received shared assignments from one of the four Councils (general; ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Padawan/Legends) ("Instruction")
  - Padawans still took Temple classes, but on flexible schedules. A Master could leave the Padawan at the Temple during a solo mission, to take an extra course or one-on-one classes with an instructor. Padawans moved from Control to Sense (general; ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Padawan/Legends) ("Instruction")
  - "As a Padawan, students set their own schedule" around assignments and missions. Partnerships typically lasted over a decade with no fixed term (general; ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_training/Legends) ("Training in the Old Jedi Order")
  - Masters taught practical skills (physical sciences, piloting, droid repair, astronavigation) and self-sufficiency, including meditations and gardening techniques "to stave off hunger" (ref: *TOR Encyclopedia*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Padawan/Legends) ("Instruction")
  - **Era (corrected (lore batch 13), ruling 11):** before the war Padawans needed Core World politics more than Sith warfare; the shift toward military readiness and diplomatic service began with wartime liaison with the Republic military and was completed in the Cold War after the move to Tython. The page puts the completed refocus after the move: Working with the Republic military made command structures and protocols essential, and there was "a renewed emphasis on practical military readiness and diplomatic service" (ref: *TOR Encyclopedia*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Padawan/Legends) ("Instruction")
  - **Era:** before 3681 BBY Satele Shan studied as a Padawan under Ngani Zho (ref: *The Lost Suns* 1). In 3681 BBY she went to the Republic station over Korriban to study under Battlemaster Kao Cen Darach (ref: *Journal of Master Gnost-Dural*), who taught her the double-bladed lightsaber she built and guided her through some of the Jedi Trials (ref: *TOR Encyclopedia*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satele_Shan) ("Early life")
  - **Era:** Ngani Zho trained "many Jedi" in the Great Galactic War, including Satele Shan, Bela Kiwiiks, Jaric Kaedan and Syo Bakarn. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ngani_Zho) ("Great Galactic War"); also (ref: *The Lost Suns* 1) [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bela_Kiwiiks) ("Biography")
  - **Era:** as a senior Padawan at the Coruscant Temple, Gnost-Dural began keeping a journal on the advice of Master Shol Bestros, as a personal reference and as material for the Archives (ref: *Journal of Master Gnost-Dural*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gnost-Dural) ("Apprenticeship")
  - **Era:** in 3643 BBY Council member Orgus Din took a newly arrived learner as his Padawan, his first student in a decade. He gave the Padawan full access to his personal supplies (ref: SWTOR "High-Tech Savages"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hero_of_Tython) ("Training on Tython")
  - **Era:** the same year, Master Yuon Par, a Consular scholar and archaeologist, was assigned a new Padawan on Tython (ref: SWTOR "The Path of a Jedi"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/The_Barsen%27thor) ("Arrival")
  - **Era:** when Bela Kiwiiks left on a solo assignment, Satele Shan made Kira Carsen the Padawan of the newly knighted Hero of Tython, so a Padawan could be reassigned to a new Master (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kira_Carsen) ("Life as a Jedi")
  - **Era:** Kiwiiks and Carsen travelled to many planets but always came back to Tython (ref: SWTOR "Uphrades"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bela_Kiwiiks) ("Biography")
  - **Era:** during the Galactic War, Nadia Grell continued her Padawan studies while handling Sarkhai affairs. At one point she asked her Master for time off from training, and for advice (ref: SWTOR companion conversations). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nadia_Grell) ("Here, there, and everywhere")
  - **Era:** on the Hero of Tython's ship, Kira Carsen held a lightsaber sparring session with Scourge (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kira_Carsen) ("Later conflicts")
- **Related:** Lightsaber construction (Tython Forge); Missions; Jedi Trials
- **Uncertainty:** General (post-Ruusan) sources limit each Master to one Padawan at a time. The era evidence shows Ngani Zho training several future Councillors, but it does not say whether this was at the same time [unclear]. Shigar Konshi was "selected as a Padawan by the recently appointed Grand Master, Satele Shan" [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shigar_Konshi) ("Jedi training"). With *Fatal Alliance* set c. 3643 BBY, this bears on the Grand Master date [conflict] in SW_Jedi_Order_Ranks_Council_OldRepublic.md, and fits its "by 3645 BBY" version better than 3651 BBY [inferred]. The page does not date the selection itself.

### Padawan selection (general)
- **Not adopted for 3681–3653 BBY (lore batch 13, ruling 1):** the annual selection tournament and the age limit for selection contradict this era's attested practice (Padawans served several Masters; older recruits were taken).
- **Type:** practice
- **Era:** general (post-Ruusan Coruscant)
- **Key facts:**
  - Coruscant held an annual Apprentice Tournament in which Initiates duelled with training lightsabers to attract a Master. Thirty-two apprentices fought one-on-one rounds, each ending when a student scored three burns (general; ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Temple_Apprentice_Tournament) ("Overview")
  - Masters also asked instructors for references. A Master could form a bond with a student and ask to teach them, which the Council confirmed. The Council stepped in only if a Seer foresaw a destiny (general; ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Padawan/Legends) ("Selection")
- **Related:** Padawan training; Initiate Trials
- **Uncertainty:** No tournament or selection process is attested for 3,700–3,600 BBY. The era examples are assignment (Yuon Par), a Master's own decision announced to the Council (Orgus Din), and a Grand Master's reassignment (Kira Carsen).

### Jedi instructors and trainers
- **Type:** role
- **Era:** general; named era instructors c. 3681–3637 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - General: instructors were Masters specialising in politics, lightsaber combat, unarmed combat, the sciences and other fields, drawn from all three branches. They were skilled at finding an Initiate's weak points and the role a student would fit (general; ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_instructor) (intro)
  - General: instructors served at every academy and oversaw the clans. In the Order's early days, modified starships served as mobile schools (general; ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_instructor) ("Overview")
  - General: lightsaber instructors were Masters recommended by the standing Battlemaster, the academy's chief instructor, and appointed by the High Council. They taught Forms I to VI. Before the New Sith Wars there was only one Battlemaster, overseeing all instruction at the Coruscant Temple (general; ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_instructor) ("Lightsaber instructors")
  - The Battlemaster role began with the Coruscant academy (ref: *The Jedi Path*). **Era:** in the Cold War and Galactic War the title went to Jedi who had excelled in combat. Jun Seros succeeded Kao Cen Darach (ref: *TOR Encyclopedia*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battlemaster/Legends) ("History")

### Lightsaber and combat training
- **Type:** practice
- **Era:** general; era specifics 3681–3643 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - General: training lightsabers had a strengthened containment field that caused welts and burns instead of cutting. Initiates were not trusted with real sabers until they could perform the "Sevinte" cadence with strikes stopped within a hand's breadth (ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Training_lightsaber/Legends) (intro)
  - **Era:** the electroblade training sword ("practice saber") gave a painful shock instead of cutting. Jedi Padawans used it until judged worthy to go to the Tython Forge and build their own lightsaber (ref: SWTOR codex "Jedi Weapons"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Electroblade_training_sword/Legends)
  - **Era:** a newly arrived 3643 BBY learner fought with a vibrosword before receiving a lightsaber (ref: SWTOR "Attack of the Flesh Raiders"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hero_of_Tython) ("Training on Tython"). The Consular's new Padawan went on the first training task "armed only with a practice saber" (ref: SWTOR "The Path of a Jedi"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/The_Barsen%27thor) ("Arrival")
  - **Era:** the Gnarls Outpost training grounds had merchant stalls. One sold training blades ("Apprentice's Light Foils", "Challenger's Blades", "Understudy's Blades", "Understudy's Light Foils") [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nad%27alin), and another sold stimulants and medpacs [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Adi%27son) (ref: SWTOR)
  - **Era:** the Jedi developed Juyo-Kos, a refinement of Juyo, for the frequent Jedi–Sith clashes of this period (ref: *Blood of the Empire* Act 3). ### Lightsaber construction and the Tython Forge (Vur Tepe)
- **Type:** rite / location
- **Era:** era (c. 3653–3643 BBY); general for Ilum
- **Key facts:**
  - **Era:** Vur Tepe, "the Forge", was an ancient Je'daii temple of metallurgy on Tython (ref: *Dawn of the Jedi* 0). After the Order returned to Tython following 3653 BBY, Padawans travelled to the surviving forges over the next decade to build their lightsabers "as a final rite of passage" before Knighthood (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vur_Tepe) ("Rediscovery")
  - **Era:** in 3643 BBY Orgus Din gave his Padawan the components for a lightsaber and sent the apprentice into the Forge. The Padawan built the weapon with the Force through deep meditation (ref: SWTOR "Weapon of the Jedi"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hero_of_Tython) ("Training on Tython")
  - **Era:** the Consular's Padawan found a green crystal in the caves leading to the Forge and used the machine to make a working lightsaber (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/The_Barsen%27thor) ("Inside the Fount"; "Defending the Forge")
  - **Era (other examples):** Satele Shan built her own double-bladed lightsaber as a Padawan, c. 3681 BBY [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satele_Shan) ("Early life"). Shigar Konshi built a green lightsaber after years of training, by c. 3643 BBY (ref: *Fatal Alliance*) [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shigar_Konshi) ("Jedi training"). Tau Idair built hers "as part of her training" (ref: SWTOR codex "Tau Idair") [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tau_Idair) ("Biography"). Aryn Leneer built several lightsabers over her career, first blue, then yellow, then green (ref: *Deceived*) [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aryn_Leneer) ("Early life")
  - General: later Padawans and their Masters went to Ilum's crystal caves, and a trance ritual was used to build the blade (ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Padawan/Legends) ("Instruction"). The Gathering on Ilum was a crystal-finding rite for Initiates (ref: *The Clone Wars* "The Gathering"; c. 20 BBY example). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gathering/Legends)
- **Related:** Lightsaber and combat training; Jedi Trials
- **Uncertainty:** The Gathering page says the rite was "established at the beginning of the Jedi Order", but gives no example from this window [unclear]. Detailed crystal and hilt lore belongs to the lightsaber topic file.

### Jedi Trials and Knighting in this era
- **Type:** test / ceremony
- **Era:** general; era examples 3681–3640 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Era:** around 3643 BBY two Padawans arrived separately by shuttle at the Masters' Retreat on Tython "to start their Jedi Trials" (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Masters%27_Retreat) (intro)
  - **Era:** around 3643 BBY the Padawans Mennaus, Avitla and Jerridan went into the Gnarls without their Masters "to seek knowledge of the Jedi Code" as part of their trials. A Flesh Raider attack interrupted them (ref: SWTOR mission "Early Lessons"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mennaus) ("Biography"); [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Avitla)
  - **Era:** the Padawan Unaw Aharo passed the Jedi Trials not long after the Flesh Raider conflict (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unaw_Aharo) (intro)
  - **Era (c. 3643 BBY):** the High Council, not the Master, ruled on readiness. Shigar Konshi was judged unready for the Trials for lack of self-control and tact, and his Master would not overrule the Council (ref: *Fatal Alliance*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shigar_Konshi) ("Jedi training")
  - **Era:** Grand Master Satele Shan knighted Din's Padawan before the Council for bravery and for helping stop Bengel Morr (ref: SWTOR "Weapon of the Jedi"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hero_of_Tython) ("Training on Tython"). Later she raised Kira Carsen to Knight in the Council chamber, with the blessing of both her Masters (ref: SWTOR "Fate of the Jedi"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hero_of_Tython) ("Return to Tython")
  - **Era:** Yuon Par fell ill "in the midst of the ceremony" (the Knighting Ceremony) for her Padawan after the Forge mission (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/The_Barsen%27thor) ("Defending the Forge")
  - General: the earliest Trials on Tython were set by the Master and could be harsh. Battlefield heroism against a Sith Lord once counted for the Trials of Courage and of the Flesh, until the Council ended the practice (ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_training/Legends) ("Training in the Old Jedi Order")
  - General: in the past a Master could knight their own student. In war or on long missions, Masters still knighted students in the field (ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Knighting_Ceremony/Legends) ("Old Jedi Order")
  - General (post-Ruusan): the five formal Trials were held in a Trials chamber in the Coruscant Temple, which was completed in 1000 BBY (ref: *Complete Locations* (2005)). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Trials_chamber) ("History")
- **Related:** Lightsaber construction; Jedi instructors; SW_Jedi_Order_Ranks_Council_OldRepublic.md (Jedi Knight entry)
- **Uncertainty:** Which Trials made up the era's "Jedi Trials" is not stated. On the wiki, the Mennaus trio's quest is a "trial", Satele was guided through "some" Trials, and Knighthood could also be granted for deeds [unclear].

### Jedi Temple (Tython) and its grounds, as a place to live and train
- **Type:** location
- **Era:** 3650–3637 BBY (built over three years after 3653 BBY; attacked 3637 BBY)
- **Key facts:**
  - After Coruscant, the High Council fasted for a week and meditated before choosing the site. It built on the foundations of an ancient temple, using local timber and stone (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Temple_%28Tython%29/Legends) ("History")
  - The Temple had a broad promenade with "training grounds spreading out into the surrounding, forested landscape", carved wooden statues at the entrance, and a speeder pool on the promenade (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Temple_%28Tython%29/Legends) ("Exterior"; "Interior")
  - The Jedi cleared the Gnarls, north of the Temple valley, restored the old stone paths and built an outpost and landing pads there (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tythonian_Gnarls) ("History"). The outpost served as a "Jedi training grounds" [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Liv%27trai)
  - The Masters' Retreat was a lakeside meditation temple that "Many Jedi visited ... for peace and tranquility". New Padawans landed there (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Masters%27_Retreat) (intro)
  - The Tython Archives occupied a domed second-storey chamber. It had twelve three-tier stacks, each with a holocron in a repulsor field before it, and five long study tables in the open centre for scholars. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Archives_%28Tython%29) ("Layout")
  - Grand Master Satele Shan had private chambers in the Temple, where she met a newly arrived learner, and the Council questioned the learner in its chamber (ref: SWTOR "High-Tech Savages"). Orgus Din met his Padawan in his own private chambers. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hero_of_Tython) ("Training on Tython")
  - Padawans and archaeologists explored Tython's mysteries in depth (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order/Legends) ("Locations" › "Tython"). Rediscovered ethics manuals, language ciphers and holographic teachings of the first Jedi elders were folded into the Order's teaching (ref: *TOR Encyclopedia*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tython/Legends) ("Rediscovery")
  - After the Eternal Empire conquest (3636 BBY), Tython was largely abandoned except for Jedi "serving in archival and research capacities". By 3627 BBY the Order chose not to resettle it (ref: *Onslaught*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tython/Legends) ("Rediscovery")
- **Related:** Temple duties; Jedi instructors; SW_Jedi_Order_Ranks_Council_OldRepublic.md (Jedi Temple (Tython))

### Temple duties and support roles
- **Type:** duties
- **Era:** general; era examples 3681–3628 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Archives, era:** during the Great Galactic War Gnost-Dural petitioned the Council to leave the front, and was sent back to the Coruscant Archives to research the Sith. He later became a Master and "keeper" of the Archives (ref: *Journal of Master Gnost-Dural*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gnost-Dural) ("Great Galactic War"; "Jedi Master"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Archives_%28Tython%29) ("History")
  - **Archives, era:** protocol droids worked at the Tython Temple Archives. A Padawan contacted one, A-4P0, for the Forge's location (ref: SWTOR "Weapon of the Jedi"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hero_of_Tython) ("Training on Tython")
  - **Archives, general:** the Chief Librarian was the Lore Keeper who ran the Coruscant Archives and led the Educational Corps. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chief_Librarian/Legends) ("Overview"). Gnost-Dural oversaw record-gathering in the Tython Archives before later Chief Librarians (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chief_Librarian/Legends) ("History")
  - **Guard duty:** the Temple Guards ("Temple Guardians" in SWTOR) were established thousands of years before the Galactic War (ref: SWTOR). In general, guard duty was one a Knight could adopt as part of continuing commitment (ref: *Star Wars* Databank). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Temple_Guard/Legends) ("Description"; "Anti-war protests")
  - **Coruscant, era:** in the Cold War, Jedi who stayed on Coruscant formed a Jedi delegation in the Senate, and others protected the ruins of the Temple and its remaining treasures (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order/Legends) ("The Cold War")

### Jedi Service Corps (general)
- **Type:** institution
- **Era:** general ("Old Republic era" to 19 BBY)
- **Key facts:**
  - The Service Corps was an alternative to serving as a Knight. Its members counted as Jedi, and many had failed the Initiate Trials (general; ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Service_Corps/Legends) (intro)
  - Four branches: the Agricultural, Medical, Educational and Exploration Corps. Membership was voluntary, and many ranking Jedi joined, but Initiates who failed were assigned by skill (general; ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Service_Corps/Legends) ("Overview")
  - The Reassignment Council also looked after Padawans who failed the Jedi Trials or were never chosen. It ran four satellite councils: the AgriCorps Masters (generally Ho'Din and Ithorians), the Circle of Healers, the Librarian's Assembly with the Chief Librarian, and ExplorCorps Masters (general; ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Council_of_Reassignment/Legends) ("Overview")
  - Padawans who failed the Trials could also stay on as instructors or engineers, keeping the title Jedi but no longer taking Council missions (general; ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Padawan/Legends) ("Instruction")
  - Some Master–Padawan pairs travelled with the Service Corps on diplomatic or exploration missions (general; ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_training/Legends) ("Training in the Old Jedi Order")
- **Related:** Agricultural Corps; Medical, Educational and Exploration Corps; Initiate Trials
- **Uncertainty:** Which Council oversaw the Corps [conflict]. Version A: the Council of Reassignment (ref: *The Clone Wars Campaign Guide*; *The Jedi Path*) [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Service_Corps/Legends) (infobox; intro); also [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order/Legends) ("Leadership") and [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Council_of_Reassignment/Legends). Version B: "overseen by the Council of Reconciliation" (ref: *The Jedi Path*) [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order/Legends) ("Jedi Service Corps"). Version A is on three pages and in the Jedi Order page's own "Leadership" section, so it fits best. B may be a slip, but that is not confirmed. No source dates the Corps' structure to 3,700–3,600 BBY.

### Agricultural Corps (AgriCorps)
- **Type:** institution
- **Era:** general; era attestation 3645 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - The largest Service Corps branch, made up largely of Jedi who failed academy training or were not chosen as apprentices by age thirteen (ref: *Jedi Apprentice: The Dark Rival*). Membership was voluntary, but the Reassignment Council handled assignment. It worked with the Republic Agricultural Administration to supply crops to systems hit by disaster or blight (ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Agricultural_Corps/Legends) (intro; "Overview")
  - Many lower-ranked Jedi saw assignment there as a demotion. The Corps ran research labs across the Republic, including in the Coruscant Temple, and had facilities on Ukio, Taanab, Marfa, Bandomeer and Dilonexa XXIII (ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Agricultural_Corps/Legends) ("Overview")
  - Members focused on the Living Force and plant-growth skills such as "consitor sato". Most were lifetime members, and there was a sub-branch of Jedi geologists (ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Agricultural_Corps/Legends) ("Overview")
  - Up to 1,500 students at a time, aged 6 to 40 but mostly no older than 16 (ref: Wizards.com "Before the Rise", 2002). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Agricultural_Corps/Legends) ("Overview")
  - **Era:** Marfa, a Core World, housed AgriCorps facilities during the Cold War (ref: *Red Harvest*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Marfa)
  - **Era:** in 3645 BBY, Hestizo Trace, an AgriCorps member at the Marfa agricultural labs, was kidnapped there. She was Force-bonded to a Murakami orchid and could not go far from it without it wilting (ref: *Red Harvest*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hestizo_Trace) ("Biography"); setting date 3645 BBY: [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Harvest_%28novel%29) (infobox). Her brother Rojo Trace, a Jedi Knight, knew she was the orchid's guardian. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rojo_Trace) ("Biography")
  - **Era:** afterwards Hestizo chose to return to Coruscant "where she could resume her Jedi studies" (ref: *Red Harvest*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hestizo_Trace) ("Biography")
  - **Era (related, not the Corps):** the hidden Jedi Colony on Ossus (founded after 3636 BBY, invaded 3628 BBY) developed advanced farming that let plants grow in Ossus's toxic, radioactive ground (ref: SWTOR *Knights of the Eternal Throne* "The Lost Colony"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Colony); farms: [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ossus_Farms)
- **Related:** Service Corps; Padawan training (gardening skills)
- **Uncertainty:** Hestizo "resum[ing] her Jedi studies" on Coruscant in 3645 BBY sits awkwardly with the Order's move to Tython. The Cold War Jedi presence on Coruscant may explain it, but no source links the two [unclear]. Whether her studies meant leaving the AgriCorps is not stated [unclear]. No source calls the Ossus colony farms AgriCorps work.

### Medical, Educational and Exploration Corps (general)
- **Type:** institutions
- **Era:** general; no era attestation found
- **Key facts:**
  - **MedCorps:** one of the two smallest branches, because healing was already widely taught. It ran an infirmary in the Halls of Healing linked by transport to the Republic Central Medcenter, and members served at medical facilities around the galaxy (ref: *The Jedi Path*; *The Clone Wars Campaign Guide*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Medical_Corps/Legends) (intro; "Overview")
  - **EduCorps:** teachers, scholars and archivists. It taught recruits to teach with patience and compassion, without relying on the Force, and served the disadvantaged on many worlds. Lore Keepers who wished to stay in the Archives sometimes joined (ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Educational_Corps) ("Overview"). Its Temple members catalogued and translated Archive data (ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order/Legends) ("Jedi Service Corps")
  - **ExplorCorps:** an archaeological and research branch overseen by the Reassignment Council, set up to survey and chart the galaxy with Jedi Seekers and a small fleet of second-hand survey vessels, with bases on nearly every Ordnance/Regional Depot (intro, no footnotes). Its academies trained Initiates who had failed the Trials and been reassigned; they later took piloting or navigation roles aboard the ships (ref: *The Jedi Path*). Its fleet included Corellian Colonizers and, later, the *Chu'unthor*, and its members saw the most combat of all the Service Corps (ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Exploration_Corps/Legends) (intro; "History")
- **Related:** Service Corps; Temple duties
- **Uncertainty:** Several ExplorCorps facts on that page are cited only to *Savage Spirits* and are excluded (see "Not used").

### Padawan dress and the braid (general)
- **Lore batch 13, ruling 1:** plain tunics are a later-era default adopted for 3681–3653 BBY; the Padawan braid and its severing at Knighthood are **not** adopted. A Padawan in 3661 BBY has no braid.
- **Type:** custom
- **Era:** general
- **Key facts:**
  - Padawans wore the standard brown or gray tunic, trousers and robe with a leather utility belt and boots. The standard was flexible by culture and sometimes lapsed in times of upheaval (ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Padawan/Legends) ("Dress")
  - Species that grow hair wore a single braid behind the ear, which was cut at Knighthood. Others used chains, beads, tattoos or circlets (ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Padawan/Legends) ("Dress")
- **Related:** Jedi Trials and Knighting
- **Uncertainty:** No source shows Padawan braids in 3,700–3,600 BBY [unclear].

## Game notes (optional, suggestions only)
- Suggestion: a "Tython day" in this window could combine the general *Jedi Path* routine (morning Force study, midday classes, afternoon combat, five meditations) with field tasks in the Temple valley, the Gnarls and the Masters' Retreat. The Tython-specific timetable would be the developer's invention, since none is sourced.
- Suggestion: practice weapons (electroblade, training blades) until the Forge rite, then a lightsaber, could mark the Padawan-to-Knight step.
- Suggestion: Service Corps placement (especially the AgriCorps) could be a non-combat career branch for characters who fail or skip the Trials.
- Suggestion: wartime events could allow Council-approved solo Padawan missions and Knighthood for deeds.
