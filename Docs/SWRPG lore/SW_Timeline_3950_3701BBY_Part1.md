# SWRPG Galaxy Timeline, 3,950–3,701 BBY (Part 1 of 3): The Dark Wars' End, Revan's Fate and the Republic's Recovery

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched 24 Nov 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends), as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
This three-part file covers the 250 years between the end of the Old Sith Wars (3,950 BBY) and the eve of the 3,700 BBY files (3,701 BBY): one sourced row per event. Part 1 (this file) covers 3,950 BBY, the busiest single year of the span (Revan's rescue, the Purge of the Dark Council, the failed attack on the Sith Emperor, Meetra Surik's death and Revan's imprisonment), and the Republic's recovery from 3,949 to 3,800 BBY. Part 2 covers 3,799–3,701 BBY (the hidden Empire's preparations, the Ovair affair, Darth Ikoral's expedition and the charting of the Hydian Way) and a table of events dated only "within the span". Part 3 has "Changes from the previous file", Not found, Decisions needed and Sources for the whole set. The key facts: the Old Sith Wars ended in 3,950 BBY; Revan was imprisoned in the Maelstrom for about three centuries, which slowed the Emperor's war; the Emperor purged and replaced his Dark Council and created the posts of Emperor's Voice and Emperor's Wrath; Tol Cressa is the only Supreme Chancellor named in the span (last seen 3,946 BBY); and Wookieepedia has almost nothing dated between c. 3,900 BBY and 3,760 BBY. Where the span is blank, the file says so.

## Entries
How to read the tables: the era headings, the ═ rule lines and "### Overview" follow the 3,700 BBY files so the developer's parser can read them. The columns are Date | Event | Type | Place | Who | Source. Type is one of War, Control, Leader, Death, Birth, Order, Tech or Background (as in the 3,700 BBY files). Sources are Wookieepedia pages; the work each page cites is named where it matters (for example *The Old Republic: Revan*, the SWTOR Encyclopedia, a SWTOR codex entry). Births and deaths of people who matter later are kept. Where a page gives a year only by counting back from a SWTOR date, the row says so.

## THE END OF THE OLD SITH WARS — 3,950 BBY
## ════════════════════════════════════════════

### Overview
3,950 BBY closes the Old Sith Wars. In the Republic the last remnants of Darth Revan's Sith Empire fall apart in the Sith Civil War and the Republic takes back Sith space [3950 BBY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/3950_BBY); [Sith Civil War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Civil_War). The Jedi Order, nearly wiped out in the First Jedi Purge, starts to rebuild around Meetra Surik's "Lost Jedi" [Jedi Order/Legends §First Jedi Purge and aftermath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order/Legends); [Lost Jedi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lost_Jedi). In the Unknown Regions the year is the climax of *The Old Republic: Revan*: Meetra Surik follows Revan's trail to Nathema and Dromund Kaas, frees him from Darth Nyriss with Lord Scourge's help, and the three attack the Sith Emperor. Scourge betrays them, Surik dies, and Revan is imprisoned for about three centuries [Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War) §The Emperor's machinations](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_%28Post%E2%80%93Great_Hyperspace_War%29); [Revan/Legends §Confronting evil](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Revan/Legends). The Emperor uses Scourge's information to kill his entire Dark Council and fill it with pro-war Lords [Purge of the Dark Council](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Purge_of_the_Dark_Council). On the Core World of Grizmallt, Queen Elsinoré den Tasia begins her reign and an age of colonisation [Elsinoré den Tasia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Elsinor%C3%A9_den_Tasia).

**State of the galaxy, end of 3,950 BBY (summary of the rows below).** The Republic is at peace, the Old Sith Wars (4,000–3,950 BBY) over, but exhausted; Tol Cressa is Supreme Chancellor and the Telosian Restoration Project (begun 3,955 BBY) is under way [Tol Cressa](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tol_Cressa); [Telosian Restoration Project](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telosian_Restoration_Project). The Kanz Disorders (since 3,970 BBY) go on unchecked in the Kanz sector [Kanz Disorders/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kanz_Disorders/Legends). Korriban is empty of Sith and guarded by a Republic fleet [Korriban/Legends §Great Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Korriban/Legends). The Jedi Temple on Coruscant, abandoned during the Purge, stands empty until the Lost Jedi, Bastila Shan and Atris reopen it after Surik leaves [Ruled (Jared, 24 Nov 2026)] [Jedi Order/Legends §First Jedi Purge and aftermath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order/Legends). The Mandalorians are regrouping on Dxun under Canderous Ordo, Mandalore the Preserver [Mandalore (title)/Legends §Mandalore the Preserver](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalore_%28title%29/Legends). Unknown to the Republic, the Sith Empire on Dromund Kaas has a new, wholly pro-war Dark Council, a new Emperor's Wrath (Scourge) and a captive Revan [Purge of the Dark Council](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Purge_of_the_Dark_Council); [Emperor's Wrath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Emperor's_Wrath).

## OLD REPUBLIC RECOVERY — 3,949–3,800 BBY
## ════════════════════════════════════════════

### Overview
Few events are remembered from the first century and a half after the Old Sith Wars. The Republic rebuilds under Supreme Chancellor Tol Cressa, who is remembered as a legendary chancellor for his reconstruction programme and his 3,946 BBY campaign against the rogue G0-T0 droids of the Gordian Reach [Tol Cressa](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tol_Cressa); [3946 BBY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/3946_BBY). The Jedi Order slowly recovers [note: no date for any step of it has been found; see "Undated within the span" in Part 2]. Grizmallt's colonising age ends c. 3,900 BBY with the expedition that settles Naboo, and Revan and Bastila's son Vaner Shan stands for Supreme Chancellor [3900 BBY/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/3900_BBY/Legends); [Supreme Chancellor election, 3900 BBY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Supreme_Chancellor_election,_3900_BBY). Hutt Space gains a ruler who will last two centuries, Karagga the Unyielding (c. 3,840 BBY) [Karagga](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Karagga). Hidden in the Unknown Regions, the Emperor rebuilds his Council, keeps Revan prisoner and begins the Eternal Empire on Zakuul (Part 2).
