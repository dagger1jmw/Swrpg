# Weapon and Armour Classes (all eras)

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched 23 Nov 2026 by Claude from the class pages in SW_Weapons_Armor_Sorted.md (Layer 1 priority list, 84 pages, read through the Wookieepedia API). Draft for review.

**Relationship to the older file.** SW_Weapons_Armor_AllEras.md already covers blaster mechanics and history, pulse-wave blasters, ion weapons, disruptors and rippers, slugthrowers, bowcasters, sonic weapons, stun, vibroweapons, force pikes, electrostaffs, stun batons, armour classes, personal shields, Mandalorian armour, the anti-Jedi gear and its conflicts. This file adds the class-level properties the game's class table needs (range, ammunition, legality, how each class meets armour, shields and lightsabers) and the dated facts for the Old Republic.

**Source tiers.** Prose facts come from each page's text. Costs, weights and ranges come from the page infoboxes, which draw on game books (WEG, WotC and KOTOR guides), so every number below is tagged (game stat). The [game stat] numbers span eras and are only for scale.

## Summary
Blasters are the galaxy's standard weapon at every scale. Disruptors are deadly, short-ranged and illegal. Sonic weapons and charrics can't be deflected by a lightsaber. Rippers, needlers and disruptors get past personal shields. In the Old Republic era, cortosis-weave vibroblades and vibroswords were common because lightsaber-wielding enemies were common. Blast vests barely stop blasters. Heavy battle armour was the heaviest armour available in the Jedi Civil War.

### Toxins
- Poisons act through liquid or gas, and some affect certain species more or not at all (for example white fieljine, which is species-specific). [Poison/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Poison/Legends)

- Poison gas is used both to attack and to defend; nerve toxin is one kind. [Poison gas/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Poison_gas/Legends)
