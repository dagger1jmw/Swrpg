# The Imperial Military and Imperial Intelligence in the Great Galactic War (c. 3681–3653 BBY)

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 21, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends), as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
This file covers the Sith Empire's Army, Navy and Imperial Intelligence during the Great Galactic War (3681–3653 BBY), centred on 3661 BBY, as a home for a non-Sith Imperial character: soldier, officer, pilot or agent. It covers structure and command in the window, joining and training, gear and ships, Intelligence field work, life in service, the war as Imperials saw it around 3661 BBY, named non-Sith Imperials with dates, and a short civilian note.

The sources say:
- Every non-Force-sensitive citizen was conscripted at adulthood and served 25–30 years. Trainees fired live weapons in the Dromund Kaas jungles within weeks.
- The Harrower-class dreadnought and the Mark VI interceptor were the Navy's backbone throughout the war.
- After defeats at Bothawui (3671 BBY) and Alderaan (3667 BBY), the Empire looked for allies. The Diplomatic Service courted the underworld, and the Ministry of Intelligence, staffed entirely by non-Force-sensitives, built up the Mandalorians. That work led to the 3661 BBY blockade of the Hydian Way.
- In 3660 BBY the blockade collapsed, Ord Radama was lost and Republic forces pushed into Sith space. After that the Emperor and the Council wanted a quick end to the war.
- Few non-Sith commanders are named for 3661 BBY itself. Minister of War Shareis and Minister of Logistics Shullis Khamarr are the clearest.

### A. Structure and command

#### The Imperial Military in the window
- **Type:** institution
- **Era:** whole window
- **Key facts:**
  - The Imperial Military had two branches, the Army and the Navy. Both worked with Imperial Intelligence. Together they fielded "millions, if not billions" of troops and ships [Imperial Military (Sith Empire)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Military_%28Sith_Empire%29).
  - It was under the Emperor's direct command but overseen by the Minister of War [Imperial Military (Sith Empire)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Military_%28Sith_Empire%29). The Navy fell under the Ministry of War alongside the Army [Imperial Navy (Sith Empire) §Organization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Navy_%28Sith_Empire%29).
  - The Navy carried Army troops between worlds. It fought in the Tingel Arm assault, both battles of Bothawui and the Sacking of Coruscant, and stood down under the Treaty [Imperial Navy (Sith Empire) §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Navy_%28Sith_Empire%29).
  - The Army suffered a "devastating defeat" at Bothawui and helped carry out the Sacking [Imperial Army (Sith Empire)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Army_%28Sith_Empire%29). At the second battle of Bothawui it relied on sheer numbers instead of tactics and lost [Imperial Army (Sith Empire) §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Army_%28Sith_Empire%29)[Imperial Military (Sith Empire) §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Military_%28Sith_Empire%29).
  - Other wartime operations included the Minos Cluster Campaign, the third battle of the Seswenna sector and the Tingel Arm campaign. At the Sacking, Army troops secured Galactic City while Sith Warriors raided the Temple [Imperial Military (Sith Empire) §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Military_%28Sith_Empire%29).
  - The military was "particularly close" with Darth Malgus, and he returned the regard, which was rare for a Sith [Imperial Military (Sith Empire)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Military_%28Sith_Empire%29).
- **Related:** Power Structures Part1 (Ministry of War, the high command).
- **Uncertainty:** none.

#### How Sith commanded and interfered
- Army "Assault Groups" or "Operations Groups" of over 40,000 served under a Moff or a high-ranking Sith Lord for one assignment and were then broken up [Imperial Army (Sith Empire) §Organization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Army_%28Sith_Empire%29).
- At Ord Ibanna the fleet's Sith commander was killed, and his tactical advisor Kilran took over and won [Battle of Ord Ibanna](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Ord_Ibanna). Kilran had been a capable right hand to the Sith in the Brema sector, and in time both the Sith and the Minister of War came to respect his unorthodox methods [Rycus Kilran §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rycus_Kilran).
- At Ord Radama in 3660 BBY, Malgus ordered Venemal to take out the gun crews while their soldiers stayed under cover [Battle of Ord Radama §Early invasion and the conquest of Livien Magnus](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Ord_Radama). When a Harrower crashed, Malgus's rage buckled the *Lindworm*'s bridge viewport and made the crew's ears bleed [Lindworm](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lindworm).
- Losing Ord Radama cost Malgus political standing with the Dark Council and Darth Angral [Battle of Ord Radama §Aftermath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Ord_Radama).
- A Keeper who failed in the eyes of the Minister or the Sith on the Dark Council "could be swiftly replaced" [undated] [Keeper (Imperial Intelligence) §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Keeper_%28Imperial_Intelligence%29).
- Agents faced "the capriciousness of the Sith Lords" [Imperial Agent §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Agent). Some quietly undermined Sith efforts or disobeyed orders [Imperial Intelligence (Sith Empire) §Reputation](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Intelligence_%28Sith_Empire%29).
- Even the Dark Council feared the Imperial Guard, and no Sith Lord dared resist them [Imperial Guard](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Guard)[Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War) §Society and culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_%28Post%E2%80%93Great_Hyperspace_War%29).
- **Uncertainty:** No source in the window describes a Sith killing an officer for failure. The *Lindworm* crew's injuries are the nearest example.

#### Imperial Guard (only where it touches soldiers)
- Guardsmen were picked from the Imperial Military's best non-Force-sensitive warriors and mentally bonded to the Emperor [Imperial Guard §Organization and philosophy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Guard).
- Their armour was stark white with red markings, unlike regular infantry [Imperial Guard §Equipment and training](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Guard).
- From 3681 BBY they guarded the Citadel and the Korriban academy. Several fought beside Zellos's army at Second Bothawui in 3671 BBY [Imperial Guard §Protecting the Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Guard).
- To a Dromund Kaas child like Quinn, joining the Guard was "the highest honor a non-Sith could achieve" [Malavai Quinn §Early life and service](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Malavai_Quinn).
- More detail is in Power Structures Part1.

### B. Joining and training

#### Service obligation
- Every non-Force-sensitive was conscripted at adulthood and served until retirement or disability [Imperial Military (Sith Empire) §Organization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Military_%28Sith_Empire%29). Typical service was 25–30 years [Imperial Army (Sith Empire) §Organization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Army_%28Sith_Empire%29). Power Structures and the Dromund Kaas file cover this.
- Vaiken's training regimen was used "for over a millennium" [Imperial Military training regimen](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Military_training_regimen).
- Military families: promotion let officers found service dynasties, but their children got no special treatment [Imperial Military (Sith Empire) §Organization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Military_%28Sith_Empire%29). The Dromund Kaas file gives examples.
- Window examples: Vasil Dorne enlisted in 3685 BBY following family tradition [Vasil Dorne §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vasil_Dorne). Quinn followed his father Rymar into the military [Malavai Quinn](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Malavai_Quinn).

#### Training
- Ground troops began live-fire exercises in the Dromund Kaas jungles within weeks of enlisting. Even non-combatants learned basic weapons, survival and first aid [Imperial Army (Sith Empire) §Organization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Army_%28Sith_Empire%29).
- Many naval officers started their careers captaining a Gage-class transport [undated] [Gage-class transport §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gage-class_transport).
- Intelligence: Watcher X was genetically altered in the womb by a joint Science Bureau and Intelligence programme and joined at 21 (3664 BBY) [Watcher X §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Watcher_X). Shara Jenn was raised in an Imperial facility programme to sharpen her thinking, date not given [Shara Jenn §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shara_Jenn).
- Vasil Dorne trained Intelligence operatives in combat specialities after 3653 BBY (post-window) [Vasil Dorne §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vasil_Dorne).
- **Uncertainty:** **"Imperial academy on Dromund Kaas" premise:** not attested for soldiers. The only Dromund Kaas academy found is the Sith Academy, where Malgus and Scourge trained [Sith Academy (Dromund Kaas)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Academy_%28Dromund_Kaas%29). The "Imperial Academy" and "Imperial Naval Academy" pages are Canon or Galactic Empire era. No Sith Empire naval academy was found.

#### Aliens and non-Humans
- Humans and Sith purebloods were seen as master races. Aliens were restricted, and humanoid aliens were thought fit only to be slaves [Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War) §Society and culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_%28Post%E2%80%93Great_Hyperspace_War%29).
- Field hospitals limited treatment to favoured humanoid species: Humans, Chiss, Duros, Kaleesh, Twi'leks, Aqualish, Zabraks and purebloods [Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War) §Society and culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_%28Post%E2%80%93Great_Hyperspace_War%29).
- The Chiss Ascendancy was the Empire's only true ally from 3681 BBY, and some Chiss served as Imperial Agents [Chiss Ascendancy/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chiss_Ascendancy/Legends)[Imperial Agent](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Agent). A Chiss enclave on Hoth passed intercepted Republic messages to Imperial Intelligence in 3665 BBY [Battle of Hoth (Great Galactic War) §Prelude](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Hoth_%28Great_Galactic_War%29).
- Aliens were formally admitted to the forces only after Ilum in 3640 BBY (post-window). Aliens and Force-sensitive slaves could already train on Korriban [Imperial Military (Sith Empire) §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Military_%28Sith_Empire%29).
- Ula Vii, an Epicanthix, was an Intelligence informer in the Cold War (post-window) [Ula Vii](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ula_Vii).
- **Uncertainty:** [inferred] Non-Human soldiers were rare or absent in the window, since alien induction is dated to 3640 BBY. No window example of an alien soldier was found.

### C. Gear

#### What a soldier carried
- Soldiers carried blaster rifles. The TH-17 was used in 3681 BBY [TH-17 blaster rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TH-17_blaster_rifle), and the Army page names the TH-17 and DLA-13 [Imperial Army (Sith Empire) §Organization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Army_%28Sith_Empire%29).
- Heavy troopers wore full-body duraplast armour, about a hundred pounds of it. Coloured markings showed unit or assignment. The leg armour protected against shrapnel, the bracers held environmental and targeting controls, and a chest readout gave medical diagnostics [Imperial Army (Sith Empire) §Organization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Army_%28Sith_Empire%29)[Imperial Military (Sith Empire) §Army](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Military_%28Sith_Empire%29).
- Most soldiers carried a rifle, sidearm, grenades and five days of rations [Imperial Army (Sith Empire) §Organization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Army_%28Sith_Empire%29).
- Army vehicles and droids: crawler tanks (the "Sith siege tank", used throughout the Great War as troop carriers and mobile artillery) [Imperial crawler tank](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_crawler_tank), walkers, and war droids such as the Sith war droid Mark II, deployed at the Sacking [Imperial Military (Sith Empire) §Army](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Military_%28Sith_Empire%29)[Sith war droid Mark II](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_war_droid_Mark_II).
- Flamethrower troopers cleared Republic ambushers at Ord Radama [Battle of Ord Radama §Early invasion and the conquest of Livien Magnus](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Ord_Radama).
- The three-legged walker on its own page is dated to the Cold War (post-window) [Unidentified military walker](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_military_walker).
- **Uncertainty:** [conflict] Armour colour: usually gray, with black, white, green and red variants [Imperial Army (Sith Empire) §Organization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Army_%28Sith_Empire%29); black [Imperial Military (Sith Empire) §Army](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Military_%28Sith_Empire%29); black and red [Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War) §Army](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_%28Post%E2%80%93Great_Hyperspace_War%29).

### D. Imperial Intelligence in the window

#### What is attested for the war years
- Imperial Intelligence was the Empire's main intelligence body in the Great War. It answered to the Minister of Intelligence, and its agents were skilled at manipulation, intimidation and assassination [Imperial Intelligence (Sith Empire)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Intelligence_%28Sith_Empire%29).
- The Ministry of Intelligence was "staffed entirely by non-Force-sensitive Imperials" [Blockade of the Hydian Way §The Mandalorians enter the war](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way).
- Before 3653 BBY it blocked Republic attempts to win over the Hutt Cartel. It also made Mandalore the Lesser, rigging his gladiator bouts on Geonosis and spreading rumours that he was the next Mandalore [Imperial Intelligence (Sith Empire) §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Intelligence_%28Sith_Empire%29)[Blockade of the Hydian Way §The Mandalorians enter the war](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way).

- Its reputation: spies on nearly every non-Imperial world, including Tython and Coruscant. Field agents carried a poison capsule or shot so they could die rather than talk [Imperial Intelligence (Sith Empire) §Reputation](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Intelligence_%28Sith_Empire%29).

### E. Life in service

#### Pay, discipline and punishment
- No pay scales were found.
- Demotion to a Gage transport was a known punishment for captains who angered their commanders [Gage-class transport §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gage-class_transport).
- Quinn was court-martialled for insubordination after Druckenwell (3653 BBY) even though he saved the fleet. Broysc took the credit [Battle of Druckenwell](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Druckenwell)[Malavai Quinn §Early life and service](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Malavai_Quinn).
- Kilran sent operatives to discipline a captain who refused a suicidal order (post-window, Cold War) [Black Talon §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Talon).
- Malgus's rage on the *Lindworm* [Lindworm](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lindworm) and the Keeper's exposure to "swift replacement" [Keeper (Imperial Intelligence) §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Keeper_%28Imperial_Intelligence%29) show the risk of Sith displeasure.

#### Medals and honours
- No Sith Empire medal was found for the window. The "Imperial" Medal of Valor and Medal of Honor pages are Galactic Empire era.
- Kilran was called "Hero of the Battle of Coruscant" within the Empire and was later given the honorary title of Grand Moff (3653 BBY and after) [Sacking of Coruscant §Rycus Kilran](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sacking_of_Coruscant).

#### Propaganda and morale
- The Ministry's news, usually through the Outreach Bureau, slanted the facts and cast the Republic as evil [undated] [Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War) §Ministry of Intelligence](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_%28Post%E2%80%93Great_Hyperspace_War%29)[Outreach Bureau](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Outreach_Bureau).
- The image of the *Star of Coruscant* falling at Hoth (3665 BBY) became a strong propaganda tool [Battle of Hoth (Great Galactic War) §Aftermath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Hoth_%28Great_Galactic_War%29).
- Nearly 50,000 Imperial Army troops were massacred at Bothawui. Zellos lost ten men for every Republic trooper [Blockade of the Hydian Way §The Sith in reflection](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way)[Zellos §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zellos).
- Alderaan (3667 BBY) forced a rethink. The Emperor himself was humbled that the war had dragged on [Blockade of the Hydian Way §The Sith in reflection](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way).
- After the blockade failed, the Emperor and the Council wanted a quick end, which led to the Alderaan ruse and the Sacking [Blockade of the Hydian Way §Aftermath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way).

#### Slang and views
- **"Talon fighter":** the Imperial name for the Republic Liberator [Liberator-class starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Liberator-class_starfighter).
- **"Skulker":** the Republic soldiers' slur for snipers, perhaps aimed at the Imperial sniper corps. The former corps sniper Xizor took it as an insult [Skulker](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Skulker)[Vrath Xizor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vrath_Xizor).
- **"Butcher of Coruscant":** a Republic nickname Kilran adopted with irony [Rycus Kilran §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rycus_Kilran).
- No general statement of how Imperial soldiers saw the Jedi, the Republic or the Mandalorians was found. At Bothawui, Zellos offered Allusis his life if he surrendered, and his overconfidence turned the defenders into martyrs [Zellos §Personality and traits](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zellos).
