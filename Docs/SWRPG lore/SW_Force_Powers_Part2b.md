# Force Powers, Part 2b of 2: Dark-side powers, Sith sorcery and Sith alchemy

## Summary
This part covers the dark-side and Sith-specific powers: Force lightning and its light-side twin Electric Judgment, Force drain and the other life- and spirit-stealing powers (dark transfer, essence transfer, possession, Force walk, midi-chlorian manipulation), destructive powers (Force Destruction, Force blast, Deadly Sight, Force storm (wormhole), the thought bomb), and Sith sorcery and alchemy. The key classification conflicts are lightning (dark, yet Jedi users and a light-side "Electric Judgment" that was once forbidden) and drain (dark, yet New Jedi Order members were "permitted" to use it in one source). The scale-anchor table for both halves is at the end of Part 2a.

**Division of work:** see Part 2a (Parts 1a/1b, 2a/2b, 3a techniques, 3b catalog check). Telekinesis, mind powers and environmental powers are in SW_Force_Powers_Part2a.md.

**Classification key:** as in Part 2a (Jedi class / Sith school / Side). Fields 1–9 of the brief are marked F1–F9.

## Section D: Force lightning and its variants

### Force lightning
- **Type:** F1. Aliases: Sith lightning (Jedi vs. Sith). Jedi: Alter. Sith school: Body ([Force lightning](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_lightning/Legends) (infobox; intro); [Force power](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_power/Legends) ("Body")). Side: "Dark side of the Force (typically)" and "Light side of the Force (Electric Judgment)" in the same infobox. [conflict] See F6.
- **Era:** F4. All eras. The Je'daii used it openly (Dawn of the Jedi); most later Jedi Councils forbade it; Sidious and the Sith used it throughout ([Force lightning](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_lightning/Legends) ("Use by Jedi")).
- **Key facts:**
  - F2. Bolts of energy from the hands that fork to hit several targets; it can kill on contact or be held for torture (Malak on Bastila). It needs a living conduit (Book of Sith), and has once been conjured from thin air ([Force lightning](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_lightning/Legends) ("Description"; "Force lightning users")).
  - F3. Users: Sidious; Tyranus; the Sith Emperor, whose lightning melted Revan's mask into his face and who, as Valkorion, killed Darth Marr; Jorak Uln; Darth Bane; Galen Marek, who could kill with one blast, hit many foes at once and block lightning with his bare hands ([Force lightning](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_lightning/Legends) ("Force lightning users"; "Use by Jedi")).
  - F3. Jedi users: Revan, Meetra Surik, Jolee Bindo, Bastila Shan and Vrook Lamar; Plo Koon; Dooku before his fall (Galidraan); Aayla Secura; Quinlan Vos; Darrus Jeht; Yoda ("Emerald Lightning"); Shaak Ti; Luke, Jacen Solo and Jaden Korr (light-side "Emerald Lightning"); dark-version Jedi users include Relin Druur, Kyp Durron, Jaina Solo, Kyle Katarn, Mara Jade Skywalker and Luke; Kenth Hamner used it on Jedi he defeated ([Force lightning](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_lightning/Legends) ("Use by Jedi")).
  - F5. Sith learn it in the Body school; Jedi users are self-taught or instructed under controlled study (see Electric Judgment) ([Force power](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_power/Legends) ("Body")).
  - F6. **Costs:** prolonged exposure causes sudden skeletal calcification and muscle micro-seizures, chronic for years without treatment; Luke needed a hoverchair at Bakura (4 ABY) (*The Truce at Bakura*). It drains the user, may deform them, and once charred holes in Jacen's palm ([Force lightning](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_lightning/Legends) ("Effect of Force lightning")).
  - F6. [conflict] **Is it a dark act?** Version 1: "most often" a dark-side power, "Sith lightning" (Jedi vs. Sith). Version 2: strong-willed Jedi "could" learn it without falling, but it was "viewed as inherently corrupting" and most Councils forbade it. Version 3: the Je'daii used it without moral concern, while admitting safety risks and calling it a weapon of Bogan. Version 4: some Jedi peacekeepers kept it as a useful tool ([Force lightning](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_lightning/Legends) (intro; "Use by Jedi")). Best fit: dark when fuelled by anger; tolerated light-side forms exist (Electric Judgment, Hassat-durr). Developer to decide.
  - F7. **Counters:** a lightsaber blade or insulating material grounds it; Yoda absorbed and redirected it; Galen and briefly Luke blocked it barehanded; tutaminis resists it (Abeloth vs Luke's Electric Judgment, 44 ABY) ([Force lightning](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_lightning/Legends) ("Description"; "Force lightning users"); [Electric Judgment](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Electric_Judgment) ("Users")). Lightning (with its variants) and Force Net were the only Force powers known to affect the Yuuzhan Vong directly (*NJO: Star by Star*) ([Force lightning](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_lightning/Legends) ("Effect of Force lightning")). Cybernetics: Vader could not cast it because of his life support (see Vader's variant below; SW_Midichlorians_Force_Potential.md, §6).
  - F6. [conflict] **Sidious's face:** the page says intense use "possibly" deforms the user, citing Sidious after Windu reflected his lightning, "although it was also implied that he was using the mask ability"; Plagueis implied users grow stronger when hit by their own lightning ([Force lightning](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_lightning/Legends) ("Effect of Force lightning")). The full conflict is in SW_DarkSide_Fall_Corruption_Redemption_Part2.md, Section 4.
- **Related:** F8. Body school; Electric Judgment; Force Maelstrom; Force storm (lightning); kinetite.
- **Uncertainty:** [conflict] on alignment and on Sidious's disfigurement, as above.

### Force lightning variants
- **Type:** F1. Lightning sub-powers; dark unless noted.
- **Era:** F4. Mostly Old Republic (KotOR, SWTOR) and Galactic Civil War (TFU) sources.
- **Key facts (F2/F3 per variant, all from [Force lightning](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_lightning/Legends) (the named subsection)):**
  - **Force shock:** a short-lived spark (about five seconds) that tracks and hurts the nearest enemy; useful for Sith apprentices.
  - **Force storm (lightning):** an explosion of lightning at every foe within a radius around the user (KotOR); Revan, Darth Nyriss, the Sith Emperor, Bane, Galen Marek, Sidious and others. Not the weather or wormhole power.
  - **Force Tempest:** lightning from all limbs in a whirlwind around the body.
  - **Chain lightning:** jumps from one target to others; stronger and harder to sustain.
  - **Sith barrage:** a bolt rising from the ground.
  - **Lightning Shield:** a protective sheath of lightning that also shocks nearby foes and can let the user float.
  - **Lightning Grenade / Lightning Bomb:** a gripped target is charged until the lightning explodes (Galen Marek, Sidious); the bomb kills bystanders too.
  - **Sith Seeker:** a push followed by an uncontrolled, expanding ball of lightning.
  - **Lightning Burst:** arcs from the body to adjacent enemies (Legacy Era Campaign Guide).
  - **Hassat-durr:** white bolts used by the Baran Do Sages; "not of the dark side" (*Fate of the Jedi: Outcast*).
  - **Vader's variant:** since he could not cast lightning normally, he called it down from a storm he raised above the target (*Masters of Teräs Käsi*).
  - **Colours:** blue is usual; Palpatine, Bane and Darth Nox used purple; the Hidden One white; Talzin, Mother and Luke green ([Force lightning](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_lightning/Legends) ("Colors")).
- **Related:** F8. Force lightning (prerequisite); Force Maelstrom.
- **Uncertainty:** [unclear] Vader's variant comes from a fighting game; it conflicts with nothing but is thinly sourced.

### Electric Judgment (Emerald Lightning)
- **Type:** F1. Aliases: Emerald Lightning; "lightning of Ashla". Jedi: Alter (infobox). Side: light ([Electric Judgment](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Electric_Judgment) (infobox; intro)).
- **Era:** F4. Studied by Jedi Masters since at least 990 BBY; listed as forbidden to Knights on Sabla-Mandibu's "Forbidden Force Techniques" list; used openly by the New Jedi Order (11–45 ABY) ([Electric Judgment](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Electric_Judgment) ("Users")).
- **Key facts:**
  - F2. Bolts like Force lightning, yellow-orange, green, white or golden. It must pass through living flesh; it can kill but was often used to stun. Its energy can be packed into a kinetite (a ball of kinetic force), raised as a shield or chained ([Electric Judgment](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Electric_Judgment) ("Description"; "Applications")).
  - F3. Plo Koon disarmed and stunned Dreed Pommel at Ektra on Metellos to save a hostage, and reported to the Council that he had felt no anger or fear. The pooled Jedi Order rained lightning on Exar Kun at his physical last stand on Yavin 4 at the end of the Great Sith War, a clash that "ravaged the planet". Kun's spirit was seared by Luke's first students (11 ABY). Anakin Solo used it just before his death (*Star by Star*). Luke killed a Slayer in Shimrra's Citadel (29 ABY). Luke and Leia wove golden shells in 45 ABY. Jaden Korr used it in 41 ABY. Yoda used it in the Clone Wars ([Electric Judgment](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Electric_Judgment) ("Users")).
  - F5. Masters studied it "under controlled conditions"; Plo Koon recorded his experience on the Great Holocron at the Council's request ([Electric Judgment](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Electric_Judgment) ("Users")).
  - F6. [conflict] **Source emotion:** Version 1: the Jedi form comes from "a sense of determined justice" (The Jedi Path). Version 2: on at least one occasion it was channelled through anger (*Destiny's Way*). Version 3: the Force lightning page says it can be fuelled by justice "or the normal method" of anger. Version 4: Wyyrlok claimed Jaden's came from fear, though Wookieepedia notes he wrongly took it for dark lightning ([Electric Judgment](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Electric_Judgment) ("Applications"; "Users"); [Force lightning](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_lightning/Legends) ("Electric Judgement")).
  - F6. [conflict] **Allowed or forbidden?** It was on a forbidden list and "uncommon and controversial", and many Jedi thought it "too close" to Force lightning (Plo Koon/Legends calls it "a forbidden Force technique"), yet Masters studied it and the New Jedi Order used it ([Electric Judgment](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Electric_Judgment) (intro; "Users"); [Plo Koon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Plo_Koon/Legends) ("Powers and abilities")).
  - F6. [conflict] **Plo Koon's own stance:** Version 1: after the Pommel incident he "claimed to have no plans to experiment with it in the future" ([Force lightning](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_lightning/Legends) ("Use by Jedi")). Version 2: after meditation he decided it would be wrong to ignore it, developed it into a technique and gained a reputation as its expert ([Electric Judgment](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Electric_Judgment) ("Users")). Best fit: Version 2 (more detailed and supported by several sources). Developer to decide.
  - F6. [conflict] **Name:** the Force lightning page equates Plo Koon's "Electric Judgment" with Force lightning used by a Jedi; the Electric Judgment page treats it as a distinct light-side technique.
  - F7. Tutaminis (Abeloth resisted Luke's) ([Electric Judgment](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Electric_Judgment) ("Users")).
- **Related:** F8. Force lightning; kinetite (Vader used kinetite near the Kaiburr crystal on Mimban while subduing Luke) ([Electric Judgment](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Electric_Judgment) ("Users")).
- **Uncertainty:** [conflict] four points above.

## Section E: Drain, life and spirit powers

### Force drain (drain life, drain Force, dark healing, death field, mass drain)
- **Type:** F1. A family: drain life and dark healing (Saga Edition), drain Force (KotOR II), death field, Force Diminish, mass Force drain. Jedi: Control, Sense and Alter combined. Sith school: Body (drain life, death field). Side: dark ([Force drain](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_drain/Legends) (infobox; "Description"); [Force power](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_power/Legends) ("Body")).
- **Era:** F4. Pioneered after the Hundred-Year Darkness, traditionally by Zelashiel the Blasphemer, then lost and rediscovered; Revan recorded it in his holocron, which Bane later found; Tenebrae used it on Nathema (4999 BBY); the Triumvirate (c. 3952 BBY); Palpatine on Byss (c. 10 ABY) ([Force drain](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_drain/Legends) ("History"; "Old Sith Wars"; "Sith Emperor"; "Age of the New Republic")).
- **Key facts:**
  - F2. Drains the Force connections that bind life until the target dies, fuelling the user's healing or Force energy. Draining close family is very hard (Sidious, *Dark Empire Sourcebook*). It is addictive and sharpens the user's sense for strong Force presences ([Force drain](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_drain/Legends) ("Description")).
  - F2. **Death field:** Bane's sphere of dark energy that withers anyone inside, too exhausting to use unaided (*Dynasty of Evil*). **Force Diminish:** weakens body and mind to death ([Force drain](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_drain/Legends) ("Description")).
  - F2/F3. **Mass drain:** Tenebrae's technique and Nihilus's Hunger left whole worlds lifeless; done right it fuels a Dark Lord or grants immortality; when the user dies, the Force can flow back in (Nathema). It can target one species or be spread over time ([Force drain](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_drain/Legends) ("Mass Force drain")).
  - F3. Scale feats: Tenebrae's Nathema ritual killed all life on the planet, including thousands of Sith Lords (4999 BBY); Nihilus consumed the Miraluka world Katarr (3952 BBY); the reborn Vitiate drained Ziost "the same way as Nathema" (SWTOR, *Shadow of Revan* era); Exar Kun drained thousands of Massassi to preserve his spirit (3996 BBY); Traya consumed the Jedi Masters Vrook, Kavar and Zez-Kai Ell on Dantooine; Malak drained life and Force at once ([Force drain](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_drain/Legends) ("Sith Emperor"; "Old Sith Wars"; "History"); [Darth Nihilus](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Nihilus) (intro)).
  - F5. [conflict] **Can it be taught?** Version 1: Kreia says it cannot be taught, only gained by instinct and by suffering it first-hand, which can leave victims with a hunger. Version 2: Revan redocumented Zelashiel's teachings in his holocron, Bane learned powers from that holocron, and drain was taught to a sect of Sith assassins to track Jedi ([Force drain](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_drain/Legends) ("Description"; "Old Sith Wars")). Best fit: Kreia speaks of the hungering form; the technique itself was taught. Developer to decide.
  - F6. [conflict] **Is it ever permissible?** Version 1: a dark-side power (infobox). Version 2: during the Disciples of Ragnos crisis (14 ABY), New Jedi Order members "were permitted to employ this power on their enemies" (*Jedi Academy*, a game) ([Force drain](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_drain/Legends) ("Age of the New Republic")). Version 3: Krayt drained both Luke and Abeloth in 44 ABY, and Luke accepted it as necessary ([Force drain](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_drain/Legends) ("Age of the New Republic")). Best fit: Version 1; Version 2 rests on one game. Developer to decide.
  - F6. Cost: addiction, the Hunger (Nihilus became "a walking void" and would have died of it), and over-reliance is more dangerous than other dark arts ([Force drain](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_drain/Legends) ("Old Sith Wars")). See SW_DarkSide_Fall_Corruption_Redemption_Part1.md, Section 2 (Nihilus) and Section 5.
  - F7. Not stated on the page beyond family ties; ysalamiri block Force use in general ([Ysalamiri](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ysalamiri/Legends) (intro)).
- **Related:** F8. Wound in the Force; Force Corrupt (Part 2a); essence transfer; Sith sorcery's twisting of life.
- **Uncertainty:** [unclear] Force Diminish carries a {{Fact}} (citation needed) tag. [unclear] The page names "Darth Krayt" in *Fate of the Jedi: Apocalypse* (44 ABY); recorded as written.

### Dark transfer (Dark Transfer of Energy)
- **Type:** F1. Jedi: Control, Sense and Alter. Side: dark (infobox) ([Dark transfer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_transfer) (infobox)).
- **Era:** F4. Discovered by Karness Muur (ancient Sith); Celeste Morne learned it from him; Cade Skywalker manifested it c. 137 ABY; Krayt learned it ([Dark transfer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_transfer) (intro; "Users")).
- **Key facts:**
  - F2. Heals the dying, or at full strength revives the slain, by drawing on the dark side and applying the Force to shatterpoints; the healer sees wounds as broken red lines; lightning arcs appear ([Dark transfer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_transfer) ("Description"; "Applications")).
  - F3. Cade revived Marasiah Fel after Darth Nihl killed her on Vendaxa (c. 137 ABY); cured two friends of coral seeds at once and himself and Azlyn Rae of the rakghoul plague; Krayt revived himself from death ([Dark transfer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_transfer) (intro; "Users")).
  - F5. Cade manifested it untaught, drawing on rage and fear ([Dark transfer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_transfer) ("Applications")).
  - F6. Some Jedi saw it as imposing one's will against the will of the Force ([Dark transfer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_transfer) ("Description")).
- **Related:** F8. Accelerate Healing (Part 1a §C2) and healing others (Part 3a §T11); Force drain; Muur's sorcerous healing ([Sith sorcery](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_sorcery/Legends) ("Healing")). See SW_DarkSide_Fall_Corruption_Redemption_Part3.md, Section 2 (Cade).
- **Uncertainty:** none.

### Essence transfer
- **Type:** F1. Jedi: Control, Sense and Alter. Side: dark ([Essence transfer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Essence_transfer) (infobox)).
- **Era:** F4. Ancient Sith (Muur, Andeddu) to the New Republic (Palpatine's clones, 10–11 ABY) ([Essence transfer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Essence_transfer) ("Users")).
- **Key facts:**
  - F2. Moves the user's spirit into another body or vessel; the user's own body disintegrates; overcoming a spirit already in a body is nearly impossible ([Essence transfer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Essence_transfer) ("Description")).
  - F3. Karness Muur (his talisman); Andeddu lived for centuries; Vitiate ruled for over 1,000 years and made the Children of the Emperor; Kun anchored his spirit in the temple; Darth Zash tried to take her apprentice Kallig's body (3643 BBY) but was trapped in Khem Val's body instead; Bane sought it from Andeddu's holocron on Prakith (980 BBY); Set Harth learned it from Andeddu's holocron; Plagueis deliberately avoided it; Cronal; Palpatine's clone bodies degenerated faster because of his power; Callista Ming moved her soul into the *Eye of Palpatine*'s gunnery computer (18 BBY) and later swapped into the willing Cray Mingla's body (12 ABY); Abeloth ([Essence transfer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Essence_transfer) ("Users")).
  - F5. Holocrons (Andeddu's) and talismans ([Essence transfer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Essence_transfer) ("Users")).
  - F6. [conflict] Dark by infobox; but Callista, committed to the light side, transferred into an inanimate computer without alchemy or extra life energy, an exception to nearly all other cases ([Essence transfer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Essence_transfer) ("Users")).
  - F7. The host's own spirit; strong-willed hosts resist ([Essence transfer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Essence_transfer) ("Description")).
- **Related:** F8. Possession; Force walk; Sith sorcery necromancy. SW_Sith_Training_AllEras_Part2.md, Era 7 (Bane's attempt).
- **Uncertainty:** [conflict] as above. [unclear] Durga the Hutt's claim to the power is unverified.

### Force walk
- **Type:** F1. Side: dark (infobox) ([Force walk](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_walk) (infobox)).
- **Era:** F4. Invented by the Sith Lord Ergast about 300 years after Tulak Hord's death, while trying to recreate Hord's ritual of devouring his enemies' spirits at Yn and Chabosh ([Force walk](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_walk) ("History")).
- **Key facts:**
  - F2. Binds restless ghosts of dead Sith to the user, who draws on their power; unwilling ghosts give more power than willing ones. Masters of it rose fast, then "mysteriously faded" ([Force walk](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_walk) ("Description")).
  - F6. Dark. Binding too many ghosts overwhelms the host with physical and spiritual sickness and uncontrolled bursts of power; a cure said to lie on Belsavis and Voss removes the limit. Only Darth Nox and Ergast bound more than one (SWTOR) ([Force walk](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_walk) ("Description")).
- **Related:** F8. Sith necromancy; possession.
- **Uncertainty:** none.

## Section F: Destructive dark-side powers

### Force Destruction
- **Type:** F1. Side: dark (infobox) ([Force Destruction](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_Destruction) (infobox)). A derivative of Force Push in Telekinesis/Legends ([Telekinesis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends) ("Force Destruction")).
- **Era:** F4. Vitiate and Vaylin (SWTOR era) to Jerec (5 ABY) and later ([Force Destruction](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_Destruction) ("Users")).

Dark energy traps: Tyranus on Korriban, 23 BBY ([Sith necromancy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_necromancy) (intro); [Dark energy trap](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_energy_trap) ("History")).
- **Key facts:**

  - F2/F3. **Dark energy traps** are faint purple shimmers of concentrated dark power that hold a skilled Force user for a time and an unskilled one indefinitely; Anakin was caught in one in the Valley of the Dark Lords ([Dark energy trap](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_energy_trap) (intro; "History")).
- **Related:** F8. Sith alchemy; Sith sorcery (necromancy).
- **Uncertainty:** none.

## Game notes (suggestions only; not lore)
- **Lightning:** dark by default; allow a light-side "Electric Judgment" talent that requires calm and stuns rather than kills, with a fall check if used in anger (*Destiny's Way*).
- **Drain:** model addiction as a rising Hunger score (Nihilus); allow mass drains only as rituals with planetary stakes.
- **Sith sorcery:** require Sith-language incantations and holocron sources; each casting adds corruption (it "grew stronger and more corrupting with each expression").
- **Force storm (wormhole):** a legendary-tier power with a loss-of-control roll that can consume the caster; Jedi groups could attempt it at a corruption cost.
- **Thought bomb:** a scenario device, not a player power: everyone in the ring dies.
