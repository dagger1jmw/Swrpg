# Coruscant: The City in the Great Galactic War (c. 3681–3653 BBY)

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 21, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends), as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
This file covers Coruscant outside the Jedi Temple as a home for non-Jedi characters (citizens, soldiers, smugglers, criminals, officials, refugees) during the Great Galactic War, 3681–3653 BBY. It focuses on 3661–3660 BBY, when the Hydian Way blockade starved the lower levels and set off riots. The sources describe the home front mainly through four events: the Senate's failed emergency session and the 3680 BBY riots and rallies; the 3661 BBY starvation riots; Hylo Visz's relief convoy (3660 BBY); and the Sacking (3653 BBY). The record names few city districts, businesses, gangs or ordinary people for this window. The file says so rather than filling the gaps; post-Sacking material is marked **post-window**.

### B. Wartime life, 3681–3661 BBY: the home front by phase
- **Type:** events / social history
- **Era:** 3681–3660 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **3681 BBY, the failed emergency session:**
    - After the Tingel Arm defeat, the Senate and the High Council called an emergency session on Coruscant. It sank under sector self-interest; senators who did not get defense priority walked out of the Grand Convocation Chamber [Senate emergency defense session §The emergency session](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Senate_emergency_defense_session).
    - The upshot was that every system and sector "was left to defend itself". Allies such as the Ithorians held back their forces [Senate emergency defense session §Aftermath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Senate_emergency_defense_session).
    - One Senate faction held that the attackers were only "another upstart species". It didn't control the Senate but helped paralyse it [Great Galactic War §Republic response](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Galactic_War).
  - **3680 BBY, riots and rallies:**
    - News from the front fed "hysteria" among Coruscanti [Coruscant riots (3680 BBY) §Riots and rallies](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_riots_%283680_BBY%29).
    - Galactic City saw days of political rallies, some for the war effort and some for restraint, plus anti-war rallies, rioting and looting in parts of the city. The Jedi still on the planet were "largely powerless" [Coruscant riots (3680 BBY) §Riots and rallies](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_riots_%283680_BBY%29).
    - The unrest ended when news of the Jedi stand in the Minos Cluster reached Coruscant [Coruscant riots (3680 BBY) §Riots and rallies](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_riots_%283680_BBY%29).
  - **Enlistment:**
    - After the Jedi heroism shown "on the news feeds", lines formed at "countless recruitment centers" across the Republic, and the Senate began real war planning [Great Galactic War §Republic response](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Galactic_War).
    - Coruscanti signed up for the Republic Military after the riots. The economy then fell into a depression that lasted "for decades" [Coruscant riots (3680 BBY) §Aftermath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_riots_%283680_BBY%29).
    - Coruscant-born Elin Garza (born 3696 BBY) forged identity papers at 15 to enlist in 3681 BBY [Elin Garza §Great Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Elin_Garza).
    - Volunteer, not conscripted, as far as the record goes (see Not found).
  - **3671 BBY, morale:** the Bothawui stand raised Republic morale, which had been "extremely low" since the war began [Senate emergency defense session §Aftermath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Senate_emergency_defense_session). Cheering crowds in Senate Plaza are covered in the GGW file.
  - **3661 BBY, the blockade reaches the city:**
    - Mandalorian cruisers took the Hydian Way, the route carrying Outer Rim goods to Core population centres and materiel to the fronts [Blockade of the Hydian Way](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way).
    - Shipping ports dried up and the Senate stalled [Blockade of the Hydian Way §A crippled Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way).
    - As Outer Rim worlds fell, starvation "ravaged" Coruscant's lower levels [Hylo Visz §Breaking the blockade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hylo_Visz).
    - The Senate lobbied the Jedi to act [Hylo Visz §Breaking the blockade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hylo_Visz). When their strike failed, "starving residents of the lower city" staged mass riots "in demand of food and economic support" [Blockade of the Hydian Way §A crippled Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way).
    - Panicked citizens protested and rioted against the Senate's inability to break the blockade [Coruscant riots (3661 BBY)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_riots_%283661_BBY%29).
  - **Surrender talk:** "many senators—even the most ardent war hawks" began to suggest surrender [Blockade of the Hydian Way §A crippled Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way). See Decision 2.
  - **Prices:**
    - By 3660 BBY raw-goods prices had "risen dramatically" [Hylo Visz §Breaking the blockade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hylo_Visz).
    - Visz judged the Republic would "pay any price" [Blockade of the Hydian Way §Smugglers take the lead](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way).
    - She was paid "well above market value" [Hylo Visz §Going into hiding](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hylo_Visz). The Blockade page says "highly inflated prices" [Blockade of the Hydian Way §Aftermath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way).
    - No numbers are given (see Not found).
  - **3660 BBY, relief:**
    - Visz brought freighters of raw materials into Coruscant and was met as a hero by cheering crowds [Hylo Visz §Going into hiding](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hylo_Visz).
    - The 3661 BBY riots ended with the blockade's breaking in 3660 BBY [Coruscant riots (3661 BBY)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_riots_%283661_BBY%29).
    - The relief let the Republic fight on seven more years [Hylo Visz §Going into hiding](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hylo_Visz).
  - **3653 BBY, the end:** the Senate accepted the peace offer after "twenty-eight years of war and several supply crises" [Berooken §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Berooken). "Several" suggests shortages other than the 3661 BBY blockade [inferred: number and dates not given].

### D. Security and law on Coruscant
- **Type:** institutions
- **Era:** as tagged
- **Key facts:**
  - **Coruscant Security Force (CSF):**
    - The planetary police. Its duties included patrols, investigations, counter-terrorism, counter-intelligence, crowd control, diplomatic protection, skylane control and sometimes planetary defense. This list isn't dated to an era [Coruscant Security Force/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_Security_Force/Legends).
    - It answered ultimately to the Supreme Chancellor and the Senate during the Republic [Coruscant Security Force/Legends §Organization and units](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_Security_Force/Legends).
    - It kept a reputation for effectiveness, diverse officers and good relations with the public [Coruscant Security Force/Legends §Beginnings and Sith invasion](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_Security_Force/Legends).
    - GGW uniform, Coruscant Control, Customs Enforcement and the 3653 BBY officers are in the Coruscant file.
  - **Underworld Police:**
    - A separate CSF division patrolled the lower levels, in heavy dark-grey armour with yellow shoulder pads [Coruscant Security Force/Legends §Organization and units](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_Security_Force/Legends); [Coruscant Security Force/Legends §Uniforms and equipment](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_Security_Force/Legends).
    - [unclear] The page gives no era for the division. The Coruscant file flags the same doubt.
  - **Patrol craft:** Coruscant police speeders and RapidResponse speeders. The era isn't stated [Coruscant Security Force/Legends §Droids and speeders](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_Security_Force/Legends).
  - **Senate security:**
    - **CSF page:** in the Old Republic the CSF guarded the Senate Building jointly with the military's Senate Security Officers (SSOs), whose role later grew into the Senate Guard [Coruscant Security Force/Legends §Senate Guard](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_Security_Force/Legends).
    - **Other pages:** Coruscant/Legends says the CSF "worked closely with the Senate Guard" in the Old Republic [Coruscant/Legends §Law enforcement](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant/Legends). The Senate Guard page says its tradition is "as old as the Galactic Senate itself" [Senate Guard/Legends §Duties and organization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Senate_Guard/Legends).
    - See Decision 7.
    - **Guard powers (era not stated):** the Guard held authority over the whole Senate District and "assisted the CSF with uprisings that occurred in the lower levels" [Coruscant Security Force/Legends §Senate Guard](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_Security_Force/Legends).
  - **Precedent [other era, c. 3956 BBY]:** after earlier wars drained manpower, corporations supplied private security guards to patrol Coruscant, which worried citizens [Coruscant Security Force/Legends §Beginnings and Sith invasion](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_Security_Force/Legends). Not attested for this window.
  - **Military presence:**
    - In 3653 BBY Republic soldiers protected civilians in the lower levels [Sacking of Coruscant §Final day](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sacking_of_Coruscant).
    - Republic Special Forces Lt. Harron Tavus testified before the Senate [Berooken §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Berooken).
    - Republic Military forces were called in to quell the post-Sacking riots [Coruscant riots (3653 BBY) §Rioting](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_riots_%283653_BBY%29).
    - No garrison size or permanent units on Coruscant are named for 3681–3654 BBY.
  - **SIS on Coruscant:**
    - **Origin:** before 3681 BBY it was a monitoring and decryption arm hidden in the Senate Library, partly "to avoid questions about its budget". The war led the Senate to make it a full espionage bureau [Republic Strategic Information Service](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Strategic_Information_Service); [Marcus Trant §Early career](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Marcus_Trant).
    - **Headquarters:** the Heorem Complex [Republic Strategic Information Service §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Strategic_Information_Service).
    - **In the blockade:** the SIS learned of Visz's attack and sent help. The relief force is a conflict (Decision 4) [Blockade of the Hydian Way §Smugglers take the lead](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way); [Hylo Visz §Going into hiding](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hylo_Visz).
    - **Cold War work (post-window):** the SIS fed the CSF intelligence on Black Sun and the Justicars [Marcus Trant §Director of SIS](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Marcus_Trant).
- **Related:** Power Structures file (SIS, Military); Coruscant file (CSF officers, Coruscant Control).

### E. Crime and the underworld before the Sacking
- **Type:** organisations / economy
- **Era:** 3681–3653 BBY; later status marked **post-window**
- **Key facts:**
  - **Migrant Merchants' Guild:**
    - A supposed trade consortium. "Even before the war's end" it fronted money laundering and smuggling, mostly nonviolent [Migrant Merchants' Guild §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Migrant_Merchants%27_Guild).
    - Mostly nonhuman, it was "a powerful economic force" in Coruscant's nonhuman immigrant population [Migrant Merchants' Guild §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Migrant_Merchants%27_Guild).
    - Its armed insurrection and the Market takeover are **post-window** [Sacking of Coruscant §Coruscant's long road back](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sacking_of_Coruscant).
  - **The Exchange:**
    - Based on Nar Shaddaa around the Galactic Wars. Chiefly active in the Cold War [Exchange §Organization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Exchange).
    - Zeerid Korr ran weapons for it in the Outer Rim and fell into "massive debt" [Zeerid Korr §Soldier then smuggler](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zeerid_Korr); [Zeerid Korr §Mission for the Exchange](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zeerid_Korr).
    - **Coruscant link (3653 BBY):** the Imperial blockade made it "nearly impossible for the criminal underworld to operate on Coruscant". Engspice users dependent on offworld supply went into withdrawal [Zeerid Korr §Mission for the Exchange](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zeerid_Korr). This implies an import trade in engspice before the Sacking [inferred].
    - The Exchange paid Korr 100,000 credits up front for the run [Zeerid Korr §Mission for the Exchange](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zeerid_Korr).
  - **Hutt Cartel:**
    - Resented the Emperor for leaving it out of his plans [Hutt Cartel/Legends §Old Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hutt_Cartel/Legends).
    - With other neutral groups it wavered "since the start of the war" between the Republic and the Empire, despite Imperial courting [Blockade of the Hydian Way §The Sith in reflection](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way).
    - Bankrolled Visz's blockade run for a return on its investment [Hylo Visz §Breaking the blockade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hylo_Visz).
    - Hutt lord Barrga used Visz for jobs [Hylo Visz §The debacle on Corellia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hylo_Visz).
    - **Coruscant presence:** none is described for the window (see Not found).
  - **Smugglers:**
    - Hylo Visz worked Hutt, corporate and government contracts and preferred government work [Hylo Visz §Working for the Cartel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hylo_Visz).
    - Her band of Zale Barrows, Tyresius Lokai and Quan met in a Nar Shaddaa cantina. It was not on Coruscant [Hylo Visz §Breaking the blockade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hylo_Visz); [Quan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Quan).
    - Lokai was "allegedly born on Coruscant" [Gault Rennow §Hylo Visz and the Hydian Way blockade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gault_Rennow).
  - **Black markets:** the general Legends page says nearly every planet had one, selling forbidden droids, drugs, weapons, banned music and bootleg holos [Black market/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Black_market/Legends). There's nothing specific to Coruscant in this era.
  - **What fills Black Sun's later space:**
    - No named pre-Sacking Coruscant gang or syndicate was found.
    - On record for the window are only the Guild (laundering and smuggling), the offworld spice trade (Exchange) and smuggler relief runs (Hutt-funded).
    - Black Sun, the Justicars and the Guild-run Market are **post-window** (see the Coruscant file).
- **Related:** Coruscant file (crime section); entry G (NPCs).
- **Uncertainty:** [inferred] pre-Sacking engspice imports; no Hutt or Exchange base on Coruscant attested.

### F. Politics a citizen would see
- **Type:** politics
