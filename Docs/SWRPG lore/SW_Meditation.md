This file covers meditation as a practice: what it does, Jedi and Sith approaches, and the named methods. It's lore for the "centered" mechanic and the study and meditation XP sources.

## Summary
Meditation is a mental discipline, used by Force-users and non-Force-users alike, for moving past the "thinking" mind into deeper calm or awareness. The Jedi treat it as essential to self-discipline and Force mastery. Their academies set Initiates five sessions a day, and instructors urge meditation at every spare moment. The Sith meditate through raw emotion to sharpen the mind, restore strength and heal. Named methods include Empty Meditation, moving meditation (with the Alchaka form from ancient Tython), floating meditation and healing trances. Chambers block outside disturbances, and the ancient Sith built mobile meditation spheres for battle meditation.

## Entries

### Meditation: general
- **Type:** practice
- **Era:** all eras
- **Key facts:**
  - A mental technique used by Force-sensitives and non-Force-sensitives to move past the "thinking" mind into deeper relaxation or awareness. The Jedi Order and the Order of the Sith Lords used it to strengthen emotional control and their connection to the Force [Meditation/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Meditation/Legends).
  - Practitioners often built meditation chambers that blocked out disturbances, and some used a rare natural meditation crystal [Meditation/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Meditation/Legends).
  - **Effects listed:** calm, inner peace and serenity; emotional control and a stronger Force connection; visions and foreknowledge; recovering strength and healing wounds (Sith meditation and healing trances) [Meditation/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Meditation/Legends).
- **Related:** SW_Lightsabers_Crystals_Materials_AllEras (the meditation crystal, grown on Tython).
- **Uncertainty:** the page's reference list is empty, so the works behind each claim can't be checked from it.

### Jedi meditation
- **Type:** practice
- **Era:** all eras
- **Key facts:**
  - The Jedi saw meditation as essential to the Third Pillar, Self-Discipline, and to inner peace, harmony and serenity. Scholars argued that mastering the Force was impossible without mastering oneself [Meditation/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Meditation/Legends).
  - The Council of First Knowledge set five meditation sessions a day for Initiates in the Order's academies. Instructors encouraged Initiates and Padawans to meditate at any spare moment [Meditation/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Meditation/Legends).
  - **Empty Meditation:** a Jedi technique for centring oneself, finding inner peace and purging negative emotions by setting an emotional anchor. Obi-Wan recommended it, and Jedi still practised it after the Yuuzhan Vong War [Meditation/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Meditation/Legends).
  - **Heji tal:** developed by Luke after the Order's reformation. It cycles control, sense and alter in changing patterns, giving the same results as Empty Meditation. Dated after the Yuuzhan Vong War [Meditation/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Meditation/Legends).
- **Uncertainty:** the era of the Council of First Knowledge rule [unclear]. It comes from *The Jedi Path* framing and isn't tied to a year; the Cold War Order's own schedule is in SW_Jedi_Daily_Life_Training_OldRepublic.

### Sith meditation
- **Type:** practice (dark side)
- **Era:** all eras
- **Key facts:**
  - Sith meditation sharpened the mind, restored strength and healed wounds. Unlike the Jedi, the Sith used dark, raw emotions to renew themselves and feed their passions [Meditation/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Meditation/Legends).
  - Many Dark Lords meditated privately in special chambers in their palaces or aboard their ships [Meditation/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Meditation/Legends).
  - The ancient Sith of Korriban built meditation spheres, mobile war craft used for battle meditation [Meditation/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Meditation/Legends).
  - **Darth Bane** relied on one or two hours of meditation a day to keep his body energised and his mind sharp, and he almost never slept [Meditation/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Meditation/Legends).
- **Related:** SW_Sith_Training_AllEras; SW_Force_Powers_Part1b §U2 (Sith battle meditation).
- **Uncertainty:** none.

### Moving, floating and healing meditation
- **Type:** practices
- **Era:** as noted
- **Key facts:**
  - **Moving meditation:** practised awake, by focusing on a movement or task to block out distractions, such as repairing starfighters or droids. Anakin and Meetra Surik favoured it; Surik went into trances working on T3-M4, and Bao-Dur only found calm working on machines [Meditation/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Meditation/Legends).
  - **Alchaka:** a personal form developed at Stav Kesh on Tython, before the Republic or the Jedi Order existed. It's endless repetition of intense stances until the practitioner is exhausted [Meditation/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Meditation/Legends).
  - **Matukai:** a non-Jedi meditative martial art, often practised with a meditation band around the head [Meditation/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Meditation/Legends).
  - **Floating (rising) meditation:** a rare skill needing deep immersion in the Force. The page gives "several meters" in one place and "several inches" in another [conflict] [Meditation/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Meditation/Legends).
  - **Healing meditation (healing trance):** heals injuries through the Force. Whiphids had a natural affinity for it, and other groups taught it to their acolytes [Meditation/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Meditation/Legends).
- **Related:** SW_Force_Abilities_Forms_v3 (Floating Meditation; Force Heal (Self)); SW_Force_Traditions_AllEras (Matukai).
- **Uncertainty:** the floating height [conflict], noted above.

### Other traditions
- **Type:** practices
- **Era:** various
- **Key facts:**
  - **Gand Findsmen:** worshipped the mists of Gand and kept their meditations private, usually three unbroken hours. They drew on the Force for abilities such as battlemind and farsight, and the results changed with location: in crowded cities they sensed nearby people's actions, in orbit they gained foreknowledge of planetary cultures, and in hyperspace they sensed what drove people [Meditation/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Meditation/Legends).
  - **Nightsisters of Dathomir:** studied the Force by meditating on green spirits, using the green mist in their temples [Meditation/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Meditation/Legends).
- **Related:** SW_Force_Traditions_AllEras.
- **Uncertainty:** none.
