# Jedi Life at the Coruscant Temple and the Order at War, c. 3681–3653 BBY — Part 1

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 21, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
This file covers the Jedi Order as a place a Jedi player character lives and trains during the Great Galactic War (3681–3653 BBY, before the Sacking), with a focus on the Order's situation in 3661 BBY.

What the sources actually attest for the era:
- **Recruitment** was mostly of young children taken to the Coruscant Temple, but the era also has youths (Shol Bestros) and a twelve-year-old (Jaric Kaedan) brought in late [Shol Bestros §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shol_Bestros); [Jaric Kaedan §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jaric_Kaedan).
- **Apprenticeship was fluid.** A Padawan could serve several Masters in turn (Satele Shan had at least three), and Masters such as Ngani Zho and Orgus Din trained many apprentices over the war [Satele Shan §Fighting the Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satele_Shan); [Ngani Zho §Great Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ngani_Zho); [Orgus Din §Great Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Orgus_Din).
- **Knighthood was sometimes granted in the field** or partly tested at the front [Satele Shan §Early life](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satele_Shan); [Gnost-Dural §Great Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gnost-Dural).
- **In 3661 BBY** the Mandalorian blockade of the Hydian Way pushed a reluctant High Council into a Jedi starfighter strike near Devaron, which failed. Riots and talk of surrender followed in the Senate [Blockade of the Hydian Way §A crippled Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way).
- **Strain on the Code** shows in secret attachments (Satele Shan's son), emotional bonds (Aryn Leneer), Padawans driven to the dark side (Malachor III), and an unknowing Sith sleeper agent inside the Order (Syo Bakarn) [Satele Shan §Fighting the Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satele_Shan); [Aryn Leneer §Early life](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aryn_Leneer); [Malachor III Expedition](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Malachor_III_Expedition); [Syo Bakarn §Seeds planted](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Syo_Bakarn).

Most of the familiar day-to-day detail of Jedi life (clans, refectories, braids, the Initiate Trials, tournaments, five meditations a day) comes from *The Jedi Path*, which describes the post-Ruusan Order (after 1000 BBY). It is **not attested for this window** and is flagged as later-era throughout.

## Entries

### A. Finding and admitting Force-sensitives
- **Type:** institution practice
- **Era:** c. 3700–3653 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Found young and taken to the Temple:**
    - Aryn Leneer, a Balmorran orphan, was found "at an early age" by Master Ven Zallow and taken to the Coruscant Temple for induction [Aryn Leneer §Early life](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aryn_Leneer).
    - Gnost-Dural was trained "from an early age" and studied at the Coruscant Temple as a Padawan [Gnost-Dural §Apprenticeship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gnost-Dural).
    - Satele Shan (born 3699 BBY, Brentaal IV) was "recruited into the Jedi Order like her mother" [Satele Shan §Early life](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satele_Shan).
    - Dar'Nala "was identified as Force-sensitive and taken into the Jedi Order" [Dar'Nala §The Great Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dar%27Nala).
  - **Wartime child recruit:** Syo Bakarn (born c. 3678 BBY, Corellia) was "taken from his homeworld" once the Order discovered his Force-sensitivity. He had earlier been abducted as an infant by the Emperor's Hand, processed at the Korriban academy as the first "Child of the Emperor", and returned to his parents with no memory of it [Syo Bakarn §Seeds planted](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Syo_Bakarn); [Children of the Emperor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Children_of_the_Emperor).
  - **Older recruits:**
    - Jaric Kaedan was "discovered by the Jedi Order at the age of twelve" and trained by Ngani Zho [Jaric Kaedan §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jaric_Kaedan). He was born "sometime before the year 3691 BBY", so he was recruited before c. 3679 BBY [inferred from dates]. Whether that was before or during the war is [unclear].
    - Shol Bestros, a Miraluka born "several years before" 3681 BBY, "turned his back on the Jedi Order" until Battlemaster Kao Cen Darach convinced him as a youth to join. He later reached the High Council [Shol Bestros §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shol_Bestros); [Kao Cen Darach §Career as a Jedi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kao_Cen_Darach).
  - **Long-lived species:** Oteg was educated by the Order "two centuries before the start" of the war [Oteg §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Oteg).
  - **Wartime acceleration:** no source says the Order lowered or raised its age limits, or took more older recruits, because of the war [Not found]. The only older cases (Kaedan, Bestros) predate or straddle 3681 BBY.
  - **Parental consent:** not attested for this era [Not found]. The general Jedi Order page describes consent, honour and resentment among families only for the post-Ruusan Order [Jedi Order/Legends §Public perception](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order/Legends).
  - **Recruiters:** Jedi recruiters (the Acquisition Division) are attested for the Old Republic era in general, for example during the Mandalorian Wars, but none is named for this window [Jedi recruiter §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_recruiter).
- **Related:** SW_Lightsaber_Training_AllEras §1 (early Orders had no age limit); SW_Jedi_Ranks_Part2 (leaving the Order).
- **Uncertainty:** [unclear] Kaedan's recruitment date. [inferred] that Bakarn was recruited during the war (born c. 3678 BBY; Padawan before the treaty).

### B. Initiate and Padawan life
- **Type:** daily life
- **Era:** 3681–3653 BBY (era-attested items only; later-era items flagged)
- **Key facts (attested for the window):**
  - **Curriculum focus:**
    - "In the peaceful years before the Great Galactic War" Padawans needed Core World politics more than Sith warfare.
    - As Jedi worked more with the Republic military, they had to learn command structures and protocols, and the emphasis shifted to "practical military readiness and diplomatic service".
    - The page dates the full transformation to the Cold War, after the move to Tython [Padawan/Legends §Instruction](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Padawan/Legends).
  - **Consular study at the Temple:** Gnost-Dural trained "as a Consular" at the Coruscant Temple. On Master Shol Bestros's advice he kept a journal meant for the Temple Archives, researched Tython and the Infinite Empire in old records, and built his own green-bladed lightsaber "as part of his training" [Gnost-Dural §Apprenticeship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gnost-Dural).
  - **Building one's own lightsaber:** Satele Shan built her blue double-bladed lightsaber and learned to fight with it under Darach [Satele Shan §Early life](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satele_Shan). Ilum and its crystal ritual are described only for the later Order [Padawan/Legends §Instruction](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Padawan/Legends).
  - **Many Masters per Padawan:**
    - Satele was apprenticed to Ngani Zho [Satele Shan §Early life](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satele_Shan), then to Battlemaster Kao Cen Darach (her "second Master") [Satele Shan §Early life](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satele_Shan).
    - She then "spent the next part of the war partnered with various Jedi Masters" before being apprenticed to Dar'Nala [Satele Shan §Fighting the Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satele_Shan).
  - **Many Padawans per Master:**
    - Ngani Zho trained "many Jedi" during the war, including Satele, Bela Kiwiiks, Jaric Kaedan and Syo Bakarn [Ngani Zho §Great Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ngani_Zho).
    - Orgus Din "trained several Padawans to Knighthood throughout the conflict", including Fortris Gall, and then took Bengel Morr [Orgus Din §Great Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Orgus_Din).
  - **How a Master is chosen:**
    - Discovery could make the finder the Master: Zallow found Aryn Leneer and trained her to Knighthood [Aryn Leneer §Early life](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aryn_Leneer); [Ven Zallow §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ven_Zallow).
    - Padawans could also be sent to study under a specialist: Satele went to Darach's Korriban station to study the dark side and the double-bladed lightsaber [Kao Cen Darach §Career as a Jedi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kao_Cen_Darach).
    - The Order's Battlemaster was "the premier instructor of lightsaber combat" [Kao Cen Darach §Career as a Jedi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kao_Cen_Darach). Darach held the post before 3681 BBY [Battlemaster/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battlemaster/Legends).
  - **Where Padawans served:**
    - Padawans went to the front: Satele at Korriban (3681 BBY) [Great Galactic War §Fall of Korriban](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Galactic_War); Jerbhen Hulis's Padawan, killed on the Exal Kressh hunt (3678 BBY) [Jerbhen Hulis §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jerbhen_Hulis); Xerender with Wyellett [Xerender §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Xerender); Syo Bakarn, who saved his Master from a "Mandalorian Jedi hunter" [Syo Bakarn §Seeds planted](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Syo_Bakarn); six Padawans sent alone to Malachor III [Malachor III Expedition](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Malachor_III_Expedition).
    - Padawans were also at the Temple (Satele in 3681 and 3671 BBY; Bengel Morr in 3653 BBY) [Satele Shan §Fighting the Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satele_Shan); [Bengel Morr](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bengel_Morr).
  - **Temple life glimpses:**
    - The Council debriefed Padawan Satele several times and let her sit in on a strategy discussion [Satele Shan §Fighting the Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satele_Shan).
    - Padawan and Master meditated at the Temple (3671 BBY) [Satele Shan §Fighting the Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satele_Shan).
    - Jedi who stayed on Coruscant during the 3680 BBY riots were "largely powerless" to stop them [Coruscant riots (3680 BBY) §Riots and rallies](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_riots_%283680_BBY%29).
  - **Possessions:** besides the lightsaber, recorded personal items are Gnost-Dural's journal [Gnost-Dural §Apprenticeship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gnost-Dural) and the Nautolan tranquillity bracelet Zallow gave Leneer at her Knighting [Ven Zallow §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ven_Zallow).
  - **Socialising with soldiers:** as a young Knight attached to Havoc Squad early in the war, Leneer went with the troopers to cantinas after battles but never drank [Aryn Leneer §Comrade of Havoc Squad](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aryn_Leneer).
  - **Nicknames and titles in use:**
    - "Plain Jedi" (Orgus Din) [Orgus Din](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Orgus_Din)
    - "the Order's Watchman" (Kaedan) [Jaric Kaedan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jaric_Kaedan)
    - "the conscience of the Jedi Order" (Tol Braga) [Tol Braga §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tol_Braga)
    - "Heroes of Karideph" (Din and Tavus) [Orgus Din](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Orgus_Din)
    - Battlemaster [Battlemaster/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battlemaster/Legends)
- **Later-era practice (post-Ruusan *Jedi Path*, NOT attested for 3681–3653 BBY):**
  - clans of about ten Initiates sorted by fosterers;
  - refectories and a daily schedule of Force studies, politics, history, physical training and five meditations;
  - tan or grey tunics, the Padawan braid;
  - the annual selection tournament and an age limit for selection.

### C. Knighthood in this era: Trials and wartime shortcuts
- **Type:** rank transition
- **Era:** 3681–3653 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Trials in the field:** Darach "guided the Padawan through some of the Jedi Trials" on the Korriban station in 3681 BBY, but her Knighthood "was not completed by the end of that year" [Satele Shan §Early life](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satele_Shan). Dar'Nala later helped her "become a full-fledged Jedi Knight" [Satele Shan §Fighting the Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satele_Shan) [conflict, see below].
  - **Knighting by the Council:** the High Council "bestowed upon Gnost-Dural the title of Jedi Knight". At the ceremony he swore to uphold the Jedi Code and defend the Republic. News of the Tingel Arm assault broke the same day, and he was promptly sent with "hundreds of other able-bodied Knights" to the Minos Cluster [Gnost-Dural §Great Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gnost-Dural).
  - **Knighted by one's own Master:** Zallow trained Leneer "to Knighthood" early in the war and marked it with a gift [Ven Zallow §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ven_Zallow). Kaedan, Kiwiiks and others reached Knighthood under Ngani Zho during the war [Jaric Kaedan §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jaric_Kaedan); [Bela Kiwiiks §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bela_Kiwiiks).
  - **Young Knights:** Syo Bakarn, born c. 3678 BBY, was a Knight by 3653 BBY [Syo Bakarn §Seeds planted](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Syo_Bakarn); [Aryn Leneer §Onslaught of the Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aryn_Leneer).
  - **General rule:** "in times of turmoil" the High Council could bypass the Trials for Padawans who had shown great skill, courage or dedication. The example given is from the New Sith Wars [Jedi Trials/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Trials/Legends). No case from this window is labelled as a bypass [Not found].
  - **Knighting rite:** the Tranquillity Spire vigil, the Hall of Knighthood and the severed braid are post-Ruusan *Jedi Path* material [Jedi Trials/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Trials/Legends); [Padawan/Legends §Instruction](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Padawan/Legends). The Hall of Knighthood is not attested for the 3653 BBY Temple (see the Coruscant file).
- **Related:** SW_Jedi_Ranks_Part1 (becoming a Master; self-declared Masters, discouraged by the Council) [Jedi Master/Legends §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Master/Legends).
- **Uncertainty:**
  - [conflict] Who completed Satele's Trials:
    - Darach "guided her through her trials to become a full Jedi Knight" [Kao Cen Darach §Career as a Jedi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kao_Cen_Darach);
    - only "some" Trials, not finished in 3681 BBY [Satele Shan §Early life](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satele_Shan);
    - Dar'Nala "helped her become" a Knight [Satele Shan §Fighting the Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satele_Shan), and Satele reached Knighthood "during the latter stages of the conflict" [Dar'Nala](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dar%27Nala).

    Best fit with most sources: some Trials under Darach (3681 BBY), Knighthood under Dar'Nala, before c. 3667 BBY (Alderaan).
  - [unclear] Dar'Nala's "latter stages" date against Satele leading forces at Alderaan in 3667 BBY [Great Galactic War §Battle of Alderaan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Galactic_War).

### D. The Order at war in 3661 BBY
- **Type:** institution at war
- **Era:** 3661 BBY (with context 3681–3653 BBY)
- **Key facts:**
  - **Numbers:**
    - No total strength is given for the Order [Not found].
    - In 3680 BBY the Council sent "a fleet of battlecruisers" carrying Jedi Knights to the Minos Cluster [Battle of Eliad §Prelude](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Eliad), "hundreds" of them [Gnost-Dural §Great Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gnost-Dural).
    - "Thousands of Jedi" were dead by the war's end [Great Galactic War §Aftermath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Galactic_War).
  - **Fronts in c. 3661 BBY:**
    - The Hydian Way, between Devaron and the Minos Cluster [Blockade of the Hydian Way §A crippled Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way).
    - The Rim Campaign (c. 3661–3660 BBY), with Jedi Master Ven Zallow leading Republic forces, scoring victories and then being driven out [Rim Campaign](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rim_Campaign).
    - Minos Cluster ground fighting, where Republic fighters "lost considerable ground" during the blockade [Blockade of the Hydian Way §A crippled Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way).
    - See SW_Great_Galactic_War_Part2 for battles and dates.
  - **Mandalorian conflict:** three years of "minor battles between Jedi and Mandalorians" preceded the 3661 BBY blockade. Mandalorians flocked to fight "the legendary Jedi Knights" [Blockade of the Hydian Way §A crippled Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way); [Great Galactic War §The Mandalorian blockade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Galactic_War). Mandalorian "Jedi hunters" were active in the war [Syo Bakarn §Seeds planted](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Syo_Bakarn).
  - **The Council's decision:**
    - As Coruscant's lower levels began to starve, the Senate appealed to the High Council.
    - The Council hesitated because it did not want to pull Knights from the Sith fronts, and it suspected the Mandalorians wanted to goad the Jedi into battle.
    - It agreed anyway, out of duty to the starving poor and to deny the Empire the boon [Blockade of the Hydian Way §A crippled Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way).
  - **The strike near Devaron:**
    - Jedi pilots in Aurek-class tactical strikefighters, under Jedi Masters including Gnost-Dural, attacked the blockade.
    - The battle was "short but fierce" and an "embarrassing defeat" [Blockade of the Hydian Way §A crippled Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way).
    - [conflict] On the outcome: the strike force "was wiped out" [Battle near Devaron](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_near_Devaron); versus the Jedi were defeated and "forced to retreat to Devaron" [Great Galactic War §The Mandalorian blockade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Galactic_War).
  - **Aftermath:**
    - News of the defeat set off mass food riots on Coruscant [Blockade of the Hydian Way §A crippled Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way); [Coruscant riots (3661 BBY)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_riots_%283661_BBY%29).
    - When smuggler Hylo Visz moved to break the blockade (3660 BBY), the SIS sent Republic Navy elements and "the Jedi Order's starfighter fleet" to help [Blockade of the Hydian Way §Smugglers take the lead](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way); [Great Galactic War §The Mandalorian blockade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Galactic_War).
  - **Satele Shan's recall:**
    - Recalled to Coruscant in 3661 BBY, she witnessed the riots and "was dismayed" to see them worsen after the Devaron failure [Satele Shan §Hero of the Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satele_Shan).
    - Before that she had served at the front, at Alderaan (3667 BBY), Gell Mattar and Hoth (3665 BBY) [Satele Shan §Fighting the Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satele_Shan); [Satele Shan §Hero of the Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satele_Shan).
  - **Elsewhere c. 3661 BBY:** Gnost-Dural had earlier been moved to the fleet under Admiral Greik. "After years of fighting" his petitions were granted and he returned to the Temple Archives to research the Sith; the date is [unclear] [Gnost-Dural §Great Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gnost-Dural).
- **Related:** SW_Great_Galactic_War_Part2 (Hydian Way, Rim Campaign, Coruscant riots); SW_SWTOR_Era_Power_Structures_Part2 (SIS).
- **Uncertainty:**
  - [conflict] Devaron outcome (above).
  - [unclear] The "five squadrons" figure in our GGW file comes from the page infobox, which the box fetch does not capture, so it could not be rechecked.
