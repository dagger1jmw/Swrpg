## Summary
This three-part file rebuilds the 3,996–3,951 BBY slice of `SW_Legends_Galaxy_Timeline.md` as one sourced row per event. Part 1 (this file) covers the Great Sith War (3,996 BBY, with its 3,997 BBY prelude), the Great Hunt (3,995–3,993 BBY) and the Restoration Period up to the eve of the Mandalorian Wars (3,977 BBY). Part 2 covers the Mandalorian Wars (3,976–3,960 BBY) and the Jedi Civil War (3,959–3,956 BBY). Part 3 covers the Dark Wars and the First Jedi Purge (3,955–3,951 BBY) and holds the back sections for the whole set (Changes from the previous file, Not found, Canon only, Not used, Game notes, Decisions needed, Sources, checklist). Key facts: Wookieepedia dates the whole Great Sith War to the single year 3,996 BBY, with the Krath coup, the Conclave at Deneba and Exar Kun's crowning in 3,997 BBY under the Krath Holy Crusade; the Dark Reaper was a Naga Sadow relic Exar Kun found on Yavin 4, used by Ulic Qel-Droma's forces at Raxus Prime and Makem and dismantled by the Jedi on Thule; the Great Hunt ran 3,995–3,993 BBY. Legends canon choices applied throughout: Revan male and light side, the Exile (Meetra Surik) female and light side.

## GREAT SITH WAR — 3,996 BBY
## ════════════════════════════════════

### Overview
The Great Sith War (also the Sith War, First Sith War, Exar Kun War, and to the Mandalorians the "Great Shadow Crusade") was a galaxy-wide war fought in 3,996 BBY, part of the Old Sith Wars [note: 4,000–3,950 BBY] [Ruled (Jared, 24 Nov 2026): the era starts in 4,000 BBY]; its result was a pyrrhic Republic and Jedi victory, the death of Exar Kun's body and the collapse of his Sith Empire and Brotherhood of the Sith [Great Sith War/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Sith_War/Legends). It grew out of the Krath Holy Crusade (3,997–3,996 BBY), begun when Aleema and Satal Keto seized the Empress Teta system [Krath Holy Crusade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Krath_Holy_Crusade). On the Sith side fought Exar Kun's Sith Empire, the Brotherhood of the Sith, the Krath, the Mandalorian Crusaders and the Mecrosa Order of the Tapani sector; on the other, the Galactic Republic (Supreme Chancellor Sidrona, Defense Minister Netus, Admiral Orley Vanicus) and the Jedi Order [Great Sith War/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Sith_War/Legends). Nearly a quarter of the Republic's civilized worlds were devastated [Great Sith War/Legends §Legacy of the Great Sith War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Sith_War/Legends).

**State of the Republic and the Jedi:** Before the war the Senate dismissed rumours of a rogue Jedi behind months of shipyard raids and refused the Jedi clearance to intervene [Sidrona](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sidrona). The Jedi lost many of their most influential Masters (Arca Jeth at Deneba, Odan-Urr on Ossus, Vodo-Siosk Baas on Coruscant, many more to their own students in the "Sith uprising") [Great Sith War/Legends §Targeting the Jedi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Sith_War/Legends); [Conclave on Exis Station](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Conclave_on_Exis_Station). With Ossus destroyed, the Order moved its headquarters to the Jedi Temple on Coruscant, tying it more closely to the Supreme Chancellor's office [Great Sith War/Legends §Legacy of the Great Sith War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Sith_War/Legends).

## AFTERMATH AND RESTORATION PERIOD — 3,995–3,977 BBY
## ═══════════════════════════════════════════════

### Overview
The Restoration Period begins in 3,995 BBY [note: to 3,966 BBY]: with the Republic near collapse after the war, a group of politicians guaranteed corporations safe passage and trade on the hyperlanes in exchange for investment, which is to rebuild the military and economy; hyperspace scouts resumed exploration [Restoration Period](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Restoration_Period). The cleanup was incomplete: under political pressure the Supreme Chancellor pushed the Jedi toward rebuilding and local conflicts rather than hunting the Brotherhood's survivors, who went underground and spread their teachings; Korriban stayed under Sith control [Great Sith War/Legends §Legacy of the Great Sith War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Sith_War/Legends).

**State of the Jedi Order:** The Council held Masters and Jedi Watchmen to stricter account and pushed uniform teaching, against opposition from Jedi used to a looser hierarchy; the Republic applauded [Restoration Period](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Restoration_Period). The secret Jedi Covenant, founded by Krynda Draay after the war, used Consular "Shadows" to watch for a Sith return [Jedi Covenant](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Covenant). The Order also rooted out the Mecrosa Order (the Cleansing of the Nine Houses) and hunted Sithspawn [Great Sith War/Legends §Legacy of the Great Sith War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Sith_War/Legends). Nomi Sunrider eventually led the whole Order [Great Sith War/Legends §Aftermath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Sith_War/Legends).
