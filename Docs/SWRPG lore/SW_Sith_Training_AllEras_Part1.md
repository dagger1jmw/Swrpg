# Sith Training and Organization Across All Legends Eras — Part 1 (to 1000 BBY)

## Summary
Sith training before 1000 BBY moved through six phases:
- the native Sith species' castes, which Dark Jedi exiles turned into an empire of Lords, Kings and a Dark Lord;
- the artifact-and-spirit tutelage of Nadd and Exar Kun;
- Vitiate's disciplined Empire of a Dark Council and academies;
- Revan's feeder academies and the Triumvirate's Trayus Academy;
- the post-3636 BBY Empire beside the non-Sith Knights of Zakuul;
- the feudal New Sith, which ended in Kaan's Brotherhood of Darkness.

Part 2 (SW_Sith_Training_AllEras_Part2.md) covers 1000 BBY onward. All of this was taken from wiki pages fetched through the Wookieepedia API (starwars.fandom.com); no mirror was used. Each fact cites the page URL and section.

**Cross-reference:** SW_Sith_Academies_Training_OldRepublic.md already covers the Korriban, Dromund Kaas and Odacer-Faustin academies of 3681–3636 BBY, the Korriban academy under Revan and Malak (c. 3959–3956 BBY), and Kaan's Korriban academy (1006–1000 BBY). This file adds only what that one does not have and points back to it where they overlap.

## Era 1: The ancient Sith Empire to the Great Hyperspace War (c. 28,000–5000 BBY)

**Era summary:** Before the Dark Jedi exiles arrived, the Sith species had no academies. Knowledge passed through a rigid caste system, with the priestly Kissai as its teachers. After the exiles of 6900 BBY made themselves "Lords of the Sith", training became personal tutelage by a Sith Lord within an Empire ruled by a Dark Lord and a ten-member Sith Council. Holocrons, tombs and hidden writings held the higher teachings. No academy or cohort system is attested for this era.

### Structure and castes of the native Sith
- **Type:** society / training structure
- **Era:** pre-6900 BBY into the Empire
- **Key facts:**
  - **Castes:** Sith society used a rigid caste system and a ranked hierarchy. The castes were the skilled Zuguruk, the priestly Kissai and the warlike Massassi (ref: *Book of Sith*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_%28species%29/Legends) ("Castes"). The infobox also lists the Grotthu as the slave caste. Slaves could be of many species (ref: *Book of Sith*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_%28species%29/Legends) (infobox; "Castes")
  - **Kissai (teachers):** the priest caste studied the dark side and practised Sith sorcery and alchemy (no footnote). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kissai) (intro). As priests and spiritual leaders they "indoctrinate[d] others in the teachings of the Sith" (ref: *KotOR Campaign Guide*). Under the later Empire, Kissai mostly stayed on Ziost as servants of other Lords, while Massassi became soldiers for various Lords. Some Kissai, such as Marka Ragnos and Naga Sadow, rose very high through their priestly status (ref: *KotOR Campaign Guide*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kissai) ("History")
  - **Massassi (soldiers):** the warrior caste. They were not very intelligent, but could be trained to carry out complex battle plans and maintain their own gear, ships and weapons. They used alchemically hardened armour and the Massassi lanvarok (ref: *KotOR Campaign Guide*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Massassi/Legends) ("Society and culture")
  - **Tribal structure:** before the exiles came, the Sith lived in tribal "circles", each led by one or more ruling sorcerers (ref: *Tales of the Jedi Companion*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_%28species%29/Legends) ("Conquest")
  - **Kings and Adas:** many Sith rose to the title of Sith King (ref: *Book of Sith*). Adas united Korriban c. 28,000 BBY and took the title *Sith'ari*, "overlord". The Rakata taught his people to build holocrons (ref: "Evil Never Dies"; *Jedi vs. Sith*; *KotOR Campaign Guide*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_%28species%29/Legends) ("Culture"; "Unification")
- **Related:** Shadow Hand; the Exiles
- **Uncertainty:** No source describes how a young Kissai or Massassi was educated, at what age, or in what groups.

### The Shadow Hand title
- **Type:** rank
- **Era:** ancient Sith (pre-6900 BBY), reused c. 3997 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - The Sith species page says the Shadow Hand originated on Korriban and was "analogous to that of a Jedi Padawan" (ref: *KotOR Campaign Guide*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_%28species%29/Legends) ("Culture")
  - The Shadow Hand page says it was the title of the Sith King's second-in-command (ref: "Evil Never Dies: The Sith Dynasties", *Insider* 88). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_Hand) (intro)
  - Ajunta Pall won over King Hakagram Graush's Shadow Hand, who betrayed the king to the exiles (no footnote at that sentence). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ajunta_Pall) ("In life")
  - In c. 3997 BBY, Marka Ragnos's spirit branded Exar Kun with the mark of a Dark Lord and Ulic Qel-Droma with the mark of a Shadow Hand (ref: *KotOR Campaign Guide*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Marka_Ragnos) ("Legacy")
- **Related:** Era 2 (Kun and Qel-Droma)
- **Uncertainty:** [conflict] What was a Shadow Hand?
  - Version A: an apprentice-like rank, comparable to a Padawan (*KotOR Campaign Guide*).
  - Version B: the King's second-in-command (*Insider* 88).

  Both describe a subordinate bound to one superior. The Qel-Droma branding as Kun's apprentice fits both, so the versions may overlap [inferred].

### The Exiles, the first Sith Lords and the Sith Council
- **Type:** origin of the ranks
- **Era:** 7003–6900 BBY onward
- **Key facts:**
  - **Origin:** Dark Jedi experimented with the dark side, broke with the Jedi Council (the Second Great Schism, c. 7003 BBY) and fought the Hundred-Year Darkness. They lost at Corbos and were exiled. They reached Korriban, where they subjugated the Sith to learn their techniques (ref: *Jedi vs. Sith*; *Insider* 88). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_%28species%29/Legends) ("Exile"; "Conquest")
  - **Founding date:** the exiles were defeated at Corbos in 6900 BBY. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Exiles) ("Exile")
  - **First ranks:** once in control, the exiles appointed themselves Lords of the Sith, and Ajunta Pall received the title *Jen'ari*, Dark Lord of the Sith (ref: *Book of Sith*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Exiles) ("Jen'ari")
  - **Ajunta Pall's own training:** he had been a Jedi Knight, then a Jedi Master, before he studied alchemy. He executed King Graush with his own sword (ref: SWTOR codex "Tomb of Ajunta Pall"; *Jedi vs. Sith*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ajunta_Pall) ("In life")
  - **Sith Council:** Pall gathered the ambitious exiles of rank into a Sith Council (ref: *Book of Sith*). It was ten Sith Lords advising the Dark Lord, each ruling at least a dozen worlds (no footnote). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Council) (intro; "History")
  - **Breeding a ruling class:** for almost two thousand years the exiles interbred with the Sith by alchemical means. They bred out superstition, loyalty and sympathy and encouraged ambition, cunning and raw Force power (ref: *KotOR Campaign Guide*). The ruling Kissai class became almost entirely Kissai–Dark Jedi hybrids (ref: Holonet "Sith Pureblood"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_%28species%29/Legends) ("Conquest")
  - **Rivalry:** Sith Lords raised private armies against rival Lords and competed for the position of Dark Lord (ref: *Insider* 88; *Essential Atlas*). Outsiders who strayed into the Empire became slaves of minor Lords (ref: *Essential Atlas*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_%28species%29/Legends) ("Conquest")
- **Related:** Sith Council; Era 3 (Vitiate)
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Succession, titles and duelling
- **Type:** promotion / custom
- **Era:** 5000 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Contested succession:** at Marka Ragnos's funeral, Sadow and Kressh both claimed the title of Dark Lord. By Sith tradition the only way to settle it was combat, and they duelled with Force-imbued blades on the threshold of the tomb (ref: *Golden Age of the Sith*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Naga_Sadow/Legends) ("Claimant to the Sith legacy"). Ragnos's ghost stopped the duel. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ludo_Kressh) (intro)
  - **Council politics:** Sadow, Kressh, Simus, Dor Gal-ram and Horak-mul were five of the ten Council Lords under Ragnos. Sadow won the title by persuading the Council that the Republic was raiding the Empire (ref: *Golden Age of the Sith* 3). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Council) ("History")
  - **Weapons:** Kressh fought with a Sith war sword he infused with the Force before a duel (no footnote). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ludo_Kressh) ("Equipment")
  - **Assassins:** Sadow's Sadow'een assassins, disguised as crew, killed two rival Lords (ref: SWTOR conversation with Horak-mul). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Naga_Sadow/Legends) ("Consolidating power")
  - **Titles:** Tulak Hord earned the titles Lord of Hate, Master of the Gathering Darkness and finally Dark Lord of the Sith (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tulak_Hord) ("Lord of Hate")
- **Related:** Sith Council
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Holocrons, sorcery and alchemy as teaching tools
- **Type:** teaching method
- **Era:** throughout the Empire
- **Key facts:**
  - Holocrons were as common among the old Empire's Dark Lords as Jedi holocrons were among Jedi Masters. A holocron's captured personality served as "the teaching element". Sith gatekeepers did not always hold back dangerous knowledge from a user (no footnote). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_holocron/Legends) ("Characteristics"; "Construction"; "Rite of Commencement")
  - Sith knowledge of sorcery was described as one of their oldest and greatest strengths. Native culture also included live sentient sacrifice and arcane alchemy (refs in section). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_%28species%29/Legends) ("Culture")
  - Exiled to Yavin 4, Sadow supervised alchemical alterations of his Massassi, making them more hunched and monstrous (no footnote at that sentence). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Massassi/Legends) ("Biology and appearance")
- **Related:** Era 2 (Nadd learning from Sadow)
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Where it happened
Korriban (the Valley of the Dark Lords and its tombs), Ziost (the capital and the Kissai), Khar Shian (Sadow's fortress), Yavin 4 (Sadow's exile) and the wider Stygian Caldera. ## Era 2: Freedon Nadd, the Krath and Exar Kun's Brotherhood of the Sith (c. 4400–3996 BBY)

**Era summary:** With no living Sith Empire to join, training in this era passed through spirits, artifacts and charismatic fallen Jedi. Freedon Nadd learned from Naga Sadow, then taught the royal line of Onderon and the Keto cousins as a spirit. Exar Kun was tested by Nadd's spirit on Korriban and crowned by Marka Ragnos's ghost. He then recruited about twenty Jedi Knights into a Brotherhood whose initiation was killing their own Jedi Masters. The academy the Brotherhood founded on Korriban is covered in SW_Sith_Academies_Training_OldRepublic.md (founding [conflict] there).

### Freedon Nadd: from Jedi prodigy to Sith teacher
- **Type:** master / lineage
- **Era:** c. 4400–4000 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Jedi background:** Nadd trained on Ossus under many Masters, Odan-Urr among them (ref: *Tales of the Jedi Companion*; *Star Wars Fact File*). Being passed over for knighthood caused a deep inner conflict (no footnote). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Freedon_Nadd) ("Jedi prodigy")
  - **Learning from Sadow:** he found the holocron of Adas on Ashas Ree (ref: *Insider* 88), then went to Yavin 4, met Sadow's Massassi and "apprenticed himself" to Sadow. When he judged he had learned everything his master could teach, he killed Sadow (intro, no footnote). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Freedon_Nadd) (intro; "Quest for the Sith")
  - **Teaching through a dynasty:** as king of Onderon he used Adas's holocron to rule (ref: *Dark Side Sourcebook*). After death his spirit lingered. The rulers of Iziz "constantly refreshed their skills" in its presence (ref: *Tales of the Jedi Companion*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Freedon_Nadd) ("King of Onderon"; "Sith Master")
  - **Pupils set against each other:** Nadd hoped one well-taught descendant could master Sadow's alchemy and build him a new body, so he made Ommin and Amanoa compete with each other (no footnote at that sentence). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Freedon_Nadd) ("Sith Master")
- **Related:** Era 1 (Sadow); the Krath
- **Uncertainty:** Nadd's apprenticeship to Sadow is told without any curriculum or duration.

### The Krath: self-taught sorcerers (3998–3996 BBY)
- **Type:** dark-side cult (Sith-influenced, not a Sith order at first)
- **Era:** c. 3998–3996 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Founding:** the cult was started by bored young Tetan nobles led by Satal Keto and his cousin Aleema. They named it after a magician-god from childhood fairy tales. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Krath/Legends) ("History")
  - **No formal training:** the early Krath had no formal Sith training. They pieced techniques together from artifacts and occasional input from Sith spirits, learning amulets, mass illusions and Sith sorcery. Female members were called Death Witches (ref: *KotOR Campaign Guide*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Krath/Legends) ("Description")
  - **Self-study:** the Ketos stole a Sith spellbook from a museum on Coruscant and took it to King Ommin. He gave Satal an amulet in exchange for a copy of the book (ref: *Freedon Nadd Uprising* 1). After Ommin's fall, Nadd's spirit promised to guide them. They then became proficient in sorcery from stolen artifacts and texts. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aleema_Keto) ("Coruscant endeavor"; "Trouble for the Republic")
  - **Recruitment by seduction:** against Satal's advice, Aleema brought the Jedi Ulic Qel-Droma into the Krath and turned him. When the jealous Satal tried to have him assassinated, Qel-Droma killed Satal and took his place as co-ruler. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Krath/Legends) ("History")
  - **Rank:** after Ragnos's intervention, Qel-Droma became a "newly-christened Sith Lord" over the Krath, under Kun as Dark Lord. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Krath/Legends) ("History")
  - **Temple guards:** guards at Krath temples during the Krath Holy Crusade wore Holy Battle Armor. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Krath/Legends) ("History")
  - **After the war:** Krath survivors scattered around Korriban. Some joined old Sith settlements. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Krath/Legends) ("History")
- **Related:** Brotherhood of the Sith; Canon "Krath" page (see Canon only, not used)
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Exar Kun's own path: Korriban trials and Ragnos's crowning
- **Type:** initiation
- **Era:** 3997 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Jedi background:** Vodo-Siosk Baas called Kun the most formidable student he ever trained. Kun honed his saber skills in training duels with Crado and Sylvar on Dantooine (ref: *Dark Lords of the Sith* 1). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Exar_Kun/Legends) ("Powers and abilities")
  - **Korriban trials:** Nadd's spirit guided Kun to Korriban and, through "a series of trials designed to test his resolve", decided he was worthy of Sith apprenticeship (intro, no footnote). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Freedon_Nadd) (intro)
  - **Forced surrender:** in the Valley of the Dark Lords, Kun fought dark-side-animated corpses. Then, broken and trapped, he accepted Nadd's offer and gave himself to the dark side, and was healed at once. In the Hall of Judgment he found he could now read Sith writing (refs in section). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Exar_Kun/Legends) ("Surrender to darkness")
  - **The amulet:** on Yavin 4 a Sith amulet magnified Kun's anger and gave him a vision of himself as the Dark Lord of Massassi legend (ref: *Dark Lords of the Sith* 4–5). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Exar_Kun/Legends) ("Seizing power")
  - **Killing his teacher:** Kun later destroyed Nadd's spirit. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Exar_Kun/Legends) ("Preparations")
  - **Crowning:** Kun hunted Nadd's other students, the Ketos, intending to kill them, and duelled Qel-Droma. Ragnos's spirit stopped the duel and named Kun Dark Lord. He burned a Dark Lord's mark into Kun's forehead and a Shadow Hand's mark onto Qel-Droma (ref: *Dark Lords of the Sith* 6; *KotOR Campaign Guide*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Exar_Kun/Legends) ("Lord of the Sith")
- **Related:** Era 1 (Shadow Hand [conflict])
- **Uncertainty:** none

## Era 3: Vitiate's hidden Empire on Dromund Kaas before 3681 BBY (c. 5113–3681 BBY)

**Era summary:** After the Great Hyperspace War, Lord Vitiate killed the old Sith Council and about 8,000 Sith in the Ritual of Nathema. He then led the survivors on a 20-year exodus to Dromund Kaas. There he set up a twelve-member Dark Council of Dark Lords, a Sith Academy on Dromund Kaas, and servants bound to him by ritual. Little about the Dromund Kaas academy's methods is stated beyond Scourge's career. Scourge's eight years there and the reconstituted Empire's academy system after 3681 BBY are already covered in SW_Sith_Academies_Training_OldRepublic.md. This section adds only the founding period and new facts.

Ragnos made him a Sith Lord with the title Lord Vitiate and gave him the right to rule Medriaas (ref: *The Old Republic: Revan*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vitiate) ("Sith Lord of Nathema"). Ragnos declined to take him as an apprentice. ### The Ritual of Nathema and the exodus
- **Type:** founding event
- **Era:** 5000–4980 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **The ritual:** more than eight thousand Sith Lords answered Vitiate's summons to Nathema. He dominated their minds and used them in a ritual that stripped the Force from the planet and killed every living thing on it. Imperial scholars later celebrated the ritual's name as one of the old Empire's greatest achievements (ref: *Revan*; SWTOR codex "Galactic History 31"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_%28Post%E2%80%93Great_Hyperspace_War%29) ("Origins")

Dromund Kaas was found in 4980 BBY (ref: *Revan*; Holonet; *SWTOR Encyclopedia*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vitiate) ("Rebuilding an empire")
- **Related:** Sith Council (Era 1)
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Structure: the Dark Council, the Emperor's servants and the spheres
- **Type:** structure / ranks
- **Era:** from c. 4980 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Dark Council:** the Emperor appointed **twelve** powerful Sith Lords, including Darth Zavakon, to the rank of Dark Lord of the Sith. Together they formed the Dark Council, the highest position a Sith could reach. They became the de facto rulers as the Emperor withdrew (ref: *Revan*; *SWTOR Encyclopedia*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_%28Post%E2%80%93Great_Hyperspace_War%29) ("Forging a new empire")
  - **Spheres:** early on, each Councilor chose an area of society as a sphere of influence. Zavakon (the Sphere of Ancient Knowledge) found that Sith refused to share what they discovered in tombs. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_%28Post%E2%80%93Great_Hyperspace_War%29) ("Forging a new empire")
  - **Emperor's Hands:** they formed an extra layer of power between the Emperor and the Council. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_%28Post%E2%80%93Great_Hyperspace_War%29) ("Central authority")
  - **Imperial Guard:** Guards were "trained in academies on dark-side worlds", then brought before the Emperor and bound to his will (ref: SWTOR codex "Imperial Guard Academy"; "The Imperial Guard"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vitiate) ("Rebuilding an empire")
  - **Emperor's Wrath:** unlike the Emperor's other servants, who were bound by a dark-side ritual, the Wrath submitted willingly. The post, ranked above the Dark Council, was created for Scourge (no footnote). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Emperor%27s_Wrath) (intro; "History")

## Era 4: Revan and Malak, the Trayus Academy and the Sith Triumvirate (c. 3961–3951 BBY)

**Era summary:** This era's Sith training split into two systems. Revan's Empire (3959–3956 BBY) ran the Korriban academy as a feeder school, already covered in SW_Sith_Academies_Training_OldRepublic.md. It also used secret bases such as the Trayus Academy on Malachor V to break captured Jedi into Sith assassins. After 3956 BBY, Kreia, now Darth Traya, re-founded Trayus. She trained Darth Sion and Darth Nihilus as fellow Dark Lords of a Triumvirate, and they in turn produced legions of assassins and marauders. Master–apprentice relations in this era were defined by betrayal: Malak fired on Revan, and Sion and Nihilus cast out Traya.

### How Revan trained Malak
- **Type:** master–apprentice relationship
- **Era:** c. 3962–3956 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Origins:** Alek (Malak) and Revan were fellow Padawans at the Dantooine Enclave. Revan was recognised as the stronger of the two (ref: *KotOR Campaign Guide*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Malak) ("Early life and Jedi training")
  - **Before the Sith:** during the Mandalorian Wars Malak was Revan's "friend and unofficial apprentice" (no footnote). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vitiate) ("Testing the Republic")
  - **Trayus:** Revan and Malak found the Trayus Academy on Malachor V and "delved deeply into the Sith secrets" there (no footnote in extract). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Malak) ("Dantooine Star Map and Malachor V")
  - **Formal apprenticeship:** after the Emperor broke them, they declared themselves leaders of a new Sith Empire and Revan took Malak as his Sith apprentice (ref: *Revan*). As Sith, Revan was Master and Malak apprentice. Malak came to resent Revan and think him weak (ref: *KotOR*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Revan/Legends) ("Dark Lord of the Sith"; "Darth Malak")
  - **Lesson by violence:** when Malak openly called Revan too soft to be a Sith, they duelled aboard Revan's flagship. Revan took off Malak's jaw, and Malak wore a metal prosthesis and vocabulator for the rest of his life (ref: *KotOR Campaign Guide*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Revan/Legends) ("The Jedi Civil War begins")
  - **Betrayal:** Malak ordered his own ships to fire on Revan's bridge when a Jedi strike team boarded (ref: *KotOR*, quote). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Malak) ("Telos IV bombardment and usurping Darth Revan")
  - **Last words:** dying, Malak heard Revan apologise for starting him on the Sith path. Revan reminded him that he had chosen to continue down it (ref: *KotOR*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Revan/Legends) ("A final confrontation")
- **Related:** Era 3 (the Emperor's conversion of both)
- **Uncertainty:** none. No source gives a curriculum Revan taught Malak.

### Malak's apprentices: Darth Bandon and Bastila Shan
- **Type:** master–apprentice relationship
- **Era:** 3956 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Bandon's selection:** Bandon fled his Jedi Master for the Korriban academy. His dark-side commitment, strength, cruelty and ruthlessness set him apart from his fellow students and drew Malak's notice. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Bandon) ("Early life"; "Jedi Civil War")
  - **One official apprentice:** "In the tradition of the Sith Order", Malak sought a single learner to become his official apprentice (ref: *KotOR Prima Guide*). He took Bandon as his **Shadow Hand**, second only to Malak in the Empire (ref: *KotOR Campaign Guide*), and taught him much of his Sith knowledge. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Bandon) ("Jedi Civil War")
  - **Expected challenge:** some in the hierarchy expected Bandon to challenge Malak eventually, reflecting the Sith belief that "the strongest must rule" and that once the apprentice surpassed the Master, "the Master must fall" (ref: Databank). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Bandon) ("Personality and traits")
  - **Bastila Shan:** Malak's last apprentice was the captured Jedi Bastila Shan. She later told Revan she had been tortured (ref: *KotOR*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Revan/Legends) ("Reaching the Temple" and the surrounding sections)
- **Related:** Era 1 (Shadow Hand)
- **Uncertainty:** none

### The Trayus Academy
- **Type:** academy
- **Era:** ancient origin; in use c. 3959–3951 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Origins:** an ancient Sith academy on Malachor V, built millennia before the Mandalorian Wars, then abandoned (ref: *KotOR II*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trayus_Academy) (intro; "History")
  - **Rediscovery:** Revan found it in 3961 BBY, and with it the location of Korriban (ref: *KotOR II*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trayus_Academy) ("History")
  - **Jedi conversion:** c. 3959–3956 BBY Revan used it as one of several secret bases for converting captured Jedi. Sith assassins and "other specially trained forces" brought Jedi there. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trayus_Academy) ("History")
  - **Traya's refounding:** Traya re-established the academy "to train, convert, and create a new generation of Sith" to destroy the Jedi Order (ref: *KotOR II*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trayus_Academy) ("History")
  - **Layout:** an antechamber off the Malachor Depths, eastern and western halls, a central area, and the innermost sanctum, the Trayus Core (ref: *KotOR II*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trayus_Academy) ("Layout")
  - **Output:** marauders "were trained in great numbers along with other Dark Jedi" there (no footnote). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Marauder) ("History"). It "continued producing legions of Sith Lords, assassins and marauders" under Sion and Nihilus. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Nihilus) ("Encounter with Visas Marr")
- **Related:** Sith assassins
- **Uncertainty:** The page carries a {{Citation}} (needs-citations) banner.

### The Sith Triumvirate: Traya, Sion and Nihilus
- **Type:** leadership / masters
- **Era:** c. 3956–3951 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Structure:** three joint Dark Lords of the Sith: Traya and her two apprentices. All three studied at Trayus, learned their own traits, and styled themselves with unique titles: Lord of Betrayal, Lord of Hunger, Lord of Pain (ref: *KotOR II*; *KotOR Campaign Guide*). They were supported by many Sith apprentices, Sith Masters and assassins. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Triumvirate) (intro; "Leadership and origins")
  - **Traya's recruitment:** Kreia, a Jedi historian and Master, had trained many students for decades, challenging them with questions about power (ref: *KotOR II*). The Council feared her teachings bred failures. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kreia) ("Jedi historian"). She hunted for "Force-wounds" and so found Sion and Nihilus. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kreia) ("Betrayal")
  - **Nihilus:** he came to Trayus seeking training, with prestige equal to Sion's. He learned to drain the Force, create wounds in it and use Traya's echoes (no footnote). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Triumvirate) ("Darth Nihilus, Lord of Hunger"). Traya judged his hunger harmful but taught him to harness it. He in turn taught his followers to sense prey across space and to leech off his Force drain (ref: *KotOR II*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Nihilus) ("Sith Triumvirate"; "Encounter with Visas Marr")
  - **Visas Marr:** Nihilus found her as the only living being left on Katarr. She later served as his apprentice, and he sent her after Surik before she turned to Surik's side (ref: *KotOR II*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Nihilus) ("Encounter with Visas Marr"; "Onderon and his downfall")
  - **Sion:** he had been a Sith Lord of Revan's Empire (ref: *KotOR Campaign Guide*). He grew tired of Traya's abstract teachings, and he and Nihilus conspired to cast her out (ref: *KotOR II*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Sion) ("Rise of a Sith Lord"; "The Sith Triumvirate"). At the end he believed that killing Surik would make Traya complete his training "as her true apprentice". [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Sion) ("The final death")
  - **Naming:** in 3963 BBY the Sith acolyte Lucien Draay suggested "Darth Sion" as a name for Haazen's new Sith (ref: *KotOR* comic 34). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Sion) ("Rise of a Sith Lord")
  - **Betrayal of the master:** Sion and Nihilus cast Traya out of the Triumvirate, then began their own campaign against the Jedi (ref: *KotOR Campaign Guide*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kreia) ("Sighting the last Jedi"); [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Triumvirate) ("Destroying the Jedi")
  - **Kreia's last pupil:** Kreia then shaped Meetra Surik as a new pupil. Surik's training ended with Kreia's death on Malachor (ref: *KotOR II*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kreia) ("Conflict and truth"); [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Nihilus) ("Legacy")
- **Related:** Trayus Academy
- **Uncertainty:** [unclear] whether Sion's arrival at Trayus came before or after he was a Lord of Revan's Empire. The Triumvirate page says he fled the post-war vacuum on Korriban to Malachor V.

### Sith assassins and marauders
- **Type:** trained roles
- **Era:** Great Sith War to New Sith Wars; peak 3956–3951 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Assassins' methods:** assassins specialised in stealth and melee, usually with saberstaffs, and some used Force camouflage. They worked alone or in small groups with stealth field generators. Most used force pikes rather than lightsabers. They drew power from their prey's Force presence (refs in sections). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_assassin/Legends) (intro; "Equipment and methods")
  - **Conversion pipeline:** a captured Jedi was tortured until they fell or died, at the hands of operatives "trained in the art of torture and temptation, such as Atton Rand". By this method the assassins' ranks swelled as the Jedi dwindled (ref: *KotOR II*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_assassin/Legends) ("History")
  - **Marauders:** Sith warriors specialising in dual lightsabers and Force Rage. Most could beat an average Jedi. They first appeared in the Great Sith War (no footnote). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Marauder) (intro; "Characteristics"; "History")
- **Related:** Era 6 (later assassin and marauder academies)
- **Uncertainty:** none

## Era 5: The Eternal Empire and the Sith remnant (3636 BBY to c. 3600 BBY)

**Era summary:** In this period the dominant Force-using order was not Sith. Valkorion (Vitiate reborn) ruled Zakuul through the Knights of Zakuul, who drew their power directly from him, and he raised his own children by fear and conditioning. The Sith Empire's Dark Council collapsed after Zakuul's conquest in 3636 BBY. Darth Acina, trained at Korriban, became Empress, rebuilt the Council and opened government to non-Sith advisers. Sources from this period (SWTOR KotFE, KotET and later updates) say almost nothing about academy routine, so many checklist items are Not found.

### Background on the eve of 3636 BBY: Kaggath and infighting
- **Type:** promotion / rivalry
- **Era:** c. 3641–3637 BBY

### The Knights of Zakuul (non-Sith)
- **Type:** Force order, non-Sith, a rival model to Sith training
- **Era:** Valkorion's reign to after 3636 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Philosophy:** Force-sensitive Zakuulans whose philosophy differed from both Jedi and Sith. They treated the Force as a tool for justice and believed their connection to it came directly from Emperor Valkorion. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Knights_of_Zakuul) (intro)
  - **Openness:** unlike the Sith of the time they were encouraged to share information collectively, and to explore their personal connection to the Force (no footnote). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Knights_of_Zakuul) ("Organization and philosophy")
  - **Leadership:** led by a High Justice, a post Vaylin held by 3636 BBY. Royal blood was not required: Yuriem had held it (ref: SWTOR KotFE codex "Indo Zal"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Knights_of_Zakuul) ("Organization and philosophy")
  - **Tactics:** heavily militarised. They favoured group tactics, personal shields, and coordination with walkers and Skytroopers. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Knights_of_Zakuul) ("Organization and philosophy")

  - **After the war:** after Zakuul fell, some Knights became guards of the Alliance leaders. Others joined splinter groups such as the Order of Zildrog. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Knights_of_Zakuul) (intro)

## Era 6: The New Sith Wars and the Brotherhood of Darkness (c. 2000–1000 BBY)

**Era summary:** Darth Ruin founded the New Sith in 2000 BBY with fifty defecting Jedi and the surviving Sith clans. His own disciples killed him, and for a thousand years Sith training was feudal: each Lord recruited, trained and fought for himself. Rivan built battlelords, Darzu bred technobeasts, and warlords such as Odion and Daiman held their own fiefs. Skere Kaan's Brotherhood of Darkness (c. 1010–1000 BBY) replaced this with one army. Everyone was a "Dark Lord" with a Sith Council at the top, Korriban was the elite school, and eight lesser academies each trained one role. The Korriban academy under Qordis is covered in SW_Sith_Academies_Training_OldRepublic.md. This section covers the rest of the system.

### Darth Ruin and the New Sith (c. 2000 BBY)
- **Type:** founder / origin of a new order
- **Era:** 2000 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Fall:** Phanius, an Umbaran Jedi Master, abandoned the Order to pursue forbidden teachings (ref: *Jedi vs. Sith*). He re-emerged as Darth Ruin, "the first Dark Lord of the Sith in a thousand years" (ref: *Insider* 88). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/New_Sith_Wars) ("Schism")
  - **Teaching source:** he found a device holding teachings of the ancient Sith Order and used it to recruit disciples (ref: *Dark Side Sourcebook*; *Insider* 88). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Ruin/Legends) ("Rise of the New Sith")
  - **Recruitment:** fifty Jedi Knights deserted to join him in the Fourth Great Schism of 2000 BBY (ref: *TPM* novelization; *The Jedi Path*; *New Essential Chronology*). He filled out his ranks by infiltrating and then uniting the surviving Sith clans (ref: *Jedi vs. Sith*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/New_Sith_Wars) ("Schism")
  - **Killed by his disciples:** Ruin regarded his disciples as literally "nothing" (ref: *Book of Sith*). His egomania brought their wrath, and they assassinated him, opening a millennium of betrayal among the Sith (ref: *Insider* 88). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Ruin/Legends) (intro; "Personality and traits")
  - **Naming:** Ruin used the title Darth. Few later Sith did until Darth Rivan and Darth Bane (ref: *Jedi vs. Sith*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth/Legends) ("The New Sith Wars and the Brotherhood of Darkness")
- **Related:** Era 7 (Bane's reforms)
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Feudal Sith Lords of the middle centuries
- **Type:** masters / private training
- **Era:** c. 2000–1010 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Dark Underlord:** after Ruin, the first quarter of the wars was dominated by the Dark Underlord, "an emotionless revenant" backed by the Black Knights faction (refs in section). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/New_Sith_Wars) ("Sith ascendancy")
  - **Darth Rivan's name:** the Zelosian Darth Rivan took his name from a corrupted manuscript that misspelled "Revan" (ref: *Jedi vs. Sith*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Rivan) (intro)
  - **Battlelords:** Rivan's fortress on Almas existed to create **Sith battlelords**. A commander was Force-bonded to his troops, so a soldier who tried to harm the commander took the wound himself. The aim was to stamp out insubordination. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Rivan) ("Biography")
  - **Rivan's training room:** he floored it with Force- and lightsaber-resistant Mandalorian iron, and wrote his autobiography in Sith on the inner walls (ref: Living Force "A Mon Alone"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Rivan) ("Biography")
  - [conflict] **Rivan's fate:**
    - Version A: he died betrayed by fellow Sith, specifically his own apprentice (ref: *Vader: The Ultimate Guide*; Wizards "Sith Battlelords").
    - Version B: the Darkstaff swept him forward in time to Ruusan (ref: *Dawn of Defiance*: "Echoes of the Jedi").

    The page presents A as the earlier belief. Both are Legends and the timeline can hold both, A being what contemporaries believed. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Rivan) ("Biography")
  - **Belia Darzu:** a Shi'ido Sith alchemist, Dark Lord in the Sictis Wars, ruling from Tython. She made technobeasts with a virus that grew metal tumours on victims and bound them to her will. Her holocron contained the Rite of Commencement, the ritual that traps the creator's mind in a holocron, which later taught Bane (ref: *Rule of Two*; *New Essential Guide to Droids*). By 1230 BBY she had fallen victim to a conspiracy. The page links it to the end of her alliance with the Mecrosa Order assassins. She left no clear successor (ref: *Book of Sith*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Belia_Darzu) ("Sictis Wars"; "Powers and abilities"; "Legacy")
  - **Warlords:** in the Dark Age, self-proclaimed Dark Lords and warlords fought each other for supremacy. Odion and Daiman squabbled over the remains of the Empire (ref: *Knight Errant* 0; *New Essential Chronology*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/New_Sith_Wars) ("Dark Age")
  - **Before the wars:** Darth Desolous (born c. 3522 BBY), an expelled Jedi Master, trained an army of Pau'ans in his own martial art of cortosis shields and melee weapons (ref: *The Force Unleashed*, PSP/PS2/Wii). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Desolous) ("The Master's fall")
- **Related:** Era 7 (Darzu's holocron)
- **Uncertainty:** [unclear] Rivan's dates. The page places him before 1250 BBY by reasoning from two sources.

### Skere Kaan's Brotherhood of Darkness: structure and ranks
- **Type:** structure / ranks / army
- **Era:** c. 1010–1000 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Origin:** Kaan was a commoner found and trained by the Jedi, then made a Master by a High Council hoping to moderate him. He left in the schism of 1010 BBY and within months had crushed the most feared Sith Lords (ref: *Essential Guide to Warfare*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Brotherhood_of_Darkness/Legends) ("Formation"); [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/New_Sith_Wars) ("The Light and Darkness War")
  - **One army:** in place of feudal warlords, Kaan organised the Sith into a single army. He made his rivals and closest allies Dark Lords and formed a Sith Council to direct the army **and train new Sith** (ref: *Path of Destruction*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Brotherhood_of_Darkness/Legends) ("Organization and structure")
  - **Equality in name:** "All are equal in the Brotherhood": every minor Lord was raised to Dark Lord of the Sith. In practice some Masters, such as Kopecz, led and relayed Kaan's orders (ref: *Path of Destruction*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kopecz) ("The Brotherhood of Darkness")
  - **Darth banned:** Kaan discouraged the title Darth as a source of rivalry. Bane called this fear of usurpation and revived the title (ref: *Path of Destruction*, quote). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth/Legends) ("The New Sith Wars and the Brotherhood of Darkness")
  - **Sith Lords:** the title Sith Lord went to high-ranking Sith who led armies. They had trained at Korriban, having shown their potential early, "lest they be dispatched to a lesser academy elsewhere". Kaan called them the Brotherhood's greatest asset (ref: *Path of Destruction*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Brotherhood_of_Darkness/Legends) ("Organization and structure")
  - **Army ladder:** Sith Lords commanded, assisted by specialised Sith adepts from academies across Sith space. Acolytes and adepts were beginning apprentices. Assassins and spies captured Jedi and handed them to Lords to be tortured until they died or turned. Warriors and marauders led non-Force infantry. Infantry units had four squads, each with a TC-17 sniper and an interference box. The Gloom Walkers served under Kopecz (ref: *Path of Destruction*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Brotherhood_of_Darkness/Legends) ("Military")
- **Related:** SW_Sith_Academies_Training_OldRepublic.md (Korriban, Qordis, Kas'im)
- **Uncertainty:** none
