# Martial Cultures and Non-Lightsaber Combat Traditions, All Eras (Part 3 of 3): Soldier, Guard and Assassin Training, and Back Sections

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched 24 Nov 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends), read through the Wookieepedia API, as cited per entry; one pre-August-2008 revision used under rule 4a. Rulings of 24 Nov 2026 applied (lore-rulings-2026-11-24c.md, 11–20; D-484).

Part 3 covers training institutions as skill sources (who trains, where, how long, what is taught, entry requirements, eras), then the back sections for the whole three-part set.

## Summary

Clone soldiers were trained on Kamino in the decade before 22 BBY (the *Cuy'val Dar* from c. 30 BBY): regulars in birth cohorts of 32 with flash-training and learning helmets, reaching combat readiness at ten years old; commandos in squads of four under the 100 *Cuy'val Dar* for close to ten years; ARCs under Jango Fett himself, then kept in stasis. Carida's academy, which existed c. 5,000 BBY and trained Republic forces long before the Empire, became the Empire's stormtrooper school (a two-year programme for non-clones), inside an academy system that also ran Raithal (Army, one year), Prefsbelt IV (Navy, usually three years), Corulag and the Anaxes War College. The Emperor's Royal Guard trained for a year on Yinchorr and graduated by killing classmates in "The Squall". Outside the state, skills came from guild colleges (Skine Bounty Hunter College), secret guilds (the GenoHaradan, from c. 25,130 BBY), the Mistryl Shadow Guards of Emberlene and the Noghri.

**Regular clones, details**

- Jango took a break from bounty hunting to oversee the clones' growth and training (*Star Wars Encyclopedia*). [Clone trooper/Legends, Training](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clone_trooper/Legends)
- The stress of accelerated growth threatened clones' mental health, so Taun We designed a training and education programme for every stage of development, with a learning environment that kept a sense of community in each batch. Cadets had half the normal time to absorb lessons, so they wore learning helmets with "mental receptivity enhancers" (*Official Star Wars Fact File* 91; *Attack of the Clones: The Visual Dictionary*). [Clone trooper/Legends, Training](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clone_trooper/Legends)
- Clones fit for leadership showed more independence and creativity (Cody, Deviss) (*Insider* 84). [Clone trooper/Legends, Training](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clone_trooper/Legends)
- **Uncertainty:** The page also cites TCW "Clone Cadets" for basic training [S-canon]; the same facts are supported by *Attack of the Clones*, so nothing here depends on it.

**Clone commandos, details**

- Conceived as a compromise: Jango would train ARCs himself while the Kaminoans created commandos, more obedient than ARCs but more independent-minded than regulars (*Hard Contact*; *Republic Commando* game). [Clone commando/Legends, Conception](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clone_commando/Legends)
- Squads of four reflected Kamino's aiwha hunting pods; each commando got the flash-training programme and was assigned to *Cuy'val Dar* trainers, mostly Mandalorians such as Kal Skirata and Walon Vau. Taun We personally guided Delta Squad's leader, Boss, for his first ten years. [Clone commando/Legends, Conception](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clone_commando/Legends)
- Live-fire exercises from early on; anti-terrorist training in the "Killing House"; many commandos died in exercises; near-death torture to train resistance to interrogation left many scarred. Squads were pitted against each other. [Clone commando/Legends, Training](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clone_commando/Legends)
- Result: unfit as ordinary line infantry and unable to run full solo operations like ARCs, but unmatched in organisation and teamwork. Many absorbed Mandalorian culture. [Clone commando/Legends, Training](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clone_commando/Legends)
- Specialisms inside each squad: sniper/assassin (Sev), medic (Fi), leader (Boss, Niner), demolitions (Scorch, Darman), technology and slicing (Fixer, Atin). [Clone commando/Legends, Specialized training](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clone_commando/Legends)
- Walon Vau: "You can't breed soldiers with flash-learning and simulation. They must come face to face with death itself." Vau's comparison: one ARC, one hundred clone troopers, or four clone commandos (*Triple Zero*). [Walon Vau](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Walon_Vau) · [Advanced Recon Commando, rev. 14 Jul 2008](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Advanced_Recon_Commando/Legends?oldid=2081656)
- After Order 66 the remaining commandos became Imperial Commandos attached to the 501st Legion; some refused the order (Ion Team) and some fled to Mandalore. [Clone commando/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clone_commando/Legends)

**The *Cuy'val Dar***

- 100 people summoned by Jango Fett; 75 Mandalorians and 25 others ("those who no longer exist" in Mando'a) (*Triple Zero*). Jango picked the best soldiers, tacticians, sappers, communicators and survival experts; they could never leave Kamino or tell anyone, and were treated as dead (*Hard Contact*). [Cuy'val Dar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cuy'val_Dar)
- The group included Mandalorian mercenaries and a few Journeyman Protectors of Concord Dawn (*Triple Zero*). [Clone trooper/Legends, Training](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clone_trooper/Legends) · [Journeyman Protector/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Journeyman_Protector/Legends)
- Founded c. 30 BBY (*Triple Zero*); trained the 10,000 commandos for close to ten years until Geonosis, 22 BBY. Afterwards most went home; a few stayed to train later clone generations on Kamino and to cross-train regular clones in commando skills. Bases: Tipoca City; Arca Barracks on Coruscant. [Cuy'val Dar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cuy'val_Dar)
- 25 ABY: New Republic Intelligence agent and Jedi Kubariet asked *Mand'alor* Boba Fett to form a new *Cuy'val Dar* of Mandalorian commandos to train planetary militias against the Yuuzhan Vong. [Cuy'val Dar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cuy'val_Dar)
- Named trainers: Kal Skirata (born Falin Mattran c. 82 BBY on Kuat; adopted Omega Squad and the Null ARCs into Clan Skirata in 19 BBY) and Walon Vau (Human from Irmenu, heir to the Count of Gesl, exiled, became Mandalorian; black armor). [Kal Skirata](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kal_Skirata) · [Walon Vau](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Walon_Vau) · [Clan Skirata](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clan_Skirata)

**Advanced Recon Commandos (ARCs)**

- **Uncertainty:** pre-TCW record (rule 4a: revision of 14 July 2008, [oldid 2081656](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Advanced_Recon_Commando/Legends?oldid=2081656))

- Jango and his Mandalorian advisers pushed for clones fit for covert missions too delicate for standard units. The Kaminoans skipped standard behaviour correction and enhanced the template for physical prowess. The first attempt failed (Null-class); the second produced the first 100 ARCs (Alpha-class).
- Under Jango's strict discipline they became "virtual one-man armies", trained on advanced weapons and on vehicles that normally needed specialist clones; then stored in stasis. Prime Minister Lama Su activated them in 22 BBY, during the CIS attack on Kamino two months after Geonosis.
- They worked alone or in small squads under ARC captains. They may train ordinary clones in ARC techniques [Ruled (Jared, 24 Nov 2026)]. They were more prone to question orders (Alpha-98 on Ord Cestus).
- Early 20 BBY: after escaping Rattatak, Alpha-17 returned to Kamino to train the second generation of clone commanders (such as Bacara and Cody) as Jango had trained him.
- After the war, surviving ARCs were offered retirement or the job of training their successors, the stormtroopers; rumoured "deep core programming"; some defected to the Rebellion, others became bounty hunters and assassins (*Insider* 89, *Insider* 84).
- Ranks shown by armor stripes: green sergeant, blue lieutenant, red captain, yellow commander. Sub-types: heavy gunner, pilot, BARC, clone heavy trooper.
- **Uncertainty:** The current Legends page adds TCW material (an "ARC Troopers" episode on Kamino) [S-canon]; not used here.

**Clone assassins**

- Created by Kaminoan cloners on Palpatine's special order; trained to kill Jedi with Teräs Käsi; the Prima game guide says their speed came from Teräs Käsi training, not genetic alteration [Ruled (Jared, 24 Nov 2026): clone assassins stay]. A unit helped in Operation Knightfall (19 BBY); the squad guarding the Temple beacon was killed by Obi-Wan Kenobi. Sources are the *Revenge of the Sith* video game, its Prima guide and *The Clone Wars Campaign Guide*. [Clone assassin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clone_assassin) · [Teräs Käsi/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Teräs_Käsi/Legends)

### 4. Stormtrooper training and the Academy of Carida (stormtrooper training; Carida Academy)

- **Type:** stormtrooper training
- **Era:** 19 BBY to 11 ABY and after.

- **Carida's role under the Empire:** the Academy came under state control as the Imperial Military Academy and became the central training facility for stormtroopers. It also ran a training centre for clones bred, like ARCs, for independence and leadership, with live training on top of flash instruction. It opened the stormtrooper ranks to non-clones through a two-year programme "well known for its brutality" that drew the best and bravest army cadets. The 501st Legion had its own training facility there. [Carida Academy/Legends, History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Carida_Academy/Legends)
- **Everyone does stormtrooper basics:** every trainee at Carida took Stormtrooper Corps basic training and field exercises before specialising. Most applicants fail the physical requirements or wash out; passing is a point of honour; those who fail can still join the Army or Navy (*Imperial Handbook*). [Carida Academy/Legends, Organization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Carida_Academy/Legends)
- **Stormtrooper officer candidates are taught:** unarmed combat (*Dark Forces: Soldier for the Empire*), wilderness survival, desert-warfare simulations, underwater battle tactics, flash-memory instruction in military history, self-discipline through emotional suppression, and the principles of the New Order (*Imperial Handbook*). Live-fire field exercises also tested new weapons; every major arms firm had test facilities on Carida. [Carida Academy/Legends, Organization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Carida_Academy/Legends)
- **Facilities:** Imperial Military Training Base (over 150,000 personnel, mostly Human males); Imperial Navy Command Officer's School (prospective ship captains); Imperial Engineering Academy; "Cliffside" stormtrooper officer academy; Storm Commando School; hospital (busy with Omega Mission casualties); the Quad, called "the grinder". Motto over the dining-room fireplace: "Cowards die a thousand deaths" (*Soldier for the Empire*). Soontir Fel: "Obedience, discipline, and loyalty were always drilled into us" (*X-Wing Rogue Squadron* 25). [Carida Academy/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Carida_Academy/Legends)
- **Destruction:** a Rebel raid before Endor [last resort: a CCG card]; destroyed in 11 ABY when Kyp Durron fired the Sun Crusher's resonance torpedoes into Carida's star, after a two-hour warning in which many Imperials escaped. [Carida Academy/Legends, History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Carida_Academy/Legends)
- **Recruitment:** after the failed Kamino uprising in 12 BBY, Palpatine added clones from other templates (*Battlefront II*) and recruited non-clone Humans (*The Force Unleashed II*). Strict physique and measurement rules made Human males the majority; women served too, and some near-Humans were eligible. Clones and recruits were balanced until the Imperial Civil War cut off the cloning centres. The Empire of the Hand first admitted non-Humans; by 130 ABY the Fel Empire's troopers included many species and more women. Recruits joined for adventure (*Empire: To the Last Man*) or to escape poverty, and were conditioned to absolute loyalty; some deserted (*Tales* 10). In the Fel Empire, children born "in the camp" were encouraged to enlist, with the Continuity of Service letting them serve in their parents' unit; the Imperial Mission was also a recruiting pool (*Legacy* 4). [Stormtrooper/Legends, Recruitment](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stormtrooper/Legends)
- **Corps:** independent branch alongside the Imperial Army, smallest of the three; founded 19 BBY from the Grand Army; fragmented 4 ABY; restored 19 ABY; folded into the Army by the Fel Empire. Pledge: "I am a stormtrooper. My skin is my armor…" (*Imperial Handbook*). Cody blamed falling effectiveness on Human recruits. [Stormtrooper Corps/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stormtrooper_Corps/Legends)
- **Other stormtrooper officer schools:** smaller complexes around the Royal Guard academy on Yinchorr trained officers of the riot troopers and other stormtrooper divisions (*Imperial Handbook*). [Imperial Royal Guard Academy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Royal_Guard_Academy)

### 8. Assassin and covert traditions

- **Type:** assassins and bodyguards

**GenoHaradan**

- **Type:** secret bounty-hunter and assassins' guild
- **Era:** c. 25,130 BBY (Xim's agents) to at least the Galactic War (c. 3,640 BBY and later); rumoured still active in the last decades of the Republic (*Darth Plagueis*).
- **Key facts:**
  - Created by Xim the Despot as secret police to remove enemies (SWTOR codex); after Xim's defeat at the Third Battle of Vontor it vanished from view and its Guildmasters hired out agents to the highest bidder; reorganised as assassins for hire c. 25,100 BBY. Over centuries it began choosing clients that served its interests and split leadership among four Overseers. From the Tionese War it worked almost entirely for the Republic, often without the government knowing. [GenoHaradan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/GenoHaradan)
  - **Methods:** secrecy above all; agents see themselves as more skilled than ordinary hunters but avoid fame; a kill must look as if the agent had no part in it, so other agents are assigned to watch and report the result; the four Overseers communicate only through agents and messengers; how Overseers are chosen is unknown. [GenoHaradan, Organization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/GenoHaradan)
  - **Recruitment:** 3,956 BBY: Overseer Hulas (Rodian) tried to recruit Revan on Manaan through "initiation bounties" on the other Overseers (Vorn Daasraad, Rulan Prolik, Ithorak Guldar) (*KOTOR*). [GenoHaradan, Fate](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/GenoHaradan)
  - c. 3,640 BBY a GenoHaradan squad failed to take the Great Hunt's champion on Hoth; late in the Galactic War Supreme Chancellor Saresh hired them to kill Empress Acina and the Outlander on Dromund Kaas (SWTOR). [note: undated: some believed they killed Kerred Santhe (*Darth Plagueis*); Tac-Spec Corporation claimed to have begun as a GenoHaradan front]. [GenoHaradan, Fate](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/GenoHaradan)

**Mistryl Shadow Guards**

- **Type:** all-female Human mercenary and bodyguard order
- **Era:** founded between 22 and 19 BBY; reorganised as a mercenary organisation 19–16 BBY; active to at least 19 ABY. The long history it tells of itself is part of its founding story [Ruled (Jared, 24 Nov 2026)].
- **Key facts:**
  - Sent from Emberlene to earn money for its devastated people; led by the Eleven Elders of the People; formed from the Silent Hand; once affiliated with the Confederacy (*Galaxy at War*; *Complete Star Wars Encyclopedia*). [Mistryl Shadow Guard](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mistryl_Shadow_Guard)
  - The Elders taught every citizen from childhood that Palpatine had destroyed Emberlene; in truth Emberlene had conquered its neighbours, who hired a mercenary army that wrecked it. The lie built loyalty and a false sense of duty. The Mistryl refuse Imperial work, though not always in practice. Loruna Scathe broke away to found the rival Azure Cabal. [Mistryl Shadow Guard, History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mistryl_Shadow_Guard)
  - **Structure and learning:** small teams under a Team Prime (in 0 BBY Manda D'ulin's team had six warriors and two heavy starfighters); several teams for larger jobs; master-and-apprentice pairs (Shada D'ukal travelled with the trainee Dunc T'racen); single guards hired out long-term as bodyguards. [Mistryl Shadow Guard, Organization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mistryl_Shadow_Guard)
  - **What they are taught:** especially strong in close combat; well trained in martial arts; extensive weapons training: hold-out blasters and vibroblades, and exotic weapons such as hand claws, shock whips and zenji needles; legendary stealth; a large database of citizen profiles lets a guard take on someone's identity. "The Mistryl shadow guards do not kill casually or without cause" (*Vision of the Future*). [Mistryl Shadow Guard, Equipment and methods](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mistryl_Shadow_Guard)
- **Requirements:** female, Human, of Emberlene.

**Noghri death commandos and bodyguards (Noghri death commandos; Noghri bodyguards)**

- **Era:** 19 BBY on.
- **Death commandos:** the first Noghri tradition formed after entering Imperial service; private commandos of the Emperor and Vader, lent to others such as Thrawn; deployed in teams on stealth missions: abduction, assassination, rescue of imprisoned officials; an agent handled their transport and logistics; based on Honoghr (*Heir to the Empire*). They went willingly to their deaths. [Noghri Death Commando](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Noghri_Death_Commando) · [Noghri/Legends, Divisions](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Noghri/Legends)
- **Bodyguards (New Republic era):** a new tradition after the Noghri learned Leia Organa Solo was Vader's daughter; two always near her. Warriors pass extensive training and testing to earn the honour; the first assignment is to guard a key person; experienced guards join elite units for strikes (for example scouting Ithor); the best serve the Skywalker and Solo families. Bribery or blackmail of a guard is the worst crime (*New Jedi Order Sourcebook*). Talon Karrde came up with hiring out individual Noghri as bodyguards. [Noghri/Legends, Divisions](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Noghri/Legends)
- Thrawn was killed by his Noghri bodyguard Rukh (*The Last Command*). [Noghri/Legends, Society and culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Noghri/Legends)

## Game notes (optional, suggestions only)

These are suggestions for the developer, not lore.

1. Styles could be grouped by how they are learned: culture-locked (Wrruushi for Wookiees, Echani for caste Echani, Stava for Noghri before 9 ABY, Iridonian art for Iridonian Zabraks), master-locked (Teräs Käsi needs a Follower of Palawa or one of their graduate masters; Matukai need an adept), institution-locked (K'tara via SpecForces; Hijkata via the Senate Guard; Echani via Royal Guard or Imperial Intelligence training) and Force-locked (Bakuuni Hand, Matukai, Sälãi Käsi).
2. Time costs that sources support: Wookiee hrrtayyk around age 12; Mandalorian *verd'goten* ages 8–13; Raithal one year; Royal Guard one year; Carida stormtrooper two years; Carida full course four years; Prefsbelt three years; flight school on Carida two years; clone regulars ten years of accelerated life; commandos close to ten years.
3. Gates and costs that sources support: Royal Guard training can kill the trainee (The Squall); commando training includes interrogation-resistance torture; Echani formal training may be limited to certain castes; Trandoshan capture wipes Jagannath standing; Mistryl recruits must be female Humans of Emberlene; the Followers can refuse a student.
4. Matchups the sources state: Teräs Käsi was built to fight Jedi; Echani reads and predicts opponents; K'thri is fast but flashy and sometimes inefficient; K'tara is quiet and close; the Sun Guard is matched only by Mandalorians; the Forbelean Defense intercepts and deflects.
5. Teräs Käsi damage and Matukai powers in the sources come from RPG rules; they are tagged [game-derived], never lore; the game can set its own numbers.
