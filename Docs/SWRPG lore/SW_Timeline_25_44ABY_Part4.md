# SWRPG Galaxy Timeline, 25–44 ABY (Part 4 of 4): Fate of the Jedi — Abeloth and the Lost Tribe of the Sith (41–44 ABY), with back sections

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 24, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends), as cited per entry. Rulings of 24 Nov 2026 applied (lore-rulings-2026-11-24c.md; D-484).

## Summary
Part 4 covers the years after the Second Galactic Civil War: the Lost Tribe of the Sith's emergence from Kesh (41–44 ABY), Natasi Daala's government and its clash with the Jedi, Luke Skywalker's exile and his hunt for Abeloth with his son Ben, the Jedi coup against Daala, Abeloth's seizure of the Alliance and the liberation of Coruscant, ending with the Imperial Election and the wedding of Jaina Solo and Jagged Fel in 44 ABY. *Crucible* (45 ABY) is out of scope. It then holds the back sections for the whole four-part set. Column layout, abbreviations and the calendar note are as in Part 1.

### Overview
After the war Natasi Daala, a former Imperial admiral, led the reunited Galactic Alliance as Chief of State; the Confederation stayed outside it and relations between the Alliance and the Jedi stayed tense [Second Galactic Civil War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Galactic_Civil_War) [Natasi Daala](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Natasi_Daala). Jagged Fel led the Imperial Remnant as Head of State; Tenel Ka's Hapes Consortium refused to rejoin the Alliance under Daala [Head of State (Imperial Remnant)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Head_of_State_(Imperial_Remnant)) [Tenel Ka Djo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tenel_Ka_Djo). Unknown to the galaxy, the Sith Meditation Sphere "Ship" had found the Lost Tribe of the Sith on Kesh, the descendants of Sith stranded there since 5000 BBY, and was helping them build a fleet [Lost Tribe of Sith emergence](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lost_Tribe_of_Sith_emergence). In 43 ABY Daala exiled Luke Skywalker for failing to prevent the damage of the last war and set Kenth Hamner as acting Grand Master, while young Jedi were driven mad by a remote "psychosis" that was the work of Abeloth, an ancient Force entity freed when Centerpoint was destroyed [43 ABY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/43_ABY) [Abeloth](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Abeloth). Luke and Ben followed the trail into the Maw; the Lost Tribe hunted them, then allied with them against Abeloth, then turned on them [Lost Tribe of Sith emergence](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lost_Tribe_of_Sith_emergence). In 44 ABY the Jedi deposed Daala and set up a Triumvirate; after the Jedi left Coruscant to chase Abeloth, she took the Chief of State's office in a senator's body, with Sith infiltrators in the Senate, until the Jedi liberated Coruscant and destroyed her [44 ABY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/44_ABY) [Triumvirate (Galactic Alliance)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Triumvirate_(Galactic_Alliance)). Darth Krayt of the One Sith briefly fought Abeloth alongside Luke [Abeloth](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Abeloth).

### Background: Abeloth's origin (for reference)
- Abeloth was once a mortal woman, "the Servant", who served the Ones (the Father, Son and Daughter) on a jungle world more than 100,000 years before the Battle of Yavin, and became "the Mother"; fearing to lose her family as she aged, she drank from the Font of Power and bathed in the Pool of Knowledge and was corrupted into an immortal entity [Abeloth](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Abeloth) (*Apocalypse*). [S-canon: the Ones are the Mortis family of *The Clone Wars* (2008); ruled usable under rule 4a; Ruled (Jared, 24 Nov 2026): this origin stands]
- The Son and Daughter used the Killiks to build Centerpoint Station and Sinkhole Station to imprison her in the Maw; per the Thuruht Killiks she escaped whenever the Force's Current was altered and was locked up again each time, until the Ones died during the Clone Wars after Anakin Skywalker refused to take the Father's place [Abeloth](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Abeloth) [S-canon for the Ones' death and Anakin's refusal]
- Her 100,000-year imprisonment is also stated in a StarWars.com article of 24 January 2014, cited on the same page [Abeloth](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Abeloth) (note on birth date)

The crisis (35 ABY) turned into the Swarm War (36 ABY), ended at the Battle of Tenupe with Lomi Plo's death; the same year Luke formally became Grand Master, the Jedi High Council was founded, Allana was born, and the Jedi left the Alliance's ruling council [Swarm War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Swarm_War) [36 ABY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/36_ABY).

**Confirmed from the old file** (now cited): Chewbacca's death at Sernpidal; the Mission to Myrkr's makeup, including Lomi Plo and Welk as Sith agents who betrayed the team, and its six dead; Fey'lya's bomb at Coruscant (over 25,000 Yuuzhan Vong killed); Ebaq as Ackbar's trap, with Vergere's death and Jaina killing Tsavong Lah; the Galactic Alliance founded on Mon Calamari (Dac) in 28 ABY with Cal Omas as first Chief of State; Shimrra killed by Luke and Onimi by Jacen; Raynar Thul as UnuThul; Jacen's five-year journey; the Solo–Niathal coup; Jaina's training with Boba Fett; Uroro Station in the Maad system with Jaina killing Caedus and Tahiri arrested; *Fate of the Jedi* in 43–44 ABY.
