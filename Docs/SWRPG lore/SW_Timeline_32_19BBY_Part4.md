# SWRPG Galaxy Timeline, 32–19 BBY (Part 4 of 4): The End of the Clone Wars, Order 66 and the New Order (19 BBY)

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 24, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends), current pages and pre-August-2008 revisions, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
Part 4 covers the last weeks of the Republic in 19 BBY (16:4–16:7 GrS and the rest of the year's first Imperial acts): the hunt for Darth Sidious, the Battle of Coruscant and Dooku's death, Utapau, the arrest attempt on Palpatine, Order 66 and Operation: Knightfall, Mustafar, Polis Massa, the Declaration of a New Order and the Empire's first measures. It then holds the TCW-versus-primary conflicts table, "Changes from the previous file", the Not found / Canon only / Not used lists, Decisions needed and Sources for all four parts. Order 66 here follows rule 4a: one of the Grand Army's 150 contingency orders, obeyed through conditioning and choice, with no inhibitor chip.

### Overview
In the war's last months the Jedi tried to find the Sith Lord behind the war, hoping to persuade Palpatine to give up powers that had grown far beyond the Emergency Powers Act through the Reflex Amendment and the Sector Governance Decree ([Clone Wars/Legends §The hunt for Darth Sidious](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clone_Wars/Legends)). Gunray's lost mechno-chair led them to the Works on Coruscant; Sidious answered with the strike on Coruscant through the Deep Core. Dooku died over Coruscant, Grievous on Utapau; Palpatine, exposed to Anakin as Sidious, survived Mace Windu's arrest attempt, turned Anakin, and issued Order 66. In two days the Jedi were nearly destroyed, the Separatist leaders killed, the droid army shut down and the Republic proclaimed an Empire.
