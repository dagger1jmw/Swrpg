# Star Wars Legends: Power Structures of the SWTOR Era (Sith Empire and Galactic Republic, c. 3681–3600 BBY), Part 2: Galactic Republic

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 21, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
This is Part 2 of 2: the Galactic Republic's power structure, 3681–c. 3627 BBY.

- **Government:** the Supreme Chancellor, elected by the Senate for four-year terms and removable by a no-confidence vote, represented the Senate in strategic military matters. The Supreme Commander advised on and oversaw military plans, and the SIS supplied intelligence to both the military and the Jedi. [Supreme Chancellor/Legends §Appointment of the Chancellor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Supreme_Chancellor/Legends); [Republic Military/Legends §Organization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Military/Legends); [Republic Strategic Information Service §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Strategic_Information_Service)
- **Chancellors:** six Chancellors span the era, from Berooken (killed 3653 BBY) to Galena Rans. Saresh (3640–3632 BBY) pushed executive power under emergency rule and kept control through the puppet Madon. [Berooken](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Berooken); [Leontyne Saresh §Eternal Empire's Conquests](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Leontyne_Saresh)
- **Jedi and government:** this part adds only new facts: the Senate overruling the High Council in 3653 BBY, Chancellors asking the Council directly for help, the Jedi delegation left in the Senate, and Saresh's clashes with Satele and Theron Shan. [Berooken §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Berooken); [Jedi Order/Legends §The Cold War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order/Legends); [Leontyne Saresh §Revanite Conspiracy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Leontyne_Saresh)
- **Cross-reference:** the Order's ranks, Councils, Grand Masters and wartime succession are in SW_Jedi_Ranks_Councils_Roles_AllEras_Part1.md and in the developer's SW_Jedi_Order_Ranks_Council_OldRepublic.md.

## Entries
### Supreme Chancellor and the Senate
- **Type:** office and institution
- **Era:** 3681–c. 3627 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Election and term:**
    - The Chancellor was elected by and from the Senate for four-year terms and could be removed at any time by a vote of no confidence. [Supreme Chancellor/Legends §Appointment of the Chancellor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Supreme_Chancellor/Legends)
    - In this era the "thousands of representatives" of the Senate elected the Chancellor. Elections in 3649 and 3645 BBY were "small affairs" because candidates feared Sith assassination. [Supreme Chancellor election, 3653 BBY §Election and aftermath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Supreme_Chancellor_election,_3653_BBY); [Supreme Chancellor election, 3649 BBY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Supreme_Chancellor_election,_3649_BBY); [Supreme Chancellor election, 3645 BBY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Supreme_Chancellor_election,_3645_BBY)
  - **Office holders:** see the offices table below.
  - **Senate powers shown in this era:**
    - The Senate overruled the Jedi High Council and accepted the Dark Council's peace offer (offer 3654 BBY; conference and Sacking 3653 BBY (lore batch 14)); see the Jedi entry below. [Berooken §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Berooken)
    - Although only a small delegation signed the Treaty of Coruscant, only a vote of the full Senate could overturn it. [Treaty of Coruscant §Terms](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Treaty_of_Coruscant)
    - The treaty made the Senate revoke the membership of several outlying worlds, including Bothawui and Rodia. [Treaty of Coruscant §A discontented Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Treaty_of_Coruscant)
    - The Senate shut down General Garza's rescue of Havoc Squad on Ando Prime, because the mission lacked its consent and risked breaking the treaty. [Havoc Squad §Defection](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Havoc_Squad)
    - It held inquiries into military units (Senator Grom Zian's inquiry into Havoc, a sham run for the Empire). [Havoc Squad §The new Havoc Squad](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Havoc_Squad); [Elin Garza §Turning the Tide](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Elin_Garza)
    - It held budget and oversight hearings on the SIS. [Marcus Trant §Director of SIS](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Marcus_Trant)
    - It overrode Chancellor Saresh and sued for peace with Zakuul (c. 3636 BBY). [Eternal Empire conquest §The war](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Empire_conquest)
  - **Saresh's executive turn:** under the post-conquest state of emergency (after c. 3636 BBY), Saresh "used her influence to sever the Senate's say in military manners" and pushed executive action. She discharged Admiral Bey'wan Aygo for protesting. [Leontyne Saresh §Eternal Empire's Conquests](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Leontyne_Saresh)
  - **Term limits:** Saresh tried to have the Senate extend her term indefinitely and failed, because the spymaster the Shroud blackmailed the Senate. She then installed Jebevel Madon as a puppet in 3632 BBY. [Leontyne Saresh §Eternal Empire's Conquests](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Leontyne_Saresh); [Jebevel Madon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jebevel_Madon)
  - **Jedi in the Senate:** Jedi who stayed on Coruscant after the move to Tython formed "a special Jedi delegation within the Senate". [Jedi Order/Legends §The Cold War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order/Legends)
- **Related:** Republic offices table; Jedi Order–government relations (this part)
- **Uncertainty:**
  - [conflict] Janarus's last year: "c. 3653—3641 BBY" and a "Chancery election, 3641 BBY" [Supreme Chancellor/Legends §Elections for Chancellor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Supreme_Chancellor/Legends); [Supreme Chancellor/Legends §Powers and functions of the Chancellorship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Supreme_Chancellor/Legends) versus an emergency election in 3640 BBY [Supreme Chancellor election, 3640 BBY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Supreme_Chancellor_election,_3640_BBY); [Leontyne Saresh §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Leontyne_Saresh).
  - Game-choice note: the *Bounty Hunter* story lets the player kill Janarus or spare him so that he retires. Wookieepedia follows the kill as the "implied canon choice". [Supreme Chancellor election, 3640 BBY §Behind the scenes](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Supreme_Chancellor_election,_3640_BBY)

### Chancellors' powers in practice (Janarus and Saresh)
- **Type:** office in practice
- **Era:** 3653–3632 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Janarus:**
    - He lost support over the cost of rebuilding. His "military advisers grew increasingly frustrated with his refusal to take sterner action to stamp out sedition". [Dorian Janarus §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dorian_Janarus)
    - He created the Republic Privateer Service Corps. [Dorian Janarus §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dorian_Janarus)
    - He asked the Jedi High Council for a diplomat to keep the Rift Alliance in the Republic. [Dorian Janarus §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dorian_Janarus)
    - His closest adviser was Jedi Battlemaster Jun Seros, who secretly set a bounty hunter up with false charges. [Dorian Janarus §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dorian_Janarus)
    - A commander with "Supreme Chancellor Dorian Janarus' complete confidence" could not be overruled even by Garza. [Elin Garza §Turning the Tide](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Elin_Garza)
  - **Saresh:**
    - She was the "Senatorial Supervisory Governor of Planetary Affairs" on Taris before her election. [Leontyne Saresh §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Leontyne_Saresh)
    - She promised "not peace, but victory". [Leontyne Saresh §Personality and traits](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Leontyne_Saresh)
    - She traded Imperial General Rakton for thousands of POWs. [Elin Garza §Battle of Corellia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Elin_Garza)
    - She promised reparations to the Rift Alliance. [Supreme Chancellor election, 3640 BBY §Election and aftermath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Supreme_Chancellor_election,_3640_BBY)
    - She fought Zakuul and the Sith Empire at once at the cost of millions of lives. [Eternal Empire conquest §The war](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Empire_conquest)
    - After her term she kept dictating policy through Madon. [Eternal Empire conquest §The war](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Empire_conquest)
    - In 3630 BBY she tried to seize the Alliance and plotted with Imperial Minister Lorman. [Leontyne Saresh §Attempted Coup](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Leontyne_Saresh)
- **Related:** Supreme Chancellor and the Senate
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Republic Military
- **Type:** institution
- **Era:** reorganized after the Jedi Civil War; active through 3681–c. 3627 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Chain of command:** all forces "ultimately answer to the Senate", which the Chancellor usually represents in strategic matters. The Supreme Commander, the highest-ranking officer, "advises on and oversees the execution of military plans". Orders flow down to Army generals and Navy admirals. [Republic Military/Legends §Organization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Military/Legends)
  - **Reforms after the Jedi Civil War:**
    - Ranks, uniforms and equipment were standardized and units were mixed.
    - Troops trained to fight with Jedi and also "without any Jedi at all", so as not to depend on them.

    [Republic Military/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Military/Legends)
  - **Duties:** also anti-piracy patrols, relief work and colonization support, such as the Taris resettlement. [Republic Military/Legends §Role](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Military/Legends)
  - **Branch rivalry:** the Navy was the most valued branch and demanded higher pay and the cooperation of ground forces; ground forces were paid less. [Republic Military/Legends §Branch Relations](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Military/Legends)
  - **Form of address:** superiors were addressed by rank or "sir" regardless of gender. [Republic Military/Legends §Proper Address](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Military/Legends)
  - **Army ranks in the Cold War:** Grand Marshal, General, Colonel, Major, Captain, Lieutenant, Ensign, Sergeant, Corporal, Specialist, Private. "Commander" was an honorific for respected veteran officers, not a rank. [Republic Army §Ranks](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Army)
  - **Army units:** fire team (2), squad (6–10), platoon (50–100), company (150–250), battalion (750–1,500), regiment (4,000–6,000), division (20,000–40,000), task force (40,000+). [Republic Army §Tactical organization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Army)
  - **Training:** basic training lasted 18 weeks, followed by up to 12 months of specialist training (Cold War). [conflict] Great War vs Cold War attestation; resolved (lore batch 17), ruling 5: used for the Great War window too, tagged [conflict]. [Republic Army §Specializations](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Army)
  - **Navy:**
    - It won the Republic's first major victory at Bothawui. [Republic Navy/Legends §The Sith Wars](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Navy/Legends)
    - The Jedi Master Oteg served as a Fleet Admiral and led the attack on Maelstrom Prison. [Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War) §Battling the Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_%28Post%E2%80%93Great_Hyperspace_War%29)
- **Related:** Supreme Commander; Special Forces; SIS
- **Uncertainty:** [unclear] The Republic Military/Legends page names a Minister and Ministry of Defense as leading the military, but the Ministry of Defense page says it was dissolved only at Ruusan. No Minister of Defense is named for 3681–3627 BBY. [Republic Military/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Military/Legends); [Ministry of Defense](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ministry_of_Defense)

### Supreme Commander, and General Garza's role
- **Type:** office
- **Era:** Great Galactic War to c. 3629 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Rans:** Supreme Commander during the Great Galactic War and the Cold War. He was "totally outplayed" by the Sacking of Coruscant, commanded at the Battle of Ilum and then retired, and the post passed to Jace Malcom. [Rans §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rans)
  - **Stantorrs** (Duros): Supreme Commander in the Cold War. In 3643 BBY the Imperial spy Ula Vii reported directly to him and he made her envoy to Nal Hutta. He was replaced by Rans. [Stantorrs §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stantorrs)
  - **Jace Malcom:**
    - He was promoted to Supreme Commander in 3640 BBY as successor to Rans. [Jace Malcom §Supreme Commander Malcom](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jace_Malcom)
    - He worked with Grand Master Satele Shan and with SIS Director Trant. His office was in the Senate Building. [Marcus Trant §Renewed war](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Marcus_Trant)
    - He sent forces to Iokath in 3629 BBY. [Jace Malcom §War on Iokath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jace_Malcom)
  - **Elin Garza:**
    - A Republic Army General who helped found and train the Special Forces Division and commanded it in the Great War and the Cold War. [Elin Garza](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Elin_Garza); [Republic Special Forces Division §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Special_Forces_Division)
    - She was not Supreme Commander. She directed Havoc Squad, promoted its commander (to Captain, then Major) and ordered secrecy "including non-disclosure to non-SpecForce personnel". [Elin Garza §Cold War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Elin_Garza); [Elin Garza §Second Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Elin_Garza)
    - She was suspected of authorizing strikes on Imperial targets in breach of the treaty (unproven). [Treaty of Coruscant §A discontented Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Treaty_of_Coruscant)
    - After the Eclipse Squad affair she expected the Senate to strip her of her uniform and imprison her. [Elin Garza §Last days of service](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Elin_Garza)
- **Related:** Republic Military; Special Forces
- **Uncertainty:** [conflict] Succession of Supreme Commanders:

  - "Stantorrs, replaced by Rans" [Stantorrs](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stantorrs).

### Republic Special Forces and Havoc Squad
- **Type:** unit
- **Era:** Great Galactic War to c. 3631 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Recruitment:** the best troopers were drawn from the Republic Army to defend the Republic in the Great Galactic War and the Cold War. [Republic Special Forces Division](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Special_Forces_Division)
  - **Origin:** after the treaty the military recruited elite troopers into new Special Forces squads, as shock troops in case the peace failed. [conflict] Resolved (lore batch 17, ruling 3): Special Forces were founded during the Great War (Garza a founder; Omas, Kaedan's team, Tavus); this post-Treaty origin is the minority version. [Treaty of Coruscant §A discontented Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Treaty_of_Coruscant)
  - **Havoc Squad:** created as part of the new special forces "following the departure of the Jedi from Coruscant for the planet Tython". [conflict] More sources make it a Great War unit: Infantry Squad 326, named "Havoc" under Malcom; Jek Kardan a founding member; Harron Tavus joining in 3667 BBY; Aryn Leneer and Zeerid Korr with it early in the war (SW_Republic_Military_GGW_Part1). Resolved (lore batch 17), ruling 2: a Great War unit; the post-Tython origin is the minority version (consistent with D-363 [12]). [Havoc Squad §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Havoc_Squad)
  - **Jedi partners:** some units worked with Jedi; Lieutenant Harron Tavus often fought alongside Master Orgus Din. In parts of the galaxy the Special Forces "constituted the only military available". [Republic Special Forces Division §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Special_Forces_Division)
  - **Defection:** by 3643 BBY Tavus led hundreds of Special Forces operatives to defect to the Empire. [Republic Special Forces Division §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Special_Forces_Division)
  - **Front-line duty:** Supreme Commander Malcom later moved Havoc from covert work to the front lines. [Elin Garza §Last days of service](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Elin_Garza)
  - **Rogue mission:** Havoc ran a rogue operation on Zakuul after c. 3631 BBY. [Eternal Alliance §Zakuul listening post](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Alliance)
- **Related:** Supreme Commander; Elin Garza
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Strategic Information Service (SIS)
- **Type:** institution (intelligence agency)
- **Era:** before 3681 BBY (as part of the Senate Library) to after 3631 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Origins:** the Senate founded it inside the Senate Library for monitoring and decryption. In the war the Senate moved it out of the Library and widened its role to intelligence for the military and the Jedi Order. By the Cold War it covered networks, counter-intelligence, foreign and military intelligence, and support to local police. [Republic Strategic Information Service §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Strategic_Information_Service); [Republic Strategic Information Service §Early operations](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Strategic_Information_Service)
  - **Structure:**
    - Headquarters: the Heorem Complex on Coruscant.
    - The Director, with special-assignment agents answering to him directly.
    - Regional directors, such as Core World Operations and Mid Rim Operations.
    - Station chiefs for sectors.
    - Bureau chiefs on planets.
    - Special Agents and agents; also Senior Agents and military-style ranks.
    - All agents learned Huttese.

    [Republic Strategic Information Service §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Strategic_Information_Service)
  - **Great War operations (added (lore batch 17)):** in 3661 BBY the SIS abducted the Cathar prince Shange and held him in stasis on Belsavis; its Outer Rim listening posts led to the capture of the Dread Masters (3668 BBY).
  - **Limits:** it had "little oversight" but stayed within its legal limits while looking for loopholes. [Republic Strategic Information Service §Early operations](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Strategic_Information_Service)
  - **Directors:** an unidentified Director (to 3650 BBY), then Marcus Trant (from 3650 BBY). [Director of Republic Strategic Information Service](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Director_of_Republic_Strategic_Information_Service); [Marcus Trant §Director of SIS](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Marcus_Trant)
  - **Trant:** he refused to give the Senate operational details. [Marcus Trant §Director of SIS](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Marcus_Trant)
  - **Relationship with the Jedi:**
    - Trant "encouraged SIS agents to work hand-in-hand with the Jedi". [Marcus Trant §Director of SIS](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Marcus_Trant)
    - A joint Jedi–SIS committee confirmed the Emperor's apparent death. [Leontyne Saresh §War with the Hutt Cartel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Leontyne_Saresh)
    - The Sixth Line were "Jedi commandos working for the SIS". When Theron Shan sent them to Ziost without authorization, Saresh and Grand Master Satele Shan put him on leave. [Leontyne Saresh §Invasion of Ziost](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Leontyne_Saresh)
  - **Later:** the SIS was eventually replaced by the Senate Bureau of Intelligence (no date). [Republic Strategic Information Service §Later history](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Strategic_Information_Service)
- **Related:** Imperial Intelligence (Part 1)
- **Uncertainty:** [conflict] The year Trant met Elin Garza on Bomodon: 3674 BBY [Marcus Trant §Early career](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Marcus_Trant) versus 3673 BBY [Elin Garza §Great Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Elin_Garza).

### Jedi Order and the Republic government, 3681–c. 3627 BBY (new facts only)
- **Type:** relationship
- **Era:** 3681–c. 3627 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **The peace offer (offer 3654 BBY; conference and Sacking 3653 BBY (lore batch 14)):**
    - The High Council advised the Senate and the Chancellor to ignore the Dark Council's peace offer.
    - The Senate "overruled the Jedi", but the Council persuaded Berooken to stay on Coruscant during the talks.

    [Berooken §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Berooken)
  - **After the treaty:**
    - The Republic "criticized the Jedi for failing to defend them". [Jedi Order/Legends §Relations with the Galactic Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order/Legends)
    - The interim Chancellor Am-Ris told Master Zym that the Temple could not be rebuilt for lack of resources. [Paran Am-Ris](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Paran_Am-Ris)
  - **Move to Tython:**
    - Satele Shan reopened the hyperlane to Tython. The High Council moved its headquarters there and built a new Temple, and Tython became the Order's "fortress world" in the Cold War. [Tython/Legends §Rediscovery](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tython/Legends); [Jedi Temple (Tython)/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Temple_%28Tython%29/Legends)
    - The Sith attacked Tython in 3637 BBY. [Tython/Legends §Rediscovery](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tython/Legends)
  - **Who asked the Jedi for help:**
    - Chancellors asked the Council directly: Janarus requested a diplomat for the Rift Alliance, and the Barsen'thor was sent. [Dorian Janarus §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dorian_Janarus)
    - Jedi also held military posts (Fleet Admiral Oteg) and political ones (Jun Seros as Janarus's closest aide). [Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War) §Battling the Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_%28Post%E2%80%93Great_Hyperspace_War%29); [Dorian Janarus §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dorian_Janarus)
  - **Tensions with Saresh:**
    - She objected to Satele Shan's fleet allying with Darth Marr against the Revanites without telling her, and demanded to be told when "her fleet" was back in position. [Leontyne Saresh §Revanite Conspiracy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Leontyne_Saresh)
    - She disciplined Theron Shan for the SIS Jedi mission to Ziost. [Leontyne Saresh §Invasion of Ziost](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Leontyne_Saresh)
    - She would not consider any alliance with the Sith against Zakuul. [Eternal Empire conquest §The war](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Empire_conquest)
  - **After the conquest:** a Jedi colony hid on Ossus. By 3627 BBY the Republic formed Task Force Nova to reunite the Order, which chose a new homeworld instead of Tython. [Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War) §Third Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_%28Post%E2%80%93Great_Hyperspace_War%29); [Tython/Legends §Rediscovery](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tython/Legends)
- **Related:** SW_Jedi_Ranks_Councils_Roles_AllEras_Part1.md, 3600–1000 BBY entry (Grand Masters Zym and Satele Shan, wartime succession protocols); SW_Jedi_Order_Ranks_Council_OldRepublic.md (the developer's file)
- **Uncertainty:** [unclear] One source says the Order eventually returned to Coruscant and rebuilt the Temple with Republic aid after a Republic victory, but gives no date. [Jedi Order/Legends §Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order/Legends)
