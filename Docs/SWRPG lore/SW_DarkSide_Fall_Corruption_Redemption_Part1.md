# Dark Side: Fall, Corruption and Redemption, Part 1 of 3 (Old Republic, to 1000 BBY)

## Summary
This part covers the dark side's pull, falls, redemptions, corruption, power, madness, places and gray paths from the Old Republic up to 1000 BBY. Its core model is Tolaris Shim's Four Stages, built on Ulic Qel-Droma's fall, and its main cases are Ulic, Exar Kun, Revan, Malak, Bastila, Nihilus, Atris and Sion. Part 2 covers 1000 BBY–4 ABY and Part 3 covers 4 ABY onward. Each part has all eight sections, limited to its eras. The main open conflicts are whether Revan's redemption was complete and whether Korriban's pull was strong or faded.

**Era boundaries:** Part 1 runs to 1000 BBY (the Ruusan Reformation and the start of the Rule of Two). Part 2 runs from 1000 BBY to 4 ABY (Endor). Part 3 runs from 4 ABY to the Legacy era.

## Section 1: How falling works (Old Republic, to 1000 BBY)

### Tolaris Shim's Four Stages of falling
- **Type:** in-universe theory of how a Jedi falls
- **Era:** written after the Great Sith War (after 3996 BBY), using Ulic Qel-Droma's fall as its model
- **Key facts:**
  - Jedi Master Tolaris Shim's thesis became the standard tool Jedi Masters used to spot corruption (ref: *Jedi vs. Sith: The Essential Guide to the Force*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("The Four Stages of the dark side")
  - **Stage 1, Temptation:** rushing to help friends, facing obvious perils, or ending disputes quickly are "acts of haste". Each becomes a step onto the dark path if the Jedi acts first and thinks later. Exar Kun's case begins with tales heard from his master, Vodo-Siosk Baas. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("Stage 1: Temptation")
  - **Stage 2, Imperilment:** the delusion that the dark side is a usable "shortcut" to good ends. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("Stage 2: Imperilment")
  - **Stage 3, Submission:** the end justifies the means and the fall is nearly complete. Some wake up at this stage (Atris). A Jedi who has fully submitted, like Kun, must be brought back or eliminated. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("Stage 3: Submission")
  - **Stage 4, Redemption or Atonement:** a slightly tainted Jedi can atone through meditation, reflection and absolution. A true Dark Jedi is beyond atonement and can be redeemed only by a selfless, heroic act done without the dark side, for example Ulic helping the Jedi defeat Kun (ref: *The Sith War* 6). A Dark Jedi unwilling to give up the powers cannot be saved. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("Stage 4: Redemption/Atonement")
- **Related:** Ulic Qel-Droma (Section 2); Part 2 and Part 3 for the later cases the stages are applied to
- **Uncertainty:** [conflict] Shim's holorecording says a Jedi who kills innocents under the dark side can never be redeemed. The page itself notes that Anakin's redemption contradicts this. See Part 2, Section 3.

### Single act or pattern?
- **Type:** synthesis of the Old Republic sources
- **Era:** 4000–1000 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Pattern view:** Shim's stages describe a progression, and the dark side's physical marks also "progress with time and immersion" rather than appearing at once. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("The Four Stages of the dark side"; "Corruption of the dark side")
  - **Single-moment view:** individual acts can be turning points. Ulic's anger as he killed Satal Keto "activated" the Sith poison, and Kun's Sith amulet multiplied his anger "a hundredfold" when the light side failed him (Section 2). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ulic_Qel-Droma/Legends) ("Path of no return"); [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Exar_Kun/Legends) ("Seizing power")
  - **Places that speed the fall:** being near a wound in the Force can cause a "rapid fall to the dark side or worse". Revan used wounds such as Malachor V to break Jedi and build his army quickly (ref: *KotOR II*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("Wounds in the Force")
- **Related:** Section 7
- **Uncertainty:** "Pattern with decisive moments" is my own summary of the two views [inferred].

### Addiction and acceptance
- **Type:** concept
- **Era:** Old Republic and New Sith Wars sources
- **Key facts:**
  - The page describes the dark side as acting "like a strong, addictive narcotic" on both the mind and the appearance. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("Corruption of the dark side")
  - Revan's teachings, as Bane read them, say a Sith must accept these changes (ref: *Darth Bane: Path of Destruction*). Bane later concluded that flesh cannot channel the power indefinitely (ref: *Darth Bane: Dynasty of Evil*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("Corruption of the dark side")
  - Nihilus shows the addiction model at its extreme: each time he indulged his hunger, the dark side consumed more of him (ref: *Sith Triumvirate*; *KotOR Campaign Guide*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Nihilus) ("Origins"; "Sith Triumvirate")
- **Related:** Section 4, Section 6
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Juyo and the New Sith Wars
- **Type:** combat discipline as a route to falling
- **Era:** New Sith Wars (c. 2000–1000 BBY)
- **Key facts:**
  - Juyo was controversial because it broke the "no emotion" tenet; Sidious later called it a "Sith style". During the New Sith Wars it led many Jedi to fall, so the Council restricted it. Skarch Vaunk taught it only to a few hand-picked students a year (ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VII/Legends) ("Juyo"; "Practitioners")
- **Related:** Part 2, Section 5 (Vaapad); see also SW_Lightsabers_Crystals_Materials_AllEras.md
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Not found (Section 1, Part 1)
- No Old Republic source gives a set number of dark acts that makes a fall final.
- Yoda's "once you start down the dark path" line is not quoted on any Legends page checked (Dark side of the Force/Legends, Yoda/Legends, Luke Skywalker/Legends). Shim's stages carry the same idea.

## Section 2: Case studies of falls (Old Republic, to 1000 BBY)

Each case gives dates, the deeds in order, time to fall, outside pressure, and whether the person came back. Background on training and ranks is in SW_Sith_Training_AllEras_Part1.md (Era 2 for Kun's Brotherhood, Era 4 for Revan, Malak and the Triumvirate). It is not repeated here.

### Ulic Qel-Droma
- **Type:** fall and redemption case
- **Era:** 4000–3986 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Before the fall:** Ulic was pure of heart and confident, but headstrong, impulsive and impressionable. His Master Arca Jeth said courage often clouded his mind (ref: *Dark Empire* endnotes; *Tales of the Jedi* audio drama). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ulic_Qel-Droma/Legends) ("Jedi Knight")
  - **Step 1, the plan:** he blamed himself for Arca Jeth's death at Deneba and vowed to infiltrate the Krath, "destroy it from within, and conquer the dark side". The Jedi assembly reacted with horror and disapproval. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ulic_Qel-Droma/Legends) ("The death of a mentor")
  - **Step 2, inside the Krath:** he posed as a fallen Jedi. Satal Keto injected him with a Sith poison he could survive only by countering it with the Force. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ulic_Qel-Droma/Legends) ("Path of no return")
  - **Step 3, the trigger:** in a wave of anger he dueled and killed Keto. The dying Keto taunted that his anger had activated the poison. Ulic then threw the other Jedi down with dark-side energy. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ulic_Qel-Droma/Legends) ("Path of no return")
  - **Step 4, denial:** he insisted he was not under the dark side and was still serving Arca Jeth's mission. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ulic_Qel-Droma/Legends) ("Destiny fulfilled")
  - **Step 5, outside pressure:** he met Exar Kun. Their Sith amulets stopped the duel, and Marka Ragnos's spirit marked him as Kun's Shadow Hand (see Sith all-eras Part1, Era 2). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ulic_Qel-Droma/Legends) ("Destiny fulfilled")
  - **As a Sith Lord:** he led militaristic campaigns and spread death "at a genocidal level", yet never renounced his title of Jedi Knight (ref: *Dark Empire* endnotes; *Dark Empire Sourcebook*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ulic_Qel-Droma/Legends) ("Sith Lord")
  - **Time to fall:** months, within a single campaign. The infiltration plan and Kun's arrival fall in c. 3997 BBY, the year the Exar Kun page gives for Kun's journey (ref: *New Essential Chronology*) [inferred]. He led the Mandalorian army by 3996 BBY. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ulic_Qel-Droma/Legends) ("Duel with the Mandalore"); [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Exar_Kun/Legends) ("Training")
  - **Turning point:** at Ossus he killed his brother Cay and was "overcome by remorse and sorrow". Nomi Sunrider then cut him off from the Force with a wall of light, which she immediately regretted (ref: *The Sith War* 5). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ulic_Qel-Droma/Legends) ("Endgame")
  - **Redemption:** he led the Jedi to Kun on Yavin 4 and taught them how to bring down the Dark Reaper. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ulic_Qel-Droma/Legends) ("Endgame")
  - **Cost:** he spent about a decade wandering, cut off from the Force, regretful, at one point wishing to die (ref: *Redemption* 4). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ulic_Qel-Droma/Legends) ("A changed man")
  - **Taught again:** from 3986 BBY he lived as a hermit on Rhen Var and trained Vima Sunrider. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ulic_Qel-Droma/Legends) ("Self-exile")
  - **Death:** he refused to fight Sylvar and was shot in the back by the spacer Hoggon. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ulic_Qel-Droma/Legends) ("Death")
  - **Remembered:** in 3980 BBY Vima recorded the story of his redemption in the Great Holocron. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ulic_Qel-Droma/Legends) ("Legacy")
- **Related:** the Four Stages thesis (Section 1) was based on his fall
- **Uncertainty:** the exact year of the fall [inferred]

### Exar Kun
- **Type:** fall case (no redemption)
- **Era:** 3997–3996 BBY; spirit destroyed 11 ABY (infobox: NEC; Champions of the Force)
- **Key facts:**
  - **Temptation:** in 3997 BBY, Kun snuck into Master Vodo-Siosk Baas's hut and used his holocron to learn about the dark side (ref: *Dark Lords of the Sith* 1; *New Essential Chronology*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Exar_Kun/Legends) ("Training"). Tolaris Shim used him as the example of a Knight seduced by "unrestrained power". [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("Stage 1: Temptation")
  - **Forced surrender:** in the Valley of the Dark Lords on Korriban he was attacked by corpses animated by the dark side. He accepted Freedon Nadd's offer, was healed, and could suddenly read Sith script. He still believed he had not been seduced. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Exar_Kun/Legends) ("Surrender to darkness")
  - **Amulet:** on Yavin 4, with his light side blocked, a Sith amulet multiplied his anger "a hundredfold" and showed him a vision of himself as the Massassi legends' Dark Lord (ref: *Dark Lords of the Sith* 4–5). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Exar_Kun/Legends) ("Seizing power")
  - **Choice:** he chose to pursue dark-side knowledge but "was surprised" by what that choice involved (ref: *Dark Lords of the Sith* 2). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Exar_Kun/Legends) ("Personality and traits")
  - **Time to fall:** within 3997 BBY, a matter of weeks or months from his first visit to Korriban to his crowning as Dark Lord [inferred]. He was waging war by 3996 BBY. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Exar_Kun/Legends) ("Training"; "Preparations")
  - **No return:** in 3996 BBY he had the Massassi sacrificed by the thousands so his spirit could escape (ref: *The Sith War* 6). His spirit stayed bound to Yavin 4. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Exar_Kun/Legends) ("Downfall")
  - **Legacy:** his spirit later reached Gantoris in dreams (ref: *Jedi Search*), corrupted Kyp Durron, and attacked Luke's spirit (ref: *Dark Apprentice*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Exar_Kun/Legends) ("Reawakenings"; "Final banishment")
- **Related:** Sith all-eras Part1, Era 2 (Kun's Brotherhood)
- **Uncertainty:** the fall's duration [inferred]

### Revan
- **Type:** fall and redemption case
- **Era:** c. 3961–3956 BBY; captivity and split until 3637 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Step 1, the war:** over the Mandalorian Wars, Revan grew "more and more cold and calculating", taking on his enemies' militant mindset in order to win. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Revan/Legends) ("Warrior and Sith")
  - **Step 2, a dark-side site (c. 3961 BBY):** he found the Trayus Academy on Malachor V and "began to be seduced by the dark side" through its Sith knowledge. He also introduced Malak to the dark side (ref: *KotOR Campaign Guide*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Revan/Legends) ("Fighting the Mandalorians")
  - **Step 3, still a Jedi in name:** although "under the thrall of the dark side", he did not renounce the Jedi ways at first and kept fighting the Mandalorians. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Revan/Legends) ("Showdown")
  - **Step 4, mass death:** in 3960 BBY the Mass Shadow Generator shattered Malachor V. The devastation disgusted him, and he built HK-47 for more surgical killing. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Revan/Legends) ("Showdown"; "Warrior and Sith")
  - **Step 5, outside pressure, Vitiate:** following the Sith trail to Dromund Kaas, he and Malak were dominated by the Emperor, who completed "their fall to the dark side" (ref: *The Old Republic: Revan*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Revan/Legends) ("Searching for answers")
  - **After breaking free:** they broke the Emperor's control but kept his commands, believing them to be their own desires. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Revan/Legends) ("Dark Lord of the Sith"; "Warrior and Sith")
  - **Korriban:** on Korriban he went deeper into the dark side while plundering Tulak Hord's tomb (ref: *New Essential Chronology*; *SWTOR Encyclopedia*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Revan/Legends) ("Dark Lord of the Sith")
  - **Time to fall:** about one to two years from Trayus (c. 3961 BBY) to the Emperor's domination after Malachor (3960 BBY), followed by war on the Republic by 3959 BBY [inferred from the dates above].
  - **Redemption, part 1 (3956 BBY):** after Bastila captured him, the Jedi Council wiped his mind and gave him a new identity. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Revan/Legends) ("Clash with Bastila Shan")
  - **Kreia's view:** the wipe did not change his personality. It erased the effects of the war and the dark side, letting his original self return. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Revan/Legends) ("Reborn")
  - **Redemption, part 2:** on Lehon he rejected Bastila's offer to rule as Sith, "the choice of a true Jedi". [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bastila_Shan) ("Malak's final apprentice")
  - **With Malak:** he apologized for starting Malak on the Sith path, but reminded him that he had chosen to stay on it. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Revan/Legends) ("A final confrontation")
  - **Drawing on emotion:** through his bond with Bastila he drew on love and happiness to reach the light side (ref: *The Old Republic: Revan*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Revan/Legends) ("Reborn")
  - **Did the dark side keep a hold? Yes.** During centuries of torture by the Emperor and the Dread Masters, he split his personality. The dark part, having embraced darkness to escape the pain, was left "insane and fully submerged in the dark side". It led the Revanites until coalition forces defeated it on Yavin 4 (SWTOR, *Shadow of Revan*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Revan/Legends) ("Light and dark"; "Freedom and fall")
  - **Appearance:** light skin and brown eyes. No physical corruption is recorded on the page. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Revan/Legends) ("Appearance and early life")
- **Related:** Sith all-eras Part1, Era 4
- **Uncertainty:** the fall's duration [inferred]

### Malak
- **Type:** fall case (no redemption)
- **Era:** c. 3961–3956 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Pressure:** Revan introduced him to the dark side at Trayus, and the Emperor dominated both of them. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Revan/Legends) ("Fighting the Mandalorians"; "Searching for answers")
  - **Corruption:** his eyes changed colour and he became slightly pale, with no other effects (ref: *KotOR*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("Corruption of the dark side")
  - **Training and the jaw duel:** see Sith all-eras Part1, Era 4.
  - **No return:** when he asked what would have happened had the Jedi captured him instead, Revan answered that Malak had chosen to continue. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Revan/Legends) ("A final confrontation")
- **Related:** Revan
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Bastila Shan
- **Type:** fall and redemption case
- **Era:** 3956 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Weaknesses:** she was brash, impulsive and overconfident because of her battle meditation, and kept trying to prove herself against the Sith despite warnings (ref: Databank). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bastila_Shan) ("Personality and traits")
  - **Self-doubt:** watching Revan stay on the light path apparently without effort made her doubt her own devotion, and this "played no small part" in her fall. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bastila_Shan) ("Personality and traits")
  - **Torture:** aboard the *Leviathan*, Saul Karath tortured her to break Revan. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bastila_Shan) ("Malak's final apprentice")
  - **Fall:** after Malak captured her, she "fell to the dark side after a week of torture". [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bastila_Shan) (intro, no inline footnote)
  - **Time to fall:** about one week under Malak's torture [unclear; from the intro].
  - **Redemption:** on the Star Forge, beaten three times, she asked to be killed. Revan refused, told her to draw strength from their Force bond, and confessed his love. She felt "too much anger, hatred and fear" to find peace on her own. She atoned by using battle meditation for the Republic (ref: *KotOR*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bastila_Shan) ("Return to the light")
  - **Time to redemption:** within a single battle [inferred].
  - **Afterwards:** late in life, her mind was recorded in the Noetikon of Secrets as a Master who had known both sides of the Force. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bastila_Shan) ("Legacy")
- **Related:** Revan
- **Uncertainty:** the "week" comes from the page intro without a footnote [unclear]

### Darth Nihilus
- **Type:** fall case (no redemption)
- **Era:** 3960–3951 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Trigger (3960 BBY):** he survived the Battle of Malachor V. His grief turned into a dark persona he used to survive, and his emotional pain became an emptiness and then an intense "hunger". Without meaning to, he drained another survivor (ref: *KotOR II*; *KotOR Campaign Guide*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Nihilus) ("Origins")
  - **Outside pressure:** Darth Traya sensed him as a wound in the Force, told him the Force fed his hunger, and trained him to devour worlds (ref: *KotOR Campaign Guide*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Nihilus) ("Sith Triumvirate")
  - **Deeds:** each time he indulged, the dark side consumed more of him. He wiped out all life on Katarr in 3952 BBY. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Nihilus) ("Sith Triumvirate"; intro; "Encounter with Visas Marr")
  - **Time to fall:** immediate in the aftermath of Malachor [inferred]. The full transformation took years.
  - **No return:** Meetra Surik killed him before going on to Malachor V. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Malachor_V) ("Final destruction"). His body dissolved (ref: *KotOR II*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("Death of dark side users")
- **Related:** Sections 4 and 6
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Atris
- **Type:** fall and restoration case
- **Era:** c. 3951 BBY (*KotOR II*)
- **Key facts:**
  - **Pressure:** the Sith holocrons she kept. She "succumbed to the dark side influence" of them. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("Stage 3: Submission")
  - **Return:** she was restored to the light after a fierce confrontation with the Jedi Exile. Shim's theory uses her to show that a Jedi in the third stage is not beyond redemption (ref: *KotOR II*; *Jedi vs. Sith*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("Stage 3: Submission")
- **Related:** Section 7 (holocrons)
- **Uncertainty:** how long the holocrons took to work on her is not stated

### Nayama, Jolee Bindo's wife
- **Type:** fall case (no redemption)
- **Era:** Great Sith War (c. 3996 BBY)
- **Key facts:**
  - Jolee married Nayama and trained her against the Council's advice. During the war with Exar Kun she fell to the dark side "like many other Jedi" and tried to turn Jolee. He beat her in a duel but could not kill her, and she went on to kill many Jedi before she died in battle (ref: *KotOR*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jolee_Bindo) ("Life as a Padawan")
- **Related:** Jolee Bindo (Section 8)
- **Uncertainty:** her time to fall is not stated

### Darth Sion
- **Type:** fall case, with a release at death
- **Era:** before the Great Sith War to 3951 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - He made his pain his identity and built up tolerance for it. Struck down, he rose again on anger and hate and found he could hold his decomposing body together with the dark side (ref: *KotOR Campaign Guide*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Sion) ("Rise of a Sith Lord")
  - **End:** Meetra Surik showed him the price of his immortality and persuaded him to let go. He "found peace and finally allowed himself to die". [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Sion) (intro)
- **Related:** Section 4
- **Uncertainty:** his first dark act and the date of his turn are not given

## Section 4: Physical corruption (Old Republic, to 1000 BBY)

### General description (applies to all eras)
- **Type:** concept
- **Era:** all; the main sources are *KotOR II* and *Revenge of the Sith*
- **Key facts:**

## Section 5: The dark side as a power source (Old Republic, to 1000 BBY)

### Gains and costs, Old Republic examples
- **Type:** examples
- **Era:** 3997–3951 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **General rule:** the Jedi view is that the dark side is a shortcut to power but also to suffering. Feats such as Katarr's destruction show the scale, but "these powers came with a price" (ref: *KotOR II*; Wizards "Power Beyond Belief"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("Strength of the dark side")
  - **Exar Kun:** when the Massassi attacked and the light side failed him, Sadow's amulet multiplied his anger "a hundredfold" and he took control (ref: *Dark Lords of the Sith* 4–5). His final act of power, sacrificing thousands of Massassi to free his spirit, left him bound to Yavin 4 for four thousand years (ref: *The Sith War* 6). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Exar_Kun/Legends) ("Seizing power"; "Downfall")
  - **Nihilus:** his hunger let him drain whole worlds, but each use consumed more of him until only his spirit in the armour remained. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Nihilus) ("Sith Triumvirate")
  - **Sion:** his hate gave him immortality "at the cost of all-consuming agony". [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Sion) (intro)
  - **Malak:** the dark side's weakness shows in hubris. Malak lost to Revan despite drawing on the Star Forge's power (ref: *KotOR*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("Strength of the dark side")
  - **Juyo:** the Jedi restricted the form after it led many Jedi to fall in the New Sith Wars (Section 1). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VII/Legends) ("Practitioners")
- **Related:** Part 2, Section 5 (Vaapad, Mustafar)
- **Uncertainty:** none

## Section 6: Madness (Old Republic, to 1000 BBY)

### Old Republic cases
- **Type:** examples
- **Era:** 3996–c. 3640 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Exar Kun's spirit:** millennia of isolation on Yavin 4 left it desperate (ref: *Champions of the Force*) and "half-mad" (ref: *New Essential Guide to Characters*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Exar_Kun/Legends) ("Personality and traits")
  - **Darth Nihilus:** less madness than an all-consuming hunger. Even at Telos, where an attack made no sense, the hunger drove him on (ref: *KotOR II*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Nihilus) ("Assault on Telos IV")
  - **Colonel Tobin:** a non-Force-sensitive officer who went mad and took on a dark-sider's look from being near Nihilus (ref: *KotOR II*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("Corruption of the dark side")
  - **Revan's dark half:** split off under torture and left "insane and fully submerged in the dark side" (SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Revan/Legends) ("Light and dark")
  - **Yuon Par:** a Jedi Master struck by "a maddening dark side illness" created by the Sith Lord Terrak Morrhage. Bastila's recording in the Noetikon of Secrets helped the Barsen'thor save her (SWTOR). This is the closest Old Republic parallel to a named dark-side mental illness. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bastila_Shan) ("Legacy")

## Game notes (suggestions only; not lore)
- **Fall speed (suggestion):** Old Republic cases cluster at months (Ulic, Kun) to a year or two (Revan). A week is possible under torture (Bastila), and a fall can be immediate where a wound in the Force is involved (Nihilus). A campaign could treat "months" as the default and let dark sites or torture compress it.
- **Deeds that matter most (suggestion):** acts done in anger that kill (Ulic and Keto), taking up a Sith artifact (Kun's amulet), and mass killing (Malachor). These could weigh more than using a dark power in a calm moment.
- **Staging corruption (suggestion):** use the general symptom list (eyes, then pallor and veins, then ageing and voice) as three or four stages tied to how long and how deeply a character is immersed. Leave room for Ulic, Kun and Revan, whom the sources show unmarked, so staging could be optional or slow for strong-willed characters.
- **Redemption (suggestion):** follow Shim's Stage 4. Minor taint can be atoned for by reflection, but a true fall needs a selfless act without the dark side, and Revan's dark half shows a lasting "hold" mechanic.
- **Places (suggestion):** Malachor-type wounds could speed falls sharply; Korriban's strength could vary by era (strong in KotOR II, faded by Bane's time).
