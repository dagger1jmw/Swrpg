### F. Layout: the Academy, the Valley and approaches
Layout rule applied: everything below is on record. Invented layout (none in this section) would be tagged `invented` and appear only under Game notes. Places attested only in other eras are flagged in the Era attested column.

**The Academy as described (SWTOR, Cold War):**
- A pyramid of stone and durasteel at the rear of the Valley of the Dark Lords, towering over the tombs [Sith Academy (Korriban) §Layout](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Academy_%28Korriban%29); [Valley of the Dark Lords/Legends §Layout](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Valley_of_the_Dark_Lords/Legends). It was erected in front of the Great Temple complex [Great Temple (Korriban) §Sith Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Temple_%28Korriban%29).
- The entrance sits at the top of a wide staircase flanked by two statues of kneeling slaves. Inside, a main hall with twin staircases holds a narrow stone monument carved with Sith faces "crushing those who vie" for power [Sith Academy (Korriban) §Layout](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Academy_%28Korriban%29).
- Lower levels hold jails, study and lecture rooms, meditation and training chambers, and dormitories [Sith Academy (Korriban) §Layout](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Academy_%28Korriban%29).
- Off the main hall is the Sith Library, lit violet and watched by armed guards. It holds the teachings of Darth Vilus, the Iratus holocron, armour and tablets [Sith Academy (Korriban) §Layout](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Academy_%28Korriban%29). A second-floor library is where the "unauthorized murder" took place [Alif](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Alif).
- Secret Ritual Chambers lie beneath (Bloodfiend; altar of skulls) [Ritual Chambers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ritual_Chambers); [Sith Academy (Korriban) §Layout](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Academy_%28Korriban%29).
- Near the summit are rooms restricted to the highest Sith, including the Dark Council chambers. Rumour places a seldom-used throne room of the Emperor deep inside [Sith Academy (Korriban) §Layout](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Academy_%28Korriban%29).
- In its depths is the machinery for the Emperor's Children, guarded by the Imperial Guard [Imperial Guard §Protecting the Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Guard).
