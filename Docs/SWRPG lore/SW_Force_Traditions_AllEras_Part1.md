# Force Traditions Outside the Jedi Order and the Sith, Part 1: traditions founded before 1000 BBY (All Legends Eras)

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 21, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends), as cited per entry. No mirrors were needed. Draft for review.

## Summary
This two-part file covers Force-using traditions that are neither the Jedi Order nor the Sith, from the pre-Republic era to the Legacy era (c. 130–137 ABY). Part 1 holds the traditions founded before 1000 BBY. Each part has its own cross-era table. The "who exists in each era" table for all traditions in both parts, and species notes (Kaminoans, Neti, ysalamiri and others), are in Part 2, for size. Part 2 holds traditions founded in or after 1000 BBY, including the Witches of Dathomir (c. 600 BBY), Nightsisters, Fallanassi and Antarian Rangers. Key facts: the Aing-Tii are the only source of flow-walking and Force teleportation ("fold space"); the Luka Sene are the main source of Field Detection; the Ysanna kept the Force-ghost technique and perfected projectile guidance. Several listed names do not exist as Legends traditions (see Not found).

**Placement rule:** each tradition sits in the part that matches its founding date. Where a tradition spans 1000 BBY, the entry says so. Where the founding date is unknown, the tradition goes by its earliest dated appearance, and the entry notes this.

**Cross-references (not redone here):** the Jedi ranks file (SW_Jedi_Ranks_Councils_Roles_AllEras_Part1/Part2.md) hands the Paladins, Iron Knights, Potentium and Corellian Jedi to this file. Paladins and Corellian Jedi are in Part 1; the Iron Knights and Potentium are in Part 2. combat for the Matukai, Jensaarai, Zeison Sha and Imperial Knights, plus Teräs Käsi and Echani, is in SW_Lightsaber_SubgroupStyles_AllEras.md. The Je'daii and Corellian Jedi are in SW_Jedi_Training_AllEras_Part1/Part2.md. Sith orders, Inquisitors, Hands, Dark Jedi and the Prophets of the Dark Side are in SW_Sith_Training_AllEras_Part1/Part2.md. Power descriptions are in SW_Force_Powers_Part1a–3b.md. Powers are named here by their Wookieepedia Legends page titles, because the power file was not available to match names against.

## Entries

### Celestials and the Ones (background, not a teachable tradition)
- **Type:** Force entities (background)
- **Era:** Celestials gone by 30,000 BBY; the Ones survived on Mortis until 20 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - The Celestials were ancient entities in the Force. Three manifested as the Ones: the Father, the Daughter (light side) and the Son (dark side). [Celestial/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial/Legends)
  - Their Servant became the Mother. She committed "the Forbidden" by drinking from the Font of Power and bathing in the Pool of Knowledge, and was corrupted into Abeloth. [Celestial/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial/Legends)
  - The Rakata revolted around 35,000 BBY. All traces of the Celestials were gone by 30,000 BBY. [Celestial/Legends §Disappearance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial/Legends) [Celestial/Legends §The Ones](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial/Legends)
  - Xendor visited Mortis before 24,500 BBY. He judged the Ones' claims no more legitimate than any other Force group's. [Mortis/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mortis/Legends)
  - In 20 BBY the Father drew Anakin Skywalker, Obi-Wan Kenobi and Ahsoka Tano to Mortis, and the Ones died there. [Mortis/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mortis/Legends) [Celestial/Legends §The Ones](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial/Legends)
  - The Nightsisters worshipped the Son and the Daughter as their Twin Deities. [The Ones/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/The_Ones/Legends)
  - The Mind Walkers (Part 2) showed Luke and Ben Skywalker the Font of Power and the Pool of Knowledge "beyond shadows" in 43.5 ABY. [Mind Walkers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_Walkers)
- **Related:** Nightsisters (Part 2); Mind Walkers (Part 2)
- **Uncertainty:** Celestials and the Ones are entities, not a school; nothing says they taught anyone.

### Order of Dai Bendu (the Legends "Bendu")
- **Type:** institution (monastic order)
- **Era:** before 36,453 BBY to at least 32 BBY; Ando Prime
- **Key facts:**
  - In 36,453 BBY a Tho Yor carried Bendu monks from the Andobi Mountains of Ando Prime to Tython, where they helped found the Je'daii Order. Some Dai Bendu symbols passed into the Je'daii and Jedi Orders. [Order of Dai Bendu](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Order_of_Dai_Bendu)
  - By the late Republic they were largely forgotten. Historians suspected they were an early form of the Jedi but had no proof. Darth Plagueis owned texts on them. [Order of Dai Bendu §Fall into oblivion](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Order_of_Dai_Bendu)
  - **Structure:** a tribal community led by a High Priest. In 32 BBY this was Ten-Abu Donba. [Order of Dai Bendu §Organization and beliefs](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Order_of_Dai_Bendu)
  - **Beliefs:** non-confrontational, though they ran and hosted podraces. They used numerology; nine stood for the Force in a unified galaxy. Their eight-spoked sigil became the Galactic Roundel and, cut down, the Imperial crest. [Order of Dai Bendu §Organization and beliefs](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Order_of_Dai_Bendu)
  - By 36 ABY the Galactic Alliance's Historical Council knew the Dai Bendu had studied the Force before the Jedi Order existed. [Order of Dai Bendu §Fall into oblivion](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Order_of_Dai_Bendu)
  - **Powers:** none named. **Weapons:** walking poles only. [Order of Dai Bendu §Organization and beliefs](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Order_of_Dai_Bendu)
- **Related:** Je'daii (SW_Jedi_Training files); Legions of Lettow
- **Uncertainty:** "Bendu" the Force entity is Canon only (see Canon only). The Legends Bendu are this order.

### Guardians of the Breath (Kashi Mer Mystics)
- **Type:** institution
- **Era:** before 25,000 BBY to at least 990 BBY; Kashi, Phelleem sector
- **Key facts:**
  - Force users serving the Kashi Mer culture, which called the Force "the Breath". They had a strong Living Force connection and used it to grow crops and heal. [Guardians of the Breath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Guardians_of_the_Breath)
  - The Kashi system was destroyed by a supernova. The Guardians survived but scattered, and *The Jedi Path* still described them in 990 BBY. [Guardians of the Breath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Guardians_of_the_Breath)
  - The Kashi Mer Dynasty exiled Xendor for combining the dark side with Teräs Käsi. [Kashi Mer Dynasty](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kashi_Mer_Dynasty)
  - Xendor drew on the Kashi Mer Guardians of the Breath, along with the Dai Bendu, the Followers of Palawa, the Baran Do and the Chatos Academy, for the Legions of Lettow. [Legions of Lettow](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Legions_of_Lettow)
- **Related:** Legions of Lettow; Followers of Palawa
- **Uncertainty:** [conflict] Supernova date: 25,000 BBY [Kashi Mer Dynasty](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kashi_Mer_Dynasty) vs "by 24,500 BBY" [Guardians of the Breath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Guardians_of_the_Breath). These can both be true. A separate oddity: the *Tales of the Jedi Companion* (set in 4000 BBY) describes the dynasty in the present tense. [Kashi Mer Dynasty §Behind the scenes](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kashi_Mer_Dynasty)

### Followers of Palawa
- **Type:** institution
- **Era:** pre-Republic to after the Clone Wars; Palawa, then Bunduki (Pacanth Reach)
- **Key facts:**
  - They studied the Force and midi-chlorians. They fled to Bunduki after Palawa was destroyed in a war involving the Jedi Council. [Followers of Palawa](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Followers_of_Palawa)
  - They created Teräs Käsi to fight the Jedi, and many fell to the dark side as a result. They also mastered a trance that let them sleep for decades without aging. [Followers of Palawa](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Followers_of_Palawa)
  - During the First Great Schism, many joined the Legions of Lettow, including Arden Lyn. [Followers of Palawa](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Followers_of_Palawa)
  - After the Clone Wars they lived as hermits on Bunduki, teaching visiting youths literature, philosophy, history and martial arts. Zan and Zu Pike were placed with them in the early Empire. [Followers of Palawa](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Followers_of_Palawa)
  - **Gear:** form-fitting Palawa tunics. [Followers of Palawa](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Followers_of_Palawa)
- **Related:** Teräs Käsi in SW_Lightsaber_SubgroupStyles_AllEras.md; Chatos Academy (also from Palawa, see minor traditions)
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Legions of Lettow (First Great Schism)
- **Type:** institution (Jedi breakaway)
- **Era:** c. 24,500 BBY; Lettow
- **Key facts:**
  - Xendor, a Kashi Mer exile and Jedi Knight on Ossus, called the Order's view of the Force "exclusionary". The Council refused his academy for the "Bogan" (the dark aspect), so he founded one on Lettow anyway. [Legions of Lettow](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Legions_of_Lettow)
  - Arden Lyn, a Follower and Steel Hand of Palawa, was his ally and lover. [Legions of Lettow](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Legions_of_Lettow)
  - Initiates forged Force-imbued steel blades, melding a crystal with the blade, and used Jar'Kai, which they renamed Niman. [Legions of Lettow](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Legions_of_Lettow)
  - Xendor had asked to teach doctrines from the Protectorate of the Hidden, the Chatos Academy, the Dai Bendu, the Followers of Palawa, the Kashi Mer, the Baran Do and the Way of the Dark. Refused, he left. [Protectorate of the Hidden](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Protectorate_of_the_Hidden)
- **Related:** Force-imbued swords and pre-saber Niman/Jar'Kai in SW_Lightsaber_SubgroupStyles_AllEras.md; Sith files
- **Uncertainty:** [conflict] Who started the war: Xendor attacked (SWTOR codex) vs the Jedi Council declared war (*Jedi vs. Sith*). Inquisitor Torbin's account, from Arden Lyn, says the Jedi raised an army first. [Legions of Lettow](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Legions_of_Lettow)

### Order of the Terrible Glare
- **Type:** institution (Jedi splinter cult)
- **Era:** Pius Dea Crusades (11,965–10,966 BBY); its last member Rur lasted to the Galactic Civil War; Garn
- **Key facts:**
  - Dissident Jedi Knights who joined the Pius Dea cause when the Order withdrew to Ossus. It became a violent cult led by a High Shaman. [Order of the Terrible Glare/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Order_of_the_Terrible_Glare/Legends) [Pius Dea Crusades](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pius_Dea_Crusades)
  - The shamans were master illusionists. They made soul snares (crystal prisms that trap a soul) and computers that store a dead mind. [Order of the Terrible Glare/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Order_of_the_Terrible_Glare/Legends)
  - A Jedi strike team destroyed them on Garn. High Shaman Rur survived as a computer mind and lured Jedi to Garn for millennia until Luke Skywalker ended it during the Galactic Civil War. [Order of the Terrible Glare/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Order_of_the_Terrible_Glare/Legends)
- **Related:** Jedi Ranks file (Recusal)
- **Uncertainty:** none. "Ordu Aspectu" is the Canon name for a different group and was not used.

### Baran Do Sages (Kel Dor), including the Hidden Ones
- **Type:** institution
- **Era:** thousands of years before the Kel Dor joined the Republic, to 43 ABY; Dorin
- **Key facts:**
  - Advisers and seers to Kel Dor leaders. They began by sensing dangerous weather, then learned to see the future and head off wars and disasters. [Baran Do §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Baran_Do)
  - The Jedi took Force-sensitive Kel Dor children, so the Sages faded and came to be seen as eccentric wizards. Their obscurity spared them in the Purge. [Baran Do §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Baran_Do)
  - By 3640 BBY a hidden cult inside the Baran Do worshipped the dead Sith Lord Darth Sion (SWTOR). [Baran Do §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Baran_Do)
  - **Philosophy:** inner peace, and waiting for the moment the Force dictates. They were not militant, built no lightsabers and were not required to train in combat. Those who trained used a wooden staff and were strong telekinetics able to summon whirlwinds, plus a white lightning technique "possibly a variation" of Electric Judgment. [Baran Do §Philosophy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Baran_Do)
  - Plo Koon came from a line of Jedi and Baran Do. [Baran Do §Philosophy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Baran_Do)
  - After the Yuuzhan Vong War, Jacen Solo learned the ayna-seff there. In 43 ABY Luke and Ben Skywalker found the Hidden Ones. [Baran Do §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Baran_Do)
  - **Hidden Ones:** founded in 19 BBY by Master Tokra Hazz to preserve Baran Do knowledge if the Order was wiped out. Members faked their deaths and lived in caverns under an absolute leader called the Hidden One. Their skills decayed because they no longer practiced. [Hidden Ones](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hidden_Ones) [Hidden Ones §Organization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hidden_Ones) [Hidden Ones §Common abilities](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hidden_Ones)
  - Koro Ziil, a Hidden One, used exceptionally strong white Force lightning and a whirlwind at the same time. [Hidden Ones §Common abilities](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hidden_Ones)
- **Related:** Kel Dor; Jedi recruitment (Jedi Training files)
- **Uncertainty:** [conflict] Kel Dor telepathy: thought to be a technique only Baran Do masters knew, but later exposed as a lie the Sages spread. [Kel Dor/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kel_Dor/Legends)

### Aing-Tii monks, and their neighbours in the Kathol sector
- **Type:** species tradition (Force-sensitive monks)
- **Era:** founding unknown; known to the Jedi by the Ruusan Reformation (c. 1000 BBY), placed in Part 1 for that reason; active to 43 ABY; Kathol Rift
- **Key facts:**
  - **View of the Force:** no light or dark side; the Force is many colours, like a rainbow. Events are "guided", not fixed. Their powers are gifts of "Those Who Dwell Beyond the Veil". They avoided using the Force when they could. [Aing-Tii/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aing-Tii/Legends) [Aing-Tii/Legends §Society and culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aing-Tii/Legends)
  - All Aing-Tii seen offworld were Force-sensitive. They left their territory only to attack slavers, and attacked those who pried. [Aing-Tii/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aing-Tii/Legends)
  - **Unique powers:**
    - They could instantly move objects, from small items up to 300-meter ships, which no Jedi or Sith had mastered (Wookieepedia: [Fold space](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fold_space)). They could teach this even to non-Force-sensitives. [Aing-Tii/Legends §Society and culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aing-Tii/Legends)
    - [Fighting-sight](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fighting-sight) (combat precognition). [Aing-Tii/Legends §Society and culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aing-Tii/Legends)
    - [Flow-walking](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Flow-walking): seeing past or future at a known place, and with emotions returned, influencing events, but only to a limited extent. A little-known secret. [Aing-Tii/Legends §Society and culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aing-Tii/Legends)
  - **Contacts:** Yoda sent Jorj Car'das, who was not Force-sensitive, to them for healing. He studied with them for decades. Jacen Solo studied with them for years after the Yuuzhan Vong War. In 43 ABY Tadar'Ro taught Luke and Ben Skywalker flow-walking and Force teleportation. [Aing-Tii/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aing-Tii/Legends) [Aing-Tii/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aing-Tii/Legends)
  - The Jedi knew of them by the Ruusan Reformation, but the monks were indifferent to the Jedi. [Aing-Tii/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aing-Tii/Legends)
  - **Neighbours:** Ta-Ree was a twisted form of the Force usable mainly inside the Kathol sector after the Kathol Rift disaster, with powers such as Pyrospray and Shadowstrike. [Ta-Ree](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ta-Ree) Ascendants (gosho-ree) were the most skilled Ta-Ree users and often became shamans or chieftains. [Ascendant (Kathol)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ascendant_%28Kathol%29) The Aing-Tii disapproved of the Mind Walkers (Part 2), calling them "Mind Drinkers". [Mind Walkers §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_Walkers)
- **Related:** Mind Walkers; Fallanassi (Jacen's journey)
- **Uncertainty:** founding date not found.

### Luka Sene (Miraluka) and the Sene Seekers
- **Type:** institution (species academy)
- **Era:** existed by c. 3960 BBY (Acaadi studied there before turning Sith at the start of the Jedi Civil War, 3959 BBY [inferred]); Alpheridies [Acaadi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Acaadi) [Jedi Civil War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Civil_War)
- **Key facts:**
  - All Miraluka see through the Force (Force sight) and have no eyes. [Miraluka/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Miraluka/Legends)
  - The Luka Sene had meditation and education chambers in most Alpheridies cities, recruited Miraluka with stronger senses, and was academic in style. Unlike the Jedi, it did not control members' personal lives. Ranks: Administrators, Masters, Mentors. A few members joined the Jedi. [Luka Sene](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Luka_Sene)
  - **Powers:** Sense powers, skilled [Farsight/Legends §Farsight](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Farsight/Legends), and the unique [Field Detection](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Field_Detection) (perceiving energy fields). [Luka Sene](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Luka_Sene)
  - **Sene Seekers:** teams sent after members suspected of falling to the dark side. They tried redemption first, then live capture with security personnel, and killed only as a last resort. [Sene Seekers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sene_Seekers) [Luka Sene](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Luka_Sene)
  - Acaadi studied there, became a Sith, and the Luka Sene sent Jirany Sha after him. [Luka Sene](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Luka_Sene) [Acaadi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Acaadi)
  - After the Jedi Civil War many Miraluka joined the Jedi. The Purge killed thousands of Miraluka. [Miraluka/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Miraluka/Legends)
- **Related:** Keetael (also hunted Acaadi)
- **Uncertainty:** founding date not found.

### Gand findsmen
- **Type:** species religious tradition
- **Era:** centuries before the Galactic Civil War; findsmen at Nar Shaddaa in 3951 BBY; dying out before 3 ABY; Gand
- **Key facts:**
  - A religious tradition of bounty hunters who worshipped Gand's mists and read omens to locate prey, people, things, places and events. Rituals took at least three hours. [Findsman/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Findsman/Legends)
  - The title was sometimes granted during a janwuine-jika. Some sects bathed apprentices in chemicals or altered their genes to grow serrated combat growths. [Findsman/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Findsman/Legends)
  - **Martial art:** "Piercing Touch" (punches through bone, chitin and armor) and "Striking Mist" (closing in so blows can't be parried or dodged). [Findsman/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Findsman/Legends)
  - Leaving the tradition could get a findsman killed as a rogue. [Findsman/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Findsman/Legends)
  - In 3951 BBY nearly a hundred Gand hunters, including findsmen, hunted Meetra Surik on Nar Shaddaa. [Findsman/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Findsman/Legends)
- **Related:** Zuckuss
- **Uncertainty:** [conflict] Wookieepedia calls them Force-sensitive, but the findsmen claimed their abilities were not from the Force. [Findsman/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Findsman/Legends)

### Jal Shey
- **Type:** institution
- **Era:** considered one of the oldest traditions by 4000 BBY; existed c. 19 BBY; nomadic
- **Key facts:**
  - They studied the Force intellectually, not spiritually, and were known for diplomacy rivalling the Jedi's. They were weaker at physical pursuits. [Jal Shey](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jal_Shey)
  - **Ranks:** mentors (equivalent to Jedi Master; e.g. Peet Sieben), advisors (diplomats), neophytes (acolytes). [Jal Shey §Hierarchy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jal_Shey)
  - Selective recruiting from many species. They turned down the dark-tainted and those drawn to combat, and were not numerous. [Jal Shey §Philosophy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jal_Shey)
  - **Gear:** armor, meditation gloves and belts imbued with the Force; their items could benefit any wearer. Many learned basic lightsaber defense. [Jal Shey](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jal_Shey) [Jal Shey §Philosophy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jal_Shey)
  - The Mabari knights of Zolan grew from Jal Shey teachings. After the Great Sith War they were regarded as militant. Roly Melusar considered hunting them in the Empire. [Jal Shey §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jal_Shey)
  - The Order of Shasa sought Jal Shey advice. [Order of Shasa](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Order_of_Shasa)
- **Related:** Order of Shasa; Sorcerers of Tund (the Jedi compared them)
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Wardens of the Sky
- **Type:** institution (loose network)
- **Era:** founded during the Great Hyperspace War (5000 BBY); regarded as myth by later eras
- **Key facts:**
  - When the Hyperspace Navigator's Guild was discredited, a Force-sensitive explorer began patrolling the spacelanes and trained others, who became the first Wardens. [Wardens of the Sky §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wardens_of_the_Sky) [Great Hyperspace War/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Hyperspace_War/Legends)
  - No central organization. Each Warden worked alone, often as a pilot or navigator, and took an apprentice when they found one with potential. [Wardens of the Sky](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wardens_of_the_Sky)
  - They fought pirates but helped smugglers, whom they saw as defying the tyrants of the spacelanes. They avoided blasters aboard ships and used an unarmed style combining the Force and martial arts. [Wardens of the Sky](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wardens_of_the_Sky)
  - Their anonymity hid them from the Sith and later the Empire. [Wardens of the Sky §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wardens_of_the_Sky)
- **Related:** none
- **Uncertainty:** no named members found.

### Sorcerers of Tund
- **Type:** institution (dark side order)
- **Era:** founded after the Great Hyperspace War, believed c. 4250 BBY; ended with Rokur Gepta; Tund (Centrality)
- **Key facts:**
  - Founded by Kissai heretics exiled from Korriban. They mixed science and magic and taught "the Unity": all sentients are Force-sensitive. [Sorcerers of Tund/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sorcerers_of_Tund/Legends) [Third Great Schism](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Great_Schism)
  - Skilled in deception, illusions and shapeshifting, and often driven mad by their studies. [Sorcerers of Tund/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sorcerers_of_Tund/Legends)
  - The Jedi first saw them as just another sect like the Jal Shey or Zeison Sha. After Karnak Tetsu's reign of terror following the Great Sith War, they called the Sorcerers' teaching heresy. A Jedi recruitment attempt failed. [Sorcerers of Tund/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sorcerers_of_Tund/Legends)
  - They only accepted "true Sith", but welcomed the remaining Massassi and Kissai. [Sorcerers of Tund/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sorcerers_of_Tund/Legends)
  - The Croke Rokur Gepta learned their secrets, then killed them all and every living thing on Tund. He died at Lando Calrissian's hands. [Sorcerers of Tund/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sorcerers_of_Tund/Legends)
  - Sidious saw no recruits there. [Sorcerers of Tund/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sorcerers_of_Tund/Legends)
- **Related:** Sith files
- **Uncertainty:** [unclear] The founding date rests on a founding note (c. 4250 BBY). [BTS] Abel G. Peña's 2001 Jedi Council forum post says he meant them to come from a peaceful Second Great Schism c. 10,000 BBY. That conflicts with the in-universe date. [Sorcerers of Tund/Legends §Behind the scenes](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sorcerers_of_Tund/Legends) The *Jedi Academy Training Manual* wrongly calls all Sorcerers Croke. [Sorcerers of Tund/Legends §Behind the scenes](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sorcerers_of_Tund/Legends)

  - Founded around 4200 BBY by Force-sensitive House Mecetti nobles sworn to protect the House. [Tapani sector/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tapani_sector/Legends) [Mecrosa Order §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mecrosa_Order)
  - Viscountess Mireya of Vjun corrupted it with Sith teachings. Under High Lady Brezwalt III it funded Exar Kun. The Jedi answered with the "Cleansing of the Nine Houses". [Mecrosa Order §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mecrosa_Order) [Tapani sector/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tapani_sector/Legends)
  - Survivors, many not Force-sensitive, turned to frinka venom, martial arts and chemical immunity to poison. [Mecrosa Order §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mecrosa_Order)
  - The Mecrosa joined the New Sith Empire in the New Sith Wars, yet poisoned Belia Darzu in 1230 BBY. [Mecrosa Order §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mecrosa_Order)
  - About a thousand years later, Talzin's Nightsisters tested them in the Pella system, and all the Mecrosa fighters died. In the Empire, the non-Force-sensitive Sir Nevil Tritum stole King Adas's holocron. After Endor they were led by Lady Estalle Balis, and Lumiya used their vaults. [Mecrosa Order §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mecrosa_Order)
  - **Other Tapani Force users:** House Pelagia gave the Jedi recruits and formed a Force-strong bloodline. In the Purge, Mecetti helped root out Pelagian Jedi. Taj Junak survived. [Tapani sector/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tapani_sector/Legends)
- **Related:** Nightsisters (Part 2); Sith files
- **Uncertainty:** [unclear] The date of the Talzin clash is only "a thousand years" after 1230 BBY. That clashes with Talzin's era (see the Nightsisters entry in Part 2).

### Krath
- **Type:** institution (dark side cult)
- **Era:** founded 3998 BBY; coup 3997 BBY; collapsed at the end of the Great Sith War (3996 BBY); later re-founded; Empress Teta system
- **Key facts:**
  - Bored Tetan nobles led by Satal Keto and Aleema Keto founded it, naming it after a magician-god of fairy tales. Freedon Nadd's spirit taught them Sith secrets. [Krath/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Krath/Legends)
  - Krath war droids killed Arca Jeth on Deneba. Ulic Qel-Droma joined, killed Satal, and became co-ruler. With Exar Kun they formed the Brotherhood of the Sith. [Krath/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Krath/Legends)
  - Temple guards wore Holy Battle Armor. Aleema's magical talents won the civil war. [Krath/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Krath/Legends)
  - Survivors scattered near Korriban. Sebban Keto quietly re-founded the Krath in Cinnagar millennia later. Palpatine studied Krath teachings. [Krath/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Krath/Legends) [Great Sith War/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Sith_War/Legends)
- **Related:** Sith files (Brotherhood of the Sith); Ysanna (Cron Supernova)
- **Uncertainty:** [unclear] Date of the re-founding not given.

### Blazing Chain

  - **View:** not light or dark. They use the Force for raiding, and a fall to the dark side matters only if it threatens the fleet. [Blazing Chain §Philosophy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blazing_Chain)
- **Related:** Sorcerers of Rhand (also Unknown Regions)
- **Uncertainty:** none

[Ysanna/Legends §Society and culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ysanna/Legends)

### Keetael (Draethos)

### Knights of Zakuul (with Scions and Horizon Guard)
- **Type:** institution (imperial Force order)
- **Era:** Eternal Empire; High Justice Vaylin by 3636 BBY; Horizon Guard formed 3630 BBY; splinters after the Empire fell; Zakuul (Wild Space)
- **Key facts:**
  - Force-sensitive warriors who saw the Force as a tool for justice and believed their connection came from Emperor Valkorion. [Knights of Zakuul](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Knights_of_Zakuul)
  - No area of the Force was off-limits, but findings were shared, unlike the Sith. Lana Beniko judged them weak duelists because they did not channel anger the way the Sith code does. [Knights of Zakuul](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Knights_of_Zakuul)
  - **Structure:** led by a High Justice; Yuriem held the title before Vaylin. Heavily militarized: shields and coordination with walkers and Skytroopers (e.g. "Zildrog's Wedge"). [Knights of Zakuul](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Knights_of_Zakuul)
  - **Scions:** a division that saw the future and believed fanatically in destiny. Arcann had them massacred. [Scions of Zakuul](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scions_of_Zakuul)
  - **Horizon Guard:** Force-sensitive elite picked by Vaylin from the Knights as her guard, 3630 BBY. [Horizon Guard](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Horizon_Guard)
  - After the fall, some Knights guarded the Alliance leaders, and others joined splinters such as the Order of Zildrog. [Knights of Zakuul](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Knights_of_Zakuul)
- **Related:** Jedi and Sith of the SWTOR era
- **Uncertainty:** none

## Decisions needed
1. Power names: the developer's SW_Force_Powers_Part1a–3b.md was not available, so powers are named by Wookieepedia Legends page titles (for example "Fold space" for Aing-Tii teleportation, "Meditation of Immersion" for Force immersion). Map these to your file's names.
2. [conflict] Legions of Lettow: who started the First Great Schism, Xendor or the Jedi? The sources are split. The most detailed in-universe account (Torbin, from Arden Lyn) says the Jedi raised an army first.
3. [conflict] Gand findsmen: model their divination as a Force power, as Wookieepedia classifies it, or as a non-Force cultural skill, as the findsmen themselves claim?
4. [conflict] Kel Dor telepathy: treat it as a Baran Do lie (the latest source, *Outcast*), or allow it as a technique?
5. Aing-Tii fold space can be taught to non-Force-sensitives. Allow that for player characters (Car'das precedent), or keep it NPC-only?
6. Sorcerers of Tund founding: c. 4250 BBY (in-universe founding note) or the [BTS] author intent of c. 10,000 BBY? The in-universe date fits the timeline.
7. Mecrosa vs Talzin: the source dates this "a thousand years" after 1230 BBY (c. 230 BBY). Accept that date, which affects Talzin's age (see Part 2)?
8. Kashi supernova and the *Tales of the Jedi Companion* present-tense description: treat the Kashi Mer as gone (25,000 BBY) with only the scattered Guardians surviving? That fits most sources.
9. Should species notes such as the Neti hibernation trance count as tradition-taught powers, or as species traits?
10. [conflict] Teepo's Paladins after the Purge: active into the New Republic (*Essential Guide to Warfare*) vs fate unknown (Gray Paladin page)? The more specific source is the Warfare guide.
11. Corellian Jedi: treat them as a Jedi sub-faction with a Corellia-first loyalty flag, or as a separate tradition? The sources treat them as a sect of the Order.
