Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 24, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends), as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
Part 2 covers the aftermath of the taking of Coruscant (*The Krytos Trap*), the Bacta War (*The Bacta War*), Wraith Squadron and the Hunt for Zsinj (*Wraith Squadron*, *Iron Fist*, *Solo Command*, *The Courtship of Princess Leia*), the post-Zsinj campaigns, the Thrawn campaign and the Hegemony theater (*Isard's Revenge*). See Part 1 for how to read the tables and the calendar note; the back sections are in Part 5.
