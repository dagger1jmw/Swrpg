## Summary
Every lightsaber has a crystal at its heart that resonates to produce the blade. The commonest come from the Adega system, Ilum and Dantooine, but Jedi and Sith used dozens of other crystals, gems and even non-crystals for different colours, effects and Force enhancements. A crystal has to be imbued with the Force by days of meditation before use. The Jedi mostly use natural crystals. The Sith usually use red synthetic ones, which give a slightly stronger blade when charged by the dark side. Synthetic crystals were developed between 6900 and 3961 BBY. From the Sith Emperor's reign the Sith used synthetics, so their blades were red. From the Seventh Battle of Ruusan to the Great Jedi Purge, the Jedi used Ilum crystals almost exclusively, so their blades were blue or green.

## Entries

### Lightsaber crystals: general
- **Type:** technology (lightsaber component)
- **Key facts:**
  - Also called jewels, focusing crystals or kyber crystals: crystals, gems, stones or other objects used as a lightsaber's energy source [Lightsaber crystal/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_crystal/Legends).
  - One crystal at the heart of every lightsaber resonates to produce the blade. The commonest come from the Adega system, Ilum and Dantooine's Crystal Cave. Some crystals enhance a particular user's Force skills. Other gems were used only for prismatic effects. Not every focus is a crystal: some Jedi and Sith used physical remains, glass shards or micro-machinery [Lightsaber crystal/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_crystal/Legends).
  - **Preparation:** before installing it, the user has to imbue the crystal with the Force by meditating on it for many days [Lightsaber crystal/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_crystal/Legends).
  - **Combining:** crystals of different colours can be used together for a new blade colour. Crystals can be compressed (a pulsing blade) or unstable (a swirling, sparkling core) [Lightsaber crystal/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_crystal/Legends).
  - **Tuned unique crystals:** several named crystals, once tuned by a Force-sensitive, can only be used in that person's lightsaber (e.g. [Bnar's Sacrifice](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bnar%27s_Sacrifice); [Kenobi's Legacy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kenobi%27s_Legacy)).
- **Uncertainty:** none.

### Synthetic crystals and the Sith
- **Type:** technology
- **Key facts:**
  - Synth-crystals are made artificially, not formed geologically. They're usually red, but can be made in any colour by special handling, often through the Force. They were first developed between 6900 and 3961 BBY and are associated with the Sith [Synthetic lightsaber crystal/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Synthetic_lightsaber_crystal/Legends).
  - Sith synthetics give a slightly stronger blade when charged by the dark side, and a Sith blade could occasionally "break" a Jedi's. Darth Maul made his four crystals in a special furnace over several days, using the Force to shape and purify them and to keep himself alive in the heat. Luke Skywalker also used a synthetic crystal, made in a furnace in Obi-Wan's home [Lightsaber crystal/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_crystal/Legends).
  - **Cleansing:** the Jedi had a technique for purging a dark-sider's synthetic crystal of its former owner's hate. In 41 ABY, Jaden Korr turned a Dark Jedi's red crystal yellow by meditation [Lightsaber crystal/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_crystal/Legends).
  - From the Sith Emperor's reign, the Sith used synthetic crystals, so their blades were red [Lightsaber crystal/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_crystal/Legends).
- **Uncertainty:** none.

### Colour and role
- **Type:** custom
- **Key facts:**
  - In 3956 BBY, Dorak told Revan that Guardians used blue crystals, Sentinels yellow and Consulars green. *The Jedi Path* says the same [Lightsaber crystal/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_crystal/Legends).
  - From the Seventh Battle of Ruusan (1000 BBY) to the Great Jedi Purge, the Jedi used Ilum crystals almost exclusively. These were always blue or green [Lightsaber crystal/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_crystal/Legends).
  - The page lists dozens of named hues by colour family (blue, green, purple, red, yellow and orange) with example owners. See the page for the full list [Lightsaber crystal/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_crystal/Legends).
- **Related:** SW_Lightsabers_Crystals_Materials_AllEras (blade colours).
- **Uncertainty:** none.

## Game notes (optional, suggestions only)
- Per decision 1, natural crystals with no stated limit are obtainable at any date from their source world. The dated rows restrict: Ruusan crystals lost after c. 1000 BBY, Eralam and Sapith rare, rubat controlled, and the unique named crystals only from their dates.
- *Suggestion:* Galaxies-only unique crystals (Kenobi's Legacy, Kun's Blood, Allya's, etc.) are named for people of their own past, so they work as rare finds in Galactic Civil War-era games only.
- *Suggestion:* the Dantari, focus and meditation crystals ("recovering spent Force energy", "retaining energy") could give a Force-pool regeneration bonus. Kasha could feed the "centered" mechanic.
