## Summary
Ossus is the Jedi's ancient library world in the Adega system. It's where the Jedi Academy and Odan-Urr's Great Jedi Library stood, and where the ideas of the light and dark sides were first written down. In 3996 BBY the Cron Cluster supernova, set off in the Great Sith War, turned it from a lush world into an irradiated desert. A few Jedi survived in the Crystal Caves, and their descendants became the Force-sensitive but untrained Ysanna tribes. It stays a ruin until, some time after the Galactic War (after 3636 BBY), Gnost-Dural leads a Jedi colony there fleeing Zakuul. The Sith attack it in 3628 BBY, and the colony is evacuated.

## Entries

### Ossus: world
- **Type:** location (planet)
- **Era:** all eras
- **Key facts:**
  - The third planet of the Adega system in the Auril sector. It follows a figure-eight orbit around Adega Prime and Adega Besh. Its moons are Mim (rocky and airless) and Nerit (temperate, high-gravity, home to Neti expatriates) [Ossus/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ossus/Legends).
  - It was lush, about two-thirds water, until the Cron Cluster's destruction in 3996 BBY turned it into an irradiated desert. It partly regrew by the Galactic Alliance era, and Yuuzhan Vong terraforming fully restored it within about a century [Ossus/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ossus/Legends).
  - Before the Cron supernova it had hundreds of millions of people in cities worldwide. The main city, Knossa, sat in the Eocho Mountains and had the largest spaceport [Ossus/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ossus/Legends).
  - **Wildlife:** kingwood and bambwood trees, gokobs, and sticky-tongued tree frogs [Ossus/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ossus/Legends).
- **Uncertainty:** none.

### Force nature
- **Type:** Force phenomenon
- **Era:** all eras
- **Key facts:**
  - Ossus is where the ideas of the light and dark sides of the Force were first set down, and where the Living and Unifying Force philosophies were established [Ossus/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ossus/Legends).
  - Nomi Sunrider described it as deeply peaceful, with the Force running through everything [Ossus/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ossus/Legends).
  - Ood Bnar, a Neti Jedi Master, used the Force to turn himself into a tree to guard ancient lightsabers [Ossus/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ossus/Legends).
- **Uncertainty:** whether Ossus counts as Force-rich in game terms [unclear] (see Decisions needed).

### Key locations
- **Type:** locations
- **Era:** as noted
- **Key facts** (all from [Ossus/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ossus/Legends)):
  - **Knossa:** the main city and spaceport. Its central plaza held two obelisks topped with Adegan crystals, and their alignment with the moons gave rise to "Ossus Day".
  - **Great Jedi Library:** built c. 5000 BBY on the Ooroo Canyon. It held more than ten thousand works, with towers for four historical eras and a deep Chamber of Antiquities. Its location was lost until Rayf Ysanna's great-grandfather found it.
  - **Jedi Academy:** a campus on a bench of land between a mountain and a rift valley, designed for meditation. Destroyed in 137 ABY.
  - **Crystal Caves:** under the Eocho Mountains, where Jedi survivors sheltered from the supernova.
- **Related:** SW_Lightsabers_Crystals_Materials_AllEras (Adegan crystals).
- **Uncertainty:** the Eye of Ashla isn't described in the page text.

### The Ysanna
- **Type:** group (people)
- **Era:** after 3996 BBY onward
- **Key facts:**
  - The descendants of the Jedi who survived in the Crystal Caves forgot their Jedi origins and became the nomadic Ysanna tribes [Ossus/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ossus/Legends).
  - They're Force-sensitive but untrained, with weak use of the Force, which they credit to magic [Ossus/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ossus/Legends).
  - They ride hardy four-legged beasts and wear ritual masks based on dark side artifacts from the Library's ruins [Ossus/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ossus/Legends).
  - Jem and Rayf were the first Ysanna to join the Jedi, in 10 ABY [Ossus/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ossus/Legends).
- **Uncertainty:** when the name "Ysanna" first applies [unclear]. They descend from the 3996 BBY survivors, but no source fetched here names them before the Imperial era.

### The Gnost-Dural colony and the invasion of Ossus
- **Type:** history
- **Era:** after 3636 BBY to 3628 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **After the Galactic War:** a Jedi colony led by Gnost-Dural, fleeing Arcann and Vaylin's Eternal Empire, settled on Ossus and worked to recover lost knowledge [Ossus/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ossus/Legends).
  - **3628 BBY:** Sith forces under Darth Malora attacked. The Republic responded under General Daerunn, and the colony was evacuated. The fighting also woke Darth Malgus [Ossus/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ossus/Legends).
  - **The Holocron of Odan-Urr:** made before his death in 3996 BBY and lost for an unknown time. The Jedi Master and archaeologist Yuon Par found it some time before 3643 BBY. By 3628 BBY it was kept in the Great Jedi Library on Ossus with other Jedi artifacts, and its security was put at risk in that year's invasion [Holocron of Odan-Urr, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/holocron_of_odan-urr).
- **Uncertainty:** the colony's arrival date. The page gives only "some time after" the Galactic War (after 3636 BBY).
