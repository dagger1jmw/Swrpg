# Star Wars Legends: Power Structures of the SWTOR Era (Sith Empire and Galactic Republic, c. 3681–3600 BBY), Part 1: Sith Empire

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 21, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
This is Part 1 of 2: the Sith Empire's power structure from the Great Galactic War (3681 BBY) through the Cold War, the Galactic War, the Eternal Empire conquest (c. 3636 BBY, confirmed) and the Third Galactic War (c. 3627 BBY).

- **The Emperor:** after 4980 BBY Vitiate left day-to-day rule to the twelve-member Dark Council. He ruled through his Voice, Hand, Wrath and Guard, and in the Cold War handed the Council "complete political control". [Tenebrae §Rebuilding an empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrae); [Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War) §The Cold War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_%28Post%E2%80%93Great_Hyperspace_War%29)
- **The Council:** each Councilor ran a Sphere made of rival Sith power bases. Seats passed by apprenticeship, appointment, killing or unanimous vote, and the Kaggath was the sanctioned way to settle feuds. [Dark Council §Powers and succession](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Council); [Kaggath §Rules](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kaggath)
- **Non-Sith state:** three ministries (War, Intelligence, Logistics) ran it, each answering to its Spheres. Imperial Intelligence was dissolved in 3640 BBY and replaced by Sith Intelligence. [Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War) §Central authority](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_%28Post%E2%80%93Great_Hyperspace_War%29); [Minister of Intelligence (Sith Empire) §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Minister_of_Intelligence_%28Sith_Empire%29)
- **After the conquest:** Empress Acina, then Emperor Vowrawn, ruled with a five-seat Council. [Dark Council §Council Reduced to Five Seats](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Council)

## Entries
### The Sith Emperor (Vitiate; as Valkorion in Zakuul)
- **Type:** office and ruler
- **Era:** 4980 BBY (Empire refounded on Dromund Kaas) to c. 3636 BBY (break with the Empire); Vitiate's last remnant was destroyed in 3626 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Day-to-day rule:** soon after reaching Dromund Kaas he created the Dark Council, which "soon took over much of the day-to-day running of the Empire", while he withdrew into seclusion in the Imperial Citadel in Kaas City. [Tenebrae §Rebuilding an empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrae)
  - **Form of government:** a "magocratic authoritarian government" with the Emperor at its head. The Dark Council served as an advisory board and as a combined executive, legislative and judicial branch. [Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War) §Central authority](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_%28Post%E2%80%93Great_Hyperspace_War%29)
  - **Direct powers:**
    - The Imperial Military was under his "direct command", though overseen by the Minister of War. [Imperial Military (Sith Empire)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Military_%28Sith_Empire%29)
    - He could appoint Councilors, as he did after the two purges. [Dark Council §Powers and succession](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Council)
    - He decreed that no slave could ever be freed and that slave status passed to descendants. In the Great Galactic War he ordered every slave with enough Force sensitivity sent to Korriban for training. [Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War) §Society and culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_%28Post%E2%80%93Great_Hyperspace_War%29)
  - **Peace of 3653 BBY:** around 3654 BBY he won over the seven surviving Councilors by promising each a star system from the Republic under the coming treaty. At his request Darth Baras led the peace delegation in 3653 BBY. [Tenebrae §A renewed war](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrae)
  - **Absences:**
    - After the treaty he went back into seclusion and left the Empire to the Dark Council. [Tenebrae §A renewed war](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrae)
    - In the Cold War he kept "an iron grip on the hearts and minds of his people" despite a "seemingly apathetic role in governing them". [Tenebrae §Cold War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrae)
    - He grew more reclusive until he turned "complete political control" over to the Dark Council. He then renewed conscription as war neared. [Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War) §The Cold War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_%28Post%E2%80%93Great_Hyperspace_War%29)
  - **The Voice:**
    - From c. 3950 BBY he ruled through host bodies called the Emperor's Voice. The Dark Council obeyed the Voice "the same as they would the Emperor himself". [Emperor's Voice (Sith Empire) §A new empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Emperor's_Voice_%28Sith_Empire%29)
    - In the Cold War the Voice was a Voss Mystic. Darth Baras trapped it on Voss in 3640 BBY. [Emperor's Voice (Sith Empire) §Plans for immortality](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Emperor's_Voice_%28Sith_Empire%29)
  - **Defeat on Dromund Kaas:** a Jedi Knight (the Hero of Tython) killed the next Voice during the 3640 BBY Republic assault on Dromund Kaas. The Emperor's essence fell into slumber and power passed to the Dark Council. [Emperor's Voice (Sith Empire) §Plots foiled](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Emperor's_Voice_%28Sith_Empire%29); [Dromund Kaas/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dromund_Kaas/Legends); [Dark Council §Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Council)
  - **Break with the Empire:**
    - After the Revanite crisis and his revival on Yavin 4, the Dark Council branded him a traitor and renounced him. [Dark Council](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Council); [Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War) §Central authority](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_%28Post%E2%80%93Great_Hyperspace_War%29)
    - He consumed all life on Ziost in 3636 BBY. [Ziost/Legends §Geography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ziost/Legends)
  - **Valkorion:** he secretly ruled the Eternal Empire through the Valkorion body and was killed there c. 3636 BBY. The Alliance Commander destroyed his true body in 3626 BBY. [Eternal Empire conquest §Prelude](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Empire_conquest); [Emperor's Voice (Sith Empire) §Plots foiled](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Emperor's_Voice_%28Sith_Empire%29)
- **Related:** Emperor's Hand, Emperor's Wrath, Imperial Guard, Dark Council (this file); Knights of Zakuul in SW_Force_Traditions_AllEras_Part1.md
- **Uncertainty:** [conflict] When the first Voice appeared: *The Old Republic: Revan* has Darth Nyriss mention the Voice in 3954 BBY, while the *TOR Encyclopedia* dates the first Voice to after Revan's attack c. 3950 BBY. [Emperor's Voice (Sith Empire) §Behind the scenes](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Emperor's_Voice_%28Sith_Empire%29)

### Emperor's Hand, later the Hand of the Empire
- **Type:** institution (the Emperor's secret agents)
- **Era:** from soon after 4980 BBY to c. 3636 BBY; re-formed as the Hand of the Empire after the Battle of the Meridian Complex (Third Galactic War, c. 3627 BBY [unclear: no year given])
- **Key facts:**
  - **Membership:** twelve Force-sensitive pureblood Sith "Servants" who shared the Emperor's longevity. Servant One led them; Servant Two gained prophetic powers. [Tenebrae §Rebuilding an empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrae)
  - **Role:**
    - They formed "another layer of power" between the Dark Council and the Emperor. Only the Hand stayed in constant contact with him and knew his goals. [Hand of the Empire §Organization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hand_of_the_Empire)
    - They did not take part in running the Empire. [Hand of the Empire §Organization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hand_of_the_Empire)
  - **Powers over Sith and officials:**
    - When a Voice died, the Hand received the Emperor's essence and found a new Voice. [Emperor's Voice (Sith Empire) §Role and powers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Emperor's_Voice_%28Sith_Empire%29)
    - In the Galactic War they chose Darth Baras' apprentice as the new Emperor's Wrath. [Hand of the Empire §The false Voice](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hand_of_the_Empire)
    - After Darth Karrid's death at Duro in 3640 BBY, the Hand chose Darth Acina to head the Sphere of Technology. [Darth Acina §Dark Councilor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Acina)
    - After the Emperor's defeat on Dromund Kaas, they ordered the Imperial Guard to hide his body. [Hand of the Empire §A weakened Emperor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hand_of_the_Empire)
  - **End:** after Ziost, Valkorion told the Outlander he no longer needed his Hand, Voice or Children. [Hand of the Empire §Betrayed](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hand_of_the_Empire); [Children of the Emperor §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Children_of_the_Emperor)
  - **Hand of the Empire:**
    - Emperor Vowrawn re-formed it without the Dark Council's knowledge, so it could work autonomously instead of under individual Sith Lords, and appointed Darth Rivix to it. [Hand of the Empire §Betrayed](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hand_of_the_Empire)
    - The Servants who stayed loyal to Vitiate were purged. [Sith Emperor's power base](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Emperor's_power_base)
- **Related:** The Sith Emperor; Emperor's Wrath; Children of the Emperor
- **Uncertainty:**
  - [conflict] One account says the Hand was "known only to a few members of the Dark Council" [Tenebrae §Rebuilding an empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrae). Another says "the Dark Council were aware of their existence" [Hand of the Empire §Organization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hand_of_the_Empire).
  - Game-choice note: in *Onslaught* the Hand's re-formation is announced by Emperor Vowrawn or Empress Acina, depending on the player's earlier choices. This is video-game branching. [Hand of the Empire §Behind the scenes](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hand_of_the_Empire)

### Emperor's Wrath, later the Empire's Wrath
- **Type:** title (personal enforcer)
- **Era:** c. 3950 BBY (created for Lord Scourge) to c. 3636 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Creation:** created for Lord Scourge as "a position higher than even a Dark Lord on the Council". [Emperor's Wrath §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Emperor's_Wrath)
  - **Powers:**
    - The Wrath took orders only from the Emperor. Unlike his other servants, the Wrath was not bound by a ritual, so as to keep full autonomy. [Emperor's Wrath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Emperor's_Wrath); [Sith Emperor's power base](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Emperor's_power_base)
    - The Minister of War had no jurisdiction over the Wrath. [Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War) §Ministry of War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_%28Post%E2%80%93Great_Hyperspace_War%29)
  - **Second Wrath:**
    - Baras' former apprentice, appointed by the Hand during the Galactic War.
    - After the Wrath killed Baras before the Council, the Dark Council formally acknowledged the Wrath. [Emperor's Wrath §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Emperor's_Wrath)
    - Darth Vowrawn, speaking for the Council, declared the Wrath had "free rein", answerable only to the Emperor. [Darth Vowrawn §Defeat of Baras](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Vowrawn)
  - **Under Marr:**
    - When the Wrath renounced Vitiate, Darth Marr renamed the post the Empire's Wrath, answerable only to Marr. [Emperor's Wrath §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Emperor's_Wrath); [Sith Emperor's power base](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Emperor's_power_base)
    - The Empire's Wrath remained second-in-command of the military after Marr. [Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War) §Central authority](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_%28Post%E2%80%93Great_Hyperspace_War%29)
  - **End:** the Wrath vanished around the time the Eternal Empire emerged (c. 3636 BBY). [Empire's Wrath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Empire's_Wrath)
- **Related:** Emperor's Hand; Dark Council; Sith ranks in SW_Sith_Academies_Training_OldRepublic.md
- **Uncertainty:** Game-choice note: in *Onslaught* a Sith Warrior player can have the Empire's Wrath rank restored and lead the Hand. This is player-class branching, not a fixed event. [Hand of the Empire §Behind the scenes](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hand_of_the_Empire)

### Imperial Guard (Emperor's Guard; later the Dark Honor Guard)
- **Type:** institution (the Emperor's guard)
- **Era:** founded before 3959 BBY; active through the Galactic War; kept under Empress Acina (c. 3630 BBY)
- **Key facts:**
  - **Membership:** non-Force-sensitive warriors chosen from the Imperial Military and mentally bound to the Emperor. Even Dark Councilors feared them. [Imperial Guard](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Guard); [Imperial Guard §Organization and philosophy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Guard)
  - **Independence:** the Dark Council "had no authority over or insight into" the Guard's activities. [Imperial Guard §Organization and philosophy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Guard)
  - **Duties:** they guarded the Sith Sanctum and the Imperial Citadel on Dromund Kaas, and the Sith Academy on Korriban. [Imperial Guard §Organization and philosophy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Guard)
  - **Society:** membership was reserved for non-Force-sensitives, and "not even the highest ranking Sith-Lord dared to resist them". [Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War) §Society and culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_%28Post%E2%80%93Great_Hyperspace_War%29)
  - **Service:** Guardsmen served for life and ended their careers as training commandants. [Imperial Guard §Equipment and training](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Guard)
  - **Later history:**
    - The Guard was later reorganized and took in more Force-sensitive members. [Imperial Guard](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Guard)
    - Empress Acina kept it as the Dark Honor Guard to protect the Dark Council and the ruler. [Sith Emperor's power base](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Emperor's_power_base)
- **Related:** The Sith Emperor; Minister of Intelligence (the Guard is the one post higher than the Minister open to non-Sith)
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Children of the Emperor
- **Type:** group (hidden agents)
- **Era:** first created c. 3678 BBY; abandoned after Ziost (3636 BBY)
- **Key facts:** people taken as infants by the Hand and given part of the Emperor's power. By c. 3641 BBY they had infiltrated the Jedi Order, Republic Military and Senate. The Jedi Councilor Syo Bakarn was the hidden "First Son" who shielded them. [Children of the Emperor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Children_of_the_Emperor)
- **Related:** Emperor's Hand; Republic part (Republic and Jedi infiltration)
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Dark Council
- **Type:** institution (ruling body)
- **Era:** 4980 BBY to c. 3636 BBY with twelve seats; five seats under Empress Acina (reorganization made known c. 3628 BBY)
- **Key facts:**
  - **Status:** the "supreme governing body" of the Empire. Twelve Dark Lords of the Sith each controlled one Sphere of Influence. Some tenures lasted months, others decades. [Dark Council](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Council)
  - **Reach:** every Sith "ultimately answered to one of the Dark Council" through a system of power bases, and every non-Sith organization fell under one or more Spheres. [Dark Council §The Twelve Spheres](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Council)
  - **How seats were won** [Dark Council §Powers and succession](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Council):
    - A fallen Councilor's apprentice was the "first choice".
    - The Emperor could pick new members.
    - Killing a Councilor "guaranteed that the victor would take the seat".
    - Failing those, the other members could choose by unanimous vote, though they were not always unanimous.
  - **Seats granted:** in 3678 BBY three Councilors sent the apprentice Teneb Kel to hunt Exal Kressh. The Council then granted him the rank of Sith Lord and the title "Darth Thanaton" in exchange for one of the Emperor's secrets. [Dark Council §Great Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Council)
  - **How seats were lost** [Dark Council §Early history](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Council); [Dark Council §Conspiracy against the Emperor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Council):
    - **Purges:**
      - Darth Lokess's plot, in the Empire's first centuries: the eleven others died on the Citadel steps.
      - The Nyriss conspiracy, 3950 BBY: the Emperor killed nine, the Imperial Guard two and Revan Darth Nyriss; twelve new members were appointed [Ruled (Jared, 24 Nov 2026)].
    - **Executions:** around 4043 BBY the other ten executed Darths Qalar and Victun after their Kaggath destroyed the Citadel.
  - **After the Great War:** only seven Councilors survived. The Council ruled the new territories from the Citadel on Dromund Kaas. [Dark Council §Great Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Council)
  - **Galactic War:**
    - The Council met in its chambers on Korriban, where Baras and Thanaton were defeated in front of it. [Dark Council §Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Council)
    - After the Emperor's defeat, Darth Marr was the "de facto leader" and tried to curb infighting. [Dark Council §Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Council); [Darth Marr](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Marr)
  - **Total control:** after the Revanite crisis and Ziost, the Council declared Vitiate a traitor and took "total control" of the Empire. [Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War) §Central authority](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_%28Post%E2%80%93Great_Hyperspace_War%29)
  - **Collapse:** most Councilors died or vanished in the Eternal Empire conquest, and Darth Acina declared herself Empress. [Dark Council §Conquest by the Eternal Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Council)
  - **Under Acina:**
    - From 3631 BBY Acina acted alone with Sith and non-Sith ministers, so it is "unclear" whether she had fully restored the Council. [Dark Council §Conquest by the Eternal Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Council)
    - She later cut it to five seats to reduce infighting. [Dark Council §Council Reduced to Five Seats](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Council); [Dark Council §Renewed Conflict](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Council)
- **Related:** the Spheres table below; Kaggath; SW_Sith_Academies_Training_OldRepublic.md (the Council's existence and Sith ranks)
- **Uncertainty:**
  - [conflict] Survivors of the conquest: Acina was "the only Dark Councilor" left, versus Darth Vowrawn as a "confirmed survivor" who kept his seat. Later game updates also show Mortis and Ravage alive. [Dark Council §Conquest by the Eternal Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Council); [Dark Council §Behind the scenes](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Council)
  - [unclear] Whether a twelve-seat Council met between c. 3636 and c. 3628 BBY.

### The twelve Spheres of Influence (Darth Vitiate's Council)
Each Sphere was also called the "Pyramid of …" and most also the "Ministry of …". Each was made up of the power bases of many Sith Lords, all answering to the Councilor at its head. ### Below the Council: Darths, Lords, power bases and the Kaggath
- **Type:** custom and structure
- **Era:** Great Galactic War to Galactic War (3681–3636 BBY)
- **Key facts:**
  - **Titles:**
    - Vitiate let high-ranking Sith Lords take the title Darth. [Darth/Legends §Vitiate's reconstituted Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth/Legends)
    - "Lord" went to mid-ranking Sith who had finished their apprenticeships and were "not officially controlled by other Lords". Lords ranked above Overseers and below Darths. [Sith Lord/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Lord/Legends)
    - The full rank ladder and training are in the developer's Sith files.
  - **Power bases:**
    - Lord Kallig's power base held Sith, Imperial Military followers (notably Moff Valion Pyron), Imperial Reclamation Service staff and a captured cult, all under the Sphere of Ancient Knowledge. [Kallig's powerbase](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kallig's_powerbase)
    - Kallig inherited his master Darth Zash's assets and followers when she died. [Kallig's powerbase §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kallig's_powerbase)
    - His superior, Darth Thanaton, ordered the power base purged. [Kallig's powerbase §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kallig's_powerbase)
    - When Kallig took the seat, the power base was absorbed into the Sphere. [Kallig's powerbase §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kallig's_powerbase)
  - **Chains of command:** Plan Zero ran from Darth Vengean (directive) to Darth Baras (operations) to Baras' apprentice (execution). "No Sith was allowed to interfere" with it. Baras and the apprentice later turned on Vengean, and Baras took the seat. [Plan Zero](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Plan_Zero); [Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War) §The Cold War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_%28Post%E2%80%93Great_Hyperspace_War%29)
  - **Kaggath, the sanctioned feud** [Kaggath §Rules](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kaggath):
    - Only a Sith the Dark Council recognized as having enough power and authority could declare one.
    - The challenger chose the arena: a planet, a system or the whole galaxy.
    - Each side fought with its own armies, bases and ships, with no outside help.
    - Anyone breaking the rules was usually executed by uninvolved Sith.
    - A Sith who declined a challenge lost their rank to the challenger.
    - The winner could kill or spare the loser, take or destroy their assets, and erase their name.
  - **Example:** Thanaton fled his Kaggath to ask the Council for help, so Darth Mortis executed him. [Kaggath §Games](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kaggath)
  - **Infighting:** the Council was "astonished" that a member could not beat "a mere Lord" and let Kallig challenge Thanaton. Marr admitted on Makeb that infighting had weakened the Empire. [Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War) §Fragmentation](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_%28Post%E2%80%93Great_Hyperspace_War%29)
  - **Academy instructors:** Overseers taught acolytes on Korriban. They were often Sith Lords but did not have to be, and some took apprentices. [Overseer (Sith)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Overseer_%28Sith%29)
- **Related:** Dark Council; SW_Sith_Academies_Training_OldRepublic.md and SW_Sith_Training_AllEras_Part1.md (ranks, academies, apprentices)
- **Uncertainty:** none

### The three Ministries and the non-Sith state
- **Type:** institutions
- **Era:** from 4980 BBY to after 3630 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Structure:**
    - The three ministries (War, Intelligence, Logistics) were the "central pillars" of rule. Each minister reported to one Councilor, or to several. [Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War) §Central authority](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_%28Post%E2%80%93Great_Hyperspace_War%29)
    - Some Spheres had their own agencies instead, such as the Imperial Diplomatic Service and the Imperial Conquest Consolidation Corps. [Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War) §Central authority](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_%28Post%E2%80%93Great_Hyperspace_War%29)
    - The Imperial Citadel held the headquarters of all three ministries. [Imperial Citadel (Sith Empire)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Citadel_%28Sith_Empire%29)
  - **Ministry of War:**
    - Ran the Navy's deployments, repairs and starfighters, and the Army's deployments, tactics, special forces and black ops. [Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War) §Ministry of War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_%28Post%E2%80%93Great_Hyperspace_War%29); [Ministry of War (Sith Empire)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ministry_of_War_%28Sith_Empire%29)
    - Rebuilt the fleet after the Great War. [Ministry of War (Sith Empire)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ministry_of_War_%28Sith_Empire%29)
    - Its mercenary recruitment division hired freelancers. [Ministry of War (Sith Empire)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ministry_of_War_%28Sith_Empire%29)
    - In 3640 BBY its personnel under Lord Razer took over Imperial Intelligence headquarters to transfer its assets. [Ministry of War (Sith Empire) §Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ministry_of_War_%28Sith_Empire%29)
    - **Minister of War:** Shareis held the post. In the Cold War Grand Moff Rycus Kilran was "second only to the Minister of War". [Minister of War (Sith Empire)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Minister_of_War_%28Sith_Empire%29)
  - **Ministry of Logistics:**
    - Handled war logistics and finance, the nationalized war factories and the agricultural worlds feeding the military. [Ministry of Logistics](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ministry_of_Logistics)
    - Supervised slave labour. [Ministry of Logistics](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ministry_of_Logistics)
    - Ran civilian transport and trade, including Dromund Kaas transit, which was open only to citizens with passes. [Ministry of Logistics](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ministry_of_Logistics)
    - The Imperial Citizenship Bureau, which kept records of all citizens, fell under it. [Imperial Citizenship Bureau](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Citizenship_Bureau)
    - Its minister "technically" answered to Production and Logistics, but could report to other Councilors, such as Defense of the Empire on military matters. [Ministry of Logistics](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ministry_of_Logistics)
    - **Ministers:** Shullis Khamarr (3660 BBY to ?), Davidge (3640 BBY), Gelmid Lorman (? to 3630 BBY). [Minister of Logistics](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Minister_of_Logistics)
    - **Surrender to Zakuul:** it was the only department "functional enough" to negotiate the surrender. Darth Vowrawn, as head of its Sphere, had to represent the Sith before the Eternal Throne. [Ministry of Logistics §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ministry_of_Logistics)
    - **Lorman's plot:** in 3630 BBY Lorman plotted with Leontyne Saresh to kill Acina and the Outlander. [Ministry of Logistics §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ministry_of_Logistics)
  - **Other agencies:**
    - **Outreach Bureau** (later Imperial Outreach Division), under the Ministry of Intelligence: news and propaganda. It took over HoloNet feeds for months in the Cold War. [Outreach Bureau](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Outreach_Bureau); [Ministry of Intelligence (Sith Empire)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ministry_of_Intelligence_%28Sith_Empire%29)
    - **Imperial Conquest Consolidation Corps:** an Army division that secured newly conquered worlds. It was headed by Colonel Vrain in the Cold War and controlled by the Sphere of Military Strategy. [Imperial Conquest Consolidation Corps](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Conquest_Consolidation_Corps)
- **Related:** Imperial Military; Imperial Intelligence
- **Uncertainty:** [conflict] Whom the Minister of War answered to: the three military Councilors [Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War) §Ministry of War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_%28Post%E2%80%93Great_Hyperspace_War%29) versus "all members of the Dark Council", working most closely with the three [Ministry of War (Sith Empire)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ministry_of_War_%28Sith_Empire%29).

### Imperial Military
- **Type:** institution
- **Era:** founded after the Great Hyperspace War (on Dromund Kaas); fought 3681–3653 BBY and from c. 3641 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Service:**
    - Every non-Force-sensitive was conscripted on reaching adulthood and served until retirement or disability. A soldier typically served 25–30 years. [Imperial Military (Sith Empire) §Organization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Military_%28Sith_Empire%29); [Imperial Military (Sith Empire) §Army](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Military_%28Sith_Empire%29)
    - Children of military families had to earn their own place.
  - **Armour and identification (added (lore batch 18)):** [conflict] on armour colour, resolved per ruling 4: gray is the standard; the sourced variants are unit markings (black and white for commandos, green for grenadiers; black and red also attested); the Imperial Guard wear white with red. Officers carried code cylinders. [Imperial Military (Sith Empire) §Organization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Military_%28Sith_Empire%29)
  - **Army ranks:** Private, Specialist, Corporal, Sergeant, Ensign, Lieutenant, Captain, Major, Colonel, General. Units ran from fire team to division (20,000–40,000). An "Assault Group" or "Operations Group" of over 40,000 served under a Moff or a high-ranking Sith Lord. [Imperial Military (Sith Empire) §Army](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Military_%28Sith_Empire%29)
  - **Navy ranks:** Private, Specialist, Corporal, Petty Officer, Ensign, Lieutenant, (Commander), Captain, Group Captain, Commodore, Admiral. [Imperial Military (Sith Empire) §Navy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Military_%28Sith_Empire%29); [Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War) §Navy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_%28Post%E2%80%93Great_Hyperspace_War%29)
  - **High command:**
    - Above the generals and admirals stood the Moffs and Imperial High Command, answering to the Ministry of War. [Imperial Military (Sith Empire) §Organization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Military_%28Sith_Empire%29)
    - The Grand Moff answered directly to the Minister of War, had special privileges and often close ties to the Dark Council. The title was first made for Odile Vaiken. [Imperial Military (Sith Empire) §Organization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Military_%28Sith_Empire%29)
    - Known Grand Moffs: Vaiken, Zellos, Rycus Kilran, Ilyan Regus. [Grand Moff/Legends §Sith Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grand_Moff/Legends)
  - **Officers and Sith:**
    - The military was "particularly close" with Darth Malgus, which was "highly uncommon" for a Sith. [Imperial Military (Sith Empire)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Military_%28Sith_Empire%29)
    - Some Intelligence agents secretly undermined Sith efforts or disobeyed orders. [Imperial Intelligence (Sith Empire) §Reputation](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Intelligence_%28Sith_Empire%29)
  - **Aliens:** after Malgus' attempted coup at Ilum in 3640 BBY, the Empire began admitting aliens to its forces to secure their loyalty, despite humanocentrist objections (Grand Moff Ilyan Regus). [Imperial Military (Sith Empire) §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Military_%28Sith_Empire%29)
- **Related:** Ministry of War; Moffs (next entry)
- **Uncertainty:**

### Imperial Intelligence and Sith Intelligence
- **Type:** institutions
- **Era:** Imperial Intelligence from the founding on Dromund Kaas to 3640 BBY; Sith Intelligence from 3640 BBY to after 3627 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Place in the state:**
    - The Ministry of Intelligence answered to the Sphere of Imperial Intelligence. Imperial Intelligence was its largest organ; the Outreach Bureau was another. [Ministry of Intelligence (Sith Empire)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ministry_of_Intelligence_%28Sith_Empire%29)
    - The Keeper ran day-to-day operations and the Operations Division. [Ministry of Intelligence (Sith Empire)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ministry_of_Intelligence_%28Sith_Empire%29)
  - **Ranks** [Imperial Intelligence (Sith Empire) §Ranks](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Intelligence_%28Sith_Empire%29):
    - Informer: fixed-post spies.
    - Cipher: assassination, infiltration and sabotage.
    - Minder: analysis and internal security.
    - Fixer: technical work.
    - Watcher: analysis, reporting to Keeper.
    - Keeper: operational leader.
    - Minister of Intelligence: the "true leader". Besides the Imperial Guard, it was "the highest position a non-Sith can achieve".
  - **Code names:** real names of Ciphers, Minders, Fixers and Watchers were classified. Numbers marked individuals, not rank. Only Basic speakers received official designations. [Imperial Intelligence (Sith Empire) §Ranks](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Intelligence_%28Sith_Empire%29)
  - **Ciphers:** "on par with the ranking of Sith aside from Sith Lords and the Dark Council". [Cipher Agent §Equipment and training](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cipher_Agent)
  - **Keeper's powers:**
    - Approved "every infiltration into enemy territory and every execution of an enemy of the state". [Keeper (Imperial Intelligence) §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Keeper_%28Imperial_Intelligence%29)
    - Could be "swiftly replaced" by the Minister or the Council. [Keeper (Imperial Intelligence) §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Keeper_%28Imperial_Intelligence%29)
    - In the Cold War the Keeper (a human male) became Minister, and Shara Jenn (formerly Watcher Two) became Keeper. [Keeper (Imperial Intelligence) §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Keeper_%28Imperial_Intelligence%29)
  - **Minister's autonomy:** the Minister kept "total control" of the agency "so long as it represented Sith interests". In the Cold War he served under Darth Jadus, then Darth Zhorrid. [Minister of Intelligence (Sith Empire) §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Minister_of_Intelligence_%28Sith_Empire%29)
  - **Dissolution:**
    - The Star Cabal got the Operations branch dissolved. Its people were drafted into the armies of individual Sith Lords and officers. [Imperial Intelligence (Sith Empire) §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Intelligence_%28Sith_Empire%29)
    - In 3640 BBY the minister's post was abolished, and the new Sith Intelligence reported directly to the Dark Council. [Minister of Intelligence (Sith Empire) §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Minister_of_Intelligence_%28Sith_Empire%29)
  - **Sith Intelligence:**
    - Overseen directly by the Sith, unlike its semi-independent predecessor. [Sith Intelligence §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Intelligence)
    - After Yavin 4, Marr made Lana Beniko its minister. [Sith Intelligence §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Intelligence); [Minister of Sith Intelligence](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Minister_of_Sith_Intelligence)
    - It moved operations to Ziost and was left in disarray when Vitiate destroyed the planet. [Sith Intelligence §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Intelligence)
    - By 3631 BBY most of its assets were gone, and Acina relied on technological surveillance. [Sith Intelligence §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Intelligence)
    - Defectors attacked the Alliance in 3630 BBY. [Sith Intelligence §Order of Zildrog campaign](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Intelligence)
    - Under Darth Xarion it ran the Nova Blades raids on Dantooine (c. 3627 BBY). [Sith Intelligence §Third Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Intelligence)
- **Related:** Ministry of Intelligence; Republic SIS (Part 2)
- **Uncertainty:**
  - [conflict] To whom the Minister of Intelligence reported: "directly to the Dark Council" [Imperial Intelligence (Sith Empire) §Ranks](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Intelligence_%28Sith_Empire%29) versus the Councilor of the Sphere [Sphere of Imperial Intelligence §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sphere_of_Imperial_Intelligence); [Minister of Intelligence (Sith Empire) §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Minister_of_Intelligence_%28Sith_Empire%29).
