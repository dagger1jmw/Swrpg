Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 24, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends), as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
Part 3 covers the Dark Wars (3,955–3,951 BBY): the Sith Civil War, the Sith Triumvirate and the First Jedi Purge (3,954–3,951 BBY), Revan's departure and the Exile's campaign; it then holds the back sections for all three parts. Key facts: the Triumvirate (Darth Traya, Darth Nihilus, Darth Sion) ruled from Malachor V by 3,954 BBY; Sion and Nihilus stripped Traya of the Force and exiled her in 3,954 BBY; Nihilus destroyed all life on Katarr in 3,952 BBY; in 3,951 BBY Meetra Surik killed Nihilus at Telos, Sion and Kreia on Malachor V, and the planet was destroyed. Column key as in Part 1.

## ══════════════════════════════════════════

### Overview
The Dark Wars (3,955–3,951 BBY) were the conflicts that grew from the defeat of Malak's empire, among them the Sith Civil War and the First Jedi Purge; they ended in a Republic–Jedi victory, the Triumvirate's dissolution and the absorption of the Sith remnants into the Republic [Dark Wars](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Wars). The Sith Civil War (3,956–3,950 BBY) fractured the empire into warlord kingdoms; many warlords united under the Triumvirate, the fighting resumed after its leaders died, and by 3,950 BBY the remnants had disintegrated and the Republic reclaimed Sith space [Sith Civil War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Civil_War).

**State of the Republic:** Billions were dead and refugees crowded Nar Shaddaa; the Navy was understrength, remote worlds such as Dantooine and Onderon were slipping, and the Exchange and the Hutts built mini-empires [Jedi Civil War §Aftermath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Civil_War). Supreme Chancellor Tol Cressa launched rebuilding programmes, notably the Telosian Restoration Project [3955 BBY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/3955_BBY), and was still in office in 3,946 BBY [Tol Cressa](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tol_Cressa). The Kanz Disorders went on without Republic intervention [3955 BBY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/3955_BBY).

**State of the Jedi Order:** "Barely a hundred" Jedi survived the civil war [Jedi Civil War §Aftermath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Civil_War); the Purge then nearly destroyed the Order, which survived in exile [First Jedi Purge](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/First_Jedi_Purge). The Temple on Coruscant stood empty [First Jedi Purge](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/First_Jedi_Purge). Its rebirth came through the Jedi the Exile trained [Jedi Civil War §Aftermath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Civil_War).

Surik "command[ed] a third of the fleet" and "personally activate[d]" the Mass Shadow Generator — Revan gave her half his forces, and one account has her order Bao-Dur to activate it [Mandalorian Wars §Republic counteroffensive](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalorian_Wars) [Ruled (Jared, 24 Nov 2026): on her order Bao-Dur activates it]. Fett is credited with the bombardment of Serroco; it was Mandalore the Ultimate's decision [Mandalorian Wars §The Mandalorian Invasion](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalorian_Wars) (Jebble was Fett's [Mandalorian Wars §The Mandalorian Invasion](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalorian_Wars)).
