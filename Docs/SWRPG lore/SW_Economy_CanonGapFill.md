# Star Wars Economy — Canon Gap-Fill Prices (tier 3, [canon])

*File: SW_Economy_CanonGapFill.md · first version · compiled 23 Nov 2026 (America/New_York) from Canon pages on Wookieepedia (no /Legends) via the MediaWiki API.*

## Summary
Under this topic's special Canon rule, Canon figures are collected only as the **lowest tier, tagged [canon], to fill gaps**: an item that **has a Legends page but no Legends price**. Where Canon gives a different figure for an item that Legends also prices, **Legends wins** and the Canon figure is listed under Not used with the reason.

**Counts:** 146 [canon] gap-fill figures (158 before 12 broken rows with no price were deleted, Jared, D-464) (items with a Legends page but no Legends price). 198 Canon figures agree with the Legends price (within 5%) and are not repeated. 85 Canon figures differ from the Legends price and are in Not used. 656 Canon-only cost fields (items with no Legends page at all, mostly FFG gear from 2016–2020) are not listed (see Decisions needed).

Most Canon figures here are themselves game stats from Fantasy Flight Games books published after early 2016 (Canon era); they are labelled with the book and its publication date. A few come from Canon novels, reference books or *Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes*/other games, labelled accordingly. Only infobox "Cost" fields were read for Canon; Canon article text (menus, fares) was not scanned.

**Anchors:** none of the main anchors (meal, room, blaster, starfighter, freighter, droid, bacta) needed Canon gap-fill; all have Legends tier-1 or tier-2 figures.
