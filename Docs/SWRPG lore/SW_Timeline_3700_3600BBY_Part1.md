# SWRPG Galaxy Timeline, c. 3,700–3,600 BBY (Part 1 of 3): Old Republic Recovery and the Great Galactic War

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 21, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
This three-part file rebuilds the c. 3,700–3,600 BBY slice of `SW_Legends_Galaxy_Timeline.md` as one sourced row per event: the last pre-war years, the Great Galactic War (3,681–3,653 BBY), the Cold War, the Galactic War, the war against Zakuul and the Third Galactic War of the Legacy of the Sith years (the last dated Legends year found is c. 3,622 BBY). Part 1 (this file) covers 3,700–3,653 BBY and has a "state of the galaxy" paragraph for each year 3,665–3,655 BBY. Part 2 covers 3,653–3,622 BBY. Part 3 has "Changes from the previous file", conflicts with the topic files, Decisions needed and Sources. The key facts for a 3,661 BBY start: the Mandalorian blockade of the Hydian Way began in 3,661 BBY and was broken in 3,660 BBY (one page says 3,661 BBY [conflict]); Mandalore the Lesser held the title from 3,667 BBY; Geonosis and Minos were still active fronts (Rhen Var came later, c. 3,654–3,653 BBY); and the war ended with the Sacking and the Treaty of Coruscant in 3,653 BBY.

## Entries
How to read the tables: the era headings, the ═ rule lines, "### Overview" and the "#### <year> — Year N" subheadings are kept from the old file so the developer's parser can read them. The columns are the new ones requested: Date | Event | Type | Place | Who | Source. Type is one of: War (battles, campaigns, treaties), Control (a key world changes hands), Leader (leadership change), Order (Jedi or Sith institutions, academies, councils), Birth, Death, Tech (technology and culture), Background (plagues, disasters, riots, crimes). Births and deaths come from `SW_Characters_09_GGW_ColdWar_Index` and its parts (no new research), cited to the same Wookieepedia pages. Class-story protagonists appear by title only. Where a topic file already tells the story, the row gives only the date and points to that file. The year labels follow Year = 3,681 − year: 3,681 BBY is Year 0, when the war begins (lore batch 20, ruling 2).

### Overview
A twenty-eight-year war between the Galactic Republic and the Sith Empire, from 3,681 BBY to 3,653 BBY [Great Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Galactic_War). The Emperor had planned the invasion since as early as 3,960 BBY [Great Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Galactic_War). The narrative is in `SW_Great_Galactic_War_Part1–3`; the rows below keep only dates, places and people. Undated battles of the war (Agamar, Utapau, Dathomir, Ilum, the Devastation of Manaan) sit in the last table of this era, not under a year.
