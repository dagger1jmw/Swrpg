## Summary
Malachor V is an ancient Sith world in the Outer Rim. Its Trayus Academy sat over an upwelling of dark side energy, and the planet corrupted those who walked on it: a dark nexus. The Mass Shadow Generator in the last battle of the Mandalorian Wars (3960 BBY) left it a shattered wasteland and a wound in the Force. Revan, then Darth Traya and her apprentices, used the academy until the generator was set off again in 3951 BBY, destroying the planet. By the Old Republic era it's a field of asteroids and wreckage, surveyed and picked over by treasure hunters.

## Entries

### Malachor V (the planet)
- **Type:** location (planet; later asteroids and wreckage)
- **Era:** Sith colony from before the fall of the Infinite Empire; destroyed 3951 BBY; remains still there by 3640 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Location:** an Outer Rim planet in the Malachor system [Malachor V](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Malachor_V). Grid S-4, Chorlian sector, Trans-Hydian (SW_Planets_Travel_OldRepublic, Part 3).
  - **Before the cataclysm:** fertile and hospitable [Malachor V](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Malachor_V).
  - **The dark side:** Kreia calls it a place where the dark side runs strong, "something of the Sith" but fuelled by war, that corrupts everyone who walks on it [Malachor V](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Malachor_V).
  - **After the first cataclysm (3960 BBY):** cracked and twisted, with jagged cliffs and constant lightning storms, home to monstrous storm beasts. Its unstable gravity made it dangerous for ships to stay long [Malachor V](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Malachor_V).
  - **After the second (3951 BBY):** destroyed, leaving asteroids and wreckage [Malachor V](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Malachor_V).
- **Related:** Malachor III, in the same system: a Sith Empire member long before the Cold War, with the tomb of Terrak Morrhage. Parkanas Tark is lost there c. 3665 BBY (SW_Planets_Travel_OldRepublic; the roster).
- **Uncertainty:** whether any dark presence remains in the wreckage after 3951 BBY [unclear] (see Decisions needed).

### The Trayus Academy and the Trayus Core
- **Type:** location (Sith academy; site-level dark nexus)
- **Era:** built "thousands of years" before the Mandalorian Wars; destroyed 3951 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - A Sith Empire built the academy "thousands of years" before the Mandalorian Wars [Malachor V](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Malachor_V).
  - **The Trayus Core:** the innermost sanctum, a claw-shaped altar suspended over a powerful upwelling of dark side energy and held by three bridges [Trayus Core, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/trayus_core).
  - **Revan's use:** Revan used Malachor's dark side energies to bind his most trusted followers to his will, and the place's corrupting influence weakened Jedi so they could be turned. That's how the Sith assassin order was founded [Trayus Academy, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/Trayus_Academy).
  - **The academy survived the first cataclysm.** In the second, the Trayus Core imploded as the planet and the academy were destroyed, releasing the dark side energies it held [Trayus Core, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/trayus_core).
- **Related:** SW_Force_Nexus_Vergence (Malachor V).
- **Uncertainty:** none.

### Wound in the Force
- **Type:** Force phenomenon
- **Era:** 3960 BBY to its sealing in 3951 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - The battle created a wound in the Force [Battle of Malachor V, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/battle_of_malachor_v). Malachor V is the standard example of a place-centred wound [Wound in the Force/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wound_in_the_Force/Legends).
  - The generator's reactivation destroyed the planet and sealed the wound [Disturbance in the Force, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/disturbance_in_the_force).
- **Uncertainty:** none.
