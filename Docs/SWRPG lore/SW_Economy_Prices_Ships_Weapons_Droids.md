# Star Wars Legends Economy — Prices of Ships, Weapons, Armour and Droids (tier 1, in-universe)

*File: SW_Economy_Prices_Ships_Weapons_Droids.md · first version · compiled 23 Nov 2026 (America/New_York) from Legends pages on Wookieepedia via the MediaWiki API.*

## Summary
This file collects **in-universe Legends price figures** for starships (purchase, value, running costs such as docking, fuel, repairs and navigation fees), vehicles, weapons and armour, droids, and personal equipment. It includes infobox "Cost" fields only where the page sources them to an in-universe work (e.g. *The Official Star Wars Fact File*, the Essential Guides, *Death Star Owner's Technical Manual*); cost fields sourced to WEG, WotC, FFG or game shops are game stats and are in **SW_Economy_GamePriceLists_Part1–Part9.md**.

**Counts in this file:** 128 dated tier-1 figures, 13 tier-1 figures with no stated date, 0 [gray] figures; 6 in-universe vs game-stat clashes listed under Conflicts.

**Key anchors (in-universe):**
- **Blaster pistol:** DH-17 550–800 cr on the black market (Fact File, GCW era); typical blaster rifle c. 1,000 cr legally (see SW_Weapons_Armor_AllEras.md). Thermal detonator 2,000 cr (Fact File).
- **Starfighter:** T-65 X-wing 149,999 cr new / 90,000 used; BTL Y-wing 134,999 new / 65,000 used (Fact File, GCW era); TIE/D Defender 300,000 cr new (*Essential Guide to Vehicles and Vessels*; [conflict] with game lines). The game anchor Z-95 80,000 new / 45,000 used is in the game lists (Saga, 2007).
- **Freighter:** YT-1000 85,000 cr new / 20,000 used (*Millennium Falcon Owner's Workshop Manual*); the submarine-capable freighter *Aay'han* was bought used for 80,000 cr (c. 21 BBY). The game anchor YT-1300 100,000 new / 25,000 used is in the game lists.
- **Charter vs purchase:** Han Solo's 17,000-cr Alderaan charter (0 BBY) — see SW_Economy_Prices_Goods.md.
- **Docking:** Docking Bay 87, Mos Eisley, 30 cr a day (c. 19 BBY, *Kenobi*); Ahto City docking 100 / 500 cr and Dreshdae 25 cr (KOTOR / KOTOR II dialogue, 3956 / 3951 BBY).
- **Droid:** a 3PO protocol droid c. 3,000 cr on the open market; R2 units 4,000–10,000 cr (c. 18 ABY); MD-series medical droid c. 2,000; T3-M4 bought for 2,000 cr on Taris (3956 BBY, IU-game).

Currencies are reported as written; see SW_Economy_Currencies for conversions.
