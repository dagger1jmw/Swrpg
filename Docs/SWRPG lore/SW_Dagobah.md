## Summary
Dagobah is a remote swamp world in the Outer Rim's Sluis sector, with no sentient civilisation. Its Dark Side Cave formed around 700 BBY, when a Jedi killed a Bpfasshi Dark Jedi leader there. Yoda later lived near the cave because it masked his presence, and Luke Skywalker trained there. Before c. 700 BBY the cave isn't a dark nexus.

### The Dark Side Cave
- **Type:** location (site-level dark nexus)
- **Era:** a dark nexus from c. 700 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Origin, c. 700 BBY:** the Jedi Council sent a team after Bpfasshi Dark Jedi. The Jedi Knight Minch tracked their leader to a cave on Dagobah and killed him there [Minch, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/minch); [Duel on Dagobah (700 BBY), archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/duel_on_dagobah_(700_bby)).
  - His blood and death tainted the cave with the dark side, and over time it became a focus of malevolent Force energy [Minch, archivum](https://www.archivum.wiki/article/minch). The place absorbed his energies and became the home of the Dark Spirit [Dark Spirit, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/Dark_Spirit).
  - **Effects:** the cave lies deep in the equatorial swamps and holds a vergence and the apparition of a dark Jedi. Those who enter often have visions of the past, the future, or possible futures [Dark Side Cave, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/Dark_Side_Cave).
  - **Masking:** in the Purge, Yoda found that living near the cave helped neutralise his strong light side signature, so the Sith couldn't find him [Dark Side Cave, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/Dark_Side_Cave).
  - **Luke Skywalker** trained there [Vergence/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vergence/Legends). The Trials article counts the cave as his Trial of the Spirit (SW_Jedi_Training_AllEras_Part2).
- **Related:** SW_Force_Nexus_Vergence (Dagobah, decision 3).
- **Uncertainty:** none.
