# SWRPG Galaxy Timeline, 36,453–5,001 BBY (Part 2 of 3): The Republic's First Eighteen Thousand Years

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 24, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends), as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
Part 2 covers 25,053–7,004 BBY: the founding of the Galactic Republic (25,053 BBY), the Jedi on Ossus joining it (24,953 BBY), the First Great Schism and the Tionese War, the Great Manifest and Indecta eras with the Alsakan Conflicts, the Hutt Cataclysms, the Pius Dea crusades and their end at Uquine (10,966 BBY), and the long quiet of the Ductavis, Rianitus and Subterra periods. The Second Great Schism, the Hundred-Year Darkness, the Exiles, the Sith Empire and the Sith Golden Age under Marka Ragnos (7,003–5,001 BBY) open Part 3, which also holds the back sections.

## FOUNDING OF THE REPUBLIC AND THE EXPANSIONIST ERA — 25,053–20,001 BBY
## ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

### Overview
With the Rakata gone, Core worlds reverse-engineer their hyperdrives, fight the Unification Wars, and sign the Galactic Constitution in 25,053 BBY with Coruscant as capital. The Perlemian Trade Route reaches Ossus and Tion; the Jedi, contacted on Ossus, pledge themselves to the Republic (24,953 BBY) but stay on Ossus. Within the Republic's first five centuries come the First Great Schism (c. 24,500 BBY) and the Hutt "Devouring" of the Tionese colonies; the century-long Tionese War follows (c. 24,000–23,900 BBY). **Blank centuries:** after the Tionese War almost nothing is dated until 20,000 BBY: only c. 23,643 (Uphrades volcanism), c. 22,800 (Ilum found) and c. 22,000 BBY (Nubia settled).

## GREAT MANIFEST PERIOD AND INDECTA ERA — 20,000–15,001 BBY
## ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

### Overview
A boom of colonisation into the Slice and the new Expansion Region, led by Alsakan, Corellia and Coruscant, turns into rivalry. From 17,018 BBY Alsakan and Coruscant fight the first two of **seventeen Alsakan Conflicts** (the last in 3,017 BBY), mostly in the Expansion Region over corporate colonisation rights; Coruscant usually wins. In 15,500 BBY the Republic meets the Star Dragons. **Blank stretches:** 19,997–18,780 BBY and 18,780–17,018 BBY have one or two events each; c. 16,700–16,200 BBY and c. 15,400–15,000 BBY are empty.
