# Star Wars Legends: Dromund Kaas and Kaas City, c. 3700–3653 BBY, Part 1: The planet, Kaas City and the Citadel

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 21, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
- **The planet:** Dromund Kaas was a lost Sith colony, rediscovered by Vitiate's exodus in 4980 BBY. It is a jungle-and-ocean world whose sky was filled with near-constant lightning and rain by the Emperor's dark-side rituals. [Dromund Kaas §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dromund_Kaas/Legends); [Tenebrae §Rebuilding an empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrae)
- **Kaas City:** cleared from the jungle by Odile Vaiken's army and built around the Imperial Citadel. The Citadel held the Emperor's Sith Sanctum, the Dark Council and the ministries. [Dromund Kaas wilderness campaign](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dromund_Kaas_wilderness_campaign); [Imperial Citadel (Sith Empire)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Citadel_(Sith_Empire))
- **Dark Temple:** to the west, sealed for the whole window. [Dark Temple §Early history and closure](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Temple)
- **Attested in the window:** only 3678 BBY victory parades, air taxis, the Citadel's chambers and the Council chamber. [Darth Thanaton §Hunting for power](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Thanaton)
- **Other periods:** street-level detail comes either from the Revan novel (c. 3954–3950 BBY: customs, Larvit, the Nexus Room, Nyriss's stronghold) or from SWTOR's Cold War city (hubs, markets, monuments). It is labelled accordingly.

### Dromund Kaas: the planet
- **Type:** location (planet, Imperial capital world)
- **Era:** colony of the old Sith Empire; lost before c. 5000 BBY; rediscovered 4980 BBY; capital through the window and after.
- **Key facts:**
  - **Location:**
    - third planet of the Dromund system, in the Sith Worlds region of the Esstran sector (Outer Rim), inside the Stygian Caldera nebula;
    - two moons;
    - on the Kamat Krote hyperlane between Jaguada and Bosthirda.

    [Dromund Kaas §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dromund_Kaas/Legends); [Kamat Krote](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kamat_Krote)
  - **Name:** the system is called the "Dromund system" on its Legends page. The Stygian Caldera made space look reddish-brown from inside the system. [Dromund system §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dromund_system)
  - **Surface:** largely jungles and oceans by the time of the Great Hyperspace War. Apart from Kaas City and a few other sites it stayed jungle, ocean and swamp. [Dromund Kaas §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dromund_Kaas/Legends)
  - **Storms:** the atmosphere was "heavily charged with electricity". Lightning was near-constant under an almost permanently clouded sky. This was a result of the Emperor's experiments in forbidden uses of the dark side. [Dromund Kaas §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dromund_Kaas/Legends)
  - **How the storms started:** the Emperor's rituals in the Dark Temple "soon" affected the atmosphere, filling the skies with perpetual rainstorms and lightning. [Tenebrae §Rebuilding an empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrae)
  - **"Force Storms":** another page speaks of "tainted Force Storms" raging in the atmosphere from the Emperor's rituals. [Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War) §Dromund Kaas](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_(Post–Great_Hyperspace_War))
  - **Rain:** near-constant in the Revan novel (c. 3954 BBY): Scourge and Sechel walk "through the rain that was constantly falling", and a speeder ride is through "drizzling rain". [Scourge (Sith) §Political machinations](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scourge_(Sith)); [Scourge (Sith) §Confronting the Emperor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scourge_(Sith))
  - **Energy from the storms:** by the Cold War (post-window), lightning pylons in the Riverfall Wilds drew power from the "eternal storms" for Kaas City. [Riverfall Wilds](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Riverfall_Wilds)
  - **Species claim:** Vitiate claimed Dromund Kaas was the true homeworld of the Sith species. The claim is unverified; the Revan author said it could be propaganda [BTS: Drew Karpyshyn, per Wookieepedia]. [Dromund Kaas §Early history](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dromund_Kaas/Legends); [Dromund Kaas §Behind the scenes](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dromund_Kaas/Legends)
- **Related:** Dark Temple; Kaas City; Dromund system.
- **Uncertainty:**
  - [conflict] Placement: Jedi vs. Sith once places the planet in the Thurra system, but elsewhere (and The Dark Forces Saga) in the Dromund system. [Dromund Kaas §Behind the scenes](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dromund_Kaas/Legends) Best fit: the Dromund system.
  - Later swamps (after the Galactic War) are not this era. [Dromund Kaas §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dromund_Kaas/Legends)

### History of Imperial settlement (4980 BBY to the window)
- **Type:** history
- **Era:** 4999–3653 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Finding the world:** after the Great Hyperspace War, Vitiate's researchers found the location; the Dromund system page dates this to 4999 BBY. He then drained Nathema and led a 20-year wandering exodus that "rediscovered" Dromund Kaas in 4980 BBY. [Dromund Kaas §Capital of the Sith Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dromund_Kaas/Legends); [Dromund system §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dromund_system)
  - **Arrival:** a scout ship found the planet, and on the surface the Emperor declared a new Empire "from the ashes of the old". He offered Sith and non-Sith servants an orderly, powerful society that would one day take revenge on the Republic. [Tenebrae §Rebuilding an empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrae)
  - **Government:** a twelve-member Dark Council was created soon after arrival. The Emperor retreated into seclusion in the new Imperial Citadel and appeared in public less and less. [Tenebrae §Rebuilding an empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrae); [Dromund Kaas §Capital of the Sith Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dromund_Kaas/Legends)
  - **Clearing the jungle:** Odile Vaiken founded the Imperial Military and led the wilderness campaign to clear jungle and wildlife for the capital. His troops wore black armour and carried blaster rifles. [Dromund Kaas wilderness campaign §The campaign](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dromund_Kaas_wilderness_campaign); [Odile Vaiken §Exiled](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Odile_Vaiken)
  - **Training Regiment:** the Imperial Training Regiment was made up of non-Force-sensitives and helped clear the wilderness. [Imperial Training Regiment](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Training_Regiment)
  - **The first Citadel:**
    - building began soon after the 4980 BBY arrival, resembling the Korriban academy and the Ziost citadel;
    - it was destroyed in 4043 BBY in a Kaggath between Darth Qalar and Darth Victun;
    - the Emperor designed its replacement and locked Darth Nostrem in its labyrinthine depths to test them.

    [Imperial Citadel (Sith Empire) §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Citadel_(Sith_Empire))
  - **Dark Temple:** the Emperor expanded the Dark Temple. He sealed it not long after 4606 BBY. [Dark Temple §Early history and closure](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Temple)
  - **The Revan years (c. 3960–3950 BBY):**
    - Revan and Malak reached Dromund Kaas c. 3960 BBY;
    - Revan was imprisoned at Nyriss's estate in 3954 BBY;
    - Surik's arrival and the Purge of the Dark Council followed.

    [Dromund Kaas §Capital of the Sith Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dromund_Kaas/Legends)
  - **Unrest after the Purge (c. 3950 BBY):** riots across Kaas City. The Emperor imposed martial law and a planetary curfew, and locked down all traffic. Troops restored order, and the population stayed "fiercely loyal" up to the Great Galactic War. [Purge of the Dark Council §Public unrest](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Purge_of_the_Dark_Council); [Purge of the Dark Council §Aftermath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Purge_of_the_Dark_Council)
  - **Before the war:** the Empire accelerated war plans "in the three centuries that followed the Jedi Civil War". [Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War) §Preparations for war](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_(Post–Great_Hyperspace_War))
  - **Wartime safety:** in 3681 BBY the war began; Dromund Kaas "remained safe from Republic reprisal" throughout it. [Dromund Kaas](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dromund_Kaas/Legends); [Tenebrae §A renewed war](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrae)
- **Related:** Imperial Citadel; Dark Temple; Odile Vaiken (Part 2 NPCs).
- **Uncertainty:**
  - [conflict] Size of the exodus: "hundreds of his countrymen" on "eight transports" [Odile Vaiken §Exiled](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Odile_Vaiken) vs "the remaining people of the Sith Empire" [Dromund Kaas §Capital of the Sith Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dromund_Kaas/Legends).
  - [conflict] Exodus departure: from Korriban [Dromund system §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dromund_system); [Tenebrae §Rebuilding an empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrae) vs after the Nathema ritual. These fit together: Nathema came first, then the fleet departed from Korriban.

### Dromund Kaas during the Great Galactic War (window)
- **Type:** era snapshot
- **Era:** 3681–3653 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Victory celebrations (c. 3678 BBY):** Teneb Kel arrived "during a time of celebration". The Empire marked recent victories in Kaas City with "parades and ceremonial observances". He watched the revelry from an air taxi on the way to the Sith Sanctum in the Imperial Citadel. [Darth Thanaton §Hunting for power](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Thanaton)
  - **Red Legion:** during a celebration in Kaas City in 3678 BBY, Darth Ekkage personally branded members of the Red Legion. [Darth Ekkage §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Ekkage)
  - **The Emperor's Voice:** in 3678 BBY it was a young Human male, present in the Citadel. [Unidentified Human Emperor's Voice (3678 BBY) §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Human_Emperor's_Voice_(3678_BBY)); [Tenebrae §A renewed war](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrae)
  - **Calypho:** held prisoner in the Council headquarters on Dromund Kaas, then tortured for visions. [Calypho §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Calypho)
  - **The Emperor's chambers:** the Great Hyperspace War Bas-Relief, or a similar rendering, hung on a wall there in 3678 BBY. [Great Hyperspace War Bas-Relief §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Hyperspace_War_Bas-Relief); [Imperial Citadel (Sith Empire) §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Citadel_(Sith_Empire))
  - **Council seat:** the Dark Council chamber on Dromund Kaas was smaller than the one on Korriban, but was the Council's headquarters during the Great Galactic War. [Dark Council chamber (Dromund Kaas)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Council_chamber_(Dromund_Kaas))
  - **Late-war strain:** "as it dragged on", the war forced the Empire "to strip entire worlds of their resources and peoples" to fuel the war machine. [Tenebrae §A renewed war](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrae)
  - **Malgus's childhood (pre-war):** young Force-sensitives were sent to Dromund Kaas's Sith Academy. Malgus was born on Dromund Kaas in 3700 BBY. [Darth Malgus §Early life](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Malgus)
- **Uncertainty:** no source describes the city's physical changes during 3681–3653 BBY. [inferred: none recorded]

### Kaas City: overview and districts
- **Type:** location (capital city)
- **Era:** founded in the Sith's first century on Dromund Kaas (after 4980 BBY); capital through the window; ruins by 44 ABY.
- **Key facts:**
  - **Growth:** grew from a small settlement deep in the jungle into a vast metropolis, home to many Sith Lords, the Emperor and the Dark Council. [Kaas City §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kaas_City)
  - **Purpose:** built "deep in the jungle" within the Sith's first century there, as a testament to re-establishing the lost Imperial society. [Imperial Citadel (Sith Empire)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Citadel_(Sith_Empire))
  - **Six hubs (post-window):** in the Cold War the city was divided into six districts called "hubs". These are known from a SWTOR map kiosk; only Hub 3 is named. [Kaas City §Layout](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kaas_City); [Hub 3](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hub_3)
  - **Hub 3's office** [conflict]:
    - the Ministry of Travel's office [Kaas City §Layout](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kaas_City);
    - the Ministry of Logistics office [Hub 3](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hub_3); [Ministry of Logistics §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ministry_of_Logistics).

    Best fit: Ministry of Logistics, which ran public transit (two pages agree).
  - **City walls:** the expansion district lay "just outside the walls of Kaas City". [Kaas City expansion district](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kaas_City_expansion_district)
  - **Expansion Project (post-window):** a major undertaking to build a large extension of the city. [Kaas City Expansion Project](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kaas_City_Expansion_Project)
  - **Government district:** a "government district" is named in the Jedi Knight finale, 3640 BBY (post-window). [Scourge (Sith) §March to doomsday](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scourge_(Sith))
- **Uncertainty:** hub numbering, the full district list and the street layout during the window are not found.

### Imperial Citadel
- **Type:** location (government seat and fortress)
- **Era:**
  - first Citadel: begun after 4980 BBY, destroyed 4043 BBY;
  - second Citadel: designed by the Emperor, standing through the window.
- **Key facts:**
  - **Role:** in the centre of Kaas City. It housed the Emperor and his Dark Council, and was a visible symbol of the Empire's restoration. [Imperial Citadel (Sith Empire)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Citadel_(Sith_Empire)); [Kaas City §Layout](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kaas_City)
  - **Headquarters inside:** the Ministries of War, Intelligence and Logistics, and the Spheres of Influence. The Sphere of Mysteries was supposedly housed with the Sphere of Ancient Knowledge, but did not exist. [Imperial Citadel (Sith Empire)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Citadel_(Sith_Empire)); [Kaas City §Layout](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kaas_City)
  - **Mandalorian enclave:** the Citadel also held a Mandalorian "embassy of sorts". Undated; SWTOR. [Imperial Citadel (Sith Empire)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Citadel_(Sith_Empire))
  - **Sith Sanctum:**
    - the Sith Order's headquarters, "as old as the Imperial Citadel itself";
    - the Emperor's chambers were here, and only a few Sith could enter them;
    - only the Dark Council and the Emperor's personal guard were allowed in.

    [Sith Sanctum §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Sanctum); [Tenebrae §Rebuilding an empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrae); [Sith Order (Sith Empire)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Order_(Sith_Empire))
  - **Imperial Intelligence:** its headquarters was in the Citadel, with a secured section called the Intelligence Archives (searched c. 3642 BBY, post-window). [Imperial Intelligence Headquarters (Dromund Kaas)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Intelligence_Headquarters_(Dromund_Kaas)); [Intelligence Archives](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Intelligence_Archives); [Ministry of War (Sith Empire) §Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ministry_of_War_(Sith_Empire))
  - **Defences:** the second Citadel had "labyrinthine defenses". [Imperial Citadel (Sith Empire) §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Citadel_(Sith_Empire))
  - **Dark Council chamber:** a large central platform with doors at each side, one per Council member. [Dark Council chamber (Dromund Kaas)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Council_chamber_(Dromund_Kaas))
  - **Dark-side pull:** young Scourge felt the dark side "emanated from" the Citadel and drew on its power during training (c. 3964 BBY). [Scourge (Sith) §Early life](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scourge_(Sith))
  - **The Emperor's quarters (c. 3678 BBY):** deep inside, past the Council chambers. Kel was led "even deeper" to the Emperor's sanctum. [Darth Thanaton §Hunting for power](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Thanaton)
- **Uncertainty:**
  - [conflict] Ministry of Logistics headquarters: in the Citadel [Imperial Citadel (Sith Empire)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Citadel_(Sith_Empire)); office in Hub 3 [Ministry of Logistics §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ministry_of_Logistics); or later the Orbital Defense Command Center on Ziost [Ziost §Post-Great Hyperspace War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ziost/Legends). Best fit: Citadel seat, Hub 3 public office, Ziost centre later.
  - No floor plan exists in the sources.

### Dark Temple
- **Type:** location (Sith temple and tomb complex)
- **Era:** built under the old Empire; expanded after 4980 BBY; **sealed from not long after 4606 BBY until c. 3643 BBY**, so it was closed throughout the window.
- **Key facts:**
  - **Origins:** built by Pharshol's followers. Early colonists lived inside it, because it was the only man-made structure on the hostile planet. Aloysius Kallig was entombed there and Ergast was buried nearby. [Dark Temple §Early history and closure](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Temple)
  - **Expansion:** the Emperor ordered a major expansion "by the people of the Empire" while Kaas City was being built. He used it for experiments and to entomb his enemies, turning it into a dark-side nexus. Kel'eth Ur was buried there in 4606 BBY, and the building was sealed soon after. [Dark Temple §Early history and closure](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Temple)
  - **Setting:** on a hill at the western end of the Dark Temple pass, a valley west of Kaas City. [Dark Temple §Layout](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Temple); [Dark Temple pass](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Temple_pass)
  - **Exterior:** dark grey or black stone, an obelisk topped by an all-seeing eye representing the Emperor, and a processional approach with bowed statues. [Dark Temple §Layout](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Temple)
  - **Interior:**
    - an entrance hall;
    - a main hall with bowed statues and a giant Sith pureblood sculpture;
    - a mezzanine;
    - a reliquary;
    - a shrine to Ergast;
    - access tunnels for power conduits.

    [Dark Temple §Layout](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Temple)
  - **Reopening (post-window):** c. 3643 BBY, rogue Sith breached it with at least a hundred slaves, soldiers and archaeologists. Outpost Warden later guarded the pass. [Dark Temple §Opening](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Temple); [Outpost Warden](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Outpost_Warden)
  - **Final battle (post-window):** the Emperor was defeated there in 3640 BBY. [Dark Temple](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Temple)
- **Uncertainty:** the Cold War ruin state of the processional approach may not reflect the window.
