# Martial Cultures and Non-Lightsaber Combat Traditions, All Eras (Part 1 of 3): Unarmed and Melee Fighting Styles

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched 24 Nov 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends), read through the Wookieepedia API, as cited per entry. Rulings of 24 Nov 2026 applied (lore-rulings-2026-11-24c.md, 11–20; D-484).

Part 1 covers unarmed and melee fighting styles. Part 2 covers armed traditions and the warrior cultures of species and orders. Part 3 covers the soldier and assassin training institutions, then the back sections for the whole set (Timeline, Not found, Canon only, Not used, Game notes, Decisions needed, Sources, checks).

## Summary

The Legends galaxy has a few great unarmed arts with a known origin, a teaching method and named masters: Teräs Käsi (Followers of Palawa, Bunduki, before 24,500 BBY), the Echani art (Eshan and the Six Sisters, Old Republic onward, later used by the Emperor's Royal Guard and Imperial Intelligence), Stava (Noghri), K'tara (Rebel SpecForces), K'thri (a sport style), Wrruushi (Wookiees) and the Matukai's Force-assisted body discipline. Around them sit many small styles known from a single source, a named practitioner or a single move. Several of the best-documented styles come mainly from tabletop RPG books (*Hero's Guide*, 2003; *Galaxy at War*, 2009) and from game guides. Where an article's "facts" were worked out from game damage values or skills, this file labels them as game stats. Lightsaber forms, Force traditions, Jedi and Sith training and the Mandalorians are covered in other files and are only cross-referenced here.

### 1. Teräs Käsi ("steel hand")

- **Type:** unarmed martial art (also uses blades and sticks)
- **Era:** founded before 24,500 BBY; still taught after the Clone Wars and into the Galactic Civil War; practitioners to c. 10 ABY (Arden Lyn) and later.
- **Key facts:**
  - Name means "steel hand" in Basic; renowned for lethality. [Teräs Käsi/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Teräs_Käsi/Legends)
  - Founded by the Followers of Palawa, native to Bunduki in the Pacanth Reach; founded before 24,500 BBY (citing *Jedi vs. Sith: The Essential Guide to the Force*). [Teräs Käsi/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Teräs_Käsi/Legends)
  - The Followers developed it to fight the Jedi after their homeworld, Palawa, was destroyed in a long-ago war involving the Jedi Order (Jedi Council). [Followers of Palawa](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Followers_of_Palawa)
  - Knowledge of the art led many Followers to fall to the dark side [note: *Star Wars Gamer* 5, "The Emperor's Pawns"]. [Teräs Käsi/Legends, History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Teräs_Käsi/Legends)
  - **How learned:** students were sent to the Followers, who also taught history, literature, metaphysics and the philosophies of the Pacanth Reach cultures and urged students to use the art for the good of society [note: *Shadows of the Empire Sourcebook*]. Off Bunduki it stayed rare: a student had to find one of the graduate masters [note: *Power of the Jedi Sourcebook*]. [Teräs Käsi/Legends, History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Teräs_Käsi/Legends)
  - The Followers did not train everyone: they refused to teach Teräs Käsi to the young mineral heir Yun, who had been sent to Bunduki to learn patience. [Bunduki/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bunduki/Legends)
  - **Core tenet:** control and mastery of one's self, to the point of complete control over the body [note: quote of Joclad Danva, HoloNet News; *Darth Maul: Shadow Hunter*]. [Teräs Käsi/Legends, Characteristics](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Teräs_Käsi/Legends)
  - **What it teaches:** close work with hands, feet, elbows, blades and sticks; half a dozen basic stances; four ranges of engagement: kicking, punching, elbowing and grappling [note: *Death Star* novel]. Moves are patterned on the movements and hunting of beasts [note: *Shadow Hunter*; *Power of the Jedi Sourcebook*]. [Teräs Käsi/Legends, Characteristics](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Teräs_Käsi/Legends)
  - Speed and anticipation: intense training develops blinding speed and senses tuned to anticipate strikes [note: Prima guide to the *Revenge of the Sith* game; *The Clone Wars Campaign Guide*]; midair acrobatics to steer a fall [note: *Shadow Hunter*]. [Teräs Käsi/Legends, Characteristics](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Teräs_Käsi/Legends)
  - [game-derived] Meditation: an immobile trance that stops bleeding and slows poison and disease; masters slowly heal wounds. The page's source is the *Star Wars Galaxies* "Meditate" skill (game mechanic). [Teräs Käsi/Legends, Characteristics](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Teräs_Käsi/Legends)
  - [game-derived] Strike power "rivaling slugthrowers and blaster pistols": the page itself derives this from damage dice in *Threats of the Galaxy* (2d10/3d10) and *Hero's Guide* (4d4). Game stat, not in-universe description. [Teräs Käsi/Legends, Characteristics](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Teräs_Käsi/Legends)
  - **Against other styles / foes:** created as a weapon against Jedi [game-derived: practitioners called "undisputed masters of melee combat", rivalling Jedi, in *Star Wars Galaxies* guide text]. In the Imperial era the Force-blind Pike sisters beat over two dozen armed opponents unarmed and could take squads armed with blasters [note: *Rebellion Era Sourcebook*]. Before 20 BBY Phow Ji dodged blaster fire from dozens of infantry with "Jedi-like" acrobatics [note: *MedStar I*]. [Teräs Käsi/Legends, Notable practitioners](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Teräs_Käsi/Legends)
  - Light and heavy sparring: Teräs Käsi could be practised as light sparring (no armor) or heavy sparring (more physical). [Light sparring](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Light_sparring) · [Heavy sparring](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Heavy_sparring)
- **Requirements:** none stated by species. Not Force-dependent: non-Force-users master it, and Force-users also learn it [note: the Pike sisters and Phow Ji were non-Force-users].
- **Related:** Followers of Palawa, Bunduki, Sälãi Käsi, Verdanaian "sliding hands", clone assassins (Part 3).
- **Uncertainty:** Several "abilities" on the page are derived from RPG damage values or MMO skills; tagged [game-derived] (ruling 13). Bunduki was a Human world that the Epicanthix conquered [Ruled (Jared, 24 Nov 2026): fits most sources; *Hero's Guide* has it "populated by" the Epicanthix].

### 2. Followers of Palawa (teaching order)

- **Type:** Force-studying order and martial-arts teachers
- **Era:** ancient (Palawa destroyed long before 24,500 BBY) to the Imperial era.
- **Key facts:**
  - Studied the Force and midi-chlorians; fled destroyed Palawa to Bunduki (Outer Rim) after a war involving the Jedi Council (*Jedi vs. Sith*). [Followers of Palawa](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Followers_of_Palawa)
  - Mastered a deep "Palawa hibernation trance" to sleep for decades without aging. [Followers of Palawa](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Followers_of_Palawa)
  - During the First Great Schism, many Followers, including Arden Lyn, joined the Legions of Lettow. [Followers of Palawa](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Followers_of_Palawa)
  - After the Clone Wars they still lived as hermits on Bunduki, teaching literature, philosophy, history, physical discipline and martial arts to young people of the Pacanth Reach "for the good of society"; teachers wore form-fitting Palawa tunics [note: *Shadows of the Empire Sourcebook*]. [Followers of Palawa](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Followers_of_Palawa)
  - Bunduki, a Human world the Epicanthix conquered [Ruled (Jared, 24 Nov 2026)]: Pacanth Reach (Outer Rim, grid H-16); city Äslajä; language Bunduki; affiliated with the Legions of Lettow and later the Empire. Yun, a Dark Jedi, came back with an Imperial force to take revenge on the Followers and met Arden Lyn there. [Bunduki/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bunduki/Legends) · [Pacanth Reach/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pacanth_Reach/Legends)
- **Requirements:** students came to Bunduki in person; the order could refuse a student (Yun).
- **Related:** Teräs Käsi; Force Traditions file.
- **Uncertainty:** none.

### 3. Sälãi Käsi ("Hidden Hand")

- **Type:** Teräs Käsi religious order
- **Era:** c. 22 BBY (known before and after the Clone Wars) to c. 0 BBY; two members left by 2 BBY.
- **Key facts:** headed by Neaed Fisto; every member was at least slightly Force-sensitive, so the order was targeted alongside the Jedi under Order 66; only two members remained by 2 BBY [note: *Shadow Games*]. [Sälãi Käsi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sälãi_Käsi) · [Eaden Vrill](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eaden_Vrill)
- **Requirements:** minimal Force-sensitivity.
- **Uncertainty:** none.

### 4. The Echani art

- **Type:** unarmed martial art and the culture that made it
- **Era:** c. 3,976 BBY (Old Republic: Mandalorian Wars and Jedi Civil War, to 3,951 BBY) through the Empire (Royal Guard, Imperial Intelligence) and later.
- **Key facts:**
  - Unarmed discipline developed by the Echani. By tradition combatants usually used no armor, weapons or Force techniques; the aim was to master the body as a weapon [note: *Knights of the Old Republic II*]. [Echani (martial art)/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Echani_(martial_art)/Legends)
  - Philosophy: combat is the only way to truly know someone; duels are rituals with strict etiquette that let fighters read one another; fighting is communication, like art. Repeated sparring could be a form of courtship. [Echani (martial art)/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Echani_(martial_art)/Legends)
  - Reading the opponent: Echani aim to know where a strike will land before it lands; Brianna says the most advanced can predict battles months ahead and the most revered the course of wars [note: *KOTOR II*]. [Echani (martial art)/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Echani_(martial_art)/Legends)
  - **Structure and learning:** three tiers, each with foundations and higher forms; Echani children start with the foundations and move up as the instructor decides. Brianna says formal training is "reserved for certain caste members", though she teaches Meetra Surik basic principles [note: *KOTOR II*]. [Echani (martial art)/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Echani_(martial_art)/Legends)
  - Spread: frequently taught to special forces units across the galaxy in the Old Republic era [note: *KOTOR II*]. After 19 BBY the Emperor's Royal Guard [note: *Crimson Empire* 1], Imperial Intelligence [note: *Star Wars Gamer* 5] and the female bodyguards Mahd Windcaller led [note: *Star Wars Handbook 2*] trained in it [note: Windcaller's are undated]. After 899 BBY the Sun Guard of Thyrsus also used it [note: *Hero's Guide*]. Outsiders learn military versions; the full art stays within the Echani castes [Ruled (Jared, 24 Nov 2026)]. [Echani (martial art)/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Echani_(martial_art)/Legends)
  - Known users in 3,951 BBY: Brianna and the Handmaiden Sisters, Yusanis, Atton Rand. In 22–20 BBY: Phow Ji. From 0 ABY: Lumiya, Mahd Windcaller [note: Windcaller undated]. [Echani (martial art)/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Echani_(martial_art)/Legends)
  - **Against other styles:** K'thri is described as the opposite: fast and fluid where Echani is "bold, brutal". [K'thri](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/K'thri)
- **Requirements:** in the home culture, caste membership for the full art; outsiders (special forces, the Royal Guard, Imperial Intelligence) learn military versions [Ruled (Jared, 24 Nov 2026)].
- **Related:** Echani culture (below); Echani Firedancers and the ritual brand (Part 2); Royal Guard (Part 3).
- **Uncertainty:** none.

### 5. The Echani (culture as a source of fighters)

- **Type:** near-Human species and martial society
- **Era:** c. 3,976 BBY (Old Republic) to the New Jedi Order era.
- **Key facts:**
  - Near-Humans from Eshan in the Inner Rim; pale skin, white hair, silver eyes; children of the same parents usually look identical to outsiders. Subspecies: Thyrsians. [Echani/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Echani/Legends)
  - Matriarchal, caste-based society; a confederacy of six worlds, the Six Sisters (including Begali and Thyrsus), ruled by the all-female council of the Echani Command [note: *Galaxy at War*; *KOTOR II*]. [Echani/Legends, Society and culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Echani/Legends)
  - A kinetic culture of communication: they identify individuals by small shifts in body language, which carries over into combat. Reading body movement was essential because siblings look alike [note: *Galaxy at War*]. [Echani/Legends, Society and culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Echani/Legends)
  - Gear: focus on unarmed and melee weapons; excellent weaponsmiths. Common vibroswords before and during the Jedi Civil War were based on Echani designs. They fought in light armor and trained in minimal clothing; anything that limited movement was avoided. Their personal energy shields were popular around Meetra Surik's time and later fell out of use [note: *KOTOR*, *KOTOR II*]. Simple adjustments to traditional Echani light armor neutralised the Maktites' thermal weapons c. 3,956 BBY [note: *KOTOR*]. [Echani/Legends, Combat Gear](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Echani/Legends)
  - History: famous duelists Raskta Fenni (before the Jedi Civil War) and Raskta Lsu (Jedi Master in Hoth's Army of Light, 1,010–1,000 BBY). A long rivalry with the Mandalorians brought the Echani into the Mandalorian Wars on the Republic side. By 3,956 BBY General Yusanis had fought beside the exiled Jedi Arren Kae until Malachor V, became a Senator, then challenged Darth Revan to a duel after Revan had an Echani Senator assassinated, and was killed. The Echani fought Revan and lost, and respected his skill [note: *KOTOR*]. [Echani/Legends, History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Echani/Legends)
  - 1,154 BBY: Begali Uprising against the Echani Command; Thyrsian crusades until the Pact of Almera recognised Thyrsus's independence in 899 BBY; Thyrsian special forces became the Sun Guard [note: *Galaxy at War*]. [Echani/Legends, History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Echani/Legends)
- **Related:** Sun Guard (Part 2), Handmaiden Sisters (Part 2), Royal Guard (Part 3).
- **Uncertainty:** none.

### 6. Stava (Noghri)

- **Type:** unarmed and armed martial art
- **Era:** 19 BBY onward, Imperial era (Noghri in Imperial service from the Galactic Civil War; post-Thrawn spread).
- **Key facts:**
  - Developed by the Noghri of Honoghr; named after a Honoghr predator. Teaches joint-locks, pressure points, nerve pinches, takedowns, and fast, deadly strikes with hands, arms, feet and legs. [Stava (skill)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stava_(skill))
  - Emphasises speed and endurance; Noghri dodge blaster bolts with acrobatics and rolls. Example: the Noghri cut down Darth Vader's stormtrooper guard when he arrived on Honoghr. [Stava (skill)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stava_(skill))
  - Also covers throwing and wielding the Noghri fighting sickle, plus quarterstaffs, vibroblades and Vrakolian spin-blades. [Stava (skill)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stava_(skill))
  - **How learned:** mainly taught only to Noghri before 9 ABY. After 9 ABY it spread to outsiders [Ruled (Jared, 24 Nov 2026)], as Noghri gave lessons in exchange for help rebuilding Honoghr and their Wayland colony. Shira Brie (Lumiya) learned it in Imperial training. [Stava (skill)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stava_(skill))
- **Uncertainty:** none. Sources: *The Thrawn Trilogy Sourcebook* (1996, first mention), *Star Wars Gamer* 5, *Hero's Guide*, *Galaxy at War*. [Stava (skill)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stava_(skill))
- **Requirements:** none stated beyond access to a Noghri teacher.
- **Related:** Noghri death commandos and bodyguards (Part 3).
- **Uncertainty:** none; the date outsiders were first taught, after 9 ABY, is ruled (it follows the Thrawn campaign).

### 7. K'tara

- **Type:** unarmed military style
- **Era:** 2 BBY on, Galactic Civil War (Rebel Alliance).
- **Key facts:** used by Alliance Special Forces and other military units; aims to do the most damage with the least noise; short, quick movements from a set position, inside the area framed by the target's shoulders and hips; a skilled user silences, disarms and disables an opponent very quickly. First in *Rules of Engagement: The Rebel SpecForce Handbook* (1997); also *Hero's Guide*, *Galaxy at War*, *Rebel Force: Renegade*. [K'tara](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/K'tara)
- **Requirements:** military (SpecForces) training.
- **Uncertainty:** none.

### 8. K'thri

- **Type:** unarmed sport and martial style
- **Era:** "dating back to the time of the Old Republic"; no start date.
- **Key facts:** fast movements, fluid strikes and well-balanced footwork, unlike Echani's bold, brutal movement. Mostly used in sporting events, so it includes flashy moves meant to impress an audience, which can make it inefficient. Sources: *The Last Command Sourcebook* (1994, first), *Thrawn Trilogy Sourcebook*, *Hero's Guide*, *Complete Star Wars Encyclopedia*, *Galaxy at War*. [K'thri](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/K'thri)
  - [game-derived] Named techniques: Attack Flurry (rapid attacks powered by the legs), Rain of Blows (a mass of flowing strikes that keeps the opponent off balance), Successive Strikes (each hit after the first concentrates damage on one weak spot).
- **Uncertainty:** the three technique write-ups read like RPG feat descriptions ("dealt more and more damage") and probably come from *Hero's Guide* or the Wizards "Martial Arts" web article. Tagged [game-derived].

### 9. Wrruushi (Wookiee)

- **Type:** unarmed martial art
- **Era:** not dated; sources from the Galactic Civil War era (1995 onward).
- **Key facts:** developed by Wookiees and often performable only by Wookiees; widely known because of the violence of Wookiee society and rites; built on huge upper-body strength: punches, jabs and throws, usually only a few strikes at a time; includes non-lethal options, able to disable with a single blow. First in *The DarkStryder Campaign* (1995); also *Hero's Guide*, Wizards "Jedi Counseling" columns, *Complete Star Wars Encyclopedia* ("Lofryyhn"), *Galaxy at War*. [Wrruushi/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wrruushi/Legends)
- **Requirements:** in practice Wookiee physiology ("often only Wookiees were capable").
- **Related:** clench fights, hrrtayyk, rrakktorr (Part 2).
- **Uncertainty:** none.

### 10. Matukai (Force adepts of the body)

- **Type:** Force tradition built on meditative martial arts
- **Era:** founded c. 4,000 BBY; active through the Purge; some survivors joined the New Jedi Order; still present in the Yuuzhan Vong War.
- **Key facts:**
  - Founded by a Human woman from Karvoss II who discovered her Force-sensitivity while practising a meditative martial art (*Jedi vs. Sith*); c. 4,000 BBY [note: *KOTOR II*]. That meditative form existed after the Great Sith War, by 3,996 BBY. [Matukai/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Matukai/Legends) · [Unidentified martial arts form](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_martial_arts_form)
  - Nomadic and spacefaring, in groups of three to five; never more than 50–60 members at once [note: *Hero's Guide*]. Ranks: apprentice and adept, each with its own robe (green and white) [note: *KOTOR II*]. Instructors could take more than one apprentice or none. [Matukai/Legends, Organization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Matukai/Legends)
  - 580 BBY: a Jedi delegation under Bodo Baas met Matukai Mendor Typhoons over Ord Radama; the Matukai called Jedi training "elitist and ineffectual"; Baas advised leaving them autonomous, since most members would never have been picked for Jedi training. [Matukai/Legends, History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Matukai/Legends)
  - Before 32 BBY Jedi Master Thame Cerulian fought one hand-to-hand and said he never wanted to again [note: *The Jedi Path*]. [Matukai/Legends, History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Matukai/Legends)
  - **What it teaches:** balance of body and spirit; exercise as meditation; meditative martial arts, physical training and somatic rituals that can strengthen a weak Force connection. Matukai were "noted for being able to develop a Force-sensitivity in many beings who were not inherently gifted with the Force". [Matukai/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Matukai/Legends)
  - **Training method:** the instructor sets the course; trainees push past their stamina while drawing on the Force; each lesson pairs a physical and a spiritual part; a common drill is holding one stance for hours or days, sometimes in harsh weather. On finishing, a Matukai receives a tattoo across the forehead and the sides of the face near the eyes [note: *Hero's Guide*]. After 11 ABY those who joined Luke Skywalker's academy also wore a facial tattoo [note: *The Jedi Path*]. [Matukai/Legends, Philosophy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Matukai/Legends)
  - [game-derived] **Abilities (mostly *Hero's Guide*, an RPG book):** tireless exertion for hours; "whirlwind" speed; stronger blows; sensing wounds and illness in one's own body; purging illness and poison and becoming immune (shrugging off a kouhun sting); raising or lowering body temperature, even burning to the touch; "Soft to Solid" body hardening; dodging or deflecting many strikes and shots. [Matukai/Legends, Abilities](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Matukai/Legends)
  - Signature weapon: the wan-shen polearm (Part 2). A meditation band was sometimes worn against dark-side influence [note: *KOTOR II*].
  - Fate: hunted by Inquisitors in the Purge, among the first groups attacked, most killed [note: *Jedi Academy Training Manual*]; a few hid and passed on the tradition; some joined the Jedi Praxeum; some fell to the Yuuzhan Vong and the Peace Brigade, but the tradition survived. [Matukai/Legends, History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Matukai/Legends)
- **Requirements:** at least a weak Force connection, which training develops. Non-Jedi path.
- **Related:** Force Traditions file (cross-reference; this entry covers only the martial side).
- **Uncertainty:** the ability list comes largely from RPG text; so the powers are tagged [game-derived] and are not lore (ruling 13). [BTS] The Matukai were created for *Hero's Guide* (2003) by Rodney Thompson and J.D. Wiker (page, Behind the scenes).
- **Canon note:** a separate Canon "Matukai" page (High Republic, Kel Dor Waran Val, 382 BBY) exists; not used. See Canon only.

### 12. Iridonian Zabrak martial tradition

- **Type:** species martial tradition
- **Era:** not dated.
- **Key facts:** Zabraks from Iridonia were thought more warlike than colony Zabraks because of their harsh homeworld; they developed a highly physical martial art that young Zabraks are required to learn, and Iridonians are often among the best hand-to-hand fighters and weaponsmiths in the galaxy. Colony Zabraks steer the same drive into other fields. As warriors they are dedicated, intense and focused. [Zabrak/Legends, Personality](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zabrak/Legends)
- **Requirements:** Iridonian upbringing.
- **Uncertainty:** this section of the page has no inline citation. [unclear] The Zabrak page carries a TCW-retcon notice (TCW made Maul a Dathomirian Zabrak); not relevant to the martial art itself.

### 13. Wookiee unarmed customs

- **Type:** species customs

- **Clench fights:** young male Wookiees fight one another while growing up to build a fearless, headlong fighting style and to discover their *rrakktorr*. [Clench fight](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clench_fight)
- **Rrakktorr:** the inner strength or fire of male Wookiees, unlocked through the hrrtayyk ceremony at the age of ascension. [Rrakktorr](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rrakktorr)
- Canderous Ordo: Wookiees are strong, "surprisingly capable warriors" who "pick up combat techniques pretty quickly" [note: *KOTOR*]. [Wookiee/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wookiee/Legends)
