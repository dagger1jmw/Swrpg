# George Lucas's Statements on Force Potential, Vader's Power, Midi-chlorians, Balance and the Dark Side

**Tags.** Every item is **[BTS]**: behind-the-scenes material, allowed under SPEC rule 5. None of it is Legends lore. Items dated after the Disney sale (30 Oct 2012) are also tagged **[late]** and sit in their own section. Other tags follow SPEC: [unclear], [conflict], [inferred].

**Quoting.** Every quotation below is Lucas's own words, copied verbatim from the source named. Curly apostrophes and quote marks have been made straight; nothing else changed. Lucas's own typos ("your become", "mitichlorians") are kept. Where a quote comes only from a secondary transcript (fan transcriptions of an audio commentary, or a quote reproduced by another outlet), it is flagged **[secondary transcript]**, and the closest primary source (disc and approximate time, or book and page) is given. Paraphrases are never put in quotation marks.

**Compared against:** `SW_Midichlorians_Force_Potential.md` (cited as **MIDI**, with § numbers) and `SW_DarkSide_Fall_Corruption_Redemption_Part2.md` (cited as **DS2**). Agree or conflict notes only record the relationship. Lucas's word does not override Legends here, and the developer decides.

## Summary

Before the Disney sale, Lucas consistently said these things:

1. **Mustafar crippled Vader's power.** Unhurt, Anakin would have been "twice as good as the Emperor". After Mustafar he was "maybe 20 percent less than the Emperor" (*Vanity Fair*, Feb 2005), "like Darth Maul or Count Dooku" (*Rolling Stone*, June 2005), and had "lost a lot of the power of the Force" (ESB commentary, 2004, [secondary transcript]).
2. **Luke was the replacement.** Palpatine wanted someone more powerful than himself, "But the son could become that." Vader and Palpatine each meant to use Luke against the other.
3. **Midi-chlorians are a metaphor for symbiosis.** They are the connectors between the Force and the living cell. "The more you have," the better your cells can "speak intuitively to the Force". Anakin's birth is "left up to the audience", although Lucas leaned toward "the Force itself created Anakin".
4. **The prophecy is true.** "Balance comes back to the Force".
5. **The dark side is selfishness and possessive love.** It puts the Force "out of balance". Vader is redeemed through his children's compassion.
6. **Who could fight Palpatine.** "You have to be either Mace or Yoda to compete with the Emperor" (*Making of ROTS*, [secondary transcript]). In the duel, Mace originally overpowered Palpatine, and Palpatine's weakness was "something that I added later" (ROTS commentary, [secondary transcript]).

**How this lines up with Legends:**
- Lucas's injury account **agrees** with MIDI §6 Version A and **conflicts** with Version B (potential intact, limits psychological).
- His "20 percent less" is close to the Legends "eight-tenths".
- His "like a thousand" midi-chlorians per cell for Anakin **conflicts** in scale with the Legends "over 20,000".

**Gaps.** Nothing verbatim was found from Lucas on Sith eyes, on the cause of Palpatine's face (only McDiarmid relaying Lucas), on redemption restoring anything, or on Obi-Wan's relative power.

## Entries

### Topic 1. Luke's potential compared with Anakin, Vader, Palpatine and Yoda

**1a. [BTS] Rolling Stone, 2005: Palpatine wanted someone stronger, and Luke could be it**
- **Quote:** "He tries it with anybody he thinks might be more powerful, which is what the Emperor was looking for in the first place: somebody who would be more powerful than he was and could help him rule the universe." … "He wasn't what he was supposed to become. But the son could become that."
- **Source:** Gavin Edwards, "George Lucas and the Cult of Darth Vader", *Rolling Stone*, interview, print and web, 2 June 2005.
- **Check:** [Rolling Stone 2005][rs] (primary, read in full). Also quoted in [Luke Skywalker/Legends §Behind the scenes][lukeL].
- **Legends comparison:**
  - **Agrees** with MIDI §4: Luke's potential was equal to his father's, and Wookieepedia ties it to the Episode V commentary.
  - **Agrees** with the Legends account that Palpatine sought Luke as a "younger and more powerful" replacement ([Darth Sidious/Legends][sidL]).
  - Lucas gives no figure for Luke against Yoda.

**1b. [BTS] Vanity Fair, 2005: "a more primo version"**
- **Quote:** "So he finds that, with Luke, he can get a more primo version if he can turn Luke to the Dark Side."
- **Source:** Jim Windolf, "Star Wars: The Last Battle", *Vanity Fair*, February 2005 print issue (web repost dated 19 Feb 2014).
- **Check:** [Vanity Fair 2005][vf]; [VF archive][vfa].
- **Legends comparison:** **Agrees** with the same Legends material as 1a.

**1c. [BTS] Episode V DVD commentary, 2004: Luke is Vader's hope [secondary transcript]**
- **What exists:**
  - Fan transcriptions agree only on the fragment "lost a lot of the power of the Force" (said of Vader; see 3c).
  - What follows is transcribed two ways: "But Luke has it, Luke is Vader's hope" in one version, and absent in another. Because the transcriptions disagree, nothing about Luke is quoted.
  - Wookieepedia paraphrases the commentary (not a quote): Lucas "has stated that Luke has the same Force potential as his father" and that "Luke's Force potential was even more powerful than Emperor Palpatine's".
- **Source:** *The Empire Strikes Back* audio commentary by Lucas, Ben Burtt, Dennis Muren and Carrie Fisher. *Star Wars Trilogy* DVD, 21 Sept 2004, around the Cloud City duel [unclear exact time].
- **Check:** [Luke Skywalker/Legends §Force powers; §Behind the scenes][lukeL]. Transcription: [SuspectInsight forum][sif].
- **Legends comparison:**
  - The paraphrase **agrees** with MIDI §4, which already carries it as BTS.
  - Luke above Palpatine has no Legends in-universe statement in MIDI; see MIDI Not found.

### Topic 2. Anakin's intended power

- **Quote:** "Anakin, as Skywalker, as a human being, was going to be extremely powerful," … "So his ability to be twice as good as the Emperor disappeared, and now he's maybe 20 percent less than the Emperor."
- **Source:** *Vanity Fair*, Feb 2005 (as 1b).
- **Check:** [Vanity Fair 2005][vf]; [Anakin Skywalker/Legends §Behind the scenes][anaL].
- **Wording discrepancy:** Wookieepedia's copy of this quote reads "losing his legs and an arm" and "20 percent less than him". The primary *Vanity Fair* text reads "losing his arms and a leg" and "20 percent less than the Emperor". This file follows the primary.

- **Check:** quoted on several fan forums, e.g. [KMC forum][kmc]. One Reddit copy reads "could've beaten" [unclear]. The lead-in sentence about exaggerating Palpatine's weakness is Rinzler's narration, not Lucas's, and is not quoted.

**2c. [BTS] Rolling Stone, 2005: "exceptional powers"**

### Topic 5. Midi-chlorians: meaning, potential versus power, and whether a count can change

**5a. [BTS] 1977 notes: some beings are born with a higher awareness of the Force**
- **Quotes:**
  - "It is said that certain creatures are born with a higher awareness of the Force than humans. Their brains are different."
  - "Anyone who studied and worked hard could learn it. But you would have to do it on your own."
- **Source:** Lucas, summer 1977, explanatory notes; published in Rinzler, *The Making of Star Wars* (2007). Rinzler says Lucas later added a midi-chlorian note to this passage before the book came out.
- **Check:** [StarWars.com, Rinzler 2013][rinz] (Lucasfilm site quoting the book); [Midi-chlorian/Legends §Behind the scenes][mcL].
- **Legends comparison:** **Agrees** with MIDI §4–5: species differences, and potential still needs training.

**5b. [BTS] 1981 *Revenge of the Jedi* story conference: everyone can use the Force**
- **Quotes:** "Yes, everybody can do it." … "It's just the Jedi who take the time to do it." … "Like Yoga. If you want to take the time to do it, you can do it".
- **Source:** transcript of the story conference with Lawrence Kasdan and Richard Marquand (1981), published in Rinzler, *The Making of Return of the Jedi* (Oct 2013, pp. 62–69 per Wookieepedia). The statement is from 1981, so it belongs in the main set even though it was published after 2012.
- **Check:**
  - [StarWars.com, Rinzler 2013][rinz] reads "everybody".
  - Wookieepedia's [Midi-chlorian (canon page) §Behind the scenes][mcC] reads "Yes, everyone can do it." [unclear] which wording the book uses. The other two sentences match.
- **Legends comparison:**
  - **Agrees** with MIDI §5: a count is not a ceiling, and skill comes through training.
  - Partly **conflicts** with the Legends need for a high count to sense the Force at all (MIDI §1, §3). Record only.

**5c. [BTS] Talks with Terry Brooks, c. 1998: Anakin has "like a thousand" per cell**
- **Quotes:**
  - "In Anakin's case, there are, instead of one or two or three midi-chlorians in each cell, there's like a thousand. It's unbelievable how many midi-chlorians are in there."
  - "But there has to be at least one in a cell, otherwise the cell can't reproduce. All cells. Plant cells. Every life form has a midi-chlorian living inside of them."
  - "The way you interact and interface with this larger energy field is through the midi-chlorians, which are sensitive to the energy." (Rinzler's bracketed gloss "[the Force]" is omitted.)
- **Source:** Lucas in conversation with Terry Brooks before Brooks wrote the Episode I novelization (c. 1998 [unclear]; Rinzler gives no date). Quoted by Rinzler, a Lucasfilm author with archive access.
- **Check:** [StarWars.com, Rinzler 2013][rinz].
- **Legends comparison:**
  - **[conflict] on scale.** Lucas says "like a thousand" per cell for Anakin, against one to three for ordinary cells. Legends says "over 20,000" per cell (Episode I film and novelization), and 27,700 [last resort] (MIDI §2).
  - **Agrees** with Legends that midi-chlorians are in every cell of every living thing (MIDI §1).

**5d. [BTS] Rinzler, 2013: Han has no midi-chlorians (a joke)**
- **Quote:** "Han Solo has no midi-chlorians. He's a zombie."
- **Source:** Lucas, undated, quoted in Rinzler 2013. Rinzler explicitly calls it a joke ("except when he was joking").
- **Check:** [StarWars.com, Rinzler 2013][rinz].
- **Legends comparison:** Taken literally, it **conflicts** with MIDI §1 (every living being has them). Its author calls it a joke, so it is recorded only.

**5e. [BTS] Vanity Fair, 2005: more midi-chlorians means a stronger link to the Force, and the Force made Anakin**
- **Quote:** "It was a virgin birth in an ecosystem of symbiotic relationships. It means that between the Force, which is sort of a life force, and reality, the connectors between these two things are what we call mitichlorians." … "The more you have, the more your cells are able to speak intuitively to the Force itself and use the powers of the Force. Ultimately, I would say the Force itself created Anakin."
- **Source and check:** [Vanity Fair 2005][vf]; [Midi-chlorian manipulation][mcm] (behind-the-scenes section).
- **Legends comparison:**
  - **Agrees** with MIDI §1: a high count means a stronger link, and midi-chlorians are symbionts.
  - **Agrees** with MIDI on *Darth Plagueis*: the Force created Anakin.
  - Lucas does not say that more midi-chlorians means more power. "The more you have" is about how easily one reaches the Force, which fits the Legends rule that a count is not a ceiling.

**5f. [BTS] Rolling Stone, 2005: midi-chlorians as metaphor; Anakin's origin left open**
- **Quotes:**
  - "Everybody said to drop the stuff about the midichlorians, it makes it too confusing. But it's a metaphor for a symbiotic relationship that allows life to exist."
  - "Is Anakin a product of a super-Sith who influenced the midichlorians to create him, or is he simply created by the midichlorians to bring forth a prophecy, or was he created by the Force through the midichlorians? It's left up to the audience to decide."
- **Source and check:** [Rolling Stone 2005][rs].
- **Legends comparison:** **Compatible** with *Darth Plagueis*, which combines Plagueis's manipulation with the Force's response (MIDI; [Midi-chlorian manipulation][mcm]).

**5g. [BTS] E! Online press conference, 1999: midi-chlorians as mitochondria [secondary transcript]**
- **Quote:** "Midichondrians are a loose depiction of mitochondria, which are necessary components for cells to divide." The article's own mis-transcription "Midichondrians" is kept.
- **Source:** Lucas press conference reported by E! Online, 11 May 1999. A shorter version ran in *Starlog* #265 (Aug 1999).
- **Check:** quoted in full in [SciFi Stack Exchange][sse]. I did not open the E! Online primary.
- **Legends comparison:** **Agrees** with MIDI §1 (symbiotic, needed for life).

**Can a count change?** Lucas said nothing verbatim on this before 2012. See [late] L2 and Not found.

### Topic 6. The Chosen One and balance

**6a. [BTS] Rolling Stone, 2005: "the prophecy is true"**
- **Quote:** "How he was born ultimately has no relationship to how he dies, because in the end, the prophecy is true: Balance comes back to the Force."
- **Further quote, same interview:** "Whether it's Hindu, Chinese or Middle Eastern, all the mythological heroes didn't have fathers. The fathers were the gods."
- **Source and check:** [Rolling Stone 2005][rs].
- **Legends comparison:** **Agrees** with the Legends account that Anakin fulfilled the prophecy by destroying Sidious ([Chosen One/Legends][coL]; DS2 §3, Vader's redemption at 4 ABY).

**6b. [BTS] Clone Wars writers' room, 2010, released on *The Complete Saga* Blu-ray, 2011: the dark side throws the Force out of balance [secondary transcript]**
- **Quotes (ellipses as in the transcript):**
  - "What happens when you go to the dark side is it goes out of balance, and then you get really selfish..."
  - "Once you become afraid that somebody's going to take it away from you, or you're going to lose it, then you start to become angry... And that anger leads to hate, and hate leads to suffering..."
- **Source:** Lucas speaking to *Star Wars: The Clone Wars* writers in 2010. The clip is the "George Lucas on the Force" feature on *Star Wars: The Complete Saga* Blu-ray (16 Sept 2011) [unclear exact disc].
- **Check:** transcribed by [Screen Rant, 5 Apr 2024][sr]. Cited (paraphrased) in [Chosen One/Legends §Behind the scenes][coL]. The primary is the Blu-ray.
- **Legends comparison:**
  - **Agrees** with DS2 §1 (the chain from attachment to hate).
  - Balance as the light keeping the dark in check fits the Legends idea that the prophecy meant destroying the Sith ([Chosen One/Legends][coL]).

**6c. [BTS] Bill Moyers, 1999 [secondary transcript]**
- **Quote:** "And we all have those two sides of us, and that we have to make sure those two sides of us are in balance."
- **Source and check:** as 4c ([Chosen One/Legends §Behind the scenes][coL]).
- **The printed *TIME* text (verified) has only this related line:** "These are the two sides--the good force and the bad force." ([TIME 1999][time])
- **Legends comparison:**
  - 6b and 6c sit uneasily together. Balance between two sides inside a person (1999) is not the same as the dark side being imbalance itself (2010). Wookieepedia notes the same tension.
  - Legends (DS2 §8, the Potentium) records a similar split.
  - [conflict] (internal to Lucas). Record only.

**7e. [BTS] Rolling Stone, 2005: the breath theme**

- **Source and check:** [Rolling Stone 2005][rs].

### Topic 8. Yoda's, Mace's and Obi-Wan's power relative to Anakin and Palpatine

**8a. [BTS] *Making of ROTS*, 2005: only Mace or Yoda could compete with the Emperor [secondary transcript]**
- **Quote:** "You have to be either Mace or Yoda to compete with the Emperor," … "If Anakin hadn't got all beat up, he could've beat the Emperor."
- **Source and check:** as 2b (p. 204 or 205 [unclear]; [KMC forum][kmc]).
- **Legends comparison:**
  - **Agrees** with [Mace Windu/Legends §Final battle and death; §Lightsaber training][maceL]: Windu disarmed Sidious through Vaapad (DS2 §5).
  - Yoda: his Episode III duel with Sidious ended without a victor; it is not covered in either Legends file, so no comparison is recorded.
  - Note Lucas's own phrasing: "beat up" refers to Mustafar.

**8b. [BTS] ROTS DVD commentary, 2005: Mace overpowered Palpatine, and the weakness was a ruse added later [secondary transcript]**
- **Quote:** "Okay, well, this sequence always started out with Mace overpowering Palpatine, and then Palpatine using his powers to try to destroy Mace, and Mace deflecting his rays with his lightsaber. And it always was that Anakin cut the lightsaber out of his hand. But this part where he pretends to lose his power and be weak was something that I added later".
- **Source:** ROTS commentary, DVD, 1 Nov 2005. Exact time [unclear]: an unofficial upload of the commentary puts the line at 31:12, but whether that is film time or clip time is [unclear].
- **Check:** [r/starwarsspeculation][rd1]; [TheForce.net thread][tfn]. The primary is the disc.
- **Legends comparison:**
  - **Agrees** with [Darth Sidious/Legends §Confrontation in the Chancellor's Suite; §Personality and traits][sidL]: Sidious was "feigning helplessness", and "his cowering was just a trick".
  - Lucas's "Mace overpowering Palpatine" also **agrees** with the Legends Mace page (8a).

**8c. Obi-Wan**
- No Lucas statement on Obi-Wan's power compared with Anakin or Palpatine was found.
- The only related verified line is "Obi-Wan screwed that up by cutting off his arms and legs" (3b), which concerns the outcome, not relative power. See Not found.

## Game notes (suggestions only)

- **Vader after Mustafar.** If the developer wants a Lucas-consistent number, two things line up:
  - Lucas's "maybe 20 percent less than the Emperor" (VF 2005).
  - The Legends "eight-tenths of the strength of the Emperor" (MIDI §6).
  - **Suggestion:** set Vader's Force rating at **80% of Palpatine's**. Set unhurt Anakin's potential ceiling at **2× Palpatine's** (Lucas) or simply "above Palpatine" (Legends). Both are [BTS] anchors and need a ruling.
  - **The "why" stays open** ([conflict]: injuries against psychology). A mechanic could cover both: a fixed cap from the injuries (Lucas; Version A), plus a "self-doubt" penalty that can be removed in story (Version B). The developer chooses.
- **Luke's potential.** Lucas puts it at Anakin's level and above Palpatine's (paraphrase only).
  - **Suggestion:** give Luke the same potential tier as unhurt Anakin, but a much lower starting skill. That keeps Lucas's view that Force use must be learned (5a, 5b).
- **Midi-chlorian numbers.** Lucas's "like a thousand" per cell clashes with the Legends 20,000+. The Legends figure is in the films and novelizations.
  - **Suggestion:** keep the Legends scale for in-game blood tests and treat Lucas's figure as a loose remark [BTS]. Developer's call.
- **Mace and Yoda.** If a power ladder is needed, Lucas puts Mace and Yoda in the tier that can "compete with" Palpatine, with crippled Vader below (Maul or Dooku level).
