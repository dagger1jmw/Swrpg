# SWRPG Galaxy Timeline, 45–138 ABY (Part 1 of 3): Crucible, the Long Gap, the Ossus Project and the Sith–Imperial War

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 24, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Rulings of 24 Nov 2026 applied (lore-rulings-2026-11-24c.md; D-484).

## Summary
This three-part file rebuilds the "LEGACY ERA — 44–139 ABY" block of `SW_Legends_Galaxy_Timeline.md` as one sourced row per event, from *Crucible* (45 ABY) to the end of *Legacy — War* (138 ABY). Part 1 (this file) covers 45–130 ABY: the last Legends novel, the nearly blank 46–121 ABY stretch, the Ossus Project and the Sith–Imperial War up to the Massacre at Ossus and Darth Krayt's coup. Part 2 covers the Second Imperial Civil War and the Third Jedi Purge (130–138 ABY) in detail, with Cade Skywalker's story. Part 3 holds "Changes from the previous file", Not found, Canon only, Not used, Game notes, Decisions needed, Sources and the checklist. The key facts: Wookieepedia has year pages for only eight years between 46 and 121 ABY, and nothing dated happens in them beyond publications and a warship's debut; the Fel Empire's founding is dated only "c. 45 ABY" or "between 45 and 127 ABY"; the Ossus Project's start is dated both 122 and 126 ABY, and the One Sith's entry into the war both 127 and 128 ABY (Part 3, Decisions needed).

## Entries
How to read the tables: the era headings, the ═ rule lines and "### Overview" follow the old file and the 3,700–3,600 BBY files so the developer's parser can read them; period subheadings ("#### <years> — <name>") replace the old file's single table. Columns: Date | Event | Type | Place | Who | Source. Type is one of War (battles, campaigns, treaties), Control (a world or region changes hands), Leader (leadership change), Order (Jedi or Sith institutions), Death, Tech (technology and culture), Background (births, politics, crime, disasters, publications). "§" names the section of a long page. Many Legacy-era battle pages carry a "conjectural title" banner: the event is sourced, only its name is Wookieepedia's; such names are kept as they appear. Where a year comes only from an uncited infobox field, the row says "(uncited infobox)". Comic issues are cited as Wookieepedia cites them, through the page that cites them: *Legacy* (2006) issues 0–50 ("L0", "L1" … in the notes), *Legacy — War* 1–6 ("LW") and *Legacy* (2013) 1–18.

### Overview
The galaxy is at peace after the defeat of the Lost Tribe of the Sith and Abeloth on Coruscant in 44 ABY, after which Wynn Dorvan was elected interim Chief of State of the Galactic Alliance [Wynn Dorvan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wynn_Dorvan); [Timeline of galactic history/Legends §The Legacy era (37 ABY-140 ABY)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Timeline_of_galactic_history/Legends). The Jedi had been ordered off Coruscant and relocated to the academy on Shedu Maad [Timeline of galactic history/Legends §The Legacy era (37 ABY-140 ABY)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Timeline_of_galactic_history/Legends); Luke Skywalker is Grand Master again from 44 ABY [Grand Master/Legends §List of Grand Masters](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grand_Master/Legends). Darth Krayt's One Sith remained hidden on Korriban; Krayt had fought beside Luke against Abeloth in 44 ABY without Luke learning who he was [Darth Krayt §The rise of Darth Krayt (c. 25 ABY–130 ABY)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Krayt).

## ════════════════════════════════════════════════

### Overview
At some point in these years, no source says when, Jagged Fel became the first Emperor of a revived Empire at Bastion; he founded the Imperial Knights and the Victory Without War program [Jagged Fel §A revived empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jagged_Fel); [Fel II](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fel_II); [Fel dynasty](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fel_dynasty). The Galactic Alliance continued under a Chief of State, then a Triumvirate [Ruled (Jared, 24 Nov 2026): no date for the change, by 130 ABY]. The Jedi were led by a High Council. The One Sith grew in secret on Korriban for "more than a hundred years", Krayt sleeping in stasis while a line of Chagrian Sith Lords named Darth Wyyrlok acted as his Voice [Darth Krayt §The rise of Darth Krayt (c. 25 ABY–130 ABY)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Krayt); [Darth Wyyrlok (I)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Wyyrlok_%28I%29).

## THE OSSUS PROJECT AND THE SITH–IMPERIAL WAR — 122–130 ABY
## ════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

### Overview
In the 120s the Galactic Alliance and the Fel Empire were allies under the Treaty of Anaxes, pledged to defend each other against another Yuuzhan Vong attack [Treaty of Anaxes](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Treaty_of_Anaxes). The Jedi High Council was headed by Kol Skywalker, who conceived the Ossus Project with the Master Shaper Nei Rin: Yuuzhan Vong terraforming to heal worlds ruined by the war [Kol Skywalker](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kol_Skywalker); [Nei Rin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nei_Rin). Emperor Roan Fel ruled the Fel Empire from Bastion with a Council of Moffs led by Morlish Veed; his Director of Imperial Intelligence was Nyna Calixte [Fel Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fel_Empire); [Morlish Veed](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Morlish_Veed). The Galactic Alliance was led by a Triumvirate and its Core Fleet by Admiral Gar Stazi [Sith–Imperial War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith%E2%80%93Imperial_War). The One Sith under Darth Krayt sabotaged the project, and in 127 ABY the Moffs, against Fel's wishes, used its failure to start a war [Sith–Imperial War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith%E2%80%93Imperial_War); [Fel Empire §The Sith–Imperial War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fel_Empire). The war ended with the Alliance's surrender after Caamas in 130 ABY, the fall of the Jedi Temple, the Massacre at Ossus and Krayt's seizure of the throne.
