# Yuuzhan Vong Personal Biotechnology (Gear) — Part 2: Outsiders, the Force, Counter-tech, Eras, Castes

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 21, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
Part 2 covers who outside the Vong mainstream used their gear and how it responded, how the gear and its users interact with the Force and lightsabers, the personal-scale counters the galaxy developed, availability by era (war 25–29 ABY, post-war, Legacy era c. 122–137 ABY), and caste rules. Gear generally obeys whoever has bonded to it or speaks to it in the right way, not by species alone: Jacen Solo commanded amphistaffs through Vongsense, Jariah Syn tamed one by speaking the Vong language, and Darth Krayt's Vong implants let him use their technology. Most direct Force powers fail on Vong and their biots, but Force lightning, telekinetically moved objects and a few dark-side powers work. Part 1 lists the gear; Part 3 holds the closing sections.
