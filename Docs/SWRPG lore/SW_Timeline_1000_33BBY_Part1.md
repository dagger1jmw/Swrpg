# SWRPG Galaxy Timeline, 999–33 BBY (Part 1 of 3): The Rule of Two and the Early Great Peace

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 24, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends), read through the MediaWiki API (current revisions, plus revisions from before August 2008 where the developer asked for them), as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
This three-part file rebuilds the "REPUBLIC ERA (GREAT PEACE)" slice of `SW_Legends_Galaxy_Timeline.md` from 999 BBY, the year after Ruusan, to 33 BBY, the eve of the Naboo crisis, as one sourced row per event. Part 1 (this file) covers 999–101 BBY: Bane and Zannah's founding of the Rule of Two (999–980 BBY), the first Chancellors after Ruusan, Fae Coven's reformed Jedi Order, Yoda's birth, the Mandalorian Excision, the Trade Federation's founding, the Dark Jedi Conflict, the Mandalorian–Ithullan war and the rise of Darth Tenebrous and Darth Plagueis. Part 2 covers 101–40 BBY: Plagueis's murder of Tenebrous, Palpatine's birth, fall and election as Senator, the Chancellors Eixes Valorum, Pecivas, Darus, Frix and Kalpana, Jaster Mereel's Mandalore, the Stark Hyperspace War, Galidraan and Valorum's election. Part 3 covers 39–33 BBY (Valorum's terms, Maul's training, the Yinchorri Uprising and the Eriadu crisis) and then holds Changes from the previous file, Not found, Decisions needed, Sources and the checks.

The key facts for a 33 BBY start: Finis Valorum is Supreme Chancellor (elected 40 BBY, re-elected 36 BBY); Palpatine has been Senator of the Chommell sector since 52 BBY and is secretly Darth Sidious, apprentice of Darth Plagueis since 65 BBY; Maul is a trained Sith Lord (final test 37 BBY); the Yinchorri Uprising has just been crushed; and the Nebula Front affair (the Dorvalla attack, two staged attempts on Valorum and the murder of the Trade Federation Directorate at the Eriadu Trade Summit, dated 32.5 BBY) has handed the Trade Federation to Nute Gunray. Three things in the period are not settled in the sources and need a ruling: Maul's origin (Iridonia in the pre-2008 article, Dathomir now), Anakin's birth year (42 or 41 BBY) and the year the trade-route tax BR-0371 passed (33 or 32 BBY). See Decisions needed in Part 3.

## THE RULE OF TWO FOUNDED — 999–980 BBY
## ════════════════════════════════════════════

### Overview
After Ruusan (1000 BBY, covered in another file) Darth Bane rebuilt the Sith as an order of two, a Master and one apprentice, with the young Zannah as his apprentice [Order of the Sith Lords](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Order_of_the_Sith_Lords). The Republic of Tarsus Valorum was reshaped by the Ruusan Reformations: the Senate was cut from millions of sectors to just over a thousand, with seats for cultural groups and functional constituencies such as corporations, and the Jedi were put under judicial oversight [Tarsus Valorum/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tarsus_Valorum/Legends). The Republic and the Order disbanded their armies; peace was kept by planetary security forces and the Judicial Department [Jedi Order/Legends §Ruusan Reformation](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order/Legends). Under Grand Master Fae Coven the Council grew stronger: adults were no longer admitted, Jedi renounced family and possessions, training became one Master to one apprentice and was centred on the Coruscant Temple, satellite schools were closed and the Trials of Knighthood were fixed; the Council of First Knowledge under Restelly Quist sent Shadows to gather or erase Sith lore, wiping most memory of the Sith in under fifty years [Jedi Order/Legends §Ruusan Reformation](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order/Legends). In 990 BBY Bane and Zannah killed a Jedi team on Tython and Zannah staged the Ambria affair so that the Jedi believed the Sith extinct [Confrontation on Ambria](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Confrontation_on_Ambria). The era ends in 980 BBY, when Zannah defeated Bane on Ambria and took the Huntress, Darth Cognus, as her apprentice [Duel on Ambria (980 BBY)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Duel_on_Ambria_(980_BBY)).

## THE EARLY GREAT PEACE — 979–501 BBY
## ════════════════════════════════════════════

### Overview
The Sith now vanish from the dated record for centuries. Zannah died at some time after 980 BBY, and Cognus later took the three-eyed mutant Darth Millennial as apprentice; Millennial rejected the Rule of Two, broke away, and founded the Dark Force religion that became the Prophets of the Dark Side on Dromund Kaas. Neither event is dated [Darth Zannah/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Zannah/Legends); [Darth Millennial](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Millennial); [Darth Cognus](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Cognus). The Republic's dated events in these centuries are mostly frontier history: Eriadu's founding, the Outer Expansion Zone, the B'omarr monastery on Tatooine and Naboo's emergence. The Jedi record is led by Yoda, born in 896 BBY and a Master by 796 BBY. The one great war of these centuries is the Mandalorian Excision of 738 BBY, a source from 2012 that ties into the TCW picture of a ruined Mandalore (see the note on that row). The Hutts appear in the record from c. 600 BBY with Jabba's birth.

## THE MIDDLE CENTURIES — 500–101 BBY
## ════════════════════════════════════════════

### Overview
The five centuries before Plagueis built the institutions of the Phantom Menace era. The Corporate Sector (490 BBY) and the Trade Federation (350 BBY) were chartered; in 124 BBY the Senate made the Outer Rim a tax-free trade zone and gave guilds and corporations seats as functional constituencies, the Trade Federation among them [124 BBY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/124_BBY). The Jedi kept the peace with few dated crises: the *Chu'unthor* teaching ship and its crash on Dathomir, Keiran Halcyon's defeat of the Afarathu, and the Dark Jedi Conflict of 188–181 BBY, in which the Padawan Kibh Jeen learned from a Sith fortress on Almas that the Sith had survived Ruusan; the Order dismissed his claims [Kibh Jeen](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kibh_Jeen); [Dark Jedi Conflict](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Jedi_Conflict). Around 200 BBY the Jedi felt the dark side growing [200 BBY/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/200_BBY/Legends), and c. 167 BBY a Twi'lek Sith Master opened a rend in the Force that let the Jedi feel the dark side for the first time in over eight centuries; his apprentice, the Bith Darth Tenebrous, killed him and took the Muun Hego Damask as Darth Plagueis [Darth Tenebrous's Sith Master](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Tenebrous%27s_Sith_Master); [167 BBY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/167_BBY). The Mandalorian clans nearly exterminated the Ithullans in 200 BBY, and in 132 BBY rogue Jedi and the bounty hunter Durge assassinated the ruling Mandalore [Mandalorian–Ithullan war](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalorian%E2%80%93Ithullan_war). On Naboo the Gungan–Naboo War ended in 121 BBY with the elected monarchy [121 BBY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/121_BBY).
