# SWRPG Galaxy Timeline, 4–25 ABY (Part 3 of 5): Dark Empire, the Praxeum, Daala and Adumar (10–13 ABY)

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 24, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends), as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
Part 3 covers the Second Battle of Coruscant and the Imperial Mutiny, the reborn Emperor and Operation Shadow Hand (10–11 ABY), the Crimson Empire, Luke's Jedi Praxeum and the *Jedi Academy* trilogy (Daala, Kyp Durron, Exar Kun), *I, Jedi*, the 11 ABY election, *Children of the Jedi*, *Darksaber* and the Imperial Reunification, *Jedi Outcast* (12 ABY), *X-Wing: Starfighters of Adumar* and *Planet of Twilight* (13 ABY). See Part 1 for how to read the tables; back sections are in Part 5.
