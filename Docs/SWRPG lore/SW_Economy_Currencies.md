## Summary
- **The credit:** The Galactic Credit Standard (GCS) is the galaxy's main currency in every era. It was called "Republic credits" or "Republic dataries" under the Republic and the "Imperial Credit" under the Empire. The New Republic reclaimed it, but by then it was only one currency among many.
- **When the credit is refused:**
  - The Outer Rim often refuses it. On Tatooine Hutt coins (peggats, truguts, wupiupi) are preferred.
  - In 22 BBY most worlds outside the Core and Inner Rim refused credits and minted their own currencies, at up to twenty new currencies a day.
  - After Endor, Republic and Remnant would not accept each other's money, and traders fell back on precious metals.
- **In-universe exchange rates are few:**
  - CIS credit: more than 50% of a Republic credit, c.21 BBY.
  - King Kikipi credit: 1 Republic credit = 15 million after eight months, 22 BBY.
  - Imperial scrip: an unofficial 5:4 or 5:3 against credits.
  - Aurodium: ingots worth about 300 million credits each in 29 BBY [inferred].
- **Hutt coin rates** (peggat ≈ 40 cr, trugut ≈ 10 cr, wupiupi ≈ 0.625 cr) are a WotC game stat (2001) in Legends. Canon reference books give the same numbers.

### Galactic Credit Standard (Republic credit, datary, Imperial credit)
- **Type:** Main galactic currency. **Era:** From the Old Republic (date of creation not stated) through the Legacy era.

**Key facts:**
- [in-universe] Names by era:
  - "Republic Dataries" under the Republic; later the "Imperial Credit". It was the galaxy's main currency since the Republic.
  - Said to have been established on Sojourn (no date given).
  - Source: W:Galactic_Credit_Standard/Legends (lead). The lead is tagged {{Citation}}: see Uncertainty.
- [in-universe] Decicred = one tenth of a credit. Sources: W:Galactic_Credit_Standard/Legends; W:Decicred/Legends.
  - In the Imperial era, decicreds bore Palpatine's face on the obverse and the Imperial crest on the reverse (c.19 BBY–4 ABY).
- [in-universe] Physical forms by era. Source: W:Galactic_Credit_Standard/Legends (§Form).
  - Credit chips became popular "sometime during the height of the Republic".
  - During the Clone Wars (22–19 BBY) the credit also came as small metallic ingots.
  - Near the end of the Empire (c.4 ABY) physical currency grew more popular, because people feared credit chips would be worthless after the war.
- [in-universe] Origin as aurodium. "In days of old" Republic credits took the form of pure aurodium ingots, and ingots were still used for large trades in the late Republic. Source: W:Ingot.
  - The aurodium standard was abolished at some point after 4225 BBY. Source: W:Aurodium/Legends; W:Aurodium_standard/Legends.
  - After abolition, the Bank of the Core became a key backer of the Republic Treasury Reserve. Source: W:Republic_Treasury_Reserve (Hyperspace story "Tinker, Tailor, Soldier, Praji", 2009).
- [in-universe] Issuer. "When the Republic established the Galactic Credit Standard", the IGBC was its guarantor and distributor (date not given). Source: W:Muunilinst/Legends (§Early events).
  - The Republic's economic power rested partly on "production of Republic dataries". Source: W:Galactic_Republic/Legends (§Economy).
- [in-universe] Imperial era (19 BBY–4 ABY):
  - The IGBC became "the creditor for restructured currency of the Galactic Empire". Source: W:InterGalactic_Banking_Clan/Legends (§Imperial Period). That sentence cites *Geonosis and the Outer Rim Worlds* (WotC, 2004), so it is really game-book prose: [game stat: Geonosis and the Outer Rim Worlds, WotC 2004].
  - Few refused the credit under Palpatine, and the Alliance used it alongside the Alliance Credit. Larger-denomination coins existed but were not widely circulated. Source: W:Galactic_Credit_Standard/Legends (§Decline), citing *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition* (WEG, 1992): [game stat: SW RPG 2e, WEG 1992].
- [in-universe] Imperial economy: "virtual non-existence of inflation" (c.19 BBY–4 ABY, galaxy-wide). Source: W:Galactic_Empire/Legends (§Economy), citing *Star Wars: Imperial Handbook*, an in-universe Legends reference book released 14 Oct 2014 (W:Star_Wars:_Imperial_Handbook).
- [in-universe] What gave the credit its value. Rare and precious metals backed it (no date given):
  - [conflict] Aargau's metals were "the basis of the value of the Imperial Credit". Source: W:Aargau/Legends (lead).
  - [conflict] Muunilinst's mineral wealth gave "weight to the Galactic credit", and its vault-spires guaranteed "the credit of the entire galaxy". Muunilinst's riches were "too important for the stability of Imperial credit". Sources: W:Muun/Legends (§Cornering the market, citing *Darth Plagueis*, 2012); W:Muunilinst/Legends (§Geographic features; §Galactic Empire).
  - [conflict] The GCS page says Aargau backed the credit "from its inception". That sentence is tagged {{Fact}} (uncited) on W:Galactic_Credit_Standard/Legends.
  - The two worlds are not exclusive, because both are IGBC worlds. Not reconciled; see Decisions needed.
- [in-universe] Post-Endor (4 ABY onward):
  - Many currencies appeared and the New Republic credit was "by no means the most accepted".
  - Exchange rates "fluctuated wildly". The Republic and the Remnant would not accept each other's currency, so traders dealt in precious metals and commodities.
  - Sources: W:Galactic_Credit_Standard/Legends (§Decline); W:Galactic_Empire/Legends (§Economy).
- [in-universe] Even while Muunilinst was part of the Imperial Remnant, it guaranteed the New Republic credit. The Moffs could not act against the IGBC without destabilizing the Imperial credit (c.19 ABY or later; [unclear]: the page gives no year). Source: W:Muunilinst/Legends; W:Muun/Legends (§Later history).
- [in-universe] The IGBC lent to both sides in the Clone Wars, and decades later to both the New Republic and the Imperial Remnant. Source: W:Galactic_Credit_Standard/Legends; W:InterGalactic_Banking_Clan/Legends (§Imperial Period).

**Where accepted or refused:**
- [in-universe] 32 BBY, Mos Espa, Tatooine: Watto refused Republic credits ("I need something more real"). Source: W:Galactic_Credit_Standard/Legends (§Decline, quoting *The Phantom Menace*, 1999).
- [in-universe] 22 BBY, galaxy-wide: "an overwhelming majority of planets outside the Core and Inner Rim would not accept credits" and began minting their own. Source: W:Galactic_Credit_Standard/Legends (§Decline, citing HNN "Currency Upheavals Deliver Profits to IBC", 2002).
- [in-universe] 8.5 ABY, Tatooine: Quenton did not recognize New Republic credits and bid in peggats. Source: W:Peggat/Legends (*Tatooine Ghost*, 2003).
- [in-universe] 12 ABY, Crseih Station: Letters of Resources were worthless outside the New Republic, so Luke and Han could not negotiate with them. Source: W:Letter_of_resources (*The Crystal Star*, 1994).

**Related:** IGBC and Bank of the Core (SW_Economy_Institutions_Debt), Aurodium, Hutt coins.

**Uncertainty:**
- The GCS lead (Sojourn, Aargau backing) is flagged {{Citation}}/{{Fact}} on Wookieepedia.
- No source gives a founding date for the credit.

### Aurodium and credit ingots (Republic store of value)
- **Type:** Precious-metal currency and bullion. **Era:** Old Republic to Clone Wars.

**Key facts:**
- [in-universe] Before the credit standard, Republic credits were pure aurodium ingots. Source: W:Ingot.
  - Aurodium ingots were still used where Republic credits were not accepted. Source: W:Aurodium/Legends.
- [in-universe] 33 BBY, Dorvalla route: the Lucrehulk freighter *Revenue* carried aurodium ingots "worth two billion credits". Sidious had Gunray sacrifice it, and the Nebula Front stole the cargo. Source: W:Trade_Federation/Legends (§Terrorist attacks; *Cloak of Deception* and *Darth Plagueis*).
- [in-universe] 33 BBY: credits stolen in the Dorvalla incident were channelled through the Bank of Aargau into House Valorum. Source: W:Bank_of_Aargau/Legends.
- [in-universe] 29 BBY, Coruscant to Zonama Sekot: Obi-Wan carried 10 aurodium ingots worth 3 billion credits to buy a Sekotan ship. That is about 300 million credits per ingot [inferred]. Source: W:Aurodium/Legends; W:Ingot (*Rogue Planet*, 2000).
- [in-universe] Clone Wars (c.21 BBY), Sundari, Mandalore: gold and silver credit ingots were used to bribe a Mandalorian customs official to pass Moogan smugglers' tea. Source: W:Credit_ingot/Legends (TCW "Corruption").

**Related:** Mandalore (SW_Mandalorians_GGW_Part1 for the SWTOR era).

**Uncertainty:** The Legends Credit ingot page deduces ingot denominations. That is editor speculation; see Not used.

### Republic-era local currencies and wartime currency chaos (c.53–19 BBY)
- **Type:** Regional currencies. **Era:** Late Republic and Clone Wars.

**Key facts:**
- [in-universe] 22 BBY, galaxy-wide (IGBC):
  - The IGBC created new currencies "at a rate of twenty per day", managed by the InterGalactic Currency Exchange System.
  - New currencies were "seduced by an introductory one-to-one exchange rate".
  - The IGBC took "a percentage of every transaction" and posted an 18th straight quarter of rising profits.
  - Source: W:InterGalactic_Banking_Clan/Legends (§Separatist Crisis); HNN "Currency Upheavals Deliver Profits to IBC" (2002).
- [in-universe] 22 BBY, Toong'L system: "After only eight months, one Republic Credit will buy you 15 million King Kikipi credits". Source: W:King_Kikipi_credit (HNN 47, 2002).
- [in-universe] c.21–20 BBY, Mid Rim and Outer Rim (CIS):
  - After the Battle of Bassadro, the CIS credit held "more than 50 percent" of the value of a Republic credit in the Mid Rim, and it was the exclusive currency of hundreds of Outer Rim worlds.
  - The IGBC's non-exclusivity pact with the CIS bolstered it.
  - Source: W:CIS_credit (*Star Wars Insider* 66, "CIS Shadowfeed Dispatch 14:3:10", 2003).
- [in-universe] Clone Wars, galaxy-wide: the IGBC "backed the currencies of both sides". Source: W:Galactic_Republic/Legends (§Economy); W:Muunilinst/Legends (§Clone Wars).
- [in-universe] 27 BBY, Radnor (karsem):
  - 5,000 karsems was the average year's pay on Radnor, and also the price of a bio-isolation suit during the bioplague panic.
  - No credit rate is given.
  - Source: W:Karsem (*Jedi Quest: The Way of the Apprentice*, 2002).
- [in-universe] 22 BBY, Aargau: the mesarc was Aargau's currency (and a unit of time) in the last days of the Republic. No rate is given. Source: W:Mesarc (*Boba Fett: Maze of Deception*, 2003).
- [in-universe] c.50 BBY, Thaere Privo: Rast'tul kept a box of "several thousand credits worth of dataries" and Thaereian Stock Certificates. Source: W:Rast'tul. The source is the *Living Force* campaign adventures (WotC web, 2003–05), so treat it as [game stat: Living Force, WotC c.2003–05] if adventures count as game books. Listed here for the term "dataries".
- [in-universe] Mandalorian Wars era (c.3964–3960 BBY), Ralltiir: the Ralltiiri Colonial was the local currency, exchangeable for Republic credits "at most places". Source: W:Ralltiiri_Colonial (Hyperspace "Labor Pains", 2008).
- [in-universe] Late Republic, Socorro: the Socorran credit became "essentially valueless" as the Republic waned (no year; c.late Republic [unclear]). Source: W:Socorran_credit (*Star Wars Adventure Journal* 2, "Out of the Cradle", 1994, fiction).

**Related:** SW_Economy_Events (22 BBY currency upheavals).

**Uncertainty:** The Thaere dataries rest on a Living Force adventure, whose tier is unclear.

### Old Republic and Sith Empire currencies (c.3960–3630 BBY)
- **Type:** Regional and state currencies. **Era:** Old Sith Wars, Great Galactic War, Cold War, Galactic War.

**Key facts:**
- [in-universe] The GCS (as credits) appears throughout KOTOR, KOTOR II and SWTOR material (3956–3630s BBY). Source: W:Galactic_Credit_Standard/Legends (Appearances).
- [in-universe] 3950 BBY, Dromund Kaas: the Sith Empire used an "Imperial credit" as its main currency. Meetra Surik converted diamonds into local currency at Larvit's store. Source: W:Imperial_credit (*The Old Republic: Revan*, 2011).
- [in-universe] c.3643 BBY, Cato Neimoidia and Coruscant:
  - Kavin bills were rare and ancient by the Galactic War, but still circulated on Cato Neimoidia.
  - Senator Gunaray Dod used them in dealings with Black Sun (SWTOR mission "Follow the Money").
  - Source: W:Kavin_bill (SWTOR, 2011; *SWTOR Explorer's Guide*, 2011).
- [in-universe] c.3653 BBY, Vulta: Zeerid Korr paid with a bearer card. Source: W:Bearer_card (*Deceived*, 2011).

**Related:** Sith Empire Ministry of Logistics and Imperial Treasury Department (SW_Economy_Institutions_Debt; SW_SWTOR_Era_Power_Structures_Part2).

**Uncertainty:** No exchange rate between the Sith Empire's credit and the Republic credit was found (see Not found).

### Hutt currencies: peggat, trugut, wupiupi, druggat
- **Type:** Coinage, Tatooine and Hutt Space. **Era:** Late Republic to New Republic.

**Key facts:**
- [game stat: Secrets of Tatooine, WotC 2001] Conversion rates (see the "Game stat figures" section):
  - Peggat ≈ 40 cr.
  - Trugut ≈ 10 cr.
  - Wupiupi ≈ 0.625 cr.
  - Sources: W:Peggat/Legends; W:Trugut/Legends; W:Wupiupi/Legends.
- [in-universe] 32 BBY, Tatooine: peggats used in big deals:
  - Jabba offered 50,000 peggats for Anakin. Source: W:Watto/Legends.
  - Boles Roor won a 5-million-peggat wager. Source: W:Boles_Roor/Legends.
- [in-universe] 8.5 ABY, Tatooine: peggats were still legal tender after Endor (Quenton bid in peggats). Source: W:Peggat/Legends (*Tatooine Ghost*).
- [in-universe] 32 BBY, Mos Espa: Watto and Qui-Gon dealt for the T-14 hyperdrive generator. The sources disagree on the currency:
  - [conflict] 50 druggats. Source: W:Druggat/Legends (*TPM* novelization, 1999).
  - [conflict] The film has Qui-Gon offer 20,000 Republic credits, which Watto refuses. Source: W:Druggat/Legends.
  - [conflict] A game source gives 100 gold peggats. Source: W:Druggat/Legends.
  - All three are listed; none is chosen.
- [in-universe] c.0 BBY, Mos Eisley: a gold coin, "Jabba's marker", marked someone as in Jabba's employ. Source: W:Jabba's_marker (*Tales from the Mos Eisley Cantina*, 1995).

**Related:** SW_NarShaddaa_HuttSpace_GGW_Part1 (Hutt Space, SWTOR era); SW_Economy_Prices_Goods for coin prices of goods.

**Uncertainty:**
- Legends gives no in-universe Hutt-coin rate; the only rate is the WotC game stat.
- Canon reference books give identical rates ([canon] gap-fill, below).

### Imperial and Rebel-era currencies and scrip (c.19 BBY–9 ABY)
- **Type:** State and partisan currencies. **Era:** Galactic Civil War.

**Key facts:**
- [game stat: The Rebel Alliance Sourcebook, WEG 1990] Alliance Credit:
  - It was the official Rebel currency. The Empire declared it worthless, but sympathizers took it at face value.
  - Black-market rate: 25 Alliance Credits = 1 Imperial credit (c.0–4 ABY).
  - Source: W:Alliance_Credit.
- [in-universe] Military scrip (Imperial scrip), Empire to New Republic era:
  - Issued to the Imperial Military, with harsh penalties for refusing it. It was interchangeable with credits "for a small fee", but not accepted outside the Empire in the New Republic era.
  - "No official rate of exchange, but unofficially … five to four … or five to three".
  - Rate dated c.9 ABY [unclear]: the page cites *Han Solo and the Lost Legacy* (1980), *Dark Force Rising* (1992) and *A Guide to the Star Wars Universe* (1984) without saying which gives the rate.
  - Source: W:Military_scrip.
- [in-universe] c.2 BBY, Dellalt: the driit was the local monetary unit around the Battle of Yavin. Source: W:Driit (*Han Solo and the Lost Legacy*, 1980).
- [game stat: Galaxy Guide 6: Tramp Freighters, WEG 1990] Shesharile 5 (moon), Imperial era: the system currency depreciated 98%, and merchants demanded Imperial credits or precious metals. Source: W:Shesharile_5.
- [in-universe] c.3 ABY, Ewok/Endor region: the Zorbian pirates' currency was Novian rubies. Krag offered 1,000 Novian rubies as a bounty on Kneesaa. Source: W:Novian_ruby/Legends (*Ewoks* comic).

**Related:** SW_Economy_Events (post-Endor fragmentation).

**Uncertainty:** The year of the scrip rate is uncertain (above).

### Corporate Sector currencies (c.19 BBY–4 ABY)
- **Type:** Corporate currencies. **Era:** Imperial era.

**Key facts:**
- [in-universe] Crystalline vertex was the CSA's primary currency. Entrants had to exchange into vertex or Authority Cash Vouchers at Authority Currency Exchanges, and many other worlds also accepted vertex. Source: W:Crystalline_vertex/Legends (*A Guide to the Star Wars Universe*, 1984).
- [in-universe] Vertex also served as a Republic-era store of value:
  - The Jedi Temple treasury held a shipment of vertex (c.44 BBY, Coruscant). Source: W:Crystalline_vertex/Legends (*Jedi Apprentice: The Captive Temple*).
  - The All Planets Relief Fund held vertex in a Bank of the Core vault on Coruscant (24 BBY). Source: W:Crystalline_vertex/Legends (*Jedi Quest: The False Peace*).
- [in-universe] c.2 BBY, Corporate Sector: Authority Cash Vouchers were debt-receipt datacards used instead of currency. Source: W:Authority_Cash_Voucher (*Han Solo at Stars' End*, 1979).
- [in-universe] c.2 BBY, Corporate Sector:
  - Fire nodes, glow-pearls and nova crystals served as currency.
  - Solo was paid in fire-node gems as an advance for gunrunning to Duroon insurgents.
  - Sources: W:Fire_node; W:Glow-pearl (*Han Solo at Stars' End*).
- [game stat: Cynabar's Fantastic Technology: Droids, WEG 1997] CSA corporate script was accepted on CSA worlds, e.g. at the StarFlight Lounge on Bonadan. Source: W:CSA_corporate_script.
- [in-universe] Imperial era: the Authority Currency Exchange was the Sector's monetary authority. Source: W:Corporate_Sector_Authority/Legends (§Government and politics).
- [in-universe] Imperial era: the Bank of the Core minted "most of the Authority's currency". Source: W:Bank_of_the_Core/Legends.

**Related:** CSA taxes (SW_Economy_Institutions_Debt).

**Uncertainty:**
- The date of the Jedi Temple vertex shipment is c.44 BBY [unclear] (Jedi Apprentice series).
- The *Guide to the SW Universe* line is undated in-universe.

### New Republic era currencies (4–40 ABY)
- **Type:** State and local currencies. **Era:** New Republic, Yuuzhan Vong War, Galactic Alliance.

**Key facts:**
- [game stat: Twin Stars of Kira, WEG 1993] Early NR era (c.5–?? ABY), Krann: in the New Republic's early years, Nova crystals mined on Krann backed much of the government's hard currency until the mine ran dry. Source: W:New_Republic/Legends (§Economy).

- [in-universe] 13 ABY, Adumar: the perat was Cartann's currency and bore the ruler's face. Source: W:Perat (*Starfighters of Adumar*, 1999).
- [in-universe] 18 ABY, Drall: Ebrihim paid 1,150 Drallish crowns against a receipt for 1,250. Source: W:Drallish_crown (*Showdown at Centerpoint*, 1995).
- [in-universe] After Ithor's destruction (25 ABY), Ithorian currency became worthless ("worth about as much as Ithorian currency", c.27 ABY). Source: W:Ithorian_currency (*NJO: Dark Journey*, 2002).
- [in-universe] Physical forms in this era:
  - Credcards: e.g. a percussionist's credcard holding 1,000 credits (44 ABY). Sources: W:Credcard; W:Credit_card.

**Related:** SW_Economy_Events (Yuuzhan Vong War collapse).

### Forms of payment (all eras)
- **Type:** Payment media.

**Key facts:**
- [game stat: Star Wars Roleplaying Game Core Rulebook, WotC 2000] Credit chips:
  - Values ran from 1 to over 1 billion credits, with the limit set by the issuer, drawing on a central bank account.
  - The technology was perfected centuries before 32 BBY. Incomes were deposited directly, and the cred stick replaced the chip after the Empire rose.
  - Sources: W:Credit_chip/Legends; W:Cred_stick.
- [in-universe] Credit chit, c.50–25 BBY onward, Outer Rim:
  - Created by the Commerce Enhancement Corp and worth about 1/100 of a credit chip.
  - Used by the very poor in the Outer Rim.
  - Source: W:Credit_chit/Legends (*Essential Guide to Characters*, 1995, et al.).
- [in-universe] 20 BBY: Palpatine paid Boba Fett with a credit cube. Source: W:Credit_cube (*Boba Fett: Pursuit*, 2002).
- [in-universe] Imperial era, galaxy-wide: Imperial notes and bullion existed. Source: W:Leonia_Tavira (*X-Wing: Rogue Squadron* 14–16 comics). The raid date is c.6–7 ABY [unclear].
- [in-universe] Imperial era: the Imperial Cash Voucher had an unlimited limit (Major Lessev). Source: W:Imperial_Cash_Voucher (*Cracken's Rebel Operatives*, WEG 1994): [game stat: Cracken's Rebel Operatives, WEG 1994].
- [game stat: Galaxy Guide 2: Yavin and Bespin, WEG 1989] Credit tokens:
  - Plastic tokens on backwater planets, forgeable "but not worth it".
  - The interchip moved credits from a chip to a store.
  - Sources: W:Credit_token; W:Interchip.
- [game stat: Galaxy Guide 4: Alien Races, WEG 1989] Voucher chip, a promissory note (Obo Rin's expenses). Source: W:Voucher_chip.
- [game stat: Star Wars Roleplaying Game Core Rulebook, WotC 2000] Electronic vouchers were traceable; con artists used bogus credit slips. Sources: W:Electronic_voucher; W:Credit_slip.
- [in-universe] Core Courier Service, Imperial era (year not given [unclear]): employers paid wages in pay vouchers, which had to be deposited. Source: W:Pay_voucher (*Adventure Journal* 7, "Retreat from Coruscant", 1995).
- [in-universe] Coruscant, era not stated [unclear]: credit vouchers paid Metrocab Robo-Hack fares. Source: W:Credit_voucher (*Essential Guide to Droids*, 1999).

**Related:** SW_Economy_Prices_Goods (transport fares).

**Uncertainty:** Several form pages draw only on game books (tagged).

### Precious metals as money
- **Type:** Commodity money.

**Key facts:**
- [game stat: Pirates & Privateers, WEG 1997] Gold ≈ 10–15 cr per gram; silver 150–200 cr per kg (Imperial era). Sources: W:Gold/Legends; W:Silver/Legends.
- [game stat: Galaxy Guide 2, WEG 1989] Platinum 15–25 cr per gram. Source: W:Platinum/Legends.
- [in-universe] c.3 ABY, Aargau: the "First Law" forbade removing precious metals without approval, and a smuggler was executed for it. Sources: W:Gold/Legends; W:Aargau/Legends (*Star Wars* (1977) 48, Marvel, 1981).
