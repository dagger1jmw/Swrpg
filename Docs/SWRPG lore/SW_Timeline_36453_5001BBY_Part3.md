Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 24, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends), as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
Part 3 covers 7,003–5,001 BBY: the Second Great Schism, the Hundred-Year Darkness and the Battle of Corbos (6,900 BBY), the Exiles' conquest of Korriban and the founding of the Sith Empire, and the Sith Golden Age down to the last years of Marka Ragnos (his death in 5,000 BBY opens the next file). It then holds the back sections for all three parts: the answer to "when did the Jedi move their centre from Tython to Coruscant" (they did not in this span: Tython → Ossus c. 25,783 BBY; Coruscant land 5,000 BBY; Coruscant headquarters 3,996 BBY), "Changes from the previous file", Not found, Canon only, Not used, Game notes, Decisions needed, Sources and the checklist.

## SECOND GREAT SCHISM, HUNDRED-YEAR DARKNESS AND THE EXILES — 7,003–6,899 BBY
## ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

### Overview
Jedi experimenting with Sith-style alchemy break away in 7,003 BBY; three years later their war on the Jedi and Republic, the **Hundred-Year Darkness**, begins. It ends at Corbos in 6,900 BBY. The twelve surviving leaders, the **Exiles** (*Jen'jidai*), are stripped of their weapons and cast out beyond the Republic. They find Korriban, kill King Hakagram Graush, and make themselves Sith Lords under Ajunta Pall. At the same time a Sith civil war on Korriban under Dathka Graush (c. 7,000–6,950 BBY) has just ended.

## MANDERON PERIOD: THE SITH GOLDEN AGE — c. 6,899–5,001 BBY
## ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

### Overview
The Republic forgets the Sith; the Sith Empire grows rich, sorcerous and isolated behind the Stygian Caldera, ruling at least 120 worlds, enslaving crews of ships that stray in. The Mandalorians begin raiding. Dark Lords include Tulak Hord (centuries before 5,000 BBY) and Marka Ragnos, who wins the title from Simus c. 5,100 BBY, rules for over a century, and **forbids expansion**, fearing the Jedi. The Koros system's Unification Wars begin in 5,010 BBY. **Blank stretches:** 6,740–6,100 BBY and 5,975–5,500 BBY are nearly empty; most Sith events of these 1,900 years are undated.

## When did the Jedi move their centre from Tython to Coruscant? (question asked for this span)
**Short answer: within 36,453–5,001 BBY they never did. The Jedi left Tython for Ossus c. 25,783 BBY and stayed centred on Ossus for this whole span. Coruscant became their main headquarters only after Ossus was devastated in 3,996 BBY. The existing timeline's row "~25,000 BBY Jedi Order relocates to Coruscant from Tython; establishes Temple" is wrong.**

1. **Tython → Ossus, c. 25,783 BBY.** After the Force Wars' cataclysm wrecked Tython's biosphere, the Order left the Deep Core with its surviving texts, went to Wild Space and colonised Ossus [TL §Pre-Republic era](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Timeline_of_galactic_history/Legends#Pre-Republic_era); [Jedi Order/Legends §Early interactions with the Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order/Legends). Ossus's article dates the founding of the Jedi school there to c. 25,783 BBY (SWTOR Encyclopedia) [Ossus/Legends §Early history](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ossus/Legends). [Ruled (Jared, 24 Nov 2026): the cataclysm; Lyn's line is her opinion] [conflict on motive: *The Essential Guide to Warfare* has Arden Lyn say the Order left Tython "to distance itself from the machinations of the growing Republic" (same source)].
2. **Joining the Republic without moving, c. 24,953 BBY.** Republic scouts reached Ossus by the new Perlemian route; Master Haune Tiar toured the Republic and the Order pledged itself to its service, "remaining isolated on Ossus"; Ossus became a fortress world guarding the Perlemian [24,953 BBY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/24,953_BBY); [Haune Tiar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Haune_Tiar); [Jedi Order/Legends §Early interactions with the Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order/Legends); [Ossus/Legends §Early history](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ossus/Legends).
3. **Still on Ossus through the crises.** In the Tionese War the Jedi withdrew "again to Ossus" before joining the fight [Jedi Order/Legends §Early interactions with the Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order/Legends); in the Recusal (11,933 BBY) they retreated to Ossus for about eight centuries [Ossus/Legends §Jedi stronghold](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ossus/Legends); a few generations after 10,966 BBY they "returned to their peaceful ways on Ossus" [Jedi Order/Legends §The beginning of the Sith](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order/Legends). Ossus was so central that many scholars wrongly thought it the Order's birthplace [Ossus/Legends §Jedi stronghold](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ossus/Legends).
4. **First foothold on Coruscant: 5,000 BBY (next file).** After the Battle of Coruscant in the Great Hyperspace War the Republic granted the Jedi land over the sacred spire, a Force nexus, hoping for a fortress; the Order built only a small meditative and training enclave [Jedi Temple/Legends §The Old Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Temple/Legends); [Jedi Order/Legends §Coruscant](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order/Legends).
5. **Coruscant as main headquarters: from 3,996 BBY (outside this span).** The Coruscant Temple was the Order's main headquarters "from the end of the Great Sith War in 3996 BBY"; after Ossus and its library were devastated, the Order moved its academy to the spire and the Four Masters began the Temple Ziggurat [Jedi Order/Legends §Coruscant](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order/Legends); [Jedi Temple/Legends §The Old Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Temple/Legends). This agrees with `SW_Coruscant_Jedi_Temple_OldRepublic_Part1.md`.

Footnote: Jedi did have isolated presences on Coruscant earlier (the insane Jedi Humat's Cell of Imu before 19,000 BBY; fighting on Coruscant in the First Great Schism and the Tionese War), but no source puts the Order's centre there before 5,000 BBY.
