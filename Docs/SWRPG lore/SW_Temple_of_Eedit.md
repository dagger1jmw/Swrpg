## Summary
The Temple of Eedit is a small Jedi temple in the jungle near Tikaroo on Devaron. It was built over a vergence in the Force as an academy for Devaronians suited to the Jedi, and as a place of renewal. Its vergence is strongest in the courtyard, where it makes learning new Force techniques and healing the spirit easier. No founding date is given. Its recorded history is in the Clone Wars.

**Source warning:** the archivum page mixes Legends and Canon. The Legends-usable works are *The Clone Wars* (2008) episodes, and much of the page's later history comes from Canon works. The vergence and founding description isn't attributed to a work on the mirror, so check the footnote on the Wookieepedia /Legends page (decision 1).

## Entries

### The Temple of Eedit
- **Type:** location (Jedi temple; site-level Force nexus)
- **Era:** founding date not given; documented in the Clone Wars
- **Key facts:**
  - A Jedi temple on the jungle planet Devaron, built on a vergence in the Force and used as a place of personal renewal and strengthening [Temple of Eedit, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/temple_of_eedit).
  - **Why it was built there:** Devaron had many Force-sensitives and a powerful vergence, and the Order built the temple at the vergence as an academy for Devaronians best suited to join the Jedi [Temple of Eedit, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/Temple_of_Eedit).
  - **The vergence:** strongest in the courtyard, it helps with learning new Force techniques and healing the spirit [Temple of Eedit, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/temple_of_eedit).
  - **Setting:** deep in the jungle near the city of Tikaroo. A grand avenue, the Eedit Road, runs straight through the jungle to the staircase at the main door. The temple is small compared with the Order's larger facilities, and its style borrows from the towers of the Coruscant Temple [Temple of Eedit, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/Temple_of_Eedit).
  - **Layout:** two grey stone towers joined at the base. A circular courtyard with a central fountain is used for meditation, combat practice and lessons. Statues of ancient Jedi stand around the towers, which hold classrooms, a dining area, living quarters and caretakers' offices. An escape tunnel runs from the main hall under the courtyard into the jungle [Temple of Eedit, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/temple_of_eedit).
  - **Clone Wars:** staffed only by a caretaker Master and his apprentice. The Republic used it as a surveillance station, which made it a Separatist target. On Count Dooku's orders Savage Opress attacked up the Eedit Road and killed the caretakers, Master Halsey and Padawan Knox [Temple of Eedit, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/temple_of_eedit). The battle is dated 21 BBY [Battle at the Temple of Eedit, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/Battle_at_the_Temple_of_Eedit).
- **Related:** SW_Force_Nexus_Vergence (decision 5); Devaron (planets file).
- **Uncertainty:**
  - The founding date isn't given, so its earliest date is [unclear].
  - The source of the vergence and founding description isn't confirmed (decision 1).
