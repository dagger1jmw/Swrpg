# SWRPG Galaxy Timeline, 45–138 ABY (Part 2 of 3): The Second Imperial Civil War and the Third Jedi Purge

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 24, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Rulings of 24 Nov 2026 applied (lore-rulings-2026-11-24c.md; D-484).

## Summary
Part 2 covers 130–138 ABY: Darth Krayt's Galactic Empire, Roan Fel's Empire-in-exile, Gar Stazi's Galactic Alliance Remnant and the hidden Jedi, through Cade Skywalker's story in *Legacy* (2006) 1–50 and *Legacy — War* 1–6, ending with Krayt's death, the Galactic Federation Triumvirate and the start of Darth Wredd's insurgency. The years 131–136 ABY are almost empty; nearly everything happens in 137 and 138 ABY. The table conventions are those of Part 1. Events from *Legacy* (2006) 48–50 are dated 137 ABY by the issues and 138 ABY by the *Extremes* collection; 138 ABY is ruled (24 Nov 2026).

### Overview
From 130 ABY Darth Krayt ruled the galaxy from Coruscant as Sith Emperor and Dark Lord of the One Sith, served by his Voice Darth Wyyrlok (III), his Hand Darth Nihl, Darth Maladi as head of Sith Intelligence and Assassination, and a Council of Moffs led by Grand Moff and Grand Admiral Morlish Veed [Darth Krayt's Galactic Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Krayt's_Galactic_Empire); [Darth Nihl](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Nihl); [Darth Maladi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Maladi); [Morlish Veed](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Morlish_Veed). Krayt controlled most of the galaxy, including Coruscant, Ralltiir and Dac [Second Imperial Civil War §Origins](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Imperial_Civil_War). Against him stood the deposed Emperor Roan Fel's Empire-in-exile, with the Imperial Knights under Antares Draco and loyal units such as the 501st Legion and the Bastion Second Fleet, and the Galactic Alliance Remnant under Admiral Gar Stazi, which waged seven years of hit-and-run warfare [Second Imperial Civil War §Origins](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Imperial_Civil_War); [Antares Draco](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Antares_Draco); [Galactic Alliance Remnant](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_Alliance_Remnant). Former Alliance Triumvirs governed former Alliance space as Imperial viceroys [Triumvirate (Galactic Alliance)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Triumvirate_%28Galactic_Alliance%29). The Mandalorians, under Yaga Auchs, stayed out [Yaga Auchs](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yaga_Auchs). The surviving Jedi hid; by 137 ABY a reconvened Jedi Council of Masters K'Kruhk, Tili Qua and T'ra Saa sat at the Hidden Temple on Taivas, and no Grand Master is named [Cade Skywalker](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cade_Skywalker); [Attack on the Hidden Temple](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Attack_on_the_Hidden_Temple). The war and the purge ended at the Battle of Coruscant in 138 ABY with Krayt's death, Roan Fel's death and the founding of the Galactic Federation Triumvirate of Empress Marasiah Fel, Admiral Stazi and Jedi Master K'Kruhk [Second Imperial Civil War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Imperial_Civil_War); [Galactic Federation Triumvirate](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_Federation_Triumvirate).
