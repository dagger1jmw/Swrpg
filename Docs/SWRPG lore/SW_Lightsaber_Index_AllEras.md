# Lightsaber Combat Research Set: Index, Combined Decisions and Catalog Check (All Legends Eras)

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 21, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
This index lists the seven lightsaber-combat research files (forms, sub-group styles, training, weapon variants, combat techniques, duels and rankings), sums up the continuity checks used across them, and collects every file's Decisions needed into one numbered list. All facts are in the topic files, each cited inline there; this file states no lore facts. The catalog check against the developer's file (coverage item 6) is done; it is in SW_Lightsaber_Forms_Catalog_Check_Part1.md and _Part2.md.

## Decisions needed
Combined and renumbered from each file. Each file numbers its own list from 1.

**Forms (Part 1)** (SW_Lightsaber_Forms_AllEras_Part1.md)

1. Lightsaber becomes the standard Jedi weapon [conflict]: 7000 BBY (Lightsaber combat/Legends, citing *Jedi Knight*) or 5000 BBY (Force-imbued sword/Legends). Neither clearly fits more sources on the pages checked.
2. Niman origin [conflict]: Kashi Mer Royale Macheteros style (Form VI/Legends, backed by Kashi Mer Dynasty) or "developed from Jar'Kai" (Lightsaber combat/Legends). The Kashi Mer version fits more sources.
3. "Vaapad" before Windu [conflict]: an in-universe old name (Path of Destruction, Book of Sith, reconciled by Insider 92) or an author error (Karpyshyn [BTS] 2006). The Insider 92 reconciliation fits all in-universe sources.
4. Juyo-Kos: a separate form or a Juyo variant? The sources call it "a refinement" of Juyo, which fits a variant.
5. "Sith Shien" (named, never described): keep it as an alias of Shien or omit it?
6. Niman mastery time: use Drallig's "10+ years" as the game's benchmark (the only mastery time given for any form)?

**Forms (Part 2)** (SW_Lightsaber_Forms_AllEras_Part2.md)

7. NJO Strong/Medium/Fast: catalog them as forms, or as stances layered on forms? The sources call them the "three forms of the New Jedi Order" ([Lightsaber combat/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends)), which fits forms.
8. Dun Möch by Jedi: the sources leave Jedi use "theorized" or doubtful. Give it a dark-side cost when a Jedi uses it?
9. Tràkata for Jedi: the sources say Jedi "refrained" from it, yet Mara Jade and Corran Horn used it. Allow it with a conduct note?
10. Trispzest: restrict it to fliers or levitators (S'kytri, Maw), which fits all sources?
11. Include thin-source entries (Lus-ma, Juyo-Kos, Su ma) as playable, or as lore only?
12. Untrained dual-wielding ("the Jar'Kai tactic", [Lightsaber combat/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends)): give it a separate penalty profile from trained Jar'Kai?

**Sub-group styles** (SW_Lightsaber_SubgroupStyles_AllEras.md)

13. Mandalorian anti-Jedi methods: model them as gear plus tactics only (fits the sources), or invent a named style (not lore)?
14. Make Matukai, Zeison Sha and Jensaarai playable traditions with their own weapons (wan-shen, discblade, cortosis armor)?
15. Sith sword dark-side amplification: a mechanical bonus or flavor only?
16. Zeison Sha origin [conflict]: the New Sith Wars (Zeison Sha page) vs Zeison Sha armor appearing in KOTOR II (3951 BBY). The New Sith Wars origin is the stated history; the armor is a game-item appearance.
17. Echani: limited to the Echani people, or learnable by anyone? It was taught to special forces, the Royal Guard and Imperial Intelligence, which fits "learnable".

**Training** (SW_Lightsaber_Training_AllEras.md)

18. First saber forging [conflict]: Initiates craft their own on Ilum (Jedi training/Legends) or Padawans forge their first (Padawan/Legends). The Padawan version fits the Padawan page's detailed ritual and the Training lightsaber page (Initiates use training sabers).
19. Form access by rank: Forms I–VI for Initiates, with Form VII behind a selection or permission flag (both sourced). Adopt as the game rule?
20. Ataru discouragement c. 1000 BBY: an era-specific penalty or a lock for Padawans?
21. NJO era: reduced form access until the forms are rediscovered (fits [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Redevelopment](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends))?

**Weapon variants** (SW_Lightsaber_WeaponVariants_AllEras.md)

22. Training saber power [conflict]: a permanent low-power setting (Lightsaber/Legends) or readjustable by a fully trained Jedi (Training lightsaber/Legends, citing *The Jedi Path*). The readjustable version is the more specific and cited one.
23. *Saga Edition* RPG claims (shoto built for throwing; Sith sword weights): use as game data or treat as lore? (They stay labelled game stats either way.)
24. Saberstaff conduct: the Jedi "frowned on" it, with no rule against it. Give it a Jedi conduct penalty or flavor only?
25. Lightwhip "short out violently when struck with enough force": make it a breakage rule?
26. Electrostaff bisection setting [gray] (*Force and Destiny*, 2015): keep the fact (fits Legends, unconfirmed) or drop it?

**Combat techniques** (SW_Lightsaber_CombatTechniques_AllEras.md)

27. Cortosis effect [conflict]: block-only (KOTOR I/II, *Obi-Wan*) or short-out (later sources). The short-out version is the page's main description. Duration [conflict]: instant re-ignition (*Vision of the Future*) or useless for minutes (*Purge*, *Legacy*).
28. Marks of contact ethics: give sai tok ("Sith-like") and lethal shiak targets a dark-side or conduct weight?
29. Tutaminis absorbing a saber blade (a "rare feat"): allow it, and from what rank?
30. Beskar impact transfer (force "still transferred in part to the wearer"): model blunt damage through beskar?
31. Ysalamiri: the sources give no saber-duel rule. Should a Force-neutral bubble simply disable Force-assisted techniques?

**Duels and rankings** (SW_Lightsaber_Duels_AllEras.md)

32. Exar Kun's record [conflict]: "never defeated in a lightsaber duel" (Form VI/Legends) vs "his previous loss against Baas' greater skill" (Duel in the Senate). The Senate page cites *Dark Lords of the Sith* 1 directly; Form VI also says he beat Baas in sparring.
33. Rankings: encode them as hard per-era tiers, or use them as soft calibration only?
34. Cross-era comparisons: none are sourced. Should the game allow them at all?
