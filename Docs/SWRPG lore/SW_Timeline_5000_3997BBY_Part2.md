# SWRPG Galaxy Timeline, 5,000–3,997 BBY (Part 2 of 3): The Post-Manderon Period, the Beast Wars and the Rise of the Krath

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 24, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
Part 2 continues the timeline from 4,975 BBY to the end of 3,997 BBY: the hidden Sith Empire on Dromund Kaas and the Lost Tribe after Yaru Korsin; the Gank Massacres (4,800–4,775 BBY); Freedon Nadd's fall (4,400 BBY) and the Beast Wars of Onderon; the Third Great Schism and Vultar Cataclysm (4,250 BBY); the Republic's growth and the frontier conflicts of the 4,000s (Taris, Lorell, the Mandalorian Crusaders at Nevoota, Basilisk and Kuar); the Great Droid Revolution (4,015 BBY); the Sunriders; the end of the Beast Wars (4,000 BBY); the Freedon Nadd Uprising and the founding of the Krath (3,998 BBY); and 3,997 BBY, the year of the Krath coup, the Conclave at Deneba, Exar Kun's fall and Marka Ragnos's anointing of Kun and Ulic Qel-Droma. Wookieepedia dates the Great Sith War itself to 3,996 BBY; it belongs to the next file. Column and Type rules are as in Part 1.

## ════════════════════════════

### Overview
The millennium after the Great Hyperspace War is the post-Manderon period (5,000–4,000 BBY); its listed conflicts are the Gank Massacres, the Beast Wars and the Third Great Schism, and its listed events the Vultar Cataclysm and the Great Droid Revolution [Post-Manderon period](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Post-Manderon_period). The Republic believed the Sith gone, while the Sith Emperor rebuilt in secret on Dromund Kaas [Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_%28Post%E2%80%93Great_Hyperspace_War%29). In 4,400 BBY the Padawan Freedon Nadd killed his Master, apprenticed himself to the slumbering Naga Sadow on Yavin 4, killed him and made himself king of Onderon; his dynasty's exiles became the Beast Riders, whose war with Iziz lasted until 4,000 BBY [Freedon Nadd](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Freedon_Nadd) [Beast Wars](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Beast_Wars). The Republic pushed its frontier outward: it met the Mon Calamari, surveyed new systems and fought pirates and raiders, while from 4,024 BBY the Mandalorian Crusaders began the campaigns that would lead them into the Great Sith War [Timeline of galactic history/Legends §Post-Manderon period (5000 BBY-4000 BBY)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Timeline_of_galactic_history/Legends) [Nevoota Extinction](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nevoota_Extinction).
