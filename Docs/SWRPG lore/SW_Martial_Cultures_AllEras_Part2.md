# Martial Cultures and Non-Lightsaber Combat Traditions, All Eras (Part 2 of 3): Armed Traditions and Warrior Cultures

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched 24 Nov 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends), read through the Wookieepedia API, as cited per entry. Rulings of 24 Nov 2026 applied (lore-rulings-2026-11-24c.md, 11–20; D-484).

Part 2 covers armed fighting traditions outside the lightsaber and the warrior cultures of species and orders. Tags and conventions as in Part 1. Back sections for the whole set are in Part 3.

## Summary

Non-lightsaber armed traditions in Legends cluster around a few cultures: the Echani (Firedancers, the ritual brand, foils and double-bladed vibroblades), the Thyrsian Sun Guard (the pike), the Matukai (the wan-shen), the Noghri (the fighting sickle), the Wookiees (bowcaster and ryyk blade, learned through the hrrtayyk), the Trandoshans (hunting for Jagannath points, honour swords), the Gamorreans (the arg'garok), Tuskens (the gaderffii), Geonosians (the Petranaki arena array), the Witches of Dathomir and the Yuuzhan Vong warrior caste (the amphistaff). Mandalorian weapons culture is summarised only, with cross-references to the Mandalorian files.

### 2. The Handmaiden Sisters

- **Type:** Echani warrior sisterhood (six sisters)
- **Era:** 3,956 BBY (after the Jedi Civil War) to 3,951 BBY.
- **Key facts:** the six daughters of the Echani General Yusanis; served Jedi Master Atris as handmaidens at her secret academy on Telos IV; trained extensively in close-quarter weapons and unarmed combat, highly skilled in the Echani art and its advanced forms, alone and in groups; their weapon was a quarterstaff that collapsed into its handgrip. The five eldest looked identical; the youngest, Brianna, had the face of her mother, the Jedi Master Arren Kae, and kept her father's staff (*KOTOR II*). [Handmaiden Sisters](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Handmaiden_Sisters)
- **Related:** Echani art (Part 1).
- **Uncertainty:** none.

### 3. The Sun Guard of Thyrsus

- **Type:** mercenary company and warrior tradition (Thyrsian Echani)
- **Era:** founded between 1,154 and 899 BBY; destroyed c. 19 BBY; restored c. 25 ABY.
- **Key facts:**
  - Thyrsian mercenary company, one of the most feared in the galaxy; formed from rebellious Thyrsian special forces after 1,154 BBY; fought the Echani Command until the Pact of Almera confirmed Thyrsus's independence in 899 BBY. [Sun Guard](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Guard)
  - Rivals of the Mandalorians; subverted by the Order of the Sith Lords; many served the Sith directly under Supreme Sun Guardian Thull Wulain; some were considered as the prime clone for the Grand Army. Its 2nd Regiment was crushed in the Clone Wars by General Solomahal, and Dooku had Asajj Ventress eliminate the rest. [Sun Guard](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Guard)
  - Survivors joined Palpatine's Red Guard and Royal Guard; the few Force-sensitive ones joined the Shadow Guard. A new generation defended Thyrsus against the Yuuzhan Vong c. 25 ABY. [Sun Guard](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Guard)
  - **What they trained in:** mostly not Force-sensitive; skilled melee fighters with weapons built into black armor; prized hand-to-hand combat; skilled in personal gladiatorial fighting and urban warfare; "unmatched by any opponents but the Mandalorians". The Sun Guard also trained in the Echani art (*Hero's Guide*). [Sun Guard](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Guard) · [Echani (martial art)/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Echani_(martial_art)/Legends)
  - **Signature weapon:** the pike, a two-handed bladed weapon built in variants for specific tasks. [Sun Guard](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Guard)
  - Legacy: Royal Guard armor derives from Death Watch and Sun Guard uniforms (Arhul Hextrophon, *Imperial Sourcebook*); also Nashiak Llalik's stormtrooper armors, Carnor Jax (son of a Sun Guard) and Moxin Tark's replica armor. [Sun Guard](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Guard)
- **Requirements:** Thyrsian (Echani subspecies) membership; Force-sensitivity not required.
- **Related:** Royal Guard and Shadow Guard (Part 3).
- **Uncertainty:** the Sun Guard page lists *SkyeWalkers: A Clone Wars Story* (a TCW-era source) as needing integration; the Clone Wars CIS affiliation cites *Kinect Star Wars* and *SkyeWalkers*. Not used for anything here.

### 4. The wan-shen (Matukai)

- **Type:** polearm
- **Era:** Matukai era (c. 4,000 BBY onward).
- **Key facts:** made by a Matukai on graduating from apprentice to adept; a polearm about 2 m long with a short single blade at one end; a focus for the Force as well as a weapon; decorated to its maker's taste, with etched patterns; most break down into pieces for carrying and concealment; trained users whirl it around themselves "as a central axis of rotation" with astonishing speed. Because they are made with the Force, many wan-shen resist lightsabers. [Wan-shen/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wan-shen/Legends) · [Matukai/Legends, Abilities](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Matukai/Legends)
- [game-derived] Game stats: 1,000 credits, 2 kg (*Jedi Academy Training Manual*). [Wan-shen/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wan-shen/Legends)

### 5. Noghri weapons culture

- **Type:** species weapons culture
- **Era:** 19 BBY onward (sources from the Imperial era)

- **Key facts:**
  - Noghri prefer primitive weapons such as knives, and unarmed combat (*The Force Unleashed Campaign Guide*). Trained in many weapons, they usually carry only a few daggers, trusting their surroundings to supply the rest. [Noghri/Legends, Society and culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Noghri/Legends)
  - Their greatest pride is the fighting sickle, a long-handled blade that marks honour and is balanced for hand-to-hand fighting; Noghri are renowned for throwing them. [Noghri/Legends, Society and culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Noghri/Legends) · [Fighting sickle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fighting_sickle)
  - Stava includes the sickle, quarterstaff, vibroblade and Vrakolian spin-blade (Part 1). [Stava (skill)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stava_(skill))
- See Part 3 for death commandos and bodyguards.

### 7. Trandoshan hunting tradition (Jagannath points; the Scorekeeper)

- **Type:** religious hunting culture
- **Era:** all eras (Old Republic sources through the New Republic).
- **Key facts:**
  - Trandoshans worship the Scorekeeper, a goddess beyond time and space, who awards Jagannath points for each kill; rarer or more prestigious prey score more; the state of the pelt does not matter. Wookiees are prized targets (rare breeds such as silverbacks, or infamous individuals, score highly). [Scorekeeper/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scorekeeper/Legends) · [Trandoshan/Legends, Society and culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trandoshan/Legends)
  - Shame or capture during a hunt wipes a hunter's points; they are restored only by killing the one responsible (*The Mandalorian Armor*). A hunter who dies without ever earning a point is said to be reborn as prey. [Jagannath point/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jagannath_point/Legends) · [Trandoshan/Legends, Society and culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trandoshan/Legends)
  - This aggressive way of life leads many Trandoshans into bounty hunting, mercenary work and slaving. Owning an X10-D draft droid requires a certain number of Jagannath points (*Essential Guide to Droids*). Trandoshans honour life debts; the first recipient is a *ghrakhowsk*. [Trandoshan/Legends, Society and culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trandoshan/Legends)
  - Physical traits: stronger and sturdier than Humans, infrared-sensitive eyes, slow regeneration of lost limbs. Allied early with the Empire, enslaving Wookiees. [Trandoshan/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trandoshan/Legends)
  - Weapons: Trandoshans kept the "primitive" sword as a badge of honour; the Trandoshan sword and double-bladed sword were traditionally forged from the rare ore chalon, sharper and heavier than Republic blades. For live capture, hunters favoured the Slavemaster stun carbine. [Trandoshan/Legends, Weaponry](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trandoshan/Legends)
  - Thrar'kor: a bladed melee form; the Trandoshan mercenary Nakaron was proficient. Nakaron was exiled from Dosha city after city for disrespecting the Elders of Forak. [Thrar'kor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Thrar'kor) · [Trandoshan/Legends, Society and culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trandoshan/Legends)
- **Uncertainty:** the Weaponry section and the Nakaron passage have no inline citations [unclear]. The Accelerated Charged Particle guns named in that section are Clone Wars items that appear to come from TCW material [S-canon, unclear]; not used.

### 8. Gamorrean warriors (arg'garok)

- **Type:** species warrior culture

- **Key facts:** porcine humanoids from Gamorr (Outer Rim); fierce warriors prized for strength and personal combat. Clans are led by a Council of Matrons; sows do all productive work while boars concentrate on martial training and war. Favourite weapon: the arg'garok war axe. [Gamorrean/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gamorrean/Legends)
- **Arg'garok:** a large vibro-ax designed for beings of great strength and a low centre of gravity; needs great skill and strength; non-Gamorreans often struggle with it, and only the strongest, best-trained warriors use it well (*Complete Star Wars Encyclopedia*; *KOTOR Campaign Guide*) [game-derived: 150 credits and 2 kg]. [Arg'garok/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Arg'garok/Legends)
- In the Imperial era, after 4 BBY, the Gamorrean Thok learned Teräs Käsi under Arden Lyn (Part 1).

### 10. Witches of Dathomir

- **Type:** clan-based warrior society of Force-users
- **Era:** founded c. 600 BBY; through the New Republic and Galactic Alliance eras.
- **Key facts:**
  - Also called Dathomiri or the Daughters of Allya, after the banished Jedi Knight Allya (c. 600 BBY, *New Essential Chronology*). Female-led clans named after landmarks: Singing Mountain, Misty Falls, Red Hills, Dreaming River, Frenzied River, Great Canyon; positions of Initiate and Mentor; a clan mother leads; the Book of Law; all clans but the Nightsisters hold to "never concede to evil" (*The Courtship of Princess Leia*). [Witches of Dathomir](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Witches_of_Dathomir)
  - **Martial life:** as a warrior society they trained daily in the art of war, using both muscle and the Force; they wore elaborate braids, sometimes helmets decorated with mementos, and lizard-skin armor. Clans raided each other for mates. Each clan lived in a fortified village; women were warriors and leaders. [Witches of Dathomir, Culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Witches_of_Dathomir)
  - Force powers were framed as chanted "spells"; dark-side use, or using anger to fuel a power, was punished by exile, and exiles who stayed out formed the Nightsisters. [Witches of Dathomir, Culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Witches_of_Dathomir)
  - Off-worlders visited Dathomir to pick suitable individuals as assassins or enforcers. [Witches of Dathomir, Culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Witches_of_Dathomir)
  - Clan count: more than a dozen in 8 ABY, nine by 12 ABY. [Witches of Dathomir, Culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Witches_of_Dathomir)
- **Requirements:** Dathomiri birth (female, for warrior status); Force-sensitivity for spells.
- **Related:** Force Traditions file (Dathomir magic, cross-reference only).
- **Uncertainty:** the Culture section carries few inline citations. [unclear] "Dathomiri mistdance" exists only in Canon (see Canon only).

### 11. Yuuzhan Vong warrior caste (brief)

- **Type:** military caste
- **Era:** Yuuzhan Vong War, 25–29 ABY.
- **Key facts:** second-largest Vong caste; conditioned from birth to be brave, fierce and merciless, and to obey superiors without question; most aggressive and self-important caste; refusing a challenge disgraces all Yuuzhan Vong; they close to melee range whenever they can (*Star Wars Roleplaying Game Revised Core Rulebook*); surrender is not an option and retreat is cowardice; captured warriors kill themselves; they respect courage in foes. In honour of their patron Yun-Yammka the Slayer they sacrifice body parts and scar and tattoo themselves. Ranks: Supreme Overlord (as commander-in-chief), Warmaster, Supreme Commander, Commander, Subcommander, Subaltern, Warrior; unofficial ranks Warleader, Battle Tactician, Field Commander, Warrior-seer. Elite group: the Hunters. Some experienced Chazrach were admitted to the lower levels. [Warrior caste](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Warrior_caste)
- **Amphistaff:** genetically engineered serpent, the Vong's main anti-personnel weapon; staff, spear and whip forms; can be thrown and can spit venom. Wedge Antilles: "With an amphistaff in his arsenal, a Vong is never truly alone" (*NEGWT*). [game-derived: reach, range and weight figures come from RPG books]. [Amphistaff](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Amphistaff)
- Unarmed moves: asth-korr throat-hold, q'urh kick (Part 1).
- **Uncertainty:** most of the Overview has no inline citations. [unclear]
