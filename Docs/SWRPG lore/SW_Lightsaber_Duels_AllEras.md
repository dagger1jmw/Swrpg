# Lightsaber Anchor Duels and Sourced Rankings (All Legends Eras)

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 21, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
Covers about 40 anchor duels from the Great Sith War (3997 BBY) to the Second Imperial Civil War (138 ABY), giving the combatants, forms or weapons, outcome and the mechanics lesson where sources state one. It also lists duelist rankings only as the sources state them, era by era. Key anchors: Lehon (1000 BBY, Force power beat Kas'im's superior swordsmanship), Coruscant (19 BBY, a form-switch feint against Dooku) and Mustafar (19 BBY, Soresu against Djem So, decided by the high ground). The duel tables are in the Timeline section.

## Game notes (optional, suggestions only)
*(Design suggestions only.)*
- Calibration anchors could include:
  - Lehon: Force power beats blade skill.
  - Tython: five-on-two loss shows the effect of armor and Force strength.
  - Coruscant 19 BBY: form-switch feint.
  - Naboo: terrain limits Ataru.
  - Byss: strength breaks a blade-lock.
- Unit tests could check that the sim reproduces these outcomes within plausible variance.
