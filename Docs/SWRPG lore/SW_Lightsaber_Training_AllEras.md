# Lightsaber Training Progression (All Legends Eras)

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 21, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
Covers how lightsaber combat was taught from the earliest Order on Tython to the Legacy era: ranks (Initiate, Padawan, Knight), drills, trials, Battlemasters and access to forms in each era. Most detail is for the post-Ruusan Order (1000–19 BBY), mainly from *The Jedi Path* (2010). Key facts: Initiates learned Forms I–VI with training sabers. Padawans spent a year or two on each of Forms II–V. Juyo/Vaapad needed hand-picked selection or Windu's permission. Training lasted nearly two decades before Knighthood.

## Entries

### 1. Early Order (Tython → Ossus, pre-Republic to Old Sith Wars)
- **Type:** training system
- **Era:** Pre-Republic to the Old Sith Wars (c. 4000 BBY)
- **Key facts:**

- **Trials and selection:**
  - Students were tested at their Master's discretion, and Knighthood came only at the Master's word.
  - The ancient Trial of Skill meant keeping focus under stress (balancing upside-down on a post in a storm).
  - Combat tests used Force-imbued swords and archaic sabers and were "extremely difficult and sometimes dangerous".
  - This testing was abandoned because Masters set the bar too high. [Jedi training/Legends §Training in the Old Jedi Order](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_training/Legends)
  - There was no age limit. Masters took adult apprentices and often had several students, running homeworld schools that met at conclaves. [Jedi training/Legends §Training in the Old Jedi Order](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_training/Legends)
- **Ossus:** Became the teaching center after Odan-Urr founded the Great Jedi Library. [Jedi training/Legends §Training in the Old Jedi Order](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_training/Legends)
- **Wars with the Sith:** Heroics against a Sith Lord on the battlefield once counted for the Trials of Courage and of the Flesh. This was later dropped, since courage was hard to tell from recklessness. [Jedi training/Legends §Training in the Old Jedi Order](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_training/Legends)
- **Pius Dea era:** The "Burning" version of the Trial of the Flesh used shock weapons, low-power blaster fire or cuts. The High Council later banned it. [Jedi training/Legends §Training in the Old Jedi Order](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_training/Legends)
- **Old Sith Wars (c. 4000 BBY):**
  - Masters had several apprentices (Arca Jeth → Ulic and Cay Qel-Droma, Tott Doneeta).
  - Knights kept training under other Masters.
  - Family members trained relatives (Nomi Sunrider → Vima). [Jedi training/Legends §Training in the Old Jedi Order](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_training/Legends)

- **Related:** Forms Part 1; WeaponVariants (Force-imbued swords)
- **Uncertainty:** none

### 2. Great Galactic War and Cold War (SWTOR era)
- **Type:** training system
- **Era:** Great Galactic War and Cold War (SWTOR; exact years not given on the cited pages)
- **Key facts:**

- Padawan training (corrected (lore batch 13), ruling 11): Core World politics before the war; the shift toward practical military readiness and diplomatic service came with wartime liaison with the Republic military, and was completed in the Cold War after the Order moved to Tython. [Padawan/Legends §Instruction](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Padawan/Legends)
- The Battlemaster title went to Jedi who excelled in combat. Jun Seros replaced the dead Kao Cen Darach. The Hero of Tython was later named Battlemaster. [Battlemaster/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battlemaster/Legends)
- The Jedi developed Juyo-Kos; Jaric Kaedan was a master. On the Sith side, Scourge studied Forms VII, IV and III. [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Practice and Training during the Sith Resurgence](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends)

- **Related:** Forms Part 2 (Juyo-Kos)
- **Uncertainty:** none

### 3. New Sith Wars (to 1000 BBY)
- **Type:** training system
- **Era:** New Sith Wars, ending 1000 BBY
- **Key facts:**

- **Jedi side:**
  - Lord Hoth was the official Battlemaster, but most Jedi learned sabers on the front lines. Army of Light lords and generals declared themselves Battlemasters by proving their skill. [Battlemaster/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battlemaster/Legends)
- **Sith side (Brotherhood of Darkness, Korriban Academy):**
  - Kas'im was saber instructor; his students included Bane. [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Practice and Training during the Sith Resurgence](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends)
  - Students fought practice duels against one another, and these could be lethal: Bane killed Fohargh in one. Fohargh had used Dun Möch to humiliate Bane. Kas'im later taught Bane in secret against Headmaster Qordis's orders. [Kas'im §The Brotherhood of Darkness](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kas%27im) [Dun Möch/Legends §Usage](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dun_Möch/Legends)
  - Kas'im discouraged Jar'Kai, though he secretly fought with two blades. [Jar'Kai/Legends §Alternative Uses](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jar%27Kai/Legends)
- **Form access around 1000 BBY:** Padawans were discouraged from Ataru, because youthful energy led to sloppy cadences. [Form IV/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_IV/Legends)

- **Related:** Duels (Korriban academy, Lehon 1000 BBY)
- **Uncertainty:** none

### 4. Post-Ruusan Order (1000 BBY → 19 BBY)
- **Type:** training system and ranks
- **Era:** 1000 BBY to 19 BBY
- **Key facts:**

#### Reforms
- Under Grand Master Fae Coven and the Council of First Knowledge, training was centralized on Coruscant and many praxeums closed. [Jedi training/Legends §Training in the Old Jedi Order](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_training/Legends)
- **Age restriction:** only infants and very young children became Initiates. Republic law required Force-sensitivity testing at birth, and parents' consent was sought. [Jedi training/Legends §Training in the Old Jedi Order](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_training/Legends) [Jedi Initiate/Legends §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Initiate/Legends)
- **One Master, one apprentice;** five formal Trials of Knighthood. [Jedi training/Legends §Training in the Old Jedi Order](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_training/Legends)
- **One Battlemaster at a time,** with satellite-academy head instructors hand-picked by and reporting to the Battlemaster. [Battlemaster/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battlemaster/Legends)
  - The line: Skarch Vaunk (a few years after Ruusan) → … → Cin Drallig (last days of the Republic; killed by Vader, which ended the line). [Battlemaster/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battlemaster/Legends)
  - Sub-instructors included Kirlocca (Almas Academy), Dooku, Sora Bulq and Anoon Bondara. [Battlemaster/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battlemaster/Legends)

#### Initiate stage (about age 3 to adolescence)
- **Clans:**
  - About ten Initiates, placed through the Force by fosterers.
  - They live and study together for "a little over a decade" under a clan instructor. [Jedi Initiate/Legends §Youngling Clans](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Initiate/Legends)
  - They stay together from about age three until chosen as Padawans around adolescence. [Jedi Initiate/Legends §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Initiate/Legends)
- **Day:**
  - Force studies in the morning (Control first).
  - Classroom work at midday.
  - Physical and lightsaber training in the afternoon.
  - Five mandatory meditations. [Jedi Initiate/Legends §Instruction](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Initiate/Legends) [Jedi training/Legends §Training in the Old Jedi Order](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_training/Legends)
- **Gate:** Students had to prove themselves in other classes before they could wield a weapon. [Jedi Initiate/Legends §Instruction](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Initiate/Legends)
- **Forms taught:** Forms I–VI ("six levels of lightsaber combat") with training sabers. [Jedi training/Legends §Training in the Old Jedi Order](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_training/Legends) [Jedi Initiate/Legends §Instruction](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Initiate/Legends)
- **Early drills:**
  - Blindfolded Shii-Cho blast-deflection to rely on the Force rather than the senses.
  - Then sequences and velocities until moves become reflexes. [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Training Methods](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends)
- **Training sabers:**
  - Real sabers with an intensified containment field: welts, bruises and minor burns, no amputation.
  - A Knight can readjust one for real use in an emergency. [Training lightsaber/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Training_lightsaber/Legends)
  - Traditionally an Initiate needed to perform the **Sevinte cadence** with strikes within a hand's breadth of the opponent before getting a real saber. [Training lightsaber/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Training_lightsaber/Legends)
- **Initiate Trials:**
  - Three tests: knowledge of the Jedi Code; meditation and dueling technique (observed); fluency with the Force.
  - Failing means a retake. Students cannot spend an extra year at the academy, so it's progress or leave.
  - Those who fail go to the Service Corps (no saber needed). [Jedi Initiate/Legends §Instruction](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Initiate/Legends)

#### Padawan stage
- **Duration:** Typically a decade or more, with no set term. Padawans keep taking flexible Temple classes and one-on-one lessons. They learn Sense after Control. [Padawan/Legends §Instruction](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Padawan/Legends) [Jedi training/Legends §Training in the Old Jedi Order](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_training/Legends)
- **Saber forging:**
  - Study saber crafting, travel with the Master to Ilum's Adegan crystals, and enter a deep trance (often with visions). [Padawan/Legends §Instruction](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Padawan/Legends)
  - [conflict] JT says Initiates trained "until they crafted their own in the caves of Ilum" (implying the Initiate stage), but PAD puts the first forging during the apprenticeship. [Jedi training/Legends §Training in the Old Jedi Order](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_training/Legends) [Padawan/Legends §Instruction](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Padawan/Legends)
- **Forms:** By the Republic's waning years, Padawans spent a year or two on each Form from II to V, including Ataru. [Form IV/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_IV/Legends)
- **Juyo/Vaapad access:**
  - After the Ruusan-era falls to the dark side, the Council restricted Juyo, and Vaunk taught it to only a few hand-picked students each year. [Form VII/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VII/Legends)
  - Drallig forbade Form VII to all but a select few and refused it to Anakin. Qui-Gon forbade Obi-Wan from learning Vaapad. [Form VII/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VII/Legends)
  - Windu allowed Vaapad only with his permission, given to Depa Billaba alone. [Form VII/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VII/Legends)
- **Drills:**
  - Sequences: hundreds per form, choreographed.
  - Sparring, including rule-bound specialty matches.
  - Dulon: a solo exercise of sequences against imaginary foes, also the name of a move.
  - Velocities: paired, ever-faster sequences until a hit or a yield ("Solah").
  - Twin Suns: paired Force-leap passes that cut a piece of clothing, then deactivate before landing.
  - Vo'ren Faalo's five cadences: strike ball bearings atop a growing ring of candles; by the fifth, no candles are needed. [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Training Methods](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends) [Dulon/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dulon/Legends)
  - Twin Suns needed great mastery. [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Twin Suns](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends)
- **Trials of Knighthood:**
  - Overseen by the Council and the Battlemaster in a Temple chamber.
  - Afterwards a night of meditation in the Tranquillity Spire; the Grand Master cuts the braid in the Hall of Knighthood.
  - Failing means a retake or the Service Corps. [Padawan/Legends §Instruction](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Padawan/Legends)
- **Trial of Skill:**
  - Saber technique, but judged mainly on avoiding distraction.
  - Opponents could be flesh or simulacra, sometimes the Battlemaster or Grand Master, several foes, or Sith Lord simulacra. [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Trial of Skill](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends)

#### Knight stage
- Further study as Guardian, Consular or Sentinel, with specializations (Watchmen, Seers). A Knight may take a Padawan. [Jedi training/Legends §Training in the Old Jedi Order](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_training/Legends)
- Training totalled "nearly two decades" before Knighthood. [Jedi training/Legends §Training in the Old Jedi Order](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_training/Legends)
- **Temple Guards:** used saber pikes and were mostly Niman users, with Soresu as well. [Jedi Temple Guard/Legends §Equipment and skills](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Temple_Guard/Legends)

#### Weapon norms in this era
- Makashi was seen as obsolete after Ruusan.
- By 32 BBY, combat use of double-bladed sabers (as opposed to training) was "practically unheard of". [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Post-Ruusan developments](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends)

### 5. Dark Times / Galactic Civil War (19 BBY → 4 ABY)
- **Type:** training system
- **Era:** 19 BBY to 4 ABY
- **Key facts:**

- Training was "hasty and brief" for scattered Force-sensitives. Luke was taught by Kenobi and then Yoda. [Jedi training/Legends §Dark years](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_training/Legends)
- Kyle Katarn trained himself in days, guided by Qu Rahn's spirit; Luke confirmed his Knighthood. [Jedi training/Legends §Dark years](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_training/Legends)

- **Related:** Duels (Cloud City 3 ABY)
- **Uncertainty:** none

### 6. New Republic / New Jedi Order (c. 11 ABY → 25 ABY)
- **Type:** training system
- **Era:** NJO academy era (Katarn Battlemaster c. 13/14 ABY)
- **Key facts:**

- Students started much older and went to an academy. Combat was simplified and techniques lost. ASP-19 droids were used for training. [Jedi training/Legends §In the New Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_training/Legends)
- Masters often had several apprentices. Training sabers went to adult Padawans (Rosh Penin). [Jedi training/Legends §In the New Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_training/Legends) [Training lightsaber/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Training_lightsaber/Legends)
- **Kam Solusar:** first head of combat instruction, created the three rings of defense. [Battlemaster/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battlemaster/Legends)
- **Kyle Katarn:** Battlemaster c. 13/14 ABY; introduced the Fast/Medium/Strong styles with katas for single, dual and double-bladed sabers. [Battlemaster/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battlemaster/Legends)
- **Rudimentary Trials:** demonstrate a skill set and build a saber. Masters could knight after a mission that proved the student (Jaden Korr after Vjun). [Jedi Knight/Legends §Overview and history](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Knight/Legends)
- **Clans:** At first Luke formed none, which he thought might explain early defections. After recovering *The Jedi Path* he started the Ronto and Veermok Clan, the first of many. [Jedi Initiate/Legends §Youngling Clans](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Initiate/Legends)

- **Related:** Forms Part 2 (NJO styles)
- **Uncertainty:** none

### 7. Galactic Alliance (post–Yuuzhan Vong War, 29 ABY →)
- **Type:** training system
- **Era:** After the Yuuzhan Vong War
- **Key facts:**

- Training became more regimented and Old Republic-like.
  - Age groups (Woodoos, Rontos, Banthas), each with set techniques (Force jump, telekinesis, persuasion, centering).
  - Students become "apprentices" once they build a saber. [Jedi training/Legends §In the Galactic Alliance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_training/Legends)
  - Skorch was a training game for referees. [Jedi training/Legends §In the Galactic Alliance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_training/Legends)

- **Related:** —
- **Uncertainty:** none

### 8. Legacy era (c. 130 ABY)
- **Type:** training system
- **Era:** Legacy era
- **Key facts:**

- **Imperial Knights:** drafted Force-sensitives trained for years on Bastion. They learn Praetoria Vonil and Ishu team styles alongside the classical forms. [Praetoria Vonil](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Praetoria_Vonil) [Praetoria Ishu](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Praetoria_Ishu) [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Redevelopment](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends)

- **Related:** SubgroupStyles (Imperial Knights)
- **Uncertainty:** none

## Game notes (optional, suggestions only)
*(Design suggestions only.)*
- Rank gates by era could be:
  - Post-Ruusan: Initiate → Forms I–VI basics; Padawan → II–V depth; Knight → specialization; Juyo only through a hand-picked or permission flag.
  - NJO: styles first, forms rediscovered later.
- The Sevinte cadence could be the "real saber unlocked" milestone.
- Faalo's five cadences could map to five precision tiers.
- Velocities and the Twin Suns drill could be training mini-games.
