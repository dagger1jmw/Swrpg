# Star Wars Legends: Dromund Kaas and Kaas City, c. 3700–3653 BBY, Part 2: Daily life, other Imperial worlds and people

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 21, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
- **Society:**
  - Imperial society put Force-sensitive Sith above everyone else, with Humans and purebloods favoured over aliens;
  - slavery was hereditary and permanent;
  - every non-Force-sensitive adult was conscripted;
  - Force-sensitives were sent to an academy "willing or not". During the window that was mainly Korriban, after its recapture in 3681 BBY. Narrowed in lore batch 12 (ruling 4): in the war only the decree sending Force-sensitive slaves is dated; the law covering all Force-sensitives in Imperial territory is attested from the Cold War. An Imperial-born child in 3661 BBY goes to Korriban by custom and family ambition [inferred], or to the lesser Dromund Kaas academy.

  [Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War) §Society and culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_(Post–Great_Hyperspace_War)); [Imperial Military (Sith Empire) §Organization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Military_(Sith_Empire)); [Sith training §Sith Training under the Empires](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_training)
- **Belief and news:** the Emperor was revered as god and king, and state news was propaganda. [Tenebrae §Legacy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrae); [Ministry of Intelligence (Sith Empire)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ministry_of_Intelligence_(Sith_Empire))
- **Other Imperial worlds:** Korriban was retaken in 3681 BBY. Ziost's New Adasta was a trade hub. Vaiken Spacedock is Cold War-only, and Ord Radama was a Republic battlefield rather than an Imperial core world. [Korriban §Great Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Korriban/Legends); [New Adasta §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/New_Adasta); [Vaiken Spacedock](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vaiken_Spacedock); [Battle of Ord Radama](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Ord_Radama)

### Society, class and species
- **Type:** society
- **Era:** general (undated), with dated items noted.
- **Key facts:**
  - **Population and class:**
    - the Imperial population was much smaller than the Republic's;
    - it was split between Force-sensitive Sith (the elite, who held the upper echelons of power) and non-Sith;
    - every citizen could still become an Imperial agent, Grand Moff or Grand Admiral.

    [Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War) §Society and culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_(Post–Great_Hyperspace_War))
  - **Ideology:**
    - Imperials believed their cause was righteous and that the strong should rule and the weak serve;
    - they saw the Republic as corrupt, chaotic and bloated, and held the Senate in contempt;
    - many saw the war as one of survival rather than conquest.

    [Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War) §Society and culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_(Post–Great_Hyperspace_War))
  - **Remembered history:** citizens were "educated about" the end of the Great Hyperspace War and the Republic and Jedi slaughter of the Sith. Sith and Imperials alike believed they must destroy the Jedi or be destroyed. [Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War) §Society and culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_(Post–Great_Hyperspace_War))
  - **Species hierarchy:**
    - Sith purebloods and Humans were treated as master races;
    - humanoid aliens were considered fit only to be slaves, though above non-humanoids;
    - Humans had "no restrictions on their movements or the ranks they could reach";
    - by implication aliens did [inferred from "unlike aliens"].

    [Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War) §Society and culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_(Post–Great_Hyperspace_War))
  - **Non-Force-sensitive purebloods:** rare and "considered an abomination". Unless their family bought them an administrative post, they were killed or enslaved. [Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War) §Society and culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_(Post–Great_Hyperspace_War))
  - **Military families:** prestigious officer families with long traditions of service existed, but their children received "no special treatment" and had to earn their place. [Imperial Military (Sith Empire) §Organization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Military_(Sith_Empire))
  - **Pureblood extremism:** Darth Ikoral refused to tolerate alien slaves in his household. He left c. 3712 BBY to find lost purebloods, and "for decades his name was synonymous with determination and sacrifice" as a supposed martyr, which spans the window. [Darth Ikoral §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Ikoral)
- **Uncertainty:** the "restrictions on movement" for aliens are not detailed in any source found.

### Slaves and aliens
- **Type:** society
- **Era:** general, with window-dated items.
- **Key facts:**
  - **Who was enslaved:** slavery was a holdover from the old Empire, mostly of alien non-citizens. The Emperor decreed that no slave could ever be freed and that slave status was inherited. As a result most slaves were Twi'leks, Duros and Zabraks, descended from captives of the Great Hyperspace War. [Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War) §Society and culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_(Post–Great_Hyperspace_War))
  - **Treatment:**
    - slaves were "expected to be treated harshly";
    - befriending one was frowned upon;
    - some Sith kept them "somewhat like pets";
    - prisoners of war and captured households added to their numbers.

    [Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War) §Society and culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_(Post–Great_Hyperspace_War))
  - **Wartime exception (window):** during the Great Galactic War, any slave of adequate Force sensitivity was sent to Korriban as an acolyte, so success or death was their "freedom". [Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War) §Society and culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_(Post–Great_Hyperspace_War)); [Korriban §Cold War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Korriban/Legends)
  - **Medical discrimination:** field medical theatres restricted treatment of non-humanoids in favour of humanoid species. [Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War) §Society and culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_(Post–Great_Hyperspace_War))
  - **Malgus (pre-war):** as a youth, Malgus killed a Twi'lek servant on his adoptive father's estate for a trivial offence. His father was pleased. [Darth Malgus §Early life](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Malgus)
  - **Malgus and Eleena:** Malgus later took the Twi'lek slave Eleena Daru as his lover, defying "anti-alien and anti inter-species marriage policies". [Darth Malgus §Early life](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Malgus)
  - **Darth Vich:** shortly after the Empire's return, he broke "a thousand years of anti-alien tradition" by recruiting Rattataki. The Council enslaved or killed them after his failed bid for power. [Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War) §Preparations for war](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_(Post–Great_Hyperspace_War))
  - **Slave labour (post-window):**
    - 300 slaves built the Spires of Victory [Spires of Victory](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Spires_of_Victory);
    - Nalis Quarry slaves revolted, c. 3643 BBY [Nalis Quarry](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nalis_Quarry); [Dromund Kaas rebels](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dromund_Kaas_rebels);
    - soldiers guarded slaves from jungle predators in the Riverfall Wilds [Molcarrus](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Molcarrus).
  - **Oversight:** the Ministry of Logistics supervised the Empire's slave labour. [Ministry of Logistics §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ministry_of_Logistics)
- **Uncertainty:** where slaves lived in Kaas City is not stated.

### Law, order and movement
- **Type:** institutions and practices
- **Era:** mixed (see each item)
- **Key facts:**
  - **Kaas City military police:** maintained order in Kaas City under the Sphere of Laws and Justice. Eckard Lokin worked as its science and medical adviser in the **pre-war years**, so the force predates the window. [Kaas City military police](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kaas_City_military_police); [Eckard Lokin §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eckard_Lokin)
  - **Imperial Guard:**
    - non-Force-sensitive guardsmen mentally bonded to the Emperor;
    - "a common and feared sight" on Dromund Kaas by 3959 BBY;
    - protectors of the Citadel during the Great Galactic War;
    - also defenders of the Korriban academy after 3681 BBY.

    [Imperial Guard §Protecting the Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Guard)
  - **Honor Guards (3678 BBY):** they dragged Calypho out of the Council chamber. [Darth Thanaton §Hunting for power](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Thanaton)
  - **Secret police:** Imperial Intelligence's "Minders" were the internal security and secret police (undated). [Ministry of Intelligence (Sith Empire)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ministry_of_Intelligence_(Sith_Empire))
  - **Emergency measures (c. 3950 BBY):** martial law, a curfew and a planetary lockdown after the Purge riots. This is the only attested curfew; none is attested for the window. [Purge of the Dark Council §Public unrest](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Purge_of_the_Dark_Council)
  - **Customs (c. 3950 BBY):** the customs official demanded registration and up-front fees, and payment in local currency. She let an unregistered ship stay "off the record" for an extra fee. [Unidentified customs official (Dromund Kaas)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_customs_official_(Dromund_Kaas))
  - **Passes (post-window):** in the Cold War, public transport was open only to Imperial citizens, with passes issued by the Ministry of Logistics. [Ministry of Logistics §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ministry_of_Logistics)
  - **Kaggath:** A Kaggath between Darths Qalar and Victun destroyed the first Citadel in 4043 BBY; the Council executed both. [Imperial Citadel (Sith Empire) §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Citadel_(Sith_Empire))
- **Uncertainty:** no window-era curfew, ID or pass system was found.

### Childhood, schooling and Force-sensitivity testing
- **Type:** practice
- **Era:** mixed; window items marked.
- **Key facts:**
  - **Sending Force-sensitives away:** "Any individual discovered to be Force-sensitive would be sent, willing or not, to the academy as an acolyte." This describes the Korriban academy after its re-establishment in 3681 BBY; as a law covering every Force-sensitive in Imperial territory it is attested from the Cold War (lore batch 12, ruling 4). [Sith training §Sith Training under the Empires](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_training)
  - **Where Sith trained:** the Sith Order trained members in academies on Korriban and Dromund Kaas. It also had lesser academies (e.g. Odacer-Faustin) and its headquarters in the Sith Sanctum. [Sith Order (Sith Empire)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Order_(Sith_Empire))
  - **Scourge (pre-war):** found to be Force-sensitive in 3964 BBY, he was sent to Dromund Kaas and enrolled as "a young acolyte". He trained for eight years before a posting in 3956 BBY. [Scourge (Sith) §Early life](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scourge_(Sith))
  - **Malgus (pre-war, born 3700 BBY):**
    - as a child he took Force lessons from "a number of instructors";
    - his father reported his killing of a servant to the Dromund Kaas Sith Academy, after which he was sent there to train as a Sith Warrior;
    - he was later chosen as apprentice by Darth Vindican.

    [Darth Malgus §Early life](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Malgus)
  - **Thana Vesh (window, after 3681 BBY):**
    - a wealthy military family on Dromund Kaas tried to suppress their daughter's Force sensitivity;
    - she killed her parents and burned a city block;
    - "the Sith took her to Korriban".

    [Thana Vesh's parents](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Thana_Vesh's_parents); [Thana Vesh §Early Life](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Thana_Vesh)
  - **Children of the Emperor (window):**
    - infants were stolen from their parents by the Emperor's Servants;
    - on Korriban they went through rituals and alchemical machinery in the academy's depths under Imperial Guard watch;
    - the project was being completed "by the third year" of the war.

    [Children of the Emperor §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Children_of_the_Emperor); [Tenebrae §A renewed war](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrae)
  - **Science Bureau project (c. 3685 BBY):** the Imperial Science Bureau and Imperial Intelligence chose a family and genetically altered a child in the womb. [Imperial Science Bureau (Sith Empire) §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Science_Bureau_(Sith_Empire))
  - **Non-Force-sensitive children:** no civilian schooling system is described. Citizens were "educated about" the Great Hyperspace War (see Society). [Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War) §Society and culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_(Post–Great_Hyperspace_War))
- **Uncertainty:**

  - [conflict] Malgus's childhood home. Best fit: born on Dromund Kaas, raised off-world.

### Conscription and military life
- **Type:** practice
- **Era:** from Vaiken (after 4980 BBY) "for centuries"; window examples.
- **Key facts:**
  - **Universal service:** every non-Force-sensitive was automatically conscripted on reaching adulthood and served until retirement or disability. [Imperial Military (Sith Empire) §Organization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Military_(Sith_Empire))
  - **Vaiken's regimen:** led to "the drafting of every able-bodied adult" and remained in use "for over a millennium". [Imperial Military (Sith Empire) §Organization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Military_(Sith_Empire)); [Imperial Military training regimen](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Military_training_regimen)
  - **Training on Dromund Kaas:** ground forces began live-fire exercises in the Dromund Kaas jungles within weeks of enlisting. Even non-combatants learned basic weapons, survival and first aid. [Imperial Military (Sith Empire) §Army](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Military_(Sith_Empire))
  - **Examples (window):**
    - Vasil Dorne (born c. 3703 BBY) enlisted in 3685 BBY, fought 20 years, and became garrison commander in 3665 BBY [Vasil Dorne §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vasil_Dorne);
    - Malavai Quinn (born on Dromund Kaas c. 3680 BBY) idolised the Imperial Guard as a child, "the highest honor a non-Sith could achieve", and was a lieutenant by 3653 BBY [Malavai Quinn §Early life and service](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Malavai_Quinn);
    - Rycus Kilran came from a wealthy Dromund Kaas military family [Rycus Kilran §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rycus_Kilran).
  - **Renewed conscription:** the Emperor renewed conscription before the next war (Cold War, post-window). [Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War) §The Cold War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_(Post–Great_Hyperspace_War))
- **Uncertainty:**
  - [conflict] Elara Dorne's enlistment year: 3648 BBY [Elara Dorne §Early life in the Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Elara_Dorne) vs 3647 BBY [Vasil Dorne §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vasil_Dorne). This is post-window and minor.

### Propaganda, religion and public life
- **Type:** culture
- **Era:** mixed
- **Key facts:**
  - **The Emperor as god and king:**
    - citizens saw him as "both a god and king", and almost all would die for him;
    - phrases such as "Glory to the Emperor", "Long Live the Emperor" and "It's enough to wake the Emperor" came from him (general).

    [Tenebrae §Legacy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrae)
  - **From the beginning:** the people "had come to see the Emperor as a kind of god" on arrival in 4980 BBY. [Tenebrae §Rebuilding an empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrae)
  - **Iconography:** the hooded figure was "the common depiction of the Emperor" in statuary (Nyriss's courtyard, c. 3954 BBY). The Dark Temple's obelisk bears an all-seeing eye representing him. [Darth Nyriss's stronghold §Layout](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Nyriss's_stronghold); [Dark Temple §Layout](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Temple)
  - **State news:**
    - the Ministry of Intelligence's Outreach Bureau gave citizens the daily news, often slanted, with "copious amounts of propaganda" (undated) [Ministry of Intelligence (Sith Empire)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ministry_of_Intelligence_(Sith_Empire));
    - the Imperial News Network broadcast by holoprojector and audio in the Cold War (post-window) [Imperial News Network (Sith Empire)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_News_Network_(Sith_Empire)).
  - **Managed truth:**
    - the Emperor publicly announced that Revan and Malak had been executed, when he had in fact sent them back as agents (c. 3960 BBY) [Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War) §The Emperor's machinations](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_(Post–Great_Hyperspace_War));
    - after Exal Kressh's betrayal (window, c. 3678 BBY), he declared the name Kressh "an abomination" and had the family line erased from the records [Tenebrae §A renewed war](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrae).
  - **Public celebrations (window):** victory parades and ceremonies in Kaas City in 3678 BBY, including Ekkage's branding of the Red Legion. [Darth Thanaton §Hunting for power](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Thanaton); [Darth Ekkage §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Ekkage)
  - **Entertainment:**
    - opera house (undated) [Kaas City opera](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kaas_City_opera);
    - cantinas, such as the Nexus Room (c. 3950 BBY; jukebox band by 3643 BBY) [Nexus Room](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nexus_Room); [Leisurenauts §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Leisurenauts);
    - a noble who gossiped about politics in his favourite cantina (undated) [Unidentified Kaas City noble](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Kaas_City_noble);
    - young thrill-seekers fighting yozusks and gundarks [Yozusk](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yozusk);
    - public hiking at Kaas Falls (undated) [Kaas Falls](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kaas_Falls);
    - the Heritage Museum (post-window) [Heritage Museum](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Heritage_Museum).
  - **Rival worship (post-window):** the Order of Revan cult worshipped Revan. [Dromund Kaas §Capital of the Sith Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dromund_Kaas/Legends)
- **Uncertainty:** no organised civic religion beyond Emperor-reverence is described.

### Economy and supply
- **Ministry of Logistics duties:**
  - wartime calculations;
  - running nationalised factories;
  - agricultural worlds for military food;
  - slave labour;
  - the civilian transport and trade network;
  - Dromund Kaas public transit.

  [Ministry of Logistics §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ministry_of_Logistics)
- **Shullis Khamarr (window):** Minister of Logistics from 3660 BBY. She "made it her priority to serve the needs of those on the planet of Dromund Kaas". Her ministry failed to resupply Malgus at Ord Radama. [Shullis Khamarr §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shullis_Khamarr); [Minister of Logistics](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Minister_of_Logistics)
- **Currency and prices (c. 3950 BBY):** a local currency existed; Surik had to convert a diamond and was pointed to Larvit for a fair price. [Unidentified customs official (Dromund Kaas)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_customs_official_(Dromund_Kaas))
- **Wartime strain (late war):** worlds stripped of resources and peoples. [Tenebrae §A renewed war](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrae)
