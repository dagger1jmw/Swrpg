# Jedi Order Ranks and the Jedi High Council (c. 3,700–3,600 BBY)

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched 21 November 2026 (revision 3: conflicts listed without picking a version, per rule 6; gray-area sources re-checked, per rule 7). Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
This file covers the Jedi ranks from Initiate to Grand Master, the period's honorary titles, and the makeup and role of the Jedi High Council, limited to about 3,700–3,600 BBY. That window takes in the Great Galactic War (3681–3653 BBY), the Cold War (3653–3641 BBY), the Galactic War (3641–3636 BBY), the Eternal Empire conquest (3636 BBY) and its aftermath up to the Order's regrouping by 3627 BBY. The key facts are these:
- In 3653 BBY the Coruscant Temple was wrecked and Grand Master Zym was killed.
- The Council moved the Order to a new Temple on Tython and made Satele Shan Grand Master.
- Satele Shan's term ended c. 3636 BBY, when the Eternal Empire's conquest scattered the Order.

In this window, *Star Wars: The Old Republic* (SWTOR) content shows ranks being granted for deeds as well as for passing trials. Detailed general rules come from *The Jedi Path*, which describes the later post-Ruusan Order. Those rules are labelled general wherever no source ties them to this window.

**How to read the citations:** I read every page from starwars.fandom.com through its MediaWiki API, because the normal page view was blocked by a Cloudflare check. No mirror was used. When the statement on a Wookieepedia page carries a footnote, the footnoted source is given in parentheses, for example "(ref: *TOR Encyclopedia*)". SWTOR mission, expansion and codex citations are named by mission or entry, for example "(ref: SWTOR *Shadow of Revan*, 'The Enemy Within')".

**About [conflict] and [gray]:** When sources disagree, every version is listed with its citation, with a note on which version fits the most other sources and the established timeline. The decision is left to the developer (rule 6). [gray] marks a fact from a gray-area source: published 2014–early 2016, not SWTOR, and not pre-2014 Legends. The only such source on the pages used is *Nexus of Power* (published 17 March 2016, a Canon-labelled reference book at the edge of the window). Its facts are kept only where SWTOR or a pre-2014 Legends source backs them up, and the backing source is cited alongside. Everything else from it is listed under "Not used" (rule 7, strictest form). ### Jedi Initiate
- **Type:** rank
- **Era:** general; attested c. 3661 BBY and 3643 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - The term covered Force-sensitive children in early academy training, from joining a clan until they passed the Initiate Trials and were chosen as Padawans (general; ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Initiate/Legends) (intro)
  - The rank came with maturity, not at a set age. Clans lived and learned together from about age three until selection around adolescence (general; ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Initiate/Legends) ("Overview")
  - In the Order's early years and after reforms or purges, the Initiate stage did not exist or was not the main source of Knights (general; ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Initiate/Legends) ("Overview")
  - A Kiffar orphan born c. 3661 BBY was handed to the Coruscant Temple as an infant for training (ref: *Fatal Alliance*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shigar_Konshi) (intro)
  - In 3643 BBY a Jedi Initiate who had trained under several Masters across the galaxy during the Cold War was sent to Tython "to learn from an official teacher" and undergo the Jedi Trials (ref: SWTOR Jedi Knight class mission "Attack of the Flesh Raiders"; date from TOR dates). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hero_of_Tython) ("Early life")
- **Related:** Padawan; Jedi Temple (Tython)
- **Uncertainty:** In the 3643 BBY example, the Hero of Tython page calls the learner an "Initiate" who will undergo the "Jedi Trials", and its intro says the learner "trained under several Masters" before Tython. Whether these were Initiate Trials or something else is [unclear]. The clan system is not tied to this window.

### Padawan
- **Type:** rank
- **Era:** general; Cold War specifics 3653–3641 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - A Padawan was chosen for individual training by one Knight or Master after the Initiate Trials (general; ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order/Legends) ("Organization" › "Jedi ranks")
  - A Knight or Master could have only one apprentice at a time. The practice was formalised after the Great Sith War, which peaked in 3996 BBY, and could be dropped if numbers fell (ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order/Legends) ("Jedi ranks"; "Locations" › "Ossus")
  - Masters taught practical skills: physical sciences, piloting, droid repair, astronavigation and self-sufficiency (ref: *TOR Encyclopedia*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Padawan/Legends) ("Instruction")
  - In the Cold War, after the move to Tython, training shifted towards working with the Republic military, military readiness and diplomatic service (ref: *TOR Encyclopedia*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Padawan/Legends) ("Instruction")
  - Rediscovered ethics manuals and teachings of the first Jedi elders from Tython were folded into the Order's teaching (ref: *TOR Encyclopedia*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tython/Legends) ("Rediscovery")
  - In 3643 BBY, on Tython, Council member Orgus Din took a newly arrived learner as his Padawan (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hero_of_Tython) (intro)
  - Students who failed the Initiate Trials or were not chosen could be placed in the Jedi Service Corps (general; ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order/Legends) ("Jedi ranks")
- **Related:** Jedi Initiate; Jedi Knight
- **Uncertainty:** No source confirms the one-apprentice rule was still in force in this window [inferred]. The Padawan page's Cold War paragraph also mentions "the peaceful years before the Great Galactic War", which is muddled [unclear].

### Jedi Knight
- **Type:** rank
- **Era:** general; examples 3681 BBY and c. 3643–3640 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - A Knight had completed academy training, Padawan training and the Jedi Trials. Knights took missions from the Councils (general; ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Knight/Legends) (intro)
  - The High Council administered the Trials to Padawans it judged worthy and could substitute other tests or a very hard mission (general). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Trials/Legends) (intro)
  - Before the High Council and academy teaching became standard, masters set the Trials informally. The five named Trials were fixed only after the Ruusan Reformation, long after this window (ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Trials/Legends) ("History")
  - The High Council granted Knighthood, sometimes more loosely in wartime (ref: *Knights of the Old Republic* 33). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council/Legends) ("Organization and politics")
  - **3681 BBY:** The High Council made a Padawan a Knight in a "Knighting Ceremony". He swore to uphold the Jedi Code and defend the Republic, on the day news broke of the Sith assault on the Tingel Arm (ref: *The Journal of Master Gnost-Dural*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gnost-Dural) ("Great Galactic War"). The war began in 3681 BBY with that assault. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Galactic_War) (infobox)
  - **Council controls access to the Trials:** In the Cold War the High Council judged Grand Master Shan's Padawan unready for the Trials despite her opinion, and she would not overrule it (ref: *Fatal Alliance*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shigar_Konshi); [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satele_Shan) ("Grand Master of the Order")
  - **Knighthood for deeds:** A Padawan who built a lightsaber and helped stop a Dark Jedi's attack on Tython met the Council, and Grand Master Shan conferred the title of Jedi Knight "in recognition for bravery" (ref: SWTOR "Weapon of the Jedi"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hero_of_Tython) ("Training on Tython")
  - In the Council chamber, the Grand Master promoted a Padawan to full Jedi Knight "with the blessings" of her master and of Master Kiwiiks (ref: SWTOR "Fate of the Jedi"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hero_of_Tython) ("Return to Tython")
  - After promotion, a new Knight chose the Guardian or Sentinel path at Carrick Station (ref: SWTOR "Advanced Training: Jedi Knight"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hero_of_Tython) ("Powers and abilities")
  - Satele Shan warned a Padawan that "the trials are difficult" (ref: *Fatal Alliance*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Trials/Legends) (opening quote)
- **Related:** Jedi High Council; Knight specialisations; Hero of Tython (title)
- **Uncertainty:** The Hero of Tython page links "newly elevated" to the Jedi Trials page, but its narrative shows Knighthood conferred for deeds. Whether formal Trials happened is [unclear]. Which Trials were used in this window was not found. The c. 3643 BBY date for *Fatal Alliance* is supported by Satele Shan's page ("Around 3643 BBY…") and by Shigar Konshi's stated birth year and age [inferred].

### Jedi Knight specialisations (Consular, Guardian, Sentinel and sub-roles)
- **Type:** rank/role
- **Era:** Great Galactic War, Cold War and Galactic War, c. 3681–3636 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - Knights could specialise in one of "the three branches of Knighthood" (general; ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order/Legends) ("Jedi ranks"). The branches were Guardian, Consular and Sentinel. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Guardian/Legends) (intro)
  - **Consular:** In the Great Galactic War and Cold War, Consulars could follow the "Jedi Sage" or "Jedi Shadow" specialisation. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Consular) ("Specializations during the Great Galactic War")
  - **Jedi Shadow:** The Republic used Shadows, specialised Consulars, against the Sith Empire in the Great Galactic War and Cold War (ref: SWTOR Fan Friday, 20 Aug 2010; HoloNet Jedi Consular class page). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Shadow) ("History")
  - **Sentinel:** In the Great Galactic War, Cold War and Galactic War, "Sentinel" meant a Knight specialising in dual lightsabers (Jar'Kai with Shii-Cho, Ataru and Juyo). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Sentinel/Legends) ("Philosophy")
  - **Guardian / Sentinel choice:** A new Knight chose between them at Carrick Station (ref: SWTOR "Advanced Training: Jedi Knight"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hero_of_Tython) ("Powers and abilities")
- **Related:** Jedi Knight; Battlemaster
- **Uncertainty:** The Consular "Specializations" and Sentinel Jar'Kai paragraphs have no footnotes on their pages [unclear]. All four labels mirror SWTOR's advanced classes, and the pages use the game term "advanced classes" [unclear].

### Jedi Master
- **Type:** rank
- **Era:** general; examples c. 3681–3640 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - Jedi Master was the highest formal rank. Normally only Masters sat on the Councils (general; ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Master/Legends) (intro)
  - The usual route was training several Padawans to Knighthood. A modified version of the Trials or notable service could also lead to promotion by the High Council (general; ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Master/Legends) ("Overview")
  - Self-promotion was discouraged. It was not unusual during the Old and New Sith Wars (ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Master/Legends) ("Overview")
  - **Promotion for service, 3651 BBY:** For rediscovering Tython, Satele Shan was granted the rank of Jedi Master and a place on the Jedi Council (ref: *TOR Encyclopedia*; *Journal of Master Gnost-Dural*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satele_Shan) ("Grand Master of the Order")
  - **Promotion for service, Galactic War:** The Grand Master publicly elevated a young Knight to Jedi Master for "actions and leadership" in the recent conflicts, saying youth did not matter (ref: SWTOR "Doomsday"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hero_of_Tython) ("Victory")
  - **Wartime promotion:** Jun Seros, a Knight for over a decade, was appointed Jedi Master when the Sith Empire returned (ref: *TOR Encyclopedia*; SWTOR codex "Jedi Master Jun Seros"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jun_Seros_%28Jedi%29) ("Biography")
- **Related:** Jedi Knight; Jedi High Council
- **Uncertainty:** [conflict] on when Satele Shan became a Jedi Master. These are the versions:
  - **Version A, 3651 BBY:** She was granted the rank of Master (and a Council seat) for rediscovering Tython (ref: *TOR Encyclopedia*; year from *Journal of Master Gnost-Dural*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satele_Shan) ("Grand Master of the Order")
  - **Version B, by 3666 BBY:** *The Old Republic: Annihilation* (2012) describes her as a Jedi Master in 3666 BBY (ref: *Annihilation*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satele_Shan) ("Contradictions")
  - **Fit:** Version A fits the most other sources and the era's timeline. She appears as "Jedi Knight Satele Shan" at the 3653 BBY peace talks (ref: *Threat of Peace*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Treaty_of_Coruscant). She is "Jedi Knight Satele Shan" when she returns with Tython's coordinates (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order/Legends) ("The Cold War"). She is "Knight Satele Shan" when she rediscovers Tython (ref: SWTOR HoloNet biography). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council/Legends) ("Early history"). The Satele Shan page also notes that *Annihilation* contradicts the *Encyclopedia* and the game. **Which version to use is the developer's decision.**

### Grand Master
- **Type:** rank/title
- **Era:** Zym: before 3681 BBY to 3653 BBY. Satele Shan: 3651 BBY or "by 3645 BBY" to c. 3636 BBY.
- **Key facts:**
  - The Grand Master was the elected head of the Order, appointed by unanimous High Council vote (general; ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grand_Master/Legends) ("History")
  - The Grand Master spoke for the Council to Republic leaders, broke tied Council votes and guided all Jedi (ref: *TOR Encyclopedia*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grand_Master/Legends) ("History")
  - Zym, a Kel Dor, was Grand Master through the Great Galactic War. He was assassinated soon after the fall of the Coruscant Temple in 3653 BBY (ref: *Threat of Peace* Act 2). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grand_Master/Legends) ("History")
  - When the Empire first attacked, Zym led the Council in agreeing to fight for the Senate (ref: *Journal of Master Gnost-Dural*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zym) ("Biography")
  - Satele Shan was the youngest known Grand Master (ref: *Journal of Master Gnost-Dural*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grand_Master/Legends) ("History")
  - **End of Satele Shan's term:** The list of Grand Masters gives her term ending c. 3636 BBY (ref: SWTOR KotFE, Chapter 12). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grand_Master/Legends) ("List of Grand Masters"); [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satele_Shan) (succession box)
  - After the Eternal Empire conquered the Republic and Sith Empire (begun 3636 BBY), she was disheartened by the Order's losses under her command, "renounced her title" and went into meditative exile. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satele_Shan) (intro); [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Empire_conquest) (infobox)
  - The succession box names her eventual successor as leader of the Order: Genarra, c. 1032 BBY, long after this window (ref: *Knight Errant*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satele_Shan) (succession box)
- **Related:** Jedi High Council; Master of the Order
- **Uncertainty:**
  - [conflict] on when Satele Shan became Grand Master. I checked the SWTOR expansions, and none gives a year. All dated versions trace back to *The Journal of Master Gnost-Dural* (2011), as read by different pages:
    - **Version A, 3651 BBY:** Zym's death left a power vacuum "filled by his successor Satele Shan two years later" (linked to 3651 BBY) (ref: *Journal*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zym) ("Biography"). The High Council member list also gives Grand Master "c. 3651 BBY". [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council/Legends) ("Known members" › "Old Republic era")
    - **Version B, after 3651 and "by 3645 BBY":** In 3651 BBY she was made Master and given a Council seat. The Order then learned the Coruscant Temple would not be rebuilt, the Council decided to move to Tython, and "by 3645 BBY" it had appointed her Grand Master (ref: *Journal*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satele_Shan) ("Grand Master of the Order"). The Grand Master list agrees: "Pre–3645 BBY" (ref: *Journal*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grand_Master/Legends) ("List of Grand Masters")
    - **Fit:** Both versions agree she was Grand Master by 3645 BBY. On the exact year, Version B's order of events fits more of the other sources and the timeline. The High Council page has the Council relocating to Tython and then appointing her (ref: SWTOR HoloNet biography). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council/Legends) ("Early history"). The Jedi Order page puts her appointment after the Temple on Tython emerged (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order/Legends) ("The Cold War"). That Temple was built 3650–3647 BBY. Version A is the only one giving a single precise year. **Which version to use is the developer's decision.**
  - [conflict] on the end of her term as Grand Master. These are the versions:
    - **Version A, term ends c. 3636 BBY:** The Grand Master list and her succession box end her term c. 3636 BBY (ref: SWTOR *Knights of the Fallen Empire* (KotFE), Chapter 12). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grand_Master/Legends) ("List of Grand Masters"); [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satele_Shan) (succession box)
    - **Version B, renounced the title:** After the Eternal Empire's conquest (begun 3636 BBY), she "renounced her title" and went into meditative exile. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satele_Shan) (intro; this sentence has no footnote)
    - **Version C, still titled Grand Master after 3636 BBY:** The KotFE narrative for 3631 BBY calls her "Grand Master Shan" (ref: KotFE ch. 5, 12). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satele_Shan) ("War against the Eternal Empire"). After the conquest, the Gnost-Dural page refers to "Grand Master Satele Shan in hiding" (ref: SWTOR codex "Tau Idair"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gnost-Dural) ("Jedi colony")
    - **Fit:** Versions A and B agree that she stopped leading the Order c. 3636 BBY, and A is footnoted to KotFE. Version C may be the narrative still using her old title, but no source says that [unclear]. **Which version to use is the developer's decision.**

### Master of the Order
- **Type:** title
- **Era:** general; not clearly attested for this window
- **Key facts:**
  - In general, this was the elected leader of the High Council, chosen unanimously by its sitting members and often the same person as the Grand Master (ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Master_of_the_Order/Legends) (intro)
  - The High Council page links Satele Shan's appointment as Council "leader" to this title (ref: SWTOR HoloNet biography). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council/Legends) ("Early history")
- **Related:** Grand Master
- **Uncertainty:** The Master of the Order page itself does not mention this era. The link to this window rests only on a wiki link [unclear].

### Honorary titles: Battlemaster, Barsen'thor, Hero of Tython
- **Type:** title
- **Era:** Great Galactic War to the Eternal Empire conquest, 3681–3636 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Battlemaster (general):** The High Council elected a Battlemaster to lead lightsaber instruction at the Coruscant academy (ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battlemaster/Legends) ("History")
  - **Battlemaster in this window:** During the Cold War and Galactic War the title went to Jedi who had gone "above and beyond" in combat (ref: *TOR Encyclopedia*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battlemaster/Legends) ("History")
  - Kao Cen Darach was Battlemaster in 3681 BBY, teaching a Padawan at the Republic station over Korriban (ref: *Journal of Master Gnost-Dural*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satele_Shan) ("Early life")
  - The High Council appointed Jun Seros Battlemaster, "allowing Seros to train hundreds of Jedi in the field", replacing the dead Darach (ref: *TOR Encyclopedia*; SWTOR codex "Jedi Master Jun Seros"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jun_Seros_%28Jedi%29) ("Biography"); [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battlemaster/Legends) ("History")
  - **3637 BBY, after the Revanite crisis:** The Jedi Council "voted unanimously" to name the Hero of Tython Battlemaster. The Grand Master appointed the Hero "after conferring with the Council" (ref: SWTOR *Shadow of Revan*, "The Enemy Within"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hero_of_Tython) ("Continued travels"); [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satele_Shan) ("Revan returned"). The Revanite crisis is dated 3637 BBY (ref: Charles Boyd, SWTOR forums). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Revanite_crisis) (infobox)
  - The role meant giving strategic insight to the Council, teaching lightsaber combat to the Order's best students, and leading the fight against the restored Emperor (ref: "The Enemy Within"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hero_of_Tython) ("Continued travels")
  - In 3636 BBY the new Battlemaster vanished around the time of the Eternal Empire's conquest (ref: SWTOR KotFE codex "T7-01"; date from Game Update 4.0). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hero_of_Tython) ("Continued travels")
  - **Barsen'thor:** The title was given only to exceptional Jedi. By the Cold War only one other person had held it before the apprentice of Master Yuon Par received it (ref: *TOR Encyclopedia*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Barsen%27thor) ("Overview"). In SWTOR it is called a title designated "by the Jedi Council" indicating "a supreme level of trust" (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Barsen%27thor) (opening quote)
  - **Hero of Tython:** Grand Master Shan gave this "ceremonial title" to a Knight for defeating Darth Angral during the Cold War (ref: SWTOR "Fate of the Jedi"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hero_of_Tython) (intro; "Return to Tython")
- **Related:** Jedi High Council; Grand Master
- **Uncertainty:** The Battlemaster page has the Jedi Order Battlemaster title ending "with the start of the New Sith Wars". That is outside this window.

### Jedi High Council (Coruscant to 3653 BBY; Tython c. 3653–3636 BBY; scattered afterwards)
- **Type:** institution
- **Era:** whole window
- **Key facts:**
  - **Role:**
    - The High Council, often just called the Jedi Council, was the Order's main governing council. Its decisions were final (ref: *TOR Encyclopedia*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council/Legends) (intro; "Organization and politics")
    - It advised the Chancellor's office (ref: *The Jedi Path*). It also chose Jedi for special missions, organised relief, planned strategy with Republic leaders and sought guidance through meditation (ref: *TOR Encyclopedia*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council/Legends) ("Organization and politics")
  - **Size and terms (general):**
    - Twelve Masters: five with life terms, four long-term, three limited-term (ref: *Power of the Jedi Sourcebook*; *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council/Legends) (intro; "Organization and politics")
    - The Council had this "permanent, twelve-member form" by the Mandalorian Wars, before this window. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council/Legends) ("Early history")
  - **Great Galactic War (3681–3653 BBY):**
    - Under Zym, the Council agreed to fight on the Senate's behalf (ref: *Journal*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zym)
    - The Council learned of Korriban's fall only after a delay (ref: Timeline 6). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Galactic_War) ("Return of the Sith" › "Fall of Korriban")
    - It formally advised the Senate to ignore the Empire's peace overture and was overruled. Zym then sent Jedi with the peace delegation (ref: *Journal*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Galactic_War) ("The Imperial advance continues" › "Sacking of Coruscant")
  - **3653 BBY:**
    - In the Sacking of Coruscant, the Temple's four towers fell, though much of the interior survived (ref: *Deceived*). Whole floors of archives were lost (ref: *TOR Encyclopedia*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Temple/Legends) ("The Old Republic")
    - The Senate put off rebuilding the Temple indefinitely (ref: *Threat of Peace* Act 2). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Temple/Legends) ("The Old Republic")
  - **Move to Tython:**
    - After Satele Shan was seated (3651 BBY), the Order learned the Coruscant Temple would not be rebuilt, and "the Council decided" to move the headquarters to Tython (ref: *Journal of Master Gnost-Dural*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satele_Shan) ("Grand Master of the Order")
    - The Council fasted and meditated for a week before choosing the Temple site (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Temple_%28Tython%29/Legends) ("History")
    - Some Jedi stayed on Coruscant as a delegation within the Senate (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order/Legends) ("The Cold War")
  - **Archives task:** During the three years of Temple construction, the Council had Lore Keeper Gnost-Dural compile records of the Great Galactic War for the new Archives. He included nineteen records. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Archives_%28Tython%29) (intro). See also [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gnost-Dural) ("Jedi Master", ref: *Journal*).
  - **Chamber on Tython:** The High Council Chamber was a domed room on the Temple's second storey, with a circular table, "ten chairs", wooden statues of Masters and a central holoprojector (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/High_Council_Chamber_%28Tython%29)
  - **Cold War roster:**
    - Named members include Satele Shan, Giffis Fane, Wens Aleusis, Oric Traless, Nikil Nobil, Tol Braga, Bela Kiwiiks, Syo Bakarn, Jaric Kaedan and Orgus Din. The Barsen'thor later replaced Syo Bakarn. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council/Legends) ("Early history"); [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Council/Legends) ("Tython")
    - Other listed members: Oteg, Gnost-Dural, Shol Bestros and Cedral Gend. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council/Legends) ("Known members")
  - **Seat change on request, c. 3643 BBY:** At the Chancellor's request, Shan agreed that Master Oteg become Fleet Admiral, and Oric Traless took Oteg's seat (ref: *TOR Encyclopedia*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satele_Shan) ("Grand Master of the Order")
  - **Wartime succession:** After the Great Galactic War, members were rarely together. Jedi summoned to replace fallen members often served only until stability returned (ref: *TOR Encyclopedia*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council/Legends) ("Early history")
  - **Debate on renewed war:** Council member Oric Traless reported about a thousand young Jedi eager for war. The elder members opposed it (ref: *Fatal Alliance*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Treaty_of_Coruscant) ("Doubts within the Jedi Order")
  - **3637 BBY:** The Sith assault on Tython killed Council member Oric Traless and badly damaged the Temple (ref: SWTOR Game Update 2.7; "Assault on Tython: Empire"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Assault_on_Tython) (intro; infobox)
  - **After the Eternal Empire conquest (from 3636 BBY):**
    - The Order scattered, and Tython became largely abandoned except for archival and research staff (ref: SWTOR *Onslaught*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tython/Legends) ("Rediscovery")
    - With "Grand Master Satele Shan in hiding", Gnost-Dural, "as one of the remaining Jedi High Council members", led Jedi and civilian volunteers to found a hidden colony on Ossus (ref: SWTOR codex "Tau Idair"; *Knights of the Eternal Throne* (KotET), "Inflection Point"). Imperials discovered the colony in 3628 BBY (ref: KotET-era "The Lost Colony"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gnost-Dural) ("Jedi colony")
    - By 3627 BBY the Republic had formed Task Force Nova to reunite the Order, which chose to settle a new homeworld rather than return to Tython (ref: SWTOR *Onslaught*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tython/Legends) ("Rediscovery")
    - In the *Onslaught* era, Gnost-Dural called a meeting of the Jedi he judged the Order's most important representatives to discuss rebuilding its numbers (ref: SWTOR *Onslaught* flashpoint "Secrets of the Enclave"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gnost-Dural) ("Dantooine")
  - **Behind the scenes:** In SWTOR *Onslaught*, a loyalist Jedi Knight player is offered a seat on the Jedi High Council at the end of the Meridian Complex arc. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hero_of_Tython) ("Behind the scenes")
- **Related:** Grand Master; Jedi Temple (Tython); Jedi Knight
- **Uncertainty:**
  - No source gives a seat count for this window. The twelve-seat, 5/4/3 structure is general. The Tython chamber has "ten chairs", which does not settle the count [unclear].
  - The Tython rosters on the High Council and Jedi Council pages, and the Archives paragraph, have no footnotes [unclear]. The "Known members" dates are not footnoted line by line [unclear].
  - The *Onslaught* Council-seat offer is a player choice described behind the scenes, not established in-universe fact.

### Jedi Temple (Tython)
- **Type:** location
- **Era:** constructed 3650–3647 BBY; headquarters c. 3653–3636 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - Also called the Jedi Academy, it replaced the Coruscant Temple as headquarters after the Great Galactic War. It was built on Tython, the Deep Core world where the Order was founded. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Temple_%28Tython%29/Legends) (intro)
  - Built 3650–3647 BBY (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Temple_%28Tython%29/Legends) (infobox). The Jedi Order page lists it as headquarters from 3653 BBY (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order/Legends) (infobox)
  - It was built near the ruins of Kaleth, on an ancient temple's foundations, from local timber and stone (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tython/Legends) ("Rediscovery"); [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Temple_%28Tython%29/Legends) ("History")
  - **Exterior:** The Council chose new architecture in keeping with the Code's limits on attachment. The design drew on Alderaanian bastions (ref: SWTOR). It had three cupolas around a large central one, two open-air hangars, hidden defences, a promenade and training grounds (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Temple_%28Tython%29/Legends) ("Exterior")
  - **Interior:**
    - The Temple housed the High Council Chamber, a domed room on the second storey (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/High_Council_Chamber_%28Tython%29). It also housed the Jedi Archives (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Archives_%28Tython%29) (infobox)
    - The Temple's main hall held the monument known as the Eye of Zallow, a dodecahedron with two smaller holocron-shaped objects orbiting it. The *Star Wars: The Old Republic Encyclopedia* (2012) and SWTOR (2011): [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eye_of_Zallow) (intro; "Behind the scenes"). (D-465: the *Nexus of Power* citation removed, not Legends.) That page adds, from the *Encyclopedia*, that it was a memorial named after Master Ven Zallow, who died in the 3653 BBY Sacking.
    - The Temple contained the Archives room and the High Council chamber, besides the monument hall. SWTOR (2011) places both rooms in the Tython Temple (D-465: the *Nexus of Power* citation removed, not Legends): [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/High_Council_Chamber_%28Tython%29); [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Archives_%28Tython%29). *Nexus of Power*'s placement of each room in a named spire is not backed and is listed under "Not used".
  - **3637 BBY:** The Sith assault damaged the Temple and raided the Archives' databanks. The Republic retook it (ref: SWTOR Game Update 2.7). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Assault_on_Tython)
  - After the 3636 BBY conquest, Tython was largely abandoned, and by 3627 BBY the Order chose not to resettle it (ref: SWTOR *Onslaught*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tython/Legends) ("Rediscovery")
- **Related:** Jedi High Council; Coruscant Temple
- **Uncertainty:**
  - The move (3653 BBY) and construction (3650–3647 BBY) are compatible: the Order was briefly homeless first [inferred].
  - [conflict] on when the Coruscant Temple was rebuilt and the Order left Tython. I checked this against SWTOR expansion sources. These are the versions:
    - **Version A, rebuilt in 3500 BBY, "several decades" after the Sacking:** The academy then returned from Tython (ref: *The Old Republic: Deceived*, 2011). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Temple/Legends) ("Temple Ziggurat"). From 3653 BBY to 3500 BBY is about 150 years, so this version is internally inconsistent.
    - **Version B, rebuilt "following the defeat of the Sith Empire":** "Major additions" began c. 3500 BBY (ref: *Star Wars: Complete Locations*, 2005). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Temple/Legends) ("The Old Republic")
    - **Version C, Order returned after the Galactic War:** The Order returned to Coruscant, "abandoning Tython indefinitely", and rebuilt the Temple slowly with Republic aid (ref: SWTOR, no mission named). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order/Legends) ("Galactic War")
    - **Version D, Tython abandoned after the conquest:** After the 3636 BBY conquest Tython was largely abandoned. By 3627 BBY the Order, being regathered by Task Force Nova, chose to settle a new homeworld rather than return to Tython (ref: SWTOR *Onslaught*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tython/Legends) ("Rediscovery")
    - **Fit:** Versions B and C agree on the order of events: the Temple was rebuilt after the Sith Empire's defeat, with major work from c. 3500 BBY. Version A agrees on the 3500 BBY date but not on the "several decades" gap. Version D says when Tython was left (by c. 3636–3627 BBY) but does not date a return to Coruscant. So the combination of B and C with A's date fits the most sources. The "several decades" phrase is the outlier. No source dates a return to Coruscant within 3,700–3,600 BBY. **Which version to use is the developer's decision.**

## Game notes (optional, suggestions only)
- Suggestion: Knighthood could be reached two ways in this era: Council-approved Trials, or a deed-based conferral by the Grand Master before the Council. In both cases the Council holds the gate.
- Suggestion: Mastery could be awarded for service or leadership, not only for training Padawans, which fits wartime 3,700–3,600 BBY.
- Suggestion: Battlemaster (chosen by unanimous Council vote), Barsen'thor and Hero of Tython could be rare honorary titles, separate from rank.
- Suggestion: after 3636 BBY, a "scattered Order" state fits the sources: no fixed Temple, a Grand Master in exile, and remaining Councillors acting independently.
