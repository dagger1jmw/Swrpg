# Era 09 Characters: Great Galactic War, Cold War and early Galactic War (c. 3681–3636 BBY), Part 3a: Republic non-Force figures

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 21, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

**Set:** SW_Characters_09_GGW_ColdWar_Index.md (master index, open questions, inclusion rules, topic-file conflicts); Part1 (Jedi and light-side users); Part2 (Sith and other dark-side users); Part3a (Republic government, military, SIS and Republic-side companions); Part3b (Imperial government, military and Intelligence, Imperial-side companions, Mandalorians, underworld and Hutts, others). The developer's group 3 was split in two to keep each file under ~70 KB. This file is Part3a.

## Summary
This file covers named non-Force figures on the Republic side from 3681 BBY to 3636 BBY: Chancellors and senators, the Army, Special Forces and SIS, the Trooper and Smuggler protagonists (by title only), and the companions of the Republic classes. Key points: Berooken was Chancellor at the Sacking and died in it (3653 BBY). Am-Ris signed the Treaty as interim Chancellor, and Janarus was elected after it. Saresh was Chancellor from 3640 BBY. Jace Malcom has no recorded birth year; he was a corporal in 3681 BBY, led Havoc Squad at Alderaan in 3667 BBY, and became Supreme Commander in 3640 BBY. Aric Jorgan was born in 3673 BBY, so he was about 12 in 3661 BBY. Theron Shan was born in 3666 BBY (about 5 in 3661 BBY). Nearly every companion was a child or not yet born in 3661 BBY.

Missing developer files: SW_Force_Powers_* and the developer's SW_Jedi_Training / Sith_Training files are **not on the box**. Narrative in SW_Great_Galactic_War_Part1–3, SW_SWTOR_Era_Power_Structures_Part1/2, SW_Republic_Military_GGW_* and SW_Coruscant_City_GGW_* is not repeated.

## Entries
Fields per entry: ID (species, sex, homeworld); life (birth and death and how each is known); ranks by year; teachers and students; forms and powers (non-Force figures: none unless stated); alignment; status in 3661 BBY and in 3653 BBY; feats. "Not stated" means no source gives it. Force-using Republic figures who are also soldiers or spies (Ardun Kothe, Warren Sedoru, Ako Domi, Aurei Eadon) are in Part1.
