# Force Traditions Outside the Jedi Order and the Sith, Part 2: traditions founded from 1000 BBY onward (All Legends Eras)

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 21, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends), as cited per entry. No mirrors were needed. Draft for review.

## Summary
Part 2 covers traditions founded in or after 1000 BBY. These run from the Witches of Dathomir (c. 600 BBY) and the Antarian Rangers (c. 620 BBY), through Clone Wars-era groups such as the Bando Gora, Altisian Jedi, Iron Knights and the Wyrd, to New Jedi Order-era contacts such as the Fallanassi, Theran Listeners and Mind Walkers. It also holds the all-eras "who exists" table and species notes. Key facts:
- The Nightsisters' founding date is contested, and the name may have been used by several dark exile clans.
- The Fallanassi are the main source of White Current illusion and Force immersion.
- The Potentium was a heresy expelled from the Jedi in 132 BBY.
- The Iron Knights were Force-sensitive crystals in droid bodies.

Part 1 holds the earlier traditions, plus the Paladins and Corellian Jedi.

## Entries

### Witches of Dathomir (Allyan clans, including the Singing Mountain Clan)
- **Type:** institution (clan society)
- **Era:** shamans on Dathomir by 36,453 BBY; the clan society from Allya, c. 600 BBY, to 43.5 ABY and later; Dathomir
- **Key facts:**
  - **Origins:** a few human shamans lived on Dathomir by 36,453 BBY, and some went to Tython with the Tho Yor and helped found the Je'daii. [Witches of Dathomir](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Witches_of_Dathomir) [Dathomir/Legends §Pre-Republic History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dathomir/Legends)
  - Around 600 BBY the Jedi Knight Allya was exiled there. She tamed rancors with the Force and wrote the Book of Law, based on the Jedi Code. Its last line was "Never concede to evil". [Witches of Dathomir §Origins](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Witches_of_Dathomir) [Nightsisters/Legends §Founding](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nightsisters/Legends)
  - **Structure:** female-led clans named after landmarks. Each has its own Force laws and its own version of the Book of Law. Leadership usually goes to the eldest, and matriarchs often live past a century. Men are labor and mates. There were more than a dozen clans in 8 ABY and nine by 12 ABY. [Witches of Dathomir §Culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Witches_of_Dathomir)
  - The Singing Mountain Clan has a clan mother presiding over a Council of Sisters. Spellcasters hold the main influence. Accepted offworlders get sanctuary and equal status. [Singing Mountain Clan §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Singing_Mountain_Clan)
  - **Force use:** framed as "spells" cast with chants, songs, dances or writing. Witches were often gifted only in some areas, such as healing or a Force whirlwind. The clans called their tradition Allyan Magic. [Dathomir Magic §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dathomir_Magic) [Witches of Dathomir §Culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Witches_of_Dathomir)
  - **Discipline:** using the dark side, or fuelling a permitted power with anger, meant exile. A witch could come back after years of purifying exile. Those who stayed out became Nightsisters. [Witches of Dathomir §Culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Witches_of_Dathomir)
  - **Gear:** braids, lizard-skin armor, rancor mounts; blasters in some clans. [Witches of Dathomir §Culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Witches_of_Dathomir) [Singing Mountain Clan §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Singing_Mountain_Clan)
  - **Jedi contacts:**
    - In 340 BBY the Chu'unthor crashed. Fourteen Jedi died in rescue attempts. Yoda gave the young Rell Jedi records to hold until a Jedi came. [Witches of Dathomir §Recorded history](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Witches_of_Dathomir)
    - In 8 ABY Luke Skywalker received the records. Teneniel Djo married Isolder. Several witches joined the Praxeum: Kirana Ti, Damaya, Sanola Ti. [Witches of Dathomir §Recorded history](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Witches_of_Dathomir) [Singing Mountain Clan §Later history](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Singing_Mountain_Clan)
    - After the Yuuzhan Vong War, Kirana Ti, Damaya and Streen ran a Jedi praxeum there, and Jacen Solo studied with the witches. [Witches of Dathomir §Recorded history](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Witches_of_Dathomir)
  - **Dark Times:** Augwynne Djo helped Jax Pavan open Darth Ramage's holocron and cleared its dark knowledge from his mind. [Singing Mountain Clan §Old Republic era](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Singing_Mountain_Clan)
- **Related:** Nightsisters; Je'daii (Jedi Training files)
- **Uncertainty:**
  - [unclear] The "single generation" founding story is partly legendary. Linguistic evidence shows a human population there for more than two millennia before Allya. [Witches of Dathomir §Origins](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Witches_of_Dathomir)
  - A Brotherhood of Darkness Sith academy stood on Dathomir shortly before 1000 BBY. Its legacy is unknown. [Witches of Dathomir §Origins](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Witches_of_Dathomir)

### Nightsisters (and Nightbrothers)
- **Type:** institution (dark side witch clans)
- **Era:** founding disputed [conflict]; active to 43.5 ABY; Dathomir
- **Key facts:**
  - **Beliefs:** they rejected "good" and "evil" and called on the Twin Deities, the Fanged God and the Winged Goddess, who are the Son and Daughter of the Ones. Their holy text was the Book of Shadows. [Nightsisters/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nightsisters/Legends) [Nightsisters/Legends §Religion and philosophy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nightsisters/Legends) [The Ones/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/The_Ones/Legends)
  - **Magic:** Gethzerion's "Shadow Magic" needed no chanting. They specialized in illusion spells, and shamans channelled spirit ichor (e.g. the Water of Life). [Dathomir Magic §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dathomir_Magic) [Nightsisters/Legends §Religion and philosophy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nightsisters/Legends)
  - **Other practices:** conjuring from ichor, divination ([Darksight](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darksight), called "heartshadow"), scrying, mesmerism, spirit-infused talismans. [Magic/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Magic/Legends)
  - **Powers taught (Wookieepedia names):**
    - [Spirit ichor/Legends §Spirit ichor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_ichor/Legends)
    - [Force weapon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_weapon): swords and spears made unbreakable even by lightsabers. [Nightsisters/Legends §Religion and philosophy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nightsisters/Legends)
    - [Control web](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Control_web): several Nightsisters together control creatures' minds. [Control web](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Control_web)
    - [Force lightning/Legends §Force lightning](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_lightning/Legends) (shown in images). [Nightsisters/Legends §Society](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nightsisters/Legends)
  - Long-lived: Zalem, Gethzerion, Baritha, Talzin and others lived one to several centuries. [Nightsisters/Legends §Religion and philosophy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nightsisters/Legends)
  - **Weapons:** mostly swords and spears. Silri used a lightwhip and Gethzerion a red lightsaber. [Nightsisters/Legends §Religion and philosophy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nightsisters/Legends)
  - **Structure:** sisterhoods under a Clan Mother. Some covens had male warrior "Brothers". [Nightsisters/Legends §Society](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nightsisters/Legends)
  - **Nightbrothers:** Force-sensitive Dathomirian Zabrak (Zabrak from Rattatak) kept as slaves and warriors. Those proven in the Crucible got Nightsister-infused weapons. A shaman and coven could empower one into a behemoth (Savage Opress). Darth Maul was born a Nightbrother. [Nightbrothers/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nightbrothers/Legends) [Nightbrothers/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nightbrothers/Legends)
  - **History:**
    - Zalem tried to destroy Coruscant with the Kwa Infinity Gate and was killed by her daughter Ros Lai, with Quinlan Vos involved. Talzin then united the covens. [Nightsisters/Legends §Queen Zalem's ambition](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nightsisters/Legends)
    - In the Clone Wars they helped and then betrayed Dooku. Grievous's attack nearly wiped them out. [Nightsisters/Legends §Clone Wars](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nightsisters/Legends)
    - Gethzerion rebuilt them. In 0 ABY Palpatine stranded her by destroying the prison's ships. In 8 ABY Zsinj killed her and her followers. [Nightsisters/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nightsisters/Legends) [Witches of Dathomir §Recorded history](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Witches_of_Dathomir)
    - In 12 ABY a reborn group allied with General Vit. [Singing Mountain Clan §Later history](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Singing_Mountain_Clan)
    - Around 23 ABY Tamith Kai's Great Canyon Nightsisters treated men as equals and sent their best to the Second Imperium's Shadow Academy. Lomi Plo came from them. [Nightsisters/Legends §Rebirth](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nightsisters/Legends)
    - In 43.5 ABY the Lost Tribe of Sith forced captured Nightsisters to teach control web. [Nightsisters/Legends §Rebirth](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nightsisters/Legends)
  - **Joining:** exiles, and daughters raised in the clan. Offworld Force users were used as mates or prey (Zalem captured a Jedi). [Nightsisters/Legends §Founding](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nightsisters/Legends) [Nightsisters/Legends §Queen Zalem's ambition](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nightsisters/Legends)
- **Related:** Mecrosa Order (Part 1); Ones (Part 1); Sith files (Maul, Ventress, Opress)
- **Uncertainty:**
  - [conflict] Founding: Gethzerion and Baritha, exiled for trying to kill Augwynne Djo, founded "the Nightsisters" "during the waning days of the Republic" [Nightsisters/Legends §Founding](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nightsisters/Legends) [Gethzerion §The Nightsisters](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gethzerion). Against that, a "Witch clan calling themselves the Nightsisters" already existed by 340 BBY [Witches of Dathomir §Recorded history](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Witches_of_Dathomir), and Talzin's covens fought the Mecrosa about a thousand years after 1230 BBY [Mecrosa Order §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mecrosa_Order). Wookieepedia reconciles this by treating "Nightsisters" as a recurring name for dark exile clans [Witches of Dathomir §Culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Witches_of_Dathomir). That reading fits the most sources.
  - [conflict] Zalem's Infinity Gate plot: c. 32 BBY [Nightsisters/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nightsisters/Legends) vs 31 BBY [Nightsisters/Legends §Queen Zalem's ambition](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nightsisters/Legends) vs 30 BBY [Singing Mountain Clan §Old Republic era](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Singing_Mountain_Clan).
  - [unclear] Gethzerion is called a Dathomirian hybrid, though her mother was fully Human. [Gethzerion §Early life](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gethzerion)
  - [BTS] Dave Wolverton (forum interview, undated) said he wanted strong female figures and was pleased that Lucas used the concept for Maul's backstory. Daniel Wallace (TheForce.net interview; *Book of Sith* endnotes, 2012) said the Twin Deities were based on Wicca and the Triple Goddess. These are not in-universe and do not conflict with it. [Nightsisters/Legends §Behind the scenes](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nightsisters/Legends)

### Antarian Rangers
- **Type:** institution (non-Force support order)
- **Era:** c. 620 BBY to the Yuuzhan Vong War (25 ABY) and later; founded on Antar 4
- **Key facts:**
  - Founded by Jedi Knight Marus Timpel and the Gotal Kaskutal, who was turned down for Jedi training and had limited Force sensitivity. It was a support force for Jedi: reconnaissance, covert operations and "dirty work". [Antarian Rangers/Legends §Origin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Antarian_Rangers/Legends) [Antarian Rangers/Legends §Organization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Antarian_Rangers/Legends)
  - **Ranks:** Explorer (trainee), Lieutenant (honorific), Ranger, Ranger Captain, Ranger General. Most members were support staff. At its peak it had over a thousand members. One to three Rangers went with a Jedi, and some Jedi had a semi-permanent Ranger aide. [Antarian Rangers/Legends §Organization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Antarian_Rangers/Legends)
  - **Recruitment:** mostly by invitation. They watched people turned down for Jedi training. Anyone who admired the Jedi was eligible. Desertion and leaking secrets were punished, and leaving to join the Jedi was an honor. [Antarian Rangers/Legends §Organization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Antarian_Rangers/Legends)
  - The Jedi Council was indifferent to them, though some Jedi married Rangers. [Antarian Rangers/Legends §Organization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Antarian_Rangers/Legends)
  - **Gear:** no uniform, a brown leather jacket, a modified Greff-Timms AR-1 blaster rifle. [Antarian Rangers/Legends §Equipment and training](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Antarian_Rangers/Legends)
  - **History:**
    - They shrank to under half before the Clone Wars, then revived. In the war they served as leaders in the army.
    - Palpatine targeted them like the Jedi. A squad saved Rahm Kota from Order 66.
    - In the Empire they became a resistance sheltering Force-sensitives, hunted by Vader and the Inquisitorius.
    - Under Luke they rebuilt to 50–60, and by 25 ABY were near their pre-Clone Wars size. [Antarian Rangers/Legends §Clone Wars](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Antarian_Rangers/Legends) [Antarian Rangers/Legends §Dark Times](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Antarian_Rangers/Legends) [Antarian Rangers/Legends §Rebirth](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Antarian_Rangers/Legends)
- **Related:** Altisian Jedi (sheltered Rangers in the Purge)
- **Uncertainty:** "Force-sensitive and not": the sources describe non-Force-users plus a founder with limited sensitivity. No Force-sensitive branch or Ranger Force techniques were found.

### Disciples of Twilight
- **Type:** institution
- **Era:** c. 900 BBY to at least 137 ABY; Dyspeth (Varada sector)
- **Key facts:**
  - Founded by "Twilight", rumored to be a former Jedi, among human separatist colonists on a dark moon. Republic surveyors found Dyspeth 500 years later. [Disciples of Twilight §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Disciples_of_Twilight)
  - They protect the weak from the strong ("shadowy justice"). Any Force-sensitive who agrees with them may join. They had not picked a side in the 137 ABY war. [Disciples of Twilight §Philosophy and techniques](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Disciples_of_Twilight) [Disciples of Twilight §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Disciples_of_Twilight)

### Potentium
- **Type:** philosophy and its followers
- **Era:** "relativism" threatened the Jedi since at least Bane's time; Leor Hal's group was expelled in 132 BBY; still recognized as heresy in 43 ABY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Belief:** the Force is wholly good, and the dark side is only a perversion inside the user. Followers used "the Potentium" as their name for the Force. Some claimed good intent made any technique safe, even lightning and choking. Others still avoided the dark side. [Potentium §Beliefs](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Potentium)
  - **The expulsion:**
    - Leor Hal's followers were mostly Padawans from aristocratic Coruscant and Alderaan families. Hal and many of them were expelled in 132 BBY, and the rest left.
    - The Council did not count them as Dark Jedi, but said Jedi should protect, not test the Force's limits. [Potentium §Followers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Potentium) [Potentium §Beliefs](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Potentium)
  - Outcasts led by their first Magister, Hal, colonized Zonama Sekot. Sekot accepted the Potentium, but later told Anakin Skywalker it was not the Potentium. [Potentium §Followers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Potentium)
  - The NJO suspected Vergere had twisted Potentium ideas. Some Jedi theorized it began as a Sith plot. Sidious was disabused of the idea by Plagueis. [Potentium §Beliefs](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Potentium) [Potentium §Followers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Potentium)
- **Related:** Jedi ranks file (expulsions); Gray Paladins (Part 1)
- **Uncertainty:** [unclear] Whether it originated as a Sith scheme is only a Jedi theory; no link to the Sith was ever found. [Potentium §Followers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Potentium)

## Decisions needed
(Numbering continues from Part 1.)
12. [conflict] Nightsisters' founding: Gethzerion and Baritha in the late Republic vs Nightsisters already known by 340 BBY and fighting the Mecrosa c. 230 BBY. The best fit is to treat "Nightsisters" as a recurring name for dark exile clans, with Gethzerion founding her own clan.
13. [conflict] Zalem's Infinity Gate plot: 32, 31 or 30 BBY? 32 BBY is the page's lead date.
14. Are Witches' "spells" mechanically different from Jedi powers (needing a chant or ritual), or flavor only? The sources say they could not cast without the ritual, except for prodigies such as Gethzerion.
15. Antarian Rangers: keep them as a non-Force background, which fits the sources, or allow Force-sensitive Rangers?
16. [conflict] Iron Knights: excommunicated before 60 BBY or after 50 BBY? Fought in the Clone Wars or hid? Rejoined through Luke in 13 ABY, or found by students? *The Complete Star Wars Encyclopedia* (Aqinos page) gives the fuller account.
17. Potentium: does a follower who uses dark techniques "with good intent" still gain dark side taint? Legends says yes: it is heresy, and the Sith also saw it as misguided.
18. [conflict] Tyia's origin: recent (30 BBY) or ancient?
19. Gray Paladins' Force use: model it as a minimal-Force fighter class, as described?
