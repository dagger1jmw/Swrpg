# SWRPG Galaxy Timeline, 45–138 ABY (Part 3 of 3): Changes, Decisions and Sources

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 24, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Rulings of 24 Nov 2026 applied (lore-rulings-2026-11-24c.md; D-484).

## Summary
Part 3 holds the back sections for the whole 45–138 ABY set (Parts 1 and 2): what the old file got wrong, what could not be found, the Canon-only and unused items, game notes, the decisions the developer needs to make, the full source list and the checklist.

## Changes from the previous file
Compared with the "LEGACY ERA — 44–139 ABY" section of `SW_Legends_Galaxy_Timeline.md` (and its "POST-VONG" and "43–44 ABY" rows where they bear on this era), and with the lore text (not the game stats) of `SW_Characters_18_LegacyEra.md`, which was added to the lore folder late in this run and is checked in its own list below.

**Errors in the old file**
1. **Ossus Project dated "~127 ABY".** The project began in 122 ABY (or 126 ABY by one year page); 127 ABY is when the Jedi discovered the sabotage and the war began [122 ABY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/122_ABY); [Ossus Project](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ossus_Project); [127 ABY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/127_ABY).
2. **Darth Talon at the Massacre at Ossus (130 ABY).** Talon is not among the attackers; she became a Sith Lady and Hand only in 137 ABY. Darth Nihl led the attack, with Rulf Yage commanding the Imperial forces [Massacre at Ossus](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Massacre_at_Ossus); [Darth Talon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Talon); [Rulf Yage](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rulf_Yage).
3. **Krayt's coup: "one of his own Imperial Knights (secretly a Sith agent) kills a decoy".** No Imperial Knight betrayed Fel. Krayt, Maladi, Nihl and Wyyrlok killed the four Imperial Knights guarding the double (among them Mohrgan Fel) and the double himself; Krayt asked for "Emperor Fel's" life as the Sith's reward [Attack on Emperor Fel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Attack_on_Emperor_Fel).
4. **Kol Skywalker as "Luke's great-great-great-grandson".** No Legends source gives the number of generations. Kol is a descendant of Anakin and Luke; whether the line runs through Ben is unconfirmed (Jan Duursema called it likely in 2006; Sue Rostoni said it was not confirmed) [BTS] [Kol Skywalker](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kol_Skywalker); [Cade Skywalker](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cade_Skywalker).
5. **"Imperial Knights — light-side Force-users".** They reject the dark side but do not strictly follow the light; the New Jedi Order regarded them as Gray. Their loyalty is to the Force as embodied by the Emperor, with a duty to kill or redeem a fallen Emperor [Imperial Knight](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Knight). They were founded by Jagged Fel [Jagged Fel §A revived empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jagged_Fel).
6. **"Krayt and the One Sith ally with the Moff Council" at the war's start in 127 ABY.** The Moffs started the war by invoking the Treaty of Anaxes; most pages say the Sith joined a year later, in 128 ABY (Decision 3) [128 ABY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/128_ABY); [Attack on Emperor Fel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Attack_on_Emperor_Fel); [Galactic Federation of Free Alliances §Fall of the Galactic Alliance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_Federation_of_Free_Alliances).
7. **Genocide on Dac: "27% of Dac's population is evacuated".** The battle page, citing *Legacy* 47, says 20% were saved and more than 80% died; 27% appears only on the uncited 137 ABY year page (Decision 5). The old row also merges two orders: Krayt's order to execute a tenth of the Mon Calamari and send the rest to camps, and Wyyrlok's later Final Protocol to kill all life on Dac [Evacuation of Dac](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Evacuation_of_Dac); [Genocide on Dac](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Genocide_on_Dac); [Second Imperial Civil War §The final protocol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Imperial_Civil_War).
8. **Remnant–Empire-in-exile talks "at Bastion".** The first talks were on the neutral station The Wheel (and failed); the alliance was agreed on Agamar with the Jedi, and joint operations followed at Ralltiir and Dac [Second Imperial Civil War §Sith intrigue](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Imperial_Civil_War); [Mission to Agamar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mission_to_Agamar).
9. **"Fate of the Jedi … Darth Krayt does not appear in this story" (43–44 ABY row).** Krayt appears in *Fate of the Jedi: Apocalypse* (44 ABY), allies with Luke against Abeloth, and is identified as the "Dark Man" of Jacen's and Luke's visions [Darth Krayt §The rise of Darth Krayt (c. 25 ABY–130 ABY)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Krayt).
10. **"~30 ABY: A'Sharad Hett survives the Vong War … becomes Darth Krayt and founds the One Sith".** Hett was captured by a Yuuzhan Vong advance scout before the main invasion, escaped and returned to Korriban; the One Sith was founded "during or prior to 27 ABY" (*The Essential Reader's Companion*), and Wookieepedia's section dates his rise from c. 25 ABY [One Sith](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/One_Sith); [Darth Krayt §The rise of Darth Krayt (c. 25 ABY–130 ABY)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Krayt).
11. **Wyyrlok "crowned Dark Lord and Emperor".** This is what the uncited 137 ABY year page says, but the war article says Wyyrlok hid Krayt's death, claimed Krayt was in seclusion and ruled as Regent on his behalf (Decision 12) [137 ABY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/137_ABY); [Second Imperial Civil War §A coup](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Imperial_Civil_War).

**Errors in `SW_Characters_18_LegacyEra.md`** (lore statements only; the stat numbers are game design and were not checked)
1. **Darth Krayt "born ~40 BBY … ~200 years old".** Legends gives 47 BBY on Tatooine (*Outlander*), and in 130 ABY "more than a hundred and seventy years old" [Darth Krayt](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Krayt); [Darth Krayt §The consolidation of power](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Krayt).
2. **Krayt "killed and resurrected repeatedly".** He died once on Had Abbadon (137 ABY, killed by Wyyrlok), returned, and died for good at Cade's hand in 138 ABY [Darth Krayt](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Krayt).
3. **Darth Talon "bred and raised from birth … raised from infancy as a weapon of the One Sith".** Not in the sources: she was trained as an apprentice by Darth Ruyn and became a Sith Lady in 137 ABY [Darth Talon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Talon).
4. **Talon "beheaded a Jedi on Krayt's order".** The one she beheaded on Krayt's order was her Sith teacher, Darth Ruyn [Darth Talon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Talon).
5. **Darth Nihl "later Hand/Wrath".** No "Wrath" title was found. Nihl was Fist, then Hand from 130 ABY, lost the title in 137 ABY, and after Krayt's death led the One Sith into hiding as Dark Lord [Darth Nihl](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Nihl); [Darth Maladi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Maladi).
6. **Cade Skywalker "a Jedi youngling who survived the Massacre".** He was Wolf Sazen's Padawan, and fought at Ossus in an X-83 TwinTail [Cade Skywalker](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cade_Skywalker); [Ossus/Legends §Resurgent Sith](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ossus/Legends). "Spice-addicted": the sources speak of death sticks [Cade Skywalker §Exile from the Order](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cade_Skywalker).
7. **Roan Fel "allied with Krayt's Sith to defeat the Galactic Alliance".** The Moffs invoked the Treaty of Anaxes against his will and allied with the One Sith behind his back; he went along with the war reluctantly and kept his Knights out of it; the attack on Ossus was against his orders [Fel Empire §The Sith–Imperial War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fel_Empire); [Rulf Yage](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rulf_Yage); [Second Imperial Civil War §Origins](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Imperial_Civil_War). The sheet also omits his fall to the dark side and death at Antares Draco's hand in 138 ABY [Roan Fel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Roan_Fel).
8. **Hosk Trey'lis "Duros … failed to cure Krayt".** He is a Bothan Jedi healer; no source says he tried to cure Krayt. He was captured by Cade's crew, tortured by Maladi and killed by Krayt in 137 ABY [Hosk Trey'lis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hosk_Trey'lis).
9. **"Bantha Rawk (Skywalker kin) — Cade's adoptive-family contact on Vendaxa".** Rawk is Nat Skywalker, Kol's older brother and Cade's uncle, a former Jedi Master who rejoined in 138 ABY; his home was on Iego, and he died on Taivas [Rawk](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rawk).
10. **Wolf Sazen and Kol Skywalker entries omit their deaths' details**: Sazen and Darth Stryfe killed each other on Coruscant in 138 ABY; Kol was head of the Jedi High Council, killed by Nihl [Wolf Sazen](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wolf_Sazen); [Kol Skywalker](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kol_Skywalker).
11. Not checked: Jariah Syn as "son of a Jedi-killer" (his page was not fetched).

Darth Stryfe also dies; the old row omits him.

- The Fel Empire is dated "c. 45 ABY" by the timeline and "between 45 and 127 ABY" by its own infobox (Decision 1) [Timeline of galactic history/Legends §The Legacy era (37 ABY-140 ABY)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Timeline_of_galactic_history/Legends); [Fel Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fel_Empire).

- Darth Nihl's fate after 139 ABY: his page gives no death; he led the One Sith into hiding after Krayt's death [Darth Nihl](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Nihl).

## Game notes (optional, suggestions only)
- The 46–121 ABY gap is genuinely empty in Legends; if play goes there, the narrator should treat any event as new invention and tag it in the save, since nothing in the lore will contradict or confirm it. *(suggestion)*
- The Treaty of Anaxes, the Ossus Project's hundred worlds and the Vongspawn virus give a ready-made "slow fuse" for a campaign set in 122–127 ABY: players could discover the sabotage before 127 ABY. *(suggestion)*
- The Hidden Temple, the bounty of ten times more for a live Jedi, and the Imperial Knights' duty to kill a fallen Emperor are strong hooks for a Jedi-in-hiding campaign in 130–137 ABY. *(suggestion)*

## Decisions needed
None. Every item was ruled on 24 Nov 2026 (lore-rulings-2026-11-24c.md, 5–10; D-484); the rulings stand in the rows. The remaining items took the best fit: the sabotage is built in from the start, visible from 126 ABY and discovered in 127 ABY; the Dac genocide is in 137 ABY; Darth Stryfe becomes Fist at no given date, by 137 ABY; the Empire-in-exile has no fixed capital from 130 to 137 ABY, then Bastion. Where no best fit was given, the row keeps what it had: Luke's retirement in 45 ABY stands, tagged [unclear]; Jedi joining the Imperial Knights in 130 ABY is left out of the row's text. Item 11 (the Remnant's leader in 44–45 ABY) follows the ruling on the 44 ABY election: Vitor Reige, with Jagged Fel back at no given date.
