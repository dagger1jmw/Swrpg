# SWRPG Galaxy Timeline, 25–44 ABY (Part 2 of 4): Yuuzhan'tar to Victory, and the Recovery Years (27–34 ABY)

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 24, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends), as cited per entry. Rulings of 24 Nov 2026 applied (lore-rulings-2026-11-24c.md; D-484).

## Summary
Part 2 continues the Yuuzhan Vong War from the months after the fall of Coruscant (27 ABY) through the founding of the Galactic Alliance and the Battle of Ebaq (28 ABY) to the recapture of Coruscant, the Sekot Accords and the Conclave on Zonama Sekot (29 ABY), then covers the recovery years to 34 ABY: the Alliance's return to Coruscant, the new Jedi academy on Ossus, Jacen Solo's five-year journey and the first stirrings of the One Sith. Column layout, abbreviations and the calendar note are as in Part 1. Back sections for the whole set are in Part 4.

### Overview
The Galactic Alliance became the first government since the Empire to take in most of the known galaxy [Galactic Federation of Free Alliances](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_Federation_of_Free_Alliances). In 30 ABY Coruscant became the capital again, replacing Denon, and the Alliance formally took up authority over the galaxy, with Cal Omas as Chief of State and the joint Jedi-political High Council in place [30 ABY/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/30_ABY/Legends) [High Council (Galactic Alliance)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/High_Council_(Galactic_Alliance)). The Jedi built a new academy on Ossus to replace the lost Praxeum [Jedi Academy (Ossus)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Academy_(Ossus)). The Imperial Remnant's former holdings were part of the Alliance, and the Chiss kept closer ties with it after the war [Imperial Remnant/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Remnant/Legends) [Chiss Ascendancy/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chiss_Ascendancy/Legends). Hidden currents ran beneath the recovery: Darth Krayt began to gather the One Sith on Korriban, Lumiya waited, Corellia secretly began building a fleet, and Jacen Solo spent five years studying other Force traditions, where a vision of a dark figure ruling the galaxy set him on his path [30 ABY/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/30_ABY/Legends) [Darth Caedus](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Caedus) [Lumiya](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lumiya). The war's dead and the Sekot Accords' leniency left lasting bitterness [Yuuzhan Vong War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong_War).
