## Summary
Tier 2: price figures from **Legends-era games** — West End Games d6 (1987–1999), Wizards of the Coast d20/Saga (2000–2010, including *Star Wars Gamer* and Wizards.com articles), video-game shops and vendors (KOTOR 2003, KOTOR II 2004, SWTOR 2011+, *Star Wars Galaxies*, *Episode I Racer*, *Jabba's Game Galaxy*, *Clone Wars Adventures* and others), and FFG (2012–early 2016, always [gray]). Every figure is labelled **(game stat: book/game, publisher, publication date)**. Infobox "Cost" fields sourced to these games are game stats.

**This part covers:** Starships and space stations — 302 item rows.
**All parts together:** 1833 dated game-stat figures and 690 with era not stated; 559 FFG [gray] figures (554 after 5 Nexus of Power rows moved to Not used, Jared, D-464).

Key game anchors (all parts): Z-95 Headhunter 80,000 cr new (*Starships of the Galaxy*, Saga, Dec 2007) / 45,000 used (*Rebel Alliance Sourcebook*, WEG 1990); YT-1300 light freighter 100,000 cr new / 25,000 used (*Starships of the Galaxy*, Saga 2007); DL-44 heavy blaster pistol 750 cr [unclear source]; 3PO protocol droid 3,000 cr and R2 astromech 4,525 cr (*Cynabar's Fantastic Technology: Droids*, WEG 1997); MD medical droid 2,000 cr (*Arms & Equipment Guide*, WotC 2002); bacta tank 3,000–100,000 cr (SWTOR/other games [unclear]); a WEG cantina meal 2–15 cr and inn room 10–15 cr/night (*Wretched Hives of Scum & Villainy*, 1997). Exact rows are in the tables.
