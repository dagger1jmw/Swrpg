Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 24, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
Part 2 covers 9–1 BBY: Han Solo's Academy years, his discharge for saving Chewbacca (5 BBY) and his smuggling career with Lando Calrissian; Lando's Centrality adventures; the Battle of Nar Shaddaa (4 BBY); the reawakening of Arden Lyn; *The Force Unleashed* (3–2 BBY, told from the novelizations per the developer's ruling); the Corellian System Meetings, the Corellian Treaty and the Declaration of Rebellion (2 BBY); the start of the Galactic Civil War (2 BBY on Wookieepedia's GCW page); and the year before Yavin (the Anti-Sith conspiracy, Dark Forces' Sulon, the Ralltiir subjugation, the Battle of Ylesia). Format, tags and column meanings are as in Part 1. "Changes from the previous file", "Decisions needed" and "Sources" are in Part 4.

### Overview
In these years the Empire reached its territorial and military height (1 BBY) [1 BBY/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/1_BBY/Legends) while resistance grew: Garm Bel Iblis, Bail Organa and Mon Mothma ran separate cells until the Corellian Treaty of 2 BBY, which began the Galactic Civil War [Galactic Civil War/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_Civil_War/Legends); [Corellian Treaty](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Corellian_Treaty). The Alliance's operation to obtain and destroy the Death Star, Operation Skyhook, ran from 2 to 0 BBY [Operation Skyhook](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Operation_Skyhook). The Death Star project moved repeatedly and reached the Horuz system (Despayre) in 3 BBY after a prototype's successful test [DS-1 Orbital Battle Station/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DS-1_Orbital_Battle_Station/Legends). The Great Jedi Purge lasted until 0 BBY, when so few Jedi remained that the Emperor no longer considered them a threat [Great Jedi Purge/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Jedi_Purge/Legends). [note: Han and Lando's early years are given here in the order that Han Solo's Legends article tells them] [Han Solo/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Han_Solo/Legends)
