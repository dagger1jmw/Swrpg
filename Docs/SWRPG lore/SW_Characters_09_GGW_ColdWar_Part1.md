Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 21, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

**Set:** SW_Characters_09_GGW_ColdWar_Index.md (master index, open questions, inclusion rules, topic-file conflicts); Part1 (this file: Jedi and light-side users); Part2 (the Emperor, the Wrath, the Dark Council, the Dread Masters, other Sith Lords, apprentices, academy staff and other dark-side users); Part3a (Republic government, military, SIS and Republic-side companions); Part3b (Imperial government, military and Intelligence, Imperial-side companions, Mandalorians, underworld and Hutts, others). ## Summary
This file covers named Jedi and other light-side Force users active between 3681 BBY (the Empire's return at Korriban) and 3636 BBY (the Eternal Empire's arrival), with each person's status in 3661 BBY (the game's default start) and in 3653 BBY (the Sacking of Coruscant). Key points: Zym was Grand Master until his death in 3653 BBY, and Satele Shan replaced him in 3651 BBY. Ngani Zho died c. 3643 BBY in the Vesla system, not at Malgus's hands. Orgus Din came from Rishi and has no recorded birth year. Dar'Nala was a Togruta who fell and died in 3653 BBY. Most Cold War companions were small children or not yet born in 3661 BBY. Narrative already in the topic files (battles, Temple life, ranks) is not repeated; entries point there.

Missing developer files: SW_Force_Powers_* and the developer's SW_Jedi_Training / Sith_Training files are **not on the box**, so I could not cross-check them. The lightsaber, rank, Temple-life and war files were checked.
## Entries

How to read an entry. "ID" gives names and titles, species, sex and homeworld. "Life" gives birth and death and how each is known. "(infobox)" means the fact is in the page's character infobox; I pulled the infoboxes for every page listed here (build file c9/ibox.tsv). "Ranks" gives rank or office by year. "Teachers/students" gives masters and apprentices. "Forms/powers" lists only lightsaber forms and Force powers that the page names; no levels are given unless stated. "Alignment" covers falls, redemptions and visible corruption. "3661 BBY / 3653 BBY" says where the person was in 3661 BBY (the game's default start) and in 3653 BBY (the Sacking). "Feats" gives feats that show scale. Ages are worked out from birth years only where a birth year is stated, and are tagged [inferred].

### Zym
- **Type:** character (Jedi Grand Master)
- **Era:** before 3681 BBY – 3653 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - ID: male Kel Dor Jedi Master; Grand Master of the Order during the Great Galactic War [Zym](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zym). Silver eyes, orange skin; homeworld not given (infobox) [Zym](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zym).
  - Life: birth not stated. Died 3653 BBY on Coruscant (infobox) [Zym](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zym); killed by the bounty hunter Braden after the Treaty was signed [Zym §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zym). [conflict] on the manner of death, see Uncertainty.
  - Ranks: Jedi Master, then a High Council seat, then Grand Master "sometime before" the war [Zym §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zym). Satele Shan succeeded him two years after his death [Zym §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zym).
  - Teachers/students: none named.
  - Forms/powers: built a blue-bladed lightsaber; no form or power named [Zym §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zym).
  - Alignment: light side; no fall.
  - 3661 BBY / 3653 BBY: in 3661 BBY he was Grand Master [inferred from holding the office from before 3681 BBY until 3653 BBY]. In 3653 BBY he advised Chancellor Berooken to stay on Coruscant, was away from Coruscant when it was attacked, told the Alderaan delegation to accept the Sith terms, and then sent Dar'Nala and Satele to oversee the troop withdrawal [Zym §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zym).
  - Feats: led the Council's decision to fight for the Senate when the Empire attacked [Zym §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zym).
- **Related:** Satele Shan; Dar'Nala; Braden (Part3b); Berooken (Part3a).
- **Uncertainty:** [conflict] the Zym page has him killed by Braden after the Treaty [Zym §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zym), but SW_Great_Galactic_War_Part2 (Decision 2) records that the Bela Kiwiiks page places his death in the Sacking. Both agree on the year, 3653 BBY.
