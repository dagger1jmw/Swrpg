# SWRPG Galaxy Timeline, 3,950–3,701 BBY (Part 3 of 3): Changes, Gaps, Decisions and Sources

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched 24 Nov 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends), as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
This part holds the back sections for the whole 3,950–3,701 BBY set (Parts 1–2): what the existing lore files get wrong or leave out for this span, what could not be found, what was left out and why, the decisions the developer needs to make, and every page used. The span is thin on Wookieepedia: only twenty year pages exist for its 250 years, and two long stretches (c. 3,899–3,841 BBY and 3,799–3,760 BBY) have no dated event at all.

## Not found
- **Supreme Chancellors** between Tol Cressa (last attested 3,946 BBY) and Berooken (in office by 3,653 BBY); when Cressa left office or died; who won the c. 3,900 BBY election that Vaner Shan contested [Template:Chancellor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Template:Chancellor); [Supreme Chancellor election, 3900 BBY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Supreme_Chancellor_election,_3900_BBY).
- **Jedi leadership:** no Grand Master or named Council member is dated in the span (Mical's seat is undated); no date for the reopening of the Coruscant Temple [Jedi Order/Legends §First Jedi Purge and aftermath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order/Legends).
- **The new Dark Council of 3,950 BBY:** none of the twelve appointees is named; no Councillor is dated between 3,950 and c. 3,700 BBY [Dark Council §Darth Vitiate's Dark Council](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Council).
- **Dated events in 3,945–3,901, 3,899–3,841 and 3,799–3,760 BBY:** none found. Year pages exist only for 3,701–3,705, 3,707, 3,708, 3,712, 3,716, 3,732, 3,743, 3,749, 3,756, 3,758, 3,809, 3,838, 3,840, 3,900, 3,946 and 3,950 BBY.
- **Eternal Empire:** no founding date; when the Emperor took Valkorion's body is given only as "centuries" before 3,636 BBY [Valkorion](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Valkorion).
- **Mandalorians:** the date of Canderous Ordo's death; who (if anyone) led the clans after him before 3,667 BBY; Mandalore the Infernal's dates [Canderous Ordo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Canderous_Ordo); [Mandalore the Infernal](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalore_the_Infernal).
- **Colonisation and trade** beyond Grizmallt's colonies, Naboo and the Hydian Way: nothing dated. The "Second Expansion Wave" has no Wookieepedia page.
- **Deaths of the KotOR heroes** (Bastila Shan, Carth Onasi, Mission Vao, Zaalbar, Jolee Bindo, Juhani, Atris, the Lost Jedi): no dates given.
- **Telosian Restoration Project:** no completion date (still under way in 3,643 BBY) [Telosian Restoration Project](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telosian_Restoration_Project).
- **The 3,950 BBY claim that "a Sith Lord" was the sole survivor of the Sith Civil War and took an apprentice:** the year page gives no source for it [3950 BBY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/3950_BBY); see Not used.

None of the subjects in this span is Canon-only; every subject searched has a Legends page. (Revan, Grizmallt, Naboo's colonisation and the Hydian Way have separate Canon pages; only the Legends versions were used.)

## Not used
- **Freedon Nadd destroying Sadow's spirit in 4,400 BBY** (*Complete Star Wars Encyclopedia*): left out of the timeline; SWTOR (2010) has Ovair destroy it c. 3,750 BBY [Ruled (Jared, 24 Nov 2026): Nadd kills Sadow's body; the spirit survives until Ovair's duel].
- **"Nine innocent" Dark Councillors** (Dark Council page): breaks the purge's arithmetic [Ruled (Jared, 24 Nov 2026)].
- **Grizmallti civil war and the Human–Gungan "mutual avoidance" treaty** (c. 3,900 BBY): both details come from *Nexus of Power* (Fantasy Flight Games, 2015), which the research rules rule out as not Legends. The Legends facts kept are the expedition, Kwilaan and the war with the Gungans (from *The New Essential Chronology*) [Grizmallti civil war/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grizmallti_civil_war/Legends); [Human-Gungan conflicts/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Human-Gungan_conflicts/Legends).
- **Darth Igrol's "lavish estate" on Dromund Fels:** the word comes from the *Force and Destiny* Core Rulebook (FFG, 2015), at the edge of the gray-area window; only *Revan*'s statement that he lived on Dromund Fels is supported [Darth Igrol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Igrol).
- **"Some time later, a sole survivor on Korriban began the rule of two by finding a Sith Apprentice"** (Galactic Republic/Legends body text, uncited; echoed without a source on the 3,950 BBY page): untraceable, and it contradicts the established origin of the Rule of Two with Darth Bane c. 1,000 BBY [Galactic Republic/Legends §Old Sith Wars](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_Republic/Legends); [3950 BBY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/3950_BBY).
- **The Hydian Way "led to the discovery of … the Corellian Trade Spine, starting the Second Expansion Wave"** (Galactic Republic/Legends body, uncited): *The Essential Atlas* has the Spine fully established by 5,500 BBY [Corellian Trade Spine/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Corellian_Trade_Spine/Legends).
- **Kreia's predictions** (Brianna succeeding Atris as the Order's lead historian; Visas Marr returning to Katarr; Mira's possible death): predictions in the game, not events [Brianna](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Brianna); [Visas Marr](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Visas_Marr); [Mira](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mira).
- **The Order of Revan's founding:** "decades before the Cold War", almost three hundred years after Revan's attack; most likely after 3,700 BBY, so left to the 3,700 BBY files [Order of Revan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Order_of_Revan).
- **Exal Kressh's apprenticeship to the Emperor** ("around the turn of the thirteenth century of his rule"): it belongs to the early Great Galactic War, after this span [Tenebrae §Final preparations and a new empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrae); [Exal Kressh](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Exal_Kressh).

## Game notes (optional, suggestions only)
- Suggestion: 3,950 BBY is a natural "prologue" year: a player in Kaas City during the purge riots and curfew could cross paths with Scourge, Surik or the Imperial Guard without changing the outcome.
- Suggestion: the Ovair affair (3,756 BBY) suits a Jedi investigation hook in a Republic-side campaign: the Senate testimony, the public duel and a Council that "suspected nothing".
- Suggestion: the long blank stretches (3,899–3,841 and 3,799–3,760 BBY) leave the narrator free to invent local events, as long as they keep to the fixed facts (Cressa, the Kanz Disorders, a fleet guarding Korriban, Revan in the Maelstrom).
