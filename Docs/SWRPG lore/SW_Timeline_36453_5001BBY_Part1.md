# SWRPG Galaxy Timeline, 36,453–5,001 BBY (Part 1 of 3): Tython, the Je'daii, the Force Wars and the Infinite Empire

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 24, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends), as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
This three-part file rebuilds the oldest slice of `SW_Legends_Galaxy_Timeline.md` (its "Dawn of the Jedi", "Old Republic Founding & First Conflicts" and "Golden Age of the Sith" sections) as one sourced row per event, from the Tho Yor Arrival (36,453 BBY) to the eve of Marka Ragnos's death (5,001 BBY; his death and funeral in 5,000 BBY open the next file). Part 1 (this file) covers 36,453–25,054 BBY: the Je'daii on Tython, the Despot War, the Rakatan invasion of the Tython system and the Force Wars that produced the Jedi Order (25,783 BBY), the Infinite Empire and its fall (c. 25,200 BBY), King Adas and the first Sith kingdom, human sleeper-ship colonisation, and Xer and Xim of the Tion. Part 2 covers 25,053–7,004 BBY: the Republic's founding, the Jedi joining it from Ossus (24,953 BBY), the First Great Schism, the Tionese War, the Alsakan Conflicts, the Pius Dea and the quiet millennia after. Part 3 covers 7,003–5,001 BBY (the Hundred-Year Darkness, the Exiles and the Sith Empire, and Marka Ragnos) and holds the back sections, including the answer to "when did the Jedi move their centre from Tython to Coruscant" (short answer: they never moved from Tython to Coruscant; they left Tython for Ossus c. 25,783 BBY, and only made Coruscant their main headquarters after Ossus was devastated in 3,996 BBY).

Key facts for play: dates before 25,783 BBY come almost entirely from the *Dawn of the Jedi* comics and novel and are given to the year; most dates after that are round numbers ("c.") from *The Essential Atlas*, *The Essential Guide to Warfare*, *Jedi vs. Sith* and the SWTOR material. Wookieepedia's own year pages show long blank stretches; these are stated in each era overview.

## DAWN OF THE JEDI — 36,453–25,806 BBY
## ═══════════════════════════════════

### Overview
Eight pyramid ships, the Tho Yor, gather Force-sensitives of over a dozen species and bring them to Tython in the Deep Core, where a ninth, larger Tho Yor waits. The settlers (Tythans) found the Je'daii Order, which seeks balance between Ashla (light) and Bogan (dark), named for Tython's two moons; Je'daii who lean too far either way are sent to the opposite moon to meditate. Nine temple-cities rise around the Tho Yor landing points by 36,019 BBY. More than a millennium after the arrival, non-Force-sensitives are sent off-world (the Second Migration) and settle the rest of the system (the Settled Worlds). **Blank centuries:** between c. 35,453 BBY and 25,823 BBY Wookieepedia records only a handful of Je'daii-side dates (28,930 and c. 28,793 BBY), plus several year pages (33,908, 32,925, 32,920, 28,916, 28,915, 28,912, 27,987, 27,453, 26,012 and 25,997 BBY) that exist only because *Into the Void* mentions them, with no event text on Wookieepedia. Nothing else is recorded on Tython for roughly ten thousand years. (The Infinite Empire, the Sith and human colonisation in the same span are in their own sections below.)

## ═══════════════════════════════════

### Overview
In 25,805 BBY the Twi'lek Despot Queen Hadiya unites Shikaakwa's Nine Houses and invades Tython; she is killed at Kaleth by the undercover Ranger Daegen Lok. ## FORCE WARS AND THE FIRST JEDI — c. 25,793–25,783 BBY
## ═══════════════════════════════════

### Overview
After the Rakata are driven off, the Je'daii split into followers of Ashla (seeking knowledge) and of Bogan (seeking power). The resulting **Force Wars** last about ten years and are counted as the first war between light and dark. The Ashla followers found the **Jedi Order** with an early council; the co-founder Rajivari turns dark and is killed; his followers die in the siege of Kaleth; a cataclysm wrecks Tython's biosphere; and the Jedi leave for Ossus. The order of events inside this decade is not dated year by year [Ruled (Jared, 24 Nov 2026): the wars begin as the Rakatan invasion, then the Ashla and Bogan followers fight]. Both sides fought with Force-imbued metal swords.

## THE INFINITE EMPIRE — c. 35,000–25,200 BBY
## ═══════════════════════════════════

### Overview
The Rakatan Infinite Empire runs in parallel with the Je'daii era. It builds the Star Forge by 35,000 BBY, reaches its peak of over five hundred Force-rich worlds c. 30,000 BBY, wars on the Celestials' other client species, and conquers worlds from Coruscant to Kashyyyk. Around 25,200 BBY slave revolts, civil war and a plague that strips the Rakata of the Force end it. **Blank centuries:** apart from the Kashyyyk computer (33,598 and 33,357 BBY) and the Korriban invasion (c. 27,700 BBY), no Rakatan event is dated between c. 30,000 and c. 25,200 BBY.
