# Starship Shields and Weapons (Part 2 of 2: carriers, capital-ship duels, back sections)

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched 25 Nov 2026 by Claude through the Wookieepedia API (Legends pages). [note: ruled by Jared, 25 Nov 2026; see Decisions. D-498, D-505] See Part 1 for the shield and weapon mechanics this part refers to.

## Summary
Armament profiles for the main warship and starfighter classes from the Old Sith Wars to the New Republic, with shield and hull ratings where any source gives them and the source type labelled (the tables are in `SW_Starships_Classes.md`). Most hard numbers come from cross-section books (treated as lore) or from game stat lines (labelled). The key qualitative lore: dedicated carriers have few weapons and weak shields; the *Venator* was a true warship that could put almost all its reactor into its guns; Mon Calamari cruisers trade total firepower for all-round arcs and strong, fast-replenishing shields; Star Destroyers are wedge-shaped to focus forward and are poor at close range; capital-ship duels are decided by bringing more guns to bear, concentrating fire, and attacking the bridge and shield domes.

**How to read the tables.** "Lore" = cross-section books, Essential Guides, *Behind the Magic*, novels, films. "Game" = RPG stat books (WEG, Wizards, KOTOR/Clone Wars Campaign Guides), flight games (SBD/RU), miniatures. [gray] = FFG 2013–2014. Arcs (fore/port/starboard/aft) come from WEG's *Imperial Sourcebook* (2nd ed.) or *Rebel Alliance Sourcebook* (2nd ed.) and are game-book figures.

## Game notes (optional, suggestions only)
These are suggestions for the developer, not lore.

**Shield recharge modelling**
- *Suggestion:* treat shields as a pool per facing (fore, aft, port, starboard for capital ships; fore/aft for fighters), matching the lore of facings worn down by focused fire and the "roll to show fresh shields" counter (nova flare), the "double front" setting and the SWTOR MD-FA fore/aft shift.
- *Suggestion:* recharge per round = a class trait, not a fixed number. Lore hooks: Mon Cal cruisers recharge faster (many generators); post-7500 BBY shields regenerate faster than older designs; PulseScreen-style fighter shields are weak but quick.
- *Suggestion:* projector burnout. When a facing hits zero, that facing's projector is out until repaired; capital ships with damage-control crews restore it after several rounds ("minutes"), fighters cannot. This matches the EGWT burn-out-and-swap description.
- *Suggestion:* power shunting as an action: move points from weapons to shields or back. The *Venator* "true warship" rule (almost all reactor to the guns) and the TIE Defender recharge settings support it. Shunting to guns could leave shields recharging at a reduced rate.
- *Suggestion:* the [S-canon] thermal shield rule ("once down, cannot be raised until fully recharged") is a good special trait for a few named Separatist ships, if TCW material is used.

**Weapon versus shield effects**
- *Suggestion:* split the pool into ray and particle layers only for ships where it matters; otherwise use one pool, and let missiles and torpedoes **bypass** the ray portion. A simple form: energy weapons hit the shield pool; torpedoes and missiles hit the shield pool only if the target has particle shields up (warships always do in combat), otherwise the hull.
- *Suggestion:* particle shields drop for a moment whenever the ship launches fighters or fires its own warheads; an attacker who times a torpedo volley to that moment ignores them.
- *Suggestion:* ion hits ignore shields (unless the target spent an action reconfiguring against ions, which halves its protection against lasers for the round) and deal "system damage" instead of hull damage: disable weapons, shields or engines for 1–3 rounds; capital ships repair faster.
- *Suggestion:* staggered missile pairs: first missile strips shields at a point, second gets a bonus against the same facing.
- *Suggestion:* shield domes and generators as called-shot targets (bridge-shield dome on ISDs, *Executor*); destroying one removes the shield over the bridge, not the whole ship's.

**Relative firepower scale (suggestion, built on the lore ratios)**
| Tier | Weapon | Suggested relative damage | Lore anchor |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fighter laser cannon | 1 | baseline |
| 2 | Quad laser / heavy laser | 2 | none exact; quad turbolasers "magnitudes" above quad lasers |
| 3 | Turbolaser (light) | 3 | turbolaser bolt = 3× laser cannon bolt (NEGWT) |
| 4 | Heavy turbolaser (XX-9, DBY-827) | 6–10 | DBY-827 seven intensity settings; *Venator*'s rivals the ISD main battery |
| 5 | Proton torpedo / concussion missile | 8 vs hull, 0 vs ray-only shields | "single warhead can destroy a starfighter" and damage capital ships (EGWT); PocketModel says one missile can beat a single turbolaser shot |
| 6 | Superweapon-class (ion pulse, agrocite) | scenario-only | [S-canon] one-shot capital kills |
- *Suggestion:* turbolasers suffer a heavy penalty against fighters (EGWT: "not designed to target" them); point-defence guns and flak get a bonus against fighters and missiles and little effect on capital ships.
- *Suggestion:* SBD/RU game numbers can seed a relative scale (X-wing 50 SBD, CR90 400, Nebulon-B 960, VSD 3200, ISD 4800, *Executor* 96,000), but they are game stats and the developer should decide whether to use them.

## Decisions (ruled)
[note: Jared agreed with every decision below, 25 Nov 2026 (D-505; number 1 first in D-498). Built in docs-v2/spec/combat-scales.md.]
1. **Ray versus particle shields** [conflict]: two layers (EGWT, so torpedoes and missiles ignore ray shields) or one combined pool that torpedoes "hit" (nova flare, concussion missile pages)? **Ruled:** one shield pool per facing; torpedoes and missiles partly bypass it unless the ship's particle shielding is strong.
2. **Recharge numbers**: no lore figure exists. **Ruled:** a game rule with invented numbers, not lore: recovery from spare reactor power over time, faster with more generators and after c. 7,500 BBY. A burnt-out projector needs repair, not recharge.
3. **Game-stat numbers**: SBD/RU, WEG/Wizards lines, or neither? **Ruled:** relative values from the lore's ratios (a turbolaser is three laser cannons); tabletop and game ratings fill gaps only, tagged invented.
4. **[S-canon] thermal shields** (Trench's ships). **Ruled:** not used generally (secondary canon, Separatist only).
5. **Ion versus shields**: EGWT's "ion passes ray shields unless reconfigured". **Ruled:** adopted; retuned against ion, the shields are then weak against lasers.
6. **TIE Interceptor armament** [conflict]: 4 lasers (*Starfighter Battle Book*) or 6 (*Behind the Magic*)? **Ruled:** the most-cited figure. [note: 4. The page also cites *The Stele Chronicles* (1994) for four L-s9.3, one gun on each wingtip.]
7. **B-wing torpedo load** [conflict, minor]: 12 (SotG Saga) or 8 heavy (SotG 2001; CCS)? **Ruled:** the most-cited figure. [note: 8.]
8. **Gray-sourced armament counts** (MC80 *Liberty*, *Dreadnaught*, *Interdictor* SD, *Secutor*, *Quasar Fire* from FFG 2014 books). **Ruled:** kept, tagged as a weak source.
9. **Turbolaser wattage** (X-wing 126–500 TW, ISD 3,000+ TW) has no reference on its page: flavour, or drop? **Ruled:** agreed with the rest. [note: read as flavour; no number in the game comes from it.]
10. **`SW_Starships_Classes.md`**: should these class profiles become that file? **Ruled:** merge the new class tables into it. [note: it did exist; it was missing from the researcher's uploads only. Merged.]
