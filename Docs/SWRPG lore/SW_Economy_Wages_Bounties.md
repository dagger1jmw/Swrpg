## Summary
- **Wages:** In-universe Legends sources give few wage figures. The firmest are:
  - Radnor's average annual pay of 5,000 karsems (27 BBY).
  - Mercenary rates of 30,000 cr per day (a whole mercenary army's commander, 19 ABY) and 50,000 cr per month (a guard force, 19 ABY).
  - A 20 million cr per month planetary contract for a Mandalorian fleet and infantry (c.40–41 ABY).
  - Game books add individual salaries: 500 cr/month for New Republic scouts, 2,000 cr/month for a colony pilot, 3,200 cr/month for a smuggler's starting salary.
- **Bounties:** These are well recorded.
  - Common criminals in the late Republic: 1,000–5,000 cr.
  - Notable Imperial-era targets: 10,000–100,000 cr.
  - Galaxy-famous targets: 250,000 cr to 5 million cr.
  - Record figures: 2.5 million cr on Cad Bane (Clone Wars); 10 million cr on the Great Hunt's champion (SWTOR, but see [conflict]).
- **Guilds and licences:** guilds take about 10%. Imperial-era licences (WEG game stat) cost 1,000 cr one-time or 10–10,000 cr per month.
- **Jedi:** Jedi take no pay. The Senate funds the Order (WotC game book), and Jedi refuse rewards (KOTOR).

### Ordinary wages and living standards
- **Type:** Wages. **Era:** Various.

**Key facts:**
- [in-universe] 27 BBY, Radnor:
  - 5,000 karsems was the average one-year pay. The same sum bought a bio-isolation suit during the bioplague panic.
  - No credit equivalent is given.
  - Source: W:Karsem (*Jedi Quest: The Way of the Apprentice*, 2002).
- [in-universe] c.4 ABY, Jabba's Palace, Tatooine: Barada, a slave, was paid a periodic salary under contract.
  - Saving it, he was 2,000 cr short of buying his freedom, and had to raise that within a year.
  - Source: W:Barada/Legends (*Tales from Jabba's Palace*, 1996; Galaxy Guide 5; *Essential Guide to Characters*).
- [in-universe] Galactic Civil War era (no year), Bothawui: annual operating taxes were "very low" and "salaries and other costs were also reasonable". No wage figure is given.
  - Source: W:Bothawui/Legends (§Laws), citing *Shadows of the Empire Planets Guide* (WEG, 1997), so effectively [game stat: SOTEPG, WEG 1997].
- [in-universe] c.3636 BBY, Zakuul:
  - Every citizen received "a monthly stipend of credits that would make them among the elite class of any Core World". Food, shelter, clothing and education were provided free.
  - No figure is given.
  - Source: W:Zakuul (§The Golden Age of the Eternal Empire; SWTOR KotFE codex).
- [in-universe] Imperial era (year not given): one semester at the Institute of Starship Engineers cost 15,000 Imperial credits. Full membership opened careers "with self-made wages". Source: W:Institute_of_Starship_Engineers [unclear: source work not identified on the page].

**Related:** SW_Economy_Currencies (karsem).

**Uncertainty:**
- In-universe pay for soldiers, officers, labourers or politicians was not found (see Not found).

### Military pay and costs
- **Type:** Military wages and expenditure. **Era:** Various.

**Key facts:**
- [game stat: Imperial Sourcebook 2e, WEG 1994] Pre-Clone Wars Republic:
  - The Navy was paid for by the Republic as a whole and its expenditures stayed stable.
  - Army spending fell because non-wealthy member worlds complained about the cost of maintaining forces.
  - Source: W:Imperial_Navy/Legends (§Role).
- [in-universe] Late Republic: the Navy "demanded higher wages from the Senate". Source: W:Republic_Military/Legends (§Branch Relations). Cross-reference: SW_SWTOR_Era_Power_Structures_Part2.
- [in-universe] 22 BBY, Chandrila:
  - 575 Public Safety Commission employees walked out over the Military Creation Act, which "funneled" local tax revenues earmarked for local forces to the Army of the Republic.
  - Source: W:Military_Creation_Act/Legends (§Galactic change; HNN, 2002).
- [in-universe] c.0–1 ABY, Empire (Dark Trooper project): the Emperor spent "over half a million credits" per Dark Trooper candidate and officer, to feed, house and educate each over four years. Source: W:Rom_Mohc (*Dark Forces* tie-ins).
- [in-universe] Imperial era: the Empire put "quintillions of credits" into expanding the military. Source: W:Galactic_Empire/Legends (§Economy, citing *Dark Empire Sourcebook*): [game stat: Dark Empire Sourcebook, WEG 1993].
- [in-universe] c.19 BBY: credits diverted from "the Republic's people" earned Skirata's group 200 million credits a week "without compound interest". Source: W:Kal_Skirata (*Imperial Commando: 501st*, 2009).

**Related:** SW_Economy_Institutions_Debt (Republic taxes); SW_Economy_Events.

**Uncertainty:** The Dark Troopers page lists both *Dark Forces* and its novels; the exact work is [unclear].

### Mercenary rates
- **Type:** Mercenary pay. **Era:** Old Republic to Galactic Alliance.

**Key facts:**
- [in-universe] 3951 BBY, Khoonda, Dantooine: mercenaries cost a 1,000 cr recruitment and training fee each, and Azkul offered a 4,000 cr bribe. Source: W:Battle_of_Khoonda (KOTOR II, 2004).
- [in-universe] c.3653–3640 BBY: under Mandalore the Vindicated, the Mandalorians were "the well-paid but cautious allies of the Sith". Source: W:Mandalorian/Legends (§Crusader's schism; SWTOR web holonet). Detail is in SW_Mandalorians_GGW_Part1.
- [in-universe] c.60 BBY: Jaster Mereel's Supercommando Codex said Mandalorians should fight as "highly paid soldiers" and honourable mercenaries. Source: W:Mandalorian/Legends (§Cultural shift; §Mercenaries).
- [in-universe] 44 BBY, Galidraan: Jango Fett's True Mandalorians went to the governor's estate to collect their payment, and found he had already called the Jedi. Source: W:Mandalorian/Legends (§The Mandalorian Civil War).
- [in-universe] c.30–22 BBY, Bank of Aargau: Walon Vau invested his mercenary earnings and his Cuy'val Dar fee there. Source: W:Bank_of_Aargau/Legends.
- [in-universe] 19 ABY, Nhora systems: the Nhoras offered Dharus "the high sum of 30,000 credits per day" to lead their army as commander of the Dhashaan Shield mercenaries. Source: W:Dharus (*Specter of the Past*, 1997).
- [in-universe] 19 ABY: Reggi asked 50,000 cr a month for the Soskin Guard to work for Calrissian; Lando refused. Source: W:Reggi_(Soskin_Guard) (*Specter of the Past*).
- [in-universe] 24 ABY: Beviin was "well paid" by Nom Anor, and Fett took Yuuzhan Vong work because the Mandalorians "needed credits". Source: W:Mandalorian/Legends (§The Yuuzhan Vong invasion).
- [in-universe] c.40–41 ABY, Bothawui:
  - The government offered the Mandalorians 20 million cr per month for an assault fleet and infantry force.
  - It added a premium for exclusive purchase rights to the Bes'uliik fighter.
  - Source: W:Mandalorian/Legends (§Restoration; *Legacy of the Force*).
- [game stat: SW RPG (core), WEG] Galactic Civil War: Imperial bureaucracy led some mercenaries to fight for the Alliance unpaid. Source: W:Mercenary/Legends (§History) [unclear edition].

**Related:** SW_Mandalorians_GGW_Part1.

**Uncertainty:** Bothawui is dated only by its *Legacy of the Force* context (c.40–41 ABY).

### Bounty guilds, licences and fees
- **Type:** Fees and permits. **Era:** Mostly Imperial era (WEG).

**Key facts:**
- [in-universe] Imperial era: the Bounty Hunters' Guild charged a "customary" 10% handling fee. Source: W:Bilman_Dowd [unclear date].
- [in-universe] c.4 ABY: the Guild split. The elders' "True Guild" had "reaped the profits", which angered the younger, bounty-earning hunters of the Guild Reform Committee. Source: W:Bounty_Hunters'_Guild/Legends (§Galactic Civil War).
- [in-universe] Imperial era: without a valid B23-1-14 permit, Sammie Staable got only the local bounty (245 cr) on Ten-Suckers Madoom, because the permit is needed to collect system, sector and regional bounties. Source: W:B23-1-14_permit (*Rookies: Rendezvous*, 2006 webstrip; *Insider* 99, 2008).
- Guild and permit fees from game books are in the "Game stat figures" table:
  - IPKC 1,000 cr.
  - Capture Permit 25% or 500 cr.
  - Target, System and Sector permits.
  - House Neuvalis and Mantis Syndicate dues.
  - The "hundred club".
- [in-universe] Imperial era: some Imperial bounties were open only to holders of an Imperial Peace-Keeping Certificate. Examples:
  - Nintuk: 75,000 cr. Source: W:Nintuk.
  - MI-726: 100,000 cr, from the Imperial Office of Criminal Investigations. Source: W:MI-726.
  - Skawn Bonduna: 30,000 cr. Source: W:Skawn_Bonduna.
  - [unclear] sources for all three.

**Related:** SW_Economy_Institutions_Debt (Imperial Penal Code fines).

### Jedi Order: no pay, and how it is funded
- **Type:** Funding. **Era:** Old Republic to Clone Wars.

**Key facts:**
- [game stat: Power of the Jedi Sourcebook, WotC 2002] Funding rules (c.1000–19 BBY):
  - In the modern era of the Republic, the Order was funded by the Republic Senate.
  - Jedi on missions got funds sized to the assignment. Padawans were not entrusted with funds; their Masters managed them.
  - Unused funds and any credits obtained on a mission were returned to the Temple.
  - Source: W:Jedi_Order/Legends (§Funding).
- [in-universe] 3956 BBY, Dantooine: offered a 500 cr reward, Shaelas refused it: "a Jedi has no need for such rewards". Source: W:Shaelas (KOTOR, 2003).
- [in-universe] c.44 BBY, Coruscant: the Jedi Temple treasury held a shipment of crystalline vertex. Source: W:Crystalline_vertex/Legends (*Jedi Apprentice: The Captive Temple*).

[conflict] Bounty scale, in-universe vs game stat:
- In-universe Imperial-era bounties on prominent people are 25,000–1,000,000 cr: Cherioer 25,000; Zardra 50,000; Evazan 1 million; Solo 300,000.
- The WEG game lists put typical NR and Imperial bounties at 8,000–90,000 cr, with "hundred club" targets as exceptional.
- The two scales broadly overlap. Listed as given, not reconciled.

[conflict] Pay of a private soldier or guard, in-universe vs game stat:
- In-universe: Reggi's whole guard force at 50,000 cr/month (19 ABY).
- Game stat: a single pilot at 2,000 cr/month (WotC 2000).
- These describe different things, not a direct clash, but they are the only two monthly anchors.
