# Playable Species of the Old Republic Era (c. 3681–3600 BBY), Part 2 of 3: further species and non-playable groups

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 21, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

Part 2 continues the species entries: 25 further species with named era individuals or a SWTOR presence, then three non-playable or variant groups (Cyborg, Zakuulan, Rakghoul). ## Entries (continued from Part 1)

### Gand
- **Type:** species (insectoid; two races, with and without lungs). **playable-ready**
- **Era:** whole era
- **Key facts:**
  1. **Body:**
     - An exoskeleton with regenerative properties [Gand/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gand/Legends). Their natural armour lets them shrug off crippling injuries [Gand/Legends §Biology and appearance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gand/Legends).
     - Lunged Gands need breathing gear off Gand, like Kel Dors. Lungless Gands do not breathe and are immune to poison gas [Gand/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gand/Legends).
     - Height 1.5 m (females) and 1.8 m (males); mass 70 kg and 80 kg (game stat: Scum and Villainy; infobox) [Gand/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gand/Legends).
  2. **Lifespan and ageing:** 61 years, with some living past 95 (game stat: Scum and Villainy; infobox) [Gand/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gand/Legends). **Era example:** Yuun, b. 3670 BBY [note: the character file] [conflict: 3668 BBY on the Gand planet page] [Yuun (Gand)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yuun_%28Gand%29)[Gand (planet)/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gand_%28planet%29/Legends).
  3. **Strengths and weaknesses:** durability. Weakness: lunged Gands depend on breathing gear.
  4. **Force:**
     - The findsmen are a Force-sensitive religious tradition of bounty hunters unique to the Gand. Some can communicate telepathically with any species within 60 m [Gand/Legends §Biology and appearance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gand/Legends).
     - [note: for more, see SW_Force_Traditions_AllEras.]
  5. **Culture and homeworld:**
     - Gand. Its society had an active slave trade and penal system, and findsmen tracked runaways [Gand/Legends §Society and culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gand/Legends).
     - Control in 3661 BBY: not stated.
  6. **Languages:** Gand, which sounds to outsiders like drones and clicks [Gand/Legends §Biology and appearance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gand/Legends).
  7. **Era notes:**
     - In the Cold War, Aulus G'luun led Gand scavengers on Coruscant who felt victimised by discriminatory laws. Gand enforcers served in Kaas City [Gand/Legends §Gands in the galaxy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gand/Legends).
     - Yuun, a Force-sensitive findsman, served in Havoc Squad [Yuun (Gand)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yuun_%28Gand%29).
- **Related:** Kel Dor (breathing gear).
- **Uncertainty:** [conflict] Yuun's birth year (Part 3).

### Kaleesh
- **Type:** species (reptilian). **playable-ready**
- **Era:** whole era
- **Key facts:**
  1. **Body:**
     - Tall and reptilian, with scales, tusks and long nostrils, usually hidden behind traditional masks [Kaleesh/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kaleesh/Legends)[Kaleesh/Legends §Biology and appearance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kaleesh/Legends).
     - Thermal pits under the eyes let them see in the dark. Five claws on each foot [Kaleesh/Legends §Biology and appearance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kaleesh/Legends).
     - Height 1.6–1.8 m; mass 80 kg and 75 kg (game stat: Clone Wars Campaign Guide; infobox) [Kaleesh/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kaleesh/Legends).
  2. **Lifespan and ageing:** up to 80 years (game stat: Clone Wars Campaign Guide; infobox) [Kaleesh/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kaleesh/Legends). **Era example:** Xalek (Xivhkalrainik), b. 3665 BBY [note: the character file] [conflict: 3664 BBY on the Kalee page] [Xivhkalrainik](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Xivhkalrainik)[Kalee/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kalee/Legends). He was a Sith apprentice by the Galactic War.
  3. **Strengths and weaknesses:** recognised by both sides as proud, honourable and deadly warriors [Kaleesh/Legends §The Old Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kaleesh/Legends).
  4. **Force:** no Kaleesh Jedi are known. Kaleesh tradition treats Force-sensitivity as a blessing from the ancestors [Kaleesh/Legends §Society and culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kaleesh/Legends).
  5. **Culture and homeworld:**
     - Kalee, in Wild Space. A spiritual people: those who do great deeds become gods, and burial places are sacred [Kaleesh/Legends §Society and culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kaleesh/Legends).
     - **In 3661 BBY:** control not stated. Kaleesh had only "recently arrived" in the wider galaxy by the Galactic War [Kalee/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kalee/Legends).
  6. **Languages:** Kaleesh (infobox) [Kaleesh/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kaleesh/Legends).
  7. **Era notes:**
     - Several Kaleesh were Imperial slaves, trained when they showed Force abilities. Razer became a Sith Lord despite segregation [Kaleesh/Legends §The Old Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kaleesh/Legends).
     - Most opposed the xenophobic Empire, but Kaleesh fought on both sides, for Malgus's New Empire, and as Hutt Cartel bodyguards [Kalee/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kalee/Legends).
     - Kaleesh were favoured in Imperial field hospitals (Imperial Military file).
- **Related:** Korriban Academy file (Xalek).
- **Uncertainty:** [conflict] Xalek's birth year.

### Nikto
- **Type:** species (reptilian; several subspecies). **playable-ready** (no lifespan)
- **Era:** whole era
- **Key facts:**
  1. **Body:**
     - Leathery skin, sometimes with spikes and horns. The average adult is 1.8 m [Nikto/Legends §Biology and appearance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nikto/Legends); 1.6–1.9 m and 60 kg (infobox) [Nikto/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nikto/Legends).
     - Five subspecies known to the galaxy, plus the water-breathing Numol'elrul, each shaped by radiation-driven mutation to its own environment [Nikto/Legends §Biology and appearance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nikto/Legends).
  2. **Lifespan and ageing:** not stated. No source found.
  3. **Strengths and weaknesses:** subspecies adaptations [Nikto/Legends §Biology and appearance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nikto/Legends).
  4. **Force:** no rate stated.
  5. **Culture and homeworld:**
     - Kintan, in Hutt Space [Nikto/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nikto/Legends).
     - The Hutts used the Nikto to hold Hutt Space and build their criminal empire [Nikto/Legends §Allegiance to the Hutts](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nikto/Legends).
     - **In 3661 BBY:** Hutt Space, under Hutt control [inferred].
  6. **Languages:** Nikto (infobox) [Nikto/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nikto/Legends).
  7. **Era notes:**
     - In the Cold War the Kintan Kings, a Nikto group, worked against the Republic. Nikto settlers on Taris were targeted by the Empire's Mandalorian allies.
     - Miners on Kintan found cave paintings by pre-civilised Nikto [Nikto/Legends §Days of the Old Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nikto/Legends).
- **Related:** Hutt.
- **Uncertainty:** none.

### Weequay
- **Type:** species. **playable-ready**
- **Era:** whole era
- **Key facts:**
  1. **Body:** thick, leathery tan-to-brown hide that resists blunt trauma, burns, blaster fire and cuts [Weequay/Legends §Biology and appearance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Weequay/Legends). Height 1.6–1.9 m (infobox) [Weequay/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Weequay/Legends).
  2. **Lifespan and ageing:** up to 90 years (game stat: Legacy Era Campaign Guide; infobox) [Weequay/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Weequay/Legends). **Era example:** Tanno Vik, b. 3676 BBY on Nar Shaddaa, so 15 in 3661 BBY [inferred] [Tanno Vik](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tanno_Vik).
  3. **Strengths and weaknesses:** a tough hide. Clan pheromone communication that no other species, or other clan, understands [Weequay/Legends §Society and culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Weequay/Legends).
  4. **Force:** many Weequays were Force-sensitive and joined the Jedi Order [Weequay/Legends §Weequays in the galaxy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Weequay/Legends).
  5. **Culture and homeworld:**
     - Sriluur, untouched by the major wars until the Galactic Civil War, though the Dread Masters targeted it during the Galactic War [Sriluur/Legends §Early history](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sriluur/Legends).
     - **In 3661 BBY:** not stated.
  6. **Languages:** Sriluurian (infobox) [Weequay/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Weequay/Legends). Their pheromone language cannot be learned by others [Weequay/Legends §Society and culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Weequay/Legends).
  7. **Era notes:** Tanno Vik, a Nar Shaddaa orphan, became a Havoc Squad demolitions expert [Tanno Vik](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tanno_Vik).
- **Related:** Houk (Sriluur colony).
- **Uncertainty:** none.

This sits badly with Cold War Talz soldiers and a Senator (Part 3).

### Epicanthix
- **Type:** species (near-Human). **playable-ready**
- **Era:** whole era
- **Key facts:**
  1. **Body:** height 1.8–2.5 m (infobox; height cites the WEG Shadows of the Empire Sourcebook) [Epicanthix](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Epicanthix). Narrow eyes and nearly universal black hair (infobox) [Epicanthix](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Epicanthix).
  2. **Lifespan and ageing:** up to 130 years (infobox, citing the novel The Old Republic: Fatal Alliance, a prose source) [Epicanthix](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Epicanthix).
  3. **Strengths and weaknesses:** some have an unconscious talent that shields their minds from Force mind tricks, influence, domination and mind reading. It was strong enough to defeat Darth Chratis, similar to the shielding of Hutts and Toydarians [Epicanthix §Characteristics](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Epicanthix).
  4. **Force:** the shielding above. No rate stated.
  5. **Culture and homeworld:**
     - Panatha, in the Pacanth Reach. A warrior culture of mountain clan-fortresses [Epicanthix](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Epicanthix)[Epicanthix §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Epicanthix).
     - Their life is both practical and aesthetic; hair is tied in ceremonial styles [Epicanthix §Culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Epicanthix).
     - Control in 3661 BBY: not stated. [note: their Palpatine-era "first contact" is post-era] [Epicanthix §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Epicanthix).
  6. **Languages:** Epicant (infobox) [Epicanthix](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Epicanthix).
  7. **Era notes:** the Imperial Minister of Intelligence sought Epicanthix as spies for this talent, for example Ula Vii [Epicanthix §Characteristics](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Epicanthix). Ula Vii posed as Stantorrs's aide c. 3643 BBY [Ula Vii](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ula_Vii).
- **Related:** Hutt (similar shielding).
- **Uncertainty:** [conflict, mild] Imperial Intelligence used Epicanthix spies in the Cold War, yet the History section dates "first contact" with the Galactic Empire c. 19 BBY [Epicanthix §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Epicanthix).

- **Uncertainty:** [conflict] lifespan (Part 3).

### Hutt
- **Type:** species (gastropod; hermaphroditic). **playable-ready** (as NPCs)
- **Era:** whole era
- **Key facts:**
  1. **Body:**
     - The skin resists all but the most corrosive chemicals. Resistant to many poisons and diseases; they rarely fall ill [Hutt/Legends §Physiology](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hutt/Legends).
     - They see ultraviolet light. Strong body odour [Hutt/Legends §Physiology](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hutt/Legends).
     - Mass 1,500 kg (game stat: UAA; infobox) [Hutt/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hutt/Legends).
  2. **Lifespan and ageing:**
     - A young Hutt can leave the brood pouch early: Jabba removed Rotta at most ten years old [Hutt/Legends §Life cycle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hutt/Legends).
     - Adult by 200 years, at about 500 kg and Human-sized [Hutt/Legends §Life cycle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hutt/Legends) [note: Databank].
     - They can live over a thousand years [Hutt/Legends §Life cycle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hutt/Legends) [note: novel Fate of the Jedi: Allies; 1,000 years in the infobox] [Hutt/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hutt/Legends).
     - **Era examples:**
       - Karagga was over 800 years old and had been Supreme Mogul for nearly two centuries by the Cold War [Karagga §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Karagga).
       - Suudaa Nem'ro lived c. 4241–c. 3641 BBY, so about 600 at death [inferred] (character file) [Suudaa Nem'ro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Suudaa_Nem%27ro).
  3. **Strengths and weaknesses:** toughness and resistances as above. Underweight Hutts are scorned, and those with wasting diseases are treated as slaves [Hutt/Legends §Lifestyle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hutt/Legends).
  4. **Force:** rare Hutts are Force-sensitive [Hutt/Legends §Physiology](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hutt/Legends). Hutt minds resist Force mind tricks (compared in [Epicanthix §Characteristics](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Epicanthix)).
  5. **Culture and homeworld:**
     - Said to come from Varl. Their adopted homeworld is Nal Hutta [Hutt/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hutt/Legends).
     - Clan-based crime lords who leave labour to thugs and slaves [Hutt/Legends §Lifestyle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hutt/Legends). Slavery is an accepted status symbol [Hutt/Legends §Slavery and indentured services](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hutt/Legends).
     - **Names:** personal, clan and family names, as in Zorba Desilijic Tiure [Hutt/Legends §Life cycle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hutt/Legends).
     - **In 3661 BBY:** Nal Hutta was neutral and untouched [Nal Hutta/Legends §Old Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nal_Hutta/Legends).
  6. **Languages:** Huttese (infobox) [Hutt/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hutt/Legends).
  7. **Era notes:**
     - The Hutt Cartel stayed out of the Great Galactic War while the underworld debated sides; its dealings were later uncovered by the Empire.
     - Neutral in the Cold War and courted by both sides. Nem'ro was tricked by Imperial Intelligence into seeking an Imperial alliance [Hutt/Legends §The Old Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hutt/Legends).
     - In the Galactic War, Hutt Ajuur-class cruisers blockaded Makeb [Hutt/Legends §Technology](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hutt/Legends).
     - Tassaa Bareesh held the 3643 BBY auction [Tassaa Bareesh](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tassaa_Bareesh).
- **Related:** Twi'lek (Ryloth); Nikto.
- **Uncertainty:** none on the numbers (all prose-sourced except the mass).

- **Uncertainty:** none.

### Voss
- **Type:** species. **thin** (no height or lifespan; culture well sourced)
- **Era:** known to the galaxy only from c. 3645 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  1. **Body:** humanoid with strong sexual dimorphism. Males have blue skin and orange eyes; females have red skin and azure eyes [Voss (species) §Biology and appearance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Voss_%28species%29). Distinct skin-marking patterns (infobox) [Voss (species)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Voss_%28species%29).
  2. **Lifespan and ageing:** not stated. No source found.
  3. **Strengths and weaknesses:** a high rate of Force-sensitivity [Voss (species) §Biology and appearance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Voss_%28species%29). Reserved, speaking little except at funerals and in hardship [Voss (species) §Society and culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Voss_%28species%29).
  4. **Force:**
     - Society is guided by the visions of the Voss Mystics, held to be infallible. The Voss have no formal knowledge of the Force and no interest in learning from Jedi or Sith [Voss (species) §Voss Mystics](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Voss_%28species%29).
     - [note: for more, see SW_Force_Traditions_AllEras.]
  5. **Culture and homeworld:**
