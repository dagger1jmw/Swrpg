# The Mandalorians in the Great Galactic War (c. 3681–3653 BBY)

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 21, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends), as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
This file covers the Mandalorians of the Great Galactic War (3681–3653 BBY) as a culture a player character can belong to, fight beside or fight against. It focuses on the Hydian Way blockade (3661–3660 BBY).

The sources tell one main story:
- For centuries after Mandalore the Preserver the clans had no Mand'alor and lived scattered as mercenaries and nomads.
- Most rejected Imperial offers of work in 3667 BBY.
- Imperial Intelligence then rigged a Geonosis gladiator's rise to Mand'alor ("Mandalore the Lesser"). He led the clans into a year-long blockade that smugglers and the Republic broke.
- The Lesser called a Great Hunt to win back support and was killed by its champion, Artus Lok (Mandalore the Vindicated). Artus crushed a pro-Republic schism and kept the clans as well-paid, wary allies of the Sith.

Much of the culture material (Resol'nare, Mando'a, daily life) comes from much later eras. It is flagged with that era and not dated to the window.

### A. Leadership: the Mand'alor and the Mandalores of the window
- **Type:** office / characters
- **Era:** c. 3960–3653 BBY (title in general: all eras)
- **Key facts:**
  - **The office (general description, not era-specific):**
    - "Mand'alor" means "sole ruler". It combined king, warlord and general and was not hereditary [Mandalore (title)/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalore_%28title%29/Legends).
    - A worthy Mandalorian could claim it when the last one fell or stepped down. If the clans rejected a claimant, their duty was to remove him [Mandalore (title)/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalore_%28title%29/Legends).
    - In peacetime the Mand'alor worked with the clan chieftains, who formed a loose government [Mandalore (title)/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalore_%28title%29/Legends).
    - Outsiders addressed the holder as "Lord Mandalore" [Mandalore (title)/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalore_%28title%29/Legends).
  - **Predecessor: Mandalore the Preserver (Canderous Ordo):**
    - Between 3954 and 3951 BBY he took the title from a dying Taung claimant and rebuilt Clan Ordo and the clans from Dxun [Mandalore (title)/Legends §First Unidentified Mandalore](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalore_%28title%29/Legends); [Clan Ordo §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clan_Ordo).
    - He promised to stand with the Republic if the Sith returned [Canderous Ordo §Mandalore the Preserver](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Canderous_Ordo).
    - He died in battle; the date is not given. His skull was in Imperial hands by 3642 BBY [Canderous Ordo §Death and Legacy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Canderous_Ordo).
    - Despite his efforts the clans "remained fragmented for nearly three centuries" [Mandalorian/Legends §The return of the Sith](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalorian/Legends).
  - **The gap:** no Mand'alor is named between the Preserver and the Lesser. The length of the gap is a [conflict]: "many decades" [Blockade of the Hydian Way §The Mandalorians enter the war](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way) vs "centuries" [Mandalore (title)/Legends §Mandalore the Lesser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalore_%28title%29/Legends) (Decision 2).
  - **Mandalore the Lesser:**
    - A young Human gladiator in the arenas of Geonosis whose name is not given [Mandalore the Lesser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalore_the_Lesser).
    - **Rise:** Imperial Intelligence bought off, drugged or entered bouts against his opponents. Its agents seeded rumours in "kennels and cantinas" until crowds chanted for him [Blockade of the Hydian Way §The Mandalorians enter the war](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way); [Geonosis/Legends §Great Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Geonosis/Legends).
    - **Title:** he took it in 3667 BBY [Mandalore the Lesser §Ascension](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalore_the_Lesser) ("around 3667 BBY" [Mandalore (title)/Legends §Mandalore the Lesser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalore_%28title%29/Legends)), or in 3665 BBY [Blockade of the Hydian Way §The Mandalorians enter the war](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way). See Decision 1.
    - **Rally:** he called the clans to Geonosis and promised "great glory", while serving the Sith [Mandalore the Lesser §Ascension](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalore_the_Lesser).
    - **Fall:** after the blockade's defeat the clans' support "wavered". He revived the Great Hunt, and its champion Artus shot him dead in a duel [Mandalore (title)/Legends §Mandalore the Lesser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalore_%28title%29/Legends); [Mandalore the Lesser §The Great Hunt](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalore_the_Lesser).
    - **The epithet:** "the Lesser" came after it was revealed that he had been a puppet [Mandalore the Lesser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalore_the_Lesser).
  - **Mandalore the Vindicated (Artus Lok, Clan Lok):**
    - Began his rise under the Lesser [Mandalore (title)/Legends §Mandalore the Vindicated](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalore_%28title%29/Legends).
    - Won the Hunt and killed the Lesser in the arenas of Geonosis [Mandalore the Vindicated §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalore_the_Vindicated).
    - Consolidated power and crushed Jicoln Cadera's uprising [Mandalore the Vindicated §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalore_the_Vindicated).
    - Kept the clans "well-paid, yet cautious, allies" of the Sith. Under him they "prospered" [Mandalore the Vindicated §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalore_the_Vindicated).
    - The date he took the title is a [conflict] (Decision 4).
  - **Clan structure (general description, not era-specific):**
    - Clans were led by chieftains, usually senior members chosen for wisdom, all subordinate to the Mand'alor [Mandalorian/Legends §Society](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalorian/Legends).
    - The society was a meritocracy with no official state [Mandalorian/Legends §Society](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalorian/Legends).
- **Related:** entry B (Empire); entry C (blockade); GGW file Decisions 5–6.

### C. The Mandalorians and the Sith Empire
- **Type:** alliance / politics
- **Era:** 3681–3653 BBY; aftermath **post-window**
- **Key facts:**
  - **Why recruit them:**
    - After Bothawui (3671 BBY) and Alderaan (3667 BBY), Imperial planners dropped the Emperor's go-it-alone approach in 3667 BBY. The Imperial Diplomatic Service courted crime lords, bounty hunters and "individual mercenaries" of the disbanded Mandalorian tradition [Blockade of the Hydian Way §The Sith in reflection](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way); [Blockade of the Hydian Way §The Mandalorians enter the war](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way).
    - Many Mandalorians still resented the Republic and the Jedi for their "brutal treatment" at the end of the Mandalorian Wars, which made them "ideal tools" [Blockade of the Hydian Way §The Mandalorians enter the war](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way).
  - **Who said yes first:**
    - "Several Mandalorians", including Shae Vizla, served alongside Imperial forces [Blockade of the Hydian Way §The Mandalorians enter the war](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way). Only "a small number" joined [Mandalore the Lesser §Ascension](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalore_the_Lesser).
    - "The vast majority" refused, choosing to "remain independent nomads" [Blockade of the Hydian Way §The Mandalorians enter the war](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way).
  - **Why they then followed:**
    - Mandalorians "flocked" to the new Mandalore to fight the Jedi and the Republic, their "worthy long-time adversaries" [Blockade of the Hydian Way §The Mandalorians enter the war](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way).
    - He urged them toward "the greatest challenge of all—fighting the legendary Jedi Knights" [Great Galactic War §The Mandalorian blockade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Galactic_War).
  - **Terms:** no formal treaty or pay terms are recorded for the Lesser. He was "subservient to the Empire that built him" and blockaded the Hydian Way "as an act of loyalty" [Mandalore the Lesser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalore_the_Lesser). Under the Vindicated they were "well-paid" [Mandalore the Vindicated §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalore_the_Vindicated).
  - **How the Sith treated them:**
    - As a "pawn" controlled for the Emperor's plans [Imperial Intelligence (Sith Empire) §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Intelligence_%28Sith_Empire%29).
    - The Lesser was a "puppet" [Blockade of the Hydian Way](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way).
    - Jedi Master Gnost-Dural later investigated whether the Vindicated was also one [Mandalore (title)/Legends §Mandalore the Vindicated](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalore_%28title%29/Legends).
    - Rumours held that the Vindicated "secretly harbored greater ambitions" than being the Sith's ally [Mandalore the Vindicated §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalore_the_Vindicated).
  - **Dissent: Crusader's schism.** Clans Ordo, Itera, Kelborn and Cadera followed Jicoln Cadera in calling for support of the Republic, as the Preserver had done [Crusader's schism](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Crusader%27s_schism).
    - The uprising was crushed. The Mandalore seized the survivors' armour and adopted their children into other clans [Crusader's schism](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Crusader%27s_schism).
    - Cadera fled into exile on Taris, where he hid for "nearly a decade". A bounty on him went onto the Blacklist [Jicoln Cadera](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jicoln_Cadera).
    - The date and the opposing Mandalore are a [conflict]: see Decision 5.
  - **Mandalorian opinion of Hutts:** the word for coward, hut'uun, comes from Mandalorian distaste for Hutts [Mandalorian/Legends §Language](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalorian/Legends). This is general, not dated.
- **Related:** entry A; GGW Part2 "Mandalorian involvement".
- **Uncertainty:** [conflict] schism date; [unclear] no terms recorded.

### D. The Hydian Way blockade, 3661–3660 BBY
- **Type:** campaign
- **Era:** c. 3665–3660 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Build-up: "three years of minor battles":**
    - After formally taking the title the gladiator summoned the clans to Geonosis. "One year later" the army left Geonosis and fought "engagements and skirmishes" with the Jedi and the Republic Military for three years [Blockade of the Hydian Way §The Mandalorians enter the war](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way).
    - Counted from the page's 3665 BBY, that gives roughly 3664–3661 BBY [inferred].
    - None of these skirmishes is named except the attack on a Republic envoy (c. 3664 BBY, entry G) [Mandalorian attack on a Republic envoy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalorian_attack_on_a_Republic_envoy).
  - **Commander:** the Mandalore himself, "at the behest of the Imperials" [Blockade of the Hydian Way §A crippled Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way).
  - **Force:**
    - "A small armada of war cruisers and support ships" with "shock troops" [Blockade of the Hydian Way §A crippled Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way).
    - Republic officials were "uncertain of the exact size and strength". The Navy would normally have cleared "the relatively small blockade" quickly, but it was still reeling from Hoth [Blockade of the Hydian Way §A crippled Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way).
    - No ship or clan count is given (see Not found).
  - **Ships named:**
    - Mandalorian cruisers, a "warship design used by the Mandalorian warrior clans" in this war [Mandalorian cruiser §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalorian_cruiser).
    - The warship *Beskad*, lost during the blockade in 3661 BBY and thought swallowed by a black hole. It actually crashed elsewhere [Beskad (starship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Beskad_%28starship%29); [Blockade of the Hydian Way §A crippled Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way).
  - **Where:** the southern Hydian Way, between Devaron (Colonies) and the Minos Cluster [Blockade of the Hydian Way §A crippled Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way).
  - **Day to day:**
    - Cruisers held a picket line. When ships approached, they hailed them, demanded they turn back, and moved to intercept if there was no answer [Blockade of the Hydian Way §Smugglers take the lead](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way); [Great Galactic War §The Mandalorian blockade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Galactic_War).
    - Nothing more on routine (rotations, boarding, supply) is recorded.
  - **The Jedi strike (3661 BBY):**
    - Jedi pilots in Aurek-class strikefighters under Masters including Gnost-Dural were "handily defeated". Survivors regrouped at Devaron [Blockade of the Hydian Way §A crippled Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way).
    - The strike force was "wiped out" according to [Battle near Devaron](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_near_Devaron). See the Jedi Temple file for the Council's side.
    - The Jedi suspected the blockade was bait to "goad the Jedi into a fight" [Blockade of the Hydian Way §A crippled Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way).
  - **Collapse (3660 BBY):**
    - Visz's unarmed freighters halted within a parsec of the pickets. When the cruisers moved on them, "hundreds of small vessels and transports" dropped out of hyperspace [Blockade of the Hydian Way §Smugglers take the lead](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way).
    - The smugglers' speed was not enough against the cruisers' guns until SIS-dispatched Navy and Jedi starfighters arrived, among them the Valor-class *Fortitude*. The surviving Mandalorians fled [Blockade of the Hydian Way §Smugglers take the lead](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way).
    - The battle's date is a [conflict] (Decision 3).
  - **After:**
    - The defeated Mandalorians "scattered once more". Some went solo; others stayed with their Mandalore. The alliance with the Sith lasted "for decades" [Blockade of the Hydian Way §Aftermath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way).
    - The defeat was "humiliating" [Great Hunt (c. 3660 BBY)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Hunt_%28c._3660_BBY%29). It prompted the Lesser's Great Hunt (entry A).
    - Seven years later the Dark Council offered peace [Great Galactic War §Sacking of Coruscant](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Galactic_War).
- **Related:** GGW Part2 battle table; Coruscant City file (home front); Jedi Temple file (Devaron).
- **Uncertainty:** [inferred] skirmish years; [conflict] battle year; [unclear] numbers.

### E. Culture: what is dated, and what comes from other eras
- **Type:** culture
- **Era:** as tagged per point
- **Key facts:**
  - **In this era's sources (SWTOR, c. 3667–3640 BBY):**
    - **Honour duels:** the Geroya be Haran ("game of annihilation") was a duel to the death in which the loser's memory was erased from Mandalorian history. It was the gravest challenge "during the period of time surrounding the Cold War" and was rarely used. It was believed to recall a Neo-Crusader victory [Geroya be Haran](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Geroya_be_Haran). Attested for c. 3642 BBY (**post-window**) [Clan Cadera §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clan_Cadera).
    - **Stigma and honour:** the Cadera clan carried the shame of a traitor, "arue'tal" [Clan Cadera §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clan_Cadera). Torian upheld "the Mandalorian code and traditions more than most other younger Mandalorians" [Torian Cadera §Personality and traits](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Torian_Cadera).
    - **Arena culture:** Geonosis gladiator games, many fought by Mandalorians, for prestige [Geonosis/Legends §Great Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Geonosis/Legends); [Mandalore the Lesser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalore_the_Lesser).
    - **The Great Hunt:** a galaxy-wide competition for glory [Mandalore the Lesser §The Great Hunt](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalore_the_Lesser). The Blacklist was "as old as the Great Hunt" and always included one Mandalorian among the Grand Champions. It is attested for the Cold War [Blacklist](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blacklist).
    - **Drink:** ne'tra gal, a black ale, is attested for the Vindicated's Cold War flagship [Spirit of Vengeance §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_of_Vengeance); [Mandalore the Vindicated §Personality and traits](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalore_the_Vindicated).
    - **Views of Jedi and Sith:** resentment of the Jedi and the Republic from the Mandalorian Wars; Jedi as "worthy" foes and the "greatest challenge" [Blockade of the Hydian Way §The Mandalorians enter the war](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way); [Great Galactic War §The Mandalorian blockade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Galactic_War). Vizla's personal hatred of Jedi dated from 3664 BBY [Mandalore the Avenger §Personality and traits](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalore_the_Avenger). Their view of the Sith in this era was "cautious" [Mandalore the Vindicated §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalore_the_Vindicated).
  - **Resol'nare, Mando'a and adoption** [other era: concept by Karen Traviss, Clone Wars-era sources]:
    - The Six Actions are armour, language, defending oneself and family, raising children as Mandalorians, contributing to the clan, and rallying to the Mand'alor [Resol'nare](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Resol%27nare).
    - Anyone not following them was dar'manda, without a soul and excluded from the manda [Resol'nare](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Resol%27nare).
    - The page dates only one point to the Old Republic: "before and during the Mandalorian Wars" all non-Mandalorians were dar'manda until they joined, and captives were inducted under threat of death. "Such zealotry faded over time" [Resol'nare](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Resol%27nare).
    - The adoption formula gai bal manda ("ni kyr'tayl gai sa'ad") is illustrated only with 22 BBY-era examples [Mandalorian/Legends §Family](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalorian/Legends).
    - Mando'a words with general dates: Mando'ade, aruetii, dar'jetii ("no longer a Jedi", meaning "Sith") and hut'uun [Mando'a/Legends §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mando%27a/Legends); [Mandalorian/Legends §Language](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalorian/Legends).
  - **Daily life** [other era: examples Clone Wars and later; not dated to the window]:
    - Nomadism and portable vheh'yaim dwellings; reciting the names of the dead each night; cremation, and keeping a fallen warrior's armour [Mandalorian/Legends §Daily life and death](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalorian/Legends).
    - Field foods: haarshun bread, gihaal, uj cake, tiingilar, tihaar, shig [Mandalorian/Legends §Cuisine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalorian/Legends).
    - The coming-of-age rite verd'goten at 13 [Mandalorian/Legends §Family](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalorian/Legends).
    - Fitting the window only [inferred].
  - **Species mix:** after the Taung declined (by the end of the Great Sith War), Mandalorians accepted other species as equals [Mandalorian/Legends §Daily life and death](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalorian/Legends).
  - **Worlds:**
    - **Geonosis:** the rallying point in the window [Blockade of the Hydian Way §The Mandalorians enter the war](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way). It is called the Mandalorian "stronghold" in the Cold War (**post-window**), and Torian was born there [Mandalorian/Legends §Other Mandalorian activity during the Cold War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalorian/Legends); [Torian Cadera §Early life](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Torian_Cadera).
    - **Mandalore:** the fallback point for defeated Neo-Crusaders after the Mandalorian Wars [Mandalore/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalore/Legends). Keldabe has been its capital "since at least 3960 BBY" [Mandalore/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalore/Legends). The planet's state in 3681–3653 BBY is not described (see Not found).
    - **Dxun:** the Preserver's headquarters c. 3951 BBY [Mandalore (title)/Legends §Mandalore the Preserver](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalore_%28title%29/Legends).
    - **Dromund Kaas:** a Mandalorian enclave in the Citadel, attested for the Cold War (**post-window**) [Mandalorian enclave](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalorian_enclave).
- **Related:** Lightsaber files; entry H.
