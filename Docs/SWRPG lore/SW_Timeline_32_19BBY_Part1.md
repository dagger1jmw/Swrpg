# SWRPG Galaxy Timeline, 32–19 BBY (Part 1 of 4): The Naboo Crisis, the Interwar Years and the Separatist Crisis

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 24, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends), current pages and pre-August-2008 revisions, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
This four-part file rebuilds the 32–19 BBY span of `SW_Legends_Galaxy_Timeline.md` (its "Rise of the Empire" and "CLONE WARS" tables) as one sourced row per event, from the Invasion of Naboo to the Declaration of a New Order and the first weeks of the Empire. Part 1 (this file) covers 32 BBY to the eve of Geonosis (22 BBY, 13:5:20 GrS). Part 2 covers the first year of the Clone Wars (22–21 BBY). Part 3 covers the second and third years (21–19 BBY) including the Outer Rim Sieges and Maul's return. Part 4 covers the war's end and the Empire's first acts in 19 BBY, then holds the conflicts table, "Changes from the previous file", Decisions needed and the full Sources list. Under rule 4a, pre-2008 Legends material (the 2003 *Clone Wars* micro-series, Dark Horse comics, novels, games, Republic Commando) is primary; facts from *The Clone Wars* (2008) and works based on it are tagged [S-canon], and the 2014 "Lost Missions" (season 6) material is excluded.

## Entries
How to read the tables: Date | Event | Type | Place | Who | Source. Type is one of War, Control, Leader, Death, Order, Tech, Background (plus Birth for a few major births, as in the format model). NNN (date)", with a link to that revision; such a revision is the primary source under the developer's instruction of 24 Nov 2026, and the current page is cited beside it.

## THE NABOO CRISIS — 32 BBY
## ════════════════════════════════════════════

### Overview
In 32 BBY the Trade Federation, protesting the taxation of Outer Rim trade routes and secretly directed by Darth Sidious, blockaded and invaded Naboo. The crisis made Chancellor Finis Valorum look powerless: Queen Amidala moved a Vote of No Confidence and the Senate elected Naboo's Senator Palpatine Supreme Chancellor; Sidious then murdered his own master, Darth Plagueis. The Battle of Naboo freed the planet, killed Qui-Gon Jinn and cut down Darth Maul, and revealed to the Jedi that the Sith had returned. In the same year Dooku left the Jedi Order and became Darth Tyranus, Sifo-Dyas ordered a clone army from Kamino and was murdered, and Jango Fett was chosen as the clones' template ([Invasion of Naboo/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Invasion_of_Naboo/Legends); [32 BBY/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/32_BBY/Legends); [Clone Wars/Legends §Tensions in the Rim](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clone_Wars/Legends)).

## THE INTERWAR YEARS — 31–25 BBY
## ════════════════════════════════════════════

### Overview
Palpatine's first term saw little of the reform he promised; the Trade Federation's demilitarization was widely seen as a slap on the wrist, and Gunray escaped serious charges ([Clone Wars/Legends, rev. 2099230 §Preparing the Way](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clone_Wars/Legends?oldid=2099230), citing *Labyrinth of Evil*). Behind the scenes the clone army grew, Kuat Drive Yards was secretly paid to equip it, and Raith Sienar drew up the battle station that became the Death Star. The Jedi fought small wars (Aurra Sing, Zonama Sekot, Outbound Flight, Radnor) and lost a Council member, Yaddle. Around the destruction of Outbound Flight (27 BBY), Republic forces fought unstable, rapidly grown clone warriors; some veterans, among them Gilad Pellaeon, later counted these fights as the real start of the Clone Wars (same source).

## THE SEPARATIST CRISIS — 24–22 BBY
## ════════════════════════════════════════════

### Overview
The Separatist Crisis (24–22 BBY) was a two-year galactic cold war that began eight years after Naboo, when Dooku reappeared on Raxus Prime and, in the Raxus Address, announced the Confederacy of Independent Systems ([Separatist Crisis/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Separatist_Crisis/Legends)). Several thousand systems seceded; billions of refugees were displaced; and the Senate debated a Military Creation Act to rebuild a central army. Palpatine's second term ended in 24 BBY, but the Emergency Powers Act let him stay until the crisis was over. The Confederacy's territory was scattered: a pocket in the New Territories around Muunilinst, the southern Rimma, a strip down the Perlemian from the Tion Cluster, and an enclave on the Corellian Run from Allanteen to Mon Gazza ([Clone Wars/Legends §The Formation of the Confederacy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clone_Wars/Legends), citing *The Essential Atlas*). The megacorporations' members on the Separatist Council came to override the elected Separatist Parliament (same section, citing *The Essential Guide to Warfare*). Dates 13:x GrS belong to the months before Geonosis (13:5:21).
