# Star Wars Legends: Coruscant and the Jedi Temple, c. 3700–3653 BBY, Part 2: Coruscant city, law, crime and people

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 21, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
- **The capital:** in 3681–3653 BBY Coruscant was the Republic capital. The Senate District was centred on the Senate Rotunda (c. 3996 BBY), with the Senate Plaza and the Coruscant Spaceport nearby. [Senate Building §Galactic Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Senate_Building/Legends); [Coruscant Spaceport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_Spaceport)
- **Below the upper city:** a bustling Galactic Market, the decaying Works and an undercity where wartime food shortages caused riots. [Old Galactic Market](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Old_Galactic_Market); [The Works](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/The_Works/Legends); [Coruscant Underworld §Galactic Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_Underworld/Legends)
- **Law and crime:** the tan-uniformed Coruscant Security Force ran orbital traffic control. The Migrant Merchants' Guild was already a quiet crime front. [Coruscant Security Force §Uniforms and equipment](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_Security_Force/Legends); [Migrant Merchants' Guild §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Migrant_Merchants'_Guild)
- **After the Sacking:** most of SWTOR's Coruscant (Black Sun, the Justicars, Guild rule of the Market) comes from the Sacking's aftermath and is post-window. [Black Sun §Formation](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Sun/Legends); [Sacking of Coruscant §Coruscant's long road back](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sacking_of_Coruscant)
- **Verified names:** Liston Spaceport was on Coruscant, but Yinta Lake spaceport was on Vulta. [Liston Spaceport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Liston_Spaceport); [Yinta Lake spaceport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yinta_Lake_spaceport)

### Coruscant and Galactic City: structure and levels
- **Type:** planet and ecumenopolis (Republic capital)
- **Era:** whole history; era-specific notes as marked
- **Key facts:**
  - **Location:** at hyperspace coordinates (0,0,0), where the Perlemian Trade Route, Corellian Run, Metellos Trade Route, Koros Trunk Line and Leisure Corridor meet. [Coruscant §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant/Legends)
  - **Names:** under the Republic, Galactic City was also called "Republic City" or the "City of Spires". In common use the name meant the Senate, Temple and Ambassadorial Districts. [Galactic City](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_City/Legends)
  - **Structure** [unclear for this era]:
    - Galactic City is divided into 5,127 levels, then megablocks, blocks and subblocks; also into quadrants, zones and numbered sectors.
    - The undercity never sees sunlight and is lit artificially.

    [Coruscant §Surface](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant/Legends); [Coruscant §Neighborhoods](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant/Legends)
  - **Population:** "over a hundred billion to several trillion, depending on the era". No figure is given for this era. [Coruscant §Surface](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant/Legends)
  - **In-system security:** orbital security stations, sponsored by Coruscant Security, monitored traffic and reported to Coruscant Control. [Orbital security station](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Orbital_security_station/Legends); [Coruscant Control](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_Control)
- **Related:** districts and the location table (below)
- **Uncertainty:** [unclear] no level counts or population specific to 3700–3653 BBY.

### Districts and areas
- **Type:** districts
- **Era:** as marked
- **Key facts:**
  - **Senate District** (Legislative/Government District):
    - The equatorial seat of government around the Senate Building.
    - It borders the Financial and Sah'c Districts and lies near The Works.

    [Senate District](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Senate_District/Legends)
  - **Senate Building:**
    - **History:** the Senate Rotunda was erected c. 3996 BBY over the old Senate Hall. It is drum- or mushroom-shaped, with a dome over two kilometres across, and was among Coruscant's largest tourist sites. [Senate Building §Galactic Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Senate_Building/Legends); [Senate Building §Exterior](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Senate_Building/Legends)
    - **Senate Tower:** SWTOR-era sources call it the "Senate Tower". [Senate Building §Exterior](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Senate_Building/Legends); [Bombing of the Senate Tower](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bombing_of_the_Senate_Tower)
    - **3653 BBY:** the Sith struck the Senate District, the security-command centre in the Temple was crippled, and Angral executed Berooken. [Senate Building §Galactic Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Senate_Building/Legends)
  - **Senate Plaza, Avenue of the Core Founders and Senate Commercial District:**
    - **Layout:** a stone plaza with landing pads, cut by the Avenue of the Core Founders. The avenue was lined with statues of the human Core Founders and flanked by the Gardens of Justice and Equality. The Senate Commercial District (Commerce Hall) lay beneath. [Senate Plaza §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Senate_Plaza/Legends); [Avenue of the Core Founders §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Avenue_of_the_Core_Founders/Legends); [Senate Commercial District](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Senate_Commercial_District)
    - **3653 BBY:** cleaned of debris and reopened after the Sacking. [Senate Plaza §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Senate_Plaza/Legends)
    - **3671 BBY:** Satele Shan heard cheering crowds from the Senate Plaza at the Bothawui news. [Satele Shan §Fighting the Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satele_Shan)
  - **Temple district (Temple Precinct/Court):**
    - Jointly overseen by the Jedi and the Republic, with ordinary Coruscanti living in the parts the Order did not use. [Temple district](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Temple_district/Legends); [Temple district §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Temple_district/Legends)
    - Razed by starfighters and war droids in 3653 BBY. [Sacking of Coruscant §Assault on the Jedi Temple](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sacking_of_Coruscant); [Sacking of Coruscant §Coruscant falls](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sacking_of_Coruscant)
  - **The Works** (Sector 943, "the Flatlands"):
    - A former industrial zone southwest of the Senate District, abandoned by manufacturers. [The Works](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/The_Works/Legends)
    - In 3653 BBY it gave access to the Temple's intact lower levels. [Sacking of Coruscant §Security breaches](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sacking_of_Coruscant)
    - **Post-window (Cold War):** overrun by Cthons and reprogrammed droids. [The Works §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/The_Works/Legends)
  - **Old Galactic Market:**
    - Called simply "the Galactic Market" in 3954 BBY, it was a "bustling commercial district" before the Great Galactic War. [Old Galactic Market](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Old_Galactic_Market)
    - **After 3653 BBY:** refugees and bombed-out Coruscanti occupied it, and the Migrant Merchants' Guild took most of it. [Sacking of Coruscant §Coruscant's long road back](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sacking_of_Coruscant); [Coruscant riots (3653 BBY) §Rioting](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_riots_(3653_BBY)); [Old Galactic Market](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Old_Galactic_Market)
  - **Lower levels / Underworld:**
    - "For millennia during the Old Republic era, the undercity stayed the same." During the Great Galactic War, food shortages caused riots there. [Coruscant Underworld §Galactic Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_Underworld/Legends)
    - **3653 BBY:** the lower levels were a refuge from the bombardment. Orgus Din and Republic soldiers defended civilians in the Underlevels, where the Sith left war droids. [Sacking of Coruscant §Coruscant falls](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sacking_of_Coruscant); [Sacking of Coruscant §Final day](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sacking_of_Coruscant)
  - **Gnawer's Roost:** a district where Black Sun became prominent after the Sacking (post-window). [Sacking of Coruscant §Coruscant's long road back](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sacking_of_Coruscant)
  - **Post-window SWTOR zones (c. 3643 BBY):**
    - Black Sun territory (Black Sun district), with the Silent Sun cantina. [Black Sun territory](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Sun_territory); [Silent Sun cantina](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Silent_Sun_cantina)
    - Justicar territory (sector 439), held by the Justicars' Brigade from the Justicars' Tower, a former CSF facility. [Justicar territory](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Justicar_territory); [Justicars' Tower](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Justicars'_Tower); [Justicars' Brigade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Justicars'_Brigade)
- **Related:** location table; crime and law enforcement (below)
- **Uncertainty:**
  - [conflict] The Senate Building page has the 3653 BBY attack "blackening the facade of the Republic Executive Building", but that page says the Executive Building was built much later, under Palpatine, over the Cold War spaceport's foundations. [Senate Building §Galactic Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Senate_Building/Legends); [Republic Executive Building §Layout](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Executive_Building/Legends)

### Spaceports, traffic and transport
- **Type:** infrastructure
- **Era:** as marked
- **Key facts:**
  - **Coruscant Spaceport:**
    - A huge spaceport near the Senate Building and the planet's main port "during the years surrounding the Great Galactic War and subsequent Cold War". The Coruscant Spaceport Authority ran it. [Coruscant Spaceport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_Spaceport); [Coruscant Spaceport Authority](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_Spaceport_Authority)
    - It includes Docking Bay 84 (post-window events). [Docking Bay 84](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Docking_Bay_84)
  - **Liston Spaceport:**
    - A civilian spaceport on Coruscant, in use "until after the Great Galactic War". [Liston Spaceport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Liston_Spaceport)
    - In 3653 BBY the Sith held it and loaded looted goods there. Leneer and Korr faked a fuel-gas leak to empty it. [Sacking of Coruscant §Final day](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sacking_of_Coruscant); [Skirmish in Liston Spaceport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Skirmish_in_Liston_Spaceport)
  - **Yinta Lake spaceport:** **not on Coruscant.** It was the main port of Yinta Lake on the Mid Rim world Vulta, where Korr and Leneer fought off Vrath Xizor in 3653 BBY. [Yinta Lake spaceport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yinta_Lake_spaceport); [Skirmish in the Yinta Lake spaceport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Skirmish_in_the_Yinta_Lake_spaceport)
  - **Coruscant Control and orbital security stations:**
    - Coruscant Control, run by the CSF, monitored space traffic in the Great Galactic War and Cold War eras.
    - In 3653 BBY Femi on Orbital Security Station Six reported all clear moments before Angral's fleet destroyed the station.

    [Coruscant Control](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_Control); [Orbital Security Station Six](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Orbital_Security_Station_Six); [Femi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Femi)
  - **Local transport:**
    - Civilian airspeeders were shot down during the Sacking, and Korr flew an Armin speeder. [Sacking of Coruscant §Coruscant falls](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sacking_of_Coruscant); [Sacking of Coruscant §Final day](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sacking_of_Coruscant)
    - **Post-window:** air-taxi stations (Old Galactic Market Taxi Station), the CSF Traffic Division keeping the skylanes, and an access elevator from the Justicars' Sector down to The Works. [Old Galactic Market Taxi Station](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Old_Galactic_Market_Taxi_Station); [Coruscant Security Force §Organization and units](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_Security_Force/Legends); [Duel in the Jedi Temple ruins §Prelude](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Duel_in_the_Jedi_Temple_ruins)
- **Related:** location table
- **Uncertainty:** [unclear] no named spaceports other than these two for the window.

### Law enforcement: the Coruscant Security Force
- **Type:** organization (planetary police)
- **Era:** whole Republic era; this window as marked
- **Key facts:**
  - **Role:**
    - "One of the oldest planetary police forces in the galaxy", working with the Senate Guard and Jedi Peacekeepers. [Coruscant §Law enforcement](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant/Legends)
    - In the Old Republic era it shared Senate Building security with the military's Senate Security Officers. [Coruscant Security Force §Senate Guard](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_Security_Force/Legends)
  - **Uniform during the Great Galactic War:** an off-white or tan shirt, tan trousers, dark knee boots, a utility belt, a grey blast vest, and a grey blast helmet or headset comlink. It was later changed to blue-grey with yellow stripes (SWTOR Coruscant). [Coruscant Security Force §Uniforms and equipment](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_Security_Force/Legends)
  - **Units:**
    - Coruscant Control (space traffic) and orbital stations in the Great Galactic War. [Coruscant Security Force §Droids and speeders](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_Security_Force/Legends); [Coruscant Control](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_Control)
    - Customs Enforcement, the Traffic Division, and a Chief of Security holding a police rank ("Old Republic era"; SWTOR sources). [Coruscant Security Force §Organization and units](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_Security_Force/Legends)
  - **3653 BBY:**
    - The CSF made first contact with the Sith (Orbital Security Station Six).
    - CSF officers fought at the Senate Building with Lieutenant Harron Tavus and Orgus Din.
    - It was afterwards drained in lives and resources.

    [Coruscant Security Force §Beginnings and Sith invasion](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_Security_Force/Legends); [Coruscant Security Force §Picking up the pieces](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_Security_Force/Legends)
  - **Named officers:**
    - **Femi:** a Twi'lek on Orbital Security Station Six; killed in 3653 BBY. [Femi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Femi)
    - **Doorn:** "achieved the rank of lieutenant before the Great Galactic War"; posted to Black Sun territory after it. [Coruscant Security Force §Doorn](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_Security_Force/Legends)
    - **Theraguin:** a veteran officer and Cold War captain. [Coruscant Security Force §Theraguin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_Security_Force/Legends)
  - **Post-window:** Chief Captain Winborn and the Special Tactical Unit (CSF and soldiers) fought Black Sun and the Guild c. 3643 BBY. [Winborn](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Winborn); [Special tactical unit](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Special_tactical_unit); [Merchants' Guild Crisis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Merchants'_Guild_Crisis)
- **Related:** crime (below)
- **Uncertainty:** [unclear] whether the Underworld Police (a Clone Wars-era unit) existed this early. [Coruscant Underworld Police](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_Underworld_Police)

### Crime and gangs
- **Type:** organizations
- **Era:** as marked
- **Key facts:**
  - **Migrant Merchants' Guild:**
    - "Even before the war's end" it was a front for money laundering and smuggling, mostly non-violent, with a largely nonhuman membership. [Migrant Merchants' Guild §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Migrant_Merchants'_Guild)
    - After the 3653 BBY riots it took the Old Galactic Market and provided food "at a price". [Merchants' Guild Crisis §Prelude](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Merchants'_Guild_Crisis); [Coruscant riots (3653 BBY)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_riots_(3653_BBY))
  - **The Exchange:** in 3653 BBY it had ex-Havoc commando Zeerid Korr run weapons, then spice. Korr's engspice cargo was bound for Coruscant. [Zeerid Korr §Mission for the Exchange](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zeerid_Korr); [Skirmish in the Yinta Lake spaceport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Skirmish_in_the_Yinta_Lake_spaceport)
  - **Black Sun:**
    - **Not in this window as a syndicate.** It was formed after the Sacking, when gangsters seized lower-level neighbourhoods ("better a black sun than none"). [Black Sun §Formation](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Sun/Legends)
    - It ran spice and weapons, and the Justicars' Brigade halted its advance. [Black Sun §Formation](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Sun/Legends); [Justicars' Brigade §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Justicars'_Brigade)
  - **Riots:** 3680, 3661 and 3653 BBY (see SW_Great_Galactic_War files). [Coruscant riots (3680 BBY)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_riots_(3680_BBY)); [Coruscant riots (3661 BBY)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_riots_(3661_BBY)); [Coruscant riots (3653 BBY)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_riots_(3653_BBY))
  - **Old Galactic Market cantina:** the Dealer's Den hosted Revan two years after the Jedi Civil War (c. 3954 BBY). In the Galactic War it held the Shroud's base (post-window). [Dealer's Den](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dealer's_Den)
- **Related:** CSF (above)
- **Uncertainty:** [unclear] no named gang leaders on Coruscant before 3653 BBY.

### Living conditions by level, 3681–3653 BBY
- **Type:** society
- **Era:** 3681–3653 BBY, then post-window as marked
- **Key facts:**
  - **Upper levels / Senate District:**
    - Senators, aides, lobbyists, petitioners and Jedi frequented the Plaza. [Senate Plaza §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Senate_Plaza/Legends)
    - Crowds gathered there in wartime (the cheering for Bothawui in 3671 BBY). [Satele Shan §Fighting the Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satele_Shan)
  - **Lower levels:**
    - Food shortages caused riots during the war. [Coruscant Underworld §Galactic Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_Underworld/Legends)
    - In the 3661 BBY blockade, starvation spread through Galactic City's lower levels. [Coruscant riots (3661 BBY)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_riots_(3661_BBY))
  - **The Sacking:**
    - The Sith looted the city, executed senators and kidnapped locals.
    - Civilians fled downward. A hospital was taken over and then overwhelmed with wounded.
    - Leneer sheltered in a bombed-out apartment block.

    [Sacking of Coruscant](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sacking_of_Coruscant); [Sacking of Coruscant §Coruscant falls](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sacking_of_Coruscant); [Sacking of Coruscant §Martial law](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sacking_of_Coruscant); [Sacking of Coruscant §Final day](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sacking_of_Coruscant)
  - **After 3653 BBY:**
    - Food riots, separatist uprisings and looting hit the lower levels.
    - The Resource Management Council set priorities, and poorer areas were left to rebuild alone.
    - Gangs took over.

    [Sacking of Coruscant §Coruscant's long road back](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sacking_of_Coruscant)
- **Related:** Game notes
- **Uncertainty:** none recorded

## Decisions needed
1. **Temple founding date:** c. 5000 BBY (land grant and enclave) vs c. 4000 BBY (Four Masters' Temple after Ossus). Best fit: both; the enclave c. 5000 BBY, the Temple proper from c. 3996 BBY.
2. **Era layout:** use only attested features vs borrow later (c. 1000–19 BBY) layouts. Best fit: attested core plus later rooms marked "campaign invention".
3. **Number of spires in 3653 BBY:** four Council spires (High Council Tower page; "four towers falling") vs five towers (later layout). Best fit: four for this era.
4. **Extent of destruction:** "destroyed" or "nearly destroying" vs "much of the interior remained intact" with intact lower levels. Best fit: exterior and towers ruined, interior partly intact.
5. **High Council Chamber:** the tower "collapsed" in 3653 BBY vs "the remains of the High Council Chamber" in the ruins in 3643 BBY. Best fit: the tower fell, but the chamber's remains survived inside.
6. **Rebuilding date:** "several years" (Archives), before 3600 BBY with 3519 BBY expansions (Coruscant), c. 3500 BBY (Temple), or no later than 1000 BBY (Temple district). Best fit: c. 3519–3500 BBY.
7. **Tower of First Knowledge rebuilt:** 1019 BBY (its own page) vs "northeastern wing" with the tower completed in 1000 BBY (First Knowledge quarter page). Best fit: 1019 BBY, northwest.
8. **Tython:** rediscovered after the Sacking by Satele Shan vs "recently rediscovered" before the migration. Best fit: after the Sacking.
9. **Genarius:** Jedi Enclave purged in the Great Galactic War vs system discovered in 232 BBY (same page). Best fit: keep the war-era Enclave and flag it as a source contradiction.
10. **Republic Executive Building:** facade blackened in 3653 BBY (Senate Building page) vs built much later under Palpatine (its own page). Best fit: it did not exist; read it as another government annex.
11. **Temple Guard in the window:** inferred from "established thousands of years prior" vs no attestation at the Sacking. Best fit: include them as a minor presence, tagged [inferred].
12. **Yinta Lake:** the brief listed it as a Coruscant spaceport, but it is on Vulta. Best fit: drop it from Coruscant and use Liston and the Coruscant Spaceport.
