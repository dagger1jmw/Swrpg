## Summary
- **Old Republic.** The Republic went through:
  - a depression and piracy crisis after the Mandalorian Wars (3966 BBY);
  - a near-collapse from "the simple inability to support itself" (3951 BBY);
  - a long depression after the Treaty of Coruscant (3653 BBY), lasting at least a decade;
  - Zakuul's tribute regime (c.3636 BBY).
- **Late Republic.** Trade-route taxation and the Naboo blockade (33–32 BBY), then a currency upheaval (22 BBY). The Battle of Muunilinst took the galaxy "to the brink of financial ruin".
- **Empire.** Near-zero inflation, but local shocks: dolovite at 760 cr a barrel, a Tyrellian ale spike to 200 cr a barrel, slave revenues down 53%.
- **After Endor.** Currencies fragmented. The Bacta War embargo cost lives (19 ABY). The Yuuzhan Vong War "practically collapsed" the economy.

### Old Republic era events (c.25,000–3630 BBY)

- **Type:** Wars, depressions and reconstruction.

**Key facts:**
- [in-universe] Taris, 4156–3956 BBY:
  - Taris turned to industry to fix its economic troubles. This polluted its oceans, eliminated its main food source, and began an "economical urban decay" that lasted 200 years.
  - It became "the poster child of an economically dying world".
  - Source: W:Taris/Legends (lead; §Decay).
- [in-universe] 3966 BBY, Republic, Mandalorian Wars aftermath:
  - Economic depression and widespread piracy forced the Senate into financial and military reforms.
  - Politicians guaranteed corporations safe passage on the hyperlanes in exchange for commercial investment, which rebuilt the military and the economy.
  - Some groups used the arrangement for profit.
  - Sources: W:Galactic_Republic/Legends (§Old Sith Wars); W:Mandalorian_Wars (§Reconstruction). Both cite the *KOTOR Campaign Guide*, so the tier is [game stat: KOTOR Campaign Guide, WotC 2008] for this item; see Decisions needed.
- [in-universe] c.3964–3960 BBY, Telerath: the Telerath Interstellar Banking Initiative, Aargau's rival, was abandoned during the Mandalorian Wars. Source: W:Aargau/Legends (§Early history).
- [in-universe] Jedi Civil War and kolto: cross-reference SW_Kolto_Bacta. That file covers the kolto price rise, the switch to bacta and Manaan's collapse. W:Kolto/Legends and W:Manaan/Legends give no year.
- [in-universe] 3955 BBY, Telos: the Republic funded the Telos Restoration Project "to help spur the galactic economy" and to test restoring other ruined Outer Rim worlds. Source: W:Citadel_Station (§History; KOTOR II; *New Essential Guide to Droids*).
- [in-universe] 3951 BBY, Republic: despite the restoration campaign, "five years later the Republic faced imminent collapse not from war, but from the simple inability to support itself". Source: W:Jedi_Civil_War (§Galactic Republic; KOTOR II).
- [in-universe] Great Galactic War (3681–3653 BBY): the Sith Empire, allied with the Mandalorian clans, crippled the Republic economy. Source: W:Sith_Empire_(Post–Great_Hyperspace_War) (lead).
- [in-universe] 3661–3660 BBY, Hydian Way blockade: cross-reference SW_Coruscant_City_GGW_Part1 and SW_Planets_Travel_OldRepublic_Part5. Not repeated.
- [in-universe] 3653 BBY onward, Republic:
  - After the Treaty of Coruscant, "a large economic depression began", slowing rebuilding. Source: W:Galactic_Republic/Legends (§Old Sith Wars).
  - The Republic suffered "a massive economic depression which lasts for at least a decade". Its economy was "shattered" and half the galaxy fell under Sith rule. Source: W:Great_Galactic_War (SWTOR website holonet, "The Galactic Republic").
- [in-universe] c.3640 BBY, Makeb: the Hutt Cartel seized Makeb by force and bribery. Afterwards the Rift Alliance members were rewarded with "several billion credits" and hyperspace-route access. Sources: W:Galactic_War (§Rise and fall of the Hutt Cartel); W:The_Barsen'thor (SWTOR). The reparations themselves are in SW_SWTOR_Era_Power_Structures_Part2.
- [in-universe] c.3636 BBY onward, galaxy-wide: the Eternal Empire took "political, military, and economic control" of both the Republic and the Sith Empire, which "paid tributes until the War against Zakuul". Sources: W:Sith_Empire_(Post–Great_Hyperspace_War); W:Eternal_Empire (SWTOR KotFE, 2015).
- [in-universe] c.3636 BBY, Zakuul: the Old World's alternative economy was run by criminals, while citizens above lived on the Emperor's stipend. Source: W:Zakuul (§Old World).
- [in-universe] c.3630 BBY, galaxy-wide: uprisings against the Eternal Alliance were funded through Outer Rim front companies. Source: W:Eternal_Alliance (§Reformation; SWTOR).

### Late Republic (124–19 BBY)

- **Type:** Tax disputes, blockades, currency upheaval and shortages.

**Key facts:**
- [in-universe] 124 BBY, Outer Rim Free Trade Zone: the Trade Federation's dominance meant other sectors lost "their economic self-determination". Source: W:Trade_Federation/Legends.
- [in-universe] 65–62 BBY, Naboo:
  - The plasma deal brought "a great period of economic prosperity".
  - Discontent grew because the Federation resold plasma at inflated prices.
  - In 62 BBY a plan to restructure Naboo's debt promised tax cuts and lower interest.
  - Source: W:Invasion_of_Naboo/Legends.
- [in-universe] 44 BBY, Stark Hyperspace War: a bacta shortage, with Stark selling raided bacta at reduced prices in the Outer Rim. Source: W:Trade_Federation/Legends.
- [in-universe] 32 BBY, Naboo: the Blockade of Naboo protested shipping-lane taxes and left Naboo "beginning to starve". Gunray feared the lost trade revenue, and Sidious promised weapons shipments would balance it. Sources: W:Trade_Federation/Legends (§The invasion of Naboo); W:Invasion_of_Naboo/Legends.
- [in-universe] 27 BBY, Radnor bioplague: a bio-isolation suit cost 5,000 karsems, an average year's pay. Source: W:Karsem.
- [in-universe] 22 BBY, galaxy-wide (Separatist Crisis):
  - Most worlds outside the Core and Inner Rim refused credits.
  - The IGBC minted twenty currencies a day, and King Kikipi credits fell to 15 million per Republic credit in eight months.
  - The IGBC posted its 18th straight quarter of profit growth.
  - Sources: W:Galactic_Credit_Standard/Legends; W:King_Kikipi_credit; W:InterGalactic_Banking_Clan/Legends (HNN 47, 2002).
- [in-universe] 22 BBY, Coruscant: the General Ministry Institutes cut funding for many Republic bodies, leaving some to rely on private funding. Source: W:Techno_Union/Legends (HNN 47).
- [in-universe] 22 BBY, Raith Sienar's company: stock up 14%, assets up 640 million cr. Source: W:Raith_Sienar/Legends (HNN) [unclear exact HNN issue].
- [in-universe] 22 BBY, Chandrila: the Military Creation Act moved local tax revenue to the Army of the Republic. Source: W:Military_Creation_Act/Legends.
- [in-universe] 22 BBY (Clone Wars), Muunilinst: the Battle of Muunilinst devastated Muun cities and "led the galaxy to the brink of financial ruin". Sources: W:Muun/Legends (§Clone Wars); W:Muunilinst/Legends (*Clone Wars* micro-series).
- [in-universe] Clone Wars: the Senate deregulated the Banking Clan to fund the war. The IGBC's 25% interest drained social programmes. The banks were then nationalized under Palpatine. Sources: W:InterGalactic_Banking_Clan/Legends; W:Bail_Prestor_Organa/Legends.
- [in-universe] c.21–20 BBY, Mid and Outer Rim: after the Battle of Bassadro, the CIS credit was worth more than 50% of a Republic credit. Source: W:CIS_credit.
- [in-universe] Clone Wars, Mandalore (the New Mandalorian enclave at Sundari, Ruled (Jared, 24 Nov 2026)): neutrality left neglected provinces "destitute and starving for funds" (22 BBY), and customs bribery let Moogan tea in. Source: W:Mandalorian/Legends (§Mandalorian peace under fire; §New Mandalorian corruption).
- [in-universe] 19 BBY, Mustafar: immediately after the Empire formed, HNN reported that an industrial accident on Mustafar drove the price of dolovite to 760 cr per barrel. Klegger Corp never recovered. Source: W:Mustafar/Legends (*Star Wars Insider* 84, "Republic HoloNet News Special Inaugural Edition 16:5:241").
- [in-universe] End of the Clone Wars, Muunilinst and Mygeeto: Separatist forces based there fled to the Ciutric sector and joined pirates and Thalassian slavers. Source: W:Muunilinst/Legends (*Essential Guide to Warfare*).

**Related:** SW_Economy_Currencies; SW_Economy_Institutions_Debt.

### Imperial era (19 BBY–4 ABY)

- **Type:** Stability and local shocks.

**Key facts:**
- [in-universe] Galaxy-wide: the Imperial period saw "virtual non-existence of inflation", in part because the Empire let the CSA operate as a mercantile zone. Source: W:Galactic_Empire/Legends (§Economy; *Imperial Handbook*, 2014).
- [game stat: Galaxy Guide 6: Tramp Freighters, WEG 1990] Shesharile 5: the system's own currency lost 98% of its value, and merchants demanded Imperial credits or precious metals. Source: W:Shesharile_5.
- [game stat: Galaxy Guide 6: Tramp Freighters, WEG 1990] A few years after 1 ABY, Yelsain/Yesain: at a yearly Grand Moot, high demand and short supply drove Tyrellian ale up to 200 cr per barrel. Source: W:Tyrellian_ale.
- [in-universe] c.3 BBY–3 ABY, Cloud City: as Baron-Administrator, Calrissian raised Tibanna gas profits by over 35%. Source: W:Lando_Calrissian/Legends.
- [in-universe] c.3.5 ABY, Black Sun sector: slave revenues fell 53% when planets joined the Alliance and banned slavery. Source: W:Slavery/Legends (*Shadows of the Empire*).
- [in-universe] c.4 ABY, galaxy-wide: physical currency grew more popular as people feared credit chips would be worthless after the war. Source: W:Galactic_Credit_Standard/Legends (§Form).

**Related:** SW_Economy_Prices_Goods (Imperial-era prices).

**Uncertainty:** The Tyrellian ale figure is a WEG game-book item.

### New Republic to Legacy (4–137 ABY)

- **Type:** Fragmentation, embargo, collapse and recovery.

**Key facts:**
- [in-universe] 4 ABY onward, galaxy-wide: after Endor:
  - Many currencies arose and exchange rates "fluctuated wildly".
  - Traders used metals and commodities, because the NR and the Remnant refused each other's money.
  - Sources: W:Galactic_Credit_Standard/Legends (§Decline); W:Galactic_Empire/Legends (§Economy).
- [in-universe] c.4 ABY onward, Tatooine: Jabba's death set off months of gang war in every community. Source: W:Tatooine/Legends (§New Republic).
- [game stat: Twin Stars of Kira, WEG 1993] Early NR, Krann: the nova-crystal mine that backed much of the NR's hard currency "ran dry". Source: W:New_Republic/Legends (§Economy).
- [in-universe] c.5 ABY, Cloud City: Zorba the Hutt's management failed to turn a profit. Source: W:Zorba_Desilijic_Tiure (*Jedi Prince* series). The "lost >18 million revenue" figure from my notes is not confirmed on the page excerpt; see Not used.
- [in-universe] 10 ABY, Core Worlds: the Dark Empire swiftly conquered the "economically, politically and strategically vital" Core Worlds. Source: W:Galactic_Empire/Legends (§Imperial resurgence).
- [in-universe] 19 ABY, Thyferra (Bacta War): Ysanne Isard's bacta embargo was brief, but it caused deaths at many New Republic medical facilities. Source: W:Bacta_War (§Aftermath).
- [in-universe] 25–27 ABY: Ithorian currency became worthless after Ithor was destroyed. Source: W:Ithorian_currency.
- [in-universe] Yuuzhan Vong War (25–29 ABY), galaxy-wide: the economy "practically collapsed". Source: W:Galactic_Federation_of_Free_Alliances (§Economy).
- [game stat: Star Wars Gamer 5, "A Campaign Guide to the Centrality", WotC 2001] Yuuzhan Vong War aftermath, the Centrality: refugees caused overpopulation and scarce jobs. Source: W:Yuuzhan_Vong_War (§Aftermath).
- [in-universe] Post-war, Ailon: crippling economic sanctions followed for the Ailon Nova Guard's support of the Vong. Source: W:Yuuzhan_Vong_War (§Aftermath; *Galaxy at War*): [game stat: Galaxy at War, WotC 2009].
- [in-universe] c.40 ABY, Galactic Alliance: members were required to pay reconstruction taxes. Source: W:Galactic_Federation_of_Free_Alliances.
- [in-universe] c.40 ABY, Mandalore:
  - Food imports caused an "economical deficit", answered by MandalMotors' half-profits pledge and Fett's multimillion-credit gift.
  - The new beskar supply made "Mandalore's economy surge", and Roche's Verpine sought shipments.
  - Source: W:Mandalorian/Legends (§Restoration).

**Related:** SW_Economy_Currencies (post-Endor currencies).
