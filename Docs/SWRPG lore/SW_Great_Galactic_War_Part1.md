# Star Wars Legends: The Great Galactic War (3681–3653 BBY), Part 1: 3681–3667 BBY

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 21, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
- **Opening:** the Sith Empire began the war in 3681 BBY after centuries of preparation. It struck the Tingel Arm, took Korriban, and destroyed the Sluis Van shipyards while the Senate deadlocked. [Assault on the Tingel Arm region](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Assault_on_the_Tingel_Arm_region); [Battle of Korriban](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Korriban); [Senate emergency defense session](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Senate_emergency_defense_session)
- **Jedi response:** the Jedi acted ahead of the Senate in the Minos Cluster (3680 BBY). [Great Galactic War §Republic response](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Galactic_War)
- **Outer Rim:** the Empire spent a decade taking it, finishing with the Seswenna sector in 3671 BBY. [Third battle of the Seswenna sector](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Third_battle_of_the_Seswenna_sector)
- **Turning back:** Republic victories at Bothawui (3671 BBY), the capture of the Dread Masters (3668 BBY) and Alderaan (3667 BBY) halted the advance into the Mid Rim and Core. [First Battle of Bothawui](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/First_Battle_of_Bothawui); [Capture of the Dread Masters](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Capture_of_the_Dread_Masters); [Battle of Alderaan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Alderaan)

**Scope and splits:**
- Part 1 covers 3681–3667 BBY plus the war overview, front lines, the Jedi at war and the Sith at war.
- Part 2 covers 3666–3660 BBY, undated battles, Mandalorians, other factions and daily life.
- Part 3 covers 3659–3653 BBY, the peace process, the Sacking, immediate effects and "who was where".

## Entries
### Overview: the war at a glance
- **Type:** event (galactic war)
- **Era:** 3681–3653 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - Also called the Great War or the Republic–Sith War: a 28-year war between the Galactic Republic and the Sith Empire, ending with the Treaty of Coruscant. [Great Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Galactic_War); [Great Galactic War (infobox)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Galactic_War)
  - The war began with the 3681 BBY assault on the Tingel Arm region. [Great Galactic War (infobox)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Galactic_War)
  - **Sides:**
    - Republic: the Republic, the Jedi Order and Hylo Visz's smugglers.
    - Empire: the Sith Empire with the Chiss Ascendancy, puppet governments and the Mandalorians.

    [Great Galactic War (infobox)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Galactic_War)
  - **Results** [Great Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Galactic_War); [Great Galactic War (infobox)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Galactic_War):
    - a decisive Imperial victory;
    - the Republic gave up many outlying systems;
    - the Republic suffered an economic depression lasting at least a decade;
    - the Jedi left Coruscant for Tython;
    - "roughly half the galaxy" ended under Sith rule.
  - **Planning:**
    - The Emperor planned the invasion from as early as 3960 BBY. Revan's efforts delayed it.
    - After a long build-up, Imperial agents secured the Chiss alliance and installed puppet governments in Outer Rim systems.
    - The Belkadan, Ruuria and Sernpidal systems were secretly under Imperial control before the first shot.

    [Great Galactic War §The Sith Empire prepares for war](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Galactic_War)
  - **Infiltration:** the Sith had already infiltrated several planetary governments, the Republic and the Jedi Order itself. [Jedi Order/Legends §The Great Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order/Legends)
  - **Republic leadership:**
    - Grand Master Zym led the Jedi Council in agreeing to fight for the Senate. [Zym §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zym)
    - Supreme Chancellor Berooken was in office in the "latter days" of the war. [Berooken](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Berooken)
    - Chancellors before Berooken: see Not found.
- **Related:** government structures, offices and treaty terms in SW_SWTOR_Era_Power_Structures_Part1.md / Part2.md; the Jedi Council and Grand Masters in SW_Jedi_Ranks_Councils_Roles_AllEras_Part1.md
- **Uncertainty:** [conflict] On the galaxy share at the treaty, see Decisions needed.

### Front lines over time
- **Type:** territorial overview
- **Era:** 3681–3653 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **3681 BBY, before the war:** Belkadan, Ruuria and Sernpidal were already secretly Imperial. [Great Galactic War §The Sith Empire prepares for war](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Galactic_War)
  - **3681 BBY, first year:** the Empire took:
    - the Tingel Arm and the Aparo sector [Assault on the Tingel Arm region §Aftermath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Assault_on_the_Tingel_Arm_region);
    - Korriban and Sluis Van [Battle of Korriban](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Korriban); [Battle of Sluis Van (Great Galactic War)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Sluis_Van_%28Great_Galactic_War%29);
    - the Rimma Trade Route (until 3680 BBY) [Rimma Trade Route/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rimma_Trade_Route/Legends);
    - a foothold in the Minos Cluster [Minos Cluster Campaign](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Minos_Cluster_Campaign).
  - **Outer Rim focus:** the Empire concentrated on the Outer Rim, a region "weak in both Galactic Republic loyalty and defense priority". [Third battle of the Seswenna sector §Prelude](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Third_battle_of_the_Seswenna_sector)
  - **c. 3671 BBY:**
    - After nearly a decade, Republic Intelligence estimated that the Empire held nearly half the Outer Rim. [Great Galactic War §Battles for the Outer Rim](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Galactic_War)
    - The 3671 BBY capture of the Seswenna sector "finalized" the conquest of the Outer Rim, and the Empire turned toward the Mid Rim. [Third battle of the Seswenna sector](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Third_battle_of_the_Seswenna_sector); [Treaty of Coruscant §The Great War ravages the galaxy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Treaty_of_Coruscant)
  - **Height of the push (3671–3667 BBY):** Mid Rim strikes at Bothawui (3671 BBY), then a Core World strike at Alderaan (3667 BBY). [First Battle of Bothawui](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/First_Battle_of_Bothawui); [Battle of Alderaan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Alderaan)
  - **After Alderaan:** the Republic liberated a number of Mid Rim systems and regained a foothold in the Minos Cluster. [Great Galactic War §Battle of Alderaan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Galactic_War)
  - **c. 3660 BBY:** the fighting reached the Seat of the Empire (Esstran sector): Ord Radama, Korriban, Ziost and Ashas Ree; see Part 2. [Battle of Ord Radama](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Ord_Radama); [Rim Campaign](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rim_Campaign)
  - **3653 BBY:** the Empire withdrew from the Minos Cluster as a gesture before the peace talks. [Satele Shan §Hero of the Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satele_Shan); [Minos Cluster/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Minos_Cluster/Legends)
  - **The treaty:** about half the galaxy under the Sith; the treaty's territorial terms are in SW_SWTOR_Era_Power_Structures_Part2.md. [Great Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Galactic_War)
  - **Dromund Kaas:** the capital stayed safe from Republic reprisal throughout the war. [Dromund Kaas/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dromund_Kaas/Legends)
- **Related:** map of the treaty division (image on [Great Galactic War §Aftermath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Galactic_War)); Part 2 for 3666–3653 BBY

### The Jedi at war
- **Type:** institution at war
- **Era:** 3681–3653 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Early independent action:**
    - While the Senate stalled, the Jedi acted alone, sending a force to the Minos Cluster. Its heroism on the news feeds spurred recruitment. [Great Galactic War §Republic response](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Galactic_War)
    - After Eliad, Republic soldiers took over front lines, freeing Jedi for other theatres. [Battle of Eliad §Aftermath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Eliad)
  - **Deployment with the military** (examples):
    - Kao Cen Darach, the Order's Battlemaster, oversaw the Jedi vigil and garrison on Korriban. [Satele Shan §Early life](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satele_Shan); [Great Galactic War §Fall of Korriban](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Galactic_War)
    - Belth Allusis, a High Council member, commanded Republic troops and Jedi at Bothawui. [Belth Allusis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Belth_Allusis)
    - Orgus Din fought with SpecForce's Havoc Squad at Karideph; he and Tavus were the "Heroes of Karideph". [Battle of Karideph](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Karideph)
    - Jaric Kaedan led a SpecForce team against the Dread Masters. [Capture of the Dread Masters](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Capture_of_the_Dread_Masters)
    - Oteg served as both Jedi Master and Fleet Admiral. [Oteg](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Oteg)
    - Satele Shan led "fellow Jedi and the forces of the Republic Military". [Satele Shan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satele_Shan)
    - Some Jedi also served on lone or rogue missions, for example Tol Braga's peace overtures to Sith Lords. [Duel on Dantooine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Duel_on_Dantooine)
