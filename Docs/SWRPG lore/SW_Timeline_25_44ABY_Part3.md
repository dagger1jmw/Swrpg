# SWRPG Galaxy Timeline, 25–44 ABY (Part 3 of 4): The Dark Nest Crisis to the Second Galactic Civil War (35–41 ABY)

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 24, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends), as cited per entry. Rulings of 24 Nov 2026 applied (lore-rulings-2026-11-24c.md; D-484).

## Summary
Part 3 covers the Dark Nest Crisis (35 ABY) and the Swarm War (36 ABY), the quiet years 37–39 ABY in which the Corellian Five Worlds government rose, and the Second Galactic Civil War (40–41 ABY): Jacen Solo's fall to the dark side as Darth Caedus, the death of Mara Jade Skywalker, the Jedi Coalition and the Battle of Uroro Station. Column layout, abbreviations and the calendar note are as in Part 1. The Lost Tribe's emergence, which begins in 41 ABY, is in Part 4. Back sections for the whole set are in Part 4.

## DARK NEST CRISIS AND SWARM WAR — 35–36 ABY
## ════════════════════════════════════════════════

### Overview
Six years after the war, the Galactic Alliance under Cal Omas governed from Coruscant with Jedi on its High Council, and the Jedi trained at Ossus under an informal Masters' Council led by Luke Skywalker [Cal Omas](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cal_Omas) [Jedi High Council (New Jedi Order)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council_(New_Jedi_Order)). In 35 ABY the insectoid Killiks, thought gone from the galaxy, were rediscovered: their Colony was led by Raynar Thul, missing since Myrkr and now the Joiner "UnuThul", and was secretly steered by the Gorog or "Dark Nest" under the Dark Jedi Lomi Plo and Welk [35 ABY/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/35_ABY/Legends) [Raynar Thul](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Raynar_Thul) [Dark Nest Crisis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Nest_Crisis). The Colony's expansion along the Chiss border, at Qoribu and then Woteba in the Utegetu Nebula, brought it into conflict with the Chiss Ascendancy; some young Jedi (among them Jaina Solo and Zekk) were Joined to the Killiks and fought on their side, and the crisis strained relations between the Jedi and the Alliance [Dark Nest Crisis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Nest_Crisis) [Jaina Solo Fel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jaina_Solo_Fel) [Cal Omas](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cal_Omas). In 36 ABY it became the Swarm War, fought between the Colony and the Chiss (supported by the Alliance, under Supreme Commander Pellaeon, and the Jedi); it ended at Tenupe with Lomi Plo dead, Raynar captured and the Killiks pushed back into the Unknown Regions [Swarm War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Swarm_War). Luke formally took the title of Grand Master and founded an official Jedi High Council; the Jedi stepped back from an active role in the Alliance government [36 ABY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/36_ABY) [Swarm War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Swarm_War). Jacen Solo, back from his travels and newly ruthless, had a daughter, Allana, with Tenel Ka in secret [Darth Caedus](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Caedus).

## THE UNEASY PEACE — 36–39 ABY
## ══════════════════════════════════════════

### Overview
The Galactic Alliance imposed Sector Defense Limits on its member worlds in 37 ABY, and its taxes for post-war rebuilding were resented [37 ABY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/37_ABY) [Second Galactic Civil War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Galactic_Civil_War). The Hutts stayed independent of both [39 ABY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/39_ABY). Far away on Kesh, the Lost Tribe of the Sith knew nothing of the wider galaxy yet [Lost Tribe of Sith emergence](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lost_Tribe_of_Sith_emergence). In the Corellian system a new government, the Five Worlds, arose between 36 and 39 ABY and questioned the value of Alliance membership; Corellia had been secretly building a fleet at Kiris since about 30 ABY [Five Worlds](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Five_Worlds) [30 ABY/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/30_ABY/Legends).

## SECOND GALACTIC CIVIL WAR — 40–41 ABY
## ══════════════════════════════════════════

### Overview
The Second Galactic Civil War (also the Corellia–GA war, CivWar Two) began in 40 ABY when the Galactic Alliance struck Corellia, whose government was moving toward independence over defence limits and reconstruction taxes; Bothawui, Bespin and others joined the Corellians to form the Confederation [Second Galactic Civil War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Galactic_Civil_War) [Operation Roundabout](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Operation_Roundabout). The Sith Lady Lumiya, who had secretly inflamed the dispute, took Jacen Solo as her apprentice; Jacen commanded the new Galactic Alliance Guard secret police, joined Admiral Cha Niathal in deposing Cal Omas, killed Mara Jade Skywalker and became Darth Caedus [Lumiya](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lumiya) [Second Galactic Civil War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Galactic_Civil_War). After he set Kashyyyk ablaze the Jedi left the Alliance and formed the Jedi Coalition with Hapes and the Wookiees, making the war three-sided; the Jedi destroyed Centerpoint Station [Battle of Kashyyyk (Second Galactic Civil War)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Kashyyyk_(Second_Galactic_Civil_War)) [Second Galactic Civil War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Galactic_Civil_War). In 41 ABY Caedus brought the Imperial Remnant in on his side; at Fondor Pellaeon was killed, and Niathal broke away as the Alliance-in-exile, joined by Natasi Daala's Maw Irregular Fleet and Boba Fett's Mandalorians [Second Battle of Fondor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Battle_of_Fondor). Jaina Solo, trained by Boba Fett, killed Caedus at Uroro Station; the Imperial Remnant switched sides under its new Head of State Jagged Fel, the Confederation surrendered, Daala became Chief of State of a reunited Alliance, and Caedus's actions had unknowingly begun to free the entity Abeloth [Battle of Uroro Station](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Uroro_Station) [Second Galactic Civil War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Galactic_Civil_War).
