## Summary
- **Je'daii (c. 36,453–25,783 BBY):** nine-Temple monastic order on Tython; initiates at Padawan Kesh → Padawan (one-on-one) → Journeyer (two-year Great Journey, usually in their teens) → Ranger → Master → Temple Master. Balance philosophy; exile to the moons for imbalance; mated couples allowed.
- **Early Order to Great Hyperspace War (c. 25,783–5000 BBY):** Ossus academy; Masters ran their own schools (often on their homeworlds), took several students, met at conclaves; Trials informal and Master-set; no age limit; adults taken. Lightsaber made belt-portable c. 7003 BBY.
- **Great Sith War era (c. 4000 BBY):** praxeums on Arkania (Arca Jeth, up to 20 students), Dantooine (Vodo-Siosk Baas), Ambria (Thon); married adults and parents trained; knighting by Masters' judgment and field tests. Ossus destroyed 3996 BBY. Afterwards: HQ to Coruscant, Masters asked to take one apprentice, families discouraged.
- **c. 3976–3950 BBY:** Dantooine Enclave + Council trained younglings and Padawans; several Masters per student was normal; Revan retrained as an adult over one Master's objection; Taris academy massacre (3964 BBY); Revanchist Padawans went to war; First Jedi Purge ended the Order's structure; Meetra Surik trained adults informally and rebuilt the Council.
- **3600–1000 BBY:** post-Zakuul regroupment (Ossus colony, Task Force Nova); New Sith Wars broke formal training (Knights recruited on campaign, Jedi Lords with hereditary titles, child soldiers in the Army of Light, lax Trials); Ruusan (1000 BBY) brought infant recruitment, Coruscant centralization, one-to-one rule, five formal Trials, end of military titles, *The Jedi Path*.

### Era 2 — Early Republic to the Great Hyperspace War

### Non-Order tradition — Corellian Jedi (semi-independent sect; listed here as non-standard)
- **Type:** Force tradition (sect *of* the Jedi Order per its source page, but outside High Council control; included here per brief and clearly marked)
- **Era:** Old Republic (SWTOR Cold War) to the late Republic
- **Key facts:**
  - Recruited mainly Humans from Corellia; after a minor schism many Corellian-born Jedi returned home and built a fortified enclave, took missions only in their sector, wore green robes ("Green Jedi" in the Cold War) ([Corellian Jedi §lead, §Old Jedi Order][cj]).
  - Customs: Jedi credits passed from master to apprentice and to friends on promotion; marriage permitted, creating bloodlines such as the Halcyon family ([Corellian Jedi §Old Jedi Order][cj], citing *SWTOR* and *I, Jedi*).
  - The enclave was sacked in the Cold War and its Council and leader Arfan Ramos killed ([Corellian Jedi §Old Jedi Order][cj]).
  - Late-Republic continuation (Nejaa Halcyon): see Part 2.
- **Related:** Halcyon family; Corran Horn (Part 2).
- **Uncertainty:** [unclear] Whether this is "non-Order": the source calls it a sect of the Order. Training ages and structure: NF.

### Non-Order tradition — Baran Do (Kel Dor)
- **Type:** Non-Order Force tradition
- **Era:** Pre-Republic contact (undated) to the New Jedi Order
- **Key facts:**
  - Kel Dor Sages first used the Force to sharpen senses and foresee weather disasters, then took government roles ([Baran Do §History][bd]).
  - Kel Dor families once sent Force-sensitive children to train with the Sages; after Dorin joined the Republic, the Jedi took such children "as was their custom", and the Sages faded into obscurity, which spared them the Great Jedi Purge ([Baran Do §History][bd]).
  - They survived by recruiting Force-sensitive Kel Dor and by traditional families still sending children; later contacted Luke Skywalker's Order; Jacen Solo learned *ayna-seff* from them; they kept a hidden bunker for Masters who faked their deaths ([Baran Do §History][bd], citing *Fate of the Jedi: Outcast*).
- **Uncertainty:** Ages, ranks and length of training: NF. Dates: NF (no BBY/ABY given on the page for the early period).
