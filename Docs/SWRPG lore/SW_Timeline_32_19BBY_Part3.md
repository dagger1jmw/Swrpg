# SWRPG Galaxy Timeline, 32–19 BBY (Part 3 of 4): The Clone Wars, Years Two and Three (21–19 BBY)

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 24, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends), current pages and pre-August-2008 revisions, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
Part 3 covers the war from about 14:5 GrS (21 BBY) to the eve of the final campaigns in early 19 BBY: the Spaarti cloning cylinders, Jabiim and Aargonar, Operation Durge's Lance and Grievous's terror in the Core, the Foerost breakout and the *Victory* Fleet, Praesitlyn and Anakin's knighthood, the Outer Rim Sieges, Boz Pity and Saleucami, and the war's political turn (the Security Act, the Reflex Amendment). It ends with Maul's return in 20 BBY and the second-to-fifth-season events of *The Clone Wars*, all [S-canon]. The year pages place these events in GrS months 14:4–14:12 (21 BBY), 15:3–15:12 (20 BBY) and 16:4 onward (19 BBY) ([21 BBY/Legends §Battles](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/21_BBY/Legends); [20 BBY/Legends §Battles](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/20_BBY/Legends); [19 BBY/Legends §Battles](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/19_BBY/Legends)).

## THE CLONE WARS, YEAR TWO — 21–20 BBY
## ════════════════════════════════════════════

### Overview
The second year was one of Separatist gains and Republic embarrassments that brought the war into the Core: Kuat and Neimoidia mined the hyperlanes between them, closing the heart of the Hydian Way; Sullust seceded, leaving Eriadu in a salient; the Republic lost Jabiim and Togoria; and Grievous's Operation Durge's Lance swept down the Corellian Trade Spine ([Clone Wars/Legends §The war in the Core](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clone_Wars/Legends), citing *The Essential Atlas*). Fear on Coruscant let Palpatine gather more power: clone shock troopers raised on Coruscant policed the capital, and the Security Act and Reflex Amendment were passed.

## THE CLONE WARS, YEAR THREE — 20–19 BBY
## ════════════════════════════════════════════

### Overview
By the third year many on both sides had died. The Republic recovered: with the Bulwark Fleet destroyed and the Rendili Dreadnaughts secured, Duro was retaken, Commenor and Balmorra won, and the Confederacy driven from the Core and Colonies; from about 19.48 BBY (two years and six months after Geonosis) the Republic used its industrial weight in the Outer Rim Sieges, fought in six theaters named for Mygeeto, Serenno, Felucia, Siskeen, Yag'Dhul and Praesitlyn ([Outer Rim Sieges/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Outer_Rim_Sieges/Legends); [Clone Wars/Legends §The Outer Rim Sieges](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clone_Wars/Legends), citing *The New Essential Chronology* and *The Essential Guide to Warfare*). Praesitlyn, Vjun and Anakin's knighthood belong to this year [note: the pre-2008 dating].
