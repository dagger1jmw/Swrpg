# Era 09 Characters: Great Galactic War, Cold War and early Galactic War (c. 3681–3636 BBY), Part 3b: Imperial non-Force figures, Mandalorians, underworld and others

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 21, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

**Set:** SW_Characters_09_GGW_ColdWar_Index.md (master index, open questions, inclusion rules, topic-file conflicts); Part1 (Jedi and light-side users); Part2 (Sith and other dark-side users); Part3a (Republic government, military, SIS and Republic-side companions); Part3b (Imperial government, military and Intelligence, Imperial-side companions, Mandalorians, underworld and Hutts, others). The developer's group 3 was split in two to keep each file under ~70 KB. This file is Part3b.

## Summary
This file covers named non-Force figures on the Imperial side (the military, ministers and Imperial Intelligence), the Agent and Bounty Hunter protagonists (by title only), the companions of the Imperial classes, the Mandalorians, and the underworld and Hutt figures, from 3681 BBY to 3636 BBY. Key points: Kilran was still a captain at the Sacking and became Grand Moff only after it. Mandalore the Lesser, an Imperial Intelligence puppet, took the title in 3667 BBY and ran the Hydian Way blockade in 3661 BBY; Artus Lok (the Vindicated) killed him, c. 3660 BBY per the infobox. Eleena Daru was a Rutian Twi'lek whom Malgus killed in 3653 BBY. Shae Vizla disabled the Temple's defence grid in the Sacking. No named Chiss or Voss figure with a dated Era 09 role was found.

Missing developer files: SW_Force_Powers_* and the developer's SW_Jedi_Training / Sith_Training files are **not on the box**. Narrative in SW_Imperial_Military_Intelligence_GGW_*, SW_Mandalorians_*, SW_NarShaddaa_*, SW_Dromund_Kaas_* and SW_Great_Galactic_War_Part1–3 is not repeated.

### Rycus Kilran ("the Butcher of Coruscant")
- **Type:** character (Imperial naval officer; Grand Moff)
- **Era:** c. 3692 BBY – c. 3641 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - ID: male Human from Dromund Kaas (infobox) [Rycus Kilran](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rycus_Kilran).
  - Life: born c. 3692 BBY on Dromund Kaas; died 3642 BBY in the Maelstrom (lore batch 20, ruling 20; the infobox has c. 3641 BBY) [Rycus Kilran](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rycus_Kilran).
  - Ranks: tactical adviser in the Brema sector. He took command of the fleet at Ord Ibanna when his Sith superior died, and won. He was still a captain at the Sacking, then made an honorary Grand Moff after the war, and commanded the Fifth Fleet [Rycus Kilran §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rycus_Kilran). Pacifier of the Togruta Uprising [Rycus Kilran](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rycus_Kilran).
  - Feats: the scars from a "heroic stand against a Jedi boarding party". His tactics at the Sacking earned his nickname [Rycus Kilran §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rycus_Kilran).
  - 3661 BBY / 3653 BBY: rank and post not stated / captain in the Sacking of Coruscant [Rycus Kilran §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rycus_Kilran).
  - Death: killed by the Republic strike team at the Maelstrom prison while guarding Revan [Rycus Kilran §Defeat at the Maelstrom](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rycus_Kilran).
- **Related:** Malgus (Part2); Revan, Oteg (Part1).
- **Uncertainty:** none.

- **Type:** character (Mandalore; Imperial puppet)
- **Key facts:**
  - ID: male Human (infobox) [Mandalore the Lesser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalore_the_Lesser).

  - Ranks: a Geonosis arena gladiator whose fights Imperial Intelligence rigged; took the title Mandalore in 3667 BBY [Mandalore the Lesser §Ascension](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalore_the_Lesser).
  - Feats: his Hydian Way blockade beat back the Jedi and caused a Core depression.   - 3661 BBY / 3653 BBY: Mandalore, running the blockade / dead per the infobox; see Uncertainty.
- **Uncertainty:** conflict over his death. The Vindicated page says the blockade fell seven years before the Sacking, i.e. c. 3660 BBY [Mandalore the Vindicated §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalore_the_Vindicated), but its lead puts the Great Hunt after the Treaty [Mandalore the Vindicated](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalore_the_Vindicated).
