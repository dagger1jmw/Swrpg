# Sith Training and Organization Across All Legends Eras — Part 2 (1000 BBY onward)

## Summary
From 1000 BBY onward, Sith training follows four phases:
- **Rule of Two:** Bane's single Master and apprentice, with the master's death as the expected end of the line. Dooku's Dark Acolytes and assassins sit beside it as non-Sith.
- **Galactic Empire:** only Sidious and Vader were Sith. A large non-Sith apparatus of Dark Side Adepts grew up around them (Inquisitorius, Emperor's Hands, Prophets, Royal Guard).
- **After Endor:** dark-side training broke into Dark Jedi academies and cults until Lumiya and Caedus, and the Lost Tribe of Sith, with its full rank ladder.
- **Legacy era:** Darth Krayt's One Sith trained many acolytes from birth on Korriban under the Rule of One.

Part 1 (SW_Sith_Training_AllEras_Part1.md) covers the period before 1000 BBY. All of this was taken from wiki pages fetched through the Wookieepedia API (starwars.fandom.com); no mirror was used. Each fact cites the page URL and section.

**Cross-reference:** SW_Sith_Academies_Training_OldRepublic.md covers Kaan's Korriban academy (1006–1000 BBY), where Bane trained, so Bane's academy years are not repeated here.

## Era 7: The Rule of Two (1000 BBY–4 ABY)

**Era summary:** Darth Bane destroyed the Brotherhood and limited the Sith to one Master and one apprentice. The apprentice was expected to kill the Master once strong enough. Training became a private, decades-long apprenticeship of tests, missions and access to the Master's holocrons and archives. The Master named the apprentice "Darth" plus a new name, or kept the birth name, on swearing allegiance. Masters repeatedly hedged against the rule with secret second apprentices: Tenebrous with Venamis, Sidious with Maul while still an apprentice, Vader with Galen Marek. Dooku's Dark Acolytes and assassins (Ventress, Opress) were trained outside the Rule of Two and were never allowed to call themselves Sith.

### The doctrine
- **Type:** structure / rule
- **Era:** from c. 1000 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **The rule:** also called the Banite system, *Chwayatyun* in Sith. Bane, "once a student at Kaan's Sith academy", created it against self-destructive infighting. There would be only two Sith at a time, Master and apprentice, and the apprentice would take the title of Master only when strong enough (intro, no footnote). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rule_of_Two/Legends) (intro)
  - **Destroying the Brotherhood:** Bane duped Kaan into using a thought bomb, then hid from the surviving Jedi. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rule_of_Two/Legends) ("Creation")
  - **Master's view only:** by Tenebrous's time the Sith thought the rule had essentially expired, "always the opinion of the Master only". The apprentice stayed as subservient as ever (ref: *Darth Plagueis*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rule_of_Two/Legends) ("Prelude to the Empire")
  - **Numbers:** Plagueis said there were "30 in a millennium" Sith, against the tens of thousands fit to be Jedi (refs in section). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rule_of_Two/Legends) ("Prelude to the Empire")
  - **Naming:** Bane's line took Darth plus a new Sith name chosen by the Master when the apprentice swore allegiance. Zannah and Maul kept their birth names (ref: *Dynasty of Evil*; *Darth Plagueis*; ROTS). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth/Legends) ("The Rule of Two")
  - **Bane's own fear:** Bane worried that a Master might spend his life training an unworthy apprentice, and came to suspect this of Zannah. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rule_of_Two/Legends) ("Imperial Period")
- **Related:** Era 6
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Bane and Zannah
- **Type:** master–apprentice
- **Era:** 1000–980 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Recruitment:** Rain, a ten-year-old recruited into the Army of Light, showed a display of rage at Ruusan that caught Bane's notice. He took her as apprentice (intro; age from *Path of Destruction*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Zannah/Legends) (intro; "Bane's apprentice")
  - **First test:** on the run, she learned to use the Force to sustain her body. Bane left a few deserters alive as a lesson in making the truth too absurd to believe (ref: *Rule of Two*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Zannah/Legends) ("First test")
  - **Secrecy:** training was on Ambria. Bane stressed secrecy, patience and ingenuity. Her first lesson was to tame a wild neek, which force alone could not do. She spent days winning its trust and grew attached to it, and Bane then snapped its neck with the Force (ref: *Rule of Two*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Zannah/Legends) ("Bane's apprentice")
  - **Sorcery:** by 996 BBY, at 14, she studied the ancient Sith through spells Bane had taken from Freedon Nadd's holocron. Bane admitted he had little gift for sorcery and threatened to destroy her if she used it on him. She built her own double-bladed lightsaber soon after (ref: *Rule of Two*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Zannah/Legends) ("Bane's apprentice")
  - **Missions:** by 990 BBY she was "an instrument of Darth Bane's will", stirring up separatist groups (ref: *Rule of Two*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Zannah/Legends) ("Her Master's will")
  - **Final duel:** in 980 BBY on Ambria, Zannah told Bane she had surpassed him. He had sworn she would not kill him until she had learned all his secrets. During the duel Bane tried essence transfer, learned from Andeddu's holocron, into her body. She won the battle of wills and declared herself Dark Lord (ref: *Dynasty of Evil*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Zannah/Legends) ("Duel with the Dark Lord")
  - **Bane's hedge:** Bane had already taken the Iktotchi assassin Cognus as his next apprentice. Cognus promised to serve whichever of them won (ref: *Dynasty of Evil*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Zannah/Legends) ("Duel with the Dark Lord")
  - **Zannah's own candidate:** Zannah enlisted the Dark Jedi Set Harth as a would-be apprentice. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Zannah/Legends) (intro)
- **Related:** Bane's holocron
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Bane's holocron
- **Type:** teaching tool
- **Era:** c. 980 BBY onward
- **Key facts:**
  - **Failed attempts:** Bane tried three times to make a holocron, and each matrix failed: once from haste, once from caution, once from trusting the Force alone (refs in section). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Bane%27s_holocron) ("History")
  - **The holocron:** a holocron of Bane did exist. Its gatekeeper still wore his orbalisk armour, as a message that the dark side demands sacrifice (ref: *Legacy* "Broken" 4). It may be the holocron Palpatine called the only true one of the Rule of Two Sith. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Bane%27s_holocron) (intro; "History")
  - **Under Krayt:** by 137 ABY Krayt held it, and the gatekeeper refused to help him because he had abolished the Rule of Two. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Bane%27s_holocron) ("History")
- **Related:** Era 10
- **Uncertainty:** [unclear] *Rule of Two* shows failures; *Legacy* shows a finished holocron. The page reconciles them as a later success. No source describes the successful attempt.

### Plagueis and Sidious
- **Type:** master–apprentice
- **Era:** c. 65–32 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Recruitment:** Plagueis's agents found that an informant was the seventeen-year-old Palpatine of the Legislative Youth Program (ref: *Darth Plagueis*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Plagueis/Legends) ("Politics")
  - **Breaking:** soon after the murder of his family, Sidious began training designed to "break" him as an individual and remake him as a Sith. Plagueis promised severe training but freedom from the Rule of Two, with no secrets or mistrust between them (ref: *Darth Plagueis*; *Jedi vs. Sith*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Plagueis/Legends) ("Training Darth Sidious")
  - **Archives:** Sidious inherited the Sith Archives, a thousand years of knowledge passed from Master to apprentice. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Plagueis/Legends) ("Training Darth Sidious")
  - **Curriculum:** training lasted decades: hunting on Buoyant, combat with battle droids on Hypori, and the ritual killing of native warriors on Kursid. Sidious had access to Plagueis's holocrons. Lessons forced him to face his fears, denied him pleasures and took away what he loved. Plagueis taught that envy and hatred were means to an end, and lectured on taking power (ref: *Darth Plagueis*; ROTS). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Plagueis/Legends) ("Training Darth Sidious")
  - **Where it began:** Plagueis first taught Sidious on Mygeeto (ref: *Darth Plagueis*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Maul/Legends) ("The Orsis Academy")
  - **The end:** Sidious killed Plagueis in 32 BBY, after his election as Chancellor. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Plagueis/Legends) (intro)
- **Related:** Maul
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Sidious and Maul (trained while Sidious was still an apprentice)
- **Type:** master–apprentice / assassin training
- **Era:** c. 54–32 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Origin:** Maul was a Dathomirian Zabrak Nightbrother. His Nightsister mother Kycina asked Sidious to take the infant away from Talzin's control (ref: *Darth Plagueis*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Maul/Legends) ("From Dathomir to Mustafar")
  - **Limits:** he was raised by droids on Mustafar. Plagueis allowed Sidious to train him but forbade sharing the deepest secrets: Maul was to be a Sith **assassin**, not a Sith Lord (ref: *Darth Plagueis*; *Wrath of Darth Maul*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Maul/Legends) ("From Dathomir to Mustafar")
  - **Orsis:** in 44 BBY, aged 10, he went to the Orsis academy to learn assassin skills, forbidden to use the Force except for heightened senses. On his first day he beat a student nearly to death (ref: *Wrath of Darth Maul*; *Shadow Hunter* "Restraint"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Maul/Legends) ("The Orsis Academy")
  - **Cruelty as lesson:** on Mygeeto, when Maul expected praise, Sidious threw him into an icy lake and ignored his calls for help (ref: *Wrath of Darth Maul*; *Star Wars Journal*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Maul/Legends) ("The Orsis Academy")
  - **Final test:** in 37 BBY, at 17, Maul was left on Hypori for a month, hunted by assassin droids. Sidious then beat the starving Maul in a duel and told him he had failed and had been secretly training a replacement. When Maul, in rage, bit his hand, Sidious declared him a Sith Lord: "Lord Maul … my instrument" (ref: *Wrath of Darth Maul*; *Star Wars Journal*; *Darth Plagueis*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Maul/Legends) ("Final test")
- **Related:** Era 6 (Umbara ruins)
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Sidious, Tyranus and Vader
- **Type:** master–apprentice
- **Era:** 32 BBY–4 ABY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Two apprentices:** Sidious broke the rule at least twice: by training Maul while still an apprentice, and by taking two Jedi at once. Dooku was said to be merely a candidate rather than a "true" apprentice (ref: *Darth Plagueis*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rule_of_Two/Legends) ("Prelude to the Empire")
  - **Replacing Tyranus:** Sidious staged his own kidnapping so that Anakin would kill Tyranus and replace him. He named Anakin Darth Vader (ROTS). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rule_of_Two/Legends) ("Prelude to the Empire")
  - **Loyalty test:** Sidious, worried that Tyranus was training Ventress to destroy him, ordered him to eliminate her to prove his loyalty, and Tyranus obeyed (TCW "Nightsisters"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dooku/Legends) ("Ventress's 'demise'")
  - **Endor:** Sidious set Luke to fight Vader so that Luke could become his new apprentice. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rule_of_Two/Legends) ("Imperial Period")
- **Related:** Era 8 (Vader's secret apprentice)
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Outside the Rule of Two: Dooku's Dark Acolytes, Ventress and Opress
- **Type:** non-Sith acolytes (explicitly not Sith)
- **Era:** 22–19 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Dark Acolytes:** the title went to Dark Jedi who joined the Confederacy and served Dooku directly. All were trained in Sith arts by Tyranus, but "none could claim the mantle of Sith due to the Rule of Two". [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Acolyte/Legends) (intro)
  - **Recruitment:** joining was relatively easy and most capable Force-users were accepted. Recruits stayed minor players until they won Tyranus's trust through fealty or dark-side depravity (ref: *Jedi: Count Dooku*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Acolyte/Legends) ("Recruitment")
  - **Equipment and tactics:** Sith-designed equipment. Tyranus insisted they travel in groups of three (ref: *The Clone Wars* video game). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Acolyte/Legends) ("Equipment")
  - **Ideology:** little formal ideology beyond opposing the Jedi, but strongly influenced by Sith philosophy. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Acolyte/Legends) ("Ideology")
  - **Not a rule violation:** Sidious and Vader regarded such agents as acolytes, not apprentices, because they were not taught the secrets of the Sith. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rule_of_Two/Legends) ("Imperial Period")
  - **Ventress's recruitment:** seeking a replacement for Sev'rance Tann, Dooku watched Ventress fight in the Cauldron gladiatorial pits on Rattatak. He knocked her out with Force lightning, later beat her in a duel, and presented her to Sidious, who approved (refs in section). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Asajj_Ventress/Legends) ("Initiation on Rattatak")
  - **Never a Darth:** Tyranus privately called her "apprentice" and she called him "Master", but he never gave her the Darth title, "perhaps out of respect for the Rule of Two" (TCW). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rule_of_Two/Legends) ("Prelude to the Empire"). He gave her the lightsabers of his fallen apprentice Komari Vosa (ref: *Jedi vs. Sith*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dooku/Legends) ("A new ally")
  - **Opress's selection:** Ventress chose Savage Opress through the Nightsisters' Selection: the Test of Fury (armed combat against her), the Test of Night (in darkness) and the Test of Elevation (among moving stone pillars) (TCW "Monster"; *Book of Sith*). He murdered his brother Feral to prove his loyalty to her. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Savage_Opress/Legends) (intro; "The Selection")
  - **Opress's training:** on Serenno, Tyranus trained Opress in lightsaber combat and Sith skills as a "hard taskmaster". He stoked anger with demeaning criticism and Force lightning, and punished failure brutally (TCW "Witches of the Mist"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dooku/Legends) ("Training the brother of Darth Maul")
  - **Maul's own apprentice:** after his exile Maul declared himself Opress's master, which broke the rule. Sidious later beat both of them (TCW "Revival"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rule_of_Two/Legends) ("Prelude to the Empire")
- **Related:** Nightsisters (non-Sith, Dathomir)
- **Uncertainty:** see "Not used" for the "Sith Acolyte" title citation

- Synthetic crystals in this era. Not covered by the pages consulted.
- Training sabers or non-lethal sparring. Not stated, apart from Tenebrous's red saber used for sparring.

## Era 8: The Galactic Empire (19 BBY–4 ABY)

**Era summary:** Under the Empire only Sidious and Vader were Sith. Every other darksider was a "Dark Side Adept", and Palpatine planned eventually to put adepts in all official posts. The adepts formed a hierarchy. At the top was the Inquisitorius, which recruited captured Padawans through a lethal audition before Vader and ran a ladder of apprentice, Inquisitor, High Inquisitor and Grand Inquisitor. Below them were the Emperor's Hands, trained as operatives rather than apprentices, and the Prophets of the Dark Side, descended from Darth Millennial. The Royal Guard ended training with a fight to the death watched by the Emperor. Vader secretly trained apprentices of his own: Lumiya and Galen Marek. None of these groups except the two Darths were Sith, and Sidious did not count them as violations of the Rule of Two (see Era 7).

### Dark Side Adepts: the umbrella category (non-Sith)
- **Type:** classification / ranks
- **Era:** 19 BBY–c. 11 ABY

  - **Definition:** under the Empire, any darksider other than Vader and Sidious counted as a Dark Side Adept, especially those in administrative posts (ref: *Dark Empire Sourcebook*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_adept/Legends) ("History")

  - **Ladder:** the Inquisitorius ranked highest. Below came the Prophets of the Dark Side, the Emperor's Hands, the Dark Side Elite, Force Adepts, some Sovereign Protectors, the Shadow Guards, some Royal Guards, the first Imperial Sentinels and the Saber Guards. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_adept/Legends) ("History")
  - **Inquisitors above Hands:** Inquisitors were the highest-ranking adepts, above the Hands, the Prophets and lesser Dark Jedi. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Inquisitorius/Legends) (intro)
- **Related:** Era 9 (Dark Side Elite)
- **Uncertainty:** none

### The Inquisitorius (non-Sith)
- **Type:** order / academy-equivalent
- **Era:** founded 19 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Purpose:** a secret division of Imperial Intelligence made up of dark-side Force-sensitive agents ("truth officers") (ref: *Coruscant Nights II*). Founded c. 19 BBY to hunt surviving Jedi. Some Inquisitors, such as Torbin, Tremayne, Jerec and Brandl, used the prefix "Lord". [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Inquisitorius/Legends) (intro; "History")
  - **Selection:** candidates were tested and examined first by Imperial Intelligence, then by the Grand Inquisitor, then by the Sith Lords themselves (ref: *Evasive Action: Recruitment*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Inquisitorius/Legends) ("History")
  - **Recruitment audition:** captured Padawans and AgriCorps members were held on Byss. Their cuffs were switched off and they were given staffs to attack an unarmed Vader. Antinnis Tremayne pressed a button on Vader's chest panel. Vader chose four, Tremayne, Gwellib Ap-Llewff, Lanu Pasiq and Halmere, and **the rest were executed** (ref: *Evasive Action: Recruitment*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Antinnis_Tremayne) ("Recruitment")
  - **Tremayne's background:** given to the Jedi at nineteen months, he became a Padawan at fifteen (no footnote in extract). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Antinnis_Tremayne) ("Jedi Order")
  - **Captured Jedi:** they were to be turned to the dark side, or killed if they would not turn. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Inquisitorius/Legends) ("History")
  - **Ranks:**
    - Grand Inquisitor: the leader of the Inquisitorius. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Inquisitorius/Legends) (infobox). Holders were Malorum (19 BBY), Hydra (18 BBY to c. 4 BBY, briefly replaced by Ferus Olin), Laddinare Torbin (by 4 BBY to c. 3 ABY) and Ja'ce Yiaso. The Grand Inquisitor had the final say on whether a candidate saw the Emperor (page carries a {{Citation}} banner). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grand_Inquisitor/Legends) ("Overview"; "Known Grand Inquisitors")
    - High Inquisitor: a senior rank, attested by 5 BBY (Tremayne, Brandl, Halmere, Mal Sikander, Mox Slosin, Volytar). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/High_Inquisitor) (intro; "Famous High Inquisitors")
    - Inquisitor.
    - Apprentice Inquisitor: wore indigo robes instead of maroon, and usually used lightfoils, though some were allowed lightsabers (ref: *The Last Jedi* [2013 Legends novel]; *Coruscant Nights II*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Inquisitorius/Legends) ("Equipment")
  - **Apprentices:** Inquisitors could train apprentices, such as Probus Tesla with Renefra Ren. They could also train Dark Jedi outside the Inquisition, such as Misha Vekkian (ref: *The Last Jedi*; *Dawn of Defiance* "A Reckoning of Wraiths"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Inquisitorius/Legends) ("Operations")

  - **Duties:** they interrogated subjects Imperial Intelligence could not break. More often they scouted for Force-sensitive candidates in the Outer Rim at the Grand Inquisitor's direction. Bases were the Jedi Temple, then the Senate Tower, then the Citadel Inquisitorius on Prakith, where captured Jedi were tortured. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Inquisitorius/Legends) ("Operations")
  - **Failed apprentices:** after the Emperor's last death, surviving Inquisitors sent "one of their failed apprentices", Brakiss, to infiltrate Skywalker's academy. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Inquisitorius/Legends) ("History")
- **Related:** Era 9 (Brakiss, Jerec)
- **Uncertainty:** [conflict] Who recruited Inquisitors?

  - Version B: Vader choosing directly on Byss.

### The Emperor's Hands (non-Sith)
- **Type:** operatives
- **Era:** before 19 BBY to 4 ABY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Origin of the idea:** Maul, "trained specifically to kill Jedi", convinced Palpatine of the value of loyal secret agents. So he developed the Hands "as operatives, not apprentices" (ref: *Dark Side Sourcebook*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Emperor%27s_Hand/Legends) ("History")
  - **Selection:** candidates were often marked in infancy and watched until maturity. Palpatine preferred to begin training once they had developed and waited for them to impress him, as thieves, warriors, assassins or entertainers (ref: *Dark Side Sourcebook*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Emperor%27s_Hand/Legends) ("Selection")
  - **Training:** comprehensive combat, intelligence-gathering and assassination. Each Hand often believed they were *the* only Hand (ref: *Children of the Jedi*). Palpatine cultivated latent Force abilities but avoided giving Jedi or Sith training. The key ability was a telepathic link to him (ref: *By the Emperor's Hand*). Mara Jade recalled training herself without the Force in running, climbing, silent movement, swimming and zero-g fighting (ref: *NJO: Onslaught*, quote). Sometimes Palpatine handed a Hand's training to the Prophets (ref: *Insider* 88). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Emperor%27s_Hand/Legends) ("Training")
  - **Numbers:** the exact number is unknown (ref: *Dark Side Sourcebook*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Emperor%27s_Hand/Legends) ("History")
  - **Mara Jade:** taken from her parents "at a very young age" and trained in the Force on Coruscant (ref: *The Last Command*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mara_Jade_Skywalker) ("A new life")
- **Related:** Lumiya
- **Uncertainty:** [conflict] When did training start?
  - Version A: after the candidate had developed (*Dark Side Sourcebook*).
  - Version B: Mara Jade was taken "at a very young age" (*The Last Command*).

  Best fit: the general rule plus a named exception. Mara is the best-documented Hand.

### The Emperor's Royal Guard and the Yinchorr academy (non-Force, with a Force-trained elite)

### Vader's secret apprentices
- **Type:** master–apprentice (outside the Rule of Two)
- **Era:** c. 19 BBY–2 BBY (Marek); Galactic Civil War (Lumiya)
- **Key facts:**
  - **Why:** Vader believed his injuries left him unable to overthrow Sidious alone, so he trained a secret apprentice, just as Sidious had trained Maul before becoming Master. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rule_of_Two/Legends) ("Imperial Period")

He was once dropped unarmed on Honoghr and hunted by Noghri commandos (ref: StarWars.com "Imperial Warlords"). Vader taught only the basics, then made him develop his own methods "through sparring matches and brutal tests". The training droid PROXY imitated Jedi and attacked him "with lethal intent" (ref: *TFU* novelization). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galen_Marek) ("Training Under Vader")

  - **Lumiya:** Shira Brie was recommended by Vader for Imperial Intelligence training. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lumiya) ("Early life"). After being rebuilt she studied the Sith in secret as Vader's apprentice while also serving as an Emperor's Hand. As the Rule of Two barred Vader from an official apprentice, she was not formally one (ref: *LotF: Sacrifice*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lumiya) (intro; "'Hands'")

## Era 9: The New Republic and New Jedi Order eras (4–c. 43 ABY)

**Era summary:** After Endor, dark-side training broke up into many competing groups, most of them Dark Jedi rather than Sith. Examples:
- The reborn Emperor's seven-member Dark Side Elite (non-Sith).
- Jerec's seven Dark Jedi.
- Brakiss's Shadow Academy for the Second Imperium, with Nightsister instructors.
- Desann's artificially empowered Reborn.
- Tavion's Disciples of Ragnos.

True Sith training resumed with Lumiya, who styled herself Dark Lady of the Sith and made Jacen Solo Darth Caedus through a doctrine of sacrifice. The Lost Tribe of Sith on Kesh, isolated since 5000 BBY, kept a full academy-style ladder from Tyro to Grand Lord.

### The Dark Empire and the Dark Side Elite (non-Sith)
- **Type:** elite unit
- **Era:** c. 10–11 ABY
- **Key facts:**
  - **The Elite:** reborn on Byss in clone bodies, Palpatine hand-selected a body of **seven** Dark Jedi, the Dark Side Elite or "Emperor's Dark Jedi", to lead Operation Shadow Hand. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Side_Elite) (intro; "Origins")
  - **Source of the number:** in his *Dark Side Compendium*, Palpatine had imagined a team of seven Dark Jedi immersed in the dark side and guided by his call (ref: Wizards "Dark Forces Saga" Part 3). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Side_Elite) ("Origins")
  - **Losses replaced:** when one member was lost, they recruited another to restore the number to seven. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Side_Elite) ("Operation Shadow Hand")
  - **Purge of the weak:** after many failures the survivors withdrew to Bast Castle on Vjun. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Side_Elite) (intro)
  - **Adepts:** Dark Side Adepts in administrative posts grew more important after the clone resurrection (ref: *Dark Empire Sourcebook*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_adept/Legends) ("History")
- **Related:** Era 8 (adepts)
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Jerec and his Seven Dark Jedi (non-Sith)
- **Type:** master and disciples
- **Era:** c. 5 ABY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Background:** Jerec, a Jedi archaeologist apprenticed to Jocasta Nu, trained his own Padawan Ameesa Darys to knighthood and so became a Master (ref: Wizards "Dark Forces Saga" Part 6). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jerec_%28Inquisitor%29) ("Jedi career")
  - **Under the Empire:** as an Inquisitor he sought out Jedi and turned many of them. As a High Inquisitor he was allowed to study Sith lore in exchange for loyalty. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jerec_%28Inquisitor%29) (intro; "Inquisitor")
  - **The Seven:** after Endor he formed a band of seven Dark Jedi, an idea of "seven Dark Jedi melded to one will", to find the Valley of the Jedi. Members included Boc, Sariss and her apprentice Yun. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jerec_%28Inquisitor%29) (intro; image caption)
  - **Brothers of the Sith:** the Emperor's Hand Cronal mutated Gorc and Pic into the "Brothers of the Sith", who became Jerec's Dark Jedi apprentices. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Prophets_of_the_Dark_Side) ("Late Galactic Civil War and the Church of the Dark Side")
- **Related:** Era 8 (Inquisitorius)
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Brakiss, the Shadow Academy and the Second Imperium (non-Sith Dark Jedi)
- **Type:** academy
- **Era:** 23–24 ABY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Brakiss's background:** recruited as a baby, he was "trained and brainwashed by the Inquisitorius". In 11 ABY he was planted as a spy in Skywalker's first class (ref: *New Essential Guide to Characters*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Brakiss) (intro)
  - **The academy:** a cloaked, torus-shaped battle station, founded by Brakiss in 23 ABY to train Dark Jedi for the Second Imperium. It was rigged with thousands of explosives so the Great Leader could punish Brakiss if he failed or rebelled. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_Academy) (intro; "Characteristics"; "Interior")
  - **Recruitment:** with exiled Nightsisters under Tamith Kai, Brakiss recruited youths. **Force-sensitives became Dark Jedi; the rest became stormtroopers and TIE pilots.** Methods included kidnapping Jedi trainees (the Solo twins and Lowbacca, who resisted indoctrination) and promising Coruscant's underclass, such as Zekk and the Lost Ones, a better life. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_Academy) ("History")
  - **Instructors:** Tamith Kai of the Great Canyon Clan was assistant instructor, "responsible for recruiting, combat training and discipline". [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tamith_Kai) ("Biography")
  - **Daily regime:** students lived in bare, cube-shaped rooms and were confined there for set rest or meditation periods. This possibly reflected Tamith Kai's belief in adversity and deprivation. Dozens of students sat on backless benches for Brakiss's lectures. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_Academy) ("Students' Quarters"; "Meeting Room")
  - **Lethal drill:** blindfolded students with wooden sticks faced projectiles. A fellow student controlled remotes to shoot them down. The three levels hurled balls, then rocks, then knives. **The drill killed at least six students.** A holo-grid chamber projected holo-remotes and holoshrouds. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_Academy) ("Training Chambers")
  - **Darkest Knight:** Brakiss groomed Zekk, who became the academy's "Darkest Knight" after killing Vilas (ref: *YJK: Darkest Knight*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_Academy) ("History"); [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Brakiss) ("Continued recruiting")
  - **Guards:** four stormtroopers promoted to Royal Guardsmen by Daala formed the Shadow Academy and used Brakiss to train an army. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Emperor%27s_Royal_Guard/Legends) ("Legacy")
- **Related:** Nightsisters (non-Sith)
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Desann's Reborn and Tavion's Disciples of Ragnos (non-Sith; a Sith cult)

- **Era:** 12–14 ABY
- **Key facts:**

He quadrupled their power at the Force nexus of the Valley of the Jedi. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Reborn) (intro; "Desann's Reborn")
  - **Style:** they fought with red lightsabers, "well-trained in the Fast and Medium styles", with a strong Force push. Katarn judged their power "warped" (ref: *Jedi Outcast*, game stats). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Reborn) ("Desann's Reborn")
  - **The Disciples:** a Sith cult formed from the Empire Reborn that worshipped Marka Ragnos, led by Desann's former apprentice Tavion Axmis. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Disciples_of_Ragnos) (intro; "Origins")

They wore battle suits coloured by ability. Surviving Reborn became the New Reborn, superior in skill. The secretive elite were the **Reborn Masters** (ref: *Jedi Academy*, game). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Disciples_of_Ragnos) ("Sith Cultist"; "New Reborn"; "Elite Members")
  - **Mission:** they siphoned residual energies from dark- and light-side sites, such as the Massassi temples and Dagobah's cave. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Disciples_of_Ragnos) ("Rise of the Disciples")
- **Related:** Era 1 (Ragnos)

### Lumiya and Darth Caedus
- **Type:** master–apprentice (Sith)
- **Era:** c. 40–41 ABY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Lumiya's title:** a former Hand and secret apprentice of Vader, she became "Mistress of the Sith" as the foremost of their pupils despite incomplete training. - **Uncertainty:** none

### The Lost Tribe of Sith on Kesh
- **Type:** Sith society with an academy ladder
- **Era:** 5000 BBY to 43 ABY (active in the galaxy from 41 ABY)
- **Key facts:**
  - **Origin:** survivors of Naga Sadow's warship *Omen* crashed on Kesh in 5000 BBY and dominated the native Keshiri. Yaru Korsin became Grand Lord, and the title passed to his daughter Nida. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lost_Tribe_of_Sith) (intro)
  - **Circle of Lords:** Korsin created the Circle of Lords when the Tribe numbered nearly six hundred Sith. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lost_Tribe_of_Sith) ("Korsins' legacy"). By 41 ABY it had thirteen Sith Lords, seven High Lords and one Grand Lord (ref: *FotJ: Omen*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lost_Tribe_of_Sith) ("Organization")
  - **Rank ladder (lowest to highest):** Sith Tyro, Sith apprentice, Sith Saber, Sith Master, Sith Lord, High Lord, Grand Consort, Grand Lord (ref: *FotJ: Omen*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lost_Tribe_of_Sith) ("Organization")
  - **Tyro:** a student of Sith teachings not yet chosen as a formal apprentice, roughly a Jedi Initiate (ref: *Omen*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Tyro) (intro)
  - **Saber:** apprentices became Sabers after completing formal training under a Master, roughly a Jedi Knight. Sabers could customise their robes and own possessions, which apprentices could not. Under Grand Lord Hilts, Sabers served as military and security forces and Keshiri could enlist. Vestara Khai was promoted to Saber in battle (no footnotes on the page). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Saber) (intro)
  - **Where:** apprentices were mentored and trained in the Sith Temple, built from the mountain over the wreck of the *Omen*. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lost_Tribe_of_Sith) ("Organization")
  - **Merit and flaws:** any flaw, including scars and deformities, counted against advancement. The Tribe believed in merit over birth. Some Keshiri were trained, and one sat on the Circle by 41 ABY (ref: *FotJ: Omen*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lost_Tribe_of_Sith) ("Organization")
  - **Sabotaged training:** Seelah kept her daughter Nida "not properly trained" and away from the capital, so she could not rival her half-brother. Korsin secretly prepared Nida instead (ref: *Lost Tribe: Savior*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lost_Tribe_of_Sith) ("Korsins' legacy")
  - **No Rule of Two:** Cilghal joked that they followed a "rule of However Many They Need" (ref: *FotJ: Backlash*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lost_Tribe_of_Sith) ("Organization")
- **Related:** Era 1 (Seelah, Kressh's slave)
- **Uncertainty:** none

## Era 10: The Legacy era, Darth Krayt's One Sith (c. 25–138 ABY)

**Era summary:** A'Sharad Hett, a fallen Jedi, refounded the Sith on Korriban as Darth Krayt and replaced the Rule of Two with the Rule of One: many Sith, one Dark Lord, and blind obedience. The One Sith trained acolytes from birth at a Sith academy on Korriban. Each Master trained one apprentice, and the apprentice's final test was to kill the Master on Krayt's order. Above the ordinary Lords sat named offices: Hand, Fist, Voice/Regent, Loremaster and Inquisitor.

### Founding and the Rule of One
- **Type:** doctrine and sect
- **Era:** before 27 ABY to 138 ABY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Krayt's turn:** held by the Yuuzhan Vong in the Embrace of Pain, Hett was taught by Vergere to use agony to open himself to the dark side, and had his vision of a new Sith Order there. He then escaped and went back to Korriban (ref: *Legacy* (2006) 18). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Krayt) ("The rise of Darth Krayt (c. 25 ABY–130 ABY)")
  - **Other teacher:** the One Sith page says Hett learned the Sith ways from XoXaan's holocron on Korriban. That intro sentence has no inline footnote. [unclear] [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/One_Sith) (intro)
  - **Founding:** founded on Korriban before 27 ABY. Krayt took his name from the krayt dragon (ref: *Ultimate Visual Guide: Special Edition*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/One_Sith) (intro); [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Krayt) ("The rise of Darth Krayt (c. 25 ABY–130 ABY)")
  - **Rule of One:** Krayt abolished the Rule of Two. The "One" is the Order itself: many minions, acolytes/adepts and lords under one Dark Lord. They were taught blind obedience instead of infighting (ref: *Legacy* 0). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rule_of_One) (intro)
  - **Hidden growth:** for more than a hundred years the Order grew in secret on Korriban, hidden by the planet's dark-side miasma. Krayt spent long periods in stasis and spoke only with close disciples such as the first Darth Wyyrlok (ref: *Legacy* (2006) 18). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/One_Sith) ("Observing the Galaxy"); [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Krayt) ("The rise of Darth Krayt (c. 25 ABY–130 ABY)")
  - **Lumiya:** during the Yuuzhan Vong War, Krayt tried to recruit Lumiya, who refused (ref: *Legacy* (2006) 18). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Krayt) ("The rise of Darth Krayt (c. 25 ABY–130 ABY)")
  - **Character:** the Order was heavily organized and bureaucratic. Acolytes were "trained from birth" and taught absolute loyalty, wore red-and-black tattoos, and carried lightsabers with yorik-coral style hilts. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/One_Sith) (intro)
- **Related:** Era 9 (Lumiya, Vergere's link to Caedus); Era 7 (Rule of Two)
- **Uncertainty:** XoXaan's holocron as teacher [unclear]

### Ranks and offices
- **Type:** hierarchy
- **Era:** 130–138 ABY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Darth:** given to members of either sex who had proven both power and loyalty. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/One_Sith) ("Organization")
  - **Sith Lady:** the title was restored for women, for example Lady Maladi, Lady Rauder and Lady Talon. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/One_Sith) ("Organization")
  - **Not every Sith a Darth:** Vul Isen, born into the Order on Korriban, was a scientist rather than a warrior and was never given the title. Darth Azard saw him as a lesser Sith because of it (ref: *Legacy* (2006) 48). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vul_Isen) (intro; "Scientist")
  - **Hand:** Krayt's personal assassins, chosen only from his most loyal and deadliest followers. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/One_Sith) ("Hands")
  - **Hand holders:** Darth Talon. Nihl was made Hand because Talon's training was unfinished when the previous Hand died at Ossus. After Nihl was demoted, Darth Stryfe became Hand. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/One_Sith) ("Hands"); [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Nihl) (intro); [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Stryfe) (intro)
  - **Fist:** supreme commander of the Sith military, held first by Nihl, a former Nagai warlord Krayt recruited, and then by Stryfe. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/One_Sith) ("Fist"); [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Nihl) (intro)
  - **Voice/Regent:** second in command. The Voice/Regent advised the Dark Lord and spoke for him while he was in stasis. The office was held by the Chagrian line of Darth Wyyrloks, with three by the Second Imperial Civil War. After Krayt's apparent death, Wyyrlok made Nihl the Voice. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/One_Sith) ("Voice/Regent")
  - **Duty to question:** part of Wyyrlok III's job was to question Krayt's decisions (ref: *Legacy* (2006) 18). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Wyyrlok_%28III%29) ("Indoctrinating Skywalker")
  - **Loremaster:** kept the Order's Sith artifacts and records. Wyyrlok III held this office. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/One_Sith) ("Loremaster")
  - **Inquisitor:** Sith Lords who got information from prisoners, by methods up to torture. Darth Havok was one. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/One_Sith) ("Inquisitor")
  - **Sith Intelligence and Assassination:** run by Darth Maladi, with both Lords (Darth Kruhl) and acolytes (Jor Torlin) as members. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/One_Sith) ("Sith Intelligence and Assassination")
  - **Sith troopers:** strong Force-sensitives secretly picked by Krayt soon after birth and given cybernetic implants to make them unquestioningly loyal. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/One_Sith) ("Troopers")
  - **Citation note:** the One Sith page carries a {{Citation}} banner, and its "Organization" subsections have no inline footnotes. Facts used from it are backed only by the page. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/One_Sith) (page banner)
- **Related:** Era 7 (Darth naming under the Rule of Two)
- **Uncertainty:** the One Sith "Organization" subsections have no footnotes

### The Korriban academy and the master–apprentice path
- **Type:** academy, then a single apprentice per Master
- **Era:** before 27 ABY to 138 ABY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Pattern:** like Jedi Masters, each Sith Master trained a single apprentice. When training was finished, the apprentice had to kill the Master to prove allegiance to the Dark Lord. A Master who judged the apprentice ready let himself be killed, if Krayt ordered it (ref: *Legacy* 0). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rule_of_One) (intro)
  - **Talon's upbringing:** a third-generation One Sith, Talon was raised and trained at the Sith Academy on Korriban. Under Darth Ruyn her powers were shaped to make her a Sith assassin, and her mind was moulded to accept One Sith teaching completely (ref: *Legacy* (2006) 2). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Talon) ("Training")
  - **Final test:** near the end of her training Krayt ordered Talon to kill Ruyn. She did so without hesitation, and with that final rite she was made a Hand (ref: *Legacy* (2006) 2). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Talon) ("Training")
  - **Ruyn's side:** an aged Twi'lek Lord who trained several apprentices, Talon the last. He made no attempt to block her decapitating strike in 137 ABY. He often took her to meditate in the Sith Art Chamber of the Temple of the Sith on Coruscant (ref: *Legacy* (2006) 2, 17). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Ruyn) (intro; "Biography")
  - **Tattoos:** Talon's black tattoos were each earned in ritual combat and inked by Krayt himself. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Talon) (intro)
  - **Children sent to Korriban:** Wyyrlok III sent his daughter to Korriban for Sith training (ref: *Legacy* (2006) 14). The One Sith page names her as Saarai, fourth in the Wyyrlok line. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Wyyrlok_%28III%29) ("Servitude"); [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/One_Sith) ("Voice/Regent")
  - **Born members:** Vul Isen was one of many Sith born on Korriban and inducted into the Order. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vul_Isen) ("Scientist")
  - **Krayt's tomb:** Wyyrlok III put Krayt's body in a stasis chamber in XoXaan's Temple on Korriban. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Wyyrlok_%28III%29) (intro)
- **Related:** Era 7 (killing the Master as succession, by contrast)
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Captured recruits: Cade Skywalker and Darth Wredd
- **Type:** adult recruits
- **Era:** 137–138 ABY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Cade:** captured in the Temple of the Sith on Coruscant (the former Jedi Temple, taken in 130 ABY) and tortured by Maladi, Cade agreed to learn the Sith ways. Krayt named the healed Talon as his teacher. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Temple_of_the_Sith) ("History")
  - **Faster by pain:** Krayt sped up Cade's training by putting him in an Embrace of Pain. When Cade lacked focus, he was left there (ref: *Legacy* (2006) 18). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Wyyrlok_%28III%29) ("Indoctrinating Skywalker")
  - **Wredd's recruitment:** a warrior from Mala who resisted a One Sith invasion. The commanding Lord spared him for his Force potential and made him his apprentice. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Wredd) ("Sith apprenticeship")
  - **Wredd's training:** the Lord made him watch his homeworld wrecked by Vul Isen's weapon, then made him wear a mask at all times to "ensure his obedience" and teach him hatred. He took away Wredd's identity and gave him the Darth Wredd persona. Wredd spent the apprenticeship aboard his Master's starship and came to hate him (ref: *Legacy* (2013) 16). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Wredd) ("Sith apprenticeship")
  - **Heresy:** Wredd later rejected the One Sith in favour of Bane's Rule of Two (ref: *Legacy* (2013) 4). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Wredd) ("Unexpected obstacles")
- **Related:** Era 7
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Where it happened
Korriban (the Sith Academy, the Sith Temple, XoXaan's Temple) and Coruscant (the Temple of the Sith and its Sith Art Chamber), plus the Master's starship for Wredd. Sources as cited above.

### Not found (Era 10)
- The size of the academy, cohorts, or instructor ratios.
- The age at each stage and how long training took. Only "from birth" is given.
- Training sabers, non-lethal sparring, or crystal practice. The only lethal combat found is the "ritual combat" for tattoos, and its lethality is not stated.
- What happened to failures.
- A curriculum beyond assassin training (Talon), torture (Cade), and lore keeping (the Loremaster).
