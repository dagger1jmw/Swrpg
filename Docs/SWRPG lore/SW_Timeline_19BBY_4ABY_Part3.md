# SWRPG Galaxy Timeline, 19 BBY–4 ABY (Part 3 of 4): Yavin and After, 0 BBY–2 ABY

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 24, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
Part 3 covers 0 BBY to 2 ABY: the theft of the Death Star plans (Darkknell, Danuta, Toprawa, the AX-235 interception), the dissolution of the Imperial Senate, Alderaan and Yavin; the year after Yavin (the evacuation of Yavin 4, the Rebels' wandering bases, Bel Iblis's break with the Alliance, the Marvel and *Empire* comics campaigns, *Allegiance*, *Choices of One*); *Dark Forces* and the end of the Dark Trooper Project (1 ABY); the start of the second Death Star; Lando's winning of Cloud City; the *Executor*'s commissioning, Thrawn's exile; and the building of Echo Base (2 ABY). Wookieepedia splits the Yavin year in two pages: "0 BBY" for events before the battle and "0 ABY" for events after it; this file keeps that split. Format and tags as in Part 1; the closing sections are in Part 4.

### Overview
Wookieepedia dates the Galactic Civil War from the Corellian Treaty in 2 BBY to the Pellaeon–Gavrisom Treaty in 19 ABY; until Alderaan and Yavin the Empire treated the Alliance as a political tool rather than a threat, using it to justify dissolving the Senate and building up its military [Galactic Civil War/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_Civil_War/Legends). Yavin (0 BBY, 35:3:8 GrS) was one of the Rebellion's first major victories [Battle of Yavin/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Yavin/Legends); Hoth (3 ABY) one of its worst ground defeats [Battle of Hoth/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Hoth/Legends). Between them, the Expanded Universe fills the years with the Yavin blockade and evacuation, Kyle Katarn's *Dark Forces* campaign and a run of comics and novels [0 ABY/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/0_ABY/Legends); [1 ABY/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/1_ABY/Legends).
