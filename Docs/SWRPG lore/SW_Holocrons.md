## Summary
A holocron ("holographic chronicle") is a crystal-lattice device that stores knowledge behind a gatekeeper, an artificial personality usually based on its maker. The Sith made them at least 3,000 years before the Jedi, using technology the Rakata gave King Adas. Jedi holocrons are usually cubes and Sith holocrons pyramids. Opening one usually takes the Force (the dark side for Sith holocrons). Once one is open, anyone can talk to the gatekeeper. Jedi gatekeepers withhold material a user isn't ready for. Sith gatekeepers may not, and can corrupt. Making a Sith holocron takes weeks to months and the Rite of Commencement, which traps the maker's mind in the capstone crystal. Only the most powerful Sith Masters can perform it.

## Entries

### Holocrons: how they work
- **Type:** technology (Force device)
- **Key facts:**
  - "Holographic chronicle": an organic crystal-lattice device storing very large amounts of data behind a gatekeeper. Force users stored techniques and instruction in them, the Sith first and the Jedi about 3,000 years later [Holocron/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Holocron/Legends).
  - **Shape:** most are polyhedra small enough to hold in one hand. Jedi holocrons are usually cubes and Sith holocrons pyramids [Holocron/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Holocron/Legends). Exceptions: Quinlan Vos's Jedi holocron was a pyramid [Quinlan Vos's holocron](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Quinlan_Vos%27s_holocron). The Codex of Tython was an icosahedron [Codex of Tython](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Codex_of_Tython). Satele's was two octahedrons joined by a ring [Master Satele's holocron](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Master_Satele%27s_holocron). The Telos Holocron was an unusual design [Telos Holocron](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telos_Holocron).
  - **Force requirement:** usually only Force-sensitives can open one. Once one is open, non-Force-users can talk to the gatekeeper [Holocron/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Holocron/Legends); [Jedi holocron/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_holocron/Legends).
  - **Activation crystal:** some open only when taken apart with the Force and fitted with a special crystal, which the maker usually hid [Holocron/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Holocron/Legends).
  - **Storage (Jedi):** in the Republic's last era, Jedi holocrons were kept in the Holocron Vault of the Temple Archives, open only to the High Council [Jedi holocron/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_holocron/Legends). The Holocron Chamber is in the Temple rebuilt after 3653 BBY [Holocron Chamber, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/holocron_chamber).
- **Uncertainty:** the main Holocron/Legends page is flagged as needing citations.

### Gatekeepers
- **Type:** technology (part)
- **Key facts:**
  - The artificial personality that grants access to a holocron's contents. It's usually based on the maker, or on a later owner who added their own knowledge. Sith gatekeepers were sometimes the actual minds of their makers. Famous Jedi gatekeepers include Bodo Baas and, later, Luke Skywalker [Gatekeeper/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gatekeeper/Legends).
  - Jedi gatekeepers sense a user's ability and limit what they show. Sith gatekeepers have similar limits but can corrupt an unwary listener [Holocron/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Holocron/Legends), and don't always withhold dangerous knowledge [Sith holocron/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_holocron/Legends).
  - **Conditions:** the Mournful Holocron only opened once the user had comforted its grieving gatekeeper [Mournful Holocron](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mournful_Holocron). Sar Agorn's gatekeeper only released techniques to students it judged responsible [Sar Agorn's holocron](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sar_Agorn%27s_holocron).
  - **Prisons:** Tenebrae (Vitiate) imprisoned his father Dramath's spirit in a holocron [Dramath's holocron](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dramath%27s_holocron). Bo Vanda's holocron held Komok-Da's spirit alongside its gatekeeper, the Sith Lord Kla [Bo Vanda's holocron](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bo_Vanda%27s_holocron).
- **Uncertainty:** none.

### Sith holocrons: making them
- **Type:** technology (dark side)
- **Key facts:**
  - They usually need the dark side to activate. They're technologically the same as Jedi holocrons but with dark side refinements. They're usually pyramids with a black crystal at the apex. Activating one sends tremors through the Force, and some inexperienced Jedi grew ill just looking at one [Sith holocron/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_holocron/Legends).
  - **The process:** rare materials, intense meditation on the dark side, and a crystal matrix tuned at the subatomic level, unique to the maker. It takes months, sometimes weeks. The Rituals of Invocation charge the symbols with dark side power [Sith holocron/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_holocron/Legends).
  - **Capstone:** the inner crystal, both the power source and the vessel for the maker's gatekeeper essence [Capstone](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Capstone).
  - **Rite of Commencement:** a powerful Sith ritual that traps the maker's mind in the capstone, after which the holocron can be finished [Rite of Commencement](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rite_of_Commencement). Only the most powerful Sith Masters could perform it [Sith holocron/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_holocron/Legends).
  - **Jedi policy:** before Geonosis, most surviving Sith holocrons were locked in the Jedi Archives for senior Jedi only [Sith holocron/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_holocron/Legends).
- **Uncertainty:** when the knowledge of how to make them was lost [conflict]. The same page says it survived until the Great Jedi Purge, and also that it was "considered lost" by 1000 BBY.

### Variants
- **Type:** technology
- **Key facts:**
  - **Datacron (data holocron):** accessible without the Force, used mainly for encrypted data. Revan and Malak hid one on Rekkiad with Mandalore's Mask [Datacron/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Datacron/Legends).
  - **Vitalicron:** an advanced holocron-style device used by the Voss by the Cold War [Vitalicron/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vitalicron/Legends).
  - **Noetikon:** Jedi holocrons made to search the Temple Archives. Tharis Orne designed their workings, and Arca Jeth built them [Noetikon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Noetikon).
  - **Fusion:** joint light and dark holocrons were very rare, and the method was mostly lost by the Galactic Civil War [Holocron (search-result text)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Holocron) [unclear]: that text didn't clearly mark itself as the Legends page.
- **Uncertainty:** as tagged.
