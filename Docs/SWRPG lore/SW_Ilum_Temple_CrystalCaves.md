## Summary
The Jedi Temple on Ilum stands at the largest entrance to the Crystal Caves, a deep cave network that is itself a Force nexus and the Order's main source of lightsaber crystals. Its route was kept secret from the start. Jedi came to build their lightsabers, a trek that tested the student, with visions in the caves. In the Old Republic era a small garrison and honour guard kept it. The resurgent Sith Empire killed the Jedi there and held it for over a decade, until the Battle of Ilum in 3640 BBY.

## Entries

### The Jedi Temple (Ilum Temple)
- **Type:** location (Jedi temple)
- **Era:** c. 22,800 BBY onward
- **Key facts:**
  - **Founding:** early Jedi scouts discovered Ilum and its crystal caves around 22,800 BBY. The High Council chose the site for a temple and training centre and kept its coordinates and hyperlane secret [Ilum Temple, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/Ilum_Temple).
  - **SWTOR codex:** the ancient Jedi built the temple above the crystal caverns as a home and training ground. Its isolation and crystals made it ideal for schooling Padawans, despite the climate. To protect Ilum from the darkness that had threatened them on Tython, they kept the hyperspace routes secret from the rest of the Republic [SWTOR codex, "The Ilum Temple"](https://swtor.jedipedia.net/en/cdx/the-ilum-temple).
  - **Layout:** built into a frozen mountain cliff, with a large entrance hidden behind ice. The main tower has circular chambers, pillars and statues of Jedi. Windows that open with the Force let light pass through suspended crystals, which can melt the entrance ice [Ilum Temple, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/Ilum_Temple).
  - **Deeper levels:** passages lead down to caves with black stone walls, etchings left by visiting Jedi, and a hidden entrance to underground grottoes [Ilum Temple, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/Ilum_Temple).
  - **Purpose:** the temple fortifies the cave entrance and gives visiting Jedi a place to rest and recover from the harsh climate outside [Jedi Temple (Ilum), archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/Jedi_Temple_(Ilum)).
  - **SWTOR holonet:** the Order built small enclaves and discreet temples to shelter Jedi making the long journey. Ilum became a rite of passage for Jedi building new lightsabers, and a quiet refuge where respected Masters came for intense meditation [SWTOR holonet, Ilum](https://www.swtor.com/holonet/planets/ilum).
- **Related:** the Crystal Caves (below).
- **Uncertainty:** the Jedi Temple (Ilum) archivum page mixes in Canon material (Starkiller Base, 34 ABY). Only Legends and SWTOR facts are used here.

### The Crystal Caves
- **Type:** location (site-level Force nexus)
- **Era:** c. 22,800 BBY onward
- **Key facts:**
  - **Extent:** a labyrinth of tunnels, abysses and narrow passages under Ilum's largest mountain range, possibly tens of thousands of kilometres long. The Jedi mapped only the parts near the entrance [Crystal Caves, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/Crystal_Caves).
  - **Material:** glossy black volcanic stone that absorbs light. Adegan crystals (kathracite, mephite and pontite) grow on the walls, floor and ceiling and can be taken by hand or with simple tools [Crystal Caves, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/Crystal_Caves).
  - **The nexus:** the caves are a vergence, and their intense Force presence disorients most visitors. Many have hallucinations, and some Jedi have been permanently lost [Crystal Caves, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/Crystal_Caves).
  - **Visions:** the Force-rich grottoes bring on visions or hallucinations during lightsaber construction [Ilum Temple, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/Ilum_Temple).
  - **Crystal selection:** a young Padawan seeking a crystal sees only the one that resonates with them; the others are hidden [Crystal Caves, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/Crystal_Caves).
  - **The trek as a test:** the journey to Ilum requires Force-sensitivity and serves as an initial test for students [Ilum Temple, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/Ilum_Temple).
- **Related:** SW_Force_Nexus_Vergence (Ilum: the Crystal Caves); SW_Lightsabers_Crystals_Materials_AllEras (Adegan crystals).
- **Uncertainty:** none.

### The Gathering
- **Type:** ritual
- **Era:** documented for the Clone Wars (20 BBY); the practice is described as long-standing
- **Key facts:**
  - Jedi Initiates are brought to the caves to find their own crystals in a ritual called the Gathering [Crystal Caves, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/Crystal_Caves).
  - The ritual is held at the Ilum Temple [Jedi Temple (Ilum), archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/Jedi_Temple_(Ilum)).
- **Related:** SW_Jedi_Daily_Life_Training_OldRepublic (Padawans and Masters going to Ilum's caves).
- **Uncertainty:** when the Gathering, in its Clone Wars form for Initiates, begins isn't stated. In SWTOR-era sources, Ilum is described as a trek for Jedi building lightsabers (the SWTOR holonet above) [unclear].
