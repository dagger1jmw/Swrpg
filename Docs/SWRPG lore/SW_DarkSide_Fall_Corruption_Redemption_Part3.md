# Dark Side: Fall, Corruption and Redemption, Part 3 of 3 (4 ABY onward)

## Summary
This part covers the New Republic, New Jedi Order, Legacy of the Force, Fate of the Jedi and Legacy eras (4–138 ABY). Its cases are Luke on Byss, Joruus C'baoth, Kyle Katarn, Corran Horn, Kyp Durron, Jacen Solo/Darth Caedus, Tahiri Veila and Cade Skywalker. "Force psychosis" does appear in Legends (*Fate of the Jedi*, c. 43 ABY), but as a mental illness caused by the entity Abeloth, not by the victims' own dark-side use. Redemptions in this era almost always leave lasting guilt or a lingering shadow.

**Era boundaries:** Part 1 runs to 1000 BBY. Part 2 runs 1000 BBY–4 ABY. Part 3 runs from 4 ABY onward. Each part keeps all eight sections.

**Cross-references:** SW_Sith_Training_AllEras_Part2.md (Eras 9–10: Jerec's Dark Jedi, Lumiya and Caedus, the One Sith and Cade); SW_Jedi_Training_AllEras_Part2.md; SW_Midichlorians_Force_Potential.md.

## Section 1: How falling works (4 ABY onward)

### Voices from the New Republic to the Legacy era
- **Type:** in-universe views, compared
- **Era:** 5–137 ABY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Kyle Katarn (5 ABY):** "it doesn't matter how far down you follow the dark path once you have returned from it" (ref: *Dark Forces: Jedi Knight*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kyle_Katarn) ("Bringing of justice")
  - **Corran Horn (11 ABY):** he accused Luke of seeing the dark side "too broadly", ignoring minor evils and everyday temptations. He knew his own excuse for an undercover affair was "a justification". [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Corran_Horn/Legends) ("Charting a new course"; "Undercover")
  - **Rules as pressure:** some, like Kyp Durron and Kueller, fell because they found the Jedi's rules too limiting. Others, like Caedus and Revan, accepted the dark side as a necessary evil. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("Allure of the dark side")
  - **The dark side came first:** for some it was the first thing they were taught: Talon, Irek Ismaren, Vestara, Starkiller and Mara Jade. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("Allure of the dark side")
  - **Rejecting the pull:** Jaina Solo, like Kenobi, showed that temptation after a loved one's death can be rejected. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("Allure of the dark side")
  - **Addiction as a lever (40 ABY):** Caedus hooked Tahiri on flow-walking visits to Anakin Solo's last moments and kept her "never fully satisfied" (ref: *LotF: Inferno*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tahiri_Veila) ("On the path to the dark side")
  - **Living on the line (137 ABY):** Cade Skywalker walked "a fragile line between the light and dark sides". [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cade_Skywalker) ("Personality and traits")
- **Related:** Part 1, Section 1 (the Four Stages)
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Single act or pattern: the later evidence
- **Type:** synthesis
- **Era:** 4–138 ABY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Fast falls under a strong outside force:** Kyp (days to weeks under Exar Kun's spirit, 11 ABY) and Luke on Byss (within the *Dark Empire* arc). See Section 2.
  - **Slow falls:** Jacen took about 13 years from Vergere's ordeal to full Sith mastery, and Cade's struggle lasted about seven years (Section 2).
  - **Single acts as tipping points:** Krayt meant his order to kill Talon to "tip Cade over to the dark side permanently". [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cade_Skywalker) ("Duel in the Temple of the Sith")
- **Related:** Game notes
- **Uncertainty:** the durations above are inferred from page sequences [inferred]

## Section 2: Case studies of falls (4 ABY onward)

### Luke Skywalker: Endor and Byss
- **Type:** brush with the dark side (Endor); fall and return (Byss)
- **Era:** 4 ABY; 10–11 ABY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Endor, the trigger:** Luke held his emotions in check until Vader sensed Leia and threatened to corrupt her. Then, furious, he "viciously attacked" with the full power of Djem So and cut off Vader's hand (ref: Film VI). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Luke_Skywalker/Legends) ("The test of Endor")
  - **Endor, the turn back:** when Palpatine told him to finish Vader, he threw away his lightsaber, "realizing that he was becoming what he sought to destroy" (ref: Film VI). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Luke_Skywalker/Legends) ("The test of Endor")
  - **Endor, no marks:** he showed none of the dark side's physical effects during this brief bout of anger. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("Corruption of the dark side")
  - **Byss, outside pressure (10 ABY):** a Force storm brought him to the reborn Emperor on Byss. Realizing Palpatine was all-powerful, he knelt and pledged to learn the Sith ways (ref: *Dark Empire* 1–2). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Luke_Skywalker/Legends) ("Return of the Emperor")
  - **Byss, the reasoning:** he believed he could beat the dark side from inside. He "underestimated the power of the dark side" and, like Quinlan Vos, could not break free once immersed. He sabotaged some plans but "was too deep in darkness to think clearly". Troops called him Lord Skywalker (ref: *Dark Empire* 4). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Luke_Skywalker/Legends) ("Return of the Emperor")
  - **Byss as a site:** the planet was itself a dark-side nexus created by Sidious's clones and alchemy. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) (section "Manifestations of the dark side"; ref *Yoda: Dark Rendezvous*)
  - **Return:** Leia's light-side presence let him "partially shake off the dark side". He sabotaged the cloning tanks, lost a duel, and with Leia's help "fight back the darkness" and turned on his master. Their Force harmony broke Palpatine's storm (ref: *Dark Empire* 5–6). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Luke_Skywalker/Legends) ("Return of the Emperor")
  - **Time:** fall and return both happened within the *Dark Empire* arc, 10–11 ABY. Months at most [inferred].
  - **Lasting hold:** "the shadow of the dark side continued to haunt" him. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Luke_Skywalker/Legends) ("Return of the Emperor")
  - **Tolaris Shim's judgment:** her holorecording judged that Luke, like Anakin and Kyp, could not be redeemed, because he had killed innocents under the dark side (ref: *Jedi vs. Sith*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("Stage 4: Redemption/Atonement")
- **Related:** Section 7 (Byss); Sith all-eras Part2, Era 9
- **Uncertainty:** duration [inferred]; no physical corruption on Byss is recorded (Not found)

### Kyle Katarn
- **Type:** brushes with the dark side, then a short fall and return
- **Era:** 5 ABY (Valley); Operation Shadow Hand (Dromund Kaas); later, the Desann crisis
- **Key facts:**
  - **Inner pressure:** he knew he was driven "by a lust for revenge and hatred" against his father's killers, and he feared the dark side and distrusted his own motives (ref: Wizards, "Dark Forces Saga" Part 1). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kyle_Katarn) ("Personality and traits")
  - **Jerec's test (5 ABY):** Jerec offered him a place if he struck down Jan Ors. Katarn "felt the temptation for ambition, to the point of hesitating", then refused, disgusted with himself (ref: *Dark Forces II*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kyle_Katarn) ("Choosing sides")
  - **Dark acts:** he called on the dark side twice. He killed Maw in anger, and struck Boc with the "spear of midnight black" technique (ref: *Dark Forces II*; Wizards Part 1). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kyle_Katarn) ("Bringing of justice"; "Personality and traits")
  - **His conclusion:** "it doesn't matter how far down you follow the dark path once you have returned from it" (ref: *Dark Forces: Jedi Knight*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kyle_Katarn) ("Bringing of justice")
  - **The valley fight:** facing Jerec in the Valley he refused to feed his hatred, gave the disarmed Jerec his saber back, and killed him only when Jerec attacked. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kyle_Katarn) ("Bringing of justice")
  - **What held him:** Rahn's guidance, his love for Jan, and his father's memory kept him from succumbing (ref: Wizards Part 1). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kyle_Katarn) ("Personality and traits")
  - **The fall (Dromund Kaas):** investigating the Sith ruins on Dromund Kaas, a dark-side site, he "came under the influence of the dark side". His pupil Mara Jade dueled him and then lowered her guard. He stopped his killing blow in remorse and returned to the light, "for the time" (ref: *Mysteries of the Sith*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kyle_Katarn) ("Fall to the dark side")
  - **Time:** the length of the Dromund Kaas fall is not stated (Not found).
  - **Later brush:** angered by Jan's apparent death, he regained his Force powers at the Valley, "once again treading dangerously close to the dark side". [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kyle_Katarn) ("Returning to the Valley of the Jedi and reobtaining his lightsaber")
  - **Lasting change:** in the end, hope replaced hatred and compassion replaced revenge. He openly shared his dark-side experiences with apprentices such as Rosh Penin and Jaden Korr. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kyle_Katarn) ("Personality and traits")
  - **Body:** no physical corruption appeared during either dark-side episode (ref: *Jedi Outcast*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("Corruption of the dark side")
- **Related:** Section 8 (gray Jedi)
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Corran Horn
- **Type:** resisted temptation (no fall)
- **Era:** 11 ABY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Pressure:** with his wife Mirax kidnapped in 11 ABY, he trained as a Jedi to find her. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Corran_Horn/Legends) (intro; "Losing Mirax")
  - **A warning:** Luke cautioned that his talent for altering perceptions could bring him close to the dark side (ref: *I, Jedi*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Corran_Horn/Legends) ("Becoming a Jedi")
  - **Exar Kun's offer:** Kun's spirit offered him the power to destroy the Invid pirates and a vision of where Mirax was. Later Kun showed him an illusion of Mirax and his father rebuking him, "nearly driving him to use his despair, rage, and self-doubt against Kun". He resisted (ref: *I, Jedi*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Corran_Horn/Legends) ("Facing Exar Kun")
  - **Disagreement with Luke:** he accused Luke of seeing the dark side "too broadly", ignoring minor evils and everyday temptations. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Corran_Horn/Legends) ("Charting a new course")
  - **Undercover:** he recognized that sleeping with Leonia Tavira to find Mirax was only "a justification to allow him to do what he wanted" but knew was wrong. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Corran_Horn/Legends) ("Undercover")
  - **Gantoris:** a fellow student, he burned "from the inside out". Luke believed Gantoris had "unleashed dark side power beyond his control and been consumed". [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Corran_Horn/Legends) ("Murder at the Jedi Praxeum")
  - **The Jensaarai:** he met the Jensaarai, who had not learned enough Sith ways to be devoted to the dark side and saw themselves as heirs to the true Jedi (see Section 8). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Corran_Horn/Legends) ("Rescue")
- **Related:** Kyp Durron; Exar Kun
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Kyp Durron
- **Type:** fall and redemption case
- **Era:** 11 ABY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Background:** imprisoned young in the Kessel spice mines, his deep anger at his captors "was easily exploited" by Exar Kun's spirit. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kyp_Durron) (intro)
  - **Step 1, a spirit mentor:** while he meditated in the Great Temple on Yavin 4, Kun appeared and offered to unlock his full potential beyond Luke's "restrictive teachings" (ref: *Dark Apprentice*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kyp_Durron) ("Corruption")
  - **Step 2, a dark-side site:** boosted by Kun and the Sith energies of the Massassi temples, he pulled the Sun Crusher out of Yavin's core, struck Luke with black Force lightning and left him comatose. He had already wiped Qwi Xux's memory with the Force (ref: *Dark Apprentice*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kyp_Durron) ("Legacy of the Sith")
  - **Step 3, mass killing:** he destroyed the Cauldron Nebula's stars to kill Daala. Days later he set off the Carida star while trying to rescue his brother Zeth, who died in the blast. Grieving, with Kun "fueling his anger and rage", he destroyed another Imperial system (ref: *Champions of the Force*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kyp_Durron) ("Vengeful rampage")
  - **Time to fall:** days to weeks. Kun's first visit, the attack on Luke and the rampage all happened in 11 ABY [inferred from the page sequence].
  - **Turn back:** Han Solo's appeals to their friendship, and his pointing out the dark side's part in the vendetta, "began to counter" Kun's voice. Kyp was then disabled by a trick signal from Han's ship. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kyp_Durron) ("Vengeful rampage")
  - **Judgment:** ashamed and guilt-ridden, he stood before the New Republic Council. Luke let him continue training after Kun was vanquished. In Kun's tomb, Kyp dropped Sith lightning and faced a shadow with a Jedi weapon instead (ref: *Champions of the Force*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kyp_Durron) ("Judgment")
  - **Cost:** he refused to use his powers and "wished he had never been born with Jedi abilities". [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kyp_Durron) ("Judgment")
  - **Atonement:** he went alone to destroy the Death Star prototype and Daala with the Sun Crusher. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kyp_Durron) ("Righting wrongs")
  - **Hold afterward:** guilt drove his later missions, such as protecting the untrained Ghitsa from the path he had taken. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kyp_Durron) ("Guilt-ridden")
  - **Tolaris Shim's judgment:** her holorecording judged him beyond redemption for killing innocents. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("Stage 4: Redemption/Atonement")
  - **Why he fell:** the dark side page names Kyp among those who found Jedi rules too limiting. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("Allure of the dark side")
- **Related:** Exar Kun
- **Uncertainty:** duration [inferred]

### Jacen Solo / Darth Caedus
- **Type:** fall case (death in 41 ABY, partial return at the end)
- **Era:** c. 28–41 ABY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Step 1, torture and a new philosophy (Yuuzhan Vong War):** captured after Myrkr and grieving his brother Anakin, he was tortured in the Embrace of Pain. Vergere then taught him that there was "no dark side to the Force; only the Force, and the darkness within himself". He argued with her over whether intentions or results mattered, and at one point insisted he had "succumbed to the dark side". [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Caedus) ("Loss and rediscovery")
  - **Step 2, visions:** in 36 ABY he had a vision of a galaxy-consuming war sparked by the Killiks. A vision of a dark figure on a throne drove him toward actions other Jedi found morally questionable "for the sake of... a greater good" (ref: *Dark Nest*; *FotJ: Abyss*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Caedus) ("The Swarm War"; "Darksider")
  - **Step 3, killing on the strength of a vision (40 ABY):** a vision told him that sparing Thrackan Sal-Solo meant war. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Caedus) ("Corellian tensions")
  - **Step 4, a Sith mentor:** on Lorrd, Lumiya argued that he could use Sith teachings for peace without being corrupted. He blocked Nelani Dinn's killing strike against her and agreed to become a Sith, then altered Ben's memory to cover Dinn's death. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Caedus) ("The death of Nelani Dinn"; "Darksider")
  - **Step 5, sacrifice:** Lumiya taught that a true Sith must be willing to sacrifice those close to him (ref: *LotF: Tempest*). He killed Mara Jade Skywalker. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Caedus) (intro; "Darksider")
  - **Numbness:** by 40 ABY he "felt little sense of tragedy" at deaths, even deaths at his own hand. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Caedus) ("Darksider")
  - **Time to fall:** about 13 years from the Vergere ordeal (c. 28 ABY) to full Sith mastery in 40–41 ABY. The active Sith apprenticeship under Lumiya lasted about a year, in 40 ABY. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Caedus) (infobox; "Corellian tensions") [inferred span]
  - **Failure:** without Lumiya he became "increasingly vain, despondent and obsessed with vengeance", although she had warned him the path would be painful (ref: *LotF: Betrayal*; *Invincible*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Caedus) ("Last days")
  - **Partial return:** in his last moments he warned Tenel Ka about danger to her and Allana. That act, and their restored twin bond, convinced Jaina that his light-side self had partly returned (ref: *LotF: Invincible*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Caedus) ("Darksider")
  - **Afterwards:** later, in the realm beyond shadows, Jacen said he had not carried his dark-side anger into death, an experience he called "damnation". [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Caedus) ("Mixed legacy")
- **Related:** Sith all-eras Part2, Era 9 (Lumiya)
- **Uncertainty:** the duration of the fall [inferred]. The step 1 date is unconfirmed; the page gives the war years as 27–28 ABY.

### Tahiri Veila
- **Type:** fall and atonement case
- **Era:** 40–44 ABY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Pressure:** Caedus approached her on the anniversary of Anakin Solo's death, knowing she would be emotionally vulnerable. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tahiri_Veila) ("On the path to the dark side")
  - **Addiction:** through flow-walking he let her revisit her last moments with Anakin. "Driven by an addiction" to see him again, she became Caedus's spy. He fed the addiction by never leaving her fully satisfied (ref: *LotF: Inferno*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tahiri_Veila) ("On the path to the dark side")
  - **Tests:** at Fondor, Caedus dropped the ship's shields and made her decide whether to fire first on Fondorian fighters. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tahiri_Veila) ("Sith apprentice")
  - **The decisive act:** she assassinated Gilad Pellaeon on Caedus's order, after which she became his official apprentice. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tahiri_Veila) (intro)
  - **Time to fall:** months within 40 ABY [inferred]. Her loyalty rested on her hope of changing the past, not on Caedus's vision. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tahiri_Veila) ("On the path to the dark side")
  - **The break:** Caedus admitted that flow-walking could not change time. She was "completely shocked and outraged" at the betrayal. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tahiri_Veila) ("Discontent")
  - **Cost:** two years later, guilt kept her from rejoining the Jedi; she felt "neither worthy or truly redeemed" and became a bounty hunter. She was tried for Pellaeon's murder (ref: *FotJ: Omen*; *Abyss*) and escaped in 44 ABY. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tahiri_Veila) ("Rebuilding an identity"; "Arrest and trial"; intro)
  - **Atonement:** she gave herself up to Han and Leia, resolved to atone for what she had done under Caedus. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tahiri_Veila) ("Atonement")
- **Related:** Jacen Solo
- **Uncertainty:** duration [inferred]

### Cade Skywalker
- **Type:** fall and redemption case
- **Era:** 130–138 ABY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Step 1, the first dark act (130 ABY):** at Ossus, unable to accept his Master Wolf Sazen's death, Cade "focused his anger" and used dark transfer to revive him. He then vowed revenge on Darth Nihl for killing his father (ref: *Legacy* 1). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cade_Skywalker) ("Massacre at Ossus")
  - **Step 2, exile and addiction:** as a bounty hunter he took death sticks as payment, used them to block out visions of Luke Skywalker's ghost, and once threatened suicide to make Luke leave (ref: *Legacy* 2–3). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cade_Skywalker) ("Exile from the Order")
  - **Step 3, anger in battle:** he used his rage against Nihl despite Sazen's warnings. In a death-stick vision, Vader warned him about falling and scolded his lack of control (ref: *Legacy* 12). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cade_Skywalker) ("Reunion on Vendaxa"; "Vendaxa aftermath and further Jedi training")
  - **Step 4, Sith pressure:** Maladi tortured him, and he used his anger to cure his friends "at a great cost". Krayt put him in the Embrace of Pain while telling the story of his own fall (ref: *Legacy* 15, 18). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cade_Skywalker) ("Trapped by the Sith")
  - **Refusal:** he refused to kill Hosk Trey'lis, and later Talon. Krayt had meant the second order to "tip Cade over to the dark side permanently". [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cade_Skywalker) ("Duel in the Temple of the Sith")
  - **Edge:** at the Hidden Temple he used the dark side against Shado Vao, then stopped short of killing him to prove he was in control. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cade_Skywalker) ("The Hidden Temple")
  - **Turn back:** trying to heal Deliah Blue of Maladi's weapon, he was "continually dancing on the edge of the dark side" and argued the dark side was stronger. He then drew on the light side and his love for Blue, which killed the weapon, and told Sazen he had been right. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cade_Skywalker) ("Choosing a path")
  - **Time:** about seven years, from Ossus (130 ABY) to the light-side healing in 137 ABY [inferred from the page's sequence].
  - **Lasting hold:** he walked "a fragile line between the light and dark sides", alarming friends with dark-side displays. Celeste Morne said she had never met anyone who walked so close to the dark side yet resisted it so well. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cade_Skywalker) ("Personality and traits")
- **Related:** Sith all-eras Part2, Era 10
- **Uncertainty:** duration [inferred]

### Joruus C'baoth
- **Type:** fall case driven by madness (no redemption)
- **Era:** before 9 ABY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Origin:** a clone of Jorus C'baoth grown in Spaarti cylinders. Quick-grown clones were often unstable (ref: *New Essential Chronology*; *Dark Force Rising*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Joruus_C%27baoth) ("Creation")
  - **Deeds:** he killed the Guardian of Mount Tantiss and ruled the Wayland natives through the Force (ref: *Thrawn Trilogy Sourcebook*). He broke orders through "clone madness" (ref: *Dark Empire Sourcebook*) and tried to lure Luke and Leia to the dark side. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Joruus_C%27baoth) ("Creation"; "Service to Thrawn"; "Personality and traits")
  - **End (9 ABY):** stabbed by Mara Jade, he exploded in dark-side energy (ref: *The Last Command*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Joruus_C%27baoth) ("Showdown on Wayland"); [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("Death of dark side users")
- **Related:** Section 6
- **Uncertainty:** time to fall is not stated; his madness predates his dark-side use

## Section 6: Madness (4 ABY onward)

### Is dark-side madness a recognized condition?
- **Type:** synthesis
- **Era:** all, with the sources concentrated here
- **Key facts:**

It adds that all of them were disturbed or traumatized before they turned and the dark side "simply exacerbated" this. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("Corruption of the dark side")
  - No general clinical name for dark-side madness was found. The one named condition is Force psychosis, below.
- **Related:** Part 2, Section 6 (the same conflict)
- **Uncertainty:** [conflict] cause vs amplifier, as in Part 2. Best fit: amplifier.

### Force psychosis (yes, it appears in Legends)
- **Type:** named mental illness
- **Era:** c. 43–44 ABY (the *Fate of the Jedi* novels, 2009–2012)
- **Key facts:**
  - A mental disease that struck young Jedi after the Second Galactic Civil War. Victims became paranoid that everyone they knew was an impostor. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_psychosis) (intro)
  - **Cause:** the dark-side entity Abeloth influenced their minds to draw them to the Maw, where she fed (ref: *FotJ: Allies*; *Abyss*). She had done this only rarely since Old Republic days (ref: *Abyss*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_psychosis) ("Rise of the psychosis in the Jedi Knights")
  - **Cases:** Seff Hellin; Valin Horn, who was frozen in carbonite after Corran and Mirax captured him (ref: *FotJ: Outcast*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_psychosis) ("Infected beings"; "Rise of the psychosis in the Jedi Knights"); [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Valin_Horn) (intro)
  - **Signs:** Cilghal noted waking dreams and the use of rare powers Jacen Solo had learned on his travels (ref: *FotJ: Omen*). Symptoms eased when victims were cut off from the Force. Every victim had been on Shelter, in the Maw, during the Yuuzhan Vong War (ref: *Abyss*). The Caedus page records the same powers. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_psychosis) ("Symptoms"; "Rise of the psychosis in the Jedi Knights"); [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Caedus) ("Mixed legacy")
  - **Cure:** the illness lifted when Luke defeated Abeloth (ref: *FotJ: Allies*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_psychosis) ("Abeloth's defeat")
- **Related:** Jacen Solo
- **Uncertainty:** it is caused by an outside entity, not by the victims' own use of the dark side, so it is not a condition caused by falling. The novel series ran 2009–2012, pre-2014, so it is not [gray].

## Game notes (suggestions only; not lore)
- **Fall speed (suggestion):** an outside dark force (a Sith spirit, a reborn Emperor) produces falls in days to months (Kyp, Luke). Self-driven falls take years (Jacen, Cade). A campaign could let outside influences multiply the speed of a fall.
- **Deeds that matter most (suggestion):** killing a loved one or an ally (Jacen and Mara, Tahiri and Pellaeon) as near point-of-no-return acts; refusing a kill order (Cade) as a strong pull back.
- **Addiction mechanic (suggestion):** model Tahiri's flow-walking and Cade's death sticks as dependencies a dark mentor can feed, and that keep a character "never fully satisfied".
- **Staging corruption (suggestion):** use Teneniel's instant mark for a dramatic single act, Caedus's eyes-only year for a disciplined user, and Gantoris's burning for uncontrolled power.
- **Madness (suggestion):** treat madness as an amplifier of existing trauma, per the DS page. Force psychosis works as an outside-caused affliction that can be cured by defeating its source.
- **Redemption (suggestion):** every redemption here leaves guilt or a shadow, so a lingering "hold" that can flare under stress fits the sources.
