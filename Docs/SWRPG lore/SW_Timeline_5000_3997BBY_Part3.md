# SWRPG Galaxy Timeline, 5,000–3,997 BBY (Part 3 of 3): Corrections, Gaps and Decisions

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 24, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
Part 3 holds the back sections for the whole 5,000–3,997 BBY set: what the existing files get wrong, what could not be found, Canon-only items, items not used, game notes, the decisions the developer needs to make, the full source list and the submission checklist. The most important corrections: the old galactic timeline dates the counterinvasion to 4,990 BBY and the Ritual of Nathema to c. 5,000 BBY (Wookieepedia: 5,000–c. 4,999 and 4,999 BBY); it puts the Deneba conclave and Kun's fall inside a "3,997–3,996 BBY" Great Sith War that Wookieepedia dates to 3,996 BBY; it says the Jedi Council agreed to Ulic's infiltration (it was rejected) and that Kun attended Deneba (he was on Korriban); and it says Ommin was captured (he was killed). The character files make Ooroo an ammonia-breather who won Kirrek by telekinesis (he breathed cyanogen and won by releasing it), make Memit Nadill human (his species is not given), and give Odan-Urr a lifespan of "nearly 600 years" (he was over a thousand).

## Game notes (optional, suggestions only)
- Suggestion: 5,000 BBY is a single, very dense year; a campaign set then could use the war's fronts (Coruscant, Koros Major, Kirrek, Primus Goluud, Korriban) as chapters.
- Suggestion: the Lost Tribe on Kesh is cut off from the galaxy by Kesh's magnetic field; it suits a sealed "lost world" setting from 5,000 BBY onward.
- Suggestion: the 4,100–4,000 BBY frontier (Lorell Raiders, Nelori Marauders, Taris's civil war, the Mandalorian Crusaders' first campaigns, the Great Droid Revolution) gives low-Sith adventure hooks before the Tales of the Jedi era.
- Suggestion: the Krath's Iron Citadel and the Onderon royal tomb on Dxun are natural dungeons for 3,998–3,997 BBY play.
