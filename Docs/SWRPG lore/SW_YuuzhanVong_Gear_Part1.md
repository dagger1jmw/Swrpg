# Yuuzhan Vong Personal Biotechnology (Gear) — Part 1: Weapons, Armour and Concealment, Tools

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 21, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
This file covers the personal-scale living gear ("biots") of the Yuuzhan Vong in Legends across every era: the invasion and war (c. 25–29 ABY), the post-war decades, and the Legacy era (c. 122–137 ABY), with a pre-war outlier in 33 BBY. Part 1 lists the gear itself: weapons, armour and concealment, and tools, with how each fights, how it reacts to lightsabers and the Force, who controls it, and how it dies. Part 2 (`SW_YuuzhanVong_Gear_Part2.md`) covers outsiders who used the gear, Force interaction, personal-scale counter-tech, availability by era, caste use, and the Timeline. Part 3 (`SW_YuuzhanVong_Gear_Part3.md`) holds Not found, Canon only, Not used, Game notes, Decisions needed, Sources, the checklist and "For later topics". The key facts: every Vong item is a living creature with no Force presence; vonduun crab armour and amphistaffs resist lightsaber blades; the Vong and most of their gear can't be touched directly by most Force powers, although Force lightning and objects moved with telekinesis can harm them; and a handful of non-Vong (Jacen Solo, Tahiri Veila, Anakin Solo, Jariah Syn, Darth Krayt) used Vong gear through bonding, implants, language or training.

How to read the tables: each row's facts carry their own citation. "§" names the section of the cited page. Tags follow the brief: [unclear], [conflict], [inferred], [last resort], [gray]. Game statistics from tabletop sourcebooks are NOT in these tables. They are in Part 3, under Game notes.

## General rules that apply to all gear
- **Everything is alive.** The Vong regarded machines as polluting, so every tool was a shaped living organism (a "biot"), and their technology was described as more resilient than conventional technology [Yuuzhan Vong §Technology](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong). Priests "purified" any Vong contaminated by technological items [Priest caste §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Priest_caste).
- **No Force presence.** The Vong and their creations sit outside the Force. Jedi can't sense their presence, though they can find them as "holes" in the Force. Most Force powers aimed directly at them have no effect, apart from a short list of exceptions [Yuuzhan Vong §Biology and appearance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong). Voxyn were the stated exception among Vong creations, since they could sense the Force [Voxyn §Resilient Jedi hunters](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Voxyn). See Part 2, Force interaction.
- **Genetic marker.** A genetic element was present in all Vong biotech [Yuuzhan Vong §Biology and appearance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong).
- **Shapers make the gear.** The shaper caste made everything, following fixed protocols handed down by their gods. Deviation meant demotion or death [Shaper caste §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shaper_caste).
