# Lightsaber Forms Catalog Check — Part 1

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 21, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
This file checks the lightsaber content of the developer's file `SW_Force_Abilities_Forms_v3_complete.md` ("v3") against the eight `SW_Lightsaber_*.md` research files and the Wookieepedia Legends pages, which were re-read for this check where the research files were silent. The scope is v3's `## LIGHTSABER FORMS` section (forms table, Form Notes, Notes only, Form Rules M6 and the marks table) plus lightsaber-related entries elsewhere in v3: Saber Throw, Saber Barrier, Telekinetic Lightsaber Combat, the Form V note under Enhance Attribute, the Ataru note under Force Valor, the Vaapad note under Battlemind, lightsaber absorption under Tutaminis, Force Deflection, Force-imbued weapons, and the worldstate flags, life stages and unlock triggers that mention forms. Other Force powers are out of scope. Game mechanics (stats, thresholds, costs, multipliers) are design, not lore, and are not judged; they are listed once in "Design-only items" so nothing is silently skipped.

Every v3 claim gets one verdict:
- **Matches:** Legends agrees.
- **Correction:** Legends says something different; the v3 text is quoted and the Legends version given.
- **Unsourced:** no Legends source found; what was checked is listed (source rule 3 applied where relevant).
- **Conflict:** Legends sources disagree; all versions are listed and the call is left to the developer (source rule 6).

**Verdict counts (76 items):** Matches 53 · Correction 14 · Unsourced 6 · Conflict 3. (Two further Conflicts that touch v3 indirectly, Niman mastery time and Tràkata for Jedi, are listed under Decisions needed only.)

**Biggest issues, in short:**
1. v3's 2026-11-20 removal of Asajj Ventress as a Makashi user is wrong; Legends lists her as a skilled (not master) Makashi user.
2. Makashi is described as "minimal footwork"; Legends makes footwork the core of Makashi (and v3's own unlock trigger is "Makashi Precision Footwork").
3. Vaapad: "Only Mace Windu truly mastered it" is wrong (Depa Billaba became a master; Quinlan Vos completed his mastery at Saleucami), and the Yoda quote given as proof was not found on any page checked.
4. Vaapad "does not exist before then" is a sourced conflict (the name is used in-universe for the New Sith Wars era).
5. v3's worldstate flags and unlock trigger still gate Vaapad on "Mace Windu alive + Prequel era", contradicting v3's own corrected Vaapad note.
6. Saberstaff "does more damage": Legends says power and penetration styles are hard to apply with it.
7. Trispzest locked to "winged species": a legless levitating Dark Jedi (Maw) also used it.
8. Telekinetic saber combat's peak is credited to Luke Skywalker with no source; the sourced example is Darth Traya with three sabers.

The detailed tables are below. Missing from v3, Practitioner check, Not found, Canon only, Not used, Game notes, Decisions needed, Sources and the checklist are in Part 2 (`SW_Lightsaber_Forms_Catalog_Check_Part2.md`).
