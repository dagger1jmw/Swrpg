# Force Powers, Part 1b: Sense and Universal Powers (Legends, all eras)

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched 21 Nov 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) via the MediaWiki API, cited per entry. Draft for review.

**Read this with Part 1a.**
- `SW_Force_Powers_Part1a.md` holds the shared index for Parts 1–3, the scale-anchor table, the category background and the Control powers (§C1–C13).
- It also defines the **"standard counters"** used below: ysalamiri, taozin, the Yuuzhan Vong, Toydarians and Hutts, willpower, thought shield and Force resistance, and Force stealth. See Part 1a §Entries.
- Cross-references: DS1–DS3 = `SW_DarkSide_Fall_Corruption_Redemption_Part1–3.md`; MIDI = `SW_Midichlorians_Force_Potential.md`.

## Summary

- **Sense powers** are taught to Padawans: *prima vitae*, *tactus otium*, *tai vordrax* and projected telepathy are the core.
- **Ranges** run from a passive 10 m to about 1,000 km for telepathy between Force-sensitives, and to galaxy-wide for mass-death disturbances.
- **Universal powers:** the Force-bond (innate, no category); battle meditation (all three categories, used by light and dark); Force harmony (all three categories, light); Oneness (a state, filed under Control).
- **No Part 1b power is a dark act in itself**, except that Darksight (§S8b) is known only from dark-side users. The dark uses are Sith battle-meditation domination, ripping memories (psychometry on the living), and dark Oneness.

## Entries

### Sense (S1–S13, plus S8b)

#### S1. Force sense (basic; includes danger sense and "clear mind")
1. **Names and classification:** Force sense. Sense; neither light nor dark. Precognition is also called "danger sense" (§S8). [Force sense/Legends][fs]
2. **What it does:** "among the most basic of Force abilities". It lets the user feel:
   - other beings' feelings;
   - the future;
   - ripples caused by momentous or traumatic events;
   - danger;
   - light or dark presences;
   - hidden beings through barriers.
   Advanced users sense the health of nearby beings. [Force sense §Applications][fs]
3. **Users and feats:**
   - **Yoda** felt the deaths of thousands of Jedi across the galaxy (19 BBY, Film III) and dodged his clones' attack on Kashyyyk.
   - **Obi-Wan Kenobi** felt the "great disturbance" at Alderaan (0 BBY, Film IV).
   - **Qui-Gon Jinn** sensed Tahl's danger from far away and saw part of her situation (*Jedi Apprentice*).
   - **Kyle Katarn and Jaden Korr** sensed health (*Jedi Academy*).
   [Force sense §Applications][fs]
4. **Era:** all eras. It is among the first things taught.
5. **How it is learned:** first-year training. Danger sense often comes naturally to Force-sensitives.
6. **Costs and dangers:** mass-death disturbances overwhelm the user (see Force-meld, §S13). Not a dark act.
7. **Counters:** standard counters. **"Clear mind"** is a concealment some Sentinels used to avoid detection on covert missions (*Saga Edition*). [Force sense §Clear mind][fs]
8. **Related:** every Sense power; Force sight is a variant (§S5).
9. **Game stats:** (game stat: *Saga Edition* says planet destruction is sensed "from anywhere in the galaxy" and friends in mortal danger up to 10,000 light-years away; see the scale-anchor table in Part 1a.)
- **Uncertainty:** the 10,000-light-year figure is a rulebook statement presented as lore. It is used as an anchor but labelled.

#### S2. Sense Force (*tactus otium*)
1. **Names and classification:** Sense Force; also See Force or *tactus otium*. Sense; neither light nor dark. [Sense Force][sf]
2. **What it does:** senses the strength of the ambient Force in a place, person or object, and whether it is light or dark: "sickly, beneficial, or malevolent". It warns of dark-side sites before you reach them. [Sense Force §Applications][sf]
3. **Users and feats:**
   - **Luke Skywalker** at the Dagobah cave (3 ABY).
   - **Yoda** sensed Dooku's alignment (22 BBY, Film II).
   [Sense Force §Users][sf]
4. **Era:** all eras.
5. **How it is learned:** Padawans relax their perception "until everything around them became a blur" (*The Jedi Path*).
6. **Costs and dangers:** none recorded. Not a dark act.
7. **Counters:** Force stealth; a Force nexus can mask presences (*Revised Core Rulebook*). [Sense Force][sf]
8. **Related:** Force sense; Force sight; Life Detection.
9. **Game stats:** none.
- **Uncertainty:** the page also cites Yoda at the cave from *The Clone Wars* "Voices" (7 Mar 2014, Lost Missions). Wookieepedia tags that episode Canon, so it is **excluded** and listed under Canon only.

#### S3. Life Detection (*prima vitae*), Life Sense, Life web
1. **Names and classification:** Life Detection / *prima vitae* (basic Sense in *The Jedi Path*); Life Sense and Life web (WEG game powers). All Sense; neither light nor dark. [Life Detection][ld]; [Life Sense][ls]; [Life web][lw]
2. **What it does:**
   - **Life Detection** finds living beings at long range and tells friend from foe. It can tell whether a newcomer can use the Force, and who it is if known to the user. It works subconsciously within about 10 m.
   - **Life Sense** finds a specific target's presence, identity and condition (wounds, illness) and can detect a hibernation trance.
   - **Life web** finds the direction and distance of large groups of one known species. It cannot detect fewer than 100,000, and senses only one species at a time.
3. **Users and feats:**
   - **Padawans** feel their Master "light-years away" through their bond (*The Jedi Path*). [Life Detection][ld]
   - **Dev Sibwarra** learned Life web from his mother Ydra (*Truce at Bakura* era, 4 ABY [inferred]). [Life web][lw]
4. **Era:** all eras.
5. **How it is learned:** Padawan Sense training. Life web was taught by a parent (Sibwarra).
6. **Costs and dangers:** none. Not a dark act.
7. **Counters:** standard counters; the Yuuzhan Vong are invisible to it.
8. **Related:** Force sense; Force harmony (needs Life Sense, §U3).
9. **Game stats:** (game stat: 10 m passive radius, *HttE Sourcebook*; Life web 100,000 minimum.)
- **Uncertainty:** none flagged.

#### S4. Magnify Senses, Shift Sense, Force smell
- **Magnify Senses** (Sense):
  - Boosts normal senses, e.g. hearing beyond human range, binocular-level sight, reading microfilm.
  - Padawans found it hard: Sabla-Mandibu explains that the Force offers its own perspective, so using it to boost ordinary senses feels counterintuitive.
  - Temple drill: track a training remote that drifts away at one metre per minute.
  - Variants: echolocation (*Dynasty of Evil*); seeing very tiny detail (*Young Jedi Knights: Lightsabers*).
  - [Magnify Senses][mag]
- **Shift Sense** (Sense):
  - Shifts the senses into infrared or radio, chemical scents, or other sound frequencies.
  - User: **Aleema Keto** (c. 4000 BBY; *Tales of the Jedi Companion*).
  - [Shift Sense][sht]
- **Force smell** (Sense):
  - Luke smelled Niles Ferrier's cigarra (9 ABY, *Dark Force Rising*), and later the refugees aboard the *Eye of Palpatine*.
  - [Force smell][smell]
- **Dark act?** No, none of them. **Counters:** standard counters.

#### S5. Force sight (Force seeing)
1. **Names and classification:** Force sight or Force seeing; a variant of Force sense. Sense; neither light nor dark. *Jedi vs. Sith* also calls precognition, Force sight and farsight forms of Force vision. [Force sight][fsi]; [Force vision §Overview][fv]
2. **What it does:** sight in darkness and through walls. Trained users can counter Force persuasion and Force blinding.
   - Miraluka see no colours, but distinguish living things (even dead ones) and their alignment by aura, and most objects look translucent. [Force sight §Overview][fsi]
3. **Users and feats:**
   - **The Miraluka**, as a species trait.
   - **Visas Marr** taught it to **Meetra Surik** (3951 BBY).
   - **Kreia** used the Miraluka version so much that her eyes atrophied.
   - **Rahm Kota** after Galen blinded him, and later **Galen Marek**.
   - **Darth Bane** after a flash canister blinded him in the Gloom Walkers (*Path of Destruction*).
   - **Jaesa Willsaam** sensed individual personality traits.
   - **Jaina Solo** heightened her sight (*YJK: Lightsabers*).
   [Force sight][fsi]
4. **Era:** all eras.
5. **How it is learned:** Miraluka species trait (Part 3a §T7); otherwise taught.
6. **Costs and dangers:** relying on it can atrophy the eyes (Kreia). Not a dark act.
7. **Counters:** standard counters; Force blinding (Part 2a §B) can be resisted by trained users.
8. **Related:** Force sense; Luka Sene Field Detection (Part 3a).
9. **Game stats:** none.
- **Uncertainty:** none flagged.

#### S6. Telepathy (receptive and projective)
1. **Names and classification:** telepathy. Sense.
   - **Projective telepathy** is filed under Control and Sense by Wookieepedia, but its text says it "combined the perceptual abilities of Sense with the more direct discipline of Alter" (*The Jedi Path*). **[conflict]**: C+S vs S+A.
   - **Receptive telepathy** is Sense.
   - Neither light nor dark. Intrusive uses are covered elsewhere: mind trick and Memory Walk (Part 2a §B), memory rub (Part 2b §G), Drain Knowledge / mind probe (Part 3a §T11).
   [Telepathy/Legends][tel]; [Projective Telepathy][ptl]; [Receptive Telepathy][rtl]
2. **What it does and its limits:**
   - **Range:** theoretically infinite, but the user must sense or pick out the target. To non-sensitives, contact becomes "nearly impossible" beyond "a handful of kilometers". Between Force-sensitives the typical limit is about 1,000 km. Relatives or strong bonds reach far further; the Jacen–Jaina twin bond had "no spatial limit". [Telepathy §Description][tel]
   - **Skill:** most users only sense emotions (Force empathy, §S7). Most can exchange only "four or five-word sentences", and only with close bonds.
   - **Projection** is hard; "even some members of the Jedi Council did not achieve this". Sending a general warning is far easier. [Projective Telepathy][ptl]
   - **Receptive** use reads thoughts and sometimes memories. It works better on emotionally close targets, and Force users can resist it. [Receptive Telepathy][rtl]
3. **Users and feats:**
   - **Luke Skywalker** called Leia at Bespin, and Vader called to Luke (3 ABY, Film V).
   - **Saesee Tiin, Plo Koon and Kit Fisto** were the top Jedi telepaths of the Clone Wars. Tiin's natural gift distressed him as a Padawan until Omo Bouri trained him.
   - **Kreia** taught Meetra over great distances (3951 BBY). [Force-bond §Meetra Surik and Kreia][bond]
   [Telepathy §Description][tel]
4. **Era:** all eras; every Force user is potentially able.
5. **How it is learned:** basic training. As a species trait: Iktotchi, Anzati, Hortek, Quermians, Zeltrons, Umbarans and Draethos, magnified "a thousandfold or more" when Jedi-trained.
6. **Costs and dangers:** social, not physical: telepaths are banned from most casinos (*Darth Plagueis*). Receptive reading is neutral; coercive uses tip into the dark side (Part 2a §B; DS1 §Section 1).
7. **Counters:** thought shield (§C10); Force resistance; standard counters.
8. **Related:** Force-bond; Force-meld; Comprehend Speech; mind trick (Part 2a §B).
9. **Game stats:** none beyond the WEG categories.
- **Uncertainty:** none beyond the category conflict.

#### S7. Force empathy, Truth-sense, Eyes of the Force, Comprehend Speech
1. **Force empathy** (Sense; neither): reads surface emotions; the adept pick up motives and guarded secrets. [Force empathy/Legends][emp]
   - **Anakin Skywalker** first saw into Joylin's heart on Romin (24 BBY, *Jedi Quest*). Jealousy numbed it on Korriban (23 BBY). He found Shmi in the Tusken camp by her pain (22 BBY, AOTC novelization).
   - **Yoda** felt Obi-Wan and Anakin's tension on Geonosis (22 BBY).
   - **Etain Tur-Mukan** (Clone Wars).
   - **Jaesa Willsaam:** her empathy extended to predicting actions (Great Galactic War era).
   - **Ben Skywalker** as an infant shied away from the Force because of it.
   - **Danger:** selfish feelings cloud it. Not a dark act.
2. **Truth-sense** (Sense; neither):
   - Detects lies. **Limit:** a sincere believer reads as truthful.
   - **Darth Sidious** caught Nute Gunray lying about Hath Monchar (32 BBY, *Cloak of Deception*).
   - [Truth-sense][tru]
3. **Eyes of the Force** (Sense; neither):
   - Sees images in others' surface thoughts; the Temple tested it with a hidden image viewer.
   - **Mace Windu** tested Anakin with it (32 BBY, Film I). **Luke** saw through Wedge's eyes at Bakura (4 ABY, *Truce at Bakura*).
   - [Eyes of the Force][eye]
4. **Comprehend Speech** (Language Absorption; Sense; neither):
   - Understands any spoken language, but does not give the ability to speak it. Possibly an extension of telepathy.
   - **Revan** "ripped" the Rakatan language from their minds (3956 BBY).
   - **Obi-Wan Kenobi** translated the Nelvaanian shaman Orvos but missed nuances (Clone Wars).
   - **Mace Windu** understood Kar Vastor (*Shatterpoint*, 22 BBY).
   - The Witches of Dathomir call it the Spell of Interpretation. **Luke** learned Jem Ysanna's tongue through their bond (*Dark Empire II*).
   - [Comprehend Speech][cmp]; [Force-bond][bond]
- **Counters for all four:** thought shield; standard counters.

#### S8. Precognition, Force visions, Sense Path
1. **Names and classification:**
   - **Precognition** (danger sense; foresight; battle precognition) is described as a "universal" power. Wookieepedia files it under Sense.
   - **Force vision** is Sense. *Jedi vs. Sith* calls it "the cornerstone of the Unifying Force".
   - **Sense Path** is Sense.
   - All neither light nor dark; Sith receive visions too.
   [Precognition/Legends][pre]; [Force vision/Legends][fv]; [Sense Path][spa]
2. **What they do and their limits:**
   - **Precognition** gives premonitions, warnings, and foresight of an opponent's moves in combat (battle precognition).
   - **Visions** are "extremely rare, and uncontrollable"; meditation rarely brings one. "The future is always in motion"; interpreting alone is "dangerous". Ego clouds visions: Palpatine and Caedus never saw their own deaths, while Darth Tenebrous calculated his.
   - **Sense Path** shows allegorical visions of whether a course of action leads to the dark side.
   [Force vision §Overview][fv]
3. **Users and feats:**
   - **Darth Traya** saw "many thousands of years" ahead and foretold the death of the last Mandalorians at a Jedi's hands.
   - **Sidious**, "perhaps the most vision-prone Dark Lord", used visions to engineer his election and rise (to 19 BBY), and consulted the Prophets of the Dark Side.
   - **Anakin Skywalker** misread a triple vision of his own freedom (25 BBY).
   - **Qui-Gon Jinn** foresaw Obi-Wan's exile.
   - **Battle precognition:** Skere Kaan in space battles (c. 1000 BBY); Revan; Meetra Surik (learned from Brianna); Scout; Carnor Jax; Tyvokka.
   - **Suljo Warde** saw others' futures but not his own (*Chronicles of the Gatekeeper*) [gray].
   - **Nova Stihl's "Blink"** was latent Force anticipation.
   - **Obi-Wan** heeded the "small voice".
   [Precognition §Users][pre]; [Force vision §Overview][fv]
4. **Era:** all eras.
   - Jedi Seers interpreted visions for Consulars.
   - After the Order was rebuilt, the Jedi Council of Seers took over the role. [Force vision][fv]
5. **How it is learned:** precognition comes with experience. Visions cannot be taught. As a species trait: Iktotchi (Part 3a §T7).
6. **Costs and dangers:**
   - The Sith believe visions must come true, and Anakin's fall turned on a vision (see DS2 for his fall, rather than repeated here).
   - Not a dark act, but acting on fear of a vision is a classic path to the dark side.
7. **Counters:** the future is fluid. A shatterpoint or an ego blind spot can hide outcomes.
8. **Related:** farsight; Force sight; flow-walking (Part 3a); shatterpoint (Part 3a).
9. **Game stats:** none.
- **Uncertainty:** Suljo Warde's source, *Chronicles of the Gatekeeper*, is a Fantasy Flight book from 5 Nov 2015. That puts it in the **[gray]** window (rule 7). It is used because a Legends page cites it and it has no sequel-film tie.

#### S8b. Darksight (Heartshadow)
1. **Names and classification:** "Darksight" is the name used by the Sorcerers of Rhand and by Cronal; the Nightsisters of Dathomir called it **Heartshadow**, and the page adds "other names by other Force users". Wookieepedia gives it **no category and no side category**. It is filed here beside precognition because it is foresight [inferred]. Its known users all worked through the dark side or the Rhandites' "Dark". [Darksight §intro][dks]; [Sorcerers of Rhand §intro][rhand]
2. **What it does and its limits:** the user foresees several possible futures and then uses that knowledge to manipulate events so that the desired outcome happens. The Sorcerers of Rhand believed only futures that served the Dark could come about. [Darksight §intro][dks]
3. **Users and feats:**
   - **The Sorcerers of Rhand**, rulers of the twelve clusters of the Nihil Retreat (Perann Nebula, Unknown Regions). [Sorcerers of Rhand §intro][rhand]
   - **Cronal** (Lord Shadowspawn), a former Rhandite who learned it from them and used it in his war against the New Republic (the Battle of Mindor, c. 6 ABY [inferred]). He blamed his failure on having *created* something, the Legions of Shadow, instead of following the Way of the Dark toward destruction. [Darksight §intro][dks]
   - **The Nightsisters**, under the name Heartshadow. No named Nightsister user is given. [Darksight §intro][dks]
4. **Era:** undated for the Rhandites and the Nightsisters; Cronal's use is in the early New Republic era. [Darksight][dks]
5. **How it is learned:** from the Sorcerers of Rhand (Cronal) or within the Nightsister clans. No other teaching route is given. [Darksight §intro][dks]
6. **Costs and dangers:** none stated on the page. The Rhandite version is bound to the Way of the Dark ("the only real power is the power to destroy"). See DS1–DS3 for how dark-side practice corrupts; nothing here is repeated. [Sorcerers of Rhand §intro][rhand]
7. **Counters:** none recorded.
8. **Related:** precognition and Force vision (§S8); Darkshear, another Rhandite power (Part 2b §F); daen nosi "fate lines" (Part 3a §T7).
9. **Game stats:** none.
- **Uncertainty:** the pages give no mechanics, range or limit. The phrase "other names by other Force users" means the power is not strictly tied to Rhand and Dathomir. Sources are *Luke Skywalker and the Shadows of Mindor* (2008, first appearance), *The Unknown Regions* (20 Apr 2010) and the StarWars.com series *The Imperial Warlords: Despoilers of an Empire*, Part 3 (7 Jan 2014). All are Legends-era publications. [Darksight §Appearances; §Sources][dks]

#### S9. Farsight (clairvoyance)
1. **Names and classification:** farsight; also clairvoyance, far sight or farseeing. Filed under both Control and Sense; WEG requires training in both. Neither light nor dark. [Farsight/Legends][far]
2. **What it does:** visions of people or places *as they are now*, including surroundings, e.g. a friend in danger, or events on a planet while away.
3. **Users and feats:**
   - **Palpatine** sensed Vader's danger on Mustafar and used farsight to lay his traps for the Jedi (19 BBY, Film III).
   - **Luke Skywalker** foresaw his friends' danger at Bespin; it was one of the first powers Yoda taught him (3 ABY, Film V).
   - **The Keetael** (Draethos) used meditative farsight to track quarry over "even interstellar" distances (KOTOR Campaign Guide). [Force Track][trk]
4. **Era:** all eras.
5. **How it is learned:** Master training; Keetael tradition (Part 3a).
6. **Costs and dangers:** acting on it rashly (Luke at Bespin). Not a dark act.
7. **Counters:** standard counters.
8. **Related:** Force vision; Force Track.
9. **Game stats:** (game stat: WEG requires both Control and Sense.)
- **Uncertainty:** the scope of the Keetael feat is from a game supplement.

#### S10. Psychometry (*tai vordrax*, postcognition)
1. **Names and classification:** psychometry; also postcognition, *tai vordrax* or telemetry. Sense (one of the four core Sense groups in *The Jedi Path*). Neither light nor dark, but with taint risk. [Psychometry/Legends][psy]
2. **What it does:** reads the history of an object by touch, with sights, sounds and feelings. It works best on often-used personal items; objects used once or by many people are hard to read. Sometimes the user's eyes glow (*Clone Wars Campaign Guide*).
3. **Users and feats:**
   - **The Kiffar:** about 1 in 100 have it, the Vos line more often, and most become Kiffu Guardians.
   - **Quinlan Vos** was the most skilled of his time. He claimed he read Oppo Rancisis's corpse for war plans, but the claim was a lie (19 BBY, *Republic* 76–77).
   - **Boc Aseca** (Dark Jedi).
   - **Luke**, untrained, read the Tusken massacre in the Valley of the Spirits, "worse than a nightmare". Later he tracked Han's emotional "scent" to the casino where Han won Dathomir (8 ABY, *Courtship*).
   [Psychometry §Users][psy]
4. **Era:** all eras. In the Naboo period the Council frowned on it in some uses.
5. **How it is learned:** Kiffar species trait (Part 3a §T7) or Jedi training.
6. **Costs and dangers:**
   - It can **taint** the user if the object was used in dark acts such as murder.
   - The Council "strongly discouraged" reading dead bodies, because the emotions at a violent death brush the dark side.
   - Dark-side users can use it on living beings to rip out memories (mind probe, Part 3a §T11). Reading itself is not a dark act; tearing memories from the living is.
7. **Counters:** objects handled by many people.
8. **Related:** Force vision; Kiffar (Part 3a).
9. **Game stats:** none.
- **Uncertainty:** the Council's Naboo-era restriction is cut off in the source; its scope is [unclear].

#### S11. Sense Force Potential and Force Track
- **Sense Force Potential** (Sense; neither):
  - Probes a mind for Force potential. A Force-sensitive's subconscious barrier "violently rejected the probe"; Jedi stimulate it on purpose to trigger that reaction.
  - Sith used it "prolifically" to choose apprentices: **Aleema Keto** (c. 4000 BBY); **Darth Bane** judged Zannah strong and her cousin weak (1000 BBY); **Vader** found the boy Reezen but did not train him (*Courtship*).
  - For potential itself, see MIDI §3 and §7.
  - [Sense Force Potential][sfp]
- **Force Track** (Sense; neither):
  - Follows a quarry's trail within line of sight; freshness matters. A novice reads trails minutes old; a Master reads trails days old, several at once (*Jedi Academy Training Manual*).
  - The Keetael taught it extensively (Part 3a).
  - [Force Track][trk]

#### S12. Instinctive astrogation
1. **Names and classification:** instinctive astrogation (Sense). A rarer "Instinctive Astrogation Control" variant is Control. Neither light nor dark. [Instinctive astrogation][ast]
2. **What it does:**
   - **The Sense version** feels out a fast, safe hyperspace route without a navicomputer or droid.
   - **The Control version** speeds up the mind so the user can work out the equations (*Galaxy Guide 9*).
3. **Users and feats:**
   - **Tron Nixx**, unknowingly Force-sensitive, thought it was luck.
   - **Dena and Erling Tredway.**
   - **Saesee Tiin**; **Kyp Durron**.
   - Non-Force astrogators exist: Siniteen, Lucent, Givin; star dragons and the Oswaft jump without ships.
4. **Era:** all eras.
5. **How it is learned:** practice; sometimes untrained.
6. **Costs and dangers:** none recorded. Not a dark act.
7. **Counters:** standard counters.
8. **Related:** Aing-Tii ship-jumping (Part 3a); natural navigation (tasar crystal, Part 3a).
9. **Game stats:** (game stat: the Control variant is from WEG.)
- **Uncertainty:** none flagged.

#### S13. Force-meld (Jedi meld, battle meld)
1. **Names and classification:** Force-meld; also Jedi meld, combat meld, battle meld, or mockingly the "United Jedi Cluster-Mind". Filed under Sense and as light-side. *Jedi vs. Sith* calls it a refinement of battle meditation. [Force-meld][meld]
2. **What it does:** links Force-sensitives into one mind so they act as a single entity. It is easier than battle meditation because it links fewer minds. Over long use, members finish each other's sentences.
3. **Users and feats:**
   - **Grandmaster Awdrysta Pina's Jedi** beat the Legions of Lettow on Columus (ancient, c. 24,500 BBY [inferred] from Pina's era).
   - **Jorus C'baoth** formed it at least eight times.
   - **Anakin Solo** redeveloped it during the Yuuzhan Vong War.
   - **Jacen Solo** coordinated it on the Myrkr strike mission (27 ABY).
   - **Jaina Solo's Twin Suns Squadron** used it as a squadron tactic.
   - **Madurrin, Jacen and Luke** coordinated between squadrons.
   [Force-meld §Overview][meld]
4. **Era:** ancient; rare; revived 25–29 ABY.
5. **How it is learned:** Obi-Wan warned that only "a Master of great power and depth" could create the single-entity state "without killing or destroying the minds" of those in it.
6. **Costs and dangers:**
   - A great disturbance overwhelms everyone in the meld.
   - A divided meld becomes "a liability".
   - A light-side majority can stop one member from slipping to the dark side.
   - Not a dark act.
7. **Counters:** disturbance and pain sent through the link. The Yuuzhan Vong exploited this (see Vongsense, Part 3a §T5).
8. **Related:** battle meditation; Force-bond; telepathy.
9. **Game stats:** none.
- **Uncertainty:** the Columus date is [inferred] from Pina's era (*Essential Guide to Warfare*).

### Universal and multi-category powers (U1–U4)

#### U1. Force-bond
1. **Names and classification:** Force-bond; also Force chain, Jedi kinship, or bond. It has no category on Wookieepedia and is described as "innate", so it is the clearest "universal" ability. Neither light nor dark. [Force-bond/Legends][bond]
2. **What it does:** a link through which two Force-sensitives influence each other. It carries feelings, thoughts and images across distance and improves battle coordination. Strength scales with both partners' Force strength. Master–Padawan bonds form slowly; family bonds are stronger, which is one reason the Jedi separated children from parents. [Force-bond §Description][bond]
3. **Users and feats:**
   - **Bastila Shan and Revan:** Bastila saved Revan's life and used the bond to draw out his memories, absorbing some of his taint. At **Rakata Prime (3956 BBY)**, boosted by the Star Forge, her battle meditation "could touch the minds" of two enormous fleets. This is the scale anchor.
   - **Meetra Surik and Kreia** (3951 BBY): Meetra formed bonds with unusual ease. Kreia taught her telepathically over great distances. Kavar, Vrook and Zez-Kai Ell could not remove the bond. Ell said the only way to break it was to turn away from the Force, as Meetra did at Malachor V.
   - **Quillan and Dromika**, the Dyarchy twins, were "one inseparable being" that no science or Sith alchemy could separate (*Knight Errant*, 1032 BBY [inferred]).
   - **Quinlan Vos and Aayla Secura** bonded instantly (*Rite of Passage*).
   - **Luke and Jem Ysanna:** Luke learned her language through the bond.
   [Force-bond][bond]
4. **Era:** all eras.
5. **How it is learned:** it forms naturally; Revan was rumoured to have studied it deeply.
6. **Costs and dangers:** the partner's taint passes across (Bastila); a bond can "hunger" (Meetra); the death of one partner wounds the other. Not a dark act. See DS1 §Bastila Shan and §Section 8.
7. **Counters:** cutting oneself off from the Force (Meetra at Malachor); death.
8. **Related:** telepathy; Force-meld; battle meditation; Force harmony.
9. **Game stats:** none.
- **Uncertainty:** the *Knight Errant* date is [inferred].

#### U2. Battle meditation (and Sith battle meditation)
1. **Names and classification:**
   - Battle meditation, also called Enhanced Coordination.
   - Filed under Alter, Control *and* Sense, with no side category: **[conflict]** over the category.
   - **Sith Battle Coordination** (Lumiya's name) or "Sith battle meditation" (Caedus's name) is the dark variant: it dominates instead of inspiring.
   - Odan-Urr: dark-side users can command it, but rarely. Luke knew it "was not automatically a dark side power".
   [Battle meditation/Legends][bm]
2. **What it does:**
   - Boosts allies' morale, stamina and skill, and erodes the enemy's will.
   - Coordinates fleets "as a single entity".
   - Works for small skirmishes too.
   - Unlike Force-meld, it affects non-sensitives on both sides.
   [Battle meditation §Description][bm]
3. **Users and feats (light):**
   - **Odan-Urr** in the Unification Wars.
   - **Arca Jeth** in the Beast Wars of Onderon.
   - **Nomi Sunrider** in "nearly every conflict", including the Freedon Nadd Uprising (c. 3998 BBY).
   - **Bastila Shan** at Rakata Prime (3956 BBY).
   - **Worror Dowmat** for the Army of Light (1000 BBY), and later against Bane and Zannah on Tython (990 BBY).
   - **Yoda** filled the actor Palleus Chuff with hope by touch on the Vjun mission (Clone Wars, *Dark Rendezvous*).
   - **Luke** studied it from a book after Endor; **Leia** learned it from Ossus texts and used it against the reborn Palpatine (*Empire's End* audio drama, 11 ABY [inferred]).
   - **T'ra Saa** at Taivas and **K'Kruhk** at Coruscant (Second Imperial Civil War, c. 137–138 ABY [inferred]).
   [Battle meditation §Light side users][bm]
4. **Users and feats (dark):**
   - **Skere Kaan** (c. 1000 BBY).
   - **Grand Admiral Nial Declann** at Endor (4 ABY, *Insider* 66).
   - **The Emperor's influence at Endor:** its sudden removal at his death threw the Imperial Navy into chaos (ROTJ novelization). Thrawn's theory was that Palpatine's control was so tight it made the fleet into puppets.
   - **Joruus C'baoth** raised Thrawn's victory rate by **40%** and at times commanded fleets "from across star systems though at great strain" (9 ABY, *Heir to the Empire*). Clone crews were easier for him.
   - **Darth Caedus** amplified it with a meditation chair; at first he could only *see* the battle (40 ABY, *Inferno*).
   [Battle meditation §Dark side users][bm]
5. **Era:** rare in every era. Lost to the New Jedi Order until Luke and Leia studied it from texts.
6. **How it is learned:** innate talent (Nomi "naturally adept"); texts (Luke, Leia); Master instruction (Odan-Urr to Nomi).
7. **Costs and dangers:**
   - Strain (C'baoth).
   - Dependence: a fleet run by it collapses if the user is removed (Endor).
   - The Sith variant is domination of others' will, which is a dark use. The light variant is not a dark act.
8. **Counters:** removing or distracting the meditator; Force resistance; standard counters.
9. **Related:** Force-meld; Force-bond; Force harmony.
10. **Game stats:**
    - (game stat: KOTOR "Battle Meditation" gives party bonuses and enemy penalties, with Improved/Master tiers in KOTOR II.)
    - (game stat: *The Force Unleashed* talent.)
- **Uncertainty:** who drove the Endor battle meditation is a **[conflict]**: Thrawn's theory credits Palpatine (*Heir to the Empire*), while *Insider* 66 credits Declann.

#### U3. Force harmony
1. **Names and classification:** Force harmony. Light side; filed under Control, Sense *and* Alter (*Dark Empire Sourcebook*). [Force harmony][har]
2. **What it does:**
   - Joins several willing users in a pure light-side manifestation.
   - Shields them from dark-side powers and can distract dark-siders.
   - In some cases it cuts a dark-sider off from the dark side completely. It can interrupt dark-side rituals.
   - It can be held "almost indefinitely", with slight strain on the initiator.
   - The initiator can link only as many users as their Force energy allows. Closeness, strength and blood relation intensify it.
   - It "could not cancel out the dark side" in general.
   [Force harmony §Description][har]
3. **Users and feats:**
   - **Luke, Leia and the unborn Anakin Solo** wrapped Palpatine in light and cut him off from the dark side during his Force storm at Pinnacle Base (10 ABY, *Dark Empire* 6).
   - **Initiators listed:** Ood Bnar, Arca Jeth, Thon, Vima-Da-Boda, Taj Junak and Yoda.
   - **Freedon Nadd**, said to know every Force power.
   - **Valin Draco's Shadow Guard.**
   - A group in harmony can defeat Derriphans.
   [Force harmony][har]
4. **Era:** Old Jedi Order; rebuilt by 10 ABY.
5. **How it is learned:** the initiator needs Life Sense and telepathy.
6. **Costs and dangers:** slight strain. The fact that Nadd and the Shadow Guard used a "light side" power is a **[conflict]**.
7. **Counters:** too few or too weak participants; distance.
8. **Related:** Force-meld; battle meditation; Force Light (Part 2a §C).
9. **Game stats:** (game stat: WEG requires Control + Sense + Alter, with prerequisites Life Sense and telepathy.)
- **Uncertainty:** the Nadd claim rests on a sourcebook line that he "knew every Force power". It is a generalisation: [unclear].

#### U4. Oneness
1. **Names and classification:** Oneness, the state of union with the Force. Filed under Control. Usually a light-side manifestation; on rare occasions dark, with "severe temptations to the dark side". [Oneness/Legends][one]
2. **What it does:** the user becomes a living manifestation of the Force, with a sudden surge of power and healing. It cannot be summoned at will by most and is temporary.
3. **Users and feats:**
   - **Anakin Skywalker** at Praesitlyn used light *and* dark to destroy Separatist forces (22 BBY, *Jedi Trial*), and again on Mortis.
   - **Galen Marek** held back the Emperor's lightning and died (2 BBY).
   - **Anakin Solo** regained his fighting skill and struck with lightning while dying; it consumed him (27 ABY).
   - **Ganner Rhysode** held off thousands of Yuuzhan Vong (27 ABY).
   - **Luke** was a "maelstrom of luminous Force energy" on the way to Shimrra (29 ABY).
   - **Jacen Solo** became "a being of glowing light" and defeated Onimi (29 ABY).
   - **Jaina Solo** was briefly restored while fighting Caedus (41 ABY, *Invincible*).
   - **Darth Malgus** (*Deceived*).
   - **King Ommin**, who became one with the dark side.
   - **Barriss Offee**, twice, with bota.
   - **Leia** (*Crucible*).
   [Oneness §Users][one]
4. **Era:** rare in all eras.
5. **How it is learned:** not taught. It arises from crisis or surrender; also from bota (Barriss) or a place of power (Mortis).
6. **Costs and dangers:** it burns the body (Galen, Anakin Solo) and invites dark-side temptation. A dark Oneness (Ommin) is a dark state. See DS1–DS3 §Section 8 on walking between the sides.
7. **Counters:** none recorded.
8. **Related:** Force harmony; Force ghost (Part 3a).
9. **Game stats:** none.
- **Uncertainty:** Mortis episodes (TCW season 3, 2011) are cited on the Legends page; they are used as Wookieepedia cites them.

## Game notes (suggestions only)

These are suggestions for the developer, not lore claims.
- **Tiering.** Legends supports Initiate → Padawan → Knight gating, as Control → Sense → Alter (*The Jedi Path*). A simple rule:
  - Control powers available from start;
  - Sense from apprenticeship;
  - Alter, plus universal powers such as battle meditation and Force harmony, from Knight or Master. Battle meditation and Oneness could be innate or story-only.
- **Sensing ranges by tier,** using the anchors:
  - 10 m passive;
  - about 1 km and more with focus;
  - about 1,000 km telepathy between sensitives;
  - interstellar only through bonds or kin;
  - galaxy-wide only for mass-death disturbances.
- **Costs that Legends itself gives:**
  - exhaustion after Force speed;
  - body damage from Force Body;
  - taint from psychometry on murder objects or corpses;
  - helplessness during hibernation;
  - fleet dependence for battle meditation;
  - the mental overload risk of the meld.
- **Counters as zone rules:**
  - ysalamiri: no Force inside a radius;
  - taozin and the Vong: invisible to Sense, immune to direct mind powers;
  - Toydarians and Hutts: resist mind powers;
  - thought shield and Force resistance: opposed checks.
- **Dark-act flags:** none of the Part 1 powers is a dark act in itself. Flag *uses* instead: domination through Sith battle meditation; ripping memories from the living; Oneness through the dark side.
