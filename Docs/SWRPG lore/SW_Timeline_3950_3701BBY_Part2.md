# SWRPG Galaxy Timeline, 3,950–3,701 BBY (Part 2 of 3): The Long Peace and the Hidden Empire, 3,799–3,701 BBY

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched 24 Nov 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends), as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
Part 2 covers 3,799–3,701 BBY and ends with a table of events that Wookieepedia places somewhere inside the 250-year span without a year. In the Republic this was remembered as one of the most peaceful eras in its history [Timeline 7: Peace for the Republic?](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Timeline_7:_Peace_for_the_Republic%3F); it ends with Freia Kallea charting the Hydian Way (3,705–3,693 BBY). Under that peace, the Sith Emperor's spies were already inside the Republic and even the Jedi Order: in 3,756 BBY the secret Sith agent Jedi Master Barel Ovair went to Yavin 4 to destroy Naga Sadow's spirit [Mission to Yavin 4](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mission_to_Yavin_4). The Emperor also built a second empire on Zakuul and let Darth Ikoral search for lost Sith purebloods (c. 3,712 BBY) [Tenebrae §Final preparations and a new empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrae); [Darth Ikoral](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Ikoral). Columns and types as in Part 1.

### Overview
These are the last of "two centuries of peace and prosperity" in which the Republic makes progress in political stability and technology [note: SWTOR's "Galactic Timeline"] [3756 BBY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/3756_BBY), a period of peace and expansion after the Dark Wars [note: the article on the 3,756 BBY duel] [Duel on Coruscant (Inter–Sith Wars Period) §Prelude](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Duel_on_Coruscant_%28Inter%E2%80%93Sith_Wars_Period%29). The Jedi historian Gnost-Dural later suspected that the Sith spent generations of this period infiltrating Republic politics and the Jedi Order [Mission to Yavin 4 §Prelude](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mission_to_Yavin_4). The Kanz Disorders went on in the Kanz sector throughout [Kanz Disorders/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kanz_Disorders/Legends). In the Empire, the Emperor kept Revan prisoner while he advanced his plans and built up his fleet; Revan's influence through their mental link slowed him [Tenebrae §Final preparations and a new empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrae); [Revan/Legends §Confronting evil](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Revan/Legends).
