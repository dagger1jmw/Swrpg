# Force Nexuses, Vergences and Wounds in the Force

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 22, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends), the Legends mirror starwars.archivum.wiki, a 2014 web-archive snapshot of Wookieepedia, and SWTOR codex text, as cited per entry. Draft for review. Written by Claude (the lore chat), not the Grok bot, in the bot's format.

## Summary
A vergence (or nexus point) is a place, an object or, rarely, a person where the Force is especially concentrated and easier for Force-sensitives to use. A place-based vergence is a Force nexus. Nexuses range from a whole planet to a single room, and can last a moment or millennia. Many form where Force users gathered or where great tragedies happened. Some favour particular abilities, such as visions or stronger psychokinesis. Both Jedi and Sith built temples on them. A wound in the Force is a different phenomenon: a weakness caused by mass death. It masks Force presences nearby.

The sites, with their dates (the game switches each on or off by date):
- **Dark:** Korriban (with the Valley of the Dark Lords) and Dromund Kaas (with the Dark Temple).
- **Light-associated:** the Coruscant Temple's spire (until the Sacking), the Ilum Temple and the Temple of Eedit.
- **Tython:** a world unusually sensitive to the Force.

Most other named nexuses come from later eras.

## Entries

### Vergence / Force nexus (the concept)
- **Type:** Force phenomenon
- **Era:** all eras
- **Key facts:**
  - A vergence, or "nexus point", is centred on a place, an object or, rarely, a person: a spot where the Force is concentrated and easier for sensitive beings to use [Vergence/Legends, intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vergence/Legends).
  - "Force nexus" is the term for place-based vergences [Vergence/Legends, §Force nexuses](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vergence/Legends).
  - Jedi theories about how vergences form all had serious flaws [Vergence/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vergence/Legends).
  - **Scale and duration:** a nexus can be anywhere, from a whole planet down to one room. It isn't bound by time or space, can last a moment or millennia, and can grow or fade [Vergence/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vergence/Legends).
  - **How they form:** many arise where Force users gathered or where great tragedies happened [Vergence/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vergence/Legends).
  - **Effects on abilities:** some nexuses encourage particular abilities, such as visions or stronger psychokinesis [Vergence/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vergence/Legends).
  - **Temples:** the Jedi and the Sith both used nexuses and often built temples on or over them [Vergence/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vergence/Legends).
  - **Objects:** an object left near a nexus for a long time becomes saturated with the Force. Effects on record include a blade that never dulls, absorbing Force powers such as lightning, and faster perception of time. Non-Force-sensitive users can benefit too [Vergence/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vergence/Legends).
  - **Alignment:** objects saturated with light or dark can be intolerable to users of the opposite alignment [Vergence/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vergence/Legends).
- **Related:** Wound in the Force; all location entries below.
- **Uncertainty:** the page doesn't define a "balanced" nexus. It names dark nexuses and light-associated temples, and leaves some (Nam Chorios) without a stated alignment [unclear].

### Wound in the Force (distinct from a nexus)
- **Type:** Force phenomenon
- **Era:** all eras
- **Key facts:**
  - Also called an echo or hole in the Force: a catastrophic weakness in the Force caused by a traumatic event, typically many lives ending in a short time [Wound in the Force/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wound_in_the_Force/Legends).
  - Usually centred on a place (Malachor V, Alderaan), but it can form in a person (Meetra Surik, Darth Nihilus) [Wound in the Force/Legends, §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wound_in_the_Force/Legends).
  - It distorts the Force like white noise, so a Force-user close to a wound can't easily be sensed [Wound in the Force/Legends, §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wound_in_the_Force/Legends).
- **Related:** Malachor V; Dagobah's cave (which also masks a presence, by a different means).
- **Uncertainty:** none.

### Korriban and the Valley of the Dark Lords
- **Type:** location (planet-level dark nexus, with a site-level nexus inside it)
- **Era:** ancient Sith era onward; Imperial-held from 3681 BBY (see the planets file)
- **Key facts:**
  - Korriban is a dark side nexus, especially the Valley of the Dark Lords, shaped by long Sith presence [Vergence/Legends, §Force nexuses](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vergence/Legends).
- **Related:** SW_Korriban_Academy_Part1/Part2; SW_Planets_Travel_OldRepublic (Korriban).
- **Uncertainty:** none.

### Dromund Kaas and the Dark Temple
- **Type:** location (planet-level dark nexus, with a site-level nexus inside it)
- **Era:** from the Emperor's resettlement (4980 BBY) onward
- **Key facts:**
  - The Emperor's Sith magic warped the atmosphere into constant storms, and the planet was the site of a powerful dark side nexus [Dromund system, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/Dromund_system).
  - SWTOR's codex says the Emperor's rituals and experiments affected the planet itself and left it storm-wracked [SWTOR codex, "Galactic History 33: Dromund Kaas"](https://swtor.jedipedia.net/en/cdx/16141074284378475103).
  - The SWTOR codex calls the Dark Temple "a nexus of powerful dark side energy", where ancient weapons and secrets are sealed away [SWTOR codex, "The Dark Temple" (locations)](https://swtor.jedipedia.net/en/cdx/dromund-kaas-2).
  - The Dark Temple was built by Pharshol, one of the Old Sith Empire's colonists. The Emperor claimed it after 4980 BBY, expanded it, and designed it to contain dark side energy, as a prison and a vault for powerful artifacts [Dark Temple, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/Dark_Temple).
  - The grounds are dangerous to the weak-willed: digging power tunnels beneath the temple drove a thousand slaves mad. The temple stayed sealed until shortly before the Cold War-era codex entry [SWTOR codex, "The Dark Temple"](https://swtor.jedipedia.net/en/cdx/the-dark-temple).
- **Related:** SW_Dromund_Kaas_Part1/Part2.
- **Uncertainty:**
  - The Dark Temple is "sealed" in the codex until recently. The date it was opened should come from the Dromund Kaas files [unclear].
  - The later Dark Force Temple, "a dark side nexus within a dark side nexus", belongs to a later era and is noted under Not used.

### Tython
- **Type:** location (planet)
- **Era:** ancient (Je'daii) onward; lanes lost after the Force Wars; rediscovered after 3653 (the planets file and batch 20)
- **Key facts:**
  - Tython's environment was remarkably sensitive to the Force. A strong disturbance, such as a being very strong in the light or dark side, could cause immense Force storms and groundquakes [Tython (Wookieepedia, 2014 web-archive snapshot)](https://webarchive.library.unt.edu/unt/csid/20140911225948/http://starwars.wikia.com/wiki/Tython).
  - The early settlers found Tython unsafe for those who couldn't sense the Force, and spread out to other worlds in the system [same](https://webarchive.library.unt.edu/unt/csid/20140911225948/http://starwars.wikia.com/wiki/Tython).
  - Force storms struck whenever Tython's balance shifted between the light and dark sides [same](https://webarchive.library.unt.edu/unt/csid/20140911225948/http://starwars.wikia.com/wiki/Tython).
- **Related:** SW_Planets_Travel_OldRepublic (Tython, gated); the Order seat (batch 20).
- **Uncertainty:**
  - None of the fetched Legends text uses the word "nexus" for Tython. It describes Tython as exceptionally Force-sensitive, with Force storms. Calling it a planet-level nexus is a reasonable reading but not a quotation [inferred].
  - Tython's leaning is balance rather than light, per the storm description [inferred].
  - **Ruling (Jared, Nov 22, 2026):** a planet-level nexus, leaning balanced. It's too dangerous for non-Force-users to inhabit because the planet is so tied to the Force, and too much conflict causes Force storms (consistent with the settlers spreading to other worlds, cited above).
  - **Tension:** SWTOR's Twi'lek colony (Kalikori village; listed under Tython's places in SW_Planets_Travel_OldRepublic) sits awkwardly with this, and Jared considers it a break from the lore. It can stay as an on-record place, but the narrator shouldn't treat Tython as open to ordinary settlement.

### Coruscant: the sacred spire
- **Type:** location (site-level nexus, light)
- **Era:** before the Jedi through the Yuuzhan Vong War and later. The Temple over it is destroyed in the Sacking of Coruscant (3653 BBY; see the timeline file), but the spire itself remains.
- **Key facts:**
  - The sacred spire is a natural mountain peak, the foundation of the Jedi Temple. The native Coruscanti revered it as a light side vergence, and it drew various Force traditions to it [Sacred spire, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/sacred_spire).
  - The Temple Spire was built above and around the sacred spire, the mountain holding the Force nexus where the Temple began. As the building grew it covered and reshaped much of the mountain, but the peak kept its original shape inside the Spire [Temple Spire/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Temple_Spire/Legends).
  - The Temple was tainted by Lord Nyax after it was abandoned in the Yuuzhan Vong War [Vergence/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vergence/Legends). Nyax breached the wall around the spire and absorbed its energies. His malevolence lingered in the spire for a time after his death, and Jacen Solo, visiting the ruins later, still sensed the darkness [Attack on the Sacred Spire, archivum](https://archivum.wiki/article/attack_on_the_sacred_spire).
- **Related:** SW_Jedi_Temple_Life_GGW; SW_Coruscant_City_GGW.
- **Ruling (Jared, Nov 22, 2026):** the spire is a natural light side nexus that predates the Jedi and was never destroyed. The Sacking razed the Temple, not the mountain. Its only corruption is Nyax's, in a much later era.
- **Uncertainty:** no source describes the spire's state right after the 3653 Sacking. Treating the nexus as unchanged through it follows from the ruling and from the spire still standing in later eras [inferred].
- **Canon only, not used:** the Sith shrine said to have stood on the spire before the Jedi comes from Canon (High Republic) material.

### Ilum: the Crystal Caves (and the Ilum Temple)
- **Type:** location (site-level nexus, light-associated)
- **Era:** from the caves' discovery onward; in use as a Republic and Jedi staging world in the war (planets file)
- **Key facts:**
  - The Crystal Caves themselves are a powerful nexus of Force energy, forming a vergence. The Jedi Temple stands at the caves' largest natural entrance [Crystal Caves, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/Crystal_Caves).
  - The caves also affect the minds of young Jedi seeking their first crystal: an effect is described that masks the crystals from them [Crystal Caves, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/Crystal_Caves).
  - SWTOR's codex: the ancient Jedi built a temple above Ilum's crystal caverns as a home and training ground. Despite the climate, its isolation and its lightsaber crystals made it ideal for schooling Padawans [SWTOR codex, "The Ilum Temple"](https://swtor.jedipedia.net/en/cdx/the-ilum-temple).
- **Related:** SW_Planets_Travel_OldRepublic (Ilum: closed to the Empire until 3640 BBY); SW_Lightsabers_Crystals_Materials_AllEras.
- **Correction:** an earlier draft of this file said the Ilum Temple was "built over a powerful nexus", citing the Vergence page. Jared couldn't find that on the Vergence page or on the Jedi Temple (Ilum) page, and it came from a summarised fetch. The sourced statement is that the caves are the nexus; the temple sits at their entrance.
- **Uncertainty:** none.

### Temple of Eedit (Devaron)
- **Type:** location (site-level nexus, light-associated)
- **Era:** built at an unstated date; recorded history in the Clone Wars
- **Key facts:**
  - The Temple of Eedit is a Jedi temple on Devaron, built on a vergence in the Force and used as a place of personal renewal and strengthening [Temple of Eedit, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/temple_of_eedit).
  - The Order built it there because Devaron had many Force-sensitives and a powerful vergence, as an academy for Devaronians suited to the Jedi [Temple of Eedit, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/Temple_of_Eedit).
  - The vergence is strongest in the temple's courtyard, and it makes learning new Force techniques and healing the spirit easier [Temple of Eedit, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/temple_of_eedit).
- **Related:** Devaron (planets file).
- **Uncertainty:** the founding date isn't stated, so its earliest date is [unclear]. This is the clearest Legends statement that a nexus makes learning easier (see Not found).

### Dagobah and the Dark Side Cave
- **Type:** location (a Force-rich world, with a site-level dark nexus)
- **Era:** the cave is a dark nexus from c. 700 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - The cave was tainted by the death of dark side users, and Luke Skywalker trained there [Vergence/Legends, §Force nexuses](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vergence/Legends).
  - The cave lies deep in the equatorial swamps and holds a vergence and the apparition of a dark Jedi. Those who enter often have visions of the past, the future, or possible futures [Dark Side Cave, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/Dark_Side_Cave).
  - **Origin, c. 700 BBY:** a Jedi team was sent after Bpfasshi Dark Jedi, and the Jedi Knight Minch tracked their leader to a cave on Dagobah and killed him there [Minch, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/minch); [Duel on Dagobah (700 BBY), archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/duel_on_dagobah_(700_bby)). His blood and death tainted the cave with the dark side, turning it over time into a focus of malevolent Force energy [Minch, archivum](https://www.archivum.wiki/article/minch). The place absorbed his energies and became the home of the Dark Spirit [Dark Spirit, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/Dark_Spirit).
  - In the Purge, Yoda found that living near the cave helped neutralise his strong light side signature, so the Sith couldn't find him [Dark Side Cave, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/Dark_Side_Cave).
- **Related:** Wound in the Force (another way a presence is masked).
- **Uncertainty:**
  - Dagobah being Force-rich as a whole, because of its abundant life, is stated only on Canon or non-Legends pages fetched for this file. It's widely assumed in Legends, but it isn't cited here [unclear]. See Not found.

### Malachor V
- **Type:** location (wound in the Force)
- **Era:** wound from 3960 BBY; sealed when the planet was destroyed (c. 3951 BBY)
- **Key facts:**
  - Malachor V is the standard example of a place-centred wound in the Force [Wound in the Force/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wound_in_the_Force/Legends).
  - The deaths in the battle of 3960 BBY created the wound [Battle of Malachor V, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/battle_of_malachor_v).
  - Meetra Surik later had the Mass Shadow Generator activated, destroying the planet and sealing the wound [Disturbance in the Force, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/disturbance_in_the_force).
  - A Sith Empire built the Trayus Academy there "thousands of years" before the Mandalorian Wars [Malachor V](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Malachor_V).
  - **Before the wound:** Kreia describes it as a place where the dark side runs strong, "something of the Sith" but fuelled by war, which corrupts everyone who walks on it [Malachor V](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Malachor_V). The Trayus Core, the academy's innermost sanctum, was an altar suspended over a powerful upwelling of dark side energy [Trayus Core, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/trayus_core). Revan used Malachor's dark side energies to bind his followers, and the place's corrupting influence weakened Jedi so they could be turned [Trayus Academy, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/Trayus_Academy).
  - **The second destruction:** when the Mass Shadow Generator was reactivated, the Trayus Core imploded as the planet and the academy were destroyed, releasing the dark side energies contained there [Trayus Core, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/trayus_core).
  - **Ruling (Jared, Nov 22, 2026):** Malachor V was a dark side nexus before the Mass Shadow Generator turned it into a wound. No fetched source uses the word "nexus" for it, but the descriptions above match one.
- **Related:** SW_Planets_Travel_OldRepublic (Malachor V: ruined, surveyed by 3640 BBY).
- **Uncertainty:** whether any dark presence remains after the second destruction (c. 3951 BBY) isn't stated [unclear]. See Decisions needed.

## Not found
- A Legends statement that Dagobah as a whole is Force-rich because of its life.
- A Legends statement using the word "nexus" for Tython itself.
- The Temple of Eedit's founding date.
- The spire's state right after the 3653 Sacking (treated as unchanged; see the entry).
- Any Legends statement that training on a nexus speeds growth in general. The sources say nexuses make the Force easier to use and favour some abilities. The Temple of Eedit's vergence is said to make learning new Force techniques easier, the closest statement to a training bonus.

## Game notes (optional, suggestions only)
- *Suggestion:* the nexus data already in the game (planet-level and site-level, each with a leaning) fits this file. Korriban (planet) with the Valley of the Dark Lords (site) and Dromund Kaas (planet) with the Dark Temple (site) are the clearest era examples of a site inside a nexus planet.
- *Suggestion:* the training bonus is a design reading of "the Force is easier to use there" (see Not found). Some nexuses could boost only specific abilities (visions, psychokinesis), which the source does say.
- *Suggestion:* the masking effect (Dagobah's cave, wounds in the Force) could make a character harder to sense or track nearby.
- *Suggestion:* a dark nexus could tempt or corrupt light-side users (the Spintir icewolves; Dromund Kaas driving slaves mad), tied to the alignment system when it exists.
- *Suggestion:* objects kept at a nexus for a long time could gain properties (the blade that never dulls).

## Decisions needed
1. **Tython:** DECIDED (Jared, Nov 22, 2026). Treat it as a planet-level nexus, leaning balanced, tagged [inferred]. It's a planet teeming with the Force and so tied to it that it's too dangerous for non-Force-users to live on; too much conflict causes Force storms. SWTOR's Twi'lek colony at Kalikori village sits awkwardly with that, and Jared considers it a break from the lore (see the Tython entry). Keep it only as the game's on-record place, not as evidence that ordinary people settle Tython [ruling].
2. **Malachor V:** REVISED (Jared, Nov 22, 2026). It was a dark side nexus before the Mass Shadow Generator turned it into a wound. The Sith-built Trayus Academy's core sat over a geyser of dark side energy, and the planet corrupted those who walked on it (see the Malachor V entry). It became a wound in 3960 BBY. Its second destruction (c. 3951 BBY) sealed the wound, and the core imploded, releasing its dark energies. Open: whether any dark presence remains after 3951, when the shattered world is surveyed and excavated (by 3640). Best fit: a dark nexus before 3960, a wound 3960–c. 3951, and unclear after. See SW_Malachor_V.
3. **Dagobah's cave:** DECIDED. Its dark nexus dates from c. 700 BBY, when the Jedi Minch killed a Bpfasshi Dark Jedi leader there. Switch it on from 700 BBY.
4. **Coruscant sacred spire:** DECIDED. A natural light side nexus that predates the Jedi and runs continuously; the 3653 Sacking razed the Temple, not the spire. Only corruption: Lord Nyax, in the Yuuzhan Vong War era (lingering darkness Jacen Solo later sensed). No end date.
5. **Ilum and Eedit:** add the Ilum Crystal Caves (the nexus itself; the temple stands at their entrance) as a light-associated site, closed to the Empire until 3640. Add the Temple of Eedit as a light-associated site whose vergence makes learning new techniques easier, with its start date [unclear] until the founding date is found.
6. **Dromund Kaas:** DECIDED (Jared, Nov 22, 2026). A planet-level dark nexus, with the Dark Temple as a stronger site inside it.
