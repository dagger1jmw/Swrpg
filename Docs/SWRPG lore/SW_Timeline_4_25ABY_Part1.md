# SWRPG Galaxy Timeline, 4–25 ABY (Part 1 of 5): After Bakura to the Taking of Coruscant (4–7 ABY)

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 24, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends), as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
This five-part file rebuilds the "NEW REPUBLIC ERA — 4–25 ABY" block of `SW_Legends_Galaxy_Timeline.md` as one sourced row per event, from the last 4 ABY rows of `SW_Timeline_19BBY_4ABY_Part4.md` (Bakura Incident to the liberation of Kashyyyk, not repeated here) to the eve of the Yuuzhan Vong invasion. Part 1 (this file) covers the consequences of Bakura and the Ssi-ruuk, the Imperial Civil War and the first warlords, Isard's seizure of the Empire, the *X-wing* comics' "Rise of Isard", Mindor, and the Core Campaign of *X-Wing: Rogue Squadron* and *Wedge's Gamble* to the taking of Coruscant. Part 2 covers 7–9 ABY (Krytos, Bacta War, Wraith Squadron and Zsinj, Courtship, Thrawn, Isard's Revenge). Part 3 covers 10–13 ABY (Dark Empire, Praxeum, Daala, Darksaber, Adumar). Part 4 covers 14–19 ABY (to the Pellaeon–Gavrisom Treaty). Part 5 covers 19–25 ABY and holds the back sections for the whole set. Key facts: Wookieepedia dates the Imperial Civil War 4–12 ABY, not "4–5 ABY"; it gives two years for the taking of Coruscant, ruled 6 ABY on 24 Nov 2026.

## Entries
How to read the tables: the era heading, rule lines and "### Overview" follow the old file and the 3,700–3,600 BBY files. Columns: Date | Event | Type | Place | Who | Source. Type is War, Control, Leader, Death, Order, Tech or Background (as in the format model; births are given as Background). Where Wookieepedia uses *The Essential Atlas*'s Great ReSynchronization dates (year:month:day, "GrS"), they are given in brackets. **Calendar note:** Wookieepedia's own reasoning (note on [Mission to Kessel (Galactic Civil War)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mission_to_Kessel_(Galactic_Civil_War))) is that the GrS year runs about three months ahead of the ABY year (the Battle of Yavin fell in GrS 35:3), so GrS 41 ≈ 6 ABY from its third month, and a GrS date in months 1–2 belongs to the previous ABY year. [Ruled (Jared, 24 Nov 2026): adopted. Every date is given in ABY; a GrS date is only a note beside it] "X:" abbreviates the *X-Wing* novels; "XWRS" the *X-Wing Rogue Squadron* comics. "Uncited" means the Wookieepedia line itself has no reference.

### Overview
After Endor the Empire split into a central government and rival splinter factions; this internal war is the Imperial Civil War (also "Imperial Mutiny", "War of Purification", "Time of Destruction"), from shortly after Endor in 4 ABY to the Imperial Reunification of 12 ABY [Imperial Civil War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Civil_War). Sate Pestage ruled briefly as interim emperor before Ysanne Isard took over [Sate Pestage/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sate_Pestage/Legends) [Ysanne Isard](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ysanne_Isard). The New Republic, after campaigns across the two years after Endor, launched the Core Campaign in 6 ABY and took Coruscant [Core Campaign](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Core_Campaign). Isard left the capital poisoned with the Krytos virus, fled to Thyferra and was beaten in the Bacta War; Zsinj, the strongest warlord, was hunted down and killed in 8 ABY [Krytos virus](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Krytos_virus) [Bacta War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bacta_War) [Zsinj](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zsinj). Thrawn's 9 ABY campaign and the reborn Emperor's Operation Shadow Hand (10–11 ABY) nearly reversed the war [Thrawn campaign](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Thrawn_campaign) [Operation Shadow Hand](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Operation_Shadow_Hand). Luke founded the Jedi Praxeum on Yavin 4 in 11 ABY [Jedi Praxeum](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Praxeum). Daala executed the warlords and the Imperial Remnant formed in 12 ABY [12 ABY/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/12_ABY/Legends). After the Black Fleet Crisis, the Almanian Uprising, the Imperial Skirmishes and the Corellian Crisis, the Caamas Document Crisis ended with the Pellaeon–Gavrisom Treaty in 19 ABY, signed aboard the *Chimaera* [19 ABY/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/19_ABY/Legends). The years 19–24 ABY saw the Second Imperium and the Diversity Alliance, fought mostly by the young Jedi of the Praxeum [23 ABY/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/23_ABY/Legends) [24 ABY/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/24_ABY/Legends).

#### Supporting article: Ysanne Isard
- Born c. 30 BBY on Coruscant; died 9 ABY aboard the *Lusankya* [Ysanne Isard](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ysanne_Isard).
