# Lightsaber Forms (All Legends Eras), Part 1: The Seven Classic Forms

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 21, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
Covers the seven classic Jedi forms (Shii-Cho, Makashi, Soresu, Ataru, Shien/Djem So, Niman, Juyo/Vaapad) and Shien's variants, across every Legends era from the first lightsaber forms to the Legacy era. Each entry covers origin, the eras it was common or rare, teachers, strengths and weaknesses, counters, stance, Force pairing, light/dark lean and named practitioners. Key facts: each form answered the threats of its age (Makashi for saber duels, Soresu for blasters). Juyo and Vaapad carry an explicit dark-side risk, so access was restricted (hand-picked students after Ruusan; Windu's permission for Vaapad). Part 2 covers the other forms and styles.

### Era overview (how the forms arose)
- **Type:** overview of form development
- **Era:** From the first combat lightsabers (7000 or 5000 BBY [conflict]) to the Legacy era
- **Key facts:**

- Forms were usually created to answer the weapons of their age. Makashi arose when duels against Dark Jedi were common; Soresu arose when blasters were the main threat. [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends)
- Order of development: Shii-Cho (sword-to-saber transition) → Makashi (saber duels) → Soresu (blasters) → Ataru and Shien in parallel (answer to Soresu's passivity) → later Djem So (saber-dueling variant of Form V) → Niman (moderation) → Juyo existed by the First Jedi Purge. [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Early developments](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends)
- Lightsabers became the standard Jedi weapon by 7000 BBY per [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Early developments](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends) (citing *Dark Forces: Jedi Knight*). [conflict] The Force-imbued sword page says protosabers became the standard Jedi weapon by 5000 BBY. [Force-imbued sword/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force-imbued_sword/Legends)
- After Ruusan (1000 BBY), Makashi was seen as obsolete, and double-bladed sabers were almost never used in real combat by 32 BBY. [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Post-Ruusan developments](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends)
- In the Clone Wars, the moderate forms the Jedi had favored for a millennium did badly on battlefields; every Niman user at the First Battle of Geonosis died. [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Developments during the Clone Wars](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends)
- During the Great Jedi Purge, development "slowed to a crawl" and was led by the Sith, but the forms survived in records, holocrons and among the Sith. [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Developments during the Great Jedi Purge](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends)
- The New Jedi Order (NJO) first used simplified training (Strong/Medium/Fast styles, three rings of defense), then rediscovered the seven forms from *The Jedi Path* and holocrons, especially the Great Holocron's Cin Drallig recording. ### Form I — Shii-Cho
- **Type:** lightsaber form
- **Era:** From the sword-to-saber transition (no date given) to the NJO and Legacy eras; the training form throughout
- **Key facts:**

**Other names:** Way of the Sarlacc; the Determination Form. [Form I/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_I/Legends)

**Creation:** The first form, made during the shift from metal swords to lightsabers, keeping old sword attacks, parries and body zones. No named founder is given. [Form I/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_I/Legends) Early sabers were unstable and ceremonial; Shii-Cho was the first style built for combat sabers. [Form I/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_I/Legends)

**Eras common or rare:**
- It came before Dark Jedi and Sith, so it did not address saber-vs-saber fighting. Early darksiders still used it because it was the only option, and Makashi then replaced it for dueling. [Form I/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_I/Legends)
- It stayed the training form for millennia, and almost every Jedi duelist learned some of it. [Form I/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_I/Legends) [Form I/Legends §Notable practitioners](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_I/Legends)

**Who taught it:** Initiates learned it first. [Form I/Legends §Training and practice](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_I/Legends) Dooku knew it well enough to train Grievous and the IG-100 MagnaGuards in it. [Form I/Legends §Notable practitioners](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_I/Legends) The failed Jedi Haazen trained Lucien Draay. [Form I/Legends §Applications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_I/Legends)

**Strengths:**
- Wide, sweeping moves suited to fighting many opponents. [Form I/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_I/Legends)
- In a master's hands it was fluid but highly random and unpredictable. [Form I/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_I/Legends)
- A dependable fallback when nothing else worked. [Form I/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_I/Legends)

**Weaknesses:**
- Weaker against a single mobile opponent, who could find the flaws in its clumsier bladework. [Form I/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_I/Legends)
- Considered weak against Form II. [Form I/Legends §Applications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_I/Legends)

**Counters:** Makashi was built specifically to counter it. [Form II/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_II/Legends)

**Stance and footwork:**
- Deliberate tactics: advance step by step and cut off the opponent's angles. [Form I/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_I/Legends)
- Dooku: "deliberate as a lumberdroid ... clumsy but relentlessly dogged" (short quote). [Form I/Legends §Applications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_I/Legends)
- It set the six body target zones and two modes, "Ideal Form" (sparring at perfect horizontal and vertical angles) and "Live Combat Form" (diagonal strikes for speed). See CombatTechniques for the zones. [Form I/Legends §Known moves and maneuvers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_I/Legends)

**Signature moves:** Disarming Slash (a power strike that rips the weapon away; a sun djem move) and Sarlacc Sweep (a wide strike against several enemies). [Form I/Legends §Known moves and maneuvers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_I/Legends)

**Force pairing:** Early blast-deflect training was done blindfolded so students relied on the Force. Later training used sequences and velocities, carried over to all later forms. [Form I/Legends §Training and practice](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_I/Legends)

**Light/dark lean:** Its philosophy aimed at disarming without serious harm (sun djem). But it fostered an "emotionally-heated mindset" with a strong pull toward lethal intent, so it needed restraint. Kit Fisto noted how hard that pull was to control. [Form I/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_I/Legends) [Form I/Legends §Applications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_I/Legends)

### Form II — Makashi
- **Type:** lightsaber form
- **Era:** Before the Hundred-Year Darkness; revived in the New Sith Wars; rare after Ruusan (1000 BBY); rediscovered by the NJO
- **Key facts:**

**Other names:** Way of the Ysalamiri; the Contention Form. [Form II/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_II/Legends)

**Creation:** Made for saber-vs-saber combat to fix Form I's failings, in an era when fights against darksiders and rogue Jedi were "almost routine". No founder is named. [Form II/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_II/Legends) [Form II/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_II/Legends)

**Eras common or rare:**
- It faded after the Hundred-Year Darkness destroyed most Dark Jedi and as blasters spread. Soresu replaced it. [Form II/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_II/Legends)
- It came back during the New Sith Wars, then faded again after Ruusan and never regained wide use. [Form II/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_II/Legends)
- After Ruusan it was still taught (Skarch Vaunk praised its discipline), and the Mid-Year Fete held classical Makashi duels. [Form II/Legends §Practitioners](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_II/Legends)
- By the Clone Wars few practiced it; Dooku was the most notable. [Form II/Legends §Practitioners](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_II/Legends)
- The NJO rediscovered it. [Form II/Legends §Applications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_II/Legends)

**Who taught it:** Kreia taught the Exile the basics; Kas'im trained Fohargh; Dooku trained Ventress and Grievous. Grievous passed it to his MagnaGuards. Yoda had once taught Dooku the Makashi salute. [Form II/Legends §Practitioners](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_II/Legends)

**Strengths:**
- Precision, economy of motion and feints, so it attacks and defends with little energy. Best against one duelist. [Form II/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_II/Legends)
- Strong focus on not being disarmed. [Form II/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_II/Legends)
- Practitioners were "uncommonly adept" at defending against Force-based attacks. [Form II/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_II/Legends)

**Weaknesses:**
- Poor blast-deflection training (the reason it declined), though skill could make up for this. [Form II/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_II/Legends)
- Less effective against groups. [Form II/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_II/Legends)
- **Main flaw:** it generates little kinetic energy, so a strong opponent can bash through it, especially a Djem So user. Strength training or cybernetics could compensate. [Form II/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_II/Legends)

**Counters:** Shii-Cho (by design). [Form II/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_II/Legends) Most Niman users found it "almost impossible" to beat a Makashi user. [Form VI/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VI/Legends) Dooku had a named anti-Ataru tactic: thrust at the legs to bait a flip, then cut the spine. [Form IV/Legends §Applications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_IV/Legends)

**Countered by:** Djem So power. [Form II/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_II/Legends) [Form V/Legends §Djem So](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_V/Legends) Dooku's many wins over power duelists show this was not guaranteed. [Form II/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_II/Legends)

**Stance and footwork:**
- Opening stance is a one-handed low guard, blade down at the side, feet shoulder-width apart, sometimes side-on.
- The thumb points down the blade for small, accurate moves.
- Footwork runs along a single front-and-back line.
- It avoids Form IV's leaps and acrobatics; it is often one-handed.
- Salute: blade vertical before the face, then an X-shaped flourish. [Form II/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_II/Legends) [Form II/Legends §Known moves and maneuvers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_II/Legends)

**Signature moves:**
- Marks of contact: sun djem done as precise attacks on the weapon or fingers, and shiak (stab).
- Named moves: "contentious opportunity" (exploit an opening) and "Makashi riposte" (slightly alter the attack angle, then counter). [Form II/Legends §Known moves and maneuvers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_II/Legends)

**Weapons:**
- Curved-hilt sabers (better precision and control) and lightfoils (Mecrosa Order) are typical.
- Standard hilts were also used (Qui-Gon, Shaak Ti, Kento Marek, Vader).
- Ventress used it with a saberstaff. [Form II/Legends §Makashi Weapon Specifications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_II/Legends)

**Force pairing:** Notable for defending against Force attacks (above). [Form II/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_II/Legends)

**Light/dark lean:** Neutral. It was used by Jedi who fought darksiders and by darksiders who fought Jedi. [Form II/Legends §Practitioners](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_II/Legends) Dooku stressed a level head over rage. [Form II/Legends §Applications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_II/Legends) [inferred: no inherent dark lean is stated]

### Form III — Soresu
- **Type:** lightsaber form
- **Era:** From the spread of blasters to the NJO
- **Key facts:**

**Other names:** Way of the Mynock; the Resilience Form. [Form III/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_III/Legends)

**Creation:** Developed as blasters spread, building on Form I blast-deflect training. No founder is named. [Form III/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_III/Legends) [Form III/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_III/Legends)

**Eras common or rare:**
- It existed for thousands of years. [Form III/Legends §Practitioners](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_III/Legends)
- It was the most common form in the Jedi Order near the end of the Clone Wars. [Form III/Legends §Practitioners](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_III/Legends)

**Who taught it:**
- Kreia (to the Exile) and Kas'im (to several apprentices, e.g. Fohargh). [Form III/Legends §Practitioners](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_III/Legends)
- Bane taught it to Zannah because he judged her too small for aggressive forms. [Form III/Legends §Practitioners](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_III/Legends) [Form III/Legends §Applications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_III/Legends)
- Cin Drallig "trained many". [Form III/Legends §Practitioners](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_III/Legends)
- Dooku taught it to Grievous and the MagnaGuards. [Form III/Legends §Practitioners](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_III/Legends)

**Strengths:**
- The most defensive form, with tight, efficient moves and constant blade motion for near-total cover. [Form III/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_III/Legends)
- Works against one or many opponents, and against blasters and sabers. [Form III/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_III/Legends)
- Built for long fights: observe, outlast, then exploit lapses. A user can choose to kill, disarm or intimidate. [Form III/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_III/Legends)
- Core idea: stay "within the eye of the storm". [Form III/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_III/Legends)

**Weaknesses:**
- No dedicated offensive sequences; it "facilitated survival rather than victory". [Form III/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_III/Legends)
- Users struggled to take the initiative. [Form III/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_III/Legends)
- Guards sit close to the body, so small lapses mean injury. [Form III/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_III/Legends)
- It can exhaust its user against a foe who keeps up "aggressive but controlled" pressure. Cited examples: Maul, Vader on the Death Star, Shaak Ti vs Galen Marek. [Form III/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_III/Legends) [Form III/Legends §Applications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_III/Legends)

**Counters:** Kenobi's Soresu "perfectly countered" Vader's furious Form V on Mustafar. [Form III/Legends §Applications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_III/Legends) Ataru and Shien were developed as answers to Soresu's passivity. [Form III/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_III/Legends)

**Stance and footwork:**
- Opening stance: one-handed grip, blade held back and angled forward, off-hand raised in challenge, dominant foot back.
- Brace-ready stance: two-handed, hilt at the waist, blade vertical, dominant foot back, other foot forward and to the side.
- A variant holds the blade high in a drop-parry. [Form III/Legends §Known Moves and Maneuvers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_III/Legends)

**Signature moves:**
- "Deflecting slash": turn a deflection's momentum into a cut at a nearby target and close in. Careful use can send the bolt back at the shooter, though that is more a Shien skill.
- "Circle of shelter": a moving meditation that could hold off as many as twenty shooters. Long use could open the mind to visions of the future. [Form III/Legends §Known Moves and Maneuvers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_III/Legends)

**Force pairing:** Meditative defense (circle of shelter). [Form III/Legends §Known Moves and Maneuvers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_III/Legends)

**Light/dark lean:** Light. It came to be seen as the "ultimate expression of non-aggressive Jedi philosophy". [Form III/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_III/Legends) Zannah disliked it because of its Jedi associations. [Form III/Legends §Practitioners](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_III/Legends)

### Form IV — Ataru
- **Type:** lightsaber form
- **Era:** Old Republic (Mandalorian Wars) to the NJO; discouraged for Padawans c. 1000 BBY
- **Key facts:**

**Other names:** Way of the Hawk-Bat; the Aggression Form. [Form IV/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_IV/Legends)

**Creation:** No founder is named. It dates to at least the Mandalorian Wars (when it was common). [Form IV/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_IV/Legends) It was developed alongside Shien as an answer to Soresu. [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Early developments](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends) [Form III/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_III/Legends)

**Eras common or rare:**
- Common in the Mandalorian Wars and the late Republic; also used by Sith. [Form IV/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_IV/Legends) [Form IV/Legends §Applications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_IV/Legends)

- The Hawk-Bat Swoop and Saber Swarm grew popular in the New Sith Wars. [Form IV/Legends §Moves and maneuvers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_IV/Legends)
- The NJO preserved it; Luke learned it from Yoda and taught it. [Form IV/Legends §Practitioners](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_IV/Legends)

**Strengths:**
- Speed, agility and Force-assisted acrobatics (somersaults, leaping strikes, attacks from every angle).
- Effective against single opponents.
- Lets users overcome physical limits, including old age (Yoda). [Form IV/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_IV/Legends) [Form IV/Legends §Applications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_IV/Legends)

**Weaknesses:**
- Weaker in long fights; it taxes the body. *The Jedi Path* advises withdrawing if a burst of attacks fails to win. [Form IV/Legends §Applications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_IV/Legends)
- Needs open space. [Form IV/Legends §Applications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_IV/Legends)
- Not recommended against blasters, especially many shooters, or against groups. [Form IV/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_IV/Legends) [Form IV/Legends §Applications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_IV/Legends)
- Qui-Gon's death on Naboo is linked to fatigue and a lack of space. [Form IV/Legends §Applications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_IV/Legends)

**Counters / countered by:**
- Dooku claimed he knew "every weakness of the Ataru form, with its ridiculous acrobatics". [Form IV/Legends §Applications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_IV/Legends)
- Sokan users mixed in Ataru's kinetic elements. [Sokan §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sokan)

**Stance and footwork:**
- Opening stance is a balanced two-handed guard, blade vertical at the right or left side. [Form IV/Legends §Moves and maneuvers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_IV/Legends)
- The "Jedi ready" stance has much in common with the Ataru guard. [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Ready Stances](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends)
- Su ma rotations: jung su ma (spinning), ton su ma (somersaults), en su ma (cartwheels), plus Force-enhanced jumps. These made up most of Yoda's style. [Form IV/Legends §Applications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_IV/Legends)

**Signature moves:** Hawk-Bat Swoop (strike and escape before the foe reacts) and Saber Swarm (many short strikes that force a defensive posture). Both relate to the Falling Leaf. [Form IV/Legends §Moves and maneuvers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_IV/Legends)

### Form V — Shien / Djem So
- **Type:** lightsaber form (with variants)
- **Era:** Old Republic (Ulic Qel-Droma, c. 3996 BBY) to the NJO
- **Key facts:**

**Other names:** Way of the Krayt Dragon; the Perseverance Form. [Form V/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_V/Legends)

**Creation:**
- Developed by Form III masters who felt Soresu dragged fights out. [Form V/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_V/Legends) [Form V/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_V/Legends)
- It combined Form III defense with Form II's more aggressive philosophy. [Form V/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_V/Legends)
- It appeared before the Great Sith War (which began 3996 BBY). Djem So was developed before 4000 BBY. [Form V/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_V/Legends) [Form V/Legends §Practitioners](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_V/Legends)

**Eras:** Used through the Jedi Civil War, New Sith Wars, Clone Wars and beyond; it survived the Purge into the NJO. [Form V/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_V/Legends)

**General traits:**
- The most physically demanding form, and it needs more strength. [Form V/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_V/Legends)
- Some Jedi felt it encouraged domination. Cin Drallig was ambivalent about it. [Form V/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_V/Legends)
- Large Jedi favored it. Anakin held that any Jedi could use it by boosting strength with the Force. [Form V/Legends §Applications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_V/Legends)
- Most users preferred one of its two variants. [Form V/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_V/Legends)

#### Shien (classical variant)
- **Era:** Existed at least by the Jedi Civil War (began 3959 BBY). [Form V/Legends §Shien](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_V/Legends)
- **Strengths:** Guards against blaster fire and strikes while keeping strong counterattacks. Best against several opponents. [Form V/Legends §Shien](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_V/Legends)
- **Weakness:** Weak against a single opponent. [Form V/Legends §Shien](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_V/Legends)
- **Stance:** High guard, hilt two-handed above the head, blade angled up and back, dominant leg back for step-through strikes. [Form V/Legends §Shien](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_V/Legends)
- **Moves:** "Barrier of Blades" (defends against fire and sends some bolts back) and "Shien Deflection" (deflect while leaping at the foe). [Form V/Legends §Shien](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_V/Legends)
- **Blaster redirection:** Shien focused on sending bolts back to their source. [Form V/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_V/Legends) Soresu's deflecting slash could do it, but the skill was "more commonly applied by Shien practitioners". [Form III/Legends §Known Moves and Maneuvers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_III/Legends)

#### Shien variants
- **Reverse (horizontal) Shien grip:** the hilt is held backward, for long sweeping strikes against several enemies, "whipping the blade forward with the movement of throwing a punch". It was rare and "almost a matter of controversy" among masters. [Form V/Legends §Shien](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_V/Legends)
  - Users: Adi Gallia (both grips), Ahsoka Tano (Plo Koon's apprentice, Ruled (Jared, 24 Nov 2026)), Luminara Unduli (sometimes), Nikkos Tyris (who taught it to his followers, i.e. the Jensaarai), Revan, Quinlan Vos, Galen Marek, Starkiller, the Dark Apprentice. [Form V/Legends §Shien](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_V/Legends)
  - Kenobi used basic Shien with the reverse grip to deflect bolts on Naboo, before he learned Soresu. [Form V/Legends §Practitioners](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_V/Legends)
  - The Imperial Saber Guard "strongly favored" Shien-style reverse grips. [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Developments during the Great Jedi Purge](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends)
- **Sith Shien:** a Sith variant is named, but how it differed is not specified. [Form V/Legends §Shien](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_V/Legends) [unclear]
- **Adi Gallia's hybrid:** an aggressive blend of Shien and Djem So using both grips. [Form V/Legends §Shien](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_V/Legends)

#### Djem So (dueling variant)
- **Creation:** Made later by Form III masters who found Soresu too passive. It is aimed at saber duels. [Form V/Legends §Djem So](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_V/Legends) [Form V/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_V/Legends)
- **Strengths:** Block or parry, then counter at once, turning the opponent's own blow against them and pressing to dominate. It relies on raw power. A skilled user can beat Makashi. [Form V/Legends §Djem So](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_V/Legends)
- **Weakness:** Lack of mobility. [Form V/Legends §Djem So](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_V/Legends)
- **Stance:** Saber held above the head, angled back and down at about 45°. [Form V/Legends §Djem So](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_V/Legends)
- **Moves:** "Falling Avalanche" (overhand power blow) and "Fluid Riposte" (parry flowing straight into a counter). [Form V/Legends §Djem So](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_V/Legends)
- **Variant:** Bane used a hyper-aggressive Djem So. Zannah knew only a "purely offensive style of Djem So". [Form III/Legends §Practitioners](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_III/Legends) [Duel on Tython](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Duel_on_Tython)
- **Vader's post-Mustafar variant:** "even more focused on sheer power", with elements of Forms VII, IV, III and II. [Form V/Legends §Applications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_V/Legends)

**Light/dark lean:** Its stress on domination drew Jedi criticism. Luke later said its aggression had to be balanced with compassion. [Form V/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_V/Legends) [Form V/Legends §Applications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_V/Legends) [inferred: a mild dark-side risk through domination, not stated as a dark form]

### Form VI — Niman
- **Type:** lightsaber form
- **Era:** Kashi Mer origin (Kashi destroyed 25,000 BBY) through the Legacy era
- **Key facts:**

**Other names:** Way of the Rancor; the Moderation Form; the "diplomat's form". [Form VI/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VI/Legends)

**Creation:**
- From the Niman dual-blade style of the Royale Macheteros of the Kashi Mer Dynasty. [Form VI/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VI/Legends)
- The Legions of Lettow (darksiders of the First Great Schism) adopted it; after their defeat, the Jedi adopted it and refined it into Form VI. [Form VI/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VI/Legends)
- [conflict] The Lightsaber combat page [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Early developments](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends) says Niman was developed "from the Jar'Kai dual-blade combat style" and was made by conservative Jedi seeking moderation after Forms IV and V. Several noted Jedi called Form III its "pure root". [Form VI/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VI/Legends)
- Kashi itself was destroyed in 25,000 BBY. [Kashi Mer Dynasty §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kashi_Mer_Dynasty)

**Eras:**
- It became standard in training and was one of the most common forms by the start of the Clone Wars. [Form VI/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VI/Legends)
- It proved inadequate on open battlefields: every Niman user at the First Battle of Geonosis died. [Form VI/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VI/Legends)
- It was kept among the Sith during the Purge. [Form VI/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VI/Legends)
- The NJO's Medium style replaced it until it was rediscovered. [Form VI/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VI/Legends)
- Darth Krayt used it in the Legacy era. [Form VI/Legends §Practitioners](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VI/Legends)

**Strengths:**
- Balanced "jack-of-all-trades" with no serious drawbacks, and it does not leave its user exposed. [Form VI/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VI/Legends) [Form VI/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VI/Legends)
- Fine against criminals and thugs. [Form VI/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VI/Legends)
- A gateway to dual-blade work (Jar'Kai), with the shoto in the off-hand. [Form VI/Legends §Applications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VI/Legends)

**Weaknesses:**
- Relaxed bladework and no decisive edge; ill-suited to dueling or open battlefields. [Form VI/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VI/Legends)
- Criticized as "insufficiently demanding". [Form VI/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VI/Legends)
- Weak against Makashi. [Form VI/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VI/Legends)

**Mastery time:**
- Cin Drallig: at least ten years of dedicated study. [Form VI/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VI/Legends)
- [conflict] The same page calls it "easily mastered" by those who spend most of their time on study and diplomacy, yet also "a difficult process" to master. [Form VI/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VI/Legends) [Form VI/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VI/Legends) [inferred: easy to learn to a basic level, ten-plus years to true mastery]

**Stances:**
- Wide and open: one-handed, blade out from the body angled up and back, off-hand across the chest, feet evenly spaced.
- Two-handed head-height guard, dominant foot forward.
- Two-handed low guard at the waist, feet close together. [Form VI/Legends §Moves and Maneuvers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VI/Legends)

**Signature moves:** "Draw closer" (telekinetic pull into the blade) and "pushing slash" (strike, then Force push). [Form VI/Legends §Moves and Maneuvers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VI/Legends)

[Form VI/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VI/Legends) Kenobi found it "extremely difficult" but later pushed droids aside fluidly. [Form VI/Legends §Applications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VI/Legends)

**Light/dark lean:** Light by design (moderation and harmony). Ironically, it was Exar Kun's chosen form. [Form VI/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VI/Legends) [Form VI/Legends §Practitioners](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VI/Legends)

- **Eras:**
  - After the Army of Light disbanded, it stayed only partly developed for almost a thousand years because so few practiced it. [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Early developments](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends)
  - In the New Sith Wars, Juyo use led many Jedi to fall. The Council restricted it, and after Ruusan Skarch Vaunk taught it to only a few hand-picked students a year. [Form VII/Legends §Practitioners/Juyo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VII/Legends)
  - In the Great Galactic War era, the Jedi developed a refined version called **Juyo-Kos**. [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Practice and Training during the Sith Resurgence](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends) [Form VII/Legends §Practitioners/Juyo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VII/Legends)
- **Strengths:**
  - The most vicious form, built to "eviscerate a lone enemy" in one-on-one duels.
  - Unpredictable "staccato" sequences.
  - Can break even a Form III master's defense. [Form VII/Legends §Juyo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VII/Legends) [Form VII/Legends §Applications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VII/Legends)
- **Weaknesses:**
  - Exposes its user to counterattack, especially from several foes and Force powers.
  - Zez-Kai Ell and Kavar: less effective against Force-skilled foes and Dark Jedi Force attacks. [Form VII/Legends §Applications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VII/Legends)
  - It demands more energy than Form V. [Form VII/Legends §Juyo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VII/Legends)
- **Prerequisite:** Only masters skilled in several other forms could use it well. Drallig forbade it to all but a select few. [Form VII/Legends §Applications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VII/Legends)
- **Stance:** One-handed high guard, blade horizontal above the head pointing to the side, body half-twisted, off-hand swept down. [Form VII/Legends §Known moves and maneuvers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VII/Legends)
- **Moves:** "Assured Strike" (trades power for near-certain contact) and "Vornskr's Ferocity" (ferocious attack, doable without the dark side pervading it). [Form VII/Legends §Known moves and maneuvers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VII/Legends)
- **Practice rule:** The drills can be practiced, but you are not truly executing Form VII unless the excitement and passion of battle color your actions. [Form VII/Legends §Known moves and maneuvers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VII/Legends)

#### Vaapad
- **Creation:** Mace Windu, with Sora Bulq, in the late Republic. It is named after the vaapad, a creature of Sarapin (also called the juyo). [Form VII/Legends §Vaapad](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VII/Legends)
  - [conflict] Kas'im knew Form VII by the name Vaapad at the end of the New Sith Wars (*Book of Sith*). Sirak "used Vaapad" in *Path of Destruction*. [Form VII/Legends §Vaapad](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VII/Legends) [Form VII/Legends §Behind the scenes](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VII/Legends)
  - *Star Wars Insider* 92 explained "juyo" as another name for the vaapad creature, which reconciles the two usages in-universe. [Form VII/Legends §Behind the scenes](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VII/Legends)
  - The author's own statement on the Sirak passage is in the BTS section below.
- **Nature:** "A state of mind", not just a style.
  - The user must enjoy the fight and accept the opponent's fury, forming "one half of a superconducting loop" with the darkness in the foe.
  - It needs a constant, large flow of Force power. [Form VII/Legends §Vaapad](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VII/Legends)
  - Windu called it an answer to his own inner darkness. [Form VII/Legends §Applications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VII/Legends)
- **Look:** The user seems to wield many sabers at once. The constant "near-invisible weave" is its ready stance. It also works unarmed and with dual sabers. [Form VII/Legends §Vaapad](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VII/Legends)
- **Moves:** "Swift Flank" (dash or leap around the foe) and "Tempered Aggression". [Form VII/Legends §Known moves and maneuvers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VII/Legends)
- **Access rule:** Windu allowed it only with his permission and taught it only to Depa Billaba. [Form VII/Legends §Applications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VII/Legends)
  - Bulq taught Quinlan Vos some moves; Windu warned Vos off because he was already close to the dark side. [Form VII/Legends §Practitioners/Vaapad](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VII/Legends)
  - Qui-Gon forbade Obi-Wan to learn Vaapad, and Drallig refused Anakin Juyo. [Form VII/Legends §Applications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VII/Legends)
- **Lightning:** Windu used Vaapad techniques to channel Sidious's lightning through himself and send it back. [Form VII/Legends §Applications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VII/Legends)

#### Dark-side risk (Juyo and Vaapad)
- Many felt Juyo broke the Code's "there is no emotion, there is peace", because it needs controlled passion. Sidious called it a "Sith style". [Form VII/Legends §Juyo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VII/Legends)
- Knowledge was restricted over fears it led toward the dark side, and it swung between being "looked down upon" and "taboo". [Form VII/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VII/Legends)
- Old Republic Jedi would not teach it to anyone known to be reckless. [Form VII/Legends §Applications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VII/Legends)
- Vaapad "led through the penumbra of the dark side". Jedi warned it brought users perilously close to the dark side through its focus on physical combat. [Form VII/Legends §Vaapad](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VII/Legends)
- Siri Tachi: Windu could dismiss the pleasure in power Vaapad brought by acknowledging it and staying aware of it. [Form VII/Legends §Applications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VII/Legends)
- **Documented falls:**
  - Many Jedi fell through Juyo in the New Sith Wars. [Form VII/Legends §Practitioners/Juyo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VII/Legends)
  - Windu said Sora Bulq "did not master Vaapad; Vaapad mastered him". [Form VII/Legends §Practitioners/Vaapad](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VII/Legends)
  - Depa Billaba fell on Haruun Kal, with "the pull of Vaapad" a factor. [Form VII/Legends §Practitioners/Vaapad](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VII/Legends)
  - Windu found that fully surrendering to Vaapad against Billaba would make him fall too, so he conceded. [Form VII/Legends §Practitioners/Vaapad](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VII/Legends)
- Dooku called Vaapad "bridled and weak" without direct use of the dark side. [Form VII/Legends §Vaapad](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VII/Legends)

## Game notes (optional, suggestions only)
*(Design suggestions only; not lore.)*
- Each form could be a profile with tags such as `vs_single`, `vs_multi`, `vs_blaster`, `stamina_drain`, `space_needed` and `force_weave`, taken from the strengths and weaknesses above.
- The Form VII dark-side mechanic could add corruption when used in passion. Vaapad could allow bonus power from a dark-sided opponent ("superconducting loop") at a corruption cost. Access gates: Juyo needs mastery of several forms; Vaapad needs Windu-line permission.
- Makashi could reduce Force-attack effects and grant disarm resistance, with a penalty against strength-based attackers.
- Soresu could stack defense per round but have no finisher of its own; it needs a form-switch or an opponent's fatigue.

## Decisions needed
1. Lightsaber becomes the standard Jedi weapon [conflict]: 7000 BBY (Lightsaber combat/Legends, citing *Jedi Knight*) or 5000 BBY (Force-imbued sword/Legends). Neither clearly fits more sources on the pages checked.
2. Niman origin [conflict]: Kashi Mer Royale Macheteros style (Form VI/Legends, backed by Kashi Mer Dynasty) or "developed from Jar'Kai" (Lightsaber combat/Legends). The Kashi Mer version fits more sources.
3. "Vaapad" before Windu [conflict]: an in-universe old name (Path of Destruction, Book of Sith, reconciled by Insider 92) or an author error (Karpyshyn [BTS] 2006). The Insider 92 reconciliation fits all in-universe sources.
4. Juyo-Kos: a separate form or a Juyo variant? The sources call it "a refinement" of Juyo, which fits a variant.
5. "Sith Shien" (named, never described): keep it as an alias of Shien or omit it?
6. Niman mastery time: use Drallig's "10+ years" as the game's benchmark (the only mastery time given for any form)?
