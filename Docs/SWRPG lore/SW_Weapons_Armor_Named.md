# Named Weapons and Armour (Old Republic sources)

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched 23 Nov 2026 by Claude from Layer 2 of SW_Weapons_Armor_Sorted.md and the Mandalorian, Echani, Krath, Arkanian, Zabrak and Cathar items around it (about 150 pages read through the Wookieepedia API). Draft for review.

**Scope.** These are named weapons and armour that appear in Old Republic–era works. Each entry is dated by the facts on its page, not tied to one year. Most descriptions come from KOTOR, KOTOR II or SWTOR item text, or from the Tales of the Jedi comics and the Old Republic novels. Costs and weights are infobox values from game books or game items, so they're tagged (game stat).

## Summary
In the Old Republic, melee weapons were commonly built against lightsabers: Echani, Zabrak, Krath and Sith blades were often cortosis-woven. Mandalorian gear dominated the Mandalorian Wars, including rippers and disintegrators that ignore personal shields. Pulse-wave weapons were standard around 4000 BBY and were replaced by standard blasters by 3956 BBY. The armour ladder runs from cheap combat suits and light battle armour, through mesh, military suits and the Echani and Mandalorian suits, up to battle armour, heavy battle armour, Neo-Crusader assault armour and the phrik Fenelar armour.
