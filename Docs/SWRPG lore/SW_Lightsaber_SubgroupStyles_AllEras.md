# Lightsaber-Era Sub-group and Non-Jedi Fighting Styles (All Legends Eras)

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 21, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
Covers fighting traditions outside the Jedi forms that matter in saber combat: Teräs Käsi, Echani, Sith sword traditions, the pre-saber Force-imbued swords, Mandalorian anti-Jedi methods, Jedi-hunter tactics, the Matukai, Jensaarai and Zeison Sha, the Imperial Knights, and the pre-saber roots of Niman and Jar'Kai. Eras run from the Je'daii (c. 24,500 BBY) to the Legacy era. Key facts: Teräs Käsi was created specifically to fight Jedi. Legends has no named Mandalorian anti-Jedi style; it is shown through beskar gear and training, such as Boba Fett training Jaina Solo (41 ABY).

## Entries

### Teräs Käsi ("steel hand")
- **Type:** martial art
- **Era:** Origin before Xendor's exile from the Kashi Mer (pre-Republic); practiced to the Legacy era (A'Sharad Hett/Krayt)
- **Key facts:**

- **Origin:** Created on Bunduki (Pacanth Reach, Epicanthix people) by the Followers of Palawa, to fight the Jedi after their homeworld Palawa was destroyed in a war involving the Order. Its knowledge led many Followers to the dark side. [Teräs Käsi/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Teräs_Käsi/Legends)
  - Xendor was exiled from the Kashi Mer for using the dark side together with Teräs Käsi before he became a Jedi. [Kashi Mer Dynasty §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kashi_Mer_Dynasty)
- **Teaching:**
  - Students went to the Followers themselves and studied history, metaphysics and philosophy alongside the fighting.
  - Graduate masters spread it, but it stayed rare off Bunduki. [Teräs Käsi/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Teräs_Käsi/Legends)
- **Traits:**
  - Meditative mind-over-body; blinding speed and anticipation.
  - Strikes that rival slugthrowers or blasters and punch through body armor.
  - Acrobatics that can steer a fall.
  - Meditative trances that stop bleeding, slow poison and even heal. [Teräs Käsi/Legends §Characteristics](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Teräs_Käsi/Legends)
  - **Game stat (labelled):** the page bases the "rival blasters" claim on RPG damage of 2d10 and 3d10 for a master (*Threats of the Galaxy*) and 4d4 for a novice (*Hero's Guide*, equal to a pistol in that book). [Teräs Käsi/Legends §Characteristics](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Teräs_Käsi/Legends) [last resort: only RPG stat blocks quantify the claim; no narrative source gives a figure]
- **Structure:** Moves patterned on beasts (Charging Wampa, Rancor Rising, Dancing Dragonsnake, Spitting Rawl and others). Half a dozen basic stances across four ranges: kicking, punching, elbowing, grappling. Hands, feet, elbows, blades and sticks are used. [Teräs Käsi/Legends §Characteristics](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Teräs_Käsi/Legends) [Teräs Käsi/Legends §Known moves](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Teräs_Käsi/Legends)
- **With lightsabers:** Darth Maul trained extensively and folded it into his saber and unarmed fighting, crediting it for his success. Jedi students included Anoon Bondara, Joclad Danva and Bultar Swan. [Teräs Käsi/Legends §Notable Practitioners](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Teräs_Käsi/Legends) [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Unarmed combat](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends)
- **Non-Force masters:** Zan and Zu Pike beat over two dozen armed opponents unarmed. Phow Ji fought "Jedi-like" against a mercenary force. [Teräs Käsi/Legends §Notable Practitioners](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Teräs_Käsi/Legends)
- **Other listed practitioners:** A'Sharad Hett / Darth Krayt, Arden Lyn, Xizor, Nova Stihl, Eaden Vrill, Kar Vastor and others. [Teräs Käsi/Legends §Practitioners](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Teräs_Käsi/Legends)
- **Light/dark lean:** Created as an anti-Jedi weapon and associated with falls to the dark side, yet its teachers urged using it for the good of society. [Teräs Käsi/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Teräs_Käsi/Legends) [inferred: neutral art with a dark-side history]

### Echani (martial art)
- **Type:** martial art
- **Era:** Old Republic to the Galactic Empire era (Royal Guard)
- **Key facts:**

- **Origin:** The Echani people. Tradition is unarmed and unarmored, with no Force use; the body itself is the weapon. [Echani (martial art)/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Echani_(martial_art)/Legends)
- **Philosophy:** Combat is the truest communication. Combat is a means to truly know someone. Repeated sparring was "a courtship of sorts". [Echani (martial art)/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Echani_(martial_art)/Legends) [Echani/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Echani/Legends)
  - Echani Generals were reputed to predict opponents' moves. [Echani/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Echani/Legends)
  - Echani Firedancers mixed the art with exotic weapons such as ritual brands. [Echani/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Echani/Legends)
- **Structure:** Three tiers, each built on foundations, with progression at the instructor's discretion. [Echani (martial art)/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Echani_(martial_art)/Legends)
- **Spread:**
  - Taught to special forces across the galaxy in the Old Republic.
  - Later the Emperor's Royal Guard and Imperial Intelligence trained in it. [Echani (martial art)/Legends §Practitioners](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Echani_(martial_art)/Legends)
- **Users:** Handmaiden Sisters and Brianna, Yusanis, Atton Rand, Lumiya, Phow Ji, Mahd Windcaller and her bodyguards, the Sun Guard of Thyrsus. [Echani (martial art)/Legends §Users](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Echani_(martial_art)/Legends)
  - Meetra Surik "may have" learned it [unclear]. [Echani (martial art)/Legends §Practitioners](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Echani_(martial_art)/Legends) [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Unarmed combat](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends)
- **Lightsaber link:** Raskta Lsu, an "Echani Jedi Weapon Master", met Bane's charge on Tython (990 BBY). His orbalisk armor kept her from doing significant damage, and he knocked her aside with an elbow. [Duel on Tython](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Duel_on_Tython) How her Echani training appeared in her saber style is not described. [unclear]

### Sith sword traditions
- **Type:** weapon tradition
- **Era:** c. 6900–5000 BBY (Sith swords); the Dark Wars (Sith war sword); revivals to the New Sith Wars
- **Key facts:**

- **Sith swords (warblades and daggers):**
  - Used by the Sith Empire c. 6900–5000 BBY. [Sith sword §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_sword)
  - Forged by alchemy into an alloy with an affinity for dark energy; 3–6.5 kg (weights from *Dark Side Sourcebook*, an RPG source). [Sith sword §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_sword)
  - Needs great strength and Force power to be as dangerous as a saber.
  - Blocks lightsabers, deflects and redirects bolts, and mirrors lasers.
  - A dark-sider can strengthen it and make it superconducting, and it amplifies the wielder's dark power. [Sith sword §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_sword)
  - The first may predate lightsabers. Made by the Sith species and adopted by the Exiles. [Sith sword §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_sword)
  - **Users:** Naga Sadow vs Ludo Kressh (after Marka Ragnos's funeral); Sith Elite Warbots (Great Hyperspace War); Ommin → Satal and Aleema Keto; Revan's Empire (Sith Tremor Sword); Malak (rarely); the Dark Underlord (two swords, New Sith Wars). [Sith sword §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_sword) [Jar'Kai/Legends §Practitioners](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jar%27Kai/Legends)
- **Sith war sword (Dark Wars era):**
  - Double-edged and staff-like, possibly the forerunner of the saberstaff.
  - Ajunta Pall used one to behead the Sith king Hakagram Graush.
  - Later Sith used them "for the feeling of hard metal cutting through soft flesh".
  - Forged with cortosis-weave (KOTOR II). A straight-sword type relied on alchemy instead. [Sith war sword §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_war_sword)
- **Light/dark lean:** Dark; these blades are powered by and empower the dark side. [Sith sword §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_sword)

- **Related:** WeaponVariants; CombatTechniques (Sith alchemy)
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Force-imbued swords ("Jedi katanas") — pre-saber Jedi
- **Type:** weapon tradition
- **Era:** Je'daii era to c. 24,500 BBY and after; replaced by the protosaber
- **Key facts:**

- **Origin:** Conceived on Tython by the Je'daii Order. Widely used in the Force Wars (Ashla vs Bogan) and the First Great Schism (c. 24,500 BBY), by Jedi, Bogan followers and the Legions of Lettow. Replaced by the protosaber. [Force-imbued sword/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force-imbued_sword/Legends)
- **Make:**
  - Hammer high-carbon metal while channeling the Force, which reinforces the lattice.
  - Fit a crystal, then meditate until blade, crystal and smith are "as one", until the blade glows.
  - Taxing; the blade or crystal can shatter. [Force-imbued sword/Legends §Creation](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force-imbued_sword/Legends)
- **Look:** Glows (lightning striations, discoloration or fire). No power source. [Force-imbued sword/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force-imbued_sword/Legends)
- **Dual-blade note:** Awdrysta Pina wielded a pair before Niman was adopted. [Jar'Kai/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jar%27Kai/Legends)
- See WeaponVariants for parry rules (Force weapons parry sabers).

- **Related:** WeaponVariants (protosaber)
- **Uncertainty:** none

- **Era:** Mandalorian Wars era to 41 ABY
- **Key facts:**

- **No named Mandalorian anti-Jedi "style" page was found in Legends.** [unclear] What the sources give:
  - **Beskar:**
    - Armor and weapons resist lightsabers. Armor withstands saber strikes and repeated blaster fire, though impact force still carries through.
    - Beskad sabers, kal knives, crushgaunts and beskar shields are documented.
    - Beskar saber hilts resist opposing blades (*Book of Sith*). [Beskar/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Beskar/Legends) [Beskar/Legends §Usage](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Beskar/Legends)

    - Goran Beviin taught her Mandalorian fighting techniques using beskar training armor and beskad sabers. She was given beskar plates. [Beskar/Legends §War and resurgence](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Beskar/Legends)

    - At Galidraan (shortly before 44 BBY), eleven Jedi died, six of them killed by Jango Fett himself. [Mandalorian/Legends §The Mandalorian Civil War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalorian/Legends)

### Jedi hunter tactics (general, all eras)
- **Type:** tactics
- **Era:** All eras (examples from c. 3951 BBY to the Imperial era)
- **Key facts:**

- **Weapons:**
  - Slugthrowers: their slugs vaporize on a blade instead of being redirected.
  - Flamethrowers (cannot be blocked).
  - Sonic weapons (break concentration).
  - Poisoned darts.
  - High explosives and land mines (most Jedi fight up close).
  - Energy shields. [Jedi hunter/Legends §Equipment and tactics](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_hunter/Legends)
- **Lightsaber-resistant gear:** phrik electrostaffs (MagnaGuards, the 1,001st) and Cad Bane's cortosis carbine. [Jedi hunter/Legends §Equipment and tactics](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_hunter/Legends)
- **Doctrine (Revan and HK-47, KOTOR II):**
  - Target the Jedi's allies or bystanders.
  - Snipe from cover, since many powers need line of sight.
  - Don't over-plan (precognition).
  - "Hide the mind" by broadcasting strong unrelated emotion.
  - Psychological warfare. [Jedi hunter/Legends §Equipment and tactics](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_hunter/Legends)
- **Bodies:**
  - Cyborgs (Grievous, with slaved computers boosting reflexes; Vader; Durge).
  - Droids, hard to sense in the Force.
  - Bred beasts (voxyn; terentatek with armor partly resistant to sabers). [Jedi hunter/Legends §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_hunter/Legends)
- **Trophies:** Collecting lightsabers as proof of kill (Boba Fett, Sherruk, Grievous, Aurra Sing). [Jedi hunter/Legends §Equipment and tactics](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_hunter/Legends)
- **Named hunters:** Arden Lyn, Atton Rand, HK-47, Jango Fett, Maul, Durge, Grievous, Ventress, Aurra Sing, Boba Fett, Vader, Sherruk, Crimson Nova. [Jedi hunter/Legends §Notable Jedi hunters](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_hunter/Legends)

- **Related:** CombatTechniques (resistant materials)
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Matukai
- **Type:** Force tradition
- **Era:** Before the Old Sith Wars to the NJO (failed absorption 580 BBY)
- **Key facts:**

- **Origin:** Founded before the Old Sith Wars by a human woman from Karvoss II who found her Force-sensitivity through meditative martial arts. [Matukai/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Matukai/Legends)
- **History:**
  - About 50–60 nomadic members.
  - The Jedi failed to absorb them in 580 BBY.
  - Persecuted in the Purge; some later joined Luke's Praxeum. [Matukai/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Matukai/Legends)
- **Methods:**
  - Channel the Force through the body; exercise as meditation.
  - They could develop Force-sensitivity in less-gifted beings.
  - No lightsabers; they use the **wan-shen** polearm (short single-edged blade, breaks down, spun around the body as an axis). [Matukai/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Matukai/Legends) [Matukai/Legends §Abilities](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Matukai/Legends)
  - Thame Cerulian fought one hand-to-hand and never wanted to again. [Matukai/Legends §Abilities](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Matukai/Legends)
  - Highly skilled at sustained Force-assisted agility. [Force speed/Legends §Applications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_speed/Legends)

- **Related:** CombatTechniques (Force speed)
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Jensaarai
- **Type:** Force tradition
- **Era:** Clone Wars to the New Republic
- **Key facts:**

- **Origin:** Founded in the Suarbi system during the Clone Wars through the fall of Anzati Jedi Nikkos Tyris. A blend of Sith and Jedi teaching; the name is Sith for "followers of the hidden truth". [Jensaarai/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jensaarai/Legends)
- **Combat:**
  - Fought with lightsabers and knew the forms. Tyris trained his followers in reverse-grip Shien. [Jensaarai/Legends §Weapons and armor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jensaarai/Legends) [Form V/Legends §Shien](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_V/Legends)
  - Built sabers earlier in training than Jedi did, but valued their cortosis armor more. The armor came from Sith cortosis-working techniques. [Jensaarai/Legends §Weapons and armor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jensaarai/Legends) [Jensaarai/Legends §Philosophy, abilities, and practices](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jensaarai/Legends)
- **Later:** Made peace with the Jedi after the Empire; Kelbis Nu trained at the academy. [Jensaarai/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jensaarai/Legends)

- **Related:** Forms Part 1 (reverse Shien); CombatTechniques (cortosis)
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Zeison Sha
- **Type:** Force tradition
- **Era:** New Sith Wars era onward [conflict with a KOTOR II armor appearance, 3951 BBY]
- **Key facts:**

- **Origin:** Yanibar (Wild Space). Swelled by Jedi kin who fled there in the New Sith Wars. Isolationist and estranged from the Jedi. [Zeison Sha §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zeison_Sha)
  - [conflict] Zeison Sha armor appears in KOTOR II (3951 BBY), which clashes with the New Sith Wars origin. [Zeison Sha §Behind the scenes](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zeison_Sha)
- **Combat:** Telekinesis (using rocks or the Force to block projectiles, and Force shields) plus the **discblade**, a razor-edged thrown weapon. Light Initiate and Warrior armor. [Zeison Sha §Common abilities](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zeison_Sha)

- **Related:** CombatTechniques (telekinesis)
- **Uncertainty:** [conflict] Zeison Sha armor appears in KOTOR II (3951 BBY), which clashes with the New Sith Wars origin.

### Imperial Knights (Legacy era)
- **Type:** order
- **Era:** Legacy era
- **Key facts:**

- Drafted Force-sensitives trained for years on Bastion under a veteran Knight. Strict discipline makes them less prone to the dark side. [Imperial Knight](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Knight)
- They use Praetoria Vonil (offense) and Praetoria Ishu (defense), team-focused, alongside the classical forms. [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Redevelopment](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends)
- Pure cortosis gauntlets that can short out sabers (see WeaponVariants). [Cortosis/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cortosis/Legends)

- **Related:** Forms Part 2 (Praetoria styles); CombatTechniques (cortosis gauntlets)
- **Uncertainty:** none
