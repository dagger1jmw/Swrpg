Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) read via the MediaWiki API (the normal page view is blocked by Cloudflare; links below are the normal `/wiki/` URLs), as cited per entry. Draft for review.

Citations use reference-style links: `[Page §Section][key]`; every key resolves to a Wookieepedia URL in **Sources**. `§lead` = article intro. **Tags:** [unclear] source vague; [conflict] sources disagree (rule 6); [inferred] my conclusion; [gray] 2014–early-2016 non-Legends source, backed or fitting (rule 7); [last resort] figure from *Star Wars: Graphics* (5 May 2016), used only because no other Legends source gives it and accepted by the developer (rule 8). Publication dates are given for every source behind a count. **Cross-references:** recruitment ages, the Acquisition Division's daily workings and Initiate life are in `SW_Jedi_Daily_Life_Training_OldRepublic.md` and `SW_Jedi_Training_AllEras_Part1/Part2.md`; only midi-chlorian-specific facts are added here. Dark-side material is in the separate dark-side file.

## Summary
In Legends, midi-chlorians are intelligent symbionts in every living cell; a high count per cell marks Force-sensitivity and potential, but it is not a cap on ability and is not always accurate. Only a handful of named characters have a stated number: Anakin Skywalker (over 20,000, the highest known; 27,700 as a [last resort] anchor from *Star Wars: Graphics*, 2016), Nova Stihl (over 5,000) and Yoda (second highest, no number; the in-universe "four million" is explicitly wrong). No Legends count was found for Luke, Leia, Obi-Wan, Palpatine or Mace Windu. The average Jedi is anchored at 10,000 [last resort]. The main dispute is over Vader: sources say his injuries robbed him of much potential, or that his limits were psychological rather than physical (both are listed below).

### 1. What midi-chlorians are, and how counts are measured
- **Type:** Biology / Jedi practice
- **Era:** All; testing used by the Jedi from shortly after 1000 BBY to 19 BBY, by the Empire after 19 BBY, rediscovered by the New Jedi Order before 40 ABY

| Aspect | Key facts | Citation |
|---|---|---|
| Nature | Intelligent microscopic life-forms living symbiotically inside the cells of all living things, forming a collective consciousness; necessary for life; in sufficient numbers they let the host detect the Force, a link strengthened by quieting the mind. | [Midi-chlorian §Biology][mc] (TPM novelization 1999; *The New Essential Guide to Alien Species* 2006; *MedStar II* 2004; Episode I 1999) |
| Count = potential | The size of the count is a measure of Force potential (*Jedi vs. Sith*, 2007); other inheritable traits also affect Force ability (*Labyrinth of Evil*, 2005); high Force ability "often" but "not always" means a high count (Databank). "The higher the count, the greater the being's potential Force ability, though there were some exceptions." | [Midi-chlorian §Biology][mc]; [The Force §lead][tf] |
| What a count does NOT decide | A count is not an upper limit: a Jedi could reach a connection equal to a Jedi with a higher count (TPM novelization, 1999). Losing limbs does not reduce potential; any loss is mental and can be overcome by will (*Dark Lord*, 2005; *Darth Plagueis*, 2012), except powers like lightning that must pass through living flesh. Counts also do not show alignment. | [Midi-chlorian §Biology][mc] |
| Origin theories | Medical theory: midi-chlorians create the link to the Force; some Jedi: the Force created them as the link (*MedStar II*). Five Priestesses: they evolved on a luminous planet "in the heart of the galaxy" (TCW "Destiny"). | [Midi-chlorian §Biology, §Early years][mc] |
| Blood test | Measured by concentration in red blood cells (*Blueprints*, 2008); tests are imperfect and prone to fault (*NEGAS*, 2006); part of a standard medical check-up (*Death Star*, 2007). Jedi comlinks could carry a sampler; results needed a computer, e.g. a starship's, to read out a count (Episode I). Vader had a blood-testing system in his meditation chamber that measured his own count (*Blueprints*). | [Midi-chlorian §Study and analysis][mc] |
| Republic newborn testing | On all Republic worlds every newborn had a mandatory blood test; samples with a high result went to the Temple and were stored in the Acquisition Division's database (the Kyber memory crystal). Jedi asked the parents' permission but held legal authority to take custody of any Force-sensitive. (*The Jedi Path*, 2010) | [Acquisition Division §lead][ad]; [Jedi §Hierarchy][jl] |
| Limits of testing | A positive test on an older child was not decisive; counts were unreliable "particularly among older children or individuals with unusual psychologies", so the Jedi also used testing screens and a mental maze (*The Jedi Path*, 2010). | [Acquisition Division][ad]; [Midi-chlorian §Study and analysis][mc] |
| Other detection | Trained Jedi could sense strong Force-sensitives, though powerful beings (Palpatine, Zannah, Anakin) could shroud themselves, even unconsciously. Plagueis could see others' counts and tell whether they sufficed for Force-sensitivity. Jedi Force detectors tested blood for counts before the Clone Wars; the Imperial Force Detector (from Jenna Zan Arbor's plans; 10,000 units modified by Lord Cronal) read out a count and, after modification, the magnitude of potential. DRK-1 probes could scan for midi-chlorians. | [Force-sensitive §lead][fs]; [Darth Plagueis §Powers and abilities][dp]; [Force detector][fd]; [Midi-chlorian §Study and analysis][mc] |
| History of testing | Pre-Order sects studied midi-chlorians scientifically (before c. 25,783 BBY); Jedi Hestizo Trace knew of them by 3645 BBY; shortly after Ruusan (1000 BBY) the Jedi discovered or rediscovered them and blood tests became the main recruiting method until 19 BBY. The late Order kept a taboo on empirical study of the Force. | [Midi-chlorian §Early years, §Under the Empire][mc] |
| Empire | Research banned (Statute OB-CPO-1198); routine tests on Inner and Mid Rim worlds rooted out Force-sensitives, who were "rarely heard from again"; black-market drugs and blood treatments claimed to fool a test or lower a count but were largely ineffective. Dr. Kornell Divini was arrested in 0 BBY after querying an abnormally high count (Nova Stihl). | [Midi-chlorian §Under the Empire][mc]; [Kornell Divini][kd]; [Nova Stihl][ns] |
| New Jedi Order | In 11 ABY Luke had no knowledge of midi-chlorian tests and devised his own (brain-reflex test with Leia; Force-aura scanner on R2-D2; searching for unusual luck); the Order rediscovered blood testing before 40 ABY. | [Midi-chlorian §Under the Empire][mc]; [Force-sensitive §Detecting the Force][fs] |
| Drugs and transfusion | Anesthetics could thin midi-chlorians, so surgery might be done without them (*The Rise and Fall of Darth Vader*, 2007); bota was thought to boost their effect (*Street of Shadows*, 2008). Midi-chlorian-rich blood (Sifo-Dyas's) kept Grievous alive but gave him no Force-sensitivity. | [Midi-chlorian §Biology][mc]; [Grievous §Transformation][grv] |
| Manipulation | Plagueis learned to impose his will on existing midi-chlorians to halt aging and to create or end life; Vjun nobles genetically raised the planet's counts, and the untrained population went mad (29 BBY); Darth Drear's Sickness altered them; Tenebrous made long-lived "maxi-chlorians"; Thrawn's Frozen moon facility altered the midi-chlorians of cloned Force-sensitives. | [Midi-chlorian §Study and analysis, §Biology, §Early years, §Under the Empire][mc]; [Vjun §Refuge for the Sith][vjun] |

- **Related:** Sense Force Potential (a Sense power, [Sense Force Potential][sfp]).
- **Uncertainty:** [unclear] Average Human count: the article lead says "normal Human levels of 2,500", the body says an average Human had "less than 2,500" (*Death Star*) ([Midi-chlorian §lead, §Biology][mc]); best fit: "up to about 2,500".

### 6. Losing or changing potential
| Point | Key facts | Citation |
|---|---|---|
| Vader: injuries | Because of his burns, Vader "was thought to have lost much of his Force potential", yet kept a very high count (*Complete Visual Dictionary*, 2006); Sidious knew his injuries "had robbed him of much of his Force potential" (*A 3-D Reconstruction Log*, 2011). | [Anakin Skywalker §Post-Mustafar, §Anakin's immolation][ana] |
| Vader: mind | Sidious judged Vader's limits came from his psychological state, not his injuries, and that he could regain his full power if he faced his choices (*Dark Lord*, 2005); loss of limbs does not reduce potential (*Darth Plagueis*, 2012); the idea that it does is called a misconception (*Betrayal*, 2006). By 0 BBY he was stronger than before Mustafar (*Jedi Battles*, 23 Dec 2013). | [Anakin Skywalker §Post-Mustafar][ana]; [Midi-chlorian §Biology][mc] |
| Vader: share | In-universe: "eight-tenths of the strength of the Emperor" (text cites the TFU II novelization, 2010, and *The Last Jedi* novel, 26 Feb 2013; [unclear] which); had Mustafar not happened he would have been "far more powerful than the Emperor". **Behind the scenes:** Lucas (*Vanity Fair*, Feb 2005) said Anakin would have been "twice as good as the Emperor" and is now "maybe 20 percent less", because "there's not that much of him left". | [Anakin Skywalker §Post-Mustafar, §Behind the scenes][ana] |
| Cybernetics | Vader rarely used Force lightning because it risked shorting his cybernetics and life support (*The Jedi Path*); lightning-type powers are the stated exception to "no loss from lost limbs". | [Anakin Skywalker §Post-Mustafar][ana]; [Midi-chlorian §Biology][mc] |
| Severed connection | Meetra Surik instinctively severed her own connection to survive the Force wound left by the Mandalorian Wars (the Masters linked it to her actions at Malachor V); it slowly returned. | [Meetra Surik §The Mandalorian Wars, §The Dark Wars][ms] |
| Self-closing | Ben Skywalker closed himself off from the Force as a child after the anguish of the Yuuzhan Vong War, though visions still came. | [Ben Skywalker §A new threat][ben] |
| Can a count change? | Plagueis made his own count grow over eight years but could not create Force-sensitive life (*Darth Plagueis*, 2012); he also confirmed his count did not grow automatically as he became more powerful (*Maul: Lockdown*, 28 Jan 2014). Anesthetics thin midi-chlorians; black-market "count-lowering" treatments were largely ineffective. | [Darth Plagueis §Powers and abilities][dp]; [Midi-chlorian §Biology, §Under the Empire][mc] |

- **Uncertainty:** [conflict] **Why Vader's power fell.** Version A: the injuries themselves took much of his potential (*Complete Visual Dictionary*, 2006; *3-D Reconstruction Log*, 2011; Lucas BTS, 2005). Version B: his potential was intact and the limit was psychological (*Dark Lord*, 2005; *Darth Plagueis*, 2012; *Betrayal*, 2006; *Jedi Battles*, 2013). Version A's "thought to have lost" wording lets both stand: best fit is B for actual potential, with A as how it looked to others. Developer's call.
- [inferred] Plagueis's two statements fit together: the count can be raised deliberately, but does not rise by itself with power.
