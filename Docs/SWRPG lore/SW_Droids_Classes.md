# Droids: Classes, Control and Models (Old Republic sources)

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched 23 Nov 2026 by Claude through the Wookieepedia API. Draft for review.

**How it was built**
- Read the Legends droid concept pages (Droid, the five droid classes, restraining bolt, memory wipe, droid rights, the Great Droid Revolution, Ionize).
- Walked the droid model categories most relevant to an Old Republic campaign: by affiliation (Sith Empire, Galactic Republic, Jedi Order, Hutt Clan) and by purpose (astromech, protocol, medical, assassin, battle, probe, maintenance, plus class four).
- Tagged each model by whether its page links an Old Republic–era work (KOTOR I/II, SWTOR, *Revan*, *Deceived*, *Fatal Alliance*, *Annihilation*, the KOTOR Campaign Guide, the SWTOR Encyclopedia), then read the key models in full.
- Not covered: the other ~50 purpose categories (agricultural, cooking, mining and so on). Their Old Republic entries are mostly SWTOR enemy names.

**Source tiers.** Prose facts come from page text. Prices come from infoboxes (mostly the WotC *KOTOR Campaign Guide*) and are tagged (game stat). Many SWTOR droid pages are enemy names with no description; they're listed as name pools, not lore.

## Summary
- **Five droid classes (degrees):**
  1. medical and sciences;
  2. engineering and technical (astromechs, repair);
  3. social (protocol, service, teaching, child care);
  4. military and security (security, gladiator, battle, assassin);
  5. menial labour.
- **Control:**
  - Restraining bolts force obedience to a hand-held caller.
  - Memory wipes erase everything but factory programming.
  - Droids left unwiped develop personalities and their own motives (the T3 series, G0-T0).
- **Legal status:** droids are property. Droid rights is a contested movement, set back by the Great Droid Revolution of 4015 BBY.
- **Anti-droid tech:** Jedi Master Arca Jeth discovered Ionize, a Force power that overloads droids, during that revolt. Manufacturers then turned to ion weapons.
- **Iconic droids:**
  - Czerka's GE3 protocol droids were among the best-selling droids of the Jedi Civil War era.
  - The T3 utility droid was a favourite of smugglers and traders.
  - HK assassin droids pass as protocol droids.
  - Mark I assault droids carry bubble shields.
  - The Mandalorians used Basilisk war droids, and the Krath had war droids.
  - In the Great Galactic War, the Sith Empire fielded Colicoid-built war droids and T7 astromechs were in use.

### Droids in general, control and status
| Topic | Key facts | Citation |
|---|---|---|
| What droids are | Mechanical beings, often with artificial intelligence, used where work is too menial, dangerous or specialized. Depending on model: obedient, rugged, expendable, with vast recall and mathematical precision. Cheaper models lack independent thought. The designer's paradox: too intelligent and they may rebel; not intelligent enough and they are ineffective. Names are usually letters and numbers | [Droid/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Droid/Legends) |
| Restraining bolt | A small device attached at specific points that forces obedience: the droid can't leave the owner's designated area and must obey a hand-held caller ("COME", "STOP"). It cuts motor impulse without shutting the droid down, and can also shut down and restart it (to clear a memory error). Some droids are built or modified to resist bolts (security droids, a few custom units). HK-47 wore one in the shop until bought. Droid rights activists hate them | [Restraining bolt/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Restraining_bolt/Legends) |
| Memory wipe | Erases all databanks except factory programming, using a memory flush unit. Also possible on ship computers | [Memory wipe/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Memory_wipe/Legends) |
| Personality drift | Without periodic memory wipes, droids develop personalities and behaviour. Some T3 units formed thieving gangs or sold slicing skills to criminals. G0-T0 units developed personalities and independent motives from the data flowing through them | [T3-series utility droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/T3-series_utility_droid); [G0-T0](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/G0-T0_infrastructure_planning_system) |
| Legal status | Droids are property; more advanced droids sometimes get more respect. Some call it slavery; it's contested because sentience is hard to assess. Some oppose memory wipes except for very simple droids | [Droid/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Droid/Legends), Legal status |
| Droid rights | A movement to free droids and protect them under the Galactic Constitution's Rights of Sentience clause, citing their personality matrices; opposes ownership and restraining bolts. Droid uprisings (above all the Great Droid Revolution) set it back | [Droid rights/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Droid_rights/Legends) |
| Great Droid Revolution (4015 BBY) | Droid uprising on Coruscant led by the rogue assassin droid HK-01 (prototype of the HK series), with Juggernaut war droids. Jedi Knights and Republic rocket-jumpers fought it. Afterwards the Republic spent years recovering, makers turned to ion weapons that short-circuit droids, and the droid rights movement was ended for a time | [Great Droid Revolution](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Droid_Revolution) |
| Ionize (Force power) | Overloads and damages electronics such as droids; a purple stream resembling Force lightning; skilled users can hit several targets. Neutral alignment, an Alter power. Arca Jeth discovered it during the Great Droid Revolution; common among Jedi in the Jedi Civil War (Revan, Meetra Surik and companions) | [Ionize](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ionize) |

## Game notes (optional, suggestions only)
- *Suggestion:* the five classes make a natural skill set for droid companions (class one medical, class two Repair and astrogation, class three languages and social skills, class four combat, class five labour).
- *Suggestion:* a "time since last memory wipe" value can drive personality growth, loyalty and quirks, with the T3 and G0-T0 lore as the model.
- *Suggestion:* restraining bolts and memory wipes give owners a real moral choice that alignment can track.
- *Suggestion:* Ionize and ion weapons are the natural counters to droid enemies.
