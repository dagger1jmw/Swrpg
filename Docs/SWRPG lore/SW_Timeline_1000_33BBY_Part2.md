# SWRPG Galaxy Timeline, 999–33 BBY (Part 2 of 3): Plagueis, Senator Palpatine and the Mandalorian Civil War

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 24, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends), read through the MediaWiki API (current revisions, plus revisions from before August 2008 where the developer asked for them), as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
Part 2 covers 101–40 BBY: Plagueis's long apprenticeship and his murder of Tenebrous (67 BBY), Palpatine's birth (82 BBY), fall and apprenticeship as Darth Sidious (65 BBY) and election as Senator (52 BBY); the Chancellors Eixes Valorum, Vaila Pecivas, Thoris Darus, Frix and Kalpana; Jaster Mereel's Mandalore and the Mandalorian Civil War (60–44 BBY); the Jedi of Dooku's and Qui-Gon's generation; the Stark Hyperspace War and Galidraan (44 BBY); and the years to Finis Valorum's election in 40 BBY. The column rules are those of Part 1.

## THE AGE OF PLAGUEIS — 101–53 BBY
## ════════════════════════════════════════════

### Overview
Darth Plagueis, outwardly the Muun financier Hego Damask, spent decades as Tenebrous's apprentice: his first mission, in the 25th year of his apprenticeship and after 99 BBY, was the murder of Kerred Santhe the Elder at a banquet on Corulag; he inherited the Damask fortune and turned his father's annual Gathering on Sojourn into a Sith tool for shaping Republic politics [Darth Plagueis/Legends §Darth Tenebrous and Darth Plagueis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Plagueis/Legends). In 67 BBY Plagueis killed Tenebrous on Bal'demnic and declared the Rule of Two ended [Darth Plagueis/Legends §Becomes Master](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Plagueis/Legends). In 65 BBY he recruited the young Naboo noble Palpatine, who killed his own family and became Darth Sidious [Palpatine/Legends §Darth Plagueis and Darth Sidious](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Palpatine/Legends). The Chancellors of these years were Eixes Valorum (before 62 BBY), Vaila Pecivas (to 60 BBY) and Thoris Darus (from 60 BBY) [note: to 52 BBY] [Eixes Valorum](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eixes_Valorum); [Vaila Pecivas](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vaila_Pecivas); [Thoris Darus](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Thoris_Darus). On Mandalore, Jaster Mereel became Mandalore in 60 BBY and wrote the Supercommando Codex; Death Watch under Vizsla rejected it, beginning the civil war [Jaster Mereel, rev. 25 Jul 2008](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jaster_Mereel?oldid=2094657); [Death Watch/Legends, rev. 1 Jul 2008](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Watch/Legends?oldid=2064341). In the Jedi Order, Dooku was knighted and trained Qui-Gon Jinn, who in turn trained Feemor and then Xanatos, whose fall in 53 BBY began a nine-year feud [Dooku/Legends, rev. 28 Jul 2008](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dooku/Legends?oldid=2097891); [Xanatos crisis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Xanatos_crisis).

## SENATOR PALPATINE AND THE WARS OF 44 — 52–40 BBY
## ════════════════════════════════════════════

### Overview
In 52 BBY Plagueis had Senator Vidar Kim murdered and Palpatine, 30, took his seat for Naboo and the Chommell sector; within months the Gran Protectorate's assassins nearly killed Plagueis, who then withdrew for twenty years to study midi-chlorians while Sidious ran their plans and trained Maul [Palpatine/Legends §Rise of Senator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Palpatine/Legends); [Darth Plagueis/Legends §Facing death](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Plagueis/Legends). Plagueis was already talking to the Kaminoans about a clone army [Darth Plagueis/Legends §Meeting Dooku and Sifo-Dyas](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Plagueis/Legends). Chancellor Darus gave way to Frix (52–48 BBY) and Kalpana (47–40 BBY, re-elected 44 BBY), whose years saw the loss of the Katana fleet and the Stark Hyperspace War [Frix](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Frix); [Kalpana (Supreme Chancellor)/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kalpana_(Supreme_Chancellor)/Legends). Jaster Mereel died at Korda Six in 52 BBY and Jango Fett led the True Mandalorians until Galidraan in 44 BBY, where Death Watch tricked Dooku's Jedi into destroying them [Dooku/Legends, rev. 28 Jul 2008](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dooku/Legends?oldid=2097891). The Stark War killed Master Tyvokka and Senator Ranulph Tarkin and made Finis Valorum's name; he was elected Chancellor in 40 BBY [Stark Hyperspace War/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stark_Hyperspace_War/Legends).
