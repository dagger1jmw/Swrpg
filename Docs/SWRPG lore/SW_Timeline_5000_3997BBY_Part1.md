# SWRPG Galaxy Timeline, 5,000–3,997 BBY (Part 1 of 3): The Golden Age's End, the Great Hyperspace War and the Sith Exile

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 24, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
This three-part file rebuilds the "GOLDEN AGE OF THE SITH", "GREAT HYPERSPACE WAR" and "SITH EXILE & OLD REPUBLIC INTERWAR" blocks of `SW_Legends_Galaxy_Timeline.md`, and the 3,997 BBY rows of its "GREAT SITH WAR" block, as one sourced row per event. Part 1 (this file) covers the background to 5,000 BBY, the Great Hyperspace War (5,000 BBY) and the Sith exile that followed it: the counterinvasion, the Ritual of Nathema, Vitiate's exodus to Dromund Kaas (4,999–4,980 BBY) and the Lost Tribe of Sith on Kesh to 4,975 BBY. Part 2 covers 4,975–3,997 BBY: the hidden Sith Empire, the Gank Massacres, Freedon Nadd and the Beast Wars, the Third Great Schism, the Mandalorian Crusaders' early campaigns, the Great Droid Revolution, the Sunriders, the end of the Beast Wars, the Freedon Nadd Uprising, the Krath, the Conclave at Deneba, Exar Kun's fall and the anointing of Kun and Ulic Qel-Droma. Part 3 holds "Changes from the previous file", Not found, Canon only, Not used, Game notes, Decisions needed, Sources and the checklist. The key facts: Wookieepedia dates the whole Great Hyperspace War to 5,000 BBY and the Ritual of Nathema and start of the exodus to 4,999 BBY; the reconstituted Sith Empire was founded on Dromund Kaas in 4,980 BBY; Freedon Nadd's fall is dated 4,400 BBY; and the Great Sith War itself is dated 3,996 BBY, so the 3,997 BBY events (the Krath coup, Deneba, Kun's fall, the Iron Citadel duels) belong to the Krath Holy Crusade that preceded it.

## Entries
How to read the tables: the era headings, the ═ rule lines and "### Overview" follow the old file and the 3,700–3,600 BBY files so the developer's parser can read them; period subheadings ("#### <years> — <name>") break each era into blocks. Columns: Date | Event | Type | Place | Who | Source. Type is one of War (battles, campaigns, treaties), Control (a world or region changes hands), Leader (leadership change), Order (Jedi or Sith institutions), Birth, Death, Tech (technology and culture), Background (colonies, plagues, disasters, crimes, politics). Where Wookieepedia says "c." or derives a year from a source's "X years before", the row says so. "§" names the section of a long page. Many Wookieepedia articles in this span rest on the *Tales of the Jedi* comics; where an article flags itself as conflicting between the comics and the 1990s audio drama, the row says so.

### Overview
The Golden Age of the Sith was a period of stability and power in the Old Sith Empire under Dark Lord Marka Ragnos, who ruled for a century [Golden Age of the Sith](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Golden_Age_of_the_Sith). The Empire had grown in isolation from the Republic for about 1,900 years after the exiled Dark Jedi conquered the Sith species [Great Hyperspace War/Legends §The Golden Age of the Sith](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Hyperspace_War/Legends). Ragnos's death in 5,000 BBY left Naga Sadow and Ludo Kressh contesting the succession just as two Republic hyperspace scouts, Gav and Jori Daragon, blundered into Sith space; Sadow used their arrival to win the Dark Lordship and launch the Great Hyperspace War [Great Hyperspace War/Legends §Discovery of the Sith Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Hyperspace_War/Legends). The war, fought entirely in 5,000 BBY, struck Coruscant and the Koros system along the Daragon Trail; once Sadow's battle illusions failed, the Republic and Empress Teta's Koros fleet drove the Sith back, followed them to Korriban and destroyed the Empire's fleets, and Sadow fled to Yavin 4 [Great Hyperspace War/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Hyperspace_War/Legends) [Great Hyperspace War/Legends §Fall of the Sith Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Hyperspace_War/Legends). The Manderon Period (7,000–5,000 BBY) ended with the war [Manderon Period](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Manderon_Period) [5000 BBY/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/5000_BBY/Legends).

## THE SITH EXILE — 5,000–4,975 BBY
## ════════════════════════════

### Overview
After the second battle of Korriban, Supreme Chancellor Pultimo ordered a counterinvasion of Sith space centred on the Stygian Caldera, to remove any remaining threat and, in his own stated aim, to free the Sith species from dark-side corruption [Post–Great Hyperspace War counterinvasion](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Post%E2%80%93Great_Hyperspace_War_counterinvasion) [Pultimo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pultimo). The Sith remnant collapsed into civil conflict, disease, famine and ritual mass suicide [Post–Great Hyperspace War counterinvasion](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Post%E2%80%93Great_Hyperspace_War_counterinvasion). In 4,999 BBY Lord Vitiate drew some 8,000 surviving Sith Lords to Nathema, enslaved their minds and consumed them and his planet in a ritual that made him immortal, then led the survivors on a twenty-year exodus to the forgotten colony of Dromund Kaas, where he founded a new Sith Empire in 4,980 BBY [Ritual of Nathema](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ritual_of_Nathema) [Sith exodus](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_exodus) [Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_%28Post%E2%80%93Great_Hyperspace_War%29). Other Sith fled to Ambria, Thule, Tund and Vjun; Sadow hid on Yavin 4; and the crew of the *Omen*, wrecked on Kesh, founded the Lost Tribe of Sith [Great Hyperspace War/Legends §Sith](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Hyperspace_War/Legends) [Lost Tribe of Sith](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lost_Tribe_of_Sith).
