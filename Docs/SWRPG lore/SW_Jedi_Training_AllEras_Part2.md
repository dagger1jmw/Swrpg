# Jedi Training and Organization Across All Legends Eras — Part 2 (1000 BBY onward)
Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched 21 November 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) via the MediaWiki API (normal page view blocked by Cloudflare; links below are the normal `/wiki/` URLs), as cited per entry. Draft for review.

Part 2 of 2. Covers eras 6–9 (1000 BBY–c. 138 ABY) plus non-Order traditions whose dated facts fall after 1000 BBY. Part 1 (`SW_Jedi_Training_AllEras_Part1.md`) covers eras 1–5, up to and including the Ruusan Reformation (1000 BBY).

**Cross-references, not repeated here:** `SW_Jedi_Daily_Life_Training_OldRepublic.md` already holds the full *Jedi Path* system (timetable, clans, Initiate Trials, Acquisition Division, Padawan life, five Trials, Service Corps, dress). Citations use reference-style links: `[Page §Section][key]`; every key resolves to a Wookieepedia URL in **Sources**. `§lead` = article intro.

## Summary
- **Late Republic (1000–19 BBY):** the Ruusan system in steady state — infant recruitment, one Padawan per Master, Coruscant-centred academy, five Trials; *The Jedi Path* (3rd edition 115 BBY) is the period's own manual. Exceptions existed (Almas Academy took screened adults; Ki-Adi-Mundi at 4; Rahm Kota at 18; Ki-Adi-Mundi allowed to marry). Clone Wars: Knights and Masters became Generals, Padawans Commanders of regiments; faster promotions; knighting for deeds without formal Trials; heavy losses (Jabiim's Padawan Pack, Order 66, the Temple raid).
- **Dark Times (19 BBY–4 ABY):** no structure; survivors taught a few students in hiding (Jax Pavan, K'Kruhk with younglings at the Hidden Temple on Arkinnea, Shaak Ti, Kenobi's help to Ferus Olin); Kenobi and Yoda trained Luke as an adult in under a year of formal instruction.
- **New Jedi Order (4–c. 40 ABY):** Luke's Praxeum on Yavin 4 (11 ABY), first class of twelve, any age, several students per Master, students designing their own training; Yuuzhan Vong war moved the young to the Maw Shelter and Eclipse; Ossus academy c. 29–30 ABY with age-group clans; Masters' Council (by 35 ABY) → High Council and Luke as Grand Master (36 ABY). Marriage allowed.
- **Legacy (c. 130–138 ABY):** academy on Ossus destroyed in the Massacre at Ossus (130 ABY); survivors hid; Hidden Temple on Taivas sheltered the Council; Cade Skywalker's training resumed under Wolf Sazen, K'Kruhk and Shado Vao.

### Non-Order tradition — Matukai
- **Type:** Non-Order Force tradition
- **Era:** Founded before the Ruusan Reformation (undated); Jedi contact 580 BBY; survived into the New Jedi Order
- **Key facts:**
  - Founded by a woman who developed her own Force method at a time when the Order did not yet collect children at birth; her students passed the lessons on ([Matukai §History][mat], citing *Jedi vs. Sith: The Essential Guide to the Force*).
  - A nomadic order of about fifty to sixty members; training was meditative martial arts, physical training and somatic rituals; they could develop Force-sensitivity in many beings not naturally gifted; weapon: the wan-shen polearm, not lightsabers ([Matukai §lead][mat]).
  - A Jedi delegation under Master Bodo Baas tried to integrate them in 580 BBY; they declined ([Matukai §History][mat]).
  - Persecuted in the Great Jedi Purge; some survivors later joined Luke's Praxeum, finding it less stringent than the old Order ([Matukai §lead][mat]).
- **Uncertainty:** Ages, ranks, length of training: NF.

### Non-Order tradition — Antarian Rangers (non-Force-users)
- **Type:** Non-Order support organization
- **Era:** Founded c. 620 BBY; served the old and the New Jedi Order
- **Key facts:**
  - Paramilitary and security force of non-Force-users founded around 620 BBY to assist the Jedi ([Antarian Rangers/Legends §lead][ar]).
  - Over a thousand members at one point; several branches; few reached the rank of Ranger, most served as support personnel ([Antarian Rangers §Organization][ar]).
  - Jedi usually worked with one to three familiar Rangers; some had a semi-permanent Ranger aide, a relationship "similar to a Jedi Master-Padawan relationship" ([Antarian Rangers §Organization][ar]).
- **Uncertainty:** Ranger training content: NF on the sections read.

### Non-Order tradition — Jensaarai
- **Type:** Non-Order Force tradition (Jedi/Sith blend)
- **Era:** Clone Wars (founded) to the Legacy era
- **Key facts:**
  - Founded during the Clone Wars by the fallen Anzati Knight Nikkos Tyris, who trained Knights and Padawans on Susevfi (Suarbi system) in what he believed were the "true" Jedi teachings, actually Sith; the hierarchy "loosely resembled" the Jedi Order's; leader title Saarai-kaar ([Jensaarai/Legends §lead, §Origins][jen]).
  - After their founder's death they grew by recruiting local Force-sensitives; former Jedi, "even half-trained Padawans", found the transition hard ([Jensaarai §Rebirth, §Philosophy][jen]).
  - Made peace with Luke Skywalker and Corran Horn and sent students to the Yavin Praxeum (e.g. Kelbis Nu) ([Jensaarai §lead, §A certain point of view][jen]).
  - Some joined the One Sith after the Sith–Imperial War under threat; the organization was not wholly absorbed ([Jensaarai §A certain point of view][jen]).
- **Uncertainty:** Rank names other than Saarai-kaar: NF.
