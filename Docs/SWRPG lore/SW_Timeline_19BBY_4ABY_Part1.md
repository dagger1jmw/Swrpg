# SWRPG Galaxy Timeline, 19 BBY–4 ABY (Part 1 of 4): The Dark Times, 19–10 BBY

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 24, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
This four-part file rebuilds the DARK TIMES / IMPERIAL ERA and GALACTIC CIVIL WAR sections of `SW_Legends_Galaxy_Timeline.md` as one sourced row per event, from just after the Declaration of a New Order (19 BBY) to the founding of the New Republic (4 ABY). Part 1 (this file) covers 19–10 BBY: the first year of the Great Jedi Purge, the Inquisitorius, the Empire's reconquest of the Rim, and Han Solo's youth up to his escape from Garris Shrike and entry into the Imperial Academy. Part 2 covers 9–1 BBY (Han and Lando's smuggling years, *The Force Unleashed*, the Corellian Treaty, the run-up to Yavin). Part 3 covers 0 BBY–2 ABY (the Death Star plans, Yavin, the post-Yavin campaigns, *Dark Forces*). Part 4 covers 3–4 ABY (Hoth, Bespin, *Shadows of the Empire*, Endor, the Empire's fragmentation, the New Republic) and holds "Changes from the previous file", "Not found", "Canon only, not used", "Not used", "Game notes", "Decisions needed", "Sources" and the submission checks for all four parts.

The key facts for this span: the Purge begins at Order 66 (16:5:22 GrS, 19 BBY) and, per the current Legends page, ends in 0 BBY (the pre-2008 page said 1 BBY [conflict]); the Inquisitorius was founded in 19 BBY; the Ghorman Massacre is dated "approximately 19 BBY" on its own page but listed under 18 BBY on the year page [conflict]; Maul dies on Tatooine in 16 BBY (developer ruling, *Old Wounds*), where Owen Lars, not Kenobi, fires the killing shot; and Han Solo's order of life is Shrike's ship → Ylesia and Bria Tharen → Coruscant and the Academy entrance exams (10 BBY) → Carida → discharge for saving Chewbacca (5 BBY).

## Entries
How to read the tables: the era headings, the ═ rule lines and "### Overview" are kept from the old file so the developer's parser can read them; each year has a "#### <year>" subheading. Columns: Date | Event | Type | Place | Who | Source. Type is one of War, Control, Leader, Order, Birth, Death, Tech, Background. Galactic Standard (Great ReSynchronization, "GrS") dates are given in brackets where Wookieepedia gives them (for example 16:5:23 = year 16 after the ReSynchronization, month 5, day 23). Clone Wars carry-overs were checked against the article as it stood before *The Clone Wars* (2008), per the developer's instruction; those revisions are cited with `?oldid=`. Rows from *The Clone Wars* (2008) and works based on it would be tagged [S-canon]; none of the rows below rests on such a source. Video-game campaigns listed on the year pages (*Empire at War*, *X-Wing*, *TIE Fighter*, *Rogue Squadron*, *Galaxies*, *Battlefront*) are kept as background rows and named as games; whether to keep them is Decision 17 in Part 4. *The Force Unleashed* is cited to its novelization where the year page does (developer ruling).

### Overview
The Great Jedi Purge began with Order 66 in 19 BBY and lasted, on the current Legends page, until 0 BBY, when so few Jedi remained that the Emperor no longer considered them a threat [Great Jedi Purge/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Jedi_Purge/Legends). Palpatine and Vader hunted survivors through Vader himself, the Inquisitorius, the Emperor's Hands, Dark Jedi and bounty hunters [Great Jedi Purge/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Jedi_Purge/Legends). In the pre-2008 version of the article, the Empire estimated that fewer than one hundred Jedi escaped Order 66, and the Purge "itself had concluded" by 1 BBY [Great Jedi Purge, rev. 2102617, 31 Jul 2008](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Jedi_Purge?oldid=2102617). Alongside the Purge, the Empire fought a long "Reconquest of the Rim" against Separatist holdouts (the Western Reaches Operation and other campaigns in *The Essential Guide to Warfare*) [Western Reaches Operation](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Reaches_Operation), built up COMPNOR, the Imperial Senate and the Moff system, and began the Death Star [COMPNOR/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Commission_for_the_Preservation_of_the_New_Order/Legends); [DS-1 Orbital Battle Station/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DS-1_Orbital_Battle_Station/Legends).
