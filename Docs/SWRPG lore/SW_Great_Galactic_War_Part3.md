# Star Wars Legends: The Great Galactic War (3681–3653 BBY), Part 3: 3659–3653 BBY, peace and the Sacking

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 21, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
- **Rhen Var:** the costly Republic victory at Rhen Var led the Emperor and Dark Council to seek a decisive blow. [Battle of Rhen Var §Aftermath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Rhen_Var)
- **The Sacking:** while peace talks ran on Alderaan in 3653 BBY, the Empire sacked Coruscant, destroyed the Jedi Temple and killed Chancellor Berooken. The Republic then signed the Treaty of Coruscant. [Sacking of Coruscant](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sacking_of_Coruscant)
- **Aftermath:** riots, holdouts on Dantooine and Balmorra, and the Jedi move to Tython followed. [Coruscant riots (3653 BBY)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_riots_%283653_BBY%29); [Battle of Dantooine (Great Galactic War)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Dantooine_%28Great_Galactic_War%29); [Great Galactic War §Aftermath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Galactic_War)

**Scope and splits:**
- Part 1 covers 3681–3667 BBY plus the war overview, front lines, the Jedi at war and the Sith at war.
- Part 2 covers 3666–3660 BBY, undated battles, Mandalorians, other factions and daily life.
- Part 3 covers 3659–3653 BBY, the peace process, the Sacking, immediate effects and "who was where".

### Peace process and the Sacking of Coruscant (3654–3653 BBY)
- **Type:** event
- **Era:** 3654–3653 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Why the Empire offered peace:**
    - The Dark Council offered peace after the Republic's victories (Alderaan; Rhen Var) and the loss of the Hydian Way. [Treaty of Coruscant §Meeting on Alderaan and the attack on Coruscant](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Treaty_of_Coruscant); [Battle of Alderaan §Aftermath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Alderaan)
    - The offer came about seven years after the Hydian Way defeat. [Great Galactic War §Sacking of Coruscant](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Galactic_War)
  - **Reception on Coruscant:** senators and Jedi agreed that the war could not be won. Dar'Nala noted the Republic's "crippled economy and a gradually shrinking territory". [Great Galactic War §Sacking of Coruscant](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Galactic_War); [Dar'Nala §Peace negotiations on Alderaan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dar'Nala)
  - **The attack began:** Imperial ships entered Coruscant space and were hailed by Orbital Security Station Six. Shae Vizla disabled the defences. [Sacking of Coruscant §Assault on the Jedi Temple](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sacking_of_Coruscant); [Mandalore the Avenger §The Sacking of Coruscant](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalore_the_Avenger)
  - **The Jedi Temple:**
    - Malgus, Daru and Vizla entered the Temple. Padawans watched from the upper mezzanines as six Knights led by a Master confronted Malgus.
    - Adraas, one of two Sith Lords leading the strike team that Angral handpicked, was knocked out by Ven Zallow. Malgus then killed Zallow.
    - Explosives planted in the Temple brought it down.

    [Sacking of Coruscant §Assault on the Jedi Temple](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sacking_of_Coruscant); [Adraas §Sacking of Coruscant](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Adraas); [Orgus Din §Great Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Orgus_Din)
  - **The Senate:**
    - Berooken tried to reach Am-Ris and was beheaded by Angral, who set up headquarters in the Senate Building. [Sacking of Coruscant §Coruscant falls](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sacking_of_Coruscant); [Darth Angral §The Sacking of Coruscant](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Angral)
    - Angral disbanded the Senate and ordered many surviving senators executed. [Sacking of Coruscant §Martial law](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sacking_of_Coruscant)
  - **The occupation (two days):**
    - Angral was provisional governor under martial law, Malgus ran orbital security, and Roon Neele was Angral's attaché.
    - No ships could leave or enter.
    - The Sith looted the world, executed senators and kidnapped locals. Search and rescue was allowed.

    [Sacking of Coruscant](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sacking_of_Coruscant); [Sacking of Coruscant §Martial law](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sacking_of_Coruscant)
  - **Malgus:**
    - His political missteps lowered his standing. [Sacking of Coruscant §Martial law](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sacking_of_Coruscant)
    - He killed Eleena Daru. [Adraas §Sacking of Coruscant](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Adraas)
    - He later killed Adraas. [Adraas §Powers and abilities](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Adraas)
  - **Alderaan:** contact with Coruscant was lost, and the Republic delegation, faced with the hostage capital, signed the Sith-written treaty. [Zym §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zym); [Berooken §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Berooken)
  - **Notable defenders:**
    - Kellian Jarro killed over 100 Mandalorian commandos. [Kellian Jarro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kellian_Jarro)
    - Kilran's tactics earned him the name "Butcher of Coruscant". [Rycus Kilran](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rycus_Kilran)
    - Orgus Din's Padawan Bengel Morr was lost in the Temple (he survived and later fell to the dark side). [Orgus Din](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Orgus_Din)
  - **Grand Master Zym:** reported killed during the Sacking (one source) or killed afterwards (another) [conflict]. Council deaths [conflict]: "six High Council members died in the Sacking" vs "half of the Council was killed, including Grand Master Zym" (Jedi High Council/Legends). Resolved (lore batch 13, ruling 5; D-363 [2]): six died in the Sacking; Zym died afterwards in the ruins. [Bela Kiwiiks](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bela_Kiwiiks); [Zym §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zym)
- **Related:** treaty terms and Republic offices in SW_SWTOR_Era_Power_Structures_Part2.md; Grand Masters in SW_Jedi_Ranks_Councils_Roles_AllEras_Part1.md
- **Uncertainty:** [conflict] the offer year; Zym's death. See Decisions needed. Both resolved: offer 3654 BBY (D-363 [1]); Zym killed after the Sacking (D-363 [2]).

### Immediate effects (3653 BBY onward)
- **Type:** aftermath
- **Era:** 3653 BBY and after
- **Key facts:**
  - **Jedi Order:** "thousands of Jedi" were dead and the Republic blamed the Order, which withdrew to Tython. [Great Galactic War §Aftermath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Galactic_War)
  - **Jedi Temple:** rebuilding was put off indefinitely for lack of funds; Interim Chancellor Am-Ris told Zym of the vote. [Jedi Temple/Legends §The Old Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Temple/Legends); [Sacking of Coruscant §Galactic change](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sacking_of_Coruscant)
  - **Coruscant's recovery:** food riots, uprisings and looting in the lower levels. [Sacking of Coruscant §Coruscant's long road back](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sacking_of_Coruscant); [Coruscant riots (3653 BBY)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_riots_%283653_BBY%29)
  - **Holdouts and plots:**
    - Troops on Dantooine and Balmorra refused to withdraw. [Great Galactic War §Aftermath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Galactic_War); [Battle of Dantooine (Great Galactic War)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Dantooine_%28Great_Galactic_War%29)
    - On Balmorra, Zym sent Orgus Din and Tavus to persuade General Buryn's men to leave. [Battle of Balmorra (Galactic Wars) §The Great Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Balmorra_%28Galactic_Wars%29)
    - Dar'Nala's anti-treaty plot included the Senate Tower bombing. [Dar'Nala §Subterfuge in the Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dar'Nala)
  - **Alderaan:** Gaul Panteer withdrew the planet from the Republic in protest. [Silara Panteer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Silara_Panteer)
  - **Promotions:** Kilran rose to Grand Moff. [Battle of Ord Ibanna §Aftermath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Ord_Ibanna); [Rycus Kilran](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rycus_Kilran)
  - **Mandalorians:** Vizla retired on Rishi. [Mandalore the Avenger §Retirement on Rishi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalore_the_Avenger)
  - **Territory:** about half the galaxy was under Sith rule [conflict with "almost two thirds"; see Decisions needed]. [Great Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Galactic_War)
  - **Dating conventions:** see SW_SWTOR_Era_Power_Structures_Part2.md and [Treaty of Coruscant §Other conflicts](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Treaty_of_Coruscant).
- **Related:** SW_SWTOR_Era_Power_Structures_Part2.md (treaty terms, the Cold War)
- **Uncertainty:** none beyond those noted

## Game notes (optional, suggestions only)
- **The Sacking (3653 BBY):** Padawans watched from the mezzanines, and most inside the Temple died. Survival play could be set in the Underlevels or the Liston and Yinta spaceports, following Din, Tavus, Korr and Leneer, during the two-day blockade with no ships leaving.
- **After the war:** Temple rubble, food riots and gangs holding the Old Galactic Market in 3653 BBY make a grim post-war Coruscant setting.

## Decisions needed
1. **Year of the peace offer:** 3654 BBY (Berooken page) vs 3653 BBY (Treaty, conference and Malgus pages). Best fit: offer in 3654 BBY, conference and Sacking in 3653 BBY. Resolved (D-363 [1]; lore batch 14, ruling 3): offer in 3654 BBY, conference and Sacking in 3653 BBY.
2. **Grand Master Zym's death:** killed in the Sacking (Kiwiiks page, citing *Threat of Peace* Act 1) vs killed afterwards by Braden on the Envoy/Temple ruins (Zym page). Best fit: afterwards, as the more detailed account.
3. **Battle of Bomodon:** 3674 BBY (Bomodon and Trant pages) vs 3673 BBY (Garza page). Best fit: 3674 BBY. The 45th Infantry Division page also supports 3673 BBY; per D-380 [9] the campaign ran ten months across 3674–3673 BBY (lore batch 17).
4. **End of the Hydian Way blockade:** 3660 BBY (blockade infobox, riots, Barrows) vs 3661 BBY (Battle on the Hydian Way intro). Best fit: 3660 BBY.
5. **Mandalore the Lesser's death:** "during or shortly after" the blockade (GGW) vs after the c. 3660 BBY Great Hunt (Great Hunt page) vs "sometime after the Treaty" (Mandalore the Vindicated intro). Best fit: c. 3660 BBY, after the Great Hunt.
6. **Mandalorian entry:** the Lesser took the title in 3667 BBY vs "3661 BBY" for the Mandalorians' return. Best fit: both; the title in 3667 BBY, open war in 3661 BBY. [conflict] SW_Mandalorians_GGW_Part1 gives a 3665 BBY alternative for the title; resolved (lore batch 15), ruling 1: 3667 BBY (the Empire courted the Mandalorians that year and he claimed the title; the clans rallied on Geonosis over 3667–3665 and the army left a year later).
7. **Outer Rim in 3671 BBY:** "nearly half" after nearly a decade (GGW) vs conquest "finalized" (Third Seswenna). Best fit: "nearly half", treating "finalized" as the end of that campaign phase.
8. **Galaxy share at the treaty:** "roughly half" (GGW) vs "almost two thirds" (Cold War page; see the power-structures file). Best fit: "roughly half", the main war page.
9. **Malcom's rank:** Captain at Alderaan (battle infobox, Malcom page) vs "commander" (Havoc and Malgus pages) vs "General" in 3666 BBY (Gell Mattar, *Annihilation*). Best fit: Captain in 3667 BBY, with "commander" as his role; treat "General" as a later rank [unclear].
10. **Theron Shan's birth:** 3670 BBY (author's tweet, [BTS]) vs conceived after Alderaan in 3667 BBY (*Annihilation*). Best fit: after 3667 BBY, as the novel's in-text account.
11. **Rhen Var date:** "between 3660–3653 BBY" and c. 3653 BBY (Rhen Var and Bakarn pages) vs "not long after" Alderaan, during Satele's pregnancy (Theron page). Best fit: c. 3653 BBY.
12. **Havoc Squad's origin:** a Great War unit at Alderaan (Havoc intro, Alderaan battle) vs formed after the Jedi left for Tython (Havoc §History). Best fit: a Great War unit, with the history line as page error [unclear].
13. **Ord Mantell:** "never attacked outright" vs any wartime role. Best fit: no Great War battle there.
14. **Balmorra start:** "early in the war" (GGW) vs "by 3670 BBY" (infobox). Best fit: early, by 3670 BBY at the latest.
15. **Admiral Greik's victory:** 3671 BBY (battle page) vs "around 3670 BBY" (Greik page). Best fit: 3671 BBY.
16. **First Republic counter-offensive:** Korriban in 3678 BBY (GGW) vs earlier Seswenna and Minos operations (3680 BBY). Best fit: the first planned offensive into Sith space.
