## Summary
Korriban is the original Sith homeworld and a graveyard world. It holds the Valley of the Dark Lords, where native Sith kings and later the Dark Lords of the Sith Empire were buried. It sits alone in the Horuset system and was called Pesegam under Xim and Moraband in the Clone Wars. The dark side is strong there. Ghosts and preserved Dark Lords linger in the tombs, and dark side creatures guard them. The Sith have repeatedly abandoned and reclaimed it. From 3681 BBY it's the Sith Empire's academy world again. The Republic briefly holds it in 3637, and Zakuul bombards it during the Eternal Empire conquest.

## Entries

### Korriban: world and names
- **Type:** location (planet)
- **Era:** all eras
- **Key facts:**
  - Korriban is the only planet in the Horuset system, near Bosthirda, on the Nache Bhelfia and Kamat Krote hyperspace lanes. It was called Pesegam under Xim and Moraband in the Clone Wars [Korriban/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Korriban/Legends).
  - It was the original Sith homeworld and a sacred site holding the tombs of ancient Dark Lords [Korriban/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Korriban/Legends).
  - **The homeworld question:** one source calls it "the ancient Sith homeworld". The article explains that the Sith species began on Korriban and moved its capital to Ziost before the Exiles arrived [Korriban/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Korriban/Legends).
- **Related:** SW_Species_OldRepublic (Sith Pureblood).
- **Uncertainty:** none.

### Burial valleys and settlements
- **Type:** locations
- **Era:** ancient onward
- **Key facts:**
  - **Valley of the Dark Lords:** the natives built it as a burial ground for their kings, and the Exiles later added tombs. It held the ancient Sith Great Temple and Lord Khreusis's Stronghold [Korriban/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Korriban/Legends).
  - **Valley of Golg:** an equatorial burial valley built by the ancient Sith Empire [Korriban/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Korriban/Legends).
  - **Dreshdae:** a settlement founded by Exar Kun's disciples after his death. In the Cold War era, the academy's Korriban Arrival Port sat near the academy and the Valley [Korriban/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Korriban/Legends).
- **Related:** SW_Korriban_Academy_Part2 §F (era layout).
- **Uncertainty:** whether Dreshdae is still inhabited after the Jedi Civil War [unclear]. The Cold War sources name the Arrival Port, not Dreshdae.

### Force nature
- **Type:** Force phenomenon
- **Era:** all eras
- **Key facts:**
  - The dark side is strong enough on Korriban that the academy's trials and the tomb guardians are tied to it [Korriban/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Korriban/Legends).
  - Malignant spirits of the dead remained after the Sith Civil War and confronted tomb robbers [Korriban/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Korriban/Legends).
  - Sith alchemy preserved the ancient Dark Lords, who act as spirits and advisors. Darth Sidious visited them in 11 ABY, and the Disciples of Ragnos tried to raise Marka Ragnos in 14 ABY [Korriban/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Korriban/Legends).
- **Ruling (Jared, earlier this session):** a planet-level dark nexus (SW_Force_Nexus_Vergence).
- **Uncertainty:** none.

### Wildlife
- **Type:** creatures
- **Era:** as noted
- **Key facts** (all from [Korriban/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Korriban/Legends)):
  - **Terentateks:** fierce creatures that defeated Duron Qel-Droma and Shaela Nuur's attempt to wipe them out in 3993 BBY.
  - **Shyracks:** bat-like creatures living in large numbers in caves.
  - **Tuk'ata ("Sith hounds"):** common in the Valley of the Dark Lords.
  - **Hssiss:** dark side dragons that guard the tombs as familiars.
  - **Wraids.**
  - **Pelko bugs:** their venom was used in the Brotherhood of Darkness's training sabers near the end of the New Sith Wars.
  - **K'lor'slugs:** present by the Cold War.
- **Related:** SW_Korriban_Academy_Part2 §G (era dangers).
- **Uncertainty:** which creatures are present at a given date [unclear]. Most entries above are dated only by the event that mentions them. SW_Korriban_Academy_Part2 §G lists the dangers recorded for c. 3681–3640.
