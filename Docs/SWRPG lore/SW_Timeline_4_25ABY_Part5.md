# SWRPG Galaxy Timeline, 4–25 ABY (Part 5 of 5): The Long Peace, the Second Imperium and the Diversity Alliance (19–24 ABY), with back sections

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 24, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends), as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
Part 5 covers the years after the Pellaeon–Gavrisom Treaty to the eve of the Yuuzhan Vong invasion: the retirement of the Rogue Squadron veterans, the Second Imperium and the Shadow Academy, the Solo children at the Praxeum (*Junior Jedi Knights*, *Young Jedi Knights*), *Survivor's Quest*, the Diversity Alliance, and the Yuuzhan Vong's preparations (stopping before the first attacks of 25 ABY). It then holds the back sections for the whole five-part set: Changes from the previous file, Not found, Canon only, Not used, Game notes, Decisions needed, Sources and the checklist.

#### Supporting article: the BBY/ABY calendar
- The "ABY-BBY" dating system, a 12-month, 368-day calendar, was created by the New Republic Historical Council after the Galactic Civil War, with the Battle of Yavin as its zero point (chosen over Endor as more significant); around 25 ABY it became the official galactic calendar, replacing the Great ReSynchronization calendar of the Imperial era, and it stayed in use for more than a century [ABY-BBY dating system/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/%27ABY-BBY%27_dating_system/Legends).
- Wookieepedia notes that in 24 ABY the in-universe calendar had not yet changed from the older system, though an e-book says "24 A.B.Y." [24 ABY/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/24_ABY/Legends).

## Changes from the previous file

Checked against `SW_Legends_Galaxy_Timeline.md` ("NEW REPUBLIC ERA — 4–25 ABY") and `SW_Characters_14_NewRepublic_NewJediOrder.md`.

**What's new:** 240 dated rows replace the old 17. New are the Ssi-ruuk aftermath, Pestage and Isard, Mindor, the whole *X-wing* sequence (Borleias, Kessel, Coruscant, the Tycho trial, the Bacta War, Wraith Squadron, Ession, *Razor's Kiss*, Selaggis, Dathomir, the Hegemony theater, Adumar), the post-Zsinj campaigns, the Thrawn campaign's dated battles, the Crimson Empire, Daala's campaigns, the Darksaber, Nam Chorios, the Crystal Star, the Disciples of Ragnos, the Black Fleet Crisis, the Almanian Uprising, the Imperial Skirmishes, the Corellian Crisis, the Caamas crisis, Survivor's Quest, the Second Imperium, the Diversity Alliance and the Solo children.

**Errors and imprecisions in the old timeline:**
1. "First Imperial Civil War … 4–5 ABY": Wookieepedia's page is "Imperial Civil War" (aka Imperial Mutiny, War of Purification, Time of Destruction), dated 4–12 ABY and ended by Daala's execution of the warlords [Imperial Civil War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Civil_War). The "4–5 ABY" row is too narrow, and the old file's own 12 ABY row ("ends the First Imperial Civil War") contradicts it.
2. "~6 ABY Luke Skywalker recovers Jedi lore": no source found for a 6 ABY event. Dated equivalents: after Mindor (5.5 ABY) Luke resigned from the military to focus on his Jedi studies [Battle of Mindor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Mindor); he received the *Chu'unthor* records on Dathomir in 8 ABY [8 ABY/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/8_ABY/Legends); Leia took the Tedryn Holocron in 10 ABY [10 ABY/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/10_ABY/Legends).
3. "6–7 ABY Liberation of Coruscant … she retreats to Thyferra": Isard did not leave when Coruscant fell; she stayed and released the Krytos virus, then left in the *Lusankya* during the Mission above Imperial City (about 7–8 weeks later) and only then took Thyferra [Mission above Imperial City](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mission_above_Imperial_City) [Mission to Kessel (Galactic Civil War)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mission_to_Kessel_(Galactic_Civil_War)). [Ruled (Jared, 24 Nov 2026): 6 ABY.]
4. "7–8 ABY Bacta War … Rogue Squadron defeats her": the war began and ended in 7 ABY (7.5 ABY by *The New Essential Chronology*) [7 ABY/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/7_ABY/Legends) [Bacta War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bacta_War); Isard escaped and was presumed dead, dying only in 9 ABY at Bilbringi [Battle of Thyferra](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Thyferra) [Ysanne Isard](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ysanne_Isard).
5. "~10 ABY … reunites Imperial forces; reconquers much of the galaxy; captures Coruscant": Coruscant was taken by the Imperial Coalition under Palpatine's secret leadership, after which the Coalition fell into the Imperial Mutiny [Second Battle of Coruscant](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Battle_of_Coruscant); Shadow Hand ended with Imperial control of about a third of the galaxy [Operation Shadow Hand](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Operation_Shadow_Hand). "Reunites Imperial forces" is not how Wookieepedia describes it.
6. Daala row ("thirteen most powerful"): thirteen warlords is right [Imperial Civil War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Civil_War); "most powerful" is not stated in what was read.
7. The Pellaeon–Gavrisom row's terms (cloaking devices banned, alignment choice, Mon Mothma attending) are confirmed [Pellaeon–Gavrisom Treaty](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pellaeon%E2%80%93Gavrisom_Treaty).
8. The old overview's "Thrawn nearly reverses the war in 9 ABY before being assassinated", "Palpatine … permanently destroyed ~11 ABY" and "Daala executes the squabbling warlords ~12 ABY" are confirmed (9, 11 and 12 ABY) [9 ABY/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/9_ABY/Legends) [11 ABY/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/11_ABY/Legends) [12 ABY/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/12_ABY/Legends).
