# Sith Academies and Training (Sith Empire c. 3681–3636 BBY, with Jedi Civil War and Brotherhood of Darkness comparison)

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched 21 November 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends), as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
This file covers how the reconstituted Sith Empire trained its Sith during the Great Galactic War (3681–3653 BBY), the Cold War (3653–3641 BBY) and the Galactic War (3641 BBY onward). It centres on the Academy on Korriban, rebuilt after the Empire retook the planet in 3681 BBY, and also covers the academies on Dromund Kaas and Odacer-Faustin. It covers recruitment, the Overseers who ran acolyte classes, the trials in the Valley of the Dark Lords, the rules on killing rivals, the step from training weapon to lightsaber, apprenticeship to a Sith Lord, and the ranks above it (Sith Lord, Darth, the Dark Council). For comparison, two sections describe the Korriban academy under Darth Revan and Darth Malak in the Jedi Civil War (c. 3959–3956 BBY) and under Lord Kaan's Brotherhood of Darkness (from 1006 BBY, Darth Bane's time c. 1003–1000 BBY). The comparison sections hold the only detailed class-size and lesson-format material, because the SWTOR-era sources describe trials rather than a timetable.

### Sith training under the Empire: aims and methods
- **Type:** practice
- **Era:** era, with general statements marked
- **Key facts:**
  - General: Sith training aimed to weed out the weak and was often lethal (ref: *Knights of the Old Republic*). Overseers used manipulation, deceit and cruelty to shape future Sith (ref: *The Old Republic Encyclopedia*, p. 164). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_training) ("Methods and Specifications")
  - Era: some children were groomed from the moment they showed Force sensitivity. Others hid their abilities but were still forced into the trials. Children whose parents hid their Force sensitivity were taken from home, and the parents were punished (ref: *The Old Republic Encyclopedia*, p. 164). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_training) ("Methods and Specifications")
  - Era: at first only Sith Purebloods and Imperial Humans could become Sith. Later the academies pressed any Force-adept into the trials, which frustrated traditionalists (ref: *The Old Republic Encyclopedia*, p. 164). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_training) ("Methods and Specifications")
  - Era: after the Empire retook Korriban, any Force-sensitive found was sent to the academy as an acolyte, "willing or not". Groups of acolytes were taught by one Overseer and competed to be noticed by a visiting Sith Lord, hoping to become that Lord's apprentice. Trials included judging prisoners and tests of endurance, and students often went into the wilderness or the ancient tombs for tests (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_training) ("Sith Training under the Empires")
  - Era: an Imperial law required all Force-sensitives in Imperial territory to be trained at the Academy (ref: SWTOR Sith Warrior mission "Arm Yourself"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vemrin) ("Biography") The law is attested from the Cold War (Vemrin, Kory); in the war only the decree on Force-sensitive slaves is dated (lore batch 12).
  - General: academy training was competitive by design, keeping students isolated rather than cooperative, with one instructor for several students. Students were also expected to study and practise on their own (ref: *Knights of the Old Republic*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_training) ("Training Regimes")
  - General: Force training in the Sith empires was taught in class. One master taught several students to draw on anger and focus on offensive uses of the Force, starting with simple skills such as telekinesis and moving up to telepathy or Force lightning (ref: *Knights of the Old Republic*; *Darth Bane: Path of Destruction*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_training) ("Force application training")
  - General: the academies taught interrogation and torture, often on live subjects such as prisoners of war or local criminals (ref: *Knights of the Old Republic*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_training) ("Interrogation and Assassination")
- **Related:** Recruitment; Overseers; Acolyte trials
- **Uncertainty:** See "Recruitment" for the [conflict] over when slaves and aliens were first admitted.

### Recruitment and who could enter
- **Type:** practice
- **Era:** era (Great Galactic War and Cold War)
- **Key facts:**
  - During the Great Galactic War, the Emperor decreed that any slave with enough Force sensitivity be sent to Korriban to train as an acolyte. Many slaves were freed and sent, but the training ended in success or death (no footnote). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_%28Post%E2%80%93Great_Hyperspace_War%29) ("Society and culture")
  - During the Cold War, the Empire was losing more Sith than it gained, so slaves and even aliens were allowed to train as acolytes. Many Imperials frowned on this (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Academy_%28Korriban%29) ("True Sith Empire")
  - The Empire grew desperate to replace battlefield losses and let Force-sensitive slaves, captured Jedi and even aliens become acolytes (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Korriban/Legends) ("Cold War")
  - Darth Nox's page says this was a time when the Sith Order had decided to accept non-Imperials for training as Sith Inquisitors (no footnote at that sentence). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Nox) ("Biography")
  - Examples of intake:
    - Teneb Kel (later Darth Thanaton), born 3695 BBY, was a slave freed by Lord Calypho and taken as his apprentice (ref: *The Old Republic Encyclopedia*; *Blood of the Empire*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Thanaton) ("Early life and training")
    - Vemrin came from Balmorra, not the old Sith Worlds, and his family had no Sith lineage. Overseer Tremel called him "impure" (ref: SWTOR "Arm Yourself"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tremel) ("Biography")
    - The Kaleesh Xalek was captured fighting the Empire, enslaved, then sent to Korriban once found to be Force-sensitive (ref: *The Old Republic Encyclopedia*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Xivhkalrainik) ("Biography")
    - Kory was a low-born Imperial treated as a slave until she was found to be Force-sensitive (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kory) ("Biography")
  - Sith Warriors arrived on Korriban "at a young age" as acolytes (ref: SWTOR Sith Warrior class video). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Warrior) ("History")
  - Under the Empire, a Pureblood without Force sensitivity was rare and seen as an abomination. Unless the family found them an administrative post, they were killed or enslaved (no footnote). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_%28Post%E2%80%93Great_Hyperspace_War%29) ("Society and culture")
- **Related:** Sith training aims; Children of the Emperor
- **Uncertainty:** [conflict] When were slaves and aliens first admitted?
  - Version A: during the Great Galactic War, by the Emperor's decree (Sith Empire page, "Society and culture", no footnote).
  - Version B: during the Cold War, because of losses (Sith Academy page, ref: SWTOR; Korriban page, ref: SWTOR).

  Version B fits the SWTOR class stories best, because the 3643 BBY intake was still treated as new and controversial. Thanaton's case shows that a slave could already be taken as an apprentice during the Great Galactic War, which partly supports Version A. The two versions may describe a gradual change [inferred].

### The Sith Academy on Korriban (Imperial era)
- **Type:** place
- **Era:** era (rebuilt in 3681 BBY; in use through the Galactic War)
- **Key facts:**
  - **Rebuilding:** in the first year of the Great Galactic War, the Empire retook Korriban and re-established the Academy (ref: *The Old Republic Encyclopedia*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Thanaton) ("Early life and training"). The Empire ordered the Academy rebuilt, including the pyramid that formed its main levels, and it trained hundreds of Sith over the years (no footnote). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Academy_%28Korriban%29) ("True Sith Empire")
  - **Role:** after the Treaty of Coruscant, Korriban became a major centre of learning for the Empire, built over the tombs and old Dreshdae (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Korriban/Legends) ("Cold War"). The Imperial Sith Order trained its members at academies on Korriban and Dromund Kaas, with lesser academies on worlds such as Odacer-Faustin. It was governed by the Dark Council (no footnote). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Order_%28Sith_Empire%29) (intro)
  - **Layout:**
    - It was a stone and durasteel pyramid at the back of the Valley of the Dark Lords. Its entrance stood at the top of a wide stair flanked by two kneeling slave statues. The main hall had twin staircases to the upper levels and a stone monument carved with Sith faces, a reminder that success meant crushing rivals (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Academy_%28Korriban%29) ("Layout")
    - The lower levels held jails for prisoners awaiting interrogation and "many chambers for studying, lecturing, meditation, and training" (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Academy_%28Korriban%29) ("Layout")
  - **Library:** the multi-level library lay off the main hall, with scrolls, holocrons, datapads and holobooks behind armed guards. The head of the Sphere of Ancient Knowledge maintained it (Darth Thanaton from 3640 BBY; whoever held the Sphere before that (lore batch 12)). Its holdings included Darth Vilus's teachings and Darth Iratus's holocron (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Academy_%28Korriban%29) ("Layout")
  - **Living quarters and restricted areas:**
    - The Academy had dormitories for students. It also had secret Ritual Chambers guarded by the Dark Honor Guard, where alchemical creatures such as the Bloodfiend lived (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Academy_%28Korriban%29) ("Layout")
    - Rooms near the summit were restricted to the highest-ranking Sith. The Dark Council kept chambers there for conferences. Rumour held that the Emperor had a throne room deep inside (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Academy_%28Korriban%29) ("Layout")
  - **Security:** Inquisitor Arzanon was the Academy's head of security. He worked with an Intelligence officer to hunt traitor acolytes and was killed in the Republic incursion of 3637 BBY (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Arzanon) (intro). The Academy was troubled by failed acolytes causing problems outside it and by instructors suspected of not being true Sith (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Academy_%28Korriban%29) ("True Sith Empire")
  - **Arrival:** Overseer Harkun gathered his class at the Korriban Arrival Port, which overlooks the Valley (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kory) ("Biography")
  - **3637 BBY:** a Republic force invaded Korriban and briefly held the Academy (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Korriban/Legends) ("Galactic War"). Jedi Master Wytho held the library, which was left burning (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Academy_%28Korriban%29) ("Layout")
- **Related:** Overseers; Acolyte trials; Dromund Kaas academy
- **Uncertainty:** No Imperial-era headmaster of the Korriban Academy is named in the pages read (see "Not found").

### Acolyte trials on Korriban
- **Type:** process
- **Era:** era (Cold War, c. 3643 BBY, and Galactic War)
- **Key facts:**
  - All acolytes went through the training here, aiming to become apprentice to a Sith Lord or Darth. Trials grew harsher, often to the point where only one of many acolytes could survive, and the death toll was "astronomical" (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Korriban/Legends) ("Cold War")
  - **Warrior path (the Empire's Wrath, Cold War):**
    1. To speed the trials, Tremel had the acolyte recover an ancient Sith warblade from the tomb of Ajunta Pall. It became the acolyte's weapon for the rest of the trials (ref: SWTOR "Arm Yourself"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Empire%27s_Wrath) ("Trials")
    2. The acolyte faced trials in the Academy and the Valley, including killing the Beast of Marka Ragnos (ref: SWTOR "Slaying the Beast").
    3. The acolyte betrayed and killed Tremel at Darth Baras's instruction (ref: SWTOR "A New Master").
    4. The acolyte recovered shards from the tomb of Tulak Hord (ref: SWTOR "Sith Arithmetic").
    5. **Final trial:** the acolyte retrieved an ancient lightsaber from Naga Sadow's tomb, with the slave Vette's help, and killed the rival Vemrin. The acolyte moved from warblade to a red-bladed lightsaber and from acolyte to apprentice (ref: SWTOR "The Final Trial"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Empire%27s_Wrath) ("Trials")
  - **Inquisitor path (the future Darth Nox, c. 3643 BBY):**
    1. Go into Ajunta Pall's tomb to be tested by Spindrall, who set his own six acolytes on the student (ref: SWTOR "Of Mind and Matter").
    2. Interrogate a prisoner in the jails (ref: SWTOR "The Plight Of The Acolyte").
    3. Complete a "trial of intellect": recover a holocron from Marka Ragnos's tomb. A rival was given only texts to translate (ref: SWTOR "A Little Knowledge").
    4. Recover a text from Tulak Hord's tomb (ref: SWTOR "The Secret of Tulak Hord").
    5. Survive an ambush in the training facility by two classmates, who had been promised they could go home if they killed the acolyte (ref: SWTOR "Martial Law").
    6. **Final trial:** recover a star map from Naga Sadow's tomb, which meant freeing the Dashade Khem Val (ref: SWTOR "A Map for the Future").

    [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Nox) ("Biography"; "The Dashade and final trials")
  - **Judgment and execution:** Spindrall judged Kory unworthy, and Harkun executed her in front of the class as an example (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kory) ("Biography"); [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Spindrall) (intro)
  - **Rite of blood and bone:** in the Ritual Chambers, an acolyte took a skull from an altar of bones (the remains of failed apprentices), guarded by shyracks, and dipped it in a pool of blood. This summoned the Bloodfiend, which killed the unworthy and added their skulls to the altar (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ritual_Chambers) ("Overview"); [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rite_of_blood_and_bone) ("Overview"). Overseer Ragate administered it, and both the Wrath and Kallig may have passed it (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ragate) ("Biography")
  - **Second-generation class (Galactic War):** Harkun gathered another six acolytes. Darth Thanaton passed on them, and Lord Kallig chose instead. Having a Korriban acolyte as apprentice was "a sign of prestige". The item the trials required was a Sith carving artifact (ref: *The Old Republic Encyclopedia*; SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Xivhkalrainik) ("Biography"); [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Harkun) (intro)
  - **Great Galactic War example:** Teneb Kel trained under Calypho at the Academy, with many trials in the tombs and caves under the Valley. He spent days trapped in crypts, fighting with a training saber and the dark side (ref: *Blood of the Empire*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Thanaton) ("Early life and training")
- **Related:** Rules among acolytes; Weapons; Apprenticeship
- **Uncertainty:**
  - The two class stories show one Overseer's choices. No source gives a fixed, Academy-wide list of trials [unclear].
  - The Wrath and Kallig passed "at exceptional speeds", faster than usual acolytes (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Korriban/Legends) ("Cold War"). The usual length of the acolyte stage is not given (see "Not found").

### Rules among acolytes: rivalry, killing and favouritism
- **Type:** custom/law
- **Era:** era (Cold War and Galactic War)
- **Key facts:**
  - The old Sith practice of murder and betrayal had been made illegal among acolytes, even punishable by death. It still happened: acolytes set traps and ambushes in the tombs and caverns and blamed the beasts and madmen there (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Korriban/Legends) ("Cold War")
  - Harkun objected when Xalek killed a rival, because acolytes were forbidden to kill each other (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Xivhkalrainik) ("Biography"). Harkun called it a disregard for Sith tradition and against Academy rules (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Harkun) ("The last class")
  - Rules were bent by staff. Harkun dismissed a slave-born acolyte's work, bribed others to kill them and set near-impossible tasks. He suggested that a death would look more like an accident in a tomb than in his office (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Harkun) ("Turning slaves into Lords"; "Personality and traits"). Vemrin sent his friend Dolgis to ambush a rival acolyte (ref: SWTOR "Slaying the Beast"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vemrin) ("Biography")
  - Ffon Althe, a Pureblood, killed fellow acolyte Gerr with Force lightning during one of Harkun's displays (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ffon_Althe) ("Biography")
  - Lying to a Lord was fatal. When Althe presented a map he had not recovered, Zash had him confess and then killed him (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ffon_Althe) ("Biography")
  - Under Sith tradition, a corrupt Master's students usually shared the Master's fate. Teneb Kel was arrested with Calypho, but because his training was unfinished he was sent to serve with the Imperial Military instead of being imprisoned (ref: *Blood of the Empire*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Thanaton) ("Early life and training") `unverified` (not covered by SW_Korriban_Academy_Part1/2; queued for a check, lore batch 12) (Teneb Kel's arrest and military service).
- **Related:** Acolyte trials; Overseers
- **Uncertainty:** [conflict] What happened to Gerr?
  - Ffon Althe's "Biography" says Althe "executed" Gerr.
  - The same page's "Powers and abilities" says the lightning "badly injure[d]" him.
  - Spindrall's page lists Gerr among the acolytes who finished the tomb task and returned.

  Corrected (lore batch 12): Gerr was killed. SW_Korriban_Academy_Part1 (researched under the current rules) has Harkun order Ffon to kill Gerr in his chamber and lists Gerr among the acolytes killed by rivals; the "badly injured" reading is dropped.

### Weapons in training: warblade, training saber, lightsaber
- **Type:** practice
- **Era:** era, with comparison marked
- **Key facts:**
  - **Era:** the Wrath used an ancient Sith warblade as an acolyte and earned a red-bladed lightsaber with apprenticeship (ref: SWTOR "Arm Yourself", "The Final Trial"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Empire%27s_Wrath) ("Trials"). Vemrin also fought with a warblade (ref: SWTOR "The Final Trial"). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_%28Post%E2%80%93Great_Hyperspace_War%29) ("Central authority").     - Kaan gave all Sith Lords nominal equal status to stop infighting. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Lord/Legends) ("History")
    - He granted the title Dark Lord of the Sith to all Brotherhood members (ref: *Path of Destruction*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Lord_of_the_Sith/Legends) ("Rule of Two")

    - At the end, all the Academy's apprentices were raised to Dark Lords together and sent to Ruusan (ref: *Path of Destruction*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kas%27im) ("Bane's private tutelage")
  - **Graduation gift:** Kas'im gave Bane the curved-hilt lightsaber of his own late master, Na'daz. Qordis offered a synthetic crystal (ref: *Path of Destruction*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Bane/Legends) ("Training on Korriban"); [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Qordis) ("Biography")
  - **Former Jedi:** Githany, a Jedi defector, was sent to the Academy to train after giving Kaan Lord Hoth's battle plans (ref: *Path of Destruction*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Githany) ("Defection")

- **Uncertainty:** [conflict] How many students did each master teach on Korriban in the New Sith Wars?
  - Version A: "six students for each Master" at Korriban, which was reserved for potential Sith Masters or Lords. Warriors, marauders, assassins, spies, acolytes and adepts went to other worlds' academies (no footnote). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_academy) ("New Sith Wars")
  - Version B: twenty or more students per instructor under Kaan (Sith training page, ref: *Path of Destruction*).

  Version B is footnoted to the novel that depicts the Academy, so it fits best. Version A's four-tier split of academies agrees with the Brotherhood page's "lesser academy" statement.

### Founding and continuity of the Korriban Academy (background)
- **Type:** history
- **Era:** background to all eras
- **Key facts:**
  - The Academy was established in 3996 BBY (ref: *The Complete Star Wars Encyclopedia*) by disciples of Exar Kun (ref: *The Essential Atlas*). Jorak Uln became Headmaster (ref: *KotOR Campaign Guide*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Academy_%28Korriban%29) ("Founding")
  - The Sith training page says the Korriban academy was established by the old Sith Empire and had trained prospective Sith for centuries before being abandoned (ref: *KotOR*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_training) ("Sith Training under the Empires")
  - The Ritual Chambers under the Academy were built by the ancient Sith before the Great Hyperspace War. Revan's Empire built its Academy over them without knowing they were there (ref: SWTOR). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ritual_Chambers) ("Overview")
- **Related:** JCW comparison
- **Uncertainty:** [conflict] Who founded the Academy?
  - Version A: Exar Kun's disciples in 3996 BBY (*Complete Star Wars Encyclopedia*; *Essential Atlas*).
  - Version B: the old Sith Empire, centuries earlier (*KotOR*, per the Sith training page).
  - The Ritual Chambers page (SWTOR) says "Revan's Empire" built the Academy, which partly contradicts Version A.

  Version A is the most specific and best sourced. Version B and the SWTOR wording may mean an older site reused [inferred]. For an Imperial-era campaign, the 3681 BBY rebuild is what matters. Resolved (lore batch 12), ruling 3: an ancient site (the Ritual Chambers are older); the academy itself was founded in 3996 BBY by Exar Kun's disciples.
