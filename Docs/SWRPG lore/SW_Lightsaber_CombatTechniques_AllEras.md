# Lightsaber Combat Techniques, Rules and Resistant Materials (All Legends Eras)

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 21, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
Covers mechanics-level lore that applies across forms: the six body target zones, the NJO three rings of defense, the marks of contact (sun djem, sai cha and others), stances and basic moves, blaster deflection and energy absorption, saber throw and telekinetic combat, Force-assisted leaps and speed, multiple opponents, Dun Möch, anti-Jedi tactics, and lightsaber-resistant materials. Key facts: cortosis shorts out blades, while phrik, beskar and songsteel only resist them. Mestare crystals are immune to cortosis. Water shorts most blades. Protosabers could not be thrown.

## Entries

### 1. Body target zones (six)
- **Type:** technique framework
- **Era:** All eras (from Shii-Cho onward)
- **Key facts:**

Attacks and parries are named by the zone they target. In idealized drills, attacks are horizontal and parries vertical, except Zone 1, where this is reversed. [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Body Target Zones](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends)

| Zone | Target | Attack | Parry |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Head | Vertical chop (bisect) | Horizontal guard at head height |
| 2 | Right arm/side (weapon arm if right-handed) | Horizontal sideswipe | Vertical parry |
| 3 | Left arm/side | Mirror of zone 2 | Vertical parry |
| 4 | Back (whole torso) | A success is "almost always fatal" | Drop parry, blade angled down behind the back |
| 5 / 6 | Right / left leg | Low sweep | Drop parry, hilt at waist |
[Lightsaber combat/Legends §Body Target Zones](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends)

- **Related:** Form I (Part 1)
- **Uncertainty:** none

### 2. Three rings of defense (NJO, Kam Solusar; from *I, Jedi*)
- **Type:** technique framework
- **Era:** NJO era
- **Key facts:**

- **Outer ring:** grand sweeps at range; slow but powerful. Four diagonal guards.
- **Middle ring:** right-angle guards for faster blows; good for blast deflection.
- **Inner ring:** the last line, proof against lunges. One navel guard; parry with the lower third of the blade, then counter to the chest or abdomen. [Lightsaber combat/Legends §The Three Rings of Defense](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends)

- **Related:** Forms Part 2 (NJO styles); Training
- **Uncertainty:** none

### 4. Stances and basic moves
- **Type:** techniques
- **Era:** All eras
- **Key facts:**

- **Stances:**
  - Jedi Ready: dominant foot back, vertical parry on the dominant side; like the Ataru guard.
  - Defensive Neutral: feet even; maximum blade area for blast deflection and as a warning.
  - Offensive Neutral: feet even, blade at the enemy; minimum visible blade.
  - Center of Being: blade horizontal, hilt below the chin; also used for meditation. [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Ready Stances](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends)
- **Moves:**
  - Jung: 180° turn.
  - Jung ma: 360° spin for momentum.
  - Shun: one-handed 360° spin.
  - Sai: leap over a leg attack.
  - Flowing Water: follow the foe's withdrawing blade out of a bind into them.
  - Falling Leaf: spin-slash at a foe behind, then face front again.
  - Dulon: counter-slash from a midsection pommel position against torso cuts.
  - Kai-kan: re-enacting a past duel. [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Moves and Maneuvers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends) [Dulon/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dulon/Legends)
- **Form-specific maneuvers:** see Forms Part 1 and Part 2.

- **Related:** Forms Part 1 and 2
- **Uncertainty:** none

### 5. Deflection and energy defense
- **Type:** techniques and powers
- **Era:** All eras (Thon, Old Sith Wars era, to the NJO)
- **Key facts:**

- **The blade itself:** repels blaster bolts, most force fields and other blades. [Lightsaber/Legends §Cutting power](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber/Legends)
- **Form fit for blaster defense:** Soresu and Shien are the anti-blaster forms. The NJO Fast style is the best of the three NJO styles against blasters, and the Middle ring suits deflection. (Details in Forms Part 1 and Part 2.) [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Seven Forms](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends) [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Fast Style](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends) [Lightsaber combat/Legends §The Three Rings of Defense](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends)
- **Early training:** blindfolded Shii-Cho blast-deflection. [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Training Methods](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends)
- **Force Deflection ("Rebuke"):** saber-less deflection that needs much Force skill; most Jedi used their sabers instead. Example: Master Thon repelled pirate fire on Ambria (Old Sith Wars). [Force deflection/Legends §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_deflection/Legends) [Force deflection/Legends §Notable uses](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_deflection/Legends)
- **Tutaminis:**
  - Energy absorption and dispersal, taught from the first academy classes.
  - Dissipating a blaster bolt or saber blade is a rare feat.
  - It also covers heat, electricity and radiation (RPG source). [Tutaminis §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tutaminis) [Tutaminis §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tutaminis)
- **Slugthrowers:** a blade cannot redirect slugs, which vaporize on it. This is why Jedi hunters favor them. [Jedi hunter/Legends §Equipment and tactics](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_hunter/Legends)

- **Related:** Form III; Form V (Part 1)
- **Uncertainty:** none

### 6. Saber throw, Saber Barrier, telekinetic combat
- **Type:** techniques
- **Era:** Old Republic (Traya c. 3951 BBY) to the NJO
- **Key facts:**

- **Saber throw:**
  - The blade is locked on mechanically or held on by the Force, then thrown boomerang-style through obstacles, returning (usually deactivated).
  - Skilled users throw far, correct course mid-air, or hover the saber.
  - A straight "destructive" throw variant exists. [Telekinesis/Legends §Saber Throw](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends)
  - **Users:** Galen Marek (and his clone, two at once), Savage Opress, Yoda, Vader, Kyle Katarn, Jaden Korr; possibly Revan and Surik. [Telekinesis/Legends §Saber Throw](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends)
  - Protosabers could not be thrown (cable). [Protosaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Protosaber)
  - After Mustafar, Vader used saber throws instead of chasing foes on high ground. [Sokan §Applications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sokan)
- **Saber Barrier:** sabers whirl in a horizontal circle around the user as a wall against melee. NJO-era dual-saber users (Alora, New Reborn), Mace Windu; Jaden Korr possibly. [Telekinesis/Legends §Saber Barrier](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends)
- **Telekinetic lightsaber combat:**
  - Darth Traya wielded at least three sabers independently ("the most remarkable form").
  - The Reborn Revan (14 ABY) did something similar but was easily knocked down.
  - Bane used it to kill three boys; Windu unclipped and lit his saber to destroy a super battle droid (Coruscant, 19 BBY).
  - "Extremely difficult". [Telekinesis/Legends §Telekinetic lightsaber combat](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends) [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Telekinetic Lightsaber Combat](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends)

- **Related:** WeaponVariants (protosaber, shoto)
- **Uncertainty:** none

### 7. Leaps, speed and mobility
- **Type:** powers
- **Era:** 5000 BBY (Relin Druur) to the NJO
- **Key facts:**

- **Force jump:** most Masters can easily jump several times their own height. Jaden Korr could jump up to eight times higher than normal (from the *Jedi Academy* game). [Force jump/Legends §Applications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_jump/Legends)
- **Force speed:** Relin Druur crossed 100 m in about a second, by his own estimate (5000 BBY). [Force speed/Legends §Applications](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_speed/Legends)
  - It pairs with the NJO Fast style. [Fast style §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fast_style)
- **Su ma rotations** and **Sokan** terrain use: see Forms Part 2.
- **Twin Suns** aerial drill: see Training.

- **Related:** Forms Part 2 (Sokan, Fast style)

### 8. Multiple opponents
- **Type:** tactics
- **Era:** All eras
- **Key facts:**

- **Shii-Cho:** wide sweeps suit many foes, but it is weaker against a single mobile opponent. [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Form I: Shii-Cho](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends)
- **Shien:** assumes Jedi are outnumbered (blaster crowds); "lacked effectiveness against single opponents". [Lightsaber combat/Legends §Form V: Shien/Djem So](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_combat/Legends)
- **Jar'Kai:** easier to hold off several foes. With Tràkata it is "extremely effective even against multiple opponents". [Jar'Kai/Legends §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jar%27Kai/Legends) [Tràkata/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tràkata/Legends)
- **NJO Fast style:** strong against several non-melee foes. Its short reach means taking them one at a time. [Fast style §Description](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fast_style)
- **Trispzest whirlwind:** effective against several foes. [Trispzest §Techniques](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trispzest)
- **Anchor example:** Bane (with Zannah) against five Jedi on Tython (990 BBY); see Duels. [Duel on Tython](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Duel_on_Tython)
- **Battle meditation:** Worror used it to empower the Jedi team on Tython instead of fighting directly. [Duel on Tython](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Duel_on_Tython) [Battle meditation/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_meditation/Legends)

- **Related:** Forms Part 1 and 2; Duels: Tython 990 BBY
- **Uncertainty:** none

## Game notes (optional, suggestions only)
*(Design suggestions only.)*
- Zones 1–6 could serve as hit locations; marks of contact as outcome types with a Jedi ethics weight (sun djem / cho mai = light; sai tok / Sith shiak = dark-leaning).
- Material flags: `SHORTS_BLADE` (cortosis), `RESISTS_BLADE` (phrik, beskar, songsteel…), `IMMUNE_TO_SHORT` (Mestare crystal), `WATER_SHORT`.
- Saber throw could require a lock-on (mechanical or Force) and be barred for tethered protosabers.

## Decisions needed
1. Cortosis effect [conflict]: block-only (KOTOR I/II, *Obi-Wan*) or short-out (later sources). The short-out version is the page's main description. Duration [conflict]: instant re-ignition (*Vision of the Future*) or useless for minutes (*Purge*, *Legacy*).
2. Marks of contact ethics: give sai tok ("Sith-like") and lethal shiak targets a dark-side or conduct weight?

4. Beskar impact transfer (force "still transferred in part to the wearer"): model blunt damage through beskar?
5. Ysalamiri: the sources give no saber-duel rule. Should a Force-neutral bubble simply disable Force-assisted techniques?
