# Sith Academies and Sith Training Methods (all eras)

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched 23 Nov 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends), as cited per entry. Draft for review. Written by Claude from a Chrome read of the *Sith academy* page, its category *Sith academies* (14 pages), and the linked Sith order, rank and training pages.

## Summary
This file covers every Sith academy on record and the training methods each Sith order used, as dated facts. It is a companion to SW_Sith_Academies_Training_OldRepublic.md and SW_Korriban_Academy_Part1/2, which hold the detailed Old Republic Korriban material; those facts are cross-referenced, not repeated. The new material here is mainly:
- the general academy method: lightsaber basics first, then each student is steered to the form that suits their body;
- the Blademaster's role (form assignment, the morning class, supervised challenges);
- class sizes by order;
- entry rules (medallion and sponsor; Force sensitivity alone; one-year wait after failing);
- prestige as the Korriban measure of progress;
- the Brotherhood of Darkness's tiered academy network;
- the automated Korriban academy of 3951 BBY.

## Entries

### Sith training: general method (all academy-based orders)
- **Type:** practice
- **Era:** every era with academies (the Sith empires, Revan and Malak's Empire, the Brotherhood of Darkness, the One Sith)
- **Key facts:**
  - **Purpose:** to forge a Force adept into a full Sith. Training was brutal and often lethal because it was meant to weed out the weak. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_training) ("Methods and Specifications")
  - **Two systems:** the Sith empires trained in academies because they needed numbers for war. The Rule of Two scouted and recruited single apprentices to a much higher standard. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_training) ("Entry")
  - **Classroom model:** instructors taught several students at once. The system kept students competitive and isolated rather than cooperative. The most talented were glorified and the weak pushed out. Students were also expected to study and practise on their own. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_training) ("Training Regimes")
  - **Combat method:** students learned the basics of lightsaber combat first. They were then steered toward the form that best suited their physical attributes. Facilities for private practice were provided. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_training) ("Combat training")
  - **Force method:** taught in class by one master to many students. Students drew on anger and focused on offensive uses, starting with simple skills such as telekinesis and building to telepathy and Force lightning. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_training) ("Force application training")
  - **Interrogation and torture** were taught on live subjects (prisoners of war, local criminals). Assassination was important enough that whole academies were devoted to it. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_training) ("Interrogation and Assassination")
  - **Not taught in academies:** some branches, such as Sith sorcery and *mechu-deru*, were left to the student's own research. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_training) ("Other")
  - **Master and apprentice inside the academy system:** masters sometimes took an apprentice under their wing and tutored them individually (Malak and Bandon). Under Kaan this broke the rules and had to be hidden from the headmaster, but it still happened. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_training) ("Training Regimes")
- **Related:** Blademaster; Prestige; Sith apprentice
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Sith apprentice (the stage after the academy, or instead of it)
- **Type:** rank
- **Era:** all eras
- **Key facts:**
  - Usually chosen by the master, after the candidate showed an impressive act of loyalty or cruelty. Sometimes the candidate pledged themselves; sometimes the master sought them out, guided by the dark side. Occasionally the apprentice was a child. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_apprentice/Legends) (intro)
  - Unlike a Padawan, an apprentice was often sent on missions alone as the master's agent, sometimes to their death. Learning was the apprentice's job; teaching was not necessarily the master's. Apprentices were often allowed their own schemes. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_apprentice/Legends) ("Role")
  - Apprentices learned the master's lightsaber technique and the dark side, and were held to the Sith Code. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_apprentice/Legends) ("Training")
  - Sidious's view (Rule of Two): the apprentice must struggle for every scrap of knowledge, and the master withholds as much as possible for as long as possible. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_training) ("Training Regimes")
- **Related:** Sith training; the Sith Empire's acolyte stage (SW_Sith_Academies_Training_OldRepublic)
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Sith acolyte (rank)
- **Type:** rank
- **Era:** all eras (the meaning shifts)
- **Key facts:**
  - In general use, a Force-sensitive trained as a lesser agent outside the master–apprentice relationship: not a true Sith, but following its tradition. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_acolyte/Legends) (intro)
  - Malak's acolytes studied the dark side in secret in the Temple of the Ancients on Lehon (3956 BBY). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_acolyte/Legends) ("Known acolytes")
  - Under the reconstituted Sith Empire, acolytes were tested by Overseers before earning a place as an apprentice to a Sith Lord. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_acolyte/Legends) ("Known acolytes")
  - The Brotherhood of Darkness trained acolytes at its Dathomir and Iridonia academies. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_acolyte/Legends) ("Known acolytes")
  - The One Sith used the rank too; Krayt called himself an acolyte during his apprenticeship to XoXaan on Korriban. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_acolyte/Legends) ("Known acolytes")
- **Related:** Overseer; Brotherhood academies
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Blademaster
- **Type:** role
- **Era:** attested for the Brotherhood of Darkness's Korriban academy (c. 1006–1000 BBY); a Blademaster also taught at Odacer-Faustin before 3645 BBY (see SW_Sith_Academies_Training_OldRepublic)
- **Key facts:**
  - The Korriban academy's lightsaber instructor, equal in rank to the Jedi Battlemaster, and expected to know every form well enough to teach it. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Blademaster) (intro)
  - The lightsaber class began in the morning. At the end of practice, students could challenge each other under the Blademaster's supervision; without care, deaths happened. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Blademaster) (intro)
  - Kas'im analysed each student's basic style and assigned them a key form to learn. After each duel he pointed out improvements for winner and loser and lessons for the watching students. He trained students in all weathers, often heavy rain. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kas%27im) (biography)
  - He trained almost everyone with single blades and kept some techniques (dual-wield Form IV) to himself, so he could beat any student who turned on him. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kas%27im) (biography; "Powers and abilities")
  - Under Kaan, Kas'im's academy taught about twenty students together through repeated sequences. Under Revan, an instructor had three or four students and tutored them individually through sparring. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_training) ("Combat training")
  - Example: Bane was selected to learn Form V as a student. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Bane/Legends) ("Lightsaber combat")
- **Related:** Sith training saber
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Sith training saber
- **Type:** training weapon
- **Era:** used at the Korriban academy (attested for the Brotherhood of Darkness); not universal, and not used by the Rule of Two
- **Key facts:**
  - A blunt durasteel blade on a mock hilt, balanced like a lightsaber. Its surface carried microscopic toxin-filled pelko bug barbs, which burned and temporarily paralysed the area hit, simulating a lost limb. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_training_saber) ("Description"); [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kas%27im) (biography)
  - Double-bladed training sabers also existed (Sirak used one). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sirak) ("The Korriban Academy")
  - Not universal; the Rule of Two Sith did not use them at all. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_training) ("Combat training")
- **Related:** Blademaster
- **Uncertainty:** This entry confirms the item that SW_Sith_Academies_Training_OldRepublic marked `unverified`.

### Prestige (Korriban, Jedi Civil War)
- **Type:** practice
- **Era:** 3956 BBY (Uthar Wynn's academy)
- **Key facts:**
  - Prestige was how Korriban students measured success. Students earned it through great deeds or by recovering valuable Sith artifacts from the Valley of the Dark Lords, which bred cutthroat competition. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Prestige) (intro)
  - The first student to gain enough prestige became a true Sith. Wynn also rewarded the first to recite the Sith Code. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Uthar_Wynn) ("Jedi Civil War")
  - (game stat: ways to earn prestige in KOTOR included reciting the Code, interrogating a prisoner, recovering artifacts from tombs and informing on a rival; killing every rival earned none.) [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Prestige) ("Behind the scenes")
- **Related:** Entry rules (Korriban, Jedi Civil War)
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Entry rules by order
- **Type:** practice
- **Era:** dated per line
- **Key facts:**
  - **Revan and Malak's Empire (c. 3959–3956 BBY):** a prospective student had to be sponsored by an instructor or impress a teacher or student and receive a Sith Medallion as proof, or kill a student and take theirs. Fallen Jedi usually got in easily. Hopefuls competed in a group; one earned enough prestige to impress the headmaster, and the rest had to wait a year to try again, if they survived. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Academy_(Korriban)) ("Curriculum and faculty"); [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_training) ("Entry")
  - Final approval lay with Yuthura Ban, the headmaster's apprentice. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Medallion) (intro); [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yuthura_Ban) (biography)
  - Many hopefuls were ordinary people seeking a better life or the winning side; others were fallen Jedi. Students harassed and tricked hopefuls waiting in Dreshdae. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_hopeful) ("History")
  - **Brotherhood of Darkness (Kaan, c. 1006–1000 BBY):** Force sensitivity was the only requirement; the training itself weeded out the unfit. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_training) ("Entry")
  - **Reconstituted Sith Empire (from 3681 BBY):** any Force-sensitive found was sent, willing or not. See SW_Sith_Academies_Training_OldRepublic, "Recruitment".
  - **One Sith (c. 130 ABY):** acolytes were trained from birth. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/One_Sith) (organization)
- **Related:** Prestige
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Sith Academy, Korriban: dated history across orders
- **Type:** place
- **Era:** 3996 BBY onward
- **Key facts:**
  - **Founded 3996 BBY** by Exar Kun's disciples (the Brotherhood of the Sith); Jorak Uln became headmaster. The Jedi did not attack it after the Great Sith War. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Academy_(Korriban)) ("Founding")
  - Under Kun, formal training was largely absent: most of his followers gained power through possession by Sith spirits. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_training) ("Sith Training under the Empires")
  - **c. 3959–3956 BBY:** the academy trained Revan and Malak's Sith in the sublevels (by then only the entrance stood above ground). Its students were Force-sensitives choosing a side, or Jedi disillusioned with the Order, the war or the Republic. Uthar Wynn succeeded Uln, who was exiled. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Academy_(Korriban)) ("Revan's Empire")
  - Rules: students learned to care only for themselves; the academy's Master could be succeeded by his apprentice at any time. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Revan%27s_Sith_Empire) ("Government and politics")
  - Malak set up a sparring ring with caged Republic prisoners for lethal duels, or captured local beasts, and graded students on their performance. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_training) ("Combat training")
  - Headmasters could be overthrown by their second-in-command, and people tended to disappear during intrigues. Wynn had a less promising student killed to keep a promising one focused (Selene and Dustil Onasi). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Academy_(Korriban)) ("Curriculum and faculty"); [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Uthar_Wynn) ("Jedi Civil War")
  - Most Force-sensitives there wore Sith officer uniforms rather than robes. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yuthura_Ban) ("Personality and traits")
  - **After 3956 BBY:** the Sith Civil War made Korriban a graveyard, and the academy was looted and abandoned. **By 3951 BBY** there were no teachers left, and the academy ran automated, with students relying on computers for basic training. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Academy_(Korriban)) ("Revan's Empire")
  - **3681 BBY onward:** rebuilt by the reconstituted Sith Empire. See SW_Sith_Academies_Training_OldRepublic and SW_Korriban_Academy_Part1/2.
  - **1006 BBY:** reopened by the Brotherhood of Darkness, with Qordis as headmaster and Kas'im as Blademaster. Reserved for students with the potential to become Sith Lords. Six students for each Master. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Academy_(Korriban)) ("Brotherhood of Darkness"); [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_academy) ("New Sith Wars")
  - The Brotherhood's library held scrolls and tomes but no holocrons, which were lost or held by the Jedi. It was disorganised, partly by design, to make knowledge hard to reach for the undedicated. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Academy_(Korriban)) ("Brotherhood of Darkness")
  - **c. 130 ABY:** Krayt reopened it, then moved training to the Temple of the Sith on Coruscant. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_training) ("Sith training under the Rule of One")
- **Related:** Prestige; Blademaster
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Trayus Academy (Malachor V)
- **Type:** place
- **Era:** built by an ancient Sith Empire (date unknown); rediscovered 3961 BBY; destroyed 3951 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - An ancient Sith academy, abandoned long before the Mandalorian Wars. Who built it is not known. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trayus_Academy) ("History"; "Behind the scenes")
  - Revan found it in 3961 BBY and founded his Sith assassin order there. During the Jedi Civil War it was one of several secret bases for converting captured Jedi, whose wills the place weakened. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trayus_Academy) ("History")
  - From c. 3954 BBY, Darth Traya ran it as headmistress, training assassins and Marauders to destroy the Jedi. It was the Sith Triumvirate's headquarters. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trayus_Academy) ("History"); [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Triumvirate) (history)
  - It focused on assassins rather than an army of warriors, because the Triumvirate worked covertly. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_training) ("Sith Training under the Empires")
  - Layout: the Trayus Crescent (western hall with alcoves, Sith commandos), the Trayus Proving Grounds (eastern warren of cramped rooms with Dark Jedi and commandos), and the inner Trayus Core. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trayus_Academy) ("Layout")
- **Related:** SW_Malachor_V
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Rule of Two training (for comparison)
- **Type:** practice
- **Era:** from c. 1000 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - No formal regime: each master taught by preference and the student's needs. Bane taught Zannah with scheduled, repeated sequences, as Korriban had taught him. Vader gave Marek only the basics, then forced him to improvise through tests and brutal sparring. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_training) ("Sith Training under the Rule of Two"; "Combat training")
  - Concealment (Force stealth) was stressed. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_training) ("Force application training")
  - The "sacrifice": killing, or enabling the death of, a loved one, either at the start of training (to ensure commitment) or at the end (as a test). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_training) ("The Sacrifice")
- **Related:** Sith apprentice
- **Uncertainty:** none

## Changes from the previous file
Compared with SW_Sith_Academies_Training_OldRepublic.md and SW_Korriban_Academy_Part1/2:
- **New: the general combat method.** Lightsaber basics first, then a form suited to the student's body. The old files had nothing on form choice for Sith students. [Sith training](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_training) ("Combat training")
- **New: the Blademaster's role** (form assignment, morning class, supervised challenges, post-duel critique). The old file had a Blademaster only at Odacer-Faustin.
- **New: class sizes** (3–4 under Revan; 20+ under Kaan; six per Master at the Brotherhood's Korriban).
- **New: entry rules** for the Jedi Civil War academy (medallion or sponsor, one-year wait) and the Brotherhood (Force sensitivity only).
- **New: prestige** as Korriban's measure of progress.
- **New: the Brotherhood's tiered academies**, and the 3951 BBY automated Korriban academy.
- **Confirmed:** the Sith training saber's pelko-barb design (the old file marked it `unverified`).
- No conflicts found with the old files.
