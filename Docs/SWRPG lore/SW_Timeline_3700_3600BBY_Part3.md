# SWRPG Galaxy Timeline, c. 3,700–3,600 BBY (Part 3 of 3): Changes, conflicts and decisions

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 21, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
Part 3 holds the checking sections for the timeline in Parts 1 and 2: an era index, a row-by-row check of every 3,700–3,600 BBY row in `SW_Legends_Galaxy_Timeline.md` (those rows were removed from it in lore batch 20 and are kept in docs-v2/lore/superseded/), the conflicts with the cross-referenced topic files, what was not found or not used, the developer's decisions, and the source list for all three parts. Headline corrections: the old file has Darth Malgus killed c. 3,640 BBY (he returns at Ossus in 3,628 BBY), Mandalore the Lesser rising c. 3,663 BBY on Nar Shaddaa (it was 3,667 BBY in a Geonosian arena), the Dread Masters' "campaign" beginning in 3,668 BBY (they were captured that year), the Jedi moving to Tython c. 3,648 BBY (3,650 BBY), and Vaylin dying on Zakuul (she died on Odessen).
