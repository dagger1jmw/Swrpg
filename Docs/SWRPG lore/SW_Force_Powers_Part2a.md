# Force Powers, Part 2a of 2: Alter (telekinesis, mind and environment)

## Summary
This part covers the Alter discipline: telekinesis and every named variant (push, pull, grip, choke, crush, kill and the rest), mind influence (mind trick, fear, illusion, domination, Memory Walk, Mind Shard, stun and slow), and environmental and elemental powers (Alter Environment, weather storms, pyrokinesis, cryokinesis, Force weapon, Force light). Its central conflict is Force grip and choke: Wookieepedia calls the grip techniques "often regarded" as dark, yet records Jedi using them, and Bane's Book of Sith files Choke under the Sith Offense school. Lightning, drain, essence transfer, Force storms (wormhole), thought bombs, Sith sorcery and alchemy are in Part 2b. The scale-anchor table for both halves is at the end of this file.

**Division of work:** Part 1a (Control) and Part 1b (Sense and universal powers) are by another worker. Part 2a/2b (this worker) covers Alter, dark-side powers and Sith sorcery. Part 3a (another worker) covers rare, lost, species and order-specific techniques, with §T11 for other Legends powers not covered in Parts 1–2 (e.g. Force deflection, mind probe, Force cloak, Sever Force, Crucitorn, healing others, Dopplegänger); Part 3b holds the catalog check.

**Classification key:** "Jedi:" gives the Jedi Order's Control/Sense/Alter class (The Jedi Path and Jedi vs. Sith, as reported on Wookieepedia). "Sith school:" gives Bane's Book of Sith schools (Offense, Body, Mind, Sith magic) where a page names one. "Side:" gives Wookieepedia's infobox alignment, with every conflicting version. Fields 1–9 of the brief are marked F1–F9.

**Cross-references (not repeated here):** SW_DarkSide_Fall_Corruption_Redemption_Part1–3.md (how falls work; physical corruption; the dark side as a power source); SW_Sith_Training_AllEras_Part1.md (Era 1: holocrons, sorcery and alchemy as teaching tools) and _Part2.md (Era 7: Bane's holocron); SW_Midichlorians_Force_Potential.md (§3 averages; §6 Vader's cybernetics and lost potential); SW_Jedi_Training_AllEras_Part1–2.md (which rank learns what).

## Entries

### How the Jedi and the Sith classify Alter powers
- **Type:** framework
- **Era:** all eras; written c. 990 BBY–19 BBY (The Jedi Path)
- **Key facts:**
  - The Jedi taught Control to Initiates, Sense to Padawans and Alter to Knights. Alter changes "the perceptions of others" and the physical world, and it is called the hardest of the three. The fundamental Alter techniques are telekinesis, affect mind and Alter Environment ([Force power](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_power/Legends) ("Alter"); [Alter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Alter_%28Jedi_Order%29)).
  - Untrained users who stumble into Alter can cause disasters ([Alter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Alter_%28Jedi_Order%29)).
  - Bane's Book of Sith instead sorts powers into schools: Offense (Inertia, Blind, Push, Choke, Throw), Body (lightning, convection, cryokinesis, drain life, death field), Mind (Crucitorn, Hatred, Horror, Memory Walk, Mind Shard; Crucitorn is written up in Part 3a §T11, not here) and Sith magic ([Force power](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_power/Legends) ("By the Sith")).
  - Body-school powers can harm the user and work poorly through cybernetics ([Force power](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_power/Legends) ("By the Sith")).
- **Related:** Part 1a (Control) and Part 1b (Sense); Part 2b (Body-school and Sith magic powers).
- **Uncertainty:** Several powers are Alter for the Jedi but Body or Mind for the Sith; both are listed in each entry. [conflict] Where the Jedi class and the Sith school differ, neither is "wrong"; they are two in-universe systems.

## Section A: Telekinesis and its variants

### Telekinesis (Move Object)
- **Type:** F1. Aliases: Move Object, "moving objects". Jedi: Alter (a fundamental Alter technique). Sith school: Offense covers its combat forms (Push, Throw, Choke). Side: Neutral (infobox); the intro calls it a "neutral ability" that most Force-sensitives have ([Telekinesis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends) (intro); [Force power](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_power/Legends) ("Alter"; "Offense")).
- **Era:** F4. All eras, every tradition. Never lost or forbidden as such; only some derivatives were judged dark (below).
- **Key facts:**
  - F2. Precision and strength depend on concentration, willpower and emotional state. Moving large or heavy objects exhausts the user. Users usually gesture, and normally need sight or "physical awareness" of where the target is; skilled users could act on a target in another room or light-years away through a holocomm or viewscreen ([Telekinesis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends) ("Description")).
  - F3. Scale feats (see the scale-anchor table at the end of this file): Yoda lifted Luke's X-wing out of the Dagobah swamp (3 ABY, *Episode V*); Dorsk 81 pushed seventeen Star Destroyers out of the Yavin system (12 ABY); Nihilus pulled a *Centurion*-class battlecruiser out of Malachor V's gravity well (3960 BBY); Kyp Durron moved a black hole back onto the Yuuzhan Vong ship that projected it; Rahm Kota tore part of a TIE factory off and sent it into Nar Shaddaa's atmosphere while still inside it ([Telekinesis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends) ("Description"; "Users")).
  - F3. Dooku's telekinesis lessons were recorded on the Holocron of Asli Krimsan; the Zeison Sha were counted among the most skilled telekinetics; Corran Horn's family line was notably weak at it ([Telekinesis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends) ("Users")).
  - F5. Taught to Jedi Knights as part of Alter ([Force power](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_power/Legends) ("Alter")); learned by every Force tradition, often untaught. See SW_Jedi_Training_AllEras_Part1.md for when Jedi learners met it.
  - F6. Not a dark act in itself; its uses decide that (see Grip/Choke and Crush/Kill below). Cost is fatigue scaled to mass ([Telekinesis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends) ("Description")).
  - F7. Counters: breaking line of sight or awareness; mass (enough mass resists a push); a Force barrier or telekinetic shield ([Telekinesis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends) ("Force Push"; "Force Barrier")). The Yuuzhan Vong were "near" immune to direct Force effects, but Kyp hit them with an indirectly moved object ([Telekinesis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends) ("Users")). Ysalamiri create a Force-neutral bubble, kilometres wide for large groups ([Ysalamiri](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ysalamiri/Legends) (intro; "Biology and appearance")).
- **Related:** F8. Prerequisite for every entry in this section; Force Pull and Push are the parents of the named derivatives ([Telekinesis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends) ("Derivatives of Force Pull"; "Derivatives of Force Push"; "Derivatives of Force Grip")).
- **Uncertainty:** none on the base power.

### Force Pull, Force Throw, Force Disarm, Levitation and Force Flight
- **Type:** F1. Telekinesis applications. Jedi: Alter. Sith school: Offense (Throw). Side: neutral.
- **Era:** F4. All eras.
- **Key facts:**
  - F2/F3. **Pull:** draws objects or people to the user. Khem Val claimed in 3641 BBY that Tulak Hord once pulled a ship the size of the *Endar Spire* down onto a planet (SWTOR); Galen Marek pulled a falling Imperial I-class Star Destroyer out of the sky into a controlled crash on Raxus Prime (2 BBY, TFU) ([Telekinesis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends) ("Force Pull"); [Galen Marek](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galen_Marek) ("'Imperialized' Raxus Prime")).
  - **Throw:** a combination of Push and Grip that hurls objects or people; Palpatine hurled Senate repulsorpods at Yoda (19 BBY) ([Telekinesis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends) ("Force Throw")).
  - **Disarm:** a Pull derivative that tears a weapon away; Yoda disarmed Ventress (TCW "Ambush") ([Telekinesis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends) ("Force Disarm")).
  - **Levitation and Force Flight:** lifting oneself or others; Maw is named for Force Flight ([Telekinesis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends) ("Levitation"; "Force Flight")).
  - F6. Neutral. F7. As for telekinesis.
- **Related:** F8. Telekinesis; Force Rend and Force Disarm derive from Pull.
- **Uncertainty:** [unclear] Tulak Hord's feat is a character's claim, not shown.

### Force Push family: Push, Repulse, Wave, Shockwave, Blast and Burst
- **Type:** F1. Telekinesis applications. Jedi: Alter. Sith school: Offense (Push). Side: neutral, except Force blast (dark and neutral versions) and Force Destruction (dark) below.
- **Era:** F4. All eras.
- **Key facts:**
  - F2. **Push:** a shove of Force energy. It can be resisted by a telekinetic shield or by enough mass ([Telekinesis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends) ("Force Push")).
  - **Force Wave:** a "telekinetic explosion" in all directions (KotOR); Windu and Yoda used it; Bane brought the Temple of the Ancients down on his instructor; Plagueis nearly atomized six Maladians with it ([Telekinesis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends) ("Force Wave")).
  - **Force Shockwave:** a Wave variant used by Kenobi and Savage Opress ([Telekinesis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends) ("Force Shockwave")).
  - **Force Repulse:** a "gigantic telekinetic explosion" pushing outward on the scale of Force Wave; it only reaches a set distance, so it can be escaped by moving away. **Force Burst**, a sphere of stored energy that homes on a target, "light and dark side" in its infobox, blue or green for Jedi and reddish for Sith; Force protection or a protection bubble can nullify it ([Telekinesis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends) ("Force Repulse"; "Force Burst"); [Force Burst](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_Burst) (intro)).
  - **Force blast:** see its own entry in Part 2b (dark sorcerous version) and the "universal" compressed-air version used c. 40–44 ABY ([Force blast](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_blast) (intro)).
  - F3. Dorsk 81's push of seventeen Star Destroyers (12 ABY) is described as "possibly" the most powerful Force Push on record ([Dorsk 81](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dorsk_81) ("The ultimate sacrifice")).
  - F6. Neutral; strain scales with the effect.
  - F7. Mass, telekinetic shields, Force barrier ([Telekinesis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends) ("Force Push"; "Force Barrier")).
- **Related:** F8. Telekinesis. Force Destruction is the dark-side twin of Force Burst (Part 2b). Sith Seeker combines a push with a lightning ball (Part 2b).
- **Uncertainty:** none.

### Force Grip and Force Choke
- **Type:** F1. Aliases: grip, choke, "Force choke". Jedi: Alter (telekinesis). Sith school: Offense (Choke) ([Force power](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_power/Legends) ("Offense")). Side: [conflict] see below.
- **Era:** F4. All eras; used by Sith from the ancient period; no date of invention found.
- **Key facts:**
  - F2. **Grip** immobilizes or lifts a target; skilled users can grip without line of sight or through a viewscreen. **Choke** constricts the throat or windpipe ([Telekinesis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends) ("Force Grip"; "Force Choke")).
  - F3. **Grip users:** Dooku (on Turk Falso), Desann, Kyle Katarn (against Tavion), Galen Marek ([Telekinesis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends) ("Force Grip")).
  - F3. **Choke users:** Barriss Offee (TCW "The Wrong Jedi"); Sidious through a closed door (TCW "The Lawless"); Vader on Admiral Motti (0 BBY), and Vader breaking Kento Marek's neck without looking; Palpatine choking Admiral Termo through a hologram; Jacen Solo killing Patra Tebut (40 ABY); Kueller; Maw; the Jedi Exile, "regardless of alignment" ([Telekinesis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends) ("Force Choke")).
  - F3. More choke users: Vader choked Admiral Ozzel over a viewscreen from his meditation chamber (3 ABY); Anakin used it as a Jedi on Orliss Gillmunn at Jabiim and to torture Poggle the Lesser, and on Padmé on Mustafar (19 BBY); Revan choked a Republic soldier; Tyranus choked Komari Vosa; the Bimm serial killer Toki Tollivar, the "Corellian Strangler", killed all his victims this way. Luke Skywalker choked Gamorrean guards at Jabba's palace (4 ABY) and a Nightsister, both times "to disable" rather than kill (*The Courtship of Princess Leia*) ([Telekinesis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends) ("Force Choke")).
  - F2. Choke needs a live view of the victim for most users and extreme focus; any break in concentration frees the target ([Telekinesis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends) ("Force Choke")).
  - F5. Taught like telekinesis; Sith teach it formally in Offense ([Force power](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_power/Legends) ("Offense")).
  - F6. [conflict] **Is it a dark act?**
    - Version 1: the grip techniques are "often regarded as dark side techniques" ([Telekinesis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends) ("Force Grip")).
    - Version 2: a Jedi could grip to immobilize harmlessly without touching the dark side ([Telekinesis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends) ("Force Grip")).
    - Version 3: dark-siders "reveled" in choke, especially to humiliate, but it "was used by the light side as well": Luke used it only to disable (Barriss and the Exile are also listed) ([Telekinesis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends) ("Force Choke")).
    - Version 4: the Sith file Choke as a standard Offense skill ([Force power](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_power/Legends) ("Offense")).
    - Best fit: intent and use decide. Restraining is neutral; choking to kill or torture is a dark act. Developer to decide.
  - F7. Counters: blinding the user or persuading them so they lose sight of the target; leaping away; Boba Fett escaped with his jetpack; pushing the user or shooting them ([Telekinesis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends) ("Force Grip")).
- **Related:** F8. Telekinesis is the prerequisite. Derivatives: Force Wound, Force Kill, Force Crush, Force Orb ([Telekinesis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends) ("Derivatives of Force Grip")). Cross-reference: SW_DarkSide_Fall_Corruption_Redemption_Part2.md, Section 2 (Anakin, Dooku) for choke as a marker of falling.
- **Uncertainty:** [conflict] on alignment, as above.

### Force Wound, Force Kill, Force Crush, Force Rend and Injure/Kill
- **Type:** F1. Grip derivatives (Wound, Kill, Crush) and a Pull derivative (Rend). Jedi: Alter (Injure/Kill is classed Alter in The Jedi Path). Side: Rend is dark; Injure/Kill is dark (infobox); Crush is [conflict] ([Telekinesis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends) ("Force Rend"; "Force Kill"; "Force Crush"); [Injure/Kill](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Injure/Kill) (intro)).
- **Era:** F4. All eras. Sabla-Mandibu's note in The Jedi Path (c. 990 BBY on) describes Sith using Injure/Kill at range ([Injure/Kill](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Injure/Kill) (intro)).
- **Key facts:**
  - F2. **Force Wound** crushes organs such as the heart or lungs; victims may need surgery (KotOR Campaign Guide). **Force Kill** kills outright. **Force Crush** crushes objects or bodies. **Force Rend** tears at a target (Clone Wars Campaign Guide). Heart stun is named as similar to Force Kill ([Telekinesis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends) ("Force Wound"; "Force Kill"; "Force Crush"; "Force Rend")).
  - F3. **Kill:** Tyranus killed Auben (Jedi Quest: The Final Showdown); Bane killed his father (Path of Destruction); Vader, who could stop a heart or freeze lungs (Shadows of the Empire novel) and used it as a Jedi (The Clone Wars: Republic Hero) ([Telekinesis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends) ("Force Kill")).
  - F3. **Crush:** Satele Shan (Fatal Alliance); Kenobi (TCW "Escape from Kadavo"); Windu crushed Grievous's lungs (CW chapter 25); Vader crushed vehicles (Empire at War); Anakin on Nelvaan; Nejaa Halcyon; Darth Traya; Bane crushed Qordis; Galen Marek against an AT-ST; Luke Skywalker (Fate of the Jedi: Apocalypse) ([Telekinesis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends) ("Force Crush")).
  - F6. [conflict] Rend and Injure/Kill are dark side; but Jedi users of Crush include Windu, Satele and Luke, and Anakin's Force Kill as a Jedi is recorded. Best fit: killing by telekinesis is dark when done in anger; Jedi uses are presented as combat necessity. Developer to decide.
  - F7. As for grip.
  - F9. (game stat: Injure/Kill requires the user to touch the target, *The Star Wars Rules Companion*, WEG) against Sabla-Mandibu's in-universe note that Sith used it at range ([Injure/Kill](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Injure/Kill) (intro)). [conflict]
  - **Heart stun:** an arcane Sith skill that halts the heart in a kind of stasis so a target can be captured without a fight; Dooku was rumoured to have used it to keep Grievous alive after the *Martyr* wreck; Maul used a fatal version on the Moorjhoni elder Trisjon ([Heart stun](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Heart_stun) (intro)).
- **Related:** F8. Grip (prerequisite); Part 2b drain powers.
- **Uncertainty:** [conflict] touch vs range for Injure/Kill; [conflict] on Crush's alignment.

### Force Whirlwind, Force Wind and the Spell of Storm
- **Type:** F1. Jedi: Alter (telekinesis; whirlwinds also fall under Alter Environment). Side: Whirlwind neutral (as telekinesis). Force Wind: dark. Its own page calls it "a dark side Force power" (*The Jedi Academy Sourcebook*), its infobox and category say dark side, and the Telekinesis/Legends section calls it "an ancient Sith technique" (*Tales of the Jedi Companion*) ([Force Wind](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_Wind) (intro; infobox; categories); [Telekinesis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends) ("Force Wind")).
- **Era:** F4. Force Wind is called an ancient Sith technique (Tales of the Jedi Companion); Whirlwind is used in all eras ([Telekinesis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends) ("Force Whirlwind"; "Force Wind")).
- **Key facts:**
  - F2/F3. Whirlwind traps a target in a spinning vortex: Jacen Solo against Tsavong Lah, Streen, Teneniel Djo, Plo Koon and Malak used it; the Dathomir witches call it the Spell of Storm (KotOR Campaign Guide) ([Telekinesis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends) ("Force Whirlwind")).
  - Force Wind: makes destructive tornadoes from air currents ([Force Wind](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_Wind) (intro)). Streen brought down four TIE fighters with it in 12 ABY ([Telekinesis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends) ("Force Wind")). Vader summoned a wind on Cloud City to pull Luke towards a reactor shaft (3 ABY, *The Empire Strikes Back* radio drama) ([Force Wind](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_Wind) (intro)).
  - F6. [conflict] Force Wind is dark by its page, but Streen, a Jedi student under Luke, used it twice: once to lift the comatose Luke while tricked by Exar Kun's spirit, and once to defend the Jedi academy against TIE fighters (12 ABY) ([Telekinesis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends) ("Force Wind")). Best fit: dark-side technique; developer to decide.
  - F6. Neutral. F7. Not stated.
- **Related:** F8. Telekinesis; Alter Environment; Force storm (weather).
- **Uncertainty:** none.

### Force Barrier, Saber Barrier, Saber Throw, telekinetic lightsaber combat and projected fighting
- **Type:** F1. Jedi: Alter (telekinesis). Side: neutral.
- **Era:** F4. All eras.
- **Key facts:**
  - F2/F3. **Force Barrier:** a wall of Force energy around self, ally or foe that can block matter, energy and even sonic attacks, but weakens with battering and fatigue; Kenobi used it against a sonic grenade launcher, and Farfalla briefly encased Bane in one (Rule of Two). Similar: tutaminis (Part 1a §C1), protection bubble, Force deflection (Part 3a §T11; not covered here) ([Telekinesis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends) ("Force Barrier")).
  - **Saber Throw / telekinetic lightsaber combat:** throwing and guiding a lightsaber by telekinesis; Bane killed three boys with a telekinetically guided blade ([Telekinesis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends) ("Saber Throw"; "Telekinetic lightsaber combat")).
  - **Saber Barrier:** lightsabers whirling in a horizontal circle around the user as a wall of blades, used by dual-saber fighters of the New Jedi Order; Mace Windu, Alora and several New Reborn used it, and Traya's three-saber attack on Meetra Surik was a variant ([Telekinesis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends) ("Saber Barrier")).
  - **Projected fighting:** harming a target without contact, as an extension of the user's hand-to-hand blows, within line of sight; it can be pulled to stun (Jedi Academy Sourcebook); similar to Force punch, Force kick and Shadowstrike ([Telekinesis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends) ("Projected fighting")).
  - F6. Neutral.
- **Related:** F8. Telekinesis; Force Orb, a bubble of air made of Force energy that normally works only underwater; Kit Fisto used it at the Battle of Mon Calamari ([Telekinesis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis/Legends) ("Force Orb")).
- **Uncertainty:** none.

## Section B: Mind influence (affect mind)

### Mind trick (affect mind) and Force persuasion
- **Type:** F1. Aliases: Jedi mind trick, affect mind, Force persuasion. Jedi: Alter (affect mind is a fundamental Alter technique); the infobox also lists Control, Sense and the Sith Mind school. Side: no alignment in the infobox; used by Jedi as standard ([Mind trick](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_trick/Legends) (infobox; "Description"); [Force power](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_power/Legends) ("Alter")).
- **Era:** F4. All eras.
- **Key facts:**
  - F2. Alters a target's perceptions or plants a suggestion; it "usually worked on the weak-minded" (Jedi vs. Sith). It can also plant a false image or sound to distract ([Mind trick](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_trick/Legends) ("Description")).
  - F3. Range: persuasion could reach very far, even from one ship to another (Ki-Adi-Mundi, *Star Wars* (1998) 5). Revan persuaded the Hutt Motta, while Luke failed on Jabba (4 ABY) ([Mind trick](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_trick/Legends) ("Force persuasion")).
  - F3. Users: Yarael Poof (died 27 BBY; see Force illusion below); Galen Marek's clone made Imperial troops kill themselves (TFU II); Palpatine bound Strike Force Alpha to his will (Galactic Battlegrounds); Onimi secretly controlled Shimrra ([Mind trick](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_trick/Legends) ("Users")).
  - F5. Taught to Jedi Knights within Alter; it needs full concentration (Shadows of the Empire) ([Force power](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_power/Legends) ("Alter"); [Mind trick](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_trick/Legends) ("Force persuasion")).
  - F6. A Jedi moral precept: never for personal gain (Jedi vs. Sith). Yarael Poof warned of the harm it can do; Kyp Durron used it casually (Dark Journey). Not dark in itself; intent decides ([Mind trick](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_trick/Legends) ("Force persuasion")).
  - F7. Counters: strong will; resistant species named are Hutts, Toydarians, Falleen, Geonosians and Dashade (Saga Edition), plus Yinchorri and Kloodavians; some Jedi Civil War soldiers were trained to resist; Lobot's cybernetic brain made him immune ([Mind trick](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_trick/Legends) ("Description"; "Force persuasion")). Ysalamiri bubbles block all Force use inside them ([Ysalamiri](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ysalamiri/Legends) (intro)).
- **Related:** F8. Force confusion, Force fear, mind control, Force illusion (below); Part 1b §S6 (telepathy) for mind reading; mind probe / Drain Knowledge is in Part 3a §T11, not here.
- **Uncertainty:** none on the base power; Kyp's casual use vs the Jedi precept is a matter of conduct, not classification.

### Force fear, Force horror and Force insanity
- **Type:** F1. Jedi: Alter (mind). Sith school: Mind (Horror) ([Force power](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_power/Legends) ("Mind")). Side: dark, with an intent clause [conflict].
- **Era:** F4. Used c. 4000 BBY by Sith and dark-leaning Jedi ([Mind trick](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_trick/Legends) ("Force fear")).
- **Key facts:**
  - F2. Strikes at the deepest parts of the mind to cause uncontrollable, shaking fear. **Horror** puts several enemies into catatonic panic and is harder to resist; **insanity** affects more targets at greater range ([Mind trick](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_trick/Legends) ("Force fear")).
  - F6. [conflict] Version 1: dark because fear is a dark emotion. Version 2: "it was the intent behind its use that counted", and Jedi sometimes used it to defuse danger ([Mind trick](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_trick/Legends) ("Force fear")). Best fit: dark when used to torment, tolerable to prevent violence. Developer to decide.
  - F7. Will; as mind trick.
- **Related:** F8. Mind trick; Summon fear (Part 2b, the sorcerous version); Memory Walk.
- **Uncertainty:** [conflict] as above.

### Mind control (dominate mind), Force corrupt and Mind Twist
- **Type:** F1. Aliases: dominate mind, control mind; Force corruption. Side: dark ("the dark-side equivalent of the Jedi mind trick") ([Mind trick](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_trick/Legends) ("Mind control"; "Force corrupt")).
- **Era:** F4. All eras. Mind Twist belongs to the Dawn of the Jedi (Je'daii) era.
- **Key facts:**
  - F2. Mind control takes complete control of the victim's mind; greater mastery controls more people (Book of Sith). Force corrupt temporarily turns enemy soldiers to fight for the user while slowly draining their life (Empire at War). Mind Twist (Daegen Lok) twists the victim's reality: Xesh believed he was suffocating, and Bel Zana's body showed burn marks from imagined fire ([Mind trick](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_trick/Legends) ("Mind control"; "Force corrupt"; "Mind Twist")).
  - F3. Imperial Force users, possibly including Mara Jade and Palpatine, used Force corrupt in the Galactic Civil War ([Mind trick](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_trick/Legends) ("Force corrupt")). For the largest-scale domination (Vitiate's possession of Ziost), see Possession in Part 2b.
  - F6. Dark by nature: it overrides another's will.
  - F7. Whether species immune to mind tricks (Hutts, Toydarians) resist it is not confirmed ([Mind trick](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_trick/Legends) ("Mind control")).
- **Related:** F8. Mind trick; Sith sorcery's Qâzoi Kyantuska (Part 2b); Possession (Part 2b).
- **Uncertainty:** [unclear] effect on mind-trick-immune species. Note: Wookieepedia's Mind Shard page gives "mind twist" as an alias of Mind Shard as well ([Mind Shard](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_Shard) (intro)). [conflict] Two different powers share the name.

### Force illusion, masquerade and Sith mindforms
- **Type:** F1. Mind-trick subset. Side: Jedi illusion neutral; Sith mindforms are a dark sorcerous technique ([Mind trick](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_trick/Legends) ("Force illusion")).
- **Era:** F4. All eras: Naga Sadow (5000 BBY), Aleema Keto (Great Sith War era), Fallanassi, Nightsisters, Wyyrlok (c. 137 ABY).
- **Key facts:**
  - F2. Projects an image into the minds of beings in range, from a pillar of fire to a fleet of warships; it affects every sense, not just sight. It cannot make something vanish (that needs Force immersion) ([Mind trick](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_trick/Legends) ("Force illusion")).
  - F3. Scale: Naga Sadow, from his meditation sphere, projected illusions of war beasts onto the battlefields of Koros Major, Coruscant and other worlds in the Great Hyperspace War (5000 BBY). Yarael Poof projected opponents' own fears onto "wholesale armies" to end battles. Wyyrlok and the undead Andeddu fought with illusions able to cause real injury (c. 137 ABY) ([Mind trick](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_trick/Legends) ("Force illusion")).
  - Masquerade masks the user's appearance with a projected image. Fallanassi White Current illusions can be as real or insubstantial as the user wants ([Mind trick](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_trick/Legends) ("Force illusion")).
  - F5. Difficult to learn; Sadow gave Gav Daragon rudimentary instruction ([Mind trick](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_trick/Legends) ("Force illusion")).
  - F6. Neutral; Sith mindforms tend toward torment.
  - F7. Little or no effect on Force users, Hutts or the Yuuzhan Vong; concentration on the Force reveals mindforms as false ([Mind trick](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_trick/Legends) ("Force illusion")).
- **Related:** F8. Mind trick; Force projection / Dopplegänger (Part 3a §T11); Sith sorcery illusions (Part 2b).
- **Uncertainty:** [unclear] The Force projection subsection of Mind trick/Legends cites *The Last Jedi* (a Canon film) for one detail; that detail is not used here.

## Section C: Environment, elements and light-side Alter

### Alter Environment
- **Type:** F1. Jedi: Alter (one of the three fundamental Alter techniques) ([Alter Environment](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Alter_Environment) (infobox); [Force power](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_power/Legends) ("Alter")). Side: none in the infobox.
- **Era:** F4. All eras.
- **Key facts:**
  - F2. Manipulates nature: whirlwinds and fog, sub-zero cold or burning heat in seconds, electrical discharges over broad ground, vacuum pockets, air pressure that bursts eardrums, small earthquakes and fissures, flash floods; it can also steer existing storms, rain, hail, waves and whirlpools ([Alter Environment](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Alter_Environment) (intro)).
  - F2. It harnesses the target's own innate Force energy, so masters can raise killer waves and lightning storms "with minimum effort"; knowledge of physics improves control; effects must be watched or they spread disastrously ([Alter Environment](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Alter_Environment) (intro)).
  - F3. Streen and the Sith Emperor were noted adepts; Vitiate corrupted Dromund Kaas's environment; Darth Krayt controlled the weather of an entire planet while in stasis (Riptide) ([Alter Environment](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Alter_Environment) (intro)).
  - F6. Neutral; risk is loss of control. See also SW_DarkSide_Fall_Corruption_Redemption_Part1.md, Section 7, for dark-side environmental corruption.
- **Related:** F8. Force Whirlwind; Force storm (weather); pyrokinesis; cryokinesis.
- **Uncertainty:** none.

### Force storm (weather) / Summon Storm
- **Type:** F1. Alias: Summon Storm. A telekinetic weather power, "presumably" Whirlwind plus lightning. Side: "not inherently a dark side ability" but most often used by darksiders ([Force storm (weather)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_storm_%28weather%29) (intro)).
- **Era:** F4. Nightsisters and Sith; Plagueis (32 BBY); Luke (29 ABY era, Yuuzhan'tar).
- **Key facts:**
  - F2. Storms several kilometres across lasting hours, with rain, wind and lightning that can ground aircraft; outdoors only, limited by the local climate (a sandstorm in a desert, not rain) ([Force storm (weather)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_storm_%28weather%29) (intro)).
  - F3. Plagueis raised one as a last defence when Sidious killed him; Sidious withstood it. Luke conjured one about himself at the reconquest of Yuuzhan'tar ([Force storm (weather)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_storm_%28weather%29) (intro)).
  - F6. [conflict] Neutral in itself vs mostly a darksider tool "due to its indiscriminately destructive nature".
- **Related:** F8. Force Whirlwind; Alter Environment. Not to be confused with Force storm (lightning) or Force storm (wormhole) in Part 2b.
- **Uncertainty:** [inferred] Wookieepedia itself says the combination of Whirlwind and lightning is "presumable".

## Game notes (suggestions only; not lore)
- **Grip vs choke:** treat restraint as neutral and lethal choking or torture as a dark-side act, following the Luke "to disable" precedent.
- **Telekinesis by mass:** a fatigue track keyed to mass, with pooled efforts (Dorsk 81) able to exceed a single user's cap at lethal risk.
- **Tiered powers:** Stun → Stasis → Stasis Field and Slow → Affliction → Plague map neatly onto three ranks; Ionize can mirror Stun for droids.
- **Mind trick resistance:** give Hutts, Toydarians, Falleen, Geonosians, Dashade, Yinchorri and Kloodavians innate resistance; cybernetic brains (Lobot) immunity.
- **Environment powers:** Alter Environment is cheap because it uses the target's own energy, but risks runaway effects.
