## Summary
Kolto is a natural healing liquid found only in Manaan's oceans and sold by the neutral Selkath. In the KOTOR and SWTOR sources it's the standard medical fluid: it fills kolto tanks, goes into medpacs, and by the Cold War is delivered by injector darts, medical probes and aerosol missiles. Bacta is the Vratix of Thyferra's invention. The galaxy has known about it since about 4100 BBY, but it can only be made in bulk on Thyferra. Some time between the Jedi Civil War and the Galactic Civil War, kolto production fell for unknown reasons, its price rose, and bacta replaced it.

## Entries

### Kolto
- **Type:** substance (medical)
- **Era:** standard in the Old Republic era; nearly forgotten by the Galactic Civil War
- **Key facts:**
  - A liquid known for healing, found in Manaan's waters, concentrated near the Hrakert Rift and other undersea chasms [Kolto/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kolto/Legends).
  - It occurs naturally only in Manaan's depths, making it a vital export for the planet and the Selkath who control it [Kolto/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kolto/Legends).
  - **Jedi Civil War:** both sides had limited supplies, and every attempt to synthesise kolto failed. The Selkath stayed neutral, and both governments ran "equal" operations on Manaan. Selkath leaders secretly let the Republic build a harvesting station in the Hrakert Rift [Kolto/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kolto/Legends).
  - **The Progenitor:** a giant firaxan shark in the Rift that may have guarded the kolto. Selkath legend said it produced kolto. The scientists Kono Nolan and Sami thought it grew so large from feeding on it [Kolto/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kolto/Legends).
  - **Uses:** the main method was full immersion in kolto tanks, with the concentration adjusted per patient. Tanks were found on Republic starships, in government buildings and in medical facilities at the height of kolto use. Medpacs carried small amounts and were mainly first aid [Kolto/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kolto/Legends).
  - **Cold War era:** use spread among elite ranks. Imperial Agent Operatives used wrist-mounted dart launchers or hovering medical probes to inject kolto. Bounty hunters modified missiles to release kolto mist over small areas. Both sides also used substances such as spice as painkillers [Kolto/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kolto/Legends) [unclear]: this paragraph is uncited on the page and reflects SWTOR gameplay.
  - A military saying: "Kolto for your cuts" [Kolto/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kolto/Legends).
  - **Decline (no year given):** between the Jedi Civil War and the Galactic Civil War, kolto production dropped sharply and its cost rose. The market collapsed in favour of cheaper bacta. The cause is unknown and disputed. Manaan's economy collapsed, and kolto was almost forgotten [Kolto/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kolto/Legends).
  - **Later:** by the Galactic Civil War, kolto seemed less effective than bacta and was used as a cheaper option. Some historians argued it may have been more effective in the past. It was used aboard the Imperial prison barge *Purge* by 1 ABY [Kolto/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kolto/Legends).
- **Related:** Manaan, the Selkath (SW_Species_OldRepublic, Part 2).
- **Uncertainty:** side effects and prices aren't recorded.

### Bacta
- **Type:** substance (medical)
- **Era:** known galaxy-wide from c. 4100 BBY; dominant after kolto's decline
- **Key facts:**
  - An organic substance of gelatinous, translucent red alazhi and kavam bacterial particles suspended in a colourless, thick fluid called ambori. It came to be seen as the best medicine anywhere, replacing kolto [Bacta/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bacta/Legends).
  - The Vratix of Thyferra invented it in antiquity, and the wider galaxy learned of its properties around 4100 BBY. The Vratix were willing to share the technology, but it could only be made in bulk on Thyferra, where the resources were plentiful. Fights over control of bacta often caused unrest on Thyferra [Bacta/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bacta/Legends).
  - Bacta later outsold kolto and eventually replaced it. Kolto tanks were the forerunners of bacta tanks [Bacta/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bacta/Legends).
  - **Alazhi:** a bacteria grown on Thyferra, also used by the Vratix as a salve for cuts and burns. Over half of Thyferra was given to alazhi paddies [Alazhi/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Alazhi/Legends).
  - **Kavam:** a liquid chemical that can be synthesised and is also produced naturally by Vratix glands [Kavam/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kavam/Legends).
  - **How it works:** the bacterial particles seek out wounds and drive rapid tissue regrowth without scarring. It's effective against almost every injury and illness across a huge range of species [older Wookieepedia text, via The Full Wiki](http://misc.thefullwiki.org/Bacta).
  - **The Vratix and the humans:** human settlers on Thyferra exploited the Vratix's addiction to making bacta and sold it to the public [Vratix, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/Vratix).
- **Related:** the later Bacta Cartel (Xucphra and Zaltin) and the Bacta War (7–8 ABY, SW_Timeline_4_25ABY).
- **Uncertainty:** how common bacta is before kolto's decline [unclear]. It's known from c. 4100 BBY, but kolto is the standard in the KOTOR and SWTOR sources.
