# SWRPG Galaxy Timeline, 4–25 ABY (Part 4 of 5): From the Crystal Star to the Pellaeon–Gavrisom Treaty (14–19 ABY)

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 24, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends), as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
Part 4 covers *The Crystal Star* and *Jedi Academy* (14 ABY), the Black Fleet Crisis (16–17 ABY), the Almanian Uprising and the Imperial Skirmishes (17 ABY), the Corellian Crisis (18 ABY) and the Caamas Document Crisis with the Hand of Thrawn, ending in the Pellaeon–Gavrisom Treaty and Luke and Mara's wedding (19 ABY). See Part 1 for how to read the tables; back sections are in Part 5.

#### Supporting article: Mara Jade (to 19 ABY)
- Born 17 BBY; an Emperor's Hand who, after Palpatine's death, received his last command to kill Luke, went rogue and joined Talon Karrde as his second-in-command; forced to work with Luke during the Thrawn campaign; briefly trained at the Praxeum; groomed by Karrde to lead the Smugglers' Alliance, with a brief relationship with Lando that she later called a charade; her Masters were Palpatine, Kyle Katarn and Luke, and Kyle was her apprentice [Mara Jade Skywalker](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mara_Jade_Skywalker).
- Turned Kyle Katarn away from the dark side on Dromund Kaas in 10 ABY [10 ABY/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/10_ABY/Legends).
