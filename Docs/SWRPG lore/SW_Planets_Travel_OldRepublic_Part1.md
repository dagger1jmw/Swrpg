## Summary
In 3661 BBY the Great Galactic War (3681–3653 BBY) is in its twenty-first year. The Sith Empire holds the Sith Worlds (Korriban, Dromund Kaas, Ziost and their routes), the Tingel Arm puppet worlds and the Seswenna stretch of the Rimma Trade Route. That year Mandalorian cruisers under Mandalore the Lesser cut the southern Hydian Way between Devaron and the Minos Cluster, causing famine and riots on Coruscant. The Rim Campaign (Ord Radama, Serenno, Ashas Ree, Ziost, Korriban) follows in 3660 BBY [Blockade of the Hydian Way §A crippled Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way); [3661 BBY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/3661_BBY); [3660 BBY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/3660_BBY); [Great Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Galactic_War). Hyperspace travel runs on navicomputers and charted lanes. Wookieepedia gives hyperdrive class ratings and campaign lengths for the era, but almost no point-to-point trip times. A travel system therefore has to rest on class multipliers and labelled game stats (see Part C, "Travel-time model").

## How to read the planet entries
- **Fields 1–6** follow the brief: 1 location (region, grid, sector, system, routes), 2 control in 3661 plus dated changes 3681–3600 BBY, 3 climate/terrain/atmosphere/gravity/population/species/government, 4 attested places, 5 role at the 3661 start, 6 era gating. Each entry is marked **full** (enough for a playable location) or **thin** (named, with little era detail).
- **Field 1 and 3 data** come from the planet page's infobox (the "§infobox" link). The grid source is tagged per entry: "The Essential Atlas, 2009" or "Essential Atlas Online Companion, 2009" (both are Legends reference works). "Canon-only" means the infobox points to a Canon page for the grid, so no Legends grid exists.
- **Field 3 population figures** come from the infobox and may belong to another era (often the Galactic Civil War). Use them only as background.
- **Field 4** lists only infobox places whose references are SWTOR-era works. Those works are set c. 3643–3622 BBY, so each is tagged [unconfirmed in window for 3661]. Places that exist only because of a later event (e.g. the Tython Jedi Temple rebuilt after 3653 BBY, Mek-Sha, Vaiken Spacedock, the Taris resettlement) say so in the entry.
- **Role codes (field 5):** IMP-CORE = Sith Empire heartland; IMP-HELD = Imperial-occupied or allied; IMP-SECRET = hidden Imperial site; REP-CORE = Republic heartland; REP = Republic-aligned; FRONT LINE / CONTESTED = fighting in or near 3661; BLOCKADE ZONE = on the cut Hydian Way; HUTT = Hutt Space, neutral; NEUTRAL = neutral or independent; WILD = Wild Space or Unknown Regions; GATED = unknown, lost or unreachable in 3661; NOT YET = place or settlement not yet in existence; DEAD WORLD = depopulated; OFFSTAGE = no 3661 role on record. [inferred] marks roles concluded from the dated record rather than stated outright.

## The galaxy in 3661 BBY (default start)
- **The war.** Year 21 of the Great Galactic War. The Empire returned in 3681 BBY, took the Tingel Arm through puppet governments (Belkadan, Sernpidal, Ruuria) and the Aparo sector, then took Korriban [Tingel Arm campaign §Prelude](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tingel_Arm_campaign); [Great Galactic War §Fall of Korriban](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Galactic_War). The Treaty of Coruscant ends open war in 3653 BBY [Treaty of Coruscant §Terms](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Treaty_of_Coruscant).
- **Imperial space.** The Sith Worlds of the Esstran sector (Korriban R-5, Dromund Kaas R-5, Ziost R-4), joined by the Kamat Krote, Nache Bhelfia, Kamat Aegit, Zorfe Trete and Daragon Trail [Korriban/Legends §infobox](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Korriban/Legends); [Dromund Kaas/Legends §infobox](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dromund_Kaas/Legends); [Ziost/Legends §infobox](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ziost/Legends); [Nache Bhelfia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nache_Bhelfia); [Kamat Aegit](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kamat_Aegit). The Empire has held the Rimma Trade Route in the Seswenna sector since 3671 BBY [Rimma Trade Route/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rimma_Trade_Route/Legends); [3671 BBY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/3671_BBY). It also holds Sluis Van (sacked in 3681 BBY) [3681 BBY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/3681_BBY). The Minos Cluster is a long-running front [Blockade of the Rimma Trade Route §Prelude](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Rimma_Trade_Route); [Blockade of the Hydian Way §A crippled Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way).
- **The Republic.** Holds the Core Worlds and the Colonies. Won at Bothawui (3671 BBY) and Alderaan (3667 BBY), and near Gell Mattar (3666 BBY), but lost a major fleet over Hoth (3665 BBY) [3671 BBY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/3671_BBY); [3667 BBY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/3667_BBY); [Campaign near Gell Mattar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Campaign_near_Gell_Mattar); [Battle of Hoth (Great Galactic War)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Hoth_(Great_Galactic_War)). It learned the name of the Imperial capital, Dromund Kaas, when it captured the Dread Masters in 3668 BBY [Great Galactic War §Capture of the Dread Masters](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Galactic_War). Its Navy was still weak in 3661 [Blockade of the Hydian Way §A crippled Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way).
- **3661 BBY events.** Mandalorian cruisers seize the southern Hydian Way between Devaron and the Minos Cluster. A Jedi fighter strike fails near Devaron. Famine and riots hit Coruscant's lower levels [Blockade of the Hydian Way §A crippled Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way); [Coruscant riots (3661 BBY)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_riots_(3661_BBY)). The SIS secretly imprisons Prince Shange and Lord Raxxus on Belsavis [Republic Strategic Information Service §Early operations](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Strategic_Information_Service). About this time Theron Shan is taken to Monastery [3661 BBY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/3661_BBY).
- **Next (3660 BBY).** Hylo Visz's Hutt-backed smugglers, joined by the Republic Navy, break the blockade. The Rim Campaign strikes Ord Radama (taken, then retaken), Serenno, Ashas Ree and Ziost (a siege of 30+ days), with feints at Korriban. Artus Lok kills Mandalore the Lesser on Geonosis [Blockade of the Hydian Way §Smugglers take the lead](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way); [3660 BBY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/3660_BBY); [Battle of Ord Radama](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Ord_Radama); [Battle of Ziost](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Ziost).
- **Neutrals and allies.** Hutt Space is neutral under a Hutt–Imperial understanding; Hutt money funded Visz [Blockade of the Hydian Way §Smugglers take the lead](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way); [Nal Hutta/Legends §Old Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nal_Hutta/Legends). The Chiss Ascendancy is allied to the Empire [Csilla/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Csilla/Legends); [Great Galactic War §The Sith Empire prepares for war](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Galactic_War). The Mandalorian clans serve the Empire under Mandalore the Lesser [Mandalore the Lesser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalore_the_Lesser).
- **Not on the map in 3661.** Tython: lanes erased; the route is re-found after 3653 BBY [Tython/Legends §Rediscovery](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tython/Legends). Voss and Denova: found only after the 3653 BBY treaty [Voss §Cold War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Voss); [Denova §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Denova). Belsavis: kept secret by the Republic [Belsavis/Legends §Pre- and Old Republic eras](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Belsavis/Legends). Zakuul: no contact before 3636 BBY; Odessen and Iokath: unknown until c. 3631 BBY [3636 BBY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/3636_BBY); [Odessen §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Odessen); [Iokath §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Iokath). Nathema: routes erased [Nathema §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nathema). Rishi: hidden in the Rishi Maze [Rishi/Legends §Old Republic era](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rishi/Legends). Mek-Sha and Port Nowhere: not yet built [Mek-Sha §Origins](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mek-Sha); [Port Nowhere §Intro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Port_Nowhere).
