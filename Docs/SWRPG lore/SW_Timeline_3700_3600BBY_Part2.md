# SWRPG Galaxy Timeline, c. 3,700–3,600 BBY (Part 2 of 3): Cold War to the Legacy of the Sith

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 21, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
Part 2 continues the Entries of Part 1 from the Treaty of Coruscant (3,653 BBY) to the last dated Legends year found, c. 3,622 BBY. It covers the Cold War (3,653–3,642 BBY), the Galactic War (the Treaty collapsed midway through 3,641 BBY; to 3,636 BBY), the Eternal Empire's conquest and the war against Zakuul (3,636–3,630 BBY), the years after it (3,630–3,625 BBY: Iokath, Zildrog and the Third Galactic War from 3,628 BBY) and the Legacy of the Sith years with the Hidden Chain crisis (3,624–c. 3,622 BBY). Key points: the Jedi moved to Tython in 3,650 BBY; Balmorra was freed and Taris taken by the Empire in 3,642 BBY; Vitiate died for the last time on Zakuul in 3,630 BBY; and Malgus, not killed at Ilum, returned at Ossus in 3,628 BBY.
