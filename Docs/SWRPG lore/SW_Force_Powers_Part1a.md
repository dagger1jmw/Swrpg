Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched 21 Nov 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) via the MediaWiki API, cited per entry. Draft for review.

**How to read the categories.**
- The Jedi taught **Control** first (Initiates, focused on the body), then **Sense** (Padawans, awareness of the Living Force), then **Alter** (Knights, affecting the world). [Force power §The Jedi Order][fp]
- The core techniques of each:
  - **Control:** *tutaminis*, *curato salva*, *altus sopor*. [Control][ctl]
  - **Sense:** *prima vitae*, *tactus otium*, *tai vordrax* (postcognition) and projected telepathy. [Sense][sen]
- The Sith of Bane's line instead used three schools, **Offense, Body and Mind**. [Force power §The Sith][fp]
- **Behind the scenes:** the Control/Sense/Alter split began as game mechanics in West End Games' *Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game* (1987). *Jedi vs. Sith* (2007) made the names in-universe. [Force power §Behind the scenes][fp]
- **Consequence:** many minor powers exist only in RPG sourcebooks. Their category comes from the game and is marked "(from game rules)".

**Game stats** are printed as "(game stat: …)" and only where they show tiers or costs.

## Summary

- **The three categories.** Legends teaches Control (inner body powers), then Sense (perception), then Alter. Most Control and Sense powers are treated as neither light nor dark; what matters is how they are used.
- **Classification conflicts:**
  - *Tutaminis*, hibernation trance and Force resistance are filed as light-side powers but are documented in Sith or Dark Jedi hands.
  - Battle meditation is *not* filed by side. Odan-Urr says dark users can command it, but rarely. Luke distrusted it because of C'baoth, yet knew it was "not automatically a dark side power".
  - Projective telepathy is called Sense + Alter in *The Jedi Path* but Control + Sense in the RPG.
  - Battle meditation and Force harmony are filed under all three categories.
  - Art of Movement is filed as Control by Wookieepedia but as a Sith Offense power by the *Book of Sith*.
  - Oneness is filed as Control even though it is a state of being, not a technique.
- **Biggest Sense and mind-influence anchors:**
  - Galaxy-wide sensing of mass deaths (Order 66; Alderaan).
  - Bastila Shan's battle meditation over two whole fleets (3956 BBY).
  - Joruus C'baoth coordinating fleets "from across star systems" (9 ABY).
  - Ikrit's 356-year hibernation (378 BBY–22 ABY).
  - Sidious hiding his dark side from the Jedi for over three decades.
- **Main counters:** willpower, thought shields and Force resistance; ysalamiri bubbles; taozin; and the Yuuzhan Vong, who cannot be sensed at all.

## Entries

Each entry covers: (1) names and classification, (2) effect and limits, (3) users and feats, (4) era, (5) how it is learned, (6) costs, dangers and whether using it is a dark act, (7) counters, (8) related powers, (9) game stats, then uncertainty.

**Counters common to all Sense and mind powers** (stated once here, then referenced as "standard counters"):
- **Ysalamiri** push back the Force and create a bubble where it cannot be used. When many are together the bubble can be kilometres wide. They suppress the Force rather than negate it. [Ysalamiri/Legends][ysa]
- **Taozin** are invisible in the Force, and their nodules give the wearer the same masking. Inquisitors used taozin amulets. [Taozin/Legends][tao]
- **The Yuuzhan Vong** cannot be sensed through the Force, and most direct powers do not affect them. The New Jedi Order's workaround was **Vongsense** (see Part 3a §T5). [Yuuzhan Vong][vong]; [Vongsense][vs]
- **Toydarians** have brains resistant to Force influence, "similar to the Hutts". [Toydarian/Legends][toy] (*Scum and Villainy*). The Hutt page itself carries no explicit statement; see Not found.
- **Willpower, thought shields and Force resistance** (§C9, §C10).
- **Force stealth** (§C11).

### Control (C1–C13)

#### C1. *Tutaminis* (energy absorption)
1. **Names and classification:** *tutaminis*; also called energy absorption, Force absorb, or a "non-harmful Force drain". It is one of the three main Control abilities in *The Jedi Path*. Wookieepedia also files it as a light-side power, yet Sith use it: **[conflict]**, light vs neither. [Tutaminis][tut]; [Control][ctl]
2. **What it does:** absorbs or dissipates heat, electricity, light, sound, kinetic energy and blaster fire. The energy can be redirected into other powers, such as telekinesis or illusion, or used for healing. Catching a lightsaber blade by hand is called "extremely rare". [Tutaminis §Notable uses][tut]
3. **Users and feats:**
   - **Nejaa Halcyon** (Clone Wars, 22–19 BBY) used it to make up for weak telekinesis and caught lightsaber blades (*Jedi Trial*).
   - **Satele Shan** caught Malgus's blade at the Battle of Alderaan (Great Galactic War; "Hope" trailer).
   - **Corran Horn** absorbed volcanic heat to boost his telekinesis, then absorbed the heat of explosions to project a large illusion on Courkrus (*I, Jedi*).
   - **Anakin Solo** used it on Yavin 4 during the Yuuzhan Vong War (*Conquest*).
   - **Yoda** absorbed and deflected Dooku's lightning (22 BBY, Film II).
   - **Darth Plagueis** caught blaster bolts in one hand and released the energy as lightning from the other (*Darth Plagueis*; date [unclear]).
   - **Luke Skywalker** was overwhelmed by Palpatine's lightning at Endor (4 ABY), but by 10 ABY he could absorb an AT-AT's fire (*Dark Empire* 1).
   - **Revan** killed Darth Nyriss with energy absorbed from her own lightning (*The Old Republic: Revan*).
   - **Vader** could absorb bolts but could not produce lightning (*Dark Lord*).
   [Tutaminis §Notable uses][tut]
4. **Era:** "practiced by all Jedi during the Old Republic and essentially all Force-based cults to some extent". [Tutaminis][tut]
5. **How it is learned:** taught to Initiates as part of Control. [Control][ctl]
6. **Costs and dangers:** overload. Luke at Endor could not absorb enough. Not a dark act in itself; Jedi and Sith both use it. Its "Force drain" nickname is the non-harmful counterpart to the dark-side Force drain (Part 2b §E).
7. **Counters:** sheer power beyond what the user can absorb (Endor). Physical attacks bypass it.
8. **Related:** Energy resistance (§C9); Force deflection (Part 3a §T11); Force lightning (Part 2b §D).
9. **Game stats:** (game stat: "Force absorb" is a three-level defensive power in *Jedi Outcast* and *Jedi Academy*.) [Tutaminis §Behind the scenes][tut]
- **Uncertainty:** the "Hope" trailer gives no year for Satele's feat, so its date is [unclear].

#### C2. *Curato salva* (self-healing family) and Accelerate Healing
1. **Names and classification:** *curato salva* is the umbrella term for self-healing (Control). Accelerate Healing is the West End Games Control power with the same effect. Neither light nor dark. [Curato salva][cur]; [Accelerate Healing][acc]
2. **What it does:** keeps the body fit; builds endurance; extends lifespan by staving off decay; flushes poisons and disease out of the blood. Basics are mastered at the academy; real skill takes a lifetime. [Curato salva][cur]
3. **Users and feats:** **Cade Skywalker** resisted the drug *Ixetal cilona* given to him by Darth Maladi, who remarked that the technique resembled Sith powers (Legacy era, c. 137 ABY). [Curato salva §Users][cur]
4. **Era:** taught "throughout the history of the Order". The Council of First Knowledge placed it in the very first academy classes. [Curato salva][cur]
5. **How it is learned:** taught to Initiates.
6. **Costs and dangers:** none recorded. Not a dark act. Maladi's comment suggests the Sith had similar methods.
7. **Counters:** none recorded.
8. **Related:** Detoxify Poison (§C13); Force healing of others (Part 3a §T11); hibernation trance (§C8).
9. **Game stats:** (game stat: in WEG, Accelerate Healing works on oneself and can boost medpacs.) [Accelerate Healing][acc]
- **Uncertainty:** the date of Cade's feat comes from the Legacy era generally, so it is [inferred].

#### C3. *Altus sopor* (focus)
1. **Names and classification:** *altus sopor*, one of the basic Control techniques in *The Jedi Path*. Neither light nor dark. [Altus sopor][alts]
2. **What it does:** increases focus on the Force. Developed further, it lets the user "merge into the Force" and become invisible even to the most acute senses. [Altus sopor][alts]
3. **Users and feats:** no named users are given.
4. **Era:** the term is used in *The Jedi Path* (Old Republic / Imperial tradition).
5. **How it is learned:** taught to Initiates.
6. **Costs and dangers:** none recorded.
7. **Counters:** none recorded.
8. **Related:** Force stealth (§C11); Concentration (§C13); Serenity trance (§C8).
9. **Game stats:** none.
- **Uncertainty:** the source does not say whether this "invisibility" is physical or only in the Force: [unclear].

#### C4. Force speed
1. **Names and classification:** Force speed; also burst of speed, Force sprint, Force run, Force reflex, Knight Speed and Master Speed. Control; neither light nor dark. [Force speed/Legends][spd]
2. **What it does:** the user attunes the whole body to heightened reflexes and moves fast enough to seem to vanish. It can also slow the user's perception of time. It needs a long recovery and burns energy and metabolism (*Jedi vs. Sith*). [Force speed §Description][spd]
3. **Users and feats:**
   - **Relin Druur** covered about 100 m in a second (5000 BBY, *Crosscurrent*).
   - **Qui-Gon Jinn and Obi-Wan Kenobi** used it to escape droidekas (32 BBY, Film I).
   - **Luke Skywalker** felt time slow down while dodging Guri in Xizor's palace and took it as the Force guiding him (3 ABY, *Shadows of the Empire*).
   - **Darth Sidious** moved as a blur (*Darth Plagueis*).
   - **The Matukai** used it heavily (*Hero's Guide*).
   [Force speed §Applications][spd]
4. **Era:** all eras; used by Jedi, Sith and the Matukai.
5. **How it is learned:** Jedi training. Tionne's tome describes it as attunement of the whole body.
6. **Costs and dangers:** exhaustion and a long recovery period. Not a dark act.
7. **Counters:** standard counters. Precognition and battle precognition (§S8) can let a defender anticipate the move.
8. **Related:** Force jump (§C5); Art of Movement (§C6); Force Body (§C12).
9. **Game stats:**
   - (game stat: *Revised Core Rulebook* tiers: Force speed, then Knight Speed (×20), then Master Speed (×30).)
   - (game stat: KOTOR tiers: Burst of Speed, then Knight Speed, then Master Speed.)
   - (game stat: wall-running in *Jedi Academy*.)
   [Force speed §Behind the scenes][spd]
- **Uncertainty:** none flagged.

#### C5. Force jump
1. **Names and classification:** Force jump (Force leap). Filed under Control, but the page notes it can be seen as a hybrid with telekinesis (Alter): **[conflict]**, Control vs Control + Alter. Neither light nor dark. [Force jump/Legends][jmp]
2. **What it does:** jumps far beyond normal. Most Masters can jump several times their own height. It is described as the most common Force power. [Force jump/Legends][jmp]
3. **Users and feats:**
   - **Jaden Korr** jumped up to eight times a normal jump (*Jedi Academy*; game source).
   - **Trainees on Ossus** jumped a 3 m cross beam (*Dark Nest I*).
   - **Galen Marek** leapt at a Felucian rancor rider who used Force resistance, to get into lightsaber range (*The Force Unleashed* novelization). [Force resistance §History][res]
   [Force jump/Legends][jmp]
4. **Era:** all eras.
5. **How it is learned:** basic Jedi training.
6. **Costs and dangers:** none recorded. Not a dark act.
7. **Counters:** standard counters.
8. **Related:** Force speed (§C4); Art of Movement (§C6); telekinesis (Part 2a §A).
9. **Game stats:** (game stat: KOTOR Guardian-only "Force Jump"; three levels of jump in *Jedi Outcast* and *Jedi Academy*.) [Force jump §Behind the scenes][jmp]
- **Uncertainty:** the eight-times figure comes from a game, so it is a game measurement.

#### C6. Art of Movement / Inertia
1. **Names and classification:** Art of Movement; also Inertia or "Up the Walls". The Sith filed it under their Offense school (*Book of Sith*). It has no Jedi category. Neither light nor dark. [Art of Movement][aom]
2. **What it does:** shifts the body's inertia to run on walls and ceilings and boost agility past peak-athlete limits. [Art of Movement][aom]
3. **Users and feats:**
   - **Fy-Tor-Ana** taught it on the Temple obstacle course in the last days of the Republic.
   - **Jaden Korr** used it in the Disciples of Ragnos crisis (14 ABY).
   - **Quinlan Vos** tried it after reading the *Book of Sith* and dislocated his shoulder.
   [Art of Movement §History][aom]
4. **Era:** Old Republic Temple art. After Fy-Tor-Ana's death in the Purge it survived mainly through her students. The New Jedi Order knew it (*Jedi Academy Training Manual*), but it is "unknown" whether its records were recovered. [Art of Movement §History][aom]
5. **How it is learned:** Temple instruction; Sith texts.
6. **Costs and dangers:** physical injury if done wrong (Vos). Not a dark act.
7. **Counters:** none recorded.
8. **Related:** Force speed and Force jump.
9. **Game stats:** none.
- **Uncertainty:** the page contradicts itself: records "unknown" to the New Jedi Order, yet the New Jedi Order "knew of the power". **[conflict]**. The 14 ABY date for the Ragnos crisis is [inferred] from the game's setting.

#### C7. Breath control
1. **Names and classification:** breath control. Control; neither light nor dark. [Breath control][brc]
2. **What it does:** holds breath far beyond normal; "hours or even days" is believed possible. It also guards against gas and vacuum. [Breath control][brc]
3. **Users and feats:**
   - **Qui-Gon Jinn and Obi-Wan Kenobi** against dioxis gas on the Trade Federation ship (32 BBY, Film I).
   - **Obi-Wan Kenobi** underwater after the Order 66 attack on Utapau (19 BBY, ROTS novelization).
   - **Meetra Surik** (Jedi Civil War era).
   - **Luke Skywalker** (*Survivor's Quest*).
   - **Jaina Solo.**
   [Breath control][brc]
4. **Era:** all eras.
5. **How it is learned:** basic Control training.
6. **Costs and dangers:** none recorded. Not a dark act.
7. **Counters:** standard counters.
8. **Related:** hibernation trance (§C8); Force Sustenance (§C13).
9. **Game stats:** none.
- **Uncertainty:** the page dates Luke's *Survivor's Quest* use to 19 ABY. That date was not checked against the novel's own dating, so it is [unclear] and left out of the timeline.

#### C8. Hibernation trance and Serenity
1. **Names and classification:** hibernation trance. Control; Wookieepedia files it as light-side, yet Sith used it (Kreia/Traya): **[conflict]**. Serenity (trance) is a Control trance filed as light-side. [Hibernation trance/Legends][hib]; [Serenity][ser]
2. **What it does:** slows the body; the user needs one-tenth the normal air.
   - Without water: up to one week in a dry climate, one month in a wet one. Without food: about three months before starvation. With a sugar drip: about a year (game stat, see 9).
   - A pre-arranged trigger phrase can wake the user (*Vision of the Future*).
   - A Jedi can place others in the trance (*Jedi Academy Training Manual*).
   [Hibernation trance §Description][hib]
3. **Users and feats:**
   - **Ikrit** spent 356 years in hibernation trance (378 BBY–22 ABY); this is the scale anchor.
   - **Kreia** used it (3951 BBY).
   - **K'Kruhk** faked his death with it.
   - **The Palawa** had a variant lasting over 70 years (Part 3a §T8).
   - **Serenity:** Qui-Gon Jinn and Yoda. [Serenity][ser]
   [Hibernation trance §Users][hib]
4. **Era:** all eras.
5. **How it is learned:** Jedi training.
6. **Costs and dangers:** the user is helpless while in the trance and depends on outside wake signals. Not a dark act.
7. **Counters:** standard counters.
8. **Related:** breath control; Force Sustenance; Palawa hibernation (Part 3a §T8).
9. **Game stats:** (game stat: the week / month / three months / sugar-drip year figures above come from the WEG RPG.) [Hibernation trance §Description][hib]
- **Uncertainty:** none flagged beyond the light-side filing.

#### C9. Force resistance / Force immunity, and Energy resistance
1. **Names and classification:** Force resistance; called Force immunity once mastered; "Force guard" in SWTOR. Control. Wookieepedia files it as light-side, but Felucians and the Reborn used it. Energy resistance is a separate Control power from KOTOR. [Force resistance][res]; [Energy resistance][ers]
2. **What it does:**
   - **Basic resistance** is a user's natural resilience to Force powers. A stronger will and greater mastery can overcome it.
   - **Trained resistance** creates an aura against physical and mental powers. Immunity can render some powers "entirely inert".
   - **A Sentinel's mastered immunity** gives passive invulnerability to fear, disorientation, loss of senses and paralysis (*Saga Edition*).
   - **Energy resistance** reduces energy damage. It resembles *tutaminis* and Force deflection.
   [Force resistance][res]; [Energy resistance][ers]
3. **Users and feats:**
   - **Imar Deez** taught it to **Relin Druur**, who taught **Drev Hassin** and **Marr Idi-Shael** (5000 BBY). It shielded them from the dark-side influence of Lignan ore (*Lost Tribe of the Sith*; *Crosscurrent*).
   - **Revan** used similar powers to resist the Sith Emperor.
   - **A Felucian rancor rider** used it against Galen Marek (2 BBY [inferred]).
   - **The Reborn** used it (*Jedi Outcast*; *Jedi Academy*).
   [Force resistance §History][res]
4. **Era:** "one of the most common skills" taught by 5000 BBY and passed down for generations. [Force resistance §History][res]
5. **How it is learned:** Jedi training; it is a speciality of the Sentinels.
6. **Costs and dangers:** none recorded. Not a dark act.
7. **Counters:** superior willpower and mastery can overcome it. Non-Force attacks bypass it (Galen used a lightsaber).
8. **Related:** thought shield (§C10); *tutaminis* (§C1).
9. **Game stats:**
   - (game stat: KOTOR and KOTOR II tiers: Force Resistance, then Force Immunity.)
   - (game stat: SWTOR "Force guard".)
   - (game stat: Saga Edition Sentinel immunity.)
- **Uncertainty:** the source names for the Druur lineage are [inferred]; the page section cites them.

#### C10. Thought shield
1. **Names and classification:** thought shield (mental shield). Control; neither light nor dark. [Thought shield][ths]
2. **What it does:** hides thoughts, emotions and motives. In combat it stops an enemy reading your next move. It needs strong will and long concentration, and becomes subconscious with practice. [Thought shield][ths]
3. **Users and feats:**
   - **Atton Rand** "played pazaak" in his head, but could not keep Kreia out (3951 BBY, KOTOR II).
   - **Obi-Wan Kenobi** used it against Dooku's mind invasion on Geonosis (22 BBY, Hyperspace fiction).
   - **Mara Jade** used it against Joruus C'baoth (9 ABY, *The Last Command*).
   [Thought shield][ths]
4. **Era:** all eras.
5. **How it is learned:** taught "from the earliest age" to Padawans and apprentices, sometimes even to diplomats. Atton taught it to Meetra Surik.
6. **Costs and dangers:** none recorded. Not a dark act.
7. **Counters:** a stronger telepath can break it (Kreia against Atton).
8. **Related:** Force resistance; Force stealth; telepathy (§S6); mind probe (Part 3a §T11).
9. **Game stats:** none.
- **Uncertainty:** none flagged.

#### C11. Force stealth / Force concealment (and Quey'tek meditation)
1. **Names and classification:** Force stealth; also Force concealment or "Buried Presence" (*Force Unleashed Campaign Guide*). Control; neither light nor dark. Quey'tek meditation is a Sith and Dark Jedi variant. [Force stealth][stl]
2. **What it does:** masks the user's alignment, their Force ability, or their whole presence from other Force-sensitives. [Force stealth][stl]
3. **Users and feats:**
   - **Darth Sidious** hid his dark nature from the High Council "for over three decades", even in the same room; this is the scale anchor.
   - **Vergere** shrank her presence to microscopic size, out of even Luke's reach.
   - **Darth Traya** hid from her students.
   - **Revan** possibly used it at the Korriban academy.
   - **Xanatos** taught it to Bruck Chun.
   - **Yoda and Obi-Wan Kenobi** used it in exile (19 BBY–).
   - **Quey'tek users:** Ventress, Dooku (Obi-Wan and Anakin eventually saw through his), Maul, and possibly Zannah.
   - **Luke Skywalker** got past the Nightsisters with it and taught it to Teneniel Djo (8 ABY, *Courtship*). He later crept up on Jacen with it (40 ABY).
   - **Jacen Solo** hid Lumiya and taught it to Ben, who taught Mara.
   - **The Baran Do Hidden Ones** faked their deaths with it.
   - **The Agents of Ossus** evaded the Inquisitorius with it.
   [Force stealth §Effects][stl]
4. **Era:** all eras; key to Sith survival in the Rule of Two era.
5. **How it is learned:** Master to student (see the chains above). Baran Do and Agents of Ossus tradition.
6. **Costs and dangers:** none recorded. Quey'tek is used to hide the dark side, which is deceptive use, but the power itself is neutral.
7. **Counters:** sustained close contact can pierce it (Dooku was seen through). Sense Force can detect presences unless they are masked by stealth or a nexus. [Sense Force][sf]
8. **Related:** *altus sopor*; thought shield; Force cloak (Part 3a §T11); Jensaarai (Part 3a §T6f).
9. **Game stats:** (game stat: WotC "Force Stealth"; Saga Edition splits it across several powers and talents.) [Force stealth §Behind the scenes][stl]
- **Uncertainty:** "possibly Zannah" and "argued" for Revan are hedged in the source itself, so both are [unclear].

#### C12. Force Body, Enhance Attribute, Battlemind, Force Enlightenment
- **Force Body** (also Prolong Force), Control; neither light nor dark:
  - Lets the user keep drawing on the Force past the point of exhaustion.
  - **Cost:** real physical damage the longer it is used, and sometimes a temporarily withered look (*Dark Nest I: The Joiner King*).
  - It needs a solid Force connection and physical toughness. Used carelessly, "this art might well kill them".
  - [Force Body][fbd]
- **Enhance Attribute**, Control; neither light nor dark:
  - Temporarily raises a physical attribute: jump, speed, strength, senses, toughness.
  - **Feats:** Anakin Skywalker thought every Jedi should use it to fight in Form V (*The Jedi Path*). Darth Sidious, fighting Maul and Savage Opress, managed "only barely" to summon enough strength to withstand them (Clone Wars, 22–19 BBY; *Shadow Conspiracy*).
  - (game stat: a novice raises one attribute; a proficient user raises five at once for longer. *Dark Force Rising Sourcebook*.)
  - [Enhance Attribute][enh]
- **Battlemind** (Battle Strike), Control; neither light nor dark:
  - Raises morale and fighting spirit; can push past old age and fading stamina.
  - **Users:** Mace Windu, who combined it "and additional dark side powers" when he created Vaapad; Darth Maul.
  - Similar to Force valor, Force Body, Enhance Attribute and Force rage. Force rage is dark, see Force Enrage in Part 2b §F.
  - [Battlemind][bmd]
- **Force Enlightenment**, Control; filed as light-side:
  - Pushes the user's best-trained powers to the highest level they have mastered. For Meetra Surik these were Force speed, Force Defend and Force valor.
  - Meetra learned it after talking with Vrook Lamar, Kavar and Zez-Kai Ell (3951 BBY, KOTOR II).
  - **Behind the scenes:** on KOTOR II's non-canon dark path she learns Force Crush instead.
  - [Force Enlightenment][enl]
- **Dark act?** None of these is a dark act in itself. Force Body's harm is physical. Battlemind's link to Vaapad touches dark-side borderlands; see DS1 §Section 8 and Force Enrage in Part 2b §F.
