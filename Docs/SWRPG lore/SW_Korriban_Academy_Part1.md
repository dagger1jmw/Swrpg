# The Sith Academy on Korriban (c. 3681–3640 BBY, with other eras) — Part 1

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 21, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
This file covers the Sith Academy on Korriban as a place where a player character lives and trains, from arrival to apprenticeship or death. The focus is the reconstituted Sith Empire's academy from its reopening in 3681 BBY through the Great Galactic War (3681–3653 BBY), the Cold War, and the start of the Galactic War (3640 BBY). Earlier and later academies are summarised in Part 2 for comparison. Key facts:
- The Empire retook Korriban in the first year of the war (3681 BBY) and re-established the academy, which trained "hundreds" of Sith.
- During the Cold War every Force-sensitive in Imperial territory was required by law to train there. Groups of acolytes under an Overseer competed in tomb trials to catch the eye of a visiting Sith Lord.
- Murder among acolytes was formally illegal but common, usually staged as accidents in the tombs.

Part 1 covers history and status, people, acolytes, training and graduation. Part 2 (`SW_Korriban_Academy_Part2.md`) covers layout and locations, dangers, other eras, daily-life details, the Timeline, Changes from the previous file (including the Dromund Kaas cross-reference check) and the closing sections.

**SWTOR class-story note.** The Sith Warrior and Sith Inquisitor prologues (c. 3643 BBY; Kallig's second acolyte class 3640 BBY) are SWTOR material and fully usable, tagged here [SWTOR class story, c. 3643 BBY]. Their protagonists, the Sith Warrior (later the Empire's Wrath) and the Inquisitor (Kallig, later Darth Nox), are **title-only canon NPCs** in the developer's game. Their trials are listed as recorded events, not as a template the player must follow. Where Wookieepedia notes player choices (e.g. Tremel's or Harkun's survival), the page's main narrative is given and the alternative goes under Decisions.

## Entries

### A. History and status (3681–3640 BBY)
- **Type:** institution · **Era:** reopened 3681 BBY; active through the Cold War and the Galactic War
- **Key facts:**
  - **Recapture, 3681 BBY.** A Republic and Jedi guard had watched Korriban since the Jedi Civil War. Darth Vindican, his apprentice Malgus and an armada of thirty Imperial battlecruisers destroyed the small Republic force and its orbital station [Darth Vindican §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Vindican); [Korriban/Legends §Great Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Korriban/Legends). Retaking Korriban had been a top priority for the Empire [Korriban/Legends §Great Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Korriban/Legends).
  - **Rebuilding.** The Empire ordered the Academy rebuilt and the Valley "rejuvenated", rebuilding the pyramid that formed its main levels. The Academy "trained hundreds of Sith over the years" [Sith Academy (Korriban) §True Sith Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Academy_%28Korriban%29). Teams sent by Darth Thanaton excavated the tombs [Tomb of Ajunta Pall §Return of the Sith](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tomb_of_Ajunta_Pall); [Valley of the Dark Lords/Legends §Return of the Sith Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Valley_of_the_Dark_Lords/Legends) [unclear: Thanaton is credited with this work although he was born in 3695 BBY, so the dating of his role is vague] [Darth Thanaton](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Thanaton).
  - **Defence.** Turbolaser batteries protected the Academy during the war [Sith Academy (Korriban) §True Sith Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Academy_%28Korriban%29). The Imperial Guard became its protectors after 3681 BBY; those stationed there were the Dark Honor Guard [Imperial Guard §Protecting the Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Guard).
  - **Secret use.** In the Academy's depths the Imperial Guard oversaw the machinery the Emperor used to make his "Children" [Imperial Guard §Protecting the Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Guard). In 3678 BBY, Exal Kressh leaked Korriban's defences to the Republic, which assaulted the Academy while she hunted the Children in the adjacent tombs. Teneb Kel killed her, and the assault collapsed [Battle over Korriban](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_over_Korriban); [Exal Kressh](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Exal_Kressh).
  - **Cold War status.** After the Treaty of Coruscant (3653 BBY) Korriban became "a major center of learning". The Empire built over the tombs and the former Dreshdae and uncovered lost tombs and caverns [Korriban/Legends §Cold War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Korriban/Legends). Life thrived "as a training ground for the Sith and their soldiers alike" [Valley of the Dark Lords/Legends §Return of the Sith Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Valley_of_the_Dark_Lords/Legends).
  - **Council presence.** The Dark Council kept chambers near the pyramid's summit for conferences on Korriban. Rumour held that the Emperor had a seldom-used throne room deep inside [Sith Academy (Korriban) §Layout](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Academy_%28Korriban%29). The Council met on Korriban after the Treaty and for Baras's downfall [Darth Baras §Sacking of Coruscant](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Baras); [Darth Baras §Downfall](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Baras).
  - **Sphere responsible.** No page names a Sphere that runs the Academy. The Sphere of Ancient Knowledge had a strong presence: its head (named as Darth Thanaton) maintained the Academy's Sith Library [Sith Academy (Korriban) §Layout](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Academy_%28Korriban%29), and Overseer Harkun served within it under Lord Zash [Harkun](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Harkun). See Decisions.
  - **Later.** The Republic held the Academy briefly in 3637 BBY (Korriban Incursion) [Korriban/Legends §Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Korriban/Legends); [Korriban Incursion](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Korriban_Incursion). The Knights of Zakuul breached it c. 3636 BBY [Attack on Korriban](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Attack_on_Korriban). Both are outside the window.
- **Related:** SW_Great_Galactic_War_Part1 (Battle of Korriban 3681 BBY; Battle over Korriban 3678 BBY); SW_SWTOR_Era_Power_Structures_Part1 (Dark Council, Spheres, Imperial Guard).
- **Uncertainty:** [conflict] over when slaves and aliens were first admitted (section C). [unclear] which Sphere, if any, governed the Academy.

### B. People: staff, chain of command, visitors
**Chain of command as attested:** the Dark Council (chambers in the pyramid) → Sith Lords holding authority on Korriban, such as Lord Zash, a "supervisor of acolyte training" with chambers in the pyramid and "authority over the institution's overseers", and Lord Khreusis, who "also held dominion on Korriban" [Darth Zash §Studying the dark arts](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Zash) → Overseers, each instructing a group of acolytes [Sith training §Sith Training under the Empires](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_training); [Overseer (Sith)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Overseer_%28Sith%29) → Assistant Overseers (junior instructors) [Overseer (Sith)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Overseer_%28Sith%29) → acolytes. Security, the jail and the soldiers sit alongside this chain. There was no single named "Headmaster" in this era [Not found]; that title belongs to the Jedi Civil War and Brotherhood eras (Part 2).
