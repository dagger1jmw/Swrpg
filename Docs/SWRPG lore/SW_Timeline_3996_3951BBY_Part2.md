# SWRPG Galaxy Timeline, 3,996–3,951 BBY (Part 2 of 3): The Mandalorian Wars and the Jedi Civil War

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 24, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends), as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
Part 2 covers the Mandalorian Wars (3,976–3,960 BBY) and the Jedi Civil War (3,959–3,956 BBY), with the state of the Republic and the Jedi Order in each phase. Key facts: the wars began with the raid on Althir III in 3,976 BBY, but the Mandalorians fought the Republic Military only from 3,965 BBY (the "False War"); open war came with the Battle of Vanquo in 3,964 BBY and the Onslaught of 3,963 BBY; the Revanchists were founded in 3,964 BBY and sanctioned in 3,963 BBY; Tol Cressa made Revan Supreme Commander after Duro in 3,962 BBY; Malachor V ended the war in 3,960 BBY. Revan's capture is dated 3,957 BBY and the KOTOR story to 3,956 BBY. Carth Onasi was a lieutenant from 3,964 BBY, is called "commander" from 3,958 BBY, and was made admiral after the Star Forge (see the rank row and Part 3). Column key as in Part 1.

### Overview
The war between the Mandalorians and the Republic begins with the Battle of Althir in 3,976 BBY [note: sixteen years of conflict; historians disagreed on its endpoints, since the Mandalorians raided the Outer Rim for over a decade before meeting the Republic Military in 3,965 BBY] [Mandalorian Wars](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalorian_Wars). Mandalore the Ultimate, unknowingly a tool of the Sith Emperor, led the Neo-Crusaders with Cassus Fett as aide-de-camp and strategist [Mandalorian Wars §Prelude](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalorian_Wars).

**State of the Republic:** The Republic suppressed news of Cathar and guarded only Dxun and Taris [Mandalorian Wars §War in the Outer Rim](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalorian_Wars). In 3,966 BBY Taris was admitted after corporations bribed senators [Mandalorian Wars §Prelude](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalorian_Wars). Tol Cressa is Supreme Chancellor by 3,963 BBY [note: his earliest mention; when he took office is not known] [Ruled (Jared, 24 Nov 2026)] [Tol Cressa](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tol_Cressa), with Koa Delko as Defense Minister [Mandalorian Wars](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalorian_Wars). After the Onslaught the Senate mobilised the whole Navy [Mandalorian Wars §The Mandalorian Invasion](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalorian_Wars).

**State of the Jedi Order:** The increasingly conservative High Council refused to intervene, fearing the dark side [Mandalorian Wars §The coming storm](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalorian_Wars); Vandar Tokare's vision of coming destruction was read as a warning against fighting Mandalore [Mandalorian Wars §The Mandalorian Invasion](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalorian_Wars). The secret Covenant's Padawan Massacre (3,964 BBY) and its Vindication insurrection (3,963 BBY) shook public faith [Mandalorian Wars §The coming storm](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalorian_Wars); [Mandalorian Wars §The Mandalorian Invasion](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalorian_Wars). Council members of the period included Atris [Atris](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Atris), Kavar, Vrook Lamar, Zez-Kai Ell and Lonna Vash [note: all listed as High Council members in the First Jedi Purge] [First Jedi Purge](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/First_Jedi_Purge), Dorjander Kace and, from 3,963 BBY, Lucien Draay [Mandalorian Wars §The Mandalorian Invasion](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalorian_Wars). The war ended in a victory for the Republic and the Revanchists ("Jedi Crusaders") and the fragmentation of the clans [Mandalorian Wars](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalorian_Wars).

## JEDI CIVIL WAR — 3,959–3,956 BBY
## ════════════════════════════

### Overview
Also called the Second Sith War, the Old Republic Insurrection, the Malak War and, by the Mandalorians, the War of the Star Forge, the war begins with the Battle of Foerost in 3,959 BBY [Jedi Civil War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Civil_War). Revan's empire drew on Mandalorian War veterans, Brotherhood of the Sith members and Dark Jedi, and Revan aimed to take the Republic in anticipation of the hidden Sith Empire [Jedi Civil War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Civil_War).

**State of the Republic:** Large numbers of officers and soldiers defected; the Sith held about a third of the settled galaxy, including Corellia, Duro and Bellassa, but the Republic contained their pockets [Jedi Civil War §The war](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Civil_War). Tol Cressa remained Supreme Chancellor [Jedi Civil War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Civil_War).

**State of the Jedi Order:** The Council kept a second council at the Dantooine Enclave (Vandar Tokare, Vrook Lamar, Zhar Lestin, Dorak) [note: which healed and rebuilt Revan] [Jedi Civil War §The war](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Civil_War). Malak razed the Enclave in 3,956 BBY [Jedi Civil War §The war](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Civil_War). The war ended at Rakata Prime in 3,956 BBY: a tactical, pyrrhic Republic–Jedi victory, but by one view a strategic Sith victory, with "barely a hundred" Jedi left [Jedi Civil War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Civil_War).
