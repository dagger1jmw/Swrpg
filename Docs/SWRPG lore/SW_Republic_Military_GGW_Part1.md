# The Republic Military in the Great Galactic War (c. 3681–3653 BBY)

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 21, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends), as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
This file covers the Republic Military of the Great Galactic War (3681–3653 BBY), centred on 3661 BBY, as a home for a soldier, pilot, officer or spy character. It covers the branches, how people joined and trained, gear and ships, units and named people, daily life, the soldier's view of the war around 3661 BBY, the SIS in the field, and briefly what veterans faced after the Treaty.

The sources say:
- The Military answered to the Senate and Chancellor through a Supreme Commander. It had an Army, a Navy and a Starfighter Corps, and after the Jedi Civil War its ranks, uniforms and equipment were standardized.
- By 3661 BBY the Republic had fought for twenty years. Morale was lifted by Bothawui (c. 3671/3670 BBY) and Alderaan (3667 BBY), then the Navy was crippled at Hoth (3665 BBY). The Mandalorian blockade of the Hydian Way began that year.
- Most named Army people of these years come from SWTOR sources: Malcom and Havoc Squad, Garza, Tavus, Kaedan's Special Forces team, Laren Omas.
- Many details you might expect (the 18-week basic course, issued kit, rank lists, the Personnel Division) are dated only to the Cold War. Each is tagged post-window or [conflict] where it appears.

### A. Structure and command

#### The Republic Military (overview)
- **Type:** institution
- **Era:** whole window, from older roots
- **Key facts:**
  - The Republic Military was made up of the Army, the Navy and the Starfighter Corps, overseen by the Minister and Ministry of Defense [Republic Military/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Military/Legends).
  - The chain ran from the Senate to the Chancellor, then to the Supreme Commander, then to generals and admirals [Republic Military/Legends §Organization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Military/Legends).
  - After the Jedi Civil War, ranks, uniforms and equipment were standardized. Recruitment and training became the same everywhere, crews were mixed, and troops were trained to fight without relying on the Jedi [Republic Military/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Military/Legends).
  - Duties included anti-piracy and anti-smuggling patrols, and personnel rotated between combat and non-combat postings [Republic Military/Legends §Role](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Military/Legends).
  - The Navy was the most valued branch and could demand higher wages. Ground forces were paid less, and their equipment varied in quality [Republic Military/Legends §Branch Relations](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Military/Legends).
  - Personnel were addressed by rank or as "sir" [Republic Military/Legends §Proper Address](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Military/Legends).
  - Other bodies under the Military included Military Police and Republic Intelligence [Republic Military/Legends §Other](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Military/Legends).
- **Related:** Power Structures Part2 (branch rivalry, chain of command).
- **Uncertainty:** The page describes the post-Jedi Civil War Military in general and does not date each point to the war years. [inferred] It applies to the window, since standardization came first.

#### Supreme Commander in 3661 BBY
- **Type:** rank/office
- **Era:** whole window
- **Key facts:**
  - The Supreme Commander headed the Republic Military. The Duros Stantorrs held the post in the Cold War, and Jace Malcom took it when the Galactic War began [Supreme Commander (Republic)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Supreme_Commander_%28Republic%29).
  - Rans was Supreme Commander "during of the Great Galactic War and the subsequent Cold War". In his early career he had fought only scattered Mandalorian clans and was unready for total war. He rose through quick thinking and charm and built the military up to new standards [Rans §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rans).
  - Rans was a media favourite and married the war veteran Galena Rans. The Sith "totally outplayed" him with the Sacking of Coruscant. He later won at Ilum and then retired, handing the post to Malcom [Rans §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rans).
  - Further support (added (lore batch 18)): the Sacking of Coruscant page has a border deployment "by order of Supreme Commander Rans" during the war.
  - Malcom's page also says he succeeded Rans as Supreme Commander, in 3640 BBY [Jace Malcom §Supreme Commander Malcom](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jace_Malcom).
- **Related:** Power Structures Part2 (Stantorrs/Rans/Malcom conflict).
- **Uncertainty:** [conflict] Stantorrs held the post in the Cold War [Supreme Commander (Republic)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Supreme_Commander_%28Republic%29) vs Rans held it through the Cold War until Ilum [Rans §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rans)[Jace Malcom §Supreme Commander Malcom](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jace_Malcom). [inferred] Rans is the only Supreme Commander named for the Great War, but no page says he held the post as early as 3661 BBY. See Decisions 1.

### B. Joining and training

#### Enlistment
- **Type:** practice
- **Era:** Great War (some points undated)
- **Key facts:**
  - In the Great War, Republic soldiers became professionals, and millions of volunteers from thousands of planets fought in the Army [Republic soldier §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_soldier).
  - The Army enlisted more recruits each year than most other branches combined [Republic Infantry](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Infantry).
  - Garza forged her identity papers at 15 to enlist in 3681 BBY [Elin Garza §Great Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Elin_Garza). The Coruscant file covers this.
  - Navy: non-commissioned crew came from local enlistment centres. Signing bonuses were sometimes offered in times of Sith threat, and the Navy's prestige made compulsory service unnecessary [undated] [Republic Navy/Legends §Personnel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Navy/Legends).
  - Navy: experienced merchant captains were sometimes given provisional ratings to serve on bridges in wartime, often withdrawn in peacetime, which caused friction with civilian spacers [undated] [Republic Navy/Legends §Personnel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Navy/Legends).
  - Army: older eras offered signing bonuses for dangerous postings, and pay and conditions were worse than the Navy's [other era] [Republic Army §Administration](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Army).
- **Related:** Coruscant file (recruiting, volunteer-only); GGW Part3 (no conscription).
- **Uncertainty:** No minimum enlistment age was found. Garza's forgery implies 15 was too young, but no page gives the legal age. [inferred]

#### Basic and specialist training
- **Type:** practice
- **Era:** disputed
- **Key facts:**
  - Army basic training was 18 weeks of drills, tests and exercises, followed by up to 12 months of specialist training. One page gives this in its Great War paragraph [Republic soldier §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_soldier), while the other dates it to the Cold War [Republic Army §Specializations](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Army).
  - Special Forces candidates were chosen for martial skill, discipline and morale (post-Treaty context) [Republic trooper §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_trooper).
  - Troopers could specialize in doctrines such as Vanguard (heavy armour, frontline defence) and Commando (heavy blaster cannons) [Republic trooper §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_trooper). [BTS] These match the SWTOR trooper class's advanced classes [Republic trooper §Behind the scenes](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_trooper), and the page links them to Special Forces in the Cold War and after.
- **Uncertainty:** [conflict] 18 weeks plus 12 months in the Great War [Republic soldier §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_soldier) vs in the Cold War [Republic Army §Specializations](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Army). See Decisions 5.

#### Academies
- **Type:** institution
- **Era:** varies
- **Key facts:**
  - Navy officers trained mainly at the Coruscant Naval Academy, with satellite schools. The Corellian academy taught shipwrights and a Duros orbital facility taught tactical networking [undated] [Republic Navy/Legends §Personnel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Navy/Legends).
  - Carida: "At some point, the Army established a military academy on the planet Carida" [Republic Army §The Sith Wars](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Army). The Academy of Carida was one part of the Army that outlived the rest [Republic Army §Administration](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Army).
  - The Carida Academy page says it existed around 5000 BBY. It next appears around 220 BBY, as a Senate-mandated training ground [Carida Academy/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Carida_Academy/Legends). No Great War date is given.
- **Uncertainty:** **Carida premise:** not attested for the window. It is undated in Army sources, and its own page jumps from c. 5000 BBY to 220 BBY. A Carida-trained officer in 3661 BBY would be [inferred] at best. No named Army officer school for the war years was found. See Decisions 9.

### C. Gear and ships

#### What a trooper carried
- **Type:** equipment
- **Era:** war years where marked
- **Key facts:**
  - Malcom wore the standard white-and-orange Republic trooper armour of the Great War [Jace Malcom §Equipment](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jace_Malcom).
  - At Korriban in 3681 BBY, Malcom used a blaster cannon, a laser chaingun and a missile launcher. At Alderaan in 3667 BBY he went without a helmet and carried a combat knife in a chest sheath, a portable blaster cannon, grenades and signal flares [Jace Malcom §Equipment](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jace_Malcom).
  - After the Great War, engineers built heavier, more powerful rifles that took real strength and skill to use, so they were rare outside the military. The war kept pushing for stronger rifles every year [Republic trooper §Equipment](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_trooper).
  - Trooper armour was built for protection and flexibility in many environments, rated against blaster fire up to a bomb blast [Republic trooper §Equipment](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_trooper).
  - Special Forces troops' armour (see A) [Republic Special Forces Division §Characteristics](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Special_Forces_Division).
  - **Cold War kit (post-window, for comparison):** lightweight duraplast armour, a blaster rifle with an integrated grenade launcher and switchable ammunition, a pistol, grenades, an encrypted comlink and four days of rations. Special Forces had durasteel or cortosis armour, handheld blaster cannons and personal shields [Republic Army §Equipment](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Army)[Republic soldier §Arms and Equipment](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_soldier).
- **Related:** Life (rations).
- **Uncertainty:** The issued-kit list is Cold War. The red-and-yellow armour on the soldier page is from the Old Sith Wars era [other era] [Republic soldier §Arms and Equipment](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_soldier).

### D. Units and people

#### Havoc Squad in the window
- **Type:** unit
- **Era:** disputed
- **Key facts:**
  - Elite Special Forces squad of the Great War, originally Infantry Squad 326, given the name "Havoc" under Malcom. It was later led by its XO Jek Kardan, then Kardan's protégé Tavus. It served "from Ord Mantell to the Battle of Alderaan" [Havoc Squad](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Havoc_Squad).
  - Malcom captained Infantry Squad 326, which earned the "Havoc" name with risky raids. In 3667 BBY it ambushed Darth Malgus's advance on Alderaan's capital [Jace Malcom §The Alderaan triumph](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jace_Malcom).
  - The battle made Malcom and Havoc famous and showed commanders the value of small, mobile elite units [Jace Malcom §The Alderaan triumph](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jace_Malcom).
  - Jek Kardan was a founding member during the Great War, Malcom's second-in-command, and became commander when Malcom was promoted. He mentored Tavus [Jek Kardan §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jek_Kardan).
  - Tavus was recruited into Havoc in 3667 BBY [Minos Cluster Campaign §The long haul](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Minos_Cluster_Campaign).
  - Aryn Leneer was "a member of Havoc Squad with Zeerid Korr in the early days of the Great Galactic War" [Aryn Leneer §Comrade of Havoc Squad](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aryn_Leneer).
  - Korr served as a commando in "Havoc Squadron" and became friends with Leneer at the Battle of Balmorra [Zeerid Korr §Soldier then smuggler](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zeerid_Korr).

#### Morale and the war's mood
- Troopers at Bothawui fought under Jedi Master Belth Allusis. Their deaths and the losses they inflicted gave hope to a Republic that had been losing [Republic trooper §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_trooper).
- The 3667 BBY win at Alderaan "greatly boosted the morale" of Republic forces and led to a string of victories [Minos Cluster Campaign §The long haul](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Minos_Cluster_Campaign).
- Hoth (3665 BBY) was a heavy blow. Many veterans later saw it as a turning point and a main reason the Republic accepted the Treaty. Imperial propaganda made use of the image of the *Star of Coruscant* falling [Battle of Hoth (Great Galactic War) §Aftermath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Hoth_%28Great_Galactic_War%29).
- The Dread Masters could fill whole crews with fear and send them fleeing, which badly hurt the Republic war effort until their capture in 3668 BBY [Capture of the Dread Masters](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Capture_of_the_Dread_Masters).

#### Cantinas, comradeship and the Jedi
- Leneer never drank but went to the cantinas with Havoc after battles and training, and was a shoulder to lean on after a night's drinking. Her empathy lifted the squad's morale [Aryn Leneer §Comrade of Havoc Squad](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aryn_Leneer).
- Special Forces troopers sometimes worked with Jedi; Tavus and Orgus Din were friends [Republic Special Forces Division §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Special_Forces_Division)[Harron Tavus §Personality and traits](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Harron_Tavus).

### F. The soldier's war around 3661 BBY
- **Type:** situation
- **Era:** 3665–3660 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - After Hoth, the Navy was "still reeling". It could normally have broken a small blockade quickly, but couldn't when the Mandalorians blockaded the Hydian Way in 3661 BBY between Devaron and the Minos Cluster [Blockade of the Hydian Way §A crippled Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way).
  - The Military used the Hydian Way to move materiel from the Core to the deep Outer Rim fronts, so the blockade cut both commercial and military supply [Blockade of the Hydian Way §A crippled Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way).
  - The SIS scrambled starfighters to help Visz break the blockade [Republic Strategic Information Service §Early operations](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Strategic_Information_Service). The Valor-class *Fortitude* took part in 3660 BBY [Fortitude (Valor-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fortitude_%28Valor-class%29).
  - In the Rim Campaign (around 3660 BBY), the Republic under Ven Zallow struck back into the Empire's heart and won several victories, but was eventually driven out [Rim Campaign](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rim_Campaign).
  - The Minos Cluster: Special Forces and Jedi raids under Din and Tavus ran until 3654 BBY [Minos Cluster Campaign §The long haul](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Minos_Cluster_Campaign).
- **Related:** GGW Part2/3; Mandalorian and Nar Shaddaa files.
- **Uncertainty:** [conflict] The SIS page says the blockade's end reopened the "Rimma Trade Route" [Republic Strategic Information Service §Early operations](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Strategic_Information_Service), while the blockade page names the Hydian Way [Blockade of the Hydian Way §A crippled Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way).

### G. Intelligence: the SIS in the field
- **Type:** institution
- **Era:** whole window
- **Key facts:**
  - Before the war, the SIS was a Senate Library agency doing monitoring and decryption. The war removed it from the Library and widened its role, and it supplied intelligence to the Military and the Jedi [Republic Strategic Information Service §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Strategic_Information_Service)[Republic Strategic Information Service §Early operations](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Strategic_Information_Service).
  - It was small and new, with little bureaucracy and little oversight. It stayed inside the law while looking for loopholes, but found it hard to get agents into the Empire [Republic Strategic Information Service §Early operations](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Strategic_Information_Service).
  - Agents could hold military ranks (colonel, captain, lieutenant), and all were trained in Huttese [Republic Strategic Information Service §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Strategic_Information_Service).
  - **3661 BBY:** SIS operatives abducted the Cathar prince Shange, who opposed Cathar membership in the Republic, and held him and his followers in stasis in the Tomb on Belsavis [Republic Strategic Information Service §Early operations](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Strategic_Information_Service)[Shange](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shange).
  - SIS listening posts in the Outer Rim intercepted Imperial messages that led to the Dread Masters' capture (3668 BBY) [Republic Strategic Information Service §Early operations](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Strategic_Information_Service)[Capture of the Dread Masters](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Capture_of_the_Dread_Masters).
  - Field work with the Army: Trant fought beside Garza's platoon on Bomodon (3673 BBY) [45th Infantry Division §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/45th_Infantry_Division)[Elin Garza §Great Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Elin_Garza).
  - Counter-intelligence failure: a Chiss enclave on Hoth intercepted coded Republic messages about the prototype fleet's secret route. That led to the 3665 BBY ambush [Battle of Hoth (Great Galactic War) §Prelude](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Hoth_%28Great_Galactic_War%29).
  - Counter-intelligence and network management were SIS duties "by the end of the war" [Republic Strategic Information Service §Overview](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Strategic_Information_Service). The Army's Personnel Division, which hunted traitors, is Cold War only (post-window) [Personnel Division of the Army of the Galactic Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Personnel_Division_of_the_Army_of_the_Galactic_Republic).
- **Related:** Power Structures (SIS ranks and Directors); Nar Shaddaa file (Trant).
- **Uncertainty:** none beyond the above.

### H. Veterans after the Treaty (post-window, brief)
- The Treaty ordered the Military to stand down and hand war zones to their Imperial claimants. Many in government refused to accept defeat [Jace Malcom §Promotions and politics](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jace_Malcom).
- Some kept fighting covertly or openly, and many worlds left the Senate in a crisis of confidence [Treaty of Coruscant §A discontented Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Treaty_of_Coruscant).
- Korr left the army after his wife's death and his daughter's injury. He found no legal work and became an Exchange smuggler [Zeerid Korr §Soldier then smuggler](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zeerid_Korr).
- Kardan resigned after the Senate abandoned Havoc on Ando Prime, and Havoc's veterans defected by 3643 BBY [Jek Kardan §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jek_Kardan).
- Garza became a controversial figure in the peace and a political rival of Chancellor Janarus [Silver Crescent §Garza](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Silver_Crescent).
