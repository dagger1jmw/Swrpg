# Playable Species of the Old Republic Era (c. 3681–3600 BBY), Part 1 of 3: Human, Sith and the core species

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 21, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
This file covers the species a player or NPC can belong to in the Old Republic era (c. 3681–3600 BBY): body, lifespan and ageing, stated strengths and weaknesses, Force sensitivity, culture and homeworld in the era, language, and era notes. Part 1 covers Human, Sith (Purebloods and hybrids) and 18 core species. Part 2 covers 25 further species and 3 non-playable or variant groups. Part 3 holds the comparison table, the character-file conflicts and the closing sections. The main ageing finding: Legends gives real lifespan prose for only a handful of species (Human, Wookiee, Hutt, Chiss, Trandoshan, Falleen, Yoda's species, Epicanthix, Zabrak). Almost every other age band and maximum age comes from RPG books and is labelled "(game stat: …)".

## Entries

### How to read this file
- **Entry layout:** each species has the brief's seven points as numbered bullets (1 body, 2 lifespan and ageing, 3 strengths and weaknesses, 4 Force, 5 culture and homeworld, 6 languages, 7 era notes), then a readiness mark:
  - **playable-ready:** the sources cover body, culture and era well enough to play.
  - **thin:** at least two of these are missing: body numbers, lifespan, culture and era.
  - **Gaps in ready species:** a missing lifespan alone leaves a species playable-ready, with the gap shown next to the mark.
- **Infobox numbers:** heights, masses and lifespans marked "(infobox)" come from the species page's infobox. Each one names the work the infobox cites.
  - When that work is a tabletop RPG book, the number is labelled "(game stat: book)".
  - Ultimate Alien Anthology (d20, 2003) is shortened to UAA.
  - The full age-band tables are under Game notes in Part 3.
- **Ages of named people:** recorded birth and death years come from the Era 09 character files (SW_Characters_09_GGW_ColdWar_*). Ages worked out from them are tagged [inferred]. 3661 BBY is the reference year.
- **Files not on the box:** SW_Midichlorians_Force_Potential, 23-species.md and the progression spec (body caps, lifespans) are not on the box. Nothing here is taken from them, and their content is not guessed.
- **Cross-references, not repeated here:**
  - **Imperial alien policy** (master races, slave species, field-hospital triage, aliens in the forces): SW_Imperial_Military_Intelligence_GGW_Part1, section B "Aliens and non-Humans", and SW_Dromund_Kaas_KaasCity_OldRepublic_Part2, sections "Society, class and species" and "Slaves and aliens".
    - Field hospitals treated only favoured humanoid species: Humans, Chiss, Duros, Kaleesh, Twi'leks, Aqualish, Zabraks and Purebloods.
    - Most slaves were Twi'leks, Duros and Zabraks.
  - **Purebloods and alien acolytes on Korriban:** SW_Korriban_Academy_Part1, section C.
  - **Jedi recruitment of long-lived species and the named Jedi roster by species:** SW_Jedi_Temple_Life_GGW_Part1, sections A and G.

### Human
- **Type:** species (baseline; many cultural groups). **playable-ready**
- **Era:** whole era; the most common species in both the Republic and the Sith Empire
- **Key facts:**
  1. **Body:**
     - Average height 1.70–1.80 m, males usually taller and heavier; giantism up to 2.30 m, nanism down to 1.3 m [Human/Legends §Physical diversity](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Human/Legends).
     - Warm-blooded [Human/Legends §Physiology](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Human/Legends).
     - No special senses, natural weapons or environmental needs are stated.
  2. **Lifespan and ageing:**
     - Average lifespan 100–120 standard years (game stat: Saga Edition Core Rulebook) [Human/Legends §Life cycle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Human/Legends).
     - Hale people over 160 were known on high-technology worlds such as Bakura [Human/Legends §Life cycle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Human/Legends).
     - Forceful Humans could live up to 200 [Human/Legends §Life cycle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Human/Legends).
     - Medical treatments and organ transplants available to a rich elite could extend life to 300 [Human/Legends §Life cycle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Human/Legends).
     - One Forceful Human, the Jedi Master Shayoto, reached 800 and could still climb Mount Meru with a cane. This is an outlier, not a norm [Human/Legends §Life cycle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Human/Legends).
     - Signs of ageing: whitening or loss of hair, loss of teeth, wrinkles and dark blemishes [Human/Legends §Life cycle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Human/Legends).
     - Adolescence turns a child into an adult; the legal age of adulthood depends on culture [Human/Legends §Life cycle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Human/Legends).
     - **Era examples:**
       - Satele Shan, b. 3699 BBY, aged 38 in 3661 BBY [inferred] [Satele Shan §Early life](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satele_Shan).
       - Darth Malgus, b. 3700 BBY, aged 39 [inferred] [Darth Malgus §Early life](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Malgus).
       - Darth Marr, b. 3702 BBY, d. c. 3636 BBY, so about 66 at death [inferred] [Darth Marr](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Marr).
       - Darth Baras, 3697–3640 BBY, so 57 at death [inferred] [Darth Baras](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Baras).
       - Watcher X, b. 3685 BBY, joined Imperial Intelligence at 21 [Watcher X §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Watcher_X).
  3. **Strengths and weaknesses:**
     - None are stated as species traits.
     - Human societies can change very quickly to suit conditions [Human/Legends §Society and culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Human/Legends).
  4. **Force:** no species rate is stated. The page links long life to a strong connection with the Force [Human/Legends §Life cycle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Human/Legends).
  5. **Culture and homeworld:**
     - Homeworld unknown; most believe it was a Core World, possibly Coruscant [Human/Legends §Origin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Human/Legends).
     - They worked every profession: spacers, mercenaries, smugglers, merchants, soldiers, farmers and crime lords [Human/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Human/Legends).
     - In the Sith Empire, Humans and Purebloods were the favoured "master races" (see the cross-referenced Imperial files).
  6. **Languages:** Galactic Basic Standard and many others (infobox) [Human/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Human/Legends).
  7. **Era notes:**
     - Revan found evidence on Tatooine that Humans may have begun as slaves moved by the Rakata [Human/Legends §Origin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Human/Legends).
     - Near-Human offshoots include the Chiss [Human/Legends §Physiology](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Human/Legends).
     - The Zakuulans of the Eternal Empire are Humans (Part 2).
- **Related:** Sith; Chiss; Echani; Zakuulan; Cyborg (Part 2).
- **Uncertainty:** the 100–120 average comes from a game book; the 200 for Force-sensitives comes from The New Essential Guide to Alien Species (not a game book) [Human/Legends §Life cycle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Human/Legends).

### Sith (Sith Purebloods and Sith hybrids)
- **Type:** species (Red Sith; Purebloods are Sith-blooded near-Human descendants; "Sith hybrid" is an infobox label). **playable-ready** (Pureblood)
- **Era:** whole era; the Empire's privileged caste
- **Key facts:**
  1. **Body:**
     - The original Sith had red skin, cranial horns, pointed teeth, glowing yellow eyes, bone spurs, facial tentacles and a tendency towards left-handedness [Sith (species)/Legends §Biology and appearance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_%28species%29/Legends)[Sith (species)/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_%28species%29/Legends).
     - Massassi were taller and stronger than the other castes [Sith (species)/Legends §Castes](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_%28species%29/Legends).
     - To count as a Pureblood, one had to keep enough original traits to be visibly Sith [Sith (species)/Legends §Hybridization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_%28species%29/Legends).
     - The Purebloods of the Great Galactic War were more diluted than the ancient Naga Sadow [Sith (species)/Legends §Hybridization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_%28species%29/Legends).
     - Page height and lifespan: not given.
  2. **Lifespan and ageing:**
     - No number is given. Elderly Purebloods' skin wrinkles, pales and mottles with dark brown age spots, and their tendrils wither [Sith (species)/Legends §Hybridization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_%28species%29/Legends).
     - **Era examples:**
       - Darth Vowrawn (Pureblood), b. 3716 BBY, was 76 in 3640 BBY [Darth Rictus §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Rictus)[Darth Vowrawn](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Vowrawn) and was still active after 3629 BBY (character file), so at least 87 [inferred].
       - Lord Scourge (Pureblood), b. several decades before 3956 BBY, was made immortal by the Emperor and served him for over three centuries [Scourge (Sith)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scourge_%28Sith%29). This is unnatural; it is not a species trait.
  3. **Strengths and weaknesses:** the old Sith had sorcery [Sith (species)/Legends §Culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_%28species%29/Legends); Massassi were not very intelligent but trainable [Sith (species)/Legends §Castes](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_%28species%29/Legends).
  4. **Force:**
     - The original species was considered strongly Force-sensitive, with a symbiotic relationship to the dark side [Sith (species)/Legends §Biology and appearance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_%28species%29/Legends).
     - Purebloods were not wholly Force-sensitive, but one unable to use the Force was extremely rare [Sith (species)/Legends §Hybridization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_%28species%29/Legends)[Sith (species)/Legends §Cold War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_%28species%29/Legends).
     - Non-sensitive Purebloods were "an abomination" (see SW_Dromund_Kaas Part 2).
  5. **Culture and homeworld:**
     - Origin Korriban, later Ziost [Sith (species)/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_%28species%29/Legends).
     - Korriban was retaken by the Empire as a top priority at the war's start and was an Imperial centre of learning after the Treaty [Korriban/Legends §Great Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Korriban/Legends)[Korriban/Legends §Cold War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Korriban/Legends).
     - Purebloods were born into privilege and prepared from childhood for the Korriban academy [Sith (species)/Legends §Cold War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_%28species%29/Legends).
     - By 3642 BBY an estimated 97.8% of the Empire had some Sith ancestry, usually too little to show [Sith (species)/Legends §Hybridization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_%28species%29/Legends).
  6. **Languages:** Sith (infobox) [Sith (species)/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_%28species%29/Legends). The page does not say whether Purebloods of this era spoke Basic.
  7. **Era notes:**
     - The Emperor kept many Purebloods in his inner circle [Sith (species)/Legends §Cold War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_%28species%29/Legends).
     - **Sith hybrid:** Exal Kressh is listed as a hybrid showing only red skin, yellow eyes and Force-sensitivity [Sith (species)/Legends §Hybridization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_%28species%29/Legends). The same page's Cold War section calls her "a Pureblood" [Sith (species)/Legends §Cold War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_%28species%29/Legends) [conflict] (see Part 3).
     - No separate "Sith hybrid" page was found.
- **Related:** Human; Korriban Academy file (Purebloods favoured by Harkun; Ffon Althe).
- **Uncertainty:** [conflict] Exal Kressh's label.

### Chiss
- **Type:** species (near-Human). **playable-ready**
- **Era:** whole era; the Empire's only formal ally
- **Key facts:**
  1. **Body:**
     - Blue skin [Chiss/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chiss/Legends). A fully grown Chiss stands 1.6–2.1 m [Chiss/Legends §Biology and appearance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chiss/Legends).
     - The infobox lists glowing red eyes and above-average night vision (infobox) [Chiss/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chiss/Legends).
     - Most are in very good shape, attributed to an active metabolism [Chiss/Legends §Biology and appearance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chiss/Legends).
  2. **Lifespan and ageing:**
     - Chiss grow faster than Humans: at 10 a Chiss has the physical maturity of a 20-year-old Human [Chiss/Legends §Biology and appearance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chiss/Legends). This comes from an in-universe "University of Sanbra" article in Star Wars Gamer, not from a stat block.
     - Up to 80 years. Child 1–10, middle age 51–62, old 63–79, venerable 80 (game stat: Alien Anthology and UAA) [Chiss/Legends §Biology and appearance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chiss/Legends).
  3. **Strengths and weaknesses:** the page links fast growth to an emotional maturity "many times above" that of Humans [Chiss/Legends §Biology and appearance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chiss/Legends). No physical weakness is stated.
  4. **Force:** the few Force-sensitive Chiss could not develop their talent at home. Any Chiss found using the Force inside the Ascendancy was executed [Chiss/Legends §Chiss and the Force](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chiss/Legends).
  5. **Culture and homeworld:**
     - Csilla, in the Unknown Regions [Chiss/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chiss/Legends).
     - The Ascendancy kept a formal policy of non-aggression backed by strong defences [Chiss/Legends §The Ascendancy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chiss/Legends).
     - **In 3661 BBY:** the Chiss Ascendancy, allied to the Empire. A summit gave the Empire resources, tax revenue and armies in return for Chiss sovereignty [Csilla/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Csilla/Legends).
  6. **Languages:**
     - Cheunh; Sy Bisti, Minnisiat and other trade languages (infobox) [Chiss/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chiss/Legends)[Chiss/Legends §Language and names](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chiss/Legends).
     - The Chiss voice is close to the Human one, with differences that show when they speak their own language [Chiss/Legends §Biology and appearance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chiss/Legends).
  7. **Era notes:**
     - The only formal ally of the Empire in the Great Galactic War and the Cold War. They escaped the subjugation most alien species suffered.
     - Some served as Imperial Agents or bounty hunters, and a few rare Chiss held official Imperial military rank.
     - Chiss agents were loaned to the Imperial forces on Hoth for cold-weather training [Chiss/Legends §The Ascendancy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chiss/Legends).
     - The character files have no named Chiss with a dated role. The Human Raina Temple served in the Chiss forces [Raina Temple](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Raina_Temple).
- **Related:** Imperial Military file (Chiss in field hospitals; Hoth enclave).
- **Uncertainty:** the age bands are game stats. The "10 = 20" maturity line is in-universe prose in a game magazine.

### Twi'lek
- **Type:** species (subraces include the blue Rutian and red Lethan). **playable-ready**
- **Era:** whole era
- **Key facts:**
  1. **Body:**
     - Colourful skin. Two prehensile head-tails (lekku) hold part of the brain and store fat [Twi'lek/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Twi%27lek/Legends)[Twi'lek/Legends §Lekku](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Twi%27lek/Legends). Newborns have no lekku [Twi'lek/Legends §Lekku](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Twi%27lek/Legends).
     - Lethan (red) Twi'leks are perhaps the rarest subgroup, about one in a million; Rutian (blue) are more common [Twi'lek/Legends §Subraces](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Twi%27lek/Legends).
     - Height 1.6–2 m (game stat: Saga Edition Core Rulebook; infobox) [Twi'lek/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Twi%27lek/Legends).
     - Diet: they rarely refuse food, and starfarers often grow obese [Twi'lek/Legends §Biology and appearance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Twi%27lek/Legends).
  2. **Lifespan and ageing:** not stated on the page. No source found.
  3. **Strengths and weaknesses:** a standard humanoid voice able to learn most alien languages [Twi'lek/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Twi%27lek/Legends).
  4. **Force:** some Jedi scholars thought the lekku carry an inherent Force-sensitivity. Twi'leks had no religious faith in the Force and no native Force tradition [Twi'lek/Legends §Lekku](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Twi%27lek/Legends).
  5. **Culture and homeworld:**
     - Ryloth [Twi'lek/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Twi%27lek/Legends).
     - Slavery was the main currency of Ryloth and was tolerated by the Republic. Females were prized by slavers as dancers and status symbols [Twi'lek/Legends §Society and culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Twi%27lek/Legends).
     - **In 3661 BBY:** the Twi'lek clans regained Ryloth from the Hutts only "sometime after 3643 BBY" [Twi'lek/Legends §Homeworld reclaimed](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Twi%27lek/Legends). Hutt control in 3661 BBY is therefore [inferred].
     - **Names:** Twi'leks run names together so that they carry a meaning in Twi'leki. Wedge Antilles became "Wedgan'tilles", "Slayer of Stars" [Twi'lek/Legends §Twi'lek names](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Twi%27lek/Legends).
  6. **Languages:** Twi'leki (also called Ryl or Rylothean) mixes spoken words with lekku movement and was as widespread as Basic. Pure Lekku sign is used only by Twi'leks [Twi'lek/Legends §Language](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Twi%27lek/Legends).
  7. **Era notes:**
     - Twi'leks were among the most common Imperial slaves (see SW_Dromund_Kaas Part 2).
     - **Named examples:**
       - Vette, Rutian, b. 3664 BBY on Ryloth, a slave freed by Nok Drayen [Vette §Early life](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vette).
       - Eleena Daru, Rutian, d. 3653 BBY [Eleena Daru](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eleena_Daru).
       - Leontyne Saresh escaped Imperial slavery as a youth [Leontyne Saresh §Biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Leontyne_Saresh).
       - Zenith, b. 3678 BBY on Balmorra [Zenith](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zenith).
- **Related:** Hutt (Part 2).
- **Uncertainty:** no lifespan source.

### Zabrak
- **Type:** species (near-Human; Iridonian and other subspecies). **playable-ready**
- **Era:** whole era
- **Key facts:**
  1. **Body:**
     - Vestigial horns grow at puberty, signalling an approaching rite of passage [Zabrak/Legends §Biology and appearance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zabrak/Legends).
     - Two groups: Iridonians, with stumpy horns and Human-like features; and a group with sharper horns and red, yellow or orange skin [Zabrak/Legends §Biology and appearance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zabrak/Legends).
     - A second heart and great resistance to pain; ritual tattoos [Zabrak/Legends §Biology and appearance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zabrak/Legends).
     - Height 1.8 m (infobox) [Zabrak/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zabrak/Legends).
  2. **Lifespan and ageing:** 80 years (infobox, citing Star Wars: The Old Republic, not a tabletop book) [Zabrak/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zabrak/Legends). Maturity is marked by horn growth at puberty [Zabrak/Legends §Biology and appearance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zabrak/Legends).
  3. **Strengths and weaknesses:**
     - A fierce sense of self-determination and independence [Zabrak/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zabrak/Legends).
     - Young Zabraks had to learn a highly physical martial art [Zabrak/Legends §Personality](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zabrak/Legends).
     - Their strong will let them resist Imperial occupation [Zabrak/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zabrak/Legends).
  4. **Force:** no rate is stated. Jedi example: Kao Cen Darach, Satele Shan's master [Zabrak/Legends §Zabrak in the galaxy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zabrak/Legends).
  5. **Culture and homeworld:**
     - Iridonia (Mid Rim) [Zabrak/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zabrak/Legends).
     - **In 3661 BBY:** contested. The Empire and Republic fought fiercely over Iridonia, and both used it as a staging ground [Iridonia/Legends §Wars of the Sith and Mandalorians](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Iridonia/Legends).
     - Imperial scouts after 4980 BBY found isolated Zabrak colonies and valued them as slaves. Later, as Imperial prejudice relaxed, many served openly as Sith, soldiers and diplomats [Zabrak/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zabrak/Legends).
     - After the war the two groups were split between Republic and Imperial territory [Zabrak/Legends §Biology and appearance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zabrak/Legends).
  6. **Languages:** Zabraki and Basic (infobox) [Zabrak/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zabrak/Legends).
  7. **Era notes:**
     - **Named examples:**
       - Kao Cen Darach, d. 3681 BBY [Kao Cen Darach](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kao_Cen_Darach).
       - Akaavi Spar, b. 3670 BBY on Kamar [Akaavi Spar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Akaavi_Spar).
       - Jewl'a Nightbringer, d. 3641 BBY [Jewl'a Nightbringer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jewl%27a_Nightbringer).
     - Zabraks were favoured in Imperial field hospitals and were common slaves (cross-referenced files).
- **Related:** Imperial files.
- **Uncertainty:** the lifespan comes from video-game codex text.

### Mirialan
- **Type:** species (near-Human). **thin** (no height or lifespan)
- **Era:** whole era
- **Key facts:**
  1. **Body:** green skin, great agility and very quick reflexes [Mirialan/Legends §Biology](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mirialan/Legends)[Mirialan/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mirialan/Legends).
  2. **Lifespan and ageing:**
     - No number found.
     - Facial and hand tattoos mark tests or tasks completed, so the number of tattoos shows how mature or skilled a Mirialan is [Mirialan/Legends §Society and culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mirialan/Legends).
  3. **Strengths and weaknesses:** flexible, agile, fast and formidable [Mirialan/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mirialan/Legends).
  4. **Force:** deeply religious with a basic understanding of the Force. Each act ripples through the Force and shapes the species' destiny; the Force is understood as fate [Mirialan/Legends §Society and culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mirialan/Legends).
  5. **Culture and homeworld:**
     - Mirial [Mirialan/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mirialan/Legends).
     - Mirial sided with the Sith during the Jedi Civil War and returned to the Republic after Malak's death [Mirial/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mirial/Legends).
     - **In 3661 BBY:** a refuge for Outer Rim refugees from the war's start [Mirial/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mirial/Legends)[Mirialan/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mirialan/Legends). Republic alignment is [inferred].
  6. **Languages:** Mirialan and Basic (infobox) [Mirialan/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mirialan/Legends).
  7. **Era notes:**
     - In the Cold War a tectonic gap on Mirial revealed an underground city [Mirial/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mirial/Legends).
     - **Named examples:**
       - Hylo Visz led the smugglers who broke a Mandalorian blockade to supply Coruscant [Mirialan/Legends §Mirialans in the galaxy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mirialan/Legends)[Hylo Visz](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hylo_Visz).
       - Galena Rans, later Chancellor [Galena Rans](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galena_Rans).
- **Related:** Nautolan, Cathar (other Republic species).
- **Uncertainty:** none beyond the [inferred] alignment.

### Miraluka
- **Type:** species (near-Human, Force-sensitive). **playable-ready**
- **Era:** whole era
- **Key facts:**
  1. **Body:**
     - No working eyes. They perceive through Force sight instead and can "see" the Force radiating from Jedi and Sith [Miraluka/Legends §Biology and appearance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Miraluka/Legends).
     - Height 1.8 m (game stat: Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide; infobox) [Miraluka/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Miraluka/Legends).
  2. **Lifespan and ageing:** up to 84 years (game stat: UAA; infobox) [Miraluka/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Miraluka/Legends). No prose figure.

### Cerean
- **Type:** species. **playable-ready**
- **Era:** whole era
- **Key facts:**
  1. **Body:** tall, tapering skulls up to 20 cm above the forehead hold binary brains fed by an extra heart [Cerean/Legends §Biology and appearance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cerean/Legends). Height 2 m (infobox) [Cerean/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cerean/Legends).
  2. **Lifespan and ageing:** adult 15–49, old 61–69, venerable 70+ (game stat: UAA) [Cerean/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cerean/Legends).
  3. **Strengths and weaknesses:** extraordinary thinking; they can focus on many things at once [Cerean/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cerean/Legends).
  4. **Force:** about as common as in baseline Humans. Their binary brains may let some explore both light and dark [gray: cited to the Force and Destiny Core Rulebook, 2015] [Cerean/Legends §Biology and appearance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cerean/Legends).
  5. **Culture and homeworld:**
     - Cerea: a low-tech, isolationist world where offworld technology is allowed only in Outsider Citadels.
     - The government is matriarchal [Cerean/Legends §Society and culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cerean/Legends).
     - Control in 3661 BBY: not stated.
  6. **Languages:** Cerean and Basic (infobox; origin and language cite Force and Destiny [gray]) [Cerean/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cerean/Legends).
  7. **Era notes:** Paran Am-Ris was active c. 3653 BBY [Paran Am-Ris](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Paran_Am-Ris).
- **Related:** none.
- **Uncertainty:** [gray] on the Force rate and language. No pre-2014 Legends backing was found for the Force rate.

### Nautolan
- **Type:** species (amphibious). **playable-ready**
- **Era:** whole era
- **Key facts:**
  1. **Body:**
     - Average 1.8 m, with green, blue, purple or brown skin and large dark eyes [Nautolan/Legends §Biology and appearance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nautolan/Legends). Mass 75 kg (game stat: Clone Wars Campaign Guide; infobox) [Nautolan/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nautolan/Legends).
     - Low-light vision. A tough skeleton resists glancing blows. They breathe underwater and manage fine out of it [Nautolan/Legends §Biology and appearance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nautolan/Legends).
  2. **Lifespan and ageing:** up to 70 years (game stat: Clone Wars Campaign Guide; infobox) [Nautolan/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nautolan/Legends).
  3. **Strengths and weaknesses:** exceptional smell and pheromone sense through the head-tendrils, which read others' emotions, best underwater [Nautolan/Legends §Biology and appearance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nautolan/Legends).
  4. **Force:** no Force culture on Glee Anselm, but Forceful Nautolans are respected [Nautolan/Legends §Society and culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nautolan/Legends). Their "empathy" is the pheromone sense above, not a Force power.
  5. **Culture and homeworld:** Glee Anselm. Control in 3661 BBY: not stated.
  6. **Languages:** their own language cannot be fully spoken in air, so off-world they use Anselmian or Basic [Nautolan/Legends §Biology and appearance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nautolan/Legends).
  7. **Era notes:**
     - The Gynt family was a Nautolan Jedi dynasty before the war [Nautolan/Legends §Nautolans in the galaxy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nautolan/Legends).
     - **Named examples:** Bengel Morr [Bengel Morr](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bengel_Morr); Leeha Narezz [Leeha Narezz](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Leeha_Narezz).
- **Related:** Jedi Temple Life file.
- **Uncertainty:** none.

### Devaronian
- **Type:** species. **playable-ready** (no lifespan)
- **Era:** whole era
- **Key facts:**
  1. **Body:**
     - Males: red-tinted skin and large horns, aggressive by nature. Females: thick fur, no horns, prominent canine teeth [Devaronian/Legends §Biology and appearance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Devaronian/Legends).
     - Height 1.6 m (game stat: UAA; infobox) [Devaronian/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Devaronian/Legends).
  2. **Lifespan and ageing:** not stated. No source found. **Era example:** Gault Rennow, b. 3697 BBY, so 36 in 3661 BBY [inferred] [Gault Rennow](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gault_Rennow).
  3. **Strengths and weaknesses:** their bodies constantly purge toxins, so they are highly resistant to poison. Sulfur works on them as a stimulant [Devaronian/Legends §Biology and appearance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Devaronian/Legends).
  4. **Force:** Force-sensitivity is found more often among Devaronians than in other species [Devaronian/Legends §Society and culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Devaronian/Legends) (Nexus of Power, a reference book).
  5. **Culture and homeworld:** Devaron. Control in 3661 BBY: not stated.
  6. **Languages:** Basic and Devaronese (infobox) [Devaronian/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Devaronian/Legends).
  7. **Era notes:** in 3661 BBY a Jedi strike force was wiped out near Devaron trying to break the Mandalorian blockade of the Hydian Way [Battle near Devaron](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_near_Devaron). Gault took part in breaking that blockade in 3660 BBY [Gault Rennow](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gault_Rennow).
- **Related:** Mirialan (Hylo Visz).
- **Uncertainty:** none.
