# Dark Side: Fall, Corruption and Redemption, Part 2 of 3 (1000 BBY–4 ABY)

## Summary
This part covers the Rule of Two, the Clone Wars and the Empire, up to Endor (4 ABY). Its main cases are Anakin Skywalker, Dooku, Quinlan Vos, Depa Billaba, Asajj Ventress, Komari Vosa and Jerec, with the redemptions of Vader, Ventress and Vos. Key themes are Vaapad as a controlled use of darkness, the Mustafar duel, and the debate over what disfigured Sidious. For Vader's "percentage of potential lost", see SW_Midichlorians_Force_Potential.md, §6. It is not repeated here.

**Era boundaries:** Part 1 runs to 1000 BBY. Part 2 runs 1000 BBY–4 ABY. Part 3 runs from 4 ABY onward. Each part keeps all eight sections.

**Cross-references:** SW_Sith_Training_AllEras_Part2.md (Eras 7–8: Rule of Two, Dark Acolytes, Inquisitors); SW_Jedi_Training_AllEras_Part2.md; SW_Lightsabers_Crystals_Materials_AllEras.md; SW_Midichlorians_Force_Potential.md.

## Section 1: How falling works (1000 BBY–4 ABY)

### The chain from attachment to hate
- **Type:** concept
- **Era:** Clone Wars sources (22–19 BBY)
- **Key facts:**
  - Fear is "most often the starting point" and spreads like a plague. The chain runs from attachment to fear of loss, then jealousy, then hate (ref: TCW "Overlords"; *ROTS* novelization; *Labyrinth of Evil*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("Allure of the dark side")
  - Falls often follow the death of a loved one: Anakin, Shaela Nuur and Kenobi. Kenobi shows such a temptation can be rejected. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("Allure of the dark side")
  - Some fall after enduring agony. Komari Vosa, already "on the dark path" after being passed over for knighthood, succumbed while tortured in captivity by the Bando Gora. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("Allure of the dark side"; "Bando Gora")
- **Related:** Anakin and Ventress (Section 2)
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Single act or pattern: the Clone Wars evidence
- **Type:** synthesis
- **Era:** 22–19 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Pattern:** Anakin's fall ran about three years from the Tusken massacre to Mustafar (Section 2). Sidious's plan to turn him through fear of losing his wife took a decade. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Duel_on_Mustafar/Legends) ("Prelude")
  - **The decisive act:** Anakin believed his part in Windu's death ruled out any return (Section 2). Kenobi refused to kill the maimed Vader because even a mercy killing would start him "down the dark path" (ref: *Jedi vs. Sith*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Duel_on_Mustafar/Legends) ("Vader's defeat")
  - **Shim on infiltration:** her stages warn that infiltrating the Sith or trying to save loved ones from death are classic ways famous Jedi explained away their acts. These fit Quinlan Vos and Anakin [inferred]. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("Stage 3: Submission")
- **Related:** Part 1, Section 1 (the Four Stages)
- **Uncertainty:** none

## Section 2: Case studies of falls (1000 BBY–4 ABY)

Training details for Dooku, Ventress, the Dark Acolytes and the Inquisitors are in SW_Sith_Training_AllEras_Part2.md (Eras 7 and 8) and are not repeated here.

### Dooku / Darth Tyranus
- **Type:** fall case (no redemption)
- **Era:** 52–32 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Early brush:** Plagueis had noticed him after his "brush with the dark side" during the kidnapping and death of Senator Blix Annon (ref: *Darth Plagueis*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dooku/Legends) ("In the sights of the Sith")
  - **Step 1, Galidraan (44 BBY):** eleven Jedi died after the governor manipulated the task force. On Council orders, Dooku handed Jango Fett over. Learning how the Jedi had been used "deeply offended" him (ref: *Jedi vs. Sith*; *Open Seasons*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dooku/Legends) ("Galidraan")
  - **Step 2, Baltizaar:** after Jedi losses there he protested that the Council was spending Jedi lives for political reasons. He withdrew, refused missions, and brooded on prophecies of dark times (ref: *Labyrinth of Evil*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dooku/Legends) ("Galidraan"; "Leaving the Order")
  - **Belief:** he "flirted with the belief that the dark side of the Force could be called upon without personal corruption" (ref: *Star Wars Roleplaying Game Revised Core Rulebook*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dooku/Legends) ("Leaving the Order")
  - **Grooming:** from 52 BBY Palpatine, the future Sidious, became a confidant who listened to his complaints (ref: *Darth Plagueis*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dooku/Legends) ("In the sights of the Sith")
  - **Trigger (32 BBY):** after Qui-Gon's death he left the Order. He held the Council at least as responsible as the Sith, and sought an alliance rather than revenge. Sidious found him "surprisingly easy" to convince, and at seventy he left for the dark side (ref: *Jedi vs. Sith*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dooku/Legends) ("In the sights of the Sith"; "Embracing the Sith teachings")
  - **Time to fall:** about twelve years from Galidraan (44 BBY) to the turn (32 BBY), with roughly twenty years of grooming since 52 BBY [inferred from the dates above].
  - **Body:** he showed no physical degeneration; his eyes turned yellow only in anger. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("Corruption of the dark side")
  - **Lost Twenty:** he became one of the "Lost Twenty", Masters who left the Order voluntarily. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dooku/Legends) ("Leaving the Order")
- **Related:** Sith all-eras Part2, Era 7
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Anakin Skywalker / Darth Vader
- **Type:** fall and redemption case
- **Era:** 22–19 BBY (fall); 4 ABY (redemption)
- **Key facts:**
  - **Step 1, the Tusken massacre (22 BBY):** after months of nightmares about his mother, he went to Tatooine. When Shmi died in his arms, her death set off "an explosion of his long-nursed anger, fear, and greed": he slaughtered the whole Tusken camp, women and children included. Yoda and Qui-Gon's spirit felt a presence that "bordered on the dark side". He first justified the killing, then broke down in remorse (ref: Film II). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Anakin_Skywalker/Legends) ("Return to Tatooine")
  - **Step 2, a hidden sin:** Padmé told no one about the massacre. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Anakin_Skywalker/Legends) ("Return to Tatooine")
  - **Step 3, executing Dooku (19 BBY):** he unleashed "all his fear, anger and hatred" in the duel. When Palpatine ordered it, he hesitated, then executed the disarmed Dooku out of anger and a desire for revenge (ref: Film III). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Anakin_Skywalker/Legends) ("Rescuing the Chancellor")
  - **Step 4, fear of loss:** visions of Padmé dying, distrust of the Council, and Yoda's "let go" advice, which did not satisfy him. Palpatine used his fear with the story of Plagueis (ref: Film III; ROTS novelization). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Anakin_Skywalker/Legends) ("Fall to the dark side")
  - **Step 5, the betrayal:** he cut off Mace Windu's hand to save Sidious. Struck with guilt, but "too emotionally drained and dazed to resist", he believed his part in Windu's death ruled out any return. He knelt as Sidious's apprentice (ref: Film III; *Dark Lord: The Rise of Darth Vader*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Anakin_Skywalker/Legends) ("A fateful choice")
  - **Analysis:** Jedi texts tie his fall to attachment and fear of loss (ref: TCW "Overlords"; ROTS; *Labyrinth of Evil*). Tolaris Shim's Stage 2 cites him: he accepted the dark side as a power for good and was "completely given over to it" (ref: *Jedi vs. Sith*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("Allure of the dark side"; "Stage 2: Imperilment")
  - **Time to fall:** about three years from the first dark act (22 BBY) to the fall (19 BBY). The final turn took less than a day, from Windu's death to the Temple march [inferred from the film sequence on the page].
  - **Mustafar:** his blue eyes turned the "red-rimmed yellow of a Sith Lord". Burned, he kept himself alive "with the dark side of the Force, his unbreakable will, and sheer hatred". The Emperor had him kept awake through surgery so the pain would feed his rage and power (ref: Film III). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Anakin_Skywalker/Legends) ("Anakin's immolation and Vader's rise")
  - **Lost potential:** for the "percentage of potential lost" question, see SW_Midichlorians_Force_Potential.md.
  - **Redemption (4 ABY):** watching Sidious torture Luke, his compassion and love for his son returned. He threw Sidious down the reactor shaft and asked Luke to remove his mask, a "final rejection" of the dark side (ref: Film VI; ROTJ novelization). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Anakin_Skywalker/Legends) ("Redemption")
  - **Belief beforehand:** he had believed himself beyond redemption. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Anakin_Skywalker/Legends) ("Redemption")
  - **Cost:** his life.
  - **Body after redemption:** unmasked, his skin had been "paled... white" by years in the dark side, but his eyes were blue again. He appeared to Luke as a Jedi spirit. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Anakin_Skywalker/Legends) ("Redemption")
  - **Time to redemption:** minutes at Endor, 23 years after Mustafar.
  - **Tolaris Shim conflict:** her thesis says a Jedi who kills innocents under the dark side cannot be redeemed. The page counts Anakin's ghost as disproof. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("Stage 4: Redemption/Atonement")
- **Related:** Sith all-eras Part2, Era 7
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Quinlan Vos
- **Type:** fall and return (an infiltration that went too far)
- **Era:** 22–19 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **The plan:** under orders from Tholme and part of the Council, he infiltrated Dooku's inner circle, believing that killing Dooku would end the Sith (ref: *Jedi: Shaak Ti*; *Book of Sith*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Quinlan_Vos/Legends) ("Double agent")
  - **Pressure:** Dooku played on his "dark side weakness" and drew him "closer to the shadows" than ever before. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Quinlan_Vos/Legends) ("Double agent")
  - **Deeds:** for his "homicidal service" he became a Dark Acolyte and received Andeddu's crystal (ref: *Republic* 63). He executed Kh'aris Fenn, "each murder further immersing himself in the dark side", until most Jedi believed he had fallen (ref: *Republic* 66). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Quinlan_Vos/Legends) ("Dooku's servant")
  - **Ambiguity:** he told Aayla he had not truly turned, but then threatened to kill her over the disk. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Quinlan_Vos/Legends) ("Dooku's servant")
  - **The crisis:** on learning there was no second Sith he pushed Skorr into lava in anger. Sora Bulq's Vaapad "did indeed open him to the dark side". Aayla and Tholme reminded him of the light. He then gave himself completely to Vaapad, turning his anger into the "superconducting loop" and finding "a strange calmness" (ref: *Republic* 76–77). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Quinlan_Vos/Legends) ("Dance with the dark side")
  - **Time:** about two years undercover (c. 21–19 BBY) [inferred]. Luke Skywalker's page draws the parallel: like Vos, Luke could not break free once immersed too deeply. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Luke_Skywalker/Legends) ("Return of the Emperor")
  - **Afterwards:** he won back Jeisel's respect and planned to leave the Order for Khaleen Hentz and their child (ref: *Republic* 77). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Quinlan_Vos/Legends) ("Dance with the dark side")
- **Related:** Section 5 (Vaapad)
- **Uncertainty:** duration [inferred]

### Depa Billaba
- **Type:** fall case, ending in a coma
- **Era:** early Clone Wars (Haruun Kal)
- **Key facts:**
  - **Pressure:** out of contact for weeks while leading Korunnai militia on Haruun Kal, she was "plagued by nightmares and headaches" from the atrocities she witnessed. Under the influence of Kar Vastor's blood lust she "cracked" and is believed to have fallen (ref: *Shatterpoint*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Depa_Billaba/Legends) ("Summertime War")
  - **Time to fall:** weeks to months in the jungle war [inferred].
  - **Body:** a Force vision, later "largely true", showed her extremely skinny with sunken cheeks and hacked-short hair. Mace described her as frail and weakened. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Depa_Billaba/Legends) ("Summertime War")
  - **Deeds:** she and Vastor burned Pelek Baw and slaughtered civilians. She stabbed Windu, attacked him with Vaapad, and "fed off the death" of her own guards. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Depa_Billaba/Legends) ("Summertime War")
  - **End:** when the dark power she drew on faded she came out of "her madness", realized what she had done and tried to kill herself. Nick Rostu stopped her and she fell into a coma (ref: *Shatterpoint*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Depa_Billaba/Legends) ("Summertime War")
  - **Vaapad:** Windu's page notes that the only two other advanced Vaapad users, Billaba and Sora Bulq, both fell. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mace_Windu/Legends) ("Force Mastery")
- **Related:** Section 5 (Vaapad); Section 6 (madness)
- **Uncertainty:** the page carries a {{Citation}} banner, and "it is believed" she fell [unclear]

### Jerec
- **Type:** fall case (no redemption)
- **Era:** after 19 BBY to c. 5 ABY
- **Key facts:**
  - **The turn:** returning from the Unknown Regions after Order 66, he came to the attention of High Inquisitor Tremayne, and was turned by the Inquisitorius (see Sith all-eras Part2, Era 9). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jerec_%28Inquisitor%29) ("The turn")
  - **Appearance:** Sith tattoos around his mouth by 0 BBY, which he very probably did not have as a Jedi (ref: *Soldier for the Empire*). He was emaciated early in the Galactic Civil War, for unclear reasons. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jerec_%28Inquisitor%29) ("Appearance")
  - **End:** he drew on the Valley of the Jedi's power and "leeched" the captive spirits. He refused Katarn's mercy and was killed. His body dissolved into dark-side energy. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jerec_%28Inquisitor%29) ("Apotheosis"); [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("Death of dark side users")
- **Related:** Kyle Katarn
- **Uncertainty:** the length of Jerec's turn is not stated

### Asajj Ventress
- **Type:** fall and partial redemption
- **Era:** before the Clone Wars to 19 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Trigger:** her master Ky Narec's death sent her into darkness. Consumed by grief, she gave in to rage and vengeance, became a warlord on Rattatak, and blamed the Jedi for abandoning her master (ref: *The Clone Wars* novelization). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Asajj_Ventress/Legends) ("Personality and traits")
  - **Pressure:** Dooku recruited her as a Sith aspirant. She stayed angry and in pain and came to resent Dooku for not letting her join the Sith (ref: *Yoda: Dark Rendezvous*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Asajj_Ventress/Legends) ("Personality and traits")
  - **Deeds:** she killed Padawan Jang Li-Li in 19 BBY and kept a tally of dead Jedi (ref: *Yoda: Dark Rendezvous*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Asajj_Ventress/Legends) ("Vjun")
  - **Return (19 BBY):** abandoned by Dooku and mortally wounded by Anakin at Boz Pity, she told Kenobi to protect the Core from Dooku "and finally let go of her anger and rage". She survived in a Sith healing trance and fled the war, the Jedi and Dooku. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Asajj_Ventress/Legends) ("Battle of Boz Pity")
- **Related:** Section 3
- **Uncertainty:** the page carries a {{TCWRetcon}} banner (Legends material later overlaid by TCW). Her time to fall is not stated.

### Komari Vosa
- **Type:** fall case
- **Era:** before 22 BBY
- **Key facts:** Dooku's Padawan was passed over for knighthood, was "already on the dark path", and succumbed to the dark side under torture by the Bando Gora. She then killed her captors and fellow Jedi captives and took over the cult. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("Bando Gora")
- **Related:** Dooku
- **Uncertainty:** exact date not on the page

## Section 5: The dark side as a power source (1000 BBY–4 ABY)

### Combat boost and its limits
- **Type:** concept with examples
- **Era:** 22 BBY–4 ABY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Gains:** the dark side is quick to aid in combat. Ventress fended off three Jedi on Ruul, Sidious quickly killed three Council members, and Vader marched on the Temple. Ferocity, and perhaps the lack of moral limits, are offered as explanations (ref: Film V; *Jedi: Mace Windu*; TCW "Cloak of Darkness"; Film III). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("Strength of the dark side")
  - **The light/dark difference:** the dark side gives direct physical power; the light gives focus and understanding (ref: *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("Strength of the dark side")
  - **Weakness:** anger, pride and fear cloud judgment. Kenobi beat both Maul and Vader, and Dooku's pride cost him (ref: Film III; *ROTS* novelization). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("Strength of the dark side")
  - **Healing is temporary:** dark-side healing lasts only while the user keeps channelling (Vader's lungs, Section 4). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("Strength of the dark side")
- **Related:** Part 1, Section 5
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Luke versus Vader at Endor (4 ABY)
- **Type:** example
- **Era:** 4 ABY
- **Key facts:**
  - Luke drew "on his rage to bolster his combat effectiveness for a short time". [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("Strength of the dark side")
  - When Vader threatened Leia, Luke attacked furiously with Djem So and cut off Vader's hand. He then threw away his lightsaber, "realizing that he was becoming what he sought to destroy" (ref: Film VI). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Luke_Skywalker/Legends) ("The test of Endor")
  - He showed no physical corruption. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("Corruption of the dark side")
- **Related:** Section 3 (Vader)
- **Uncertainty:** none

### Vaapad: using darkness without falling
- **Type:** combat form
- **Era:** developed by Windu and Sora Bulq before the Clone Wars (ref: *Shatterpoint*)
- **Key facts:**
  - Vaapad leads "through the penumbra of the dark side". The user must enjoy the fight and relish winning, taking in the opponent's fury to become half of a "superconducting loop" (ref: *Jedi vs. Sith*). Jedi warned that it led perilously close to the dark side (ref: *Star Wars Insider* 62). It also needs a constant, sizable stream of Force use. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VII/Legends) ("Description"; "Vaapad")
  - **Windu vs Sidious (19 BBY):** Windu gave himself to the "essentials" of Vaapad, enjoying the thrill and fighting on instinct, and the redirected lightning melted Sidious's features (ref: Film III). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mace_Windu/Legends) ("Final battle and death")
  - **The cost to others:** only Windu fully mastered it, and Vos came a close second. The only two other advanced users, Depa Billaba and Sora Bulq, fell. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mace_Windu/Legends) ("Lightsaber training"; "Force Mastery")
  - **Dooku's view:** Vaapad skirts the dark side and stays weak unless it uses the dark side directly. He subverted Bulq, who claimed to have perfected it after his fall and fought Windu to a stalemate (ref: *Republic: Trackdown*; *Jedi: Mace Windu*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Form_VII/Legends) ("Vaapad")
  - **Vos vs Bulq:** Bulq's Vaapad "did indeed open" Vos to the dark side, but Vos finally mastered the light–dark flow (ref: *Republic* 77). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Quinlan_Vos/Legends) ("Dance with the dark side")
- **Related:** Part 1, Section 1 (Juyo); SW_Lightsabers_Crystals_Materials_AllEras.md
- **Uncertainty:** [conflict] **Is Vaapad a Jedi discipline or a path to the dark side?** The Jedi view (Windu, *Jedi vs. Sith*) says it channels darkness safely. Dooku and Bulq say it needs the dark side, and two of its three other masters fell. Best fit: a light-side form whose safety depends on the user, and very risky. The Gray Jedi page cites Windu's Vaapad as proof that using dark-flavoured techniques does not make someone Gray. Developer's call. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gray_Jedi) ("Traits and techniques")

### Anakin on Mustafar (19 BBY)
- **Type:** example
- **Era:** 19 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - Sidious had him massacre the Temple "younglings and all" to become strong enough. Vader became "mad with power", "no longer feared the dark side", and planned to overthrow Palpatine. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Duel_on_Mustafar/Legends) ("Prelude")
  - His ferocious Djem So assault was "degenerating into total madness". "Blinded by rage and arrogance", he leapt at Kenobi on the high ground and lost his limbs. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Duel_on_Mustafar/Legends) ("The duel"; "Vader's defeat")
  - Afterwards, the pain of his surgery was used to feed his rage and power (ref: Film III). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Anakin_Skywalker/Legends) ("Anakin's immolation and Vader's rise")
- **Related:** SW_Midichlorians_Force_Potential.md, §6 (how much potential Vader lost)
- **Uncertainty:** none

## Section 6: Madness (1000 BBY–4 ABY)

### Cases, 1000 BBY–4 ABY
- **Type:** examples
- **Era:** c. 533 BBY–4 ABY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Darth Gravid:** his alignment with the light side "eventually drove him insane" (Light side page). Light can also unbalance a user. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Light_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("Nature of the light side")
  - **Vjun:** became a dark-side nexus after a slaughter in 29 BBY driven by midi-chlorian madness (ref: *Yoda: Dark Rendezvous*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("Manifestations of the dark side")
  - **Depa Billaba:** nightmares, headaches and Vastor's blood lust "cracked" her. When the dark power faded she came out of "her madness". [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Depa_Billaba/Legends) ("Summertime War")
  - **Vader:** the dark side brings distrust, quick anger and, in Vader's case, paranoia (ref: *ROTS* novelization). On Mustafar his fighting was "degenerating into total madness". [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("Corruption of the dark side"); [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Duel_on_Mustafar/Legends) ("The duel")
- **Related:** Part 3, Section 6
- **Uncertainty:** [conflict] **Does the dark side cause madness, or worsen it?** The DS page says the dark-side users who went insane were all disturbed or traumatized before they fell, and the dark side made it worse (ref: *ROTS* novelization; *Dark Forces II*; *LotF: Tempest*). Depa's case fits this, since trauma came first. Best fit: the dark side amplifies an existing instability. Developer's call. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force/Legends) ("Corruption of the dark side")

## Section 8: Light-side counterparts and walking between the sides (1000 BBY–4 ABY)

### Qui-Gon Jinn
- **Type:** dissident Jedi sometimes called Gray
- **Era:** c. 44–32 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - An adherent of the Living Force who kept his focus on the moment and often clashed with, or openly defied, the Council. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Qui-Gon_Jinn/Legends) (intro)
  - Some in the Order thought him Gray because of these disagreements. Tyvokka was concerned, and Kenobi admitted he could see why (ref: *Star Wars* (1998) 36; *The Jedi Path*). This was disagreement, "not necessarily" dark-side experimentation. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gray_Jedi) ("New Sith Wars – Second Imperial Civil War")
- **Related:** none
- **Uncertainty:** none

### The Potentium
- **Type:** heretical philosophy and sect
- **Era:** founded by Leor Hal before 132 BBY; expelled about a century before Naboo (c. 132 BBY)
- **Key facts:**
  - Its followers held that the Force is a single whole and good, and that the dark side is a perversion by its users. The potential for light and dark lies in the user, not the Force (ref: *Jedi vs. Sith*; *The Jedi Path*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Potentium) (intro; "Beliefs")
  - The Council did not call them Dark Jedi, but said studying the Force's absolute limits leads to the dark side. Some followers believed lightning and Force-choking were fine with good intentions (ref: *Power of the Jedi Sourcebook*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Potentium) ("Beliefs")
  - Qui-Gon and Windu once ran into a group of Potentium apprentices (ref: *Rogue Planet*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Potentium) ("Followers")
- **Related:** Part 3, Section 8 (heretical by 43 ABY)
- **Uncertainty:** none

### The Gray Paladins
- **Type:** dissident sect
- **Era:** before 19 BBY to the early Empire
- **Key facts:**
  - An offshoot of the Teepo Paladins, censured by the Council for promoting blasters alongside lightsabers. Teepo's followers were shunned as possible dark siders. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gray_Paladin) ("History")
  - They argued for minimal dependence on the Force. It was a secondary aid that guided their actions, while they relied on martial skill; Laranth Tarak is the example. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gray_Paladin) (intro; "Philosophy"; "Powers and abilities")
  - The Gray Jedi page says they otherwise held to the Jedi Code. "Gray" here describes their methods, not dark-side use. [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gray_Jedi) (intro)
- **Related:** none
- **Uncertainty:** the Gray Paladin page's paragraphs carry few inline footnotes. The facts are cited to the page sections.

### The Jensaarai (origin)
- **Type:** gray sect
- **Era:** c. 19 BBY
- **Key facts:** created by accident after Nejaa Halcyon's strike on the fallen Jedi Nikkos Tyris, who studied the writings of Larad Noon, one of Kun's followers. The survivors could not interpret Noon's dark-side teachings and developed their own tradition (ref: *I, Jedi*). [source](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gray_Jedi) ("New Sith Wars – Second Imperial Civil War")
- **Related:** Part 3, Section 8 (Corran Horn)

## Game notes (suggestions only; not lore)
- **Fall speed (suggestion):** this era shows the full range, from a slow grooming of a decade or more (Dooku; Sidious's plan for Anakin) to a final turn inside a day (Anakin). A campaign could track slow "pressure" and allow a sudden "tipping act".
- **Deeds that matter most (suggestion):** killing the defenceless (the Tuskens, the Temple), betraying an ally to save a loved one (Windu), and murders committed "for the mission" (Vos). These could count heavily even when the motive is good, since Shim's Stage 3 warns of exactly this.
- **Vaapad-style rules (suggestion):** a light-side character could draw on darkness for a combat bonus at a rising risk, with failure meaning a fall (Depa, Bulq) and success meaning control (Windu, Vos).
- **Staging corruption (suggestion):** Dooku and Starkiller show "eyes only when angry" as a reversible early stage; Vader shows permanent pallor with eyes that return at redemption.
- **Redemption (suggestion):** a dying act of love (Vader) or the dark power fading (Depa) as triggers; leave a scar of guilt or pallor.
