# SWRPG Galaxy Timeline, 999–33 BBY (Part 3 of 3): The Valorum Years, Yinchorr and Eriadu

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 24, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends), read through the MediaWiki API (current revisions, plus revisions from before August 2008 where the developer asked for them), as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
Part 3 covers 39–33 BBY: Valorum's two terms, Maul's training and first missions, the Yinchorri Uprising and the Nebula Front crisis that ends at the Eriadu Trade Summit (dated 32.5 BBY by *The New Essential Chronology*, and treated here as the last event before the Naboo crisis). It then holds Changes from the previous file, Not found, Canon only, Not used, Game notes, Decisions needed, Sources and the checks. The column rules are those of Part 1.

## THE VALORUM YEARS — 39–33 BBY
## ════════════════════════════════════════════

### Overview
Valorum was re-elected in 36 BBY by a slim margin; many senators doubted him, opposition parties drew up lists of possible replacements, many naming Palpatine, one of his oldest friends, and Valorum came to lean on Palpatine's advice [Supreme Chancellor election, 36 BBY/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Supreme_Chancellor_election,_36_BBY/Legends). The Sith used these years to weaken him. In 33 BBY Plagueis and Sidious pushed the Yinchorri into a war that killed seven Jedi, one of them a Council member, and damaged Valorum badly [Yinchorri Uprising](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yinchorri_Uprising). Then the Trade Federation pressed the Senate to let it arm its ships; on Palpatine's advice Valorum would agree only if the Senate could tax the free-trade routes, and Palpatine proposed a summit on Eriadu to settle it [Eriadu Trade Summit §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eriadu_Trade_Summit). The radical Nebula Front, shut out of the summit, was secretly led by a man working for Wilhuff Tarkin and "darker powers": its staged attacks on Valorum covered the murder of the Trade Federation's non-Neimoidian directors, which put Nute Gunray in charge and set up the blockade of Naboo [Eriadu Trade Summit §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eriadu_Trade_Summit); [Trade Federation Directorate](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trade_Federation_Directorate). At the same time Sidious made his first attempt on his Master's life, through King Ars Veruna's nuclear attack on Sojourn [Palpatine/Legends §Rise of Senator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Palpatine/Legends).
