# Star Wars Legends: The Great Galactic War (3681–3653 BBY), Part 2: 3666–3660 BBY

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 21, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
- **Hoth:** the 3665 BBY Imperial victory destroyed the Republic's prototype fleet. [Battle of Hoth (Great Galactic War)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Hoth_%28Great_Galactic_War%29)
- **Hydian Way:** Imperial Intelligence's Mandalorian puppet blockaded the Hydian Way (3661–3660 BBY), starving Coruscant until Hylo Visz's smugglers and the Republic broke it. [Blockade of the Hydian Way](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way)
- **Rim Campaign:** the Empire's c. 3661–3660 BBY campaign, from Ord Radama to Serenno, pushed the Republic out of the Esstran sector. [Rim Campaign](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rim_Campaign)
- **This part also covers:** undated battles, Mandalorian involvement, other factions and daily life.

**Scope and splits:**
- Part 1 covers 3681–3667 BBY plus the war overview, front lines, the Jedi at war and the Sith at war.
- Part 2 covers 3666–3660 BBY, undated battles, Mandalorians, other factions and daily life.
- Part 3 covers 3659–3653 BBY, the peace process, the Sacking, immediate effects and "who was where".

### Mandalorian involvement (whole war)
- **Type:** faction at war
- **Era:** 3681–3653 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **3667 BBY:**
    - Imperial Intelligence made a Geonosis gladiator Mandalore (Mandalore the Lesser) as a Sith puppet. [Mandalore the Lesser §Ascension](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalore_the_Lesser); [Great Galactic War §New strategies](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Galactic_War)
    - One Mandalorian, Shae Vizla, fought with Malgus at Alderaan. [Battle of Alderaan (infobox)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Alderaan); [Mandalore the Avenger §Work for the Sith Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalore_the_Avenger)
  - **c. 3664 BBY:** Vizla's brother was killed attacking a Republic envoy. [Mandalorian attack on a Republic envoy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalorian_attack_on_a_Republic_envoy)
  - **3661–3660 BBY, the Hydian Way blockade:**
    - The blockade cut Core World supply; many senators, even the most ardent war hawks, began to suggest surrender (lore batch 14), and the Jedi strike near Devaron failed. [Blockade of the Hydian Way](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way); [Great Galactic War §The Mandalorian blockade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Galactic_War)
    - Hylo Visz's smugglers and the Republic broke the blockade. Visz sold the supplies at inflated prices, skipped her medal ceremony and disappeared. [Blockade of the Hydian Way §Aftermath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way); [Hylo Visz §Breaking the blockade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hylo_Visz)
  - **Schism:** some clans tried to side with the Republic, as Mandalore the Preserver had centuries earlier, and were crushed. [Crusader's schism](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Crusader's_schism)
  - **3653 BBY, the Sacking:**
    - Vizla disabled Coruscant's defences, entered the Temple with Malgus, and afterwards retired on Rishi. [Mandalore the Avenger §The Sacking of Coruscant](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalore_the_Avenger); [Mandalore the Avenger §Retirement on Rishi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalore_the_Avenger); [Sacking of Coruscant](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sacking_of_Coruscant)
    - Kellian Jarro killed over 100 Mandalorians. [Kellian Jarro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kellian_Jarro); [Sacking of Coruscant (infobox)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sacking_of_Coruscant)

### Other factions and side-switching
- **Type:** factions
- **Era:** 3681–3653 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Chiss Ascendancy:**
    - The Empire's "only true ally", under the Chiss–Imperial treaty. [Chiss Ascendancy/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chiss_Ascendancy/Legends); [Chiss–Imperial treaty](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chiss%E2%80%93Imperial_treaty)

    - The Hutt–Imperial treaty kept Hutt space neutral and barred aid to the Republic. [Hutt–Imperial treaty](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hutt%E2%80%93Imperial_treaty)
    - The Hutts were angered at being left out of the Treaty of Coruscant. [Hutt Cartel/Legends §Old Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hutt_Cartel/Legends)

### Daily life in wartime
- **Type:** society
- **Era:** 3681–3653 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - **Coruscant:**
    - **3680 BBY:** days of riots after the Jedi left, then an enlistment boom. [Coruscant riots (3680 BBY)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_riots_%283680_BBY%29); [Battle of Eliad §Aftermath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Eliad)

    - **3661–3660 BBY:** starvation in the lower levels, protests against the Senate, and supplies resold at inflated prices. [Coruscant riots (3661 BBY)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_riots_%283661_BBY%29); [Blockade of the Hydian Way §Aftermath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way)
    - **3653 BBY:** a two-day occupation under martial law, with no one allowed to leave the planet, then the riots of 3653 BBY. [Sacking of Coruscant §Martial law](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sacking_of_Coruscant); [Coruscant riots (3653 BBY)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_riots_%283653_BBY%29)
  - **News and propaganda:**
    - **Republic:** the Tingel Arm footage, aired after Korriban [Assault on the Tingel Arm region](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Assault_on_the_Tingel_Arm_region); HoloNet reports of Jedi heroism [Coruscant riots (3680 BBY)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_riots_%283680_BBY%29); the "Heroes of Bothawui" and "Heroes of Karideph" [Battle of Bothawui (Second; Great Galactic War)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Bothawui_%28Second;_Great_Galactic_War%29); [Battle of Karideph](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Karideph).

  - **Senate:**
    - Paralysis in 3681 BBY. [Senate emergency defense session](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Senate_emergency_defense_session)
    - Alderaan became "militaristic" after 3667 BBY. [Battle of Alderaan §Aftermath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Alderaan)
    - In 3661 BBY many senators, even the most ardent war hawks, began to suggest surrender; the Senate as a body never formally debated it (lore batch 14). [Blockade of the Hydian Way §A crippled Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_of_the_Hydian_Way)
  - **Imperial society:**
    - Every non-Force-sensitive adult served in the military. [Imperial Military (Sith Empire) §Organization](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Military_%28Sith_Empire%29)
    - The Emperor decreed that every adequately Force-sensitive slave be sent to Korriban for training. [Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War) §Society and culture](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_%28Post%E2%80%93Great_Hyperspace_War%29)
    - Captured Republic soldiers were enslaved to rebuild the Korriban academy (3678 BBY). [Battle over Korriban §Aftermath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_over_Korriban)
  - **Dromund Kaas:** the capital was never reached by Republic reprisal. Malavai Quinn was born there around 3680 BBY. [Dromund Kaas/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dromund_Kaas/Legends); [Malavai Quinn](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Malavai_Quinn)
  - **Contested worlds:**
    - **Balmorra:** a long occupation with civilians caught in the fighting. [Balmorra/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Balmorra/Legends); [Battle of Balmorra (Galactic Wars) §The Great Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Balmorra_%28Galactic_Wars%29)

    - **Alderaan:** a pre-battle rest and medical stopover for Republic soldiers. [Battle of Alderaan §Prelude](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Alderaan)
    - **Fest:** thousands of Imperial civilians killed by the Dagger Wing bombing. [Bombing of Fest](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bombing_of_Fest)
  - **Republic recruitment:**
    - Elin Garza enlisted at 15 with forged papers in 3681 BBY. [Elin Garza §Great Galactic War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Elin_Garza)
    - Jace Malcom left Barcaria young to join the Republic's defence forces, and joined the Republic Military around 3683 BBY. [Jace Malcom](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jace_Malcom); [Jace Malcom §Escape from Korriban](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jace_Malcom)
