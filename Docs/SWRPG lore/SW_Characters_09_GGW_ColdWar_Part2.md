## Summary
This file covers named Sith and other dark-side users from the Empire's return in 3681 BBY to the Eternal Empire's arrival in 3636 BBY, with each person's status in 3661 BBY and in 3653 BBY. Key points: Darth Vindican was a Pureblood (not a Human) who died in 3681 BBY at Korriban, wounded by Kao Cen Darach and executed by Malgus. Malgus was a Darth from about 3667 BBY and co-led the Rim Campaign around 3660 BBY. Marr held his Council seat about forty years and died c. 3636 BBY. Baras died in 3640 BBY. Jadus faked his death in 3643 BBY. The Dread Masters were prisoners on Belsavis from 3668 BBY until about 3641 BBY, so they were jailed in both 3661 BBY and 3653 BBY. Darth Nyriss belongs to the Era 08 period (died 3950 BBY), not this one.
