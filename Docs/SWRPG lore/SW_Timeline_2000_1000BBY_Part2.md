## Summary
Part 2 continues the timeline from Part 1 and covers 1,010–1,000 BBY: the founding of the Brotherhood of Darkness and the Army of Light, every battle of the Light and Darkness War that Wookieepedia lists, Darth Bane's path (Apatros, the Gloom Walkers on Kashyyyk and Phaseera, the Korriban academy, Lehon, Ambria, Ruusan), all seven battles of Ruusan with what is known of each, the thought bomb, the Rule of Two and the Ruusan Reformations, then the undated rows of the whole war. The pages disagree on several dates (Third and Fourth Ruusan, Kashyyyk, Hsskhor, Harpori, Balowa, Battle of Hoth) and on who broke the Sith blockade; every case is tagged [conflict] here and listed in Part 3.

#### 1,010–1,003 BBY — The Brotherhood of Darkness and the Army of Light

The Jedi High Council, wary of the Jedi Lords, groomed its own champion, the Coruscant-born Skere Kaan, a master of battle meditation; he turned on the Council and led loyal Knights to the Sith [Light and Darkness War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Light_and_Darkness_War). Within a year he had ended the Sith civil war and forged the Brotherhood of Darkness, over 20,000 strong, in which every warlord's claim to be Dark Lord was recognized [Sith civil war](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_civil_war) [Light and Darkness War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Light_and_Darkness_War). The last decade of the war was later called the Light and Darkness War (c. 1,010–1,000 BBY) [Light and Darkness War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Light_and_Darkness_War).

#### 1,002–1,000 BBY — The road to Ruusan and the Ruusan campaign

Ruusan, an ore world of the Teraab sector claimed by the Mining Guild in 2,200 BBY and then largely abandoned, had only scattered settlements of miners, farmers, trappers and fugitives by the end of the war [Ruusan/Legends §History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ruusan/Legends). The campaign there lasted about two years, from 1,002 to 1,000 BBY, in seven battles and many skirmishes; the Army of Light won four of the seven [Ruusan campaign](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ruusan_campaign). Throughout, Jedi reinforcements were lost to the Sith blockade in orbit and Sith reinforcements to the Republic Navy [Ruusan campaign](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ruusan_campaign). Win/loss by battle: 1st Sith, 2nd Sith, 3rd Jedi, 4th Sith, 5th Jedi, 6th Jedi, 7th Jedi [First Battle of Ruusan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/First_Battle_of_Ruusan) [Second Battle of Ruusan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Battle_of_Ruusan) [Third Battle of Ruusan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Battle_of_Ruusan) [Fourth Battle of Ruusan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Battle_of_Ruusan) [Fifth Battle of Ruusan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fifth_Battle_of_Ruusan) [Sixth Battle of Ruusan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sixth_Battle_of_Ruusan) [Seventh Battle of Ruusan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Seventh_Battle_of_Ruusan).
