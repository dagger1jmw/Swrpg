# SWRPG Galaxy Timeline, 32–19 BBY (Part 2 of 4): The Clone Wars, Year One (22–21 BBY)

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 24, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends), current pages and pre-August-2008 revisions, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
Part 2 covers the First Battle of Geonosis (13:5:21 GrS, 22 BBY) and the first year of the Clone Wars to about 14:4 GrS (21 BBY). The primary framework is the pre-2008 Legends war: the 2003 *Clone Wars* micro-series, the Dark Horse *Republic* comics, the novels (*Shatterpoint*, *Hard Contact*, *The Cestus Deception*), *Galactic Battlegrounds: Clone Campaigns*, the 2002 *The Clone Wars* video game and *Republic Commando*, as summarized in the last pre-TCW revision of [Clone Wars/Legends (rev. 2099230, 29 Jul 2008)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clone_Wars/Legends?oldid=2099230) and dated by the 22 BBY year page. TCW's first-season events (Christophsis, Teth, the *Malevolence*, Ryloth and others) are listed separately at the end, tagged [S-canon], with Ahsoka Tano, Captain Rex and other TCW-only characters noted as absent from the game under rule 4a.

## Entries
Columns and tags as in Part 1. The 22 BBY year page groups first-year battles by GrS month: 13:5 is the month of Geonosis, 13:6 is one month after it, and so on to 13:11 (six months after), followed by 14:1–14:3 ([22 BBY/Legends §Battles](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/22_BBY/Legends)).

### Overview
Geonosis began the war but neither side was ready for it: the Grand Army numbered only 3 million troops at first, organized into sector armies of 150,000 clones each, and most operations on both sides were raids ([Clone Wars/Legends §Strategies](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clone_Wars/Legends), citing *The Essential Guide to Warfare* and *The Essential Atlas*). The Confederacy tried superweapons (the Decimator, the Dark Reaper) and bioweapons (swamp gas, an anti-clone nanovirus), and struck at Kamino; the Republic took Muunilinst, defended Mon Calamari and Dantooine, and met General Grievous at Hypori. Six months in, Mace Windu fought on Haruun Kal and Obi-Wan Kenobi was made a Jedi Master and given a Council seat. The year ended with the Bassadro massacre and Dooku's raid on the Jedi Temple, which hardened wartime politics on both sides.
