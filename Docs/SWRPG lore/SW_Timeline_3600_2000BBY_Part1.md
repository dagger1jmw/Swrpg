# SWRPG Galaxy Timeline, c. 3,600–2,001 BBY (Part 1 of 2): The end of the Sith Wars to the eve of the Fourth Great Schism

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 24, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
Part 1 holds the Entries for the sixteen centuries between the last dated SWTOR events (c. 3,622 BBY, end of SW_Timeline_3700_3600BBY_Part2.md) and the Fourth Great Schism (2,000 BBY, not covered here). The record is thin: Wookieepedia has year pages for only 33 years in this span and none at all for 3,621–3,523 BBY. What it does give: the Sith wars of the 3600s ended before 3,600 BBY and the Sith Empire later vanished at no recorded date; the Jedi returned to Coruscant and rebuilt the Temple (expansions in 3,519 or c. 3,500 BBY and c. 2,519 BBY); the Pau'an Sith Lord Darth Desolous killed almost two thousand Jedi at some point after 3,522 BBY; the Hapes Consortium sealed its borders c. 3,100 BBY; the seventeenth and last Alsakan Conflict ended in 3,017 BBY with a Corellian-dictated peace; and on isolated Kesh the Lost Tribe of Sith ended its Time of the Rot (3,000 BBY), annexed Alanciar (2,975 BBY) and put down Dreypa's rebellion (2,974 BBY). The rest is colonisation, first contacts and local history. Part 2 holds the checking sections (changes, conflicts, decisions, sources).

## Entries
Columns and Type values as in SW_Timeline_3700_3600BBY Parts 1–2: Date | Event | Type | Place | Who | Source. Type is one of War, Control, Leader, Death, Order, Tech, Background. Where Wookieepedia itself derived a date by arithmetic (not printed in a source), the row says so and carries [inferred]. Year pages are cited as "3017 BBY" and so on; every year page in this span is a Legends page.

## THE SEVENTEENTH ALSAKAN CONFLICT — 3,017 BBY
## ════════════════════════════════════════════════════

### Overview
The Alsakan Conflicts were seventeen wars over 14,000 years between Coruscant and Alsakan for the right to be the galactic capital, fought mostly in the Expansion Region over which corporations could sponsor colonies; Corellia usually stayed neutral, and each war split the Republic into three de facto states [Alsakan Conflicts/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Alsakan_Conflicts/Legends). By 8,000 BBY they had become "minor, almost antiquarian divisions" [Alsakan Conflicts/Legends §Conclusion](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Alsakan_Conflicts/Legends). The seventeenth and last flared after the Sith wars of the 3600s, because the Axis (the Alsakan-led Perlemian worlds) kept building up its fleet [Sith Wars/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Wars/Legends); [Space warfare/Legends §The golden age of battleships](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Space_warfare/Legends).
