# SWRPG Galaxy Timeline, 2,000–1,000 BBY (Part 3 of 3): Changes, conflicts and decisions

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched Nov 24, 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) and mirrors, as cited per entry. Draft for review.

## Summary
Part 3 holds the checking sections for the timeline in Parts 1 and 2: an index of the period blocks, a row-by-row check of the twenty rows in the "NEW SITH WARS — 2,000–1,000 BBY" block of `SW_Legends_Galaxy_Timeline.md`, the in-scope errors in `SW_Characters_10_NewSithWars_DarthBane.md` and in section 4 of `world-event-tags-3640-1000BBY-draft.md`, what was not found or not used, the developer's decisions, and the source list for all three parts. Headline corrections: the old file's Valley of the Jedi row (1,000 BBY) is too early, since Senate approval came only after 990 BBY; the Zeison Sha were not founded by the New Sith Wars refugees (they already existed by 3,951 BBY); Bane did not wear orbalisk armour when he fought Kas'im (he gained it on Dxun after Ruusan); Bane's Lehon visit and the Duel on Lehon are 1,000 BBY, not c. 1,001 BBY; and Dessel reached the Korriban Academy in 1,002 BBY after Phaseera, not 1,003 BBY.
