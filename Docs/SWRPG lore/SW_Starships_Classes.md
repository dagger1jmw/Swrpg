# Starship Types and Classes (Old Republic sources)

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched 23 Nov 2026 by Claude through the Wookieepedia API. Draft for review.

**How it was built**
- Walked the Starship classes and Starship types category trees (158 categories): 1,814 Legends articles.
- Kept the 292 that appear in an Old Republic–era work (KOTOR I/II, SWTOR and its expansions, Tales of the Jedi, *Revan*, *Deceived*, *Fatal Alliance*, *Annihilation*, the SWTOR comics, *Lost Tribe of the Sith*, Dawn of the Jedi, *Into the Void*, *The Lost Suns*).
- Read each one's lead, key dated sentences and infobox.
- Rows whose only Old Republic tie is a cross-era reference (A-wing, X-wing, TIE, Lambda, CR90 and the like) are listed under Not used.

**Source tiers.** Prose facts come from the page text. Costs, lengths, hyperdrive classes, crews, cargo and armament come from infoboxes, which draw on game books (WEG *Tales of the Jedi Companion*, the WotC *KOTOR Campaign Guide*) and game data. They're tagged (game stat).

## Summary
- **Republic Navy:**
  - *Great Sith War:* Praetorian frigates, Coruscant-class couriers, Stinger fighters.
  - *Mandalorian Wars and Jedi Civil War:* Hammerhead cruisers, Inexpugnable command ships, Foray blockade runners, Interdictor and Centurion cruisers, Aurek and Chela fighters.
  - *Great Galactic War and Cold War:* Thranta-class corvettes as the workhorse; Valor-class cruisers; BT-7 Thunderclap assault ships; the Jedi Defender-class light corvette; Liberator and FT-series fighters.
- **Sith Empire:** Harrower-class dreadnoughts as the backbone for over 85 years; Terminus destroyers; Delta carriers; Keizar-Volvec bulk cruisers; Fury-class interceptors; Mk. VI and VII fighters; the X-70B Phantom for Intelligence.
- **Mandalorians:** Kyramud battleships, Kandosii dreadnaughts, Jehavey'ir assault ships, Shaadlar troopships, Teroch gunships, Davaab fighters, and Mandalorian cruisers at the Hydian Way blockade.
- **Private freighters:** Dynamic-class (Ebon Hawk), XS stock light freighter (smugglers, Great Galactic War and Cold War), D5-Mantis (rare, Cold War), Lethisk armed freighter, Quartermaster supply carrier, KT-400.
- **Hyperdrive classes vary widely by source era:** WEG figures for ships around 4000 BBY are slow (Class 7–15). KOTOR and SWTOR-era figures are Class 2–3 for ordinary ships. See Rulings.

### Hyperdrive and hyperspace
- Hyperdrives cross the galaxy in hours or days. Travel time depends on destination, origin, route and hyperdrive class. A jump needs the ship to be clear of a large gravity well, and failsafes cut the drive if a gravity well lies on the course. [Hyperdrive/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hyperdrive/Legends)
- Courses use a planet's mass as a backstop. Ships stay near starports or inhabited systems so a breakdown doesn't strand them. A damaged hyperdrive can leave a ship stuck between realspace and hyperspace. [Hyperspace/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hyperspace/Legends)
- The Ebon Hawk's Class 1 hyperdrive replaced its original Class 3, putting it among the ten fastest ships in the galaxy in its day and the fastest in the Outer Rim. The temperamental drive often needed repair. [Ebon Hawk](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ebon_Hawk)
- Deflector shields: rapid-fire turbolasers outclassed every shield until the 7500s BBY, when improvements produced stronger shields. [Deflector shield/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Deflector_shield/Legends)

## Rulings (Jared, 23 Nov 2026)
1. **Hyperdrive classes.** WEG's *Tales of the Jedi Companion* gives ships around 4000 BBY slow drives (Class 7–15); KOTOR-era and later sources give Class 2–3. Ruling: drives improved by the Mandalorian Wars. After that, ordinary ships are Class 2–3 and exceptional ones Class 1 (the Ebon Hawk). Older designs still flying keep their slow drives unless refitted.
2. **Ships with no stats.** Fill gaps from comparable ships of the same type (for example a Thranta like a Hammerhead, a Harrower like an Inexpugnable at 800 m), tagged invented.
