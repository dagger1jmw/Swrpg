## Summary
In the Cold War, Dromund Kaas is still the untouched Imperial capital and the seat of the Dark Council. In 3643 BBY it sees a secret rebellion and a ritual in the Dark Temple, and in 3640 the Republic attacks it directly. When Zakuul strikes in 3636 BBY, the Eternal Fleet blockades Dromund Kaas within months. Darth Acina, the only Dark Council member not killed or driven into hiding, declares herself Empress, swears loyalty to Arcann, and rules from Kaas for about five years. She later joins the war against Zakuul and survives an assassination attempt by Saresh on Dromund Kaas.

## Entries

### Cold War and Galactic War (3653–3637 BBY)
- **Type:** history
- **Era:** 3653–3637 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - The Empire stays home to the Emperor and the Dark Council in the war era [SWTOR Holonet, Dromund Kaas](https://www.swtor.com/holonet/planets/dromund-kaas).
  - **3643 BBY:** Darth Baras's secretly instigated rebellion (SW_Planets_Travel_OldRepublic, citing Dromund Kaas/Legends). A ritual in the Dark Temple ("Duel in the Dark Temple", the Sith Inquisitor story) is dated to 3643 BBY [Duel in the Dark Temple](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Duel_in_the_Dark_Temple).
  - **3640 BBY:** a Republic invasion fleet attacks the capital (SW_Planets_Travel_OldRepublic, citing Dromund Kaas/Legends).
- **Uncertainty:** none.

### The Eternal Empire conquest and Empress Acina (c. 3636–3630 BBY)
- **Type:** history
- **Era:** c. 3636–3630 BBY
- **Key facts:**
  - The Eternal Fleet struck both powers at once, hitting shipyards first and then cutting supply lines. Within months Dromund Kaas and Coruscant were blockaded [SWTOR codex, "State of the Galaxy"](https://swtor.jedipedia.net/en/cdx/holo-sabacc).
  - Arcann didn't use occupation armies. He placed hundreds of Star Fortresses over major worlds such as Alderaan, Belsavis and Voss [Eternal Empire conquest, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/Eternal_Empire_conquest).
  - Acina was the only Dark Council member not killed or driven into hiding. She took control, declared herself Empress of the Sith, and signed a peace treaty with the Eternal Empire [Ambush on Dromund Kaas, archivum](https://www.archivum.wiki/article/ambush_on_dromund_kaas). She declared loyalty to Arcann, who acknowledged her claim, and ruled for five years with the support of most of the population [Darth Acina, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/Darth_Acina).
  - **The war against Zakuul (3631–3630 BBY):** Alliance victories led Acina's Empire to join the revolt, while the Republic kept paying tribute [War against Zakuul](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/War_against_Zakuul).
  - **The ambush on Dromund Kaas:** Acina invited the Alliance Commander to Kaas to negotiate. Former Supreme Chancellor Saresh, working with the Imperial Minister of Logistics Gelmid Lorman, tried and failed to kill them both [Ambush on Dromund Kaas, archivum](https://www.archivum.wiki/article/ambush_on_dromund_kaas). Stranded in the wilds, Acina and the Commander had to deal with the planet's dangerous wildlife and lightning storms [Darth Acina, archivum](https://www.archivum.wiki/article/darth_acina).
  - Acina died shortly after Zakuul fell, to an Iokath superweapon she'd hoped to secure [Darth Acina, archivum](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/Darth_Acina).
- **Uncertainty:** whether the capital was itself occupied or only blockaded and made a vassal [unclear]. The sources describe a blockade and a treaty, not an occupation.
