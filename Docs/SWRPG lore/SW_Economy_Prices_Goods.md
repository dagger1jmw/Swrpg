# Star Wars Legends Economy — Prices of Goods and Services (tier 1, in-universe)

*File: SW_Economy_Prices_Goods.md · first version · compiled 23 Nov 2026 (America/New_York) from Legends pages on Wookieepedia (starwars.fandom.com) via the MediaWiki API.*

## Summary
This file collects **in-universe Legends price figures** for everyday and luxury goods and services: food and drink, lodging, fares, medical care, information and forgeries, luxury goods, substances, creatures and slaves. Tier 1 means the figure comes from a novel, comic, reference book, web fiction, TV episode or video-game *dialogue/story* (tagged IU-game). Game-book and game-shop prices (WEG, WotC, SWTOR/KOTOR vendors, FFG) are **not** here; they are in **SW_Economy_GamePriceLists_Part1–Part9.md**. Canon gap-fill figures are in **SW_Economy_CanonGapFill.md**.

**Counts in this file:** 111 dated tier-1 figures, 4 tier-1 figures with no stated date (marked "date not stated"), 4 [gray] figures, plus a 140-line Dex's Diner menu table (all dated c. 28–19 BBY).

**Key anchors (in-universe):**
- **A meal:** Dex's Diner, Coruscant (c. 28–19 BBY): sides 1.7–3.7 cr, sandwiches 4.5–8.7, breakfasts 6.5–12.7, dinner plates 9.7–14.7 (nerf steak 14.7). A nelab stew at a cantina on Nova Station c. 40 ABY cost 10 cr. Street food on Tatooine was priced in wupiupi (a gorg for 7 wupiupi, 32 BBY).
- **Drinks:** two cantina drinks on Taris 20 cr (3956 BBY); luxury bottles 100 cr (Merenzane Gold) to 1,000 cr (rimble-wine; gold membrosia per litre).
- **A room:** the liner *Kuari Princess* charged 50–150 cr a night for cabins and 250–450 for deluxe staterooms, with top suites at 1,000 [unclear source—SWAJ/WEG page-level]. The travel-guide title *Wild Space on 50 Credits a Day* implies 50 cr/day as a budget traveller's benchmark (c. 21 BBY). Game-book rooms (Exovar's Emporium 10–15 cr/night) are in the game lists.
- **Fares:** Coruscant subway 2 Republic cr (c. 19 BBY); Coruscant City Tours 100 cr (32 BBY); Han Solo's Mos Eisley–Alderaan charter 17,000 cr (0 BBY); private speeder SpeedPipe toll 100 cr (c. 40 ABY).
- **Medical:** a bacta tank alone c. 100,000 cr [unclear] (the page's infobox gives 3,000–100,000 cr from game sources — see the game lists); cybernetic implants up to 7,500 cr (3956 BBY); cyborg construct 80,000 + 70,000 surgery; cosmetic surgery 1,000,000 cr (c. 3642 BBY). Kolto/bacta trade context is in SW_Kolto_Bacta.md.
- **Slaves (historical fact):** in Mos Espa (32 BBY) a Ghostling child fetched 30,000 wupiupi and a Bothan about a third of that; smuggling a slave off-world cost 10,000 wupiupi.

Figures are reported as written; currencies (Republic credits, wupiupi, peggats, truguts) are not converted here — see SW_Economy_Currencies.

## How to read the tables
- **Tier:** [tier 1] = in-universe Legends text; [tier 1, IU-game] = video-game dialogue or story text (not a shop price); [gray] = Legends-adjacent source of uncertain status (2014–2015 non-FFG works such as the *Imperial Handbook*); [unclear] = the page gives the figure without an inline reference, so the exact source is inferred from the page's source list.
- **Date:** stated where the page gives or implies it; "[inferred]" = taken from the setting of the source; "date not stated" = no date can be fixed (the source's publication year is given).
- **Citation:** link to the Legends Wookieepedia page (and section, where the figure sits in a long article) · the source work(s) the page cites for it.

## Conflicts
- **[conflict] Krayt dragon pearls (both IU-game, KOTOR, c. 3956 BBY, Tatooine):** the [Krayt dragon pearl](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Krayt_dragon_pearl/Legends) page gives a public valuation of *upwards of 100,000 cr each*; the [Canyon krayt dragon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Canyon_krayt_dragon/Legends) §Biography and appearance page says a *crimson* pearl was worth *upwards of 1,000,000 cr*. Both are from *Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic* (2003). Not reconciled. (A paledor statue *plus* a black krayt pearl sold for nearly 10,000 cr c. 10 BBY on Corellia — *The Paradise Snare*.) The lightsaber-materials file already carries the 100,000 figure.
- **[conflict] Tokursh beskar mining rights (both IU, 19 BBY, Mandalore):** 800 million cr ([Beskar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Beskar/Legends) §A Mandalorian resource, *Imperial Commando: 501st*) vs 8 million cr (the [Tokursh](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tokursh) page). Likely a transcription difference on the wiki; not reconciled.
- **[conflict] Dex's Diner destruction date:** most menu pages say the diner was destroyed in the first years of the Clone Wars / c. 19 BBY; at least one ([Andorian jelly](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Andorian_jelly/Legends)) says around 18 BBY. Dates above use c. 28–19 BBY.
- **[conflict] Jogan fruit errand (TCW, c. 21 BBY, Coruscant):** the droids carried 40 cr; the vendor's price rose 4 → 16 → 32 cr in the same episode — recorded as a scene, not a market price.
- **In-universe vs game-stat clashes** for goods: Kuari Princess suites (1,000 cr/day on the [Kuari suite](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kuari_suite) page, from WEG's *Riders of the Maelstrom*, 1989) agree with the IU cabin table; no clash. Bacta: WEG adventure text (*Hamar-Chaktak* plan: 75,000 / 25,000 cr bacta loads) sits in the game lists. For weapons, ships and droids see the conflict list in SW_Economy_Prices_Ships_Weapons_Droids.md.
