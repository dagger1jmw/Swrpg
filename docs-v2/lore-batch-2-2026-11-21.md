### M6: Force potential

- **Legends counts:** Anakin "over 20,000" (27,700 accepted anchor); average Jedi 10,000 (anchor); Yoda second only to Anakin (no number). No Legends count exists for Luke, Obi-Wan, Palpatine or others. Average non-sensitive Human under 2,500; mildly sensitive about 5,000. Keep the Legends scale; Lucas's "like a thousand" per cell is not used.
- **Design counts (labelled design, not lore):** Luke 27,700 (Lucas: Luke had his father's potential; "the son could become that"). Obi-Wan 13,000. Palpatine about 17,400 (derived so unhurt Anakin, 27,700, has twice Palpatine's pool ceiling, per Lucas's "twice as good as the Emperor"). Orgus Din 7,000 (the reserve spec's Jedi minimum).
- **Checklist item 15, cybernetics and the Force ceiling** (replaces the original "cybernetic ceiling loss"):
  - A single lost or replaced limb never lowers the pool ceiling (Legends: losing limbs does not reduce potential; Luke's hand, Anakin's arm).
  - The ceiling falls only when a large share of the living body is lost or replaced (Lucas, Vanity Fair 2005: "as a living form, there's not that much of him left"). Propose a curve: no loss below a threshold share of body lost, rising with the share above it. Calibrate it so Vader's post-Mustafar injuries (both legs, the left arm, the earlier right forearm, severe burns, lungs) give about a 40% permanent loss.
  - A second, psychological part (about 20% for Vader) is recoverable through story events (*Dark Lord*: Sidious says Vader could regain his power by facing his choices). Total post-Mustafar loss is about 60%, so Vader's pool is about 80% of Palpatine's (Lucas: "maybe 20 percent less than the Emperor"; Legends: "eight-tenths").
  - Cybernetic hands or arms block lightning-type powers (Legends: risk of shorting the cybernetics). Separate from the ceiling.
  - The suit's immobility is a combat mobility effect (M5/M8), not a Force effect.

### M6: Alignment

- **Fall-speed targets:** self-driven falls take years (Anakin about 3, Cade about 7, Dooku about 12, Jacen about 13); outside pressure compresses them to days or months (Kyp, Luke on Byss, Ulic) or about a week under torture (Bastila); a wound in the Force can make one immediate (Nihilus); a final tipping chain can complete in under a day (Anakin).
- **Physical corruption follows immersion and heavy dark-power use, not Dark alone:** eyes yellow only in anger at moderate immersion; permanently yellow at deep immersion; pallor, decay and ageing only after long, heavy immersion; speed and severity vary by person. At redemption the eyes recover and the skin does not. This settles the canon eye-state check.
- **Redemption** follows Shim's stages: minor taint is atoned for by reflection; a true fall needs a selfless act done without the dark side. Redemption never zeroes Corruption; a lasting hold remains and can flare under stress.
- **Madness:** the dark side amplifies existing trauma; it does not cause madness by itself. "Force psychosis" is a Legends term for an Abeloth-caused affliction (c. 43 ABY, *Fate of the Jedi*), not a result of a character's own dark-side use. Reframe the pending Force psychosis design accordingly.
- Shim's "a killer of innocents can't be redeemed" is an in-universe opinion, not a rule (Anakin and Kyp contradict it).
