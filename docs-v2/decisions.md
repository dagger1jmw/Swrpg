## 2026-11-16 — Lore-check resolutions (Jared)

Resolves the flags in docs-v2/spec/lore-checks.md. Decided by Jared.

- **D-01 (L-16) Era label for 3,661 BBY.** 3,661 BBY is called the Great Galactic War (year 20), not the Cold War, in phase1-answers.md and character-creation.md. The default start stays 3,661 BBY, and the start year remains a creation choice. *Reason:* the Legends timeline puts the Great Galactic War at 3,681–3,653 BBY and the Cold War at 3,653–3,642 BBY (SW_Legends_Galaxy_Timeline.md:305-311, :377, :411-417).
- **D-02 (L-03) Temple by start date; ranks.md trial rules are a design choice.** Starts before 3,653 BBY use the Jedi Temple on Coruscant under Grand Master Zym; Tython is used only for starts from 3,653 BBY. ranks.md notes that its age and trial rules (Initiate Trials, selection by 13, Council of Reassignment, five Knight Trials) are based on prequel-era Legends practice and are not attested for 3,661. *Reason:* the Order relocates to Tython only after the Sacking and Treaty of Coruscant (Timeline :410-412, :421); the lore files state no Old Republic age or trial rules.
- **D-03 (L-17) Calendar week and month count.** 7-day weeks and 12 months are Legends-sourced. *Source:* HoloNet News and *The Essential Atlas*, per Wookieepedia "Standard week/Legends". *Reason:* Jared confirmed the source, which settles the flag that the 5-day week was the only attested Legends week.
- **D-04 (L-11) Satele Shan's lightsaber.** In 3,661 BBY Satele Shan uses a blue double-bladed lightsaber. *Reason:* Legends: she wields a double-bladed saber during the Great Galactic War and a single green blade only later, in the Cold War. The lore files are silent; this confirms the legacy lore-database entry (`legacy/index.html:9057`).
- **D-05 (L-13, L-18) Lore story facts vs game mechanics.** CLAUDE.md Golden Rule 3 now reads: "Story facts in Docs/SWRPG lore/ are authoritative. Game-mechanics sections (talent effects, ability rules, numbers) are inputs that docs-v2 specs may override; log every override in decisions.md." All four talents with no legacy code are kept, translated to v2 terms: Combat Intuition (+1 to rolls when outnumbered → a circumstance modifier); Dark Affinity (strain ×0.85 → dark-side Force load ×0.85, plus faster drift); Living Force Connection (strain-free Animal Bond → no Force load for Animal Bond); Precognitive Edge (one tier earlier → a level shift on the Precognitive Reflexes unlock). *Reason:* the lore file's mechanics were written for the legacy engine, and the v2 specs replace that engine; the Legends story facts are unaffected.
  - **Overrides logged under D-05:** the v2 Shatterpoint rules (Q20, Q21) override the lore file's Path A (learned Shatterpoint, cap at level 20), ShatterSense seeding at level 5, focus and tunnel-vision levels, and interpretation strain (SW_Force_Abilities_Forms_v3_complete.md:459-500).
- **D-06 (L-05) Shatterpoint perception bands.** Re-anchored to v2 tiers: physical weaknesses 1+, combat 21+, personal 41+, relational 61+, situational 81+, event 101+ (levels on the progression-numbers.md curve). *Reason:* the lore's bands (1/6/11/21/36/51) assumed the legacy 100 × 1.07^n scale; the new bands line up with the v2 tier starts (Apprentice 21, Adept 41, Master 61, High Master 81, Legendary 101). Logged as a game-mechanics override under D-05.
- **D-07 (L-08, L-09) Removed aliases.** The legacy aliases "ForceFortitude", "Force Fortitude", "Fortitude" (→ Battlemind) and "ForceBodyControl" (→ Force Body) are removed. Unknown names are rejected and never fall back to a nearby ability. force-catalog.md's alias check is amended to match. *Reason:* no lore or Legends source uses these names, and the mappings were guesses; Force Body in particular is the most dangerous ability in its family.
- **D-08 (L-12) Renown tracks.** Republic, Sith Empire, Jedi Order, Sith, Mandalorians and Underworld. The Chiss count as Empire. *Reason:* these are the factions the 3,661 BBY lore supports (Timeline :305-311, :374-391); the Underworld covers smugglers and Nar Shaddaa, which the lore depicts but never defines as a faction.
