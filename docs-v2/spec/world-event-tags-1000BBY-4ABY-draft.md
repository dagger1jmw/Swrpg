# World event tag map, 1000 BBY – 4 ABY (draft for review)

The post-Ruusan Republic, the Clone Wars and the Empire, plus the links that tie them together. Same format and rules as world-event-tags-draft.md (3661–3640). Drafted by Claude, 23 Nov 2026. **Reviewed by Jared 23 Nov 2026 (rulings in section 8); PP-0 triggers approved (section 5).**

**Sources and accuracy**
- **W:** checked against the Legends Wookieepedia page named (fetched 23 Nov 2026).
- **LF:** taken from SW_Legends_Galaxy_Timeline.md and **not re-checked**. Lower confidence: verify before building if the event matters to a plot point.
- **T:** the overnight timeline drafts in Docs/SWRPG lore (SW_Timeline_2000_1000BBY, 1000_33BBY, 32_19BBY, 19BBY_4ABY; 24 Nov 2026, drafts for review), each row cited to Wookieepedia.
- **Film:** the event happens on screen in a Star Wars film.
- **Tags are design calls**, not lore: replaceable or crucial, the guards, and the plot point alternatives.
- **Corrections to the lore file** found while checking are listed in section 7. The map uses the corrected facts.

**Notation** (as in the 3661 map). `E.<year>.<name>` = event; `O.<name>` = outcome. `R` = replaceable; `C` = crucial. Guards: low / medium / high. **[PP-n]** = feeds a major plot point tracker (section 5). BBY years are written plainly ("32" = 32 BBY); ABY years say ABY.

**Canon heroes as NPCs.** Following the SWTOR ruling, film and book heroes (Luke, Leia, Han, Galen Marek and others) are NPCs. If the timeline diverges, each takes a key role similar to their own in that period. Ruled yes by Jared (23 Nov 2026).

## 4. Links across the eras

These chains run through all three eras. A break anywhere along one changes everything after it. The code walks them like any other outcome chain; they're listed here so the build can test them end to end.

1. **No standing army (Ruusan → the clones → the Empire).** The Reformations dissolve the Republic Military (O.no_standing_military). So the Republic fights the Stark war with volunteers and Jedi; it can't answer the Naboo blockade; it debates the Military Creation Act; and the clone army becomes the only army it can get. If a player changes this (a standing army exists), the clone army loses its reason to exist and PP-4 diverges.
2. **The Jedi under the Chancellor (Ruusan → Order 66).** The Reformations put the Jedi under the Chancellor and the Judicial Department (O.jedi_under_judiciary). That is why Jedi lead the Grand Army as generals under Palpatine, and why they are scattered among clone units when Order 66 comes.
3. **A weak Chancellor, a strong Senate (Ruusan → Palpatine's election).** Power moved to the Senate (O.senate_empowered); a gridlocked Senate and a Chancellor who can't act are what let a Vote of No Confidence topple Valorum.
4. **The Sith line (Ruusan → Endor).** Bane's survival (O.rule_of_two) runs through Zannah, Tenebrous, Plagueis and Sidious. Sidious orchestrates the Stark war, the Yinchorri Uprising and the Naboo crisis (W). O.sith_believed_extinct holds from 1000 BBY until Tatooine in 32 BBY; even then, who the Sith are stays secret until 19 BBY. The line ends with the Emperor's death at Endor.
5. **The clone army (Galidraan → Kamino → Order 66 → stormtroopers).** Galidraan leaves Jango the sole survivor (44); he wins the Hunt to become the template (32); Sifo-Dyas's order (32) is found by Obi-Wan (22); the Senate accepts the army (22); the clones carry out Order 66 (19).
6. **The Death Star (Geonosis → Despayre → Yavin).** The designs go from Poggle to Dooku to Sidious (22); the project leaves Geonosis (19) and reaches Despayre (3); the plans are stolen (0); the station dies at Yavin (0).
7. **The Chosen One (birth → fall → redemption).** Anakin's birth (41) → freed by Qui-Gon (32) → the Tusken massacre (22) → the secret marriage (22) → Vader (19) → the reveal to Luke (3 ABY) → redemption at Endor (4 ABY). Every link is a crucial role; breaking any one diverges everything after it.
8. **The loyalists become the Rebellion.** The Delegation of 2000 (19) → Ghorman, which turns Bail Organa to resistance (19) → the Corellian Treaty (2) → the Declaration of Rebellion (2) → the Senate dissolved (0) → Yavin.
9. **The hidden twins.** Hidden at birth (19) → Leia aboard the Tantive IV and Luke called by the droids (0) → Yavin → Bespin → Endor.
10. **Back to the Old Republic.** The 3661 map ends in 3640 and this one starts in 1000 BBY. The New Sith Wars (2000–1000 BBY) link them: if the player is in a campaign that reaches Ruusan, E.1000.thought_bomb and E.1000.reformation are where the two maps join. The years 3640–2000 BBY are not mapped yet; Jared asked for 3640–1000 BBY next (23 Nov 2026).
11. **Training from infancy (Ruusan → Anakin).** The Reformations make Jedi training start in infancy (O.jedi_from_birth). Anakin is found at nine with a mother he loves, so he enters an Order built for children with no family ties; that attachment feeds O.anakin_darkness. If PP-0 diverges on this point, Anakin's fall becomes much less likely.

## 5. Major plot points (proposed)

The design notes allow authored alternatives only for massive, story-changing events. Jared approved PP-3 to PP-8 and their alternatives on 23 Nov 2026 and added PP-0, the Ruusan Reformations. **PP-0's per-reform triggers were approved by Jared on 23 Nov 2026.**

### PP-0: The Ruusan Reformations (1000 BBY)
Approved by Jared on 23 Nov 2026 (recorded in the M8 handoff; written into this map on 24 Nov 2026).
- **Hinge:** the Senate ratifies the Reformations. Each reform turns separately, by its own trigger, so a campaign can lose one reform and keep the others.
- **The attachment ban is older than Ruusan** (ruled). It was already in force in the Old Republic era. Ruusan added the enforcement: infant training and central Council control.
- **Per-reform triggers:**
  - **R1, the army** (the Republic Military dissolved; O.no_standing_military). It diverges only if the Sith are found alive within about 100 years of Ruusan. Then the Republic keeps or rebuilds a standing army, the Military Creation Act and the clone army have no reason to exist, and PP-4 diverges.
  - **R2, Jedi independence** (the Jedi under the Chancellor and the Judicial Department; O.jedi_under_judiciary). It diverges if a high-standing player character sides with Valenthyne Farfalla. The Jedi stay independent of the Chancellor, don't become clone generals, and Order 66 has no command structure to work through.
  - **R3, infant training** (O.jedi_from_birth). It diverges only if the thought bomb does not detonate **and** one other major tag is broken. More Jedi keep family ties (closer to the Corellian Jedi), an Anakin found at nine is normal rather than an exception, and his fall is much less likely.
- **The thought-bomb branch:** if the thought bomb doesn't destroy the Brotherhood, two Sith lineages exist at once (Bane's and the Brotherhood's survivors), and the Rule of Two is much harder to keep hidden. This links to PP-13 alternative 2 in the 3640–1000 map.
- **No Reformations at all:** PP-13 alternative 1 (the Brotherhood wins Ruusan) means no Reformations, so R1–R3 all stand diverged.
- **Schema note (Phase B report, gap 3):** each trigger becomes its own plot point in code (PP-0.R1 to PP-0.R3 are not valid ids yet).

### PP-3: Palpatine becomes Chancellor (33–32 BBY)
Reworked with Jared on 23 Nov 2026 to the 3640–1000 map's pattern: each hinge breaks only through steps a player can reach, and nothing re-stages history. Source: W: Supreme Chancellor election, 32 BBY/Legends; W: Eriadu Trade Summit.
- **Hinge:** Valorum falls to a Vote of No Confidence, and the Senate elects Palpatine while his identity stays secret.
- **Odds (Jared):** every step is clear and possible, but needs the player in a very specific place, so the odds are low. A player can't simply deploy there. A Jedi has to be chosen from the qualified Jedi, a Senate aide has to work for the right committee, and so on. Each step names who the player has to be.
- **The steps:**
  1. **Eriadu, 33–32.5 BBY** (guard high). The Nebula Front stages two "attempts" on Valorum, on Coruscant and at the summit. At the summit the whole Trade Federation Directorate except Gunray is murdered, in a plan Palpatine orchestrated. *Who:* a Jedi chosen for Valorum's escort, Senate or Judicial security at the summit, or someone inside the Nebula Front. Foiling the murders, or showing that the Front was manipulated, keeps the summit from collapsing and Valorum from looking helpless.
  2. **The aurodium frame** (guard medium). Gold stolen at Dorvalla turns up in Valorum's account. Bail Antilles charges him with corruption, and the Senate bars him from using the Jedi or the Judicial Forces without its consent. *Who:* an investigator, banking official, or aide to the Internal Activities Committee or the Supreme Court. Tracing the money clears Valorum.
  3. **The Naboo stall** (guard medium). Lott Dod stalls every Senate procedure while Naboo is blockaded. *Who:* a senator, a committee aide, or a Jedi carrying a report to the Senate. Forcing action keeps Valorum from looking weak.
  4. **The Queen's motion** (guard medium). Palpatine persuades Amidala that only a new Chancellor can save Naboo, and she moves the vote. *Who:* a handmaiden, Royal Security, or a Jedi on her escort. Counselling her against it breaks the step. Her sympathy is what carries Palpatine, so a motion moved by anyone else does not replace hers.
- **What breaks the hinge:** step 4, or any two of steps 1–3.
- **Alternatives:**
  1. **Valorum survives** (ruled by Jared, 23 Nov 2026). He finishes his term, which has only months left. The scheduled election that follows is ordinary politics, not a re-staged event, and Bail Antilles is the frontrunner, so this usually leads into alternative 2. Palpatine can still stand, but wins only if play allows it.
  2. **Another candidate wins:** Bail Antilles (the frontrunner) or Ainlee Teem. Palpatine stays a senator and works from there.
  3. **Sidious is exposed** (guard very high; ruled by Jared, 23 Nov 2026: "almost impossibly difficult"). It takes many steps, and every one needs an **in-world reason** to suspect Palpatine. A player's own knowledge of the films never counts: an investigation with no lead found in play scores nothing. Possible leads come only from play: the Trade Federation's contact "Darth Sidious", Maul's trail, or Hego Damask's dealings. The Jedi then hunt the Sith openly and the Grand Plan collapses.

### PP-4: The Clone Wars begin (32–22 BBY)
Reworked with Jared on 24 Nov 2026. Sources: W: Grand Army of the Republic/Legends; W: Sifo-Dyas/Legends; W: Military Creation Act; W: Kamino/Legends. Order 66 follows the source-priority ruling (section 8, ruling 6): it is a standing contingency order, not a chip.
- **Hinge:** the Republic adopts the clone army and goes to war with the Confederacy.
- **The steps:**
  1. **The order, 32 BBY** (guard high). Hego Damask (Plagueis) steers Sifo-Dyas, already off the Council for ideas judged too extreme, into ordering the army from Kamino, and funds it. *Who:* a Jedi close to Sifo-Dyas, someone inside Damask Holdings, or a Kaminoan contact. Stopping the order means there is never a clone army.
  2. **Sifo-Dyas on the Pyke mission** (guard high; not crucial). Valorum sends him to negotiate with the Pyke Syndicate on Oba Diah, a mission he keeps from the Council, and Dooku betrays and kills him. *Who:* someone on that mission (a Jedi, Valorum's staff or security). If he lives, Tyranus never takes over the project and Sifo-Dyas runs it. This changes who controls the army, not whether it is adopted, and it does not decide Order 66 (that is PP-5's per-unit condition).
  3. **Kamino erased from the Archives** (guard medium). *Who:* Archives staff under Jocasta Nu. Noticing the gap early gives the Jedi an in-world lead to look into the army before 22, which counts toward step 5.
  4. **The emergency powers vote, 22 BBY** (guard high). Amidala and the Loyalist Committee oppose the Military Creation Act. After the attempts on her life she is sent to Naboo, and Jar Jar proposes the powers in her absence. *Who:* a senator, Amidala's staff or her security. Keeping her on Coruscant, or swinging enough of the Senate, blocks the motion.
  5. **The army's origin exposed** (guard very high). Exposing a Sith hand behind the army before the vote needs in-world leads found in play (Kamino's erasure, the name Tyranus, the dead Master's order); then the Senate refuses the clones.
- **What breaks the hinge:** step 1, 4 or 5. Step 2 changes the army's leadership only.
- **Alternatives:**
  1. **No clone army** (step 1). The Separatists leave; the Republic fractures without a war, or fights with planetary forces raised in a hurry.
  2. **The Senate rejects the clones** (step 4 or 5). Secession is accepted with a cold peace between two governments, or a slow war without clones; the Jedi are not generals.
  3. **Sifo-Dyas runs the army** (step 2; not a hinge break). The war goes ahead as recorded; the narrator directs a Grand Army led by a living Jedi Master who ordered it.

### PP-5: The fall of the Jedi and the Empire (19 BBY)
Reworked with Jared on 24 Nov 2026. Order 66 follows ruling 6 (a contingency order, no chip).
- **Hinge:** Anakin turns, Sidious survives the arrest, and Order 66 goes out.
- **Part A: Anakin's fall.** Once it reaches Palpatine's office, his decision there is absolute. A player can only stop the slide before then:
  1. **A trusted Council master as confidant** (guard very high). *Who:* a player on the Council by the Clone Wars, at about Yoda's or Mace's level, who has built real respect and trust with Anakin and shows it. Anakin brings his visions to them instead of Yoda; different advice can break tags or open another outcome. Unlikely: he doesn't even tell Obi-Wan.
  2. **A fatherly advocate** (guard very high; usually the same person). Makes a case for Anakin on the Council alongside Obi-Wan, countering what Palpatine tells him (that the Council doesn't trust him, which is mostly true).
  3. **Changing the rules on attachment** (guard very high). A player becomes Grand Master and argues successfully for allowing families again: a change to the post-Ruusan Order (ties to PP-0 R3).
  4. **The marriage exposed** (usually makes it worse). While attachment is forbidden, Anakin leaves or is put out of the Order, likely guards Padmé or Palpatine, and Palpatine influences him more easily, offering the only way to save her. If step 3 has changed the rules, he can go to the Jedi openly about his visions, and they can help.
- **Part B: the arrest** (guard very high). *Who:* a player close to Windu on the Council, going in place of Saesee Tiin or Agen Kolar (still four on one). Outcomes, about equally likely:
  1. **The player survives until Anakin arrives** with Windu, and two Jedi standing deter what follows.
  2. **Palpatine drops the lightsaber act and uses the Force:** (a) he kills Windu and the player before Anakin arrives; or (b) they survive until Anakin arrives, but the "weak old man" act is gone and Anakin is left conflicted.
  **Anakin's choice in 1 and 2b:** if part A step 1 or 2 is broken **and** that trusted master is the one in the room, one is enough: he sides with the Jedi, hoping for standing and the rank of Master, believing the Jedi can help. If the trusted master isn't there, both steps are needed. If neither is broken, he takes Palpatine's side knowingly, likely ending the fight, resolved (Knightfall-level).
- **Part C: Order 66.** Each clone unit's loyalty is tracked, but only ARC troopers and commandos can refuse. Most clones think it wrong, but their loyalty is to the Republic first, and few knew their Jedi general well beyond the commanders. A refusal saves that unit's Jedi; survivors are tracked as people. Order 66 has no alternative of its own.
- **Recorded path:** Sidious and Anakin kill Windu (and the player, if present). History runs on as recorded; the event is adjusted.
- **Alternatives:**
  1. **Sidious killed or arrested.** Anakin sided with the Jedi or never arrived. The Republic survives with a huge clone army and a power vacuum.
  2. **Sidious escapes, exposed** (who lives is decided by the warning race: docs-v2/spec/m8-design-notes.md, "Order 66: the warning race (PP-5 alternative 2)"). He issues Order 66 at once, his only way out. Two or three surviving Jedi know he is a Sith Lord: they warn the Temple first, then race to send an emergency warning to Jedi across the galaxy (Order 66 was a known contingency). A partial Purge: Coruscant less bad, the field decided by whether the warning reaches each Jedi first. Variants: **(a)** Anakin escapes with him (Vader exists, and the Jedi know whom he serves); **(b)** Anakin sides with the Jedi and Sidious escapes alone (no Vader; Sidious looks for an apprentice).

### PP-6: The Alliance forms (2 BBY)
Reworked with Jared on 24 Nov 2026. Source: W: Corellian Treaty (The Force Unleashed novelization version; ruling 4).
- **What happened:** Garm Bel Iblis gathers Bail Organa and Mon Mothma on Corellia to draft the treaty. Galen Marek arranges the signing, but it is Vader's trap: Vader raids it and takes the three senators and Rahm Kota to the Emperor on the Death Star. Galen follows, nearly kills Vader and the Emperor, and dies buying their escape. The leaders found the Alliance on Kashyyyk with Leia, under the Marek family crest.
- **Hinge:** the resistance leaders sign the treaty, survive Vader's trap and found the Alliance.
- **The steps:**
  1. **Galen's recruiting** (guard high; needs in-world leads). *Who:* someone in Kota's militia or a senator's resistance cell who learns whom Galen really serves. Exposing him means the leaders sign without Galen arranging it: no trap.
  2. **The raid on Corellia** (guard high). *Who:* the leaders' security, Kota's militia, or someone who spots the Imperial build-up. Getting the leaders away means no captures.
  3. **The Death Star rescue** (guard very high). *Who:* someone on the rescue with Juno Eclipse, or an Imperial aboard the Death Star. An Imperial player who stops the escape gets the leaders executed.
- **What breaks the hinge:** step 3 going wrong for the rebels. Steps 1 and 2 change how the Alliance begins, not whether it does.
- **Alternatives:**
  1. **The cells stay fragmented longer** (the leaders executed). A slower, scattered resistance; a later, weaker rebellion only through play; the Death Star plans may never be stolen.
  2. **The Alliance forms without Vader's trap** (step 1 or 2). No captures; stronger earlier; no Marek crest. Galen is an NPC who takes a role like his own, directed by the narrator.
  3. **Galen survives the Death Star** (guard very high; ruled by Jared, 24 Nov 2026). The ending of *The Force Unleashed*, but Galen escapes too: the Alliance starts with two Jedi, Galen and Kota. Realistically needs a Force-using player in the rescue, such as an Order 66 survivor from PP-5, who is there through a tie to Bail Organa, Mon Mothma, Garm Bel Iblis or Rahm Kota.

### PP-7: Yavin (0 BBY)
Reworked with Jared on 24 Nov 2026. Source: T: SW_Timeline_19BBY_4ABY_Part2–3 (Operation Skyhook, 2–0 BBY).
- **What happened:** the plans were pieced together from several sources: Darkknell (Bel Iblis finds the first evidence), the listening post AX-235 (schematics intercepted; Biggs carries them out), Danuta (Kyle Katarn) and Toprawa (a convoy raid, combined with Danuta's set and transmitted to the *Tantive IV*). Vader captures Leia over Tatooine; the plans escape in R2-D2. Alderaan is destroyed; Leia escapes the Death Star with a homing beacon aboard, and the Death Star follows it to Yavin, where Luke destroys it.
- **Hinge:** the plans reach the Alliance, and Luke destroys the Death Star.
- **Not breakable:** Luke's shot itself. A player can't take Luke's role; a Rebel player can only fly cover for him.
- **The steps:**
  1. **The plans, gathered** (guard medium). Several sources, so breaking one isn't enough. *Who:* Imperial Intelligence, security at Danuta or Toprawa, or a traitor in a Rebel cell. Breaking Toprawa, or two of the three supply sources, means the plans never reach Leia.
  2. **The droids.** *Who:* a stormtrooper aboard the *Tantive IV* who finds the droids before they reach the escape pod (guard very high; highly unlikely), or an Imperial searching Tatooine or someone at Mos Eisley who spots them (guard high). R2-D2 captured means the plans are lost.
  3. **The homing beacon** (guard medium). *Who:* someone in Leia's rescue party suspicious of how easy the escape was. Finding it means the Death Star doesn't follow them to Yavin.
  4. **The trench run** (guard very high). *Who:* an Imperial pilot in Vader's flight. Holding the trench before Han returns, or catching the *Falcon* on its way in, means Luke never gets his shot.
- **Alderaan** is not a step of its own. It is saved only indirectly: the Empire recovers the plans (step 1 or 2), or Leia isn't captured, which needs a Jedi strong enough to beat Vader by 0 BBY (guard very high; the surviving Jedi near the Empire were out of practice, and those who kept training lived far from it).
- **Alternatives** (approved by Jared, 24 Nov 2026):
  1. **The Death Star survives** (step 4). Yavin 4 is destroyed with the Alliance leaders there; the Alliance is shattered.
  2. **The plans never reach the Alliance** (step 1 or 2). The Death Star stays in service and more worlds die; the Alliance stays hidden and small.
  3. **Yavin isn't found** (step 3). The Alliance keeps the plans and its base; no battle at Yavin on the recorded date. The Death Star keeps hunting, and the Alliance can attack it later at a time it chooses, directed by the narrator.

At Endor, Han's strike team and the Ewoks destroy the shield generator; Ackbar's fleet, with Lando and Wedge, destroys the reactor. In the throne room the Emperor tortures Luke, and Vader throws him into the reactor shaft and dies redeemed.

  1. **The plans never reach the Alliance** (guard medium). From the Imperial side this needs a high-ranking Imperial, working against the Emperor's own design. From the Rebel side, the Bothan Spynet or Madine's commandos fail.
  2. **The trap is uncovered** (guard very high). Needs in-world leads that the leak was set up; a player's own knowledge of the films never counts.
  3. **Leia never reaches the Ewoks** (guard high). *Who:* a scout trooper who kills or captures Leia in the speeder chase. With no Ewok alliance, the strike team is rounded up at the bunker, the shield holds, and the fleet is destroyed.
- **Alternatives:**
  1. **The Emperor survives** (step 3). The fleet is destroyed, the Empire endures, and the Alliance is broken or driven underground.
  2. **No Endor** (step 1, or step 2 if the Alliance stays away). No battle; the war goes on with both sides intact.
- **Dropped (Jared, 24 Nov 2026):** "the Emperor dies but Vader lives". Vader never grows powerful enough to take on the Emperor himself, so without Luke the Emperor doesn't die at Vader's hand.

## 8. Rulings (Jared, 23 Nov 2026)
1. **Canon heroes are NPCs.** The SWTOR ruling extends to film and book heroes (Luke, Leia, Han, Galen Marek and others). After a divergence each takes a role similar to their own.
2. **Maul.** Legends includes *The Clone Wars* seasons 1–6 (season 7 does not count) and *Son of Dathomir*. Maul survives Naboo and returns in 20 BBY (kept from TCW under ruling 6), and dies on Tatooine in 16 BBY (*Old Wounds*; ruled 24 Nov 2026): Obi-Wan hesitates and Owen Lars shoots him (corrected 24 Nov 2026, D-474). (Superseded in part by ruling 6: TCW is now secondary canon. Maul's survival is one of the TCW events kept.)
3. **Plot points.** PP-3 to PP-8 and their alternatives were approved, then reworked one by one from 23 Nov 2026 into player-reachable steps (PP-3 to PP-8 done). PP-0, the Ruusan Reformations, is added, with per-reform triggers approved (section 5).
4. **The Force Unleashed** stays in the timeline, using the novelizations rather than the games, because the novels keep Galen Marek's power level grounded.
5. **3640–1000 BBY** is to be mapped next.
6. **Source priority for the Clone Wars (Jared, 24 Nov 2026).** *The Clone Wars* (2008) goes to the bottom of the lore priority, as secondary canon. Pre-2008 Legends Clone Wars material (the 2003 *Clone Wars* micro-series, the comics, the novels, Republic Commando) is primary. Where TCW retcons earlier material, the earlier material wins; TCW events that don't contradict it can still happen, adjusted (Mortis does, as *Fate of the Jedi* refers to it). Order 66 is one of the Republic military's contingency orders, with no inhibitor chip: clones obeyed through Kaminoan conditioning and their own choice, which is why some units refused. The specific differences (Grievous's strength, rank dates for Anakin and Obi-Wan, characters who exist only in TCW) are checked when the Clone Wars lore file is built.
7. **Clone Wars and Maul rulings (Jared, 24 Nov 2026; full list in docs-v2/reports/lore-rulings-2026-11-24.md).** TCW characters stay, named, when their events do not conflict with primary Legends, and one is replaced only where they take a role primary Legends gives someone else (Captain Rex does not stand: Commander Appo keeps the role). Satine, Pre Vizsla, Bo-Katan, Almec and the TCW Mandalore are on hold (D-479, superseding D-477 and D-478 here). Sifo-Dyas's Pyke mission is accepted (PP-4 step 2 stands). Ahsoka Tano exists as Plo Koon's apprentice and dies in Order 66 at Cato Neimoidia with Plo Koon's forces (ruled, not lore); the only way she survives is PP-5 alternative 2's warning race. Maul's origin follows *Darth Plagueis*: born 54 BBY on Dathomir to the Nightsister Kycina, given to Sidious, trained as an assassin with Plagueis's knowledge; Savage Opress, Talzin and Satine stay as part of his kept return. Rank dates follow the pre-2008 version (Obi-Wan a Master and on the Council after Haruun Kal, 22 BBY; Anakin knighted c. 20 BBY). Anakin is born 41 BBY.
