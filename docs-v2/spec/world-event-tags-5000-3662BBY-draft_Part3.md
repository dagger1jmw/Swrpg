## 12. Major plot points (reviewed)

Numbered after the other maps (PP-0 to PP-13). Written to the reworked pattern of the 1000 BBY map (PP-0, PP-3 to PP-8): each hinge breaks only through steps a player can reach, each step names who the player has to be and where, and nothing re-stages history. **Reviewed by Jared, 24 Nov 2026:** all eight approved with the changes applied below, and every step passes his three tests (can a PC realistically be there; what standing and power; what kind of PC) and the duelist rule (Part 1, design rules). Guards follow world-events.md §2; every count or threshold in a step is *invented*.

### PP-14: The Great Hyperspace War (5000)
- **Hinge:** Sadow wins the Dark Lordship, invades along the Daragon Trail, and the Republic, given Gav's coordinates, finds and breaks the Sith Empire at Korriban (E.5000.korriban_second, O.sith_empire_broken).
- **Unbreakable:** Ragnos's ghost at the funeral (a spirit's act; nothing reaches it).
- **Who the player can be:** a Sith-side character (a Massassi officer, a servant or apprentice in a Lord's household on Ziost, Khar Shian or the *Corsair*), or a Republic-side one (a Koros soldier or technician, one of Teta's officers, a Jedi at Kirrek or on Ossus, a Senate aide). Gav and Jori are NPCs.
- **The steps:**
  1. **The frame on Ziost** (E.5000.ziost_raid, high; prerequisite: in the raid or saw the Republic-issue blaster). Exposing that Sadow killed Simus himself turns the Council to Kressh. It counts only if it reaches the Council through Kressh or another Lord; a servant's word alone changes nothing. Breaks the hinge (alternative 1).
  2. **The beacon** (E.5000.khar_delba, medium; lead: meeting Jori on her return, or working on the *Starbreaker 12*). Finding Sadow's homing beacon means he cannot follow the Trail. Breaks the hinge (alternative 1).
  3. **The sphere** (E.5000.primus_sphere, split by method). *Persuading* Gav not to fire needs closeness: high (prerequisite: close to Gav, met and trusted after Koros Major). *Fighting* Gav at the sphere: high for a trained Sith (prerequisite: on the sphere's guard and saw him come aboard), very high for anyone else (prerequisites: on the sphere's guard, and a lead on Gav's turn). Stopping Gav keeps the illusions up; Gav is not lured to the empty sphere and sends nothing. Breaks the hinge (alternative 2).
  4. **The coordinates** (E.5000.primus_goluud, high; prerequisite: on the *Corsair* or the sphere's guard with a lead on Gav's turn). Jamming or stopping the transmission. Breaks the hinge (alternative 2).
  5. **Kirrek** (E.5000.kirrek, medium). Changing the outcome needs command rank: an officer or a Knight leading troops; a rank-and-file soldier can be there but can't change it. If Kirrek falls, the Sith hold part of the Koros system and the battle at Korriban is weaker. Doesn't break the hinge alone; with step 7 it gives alternative 4.
  6. **The pursuit** (E.5000.denarii, very high; prerequisites: command of a pursuing ship, and a lead on Sadow's course). Catching Sadow before the Denarii Nova. Beating him in a fight is top tier (duelist rule). Doesn't break the hinge: alternative 3.
  7. **Gav kept loyal** (E.5000.koros_major, very high; prerequisites: a place in Gav's inner circle in Sadow's service, and a lead on Sadow's betrayal, which the player keeps from him). Gav never learns of the betrayal and never turns: no attack on the sphere, no coordinates. Breaks the hinge: alternative 4 if Kirrek also fell (step 5), otherwise alternative 2.
  The Senate warning (E.5000.warning, medium) only adjusts: a heeded warning makes Coruscant's battle shorter.
- **What breaks the hinge:** step 1, 2, 3, 4 or 7.
- **Alternatives:**
  1. **No invasion** (step 1 or 2). Either Kressh rules a still-isolated Empire (step 1; the Daragons die on Ziost), or Sadow rules but cannot find the way back along the Trail (step 2). There is no counterinvasion. The Empire is not crumbling, so the reason for the Ritual of Nathema is gone: E.4999.nathema loses its crucial input and the narrator directs Vitiate from there (he is not removed; he waits on Nathema). The Republic learns of the Sith only through Jori's story; no Sith relics reach Ossus.
  2. **The Sith Empire survives in the Caldera** (step 3 or 4, or step 7 with Kirrek held). Sadow's invasion is thrown back but never followed home. With no coordinates there is no second battle of Korriban and no counterinvasion. The Empire is a known, hostile power beyond the Daragon Trail under Sadow (or Kressh, if he wins the first battle of Korriban). Nathema's reason is weakened, so the narrator directs Vitiate; PP-15 starts from a Republic that knows the Sith live.
  3. **Sadow taken at the Denarii Nova** (step 6; the hinge holds). The war ends as recorded, but Sadow is captured or dead. O.sadow_on_yavin is never produced. Nadd (4400) finds no master on Yavin 4, the *Corsair* and the lab are not there for Kun (3997), and Ovair's mission (3756) has no spirit to hunt; each of those events diverges under the narrator.
  4. **The invasion holds** (step 7 with step 5; very high: needs a PC in Gav's inner circle). Kirrek falls and Gav never learns of Sadow's betrayal: he stays loyal, the sphere's illusions never break and no coordinates reach Teta. Sadow keeps a foothold in the Koros system and the war goes on inside Republic space under the narrator's direction. There is no second battle of Korriban and no counterinvasion, so Nathema's reason is weakened as in alternative 2 and the narrator directs Vitiate. O.sadow_koros_foothold (new).

### PP-15: The hidden Empire (4999–3681)
- **Hinge:** the Republic does not learn that a Sith Empire survives on Dromund Kaas until it strikes in 3681 (Kao Cen Darach's Empire "believed destroyed for centuries", W).
- **Unbreakable:** the Ritual of Nathema itself (E.4999.nathema). Every Lord there is under Vitiate's mind-domination and is consumed; no step outside the planet reaches it (a closed system). A Sith player can only avoid answering the summons.
- **A long tracker.** It runs across fourteen centuries and many campaigns. Each step that succeeds produces an *exposure* fact. An exposure breaks the hinge only if it is believed (as in PP-12): the player carries evidence to someone with power to act (the Jedi Council, the Senate, the SIS), and the check uses the player's standing and the strength of the evidence. Belief needs Knight standing or physical proof: a Padawan accusing a Master isn't believed. A failed check leaves the hinge intact and the fact on record as rumour, which a later player can find as a lead. No step rests on a premonition: a Jedi who "senses" the Empire would be the narrator changing the story.
- **The steps:**
  1. **The exodus** (E.4999.exodus, very high). Main route: a Sith-side character aboard the exodus fleet who takes proof (the navigation records) and later defects to the Republic with it (prerequisites: aboard the fleet, and the records taken in play). The Republic tracker route stays, with both its prerequisites: (a) a Republic scout, Jedi Shadow or soldier in Sith space who records the fleet leaving Korriban, and (b) a tracker aboard or a way to follow it through the random jumps, found in play. Either way the hardest step: twenty years of jumps in the Unknown Regions.
  2. **The emissary** (E.3978.emissary, very high; the same prerequisites as PP-17 step 1). A Mandalorian who unmasks the Emperor's agent learns that a Sith power lies beyond the Unknown Regions border. It counts for PP-15 only if the knowledge reaches the Republic: the player defects, is captured and believed, or has a Republic contact.
  3. **Revan's memory** (E.3954.revan_leaves, very high; prerequisites: Revan's confidant, with a lead that his memories returned, e.g. on Rekkiad with him, and Knight or Republic-officer standing to be believed, since the Council distrusts Revan). Revan tells the confidant what he remembers before he leaves.
  4. **The spies in the Order** (E.<3756.ovair_spies and E.~3750.ovair_duel, high; prerequisite: a lead on Ovair, e.g. Gynt met, or the Korriban visit seen by the Republic guard). Proving a Jedi Master serves a Sith Emperor. Belief needs Knight standing or physical proof.
  5. **The staging** (E.<3681.staging; also PP-21 step 1). Exposing the puppet governments and the Belkadan–Ruuria–Sernpidal ships before 3681 is possible only through a Sith-side defector (very high; prerequisites: serves in the staging and defects, and proof carried out), or once the Republic is already forewarned (high; prerequisite: a lead in the Dalonbian sector).
  PP-20 alternative 2 (Revan and Surik turn back before the Citadel and leave Dromund Kaas) also breaks this hinge. A Kaas City local who saw something of the 3950 attack carries only a rumour lead, no break.
- **Alternatives:**
  1. **Found in the cradle** (step 1, believed before c. 4900). The Republic finds Dromund Kaas while the Empire is young and few. The narrator directs the campaign that follows: the Emperor fights or flees deeper. O.sith_empire_kaas still stands if he survives there, but the Republic watches it. No surprise invasion is possible.
  2. **Known for centuries** (steps 2, 3 or 4, or PP-20 alternative 2). The Republic knows a Sith Empire lies beyond the Unknown Regions border. It keeps a border fleet and a stronger Korriban guard. The Emperor, whose plan relied on surprise, moves his timetable: the narrator sets whether he strikes sooner or later. E.3681.invasion and the opening battles fire adjusted, and PP-21 starts with O.republic_forewarned.
  3. **Exposed on the eve** (step 5 through a Sith-side defector, from 3700). The war still comes in 3681, but PP-21 starts with O.republic_forewarned and its steps open up (see PP-21).
- **Effects:** every alternative produces O.republic_forewarned (new; not an outcome of the recorded timeline). PP-21 reads it.

### PP-16: The Great Sith War (3998–3996)
- **Hinge:** Exar Kun and Ulic Qel-Droma both fall and Ragnos's spirit names them Dark Lord and apprentice (E.3997.iron_citadel, O.brotherhood_sith).
- **Unbreakable:** the Iron Citadel itself once both have fallen (the spirit's act, and the amulets' power). Kun, Ulic, Nomi, Arca, Cay and Tott are NPCs; Kun and Ulic are top duelists.
- **The steps:**
  1. **The Ketos' escape** (E.3998.onderon_second, medium). *Who:* a Knight or an officer in Iziz who meets the Ketos can detain them or take their artifacts: no Krath. A trooper or Beast Rider can only report or delay them, which doesn't break it.
  2. **Kun's road to Dxun** (E.3997.kun_dxun, high; prerequisite: a lead on what Kun read, e.g. a fellow student of Vodo-Siosk Baas who saw him at the Tedryn Holocron, or a Jedi at the Onderon outpost who met him). A delay doesn't break it. Taking it to Baas can, if the player is a student he trusts or brings proof: Baas stops Kun before Nadd's tomb. Stopping Kun by force is top tier.
  3. **Korriban** (E.3997.kun_korriban, very high; prerequisites: Kun's companion or close pursuer, with a lead that he went to Korriban, and a way into the collapsed temple). A rescue, not a fight: digging him out before he accepts the dark side.
  4. **Ulic's mission** (E.3997.ulic_falls, split by method). Talking him out of it at Deneba needs a close friend: high (prerequisite: a close friend of Ulic's, found in play; in the lore nearly everyone tried). Taking him out of Cinnagar by force is top tier: Ulic is Kun's equal. Saving Arca at Deneba (E.3997.deneba) stays medium; it removes the grief that drives Ulic and lowers the talking route to medium.
  Two more steps change the war but not the hinge: the **Cron supernova** (E.3996.cron, high; Ossus is not ruined, since Ossus's ruin stays tied to the supernova) and **Odan-Urr** (E.3996.ossus_converts): hiding the holocron stays medium (it is not smashed and the Brotherhood has fewer possessed converts); defending Odan-Urr against Kun is top tier.
- **What breaks the hinge:** Kun does not fall (step 2 or 3), or Ulic does not fall (step 1 or 4).
- **Alternatives:**
  1. **The Krath war without Kun** (Ulic falls, Kun doesn't). Ulic and Aleema lead the Krath; Ulic still wins Mandalore at Kuar. There is no Brotherhood, no killing of Odan-Urr, no Sith uprising of students, and no altered Cron weapon, so Ossus is not ruined and the Jedi stay there. A shorter, conventional war: O.krath_war_only (new).
  2. **Kun's Brotherhood without the Krath's armies** (Kun falls, Ulic doesn't). Kun has his Massassi and his Jedi converts but no fleet or Mandalorians: the Sith uprising and the Masters' murders happen, the great battles (Foerost, Coruscant, the Slice) do not. Mandalore the Indomitable raids on his own. The Republic finds Yavin 4 sooner: O.kun_isolated (new).
  3. **No Great Sith War** (neither falls). The Krath, if founded, are a local coup the Republic crushes. Ossus stands; the Jedi headquarters stays there, so O.jedi_hq_coruscant is never produced and the Coruscant Temple stays a lesser site. The Mandalorian Crusaders go on raiding under the Indomitable, who does not die at Onderon in 3996, so the Ultimate never takes the mask: PP-17 starts from a different Mandalore (the narrator names one) and the emissary of 3978 still comes. O.sith_war_averted (new).

### PP-17: The Mandalorian Wars (3978–3960)
- **Hinge:** the Mandalorians fight the Republic until Malachor V, where Revan kills Mandalore, the Mass Shadow Generator destroys the fleets and the clans surrender unconditionally (O.mandalorian_surrender).
- **Not breakable:** Revan's duel with Mandalore aboard the flagship (his own act). Revan, Malak and Surik are NPCs (protagonist); Revan and Malak are top duelists.
- **The steps:**
  1. **The emissary on Rekkiad** (E.3978.emissary, very high; prerequisites: a Mandalorian in Mandalore's escort who met the emissary, and a recorded lead that the vision was imposed). Mandalore rejects the plan.
  2. **The Revanchists stopped** (E.3963.council, very high; prerequisites: standing, a Council or Covenant seat or a Judicial officer with a warrant, and power: top tier to arrest Revan and Malak by force, or a close friendship to persuade them to submit). Carrying out Lucien's warrants means no sanction on Cathar and no Revan at the head of the war.
  3. **The Generator** (E.3960.malachor, split by method). Sabotage: high (prerequisite: on Bao-Dur's team or in Surik's half of the fleet with access to the device, having met Bao-Dur). Persuading Bao-Dur against Surik's order: very high (prerequisites: a trusted teammate of his, and a lead on what the Generator will do to both fleets). Bao-Dur does not fire, or the device fails.
  Smaller steps adjust the war: exposing the Cathar massacre (E.3973.cathar, medium; the Republic joins early), holding the Vanquo line (E.3964.vanquo, medium; changing the outcome needs command rank; the Onslaught is delayed), and the rakghoul plague on Jebble (E.3963.jebble, medium; if it does not break out, the Mandalorians strike Alderaan, adjusted).
- **What breaks the hinge:** step 1, 2 or 3.
- **Alternatives:**
  1. **Malachor without the Generator** (step 3). Revan still kills Mandalore, but the Mandalorian fleet survives and withdraws under a new leader: an armistice, not a surrender. No Disarmament; the Mask goes with the clans, not to Rekkiad. Surik keeps the Force and is never the Exile. No survivor becomes Nihilus's wound, and Malachor V is not a gravity-well ruin. The Triumvirate of 3954 lacks Nihilus: E.3954.triumvirate fires adjusted (Traya and Sion), and PP-19 is narrator-directed where it needs Nihilus. O.mandalorian_armistice (new).
  2. **The Republic fights without the Revanchists** (step 2). The Navy falls back to the Core; a negotiated peace cedes Outer Rim worlds to Mandalore: O.mandalorian_peace (new). Revan and Malak, still NPCs, take roles like their own: dissident Jedi fighting on the fringe or in exile, directed by the narrator. Their road to Dromund Kaas (E.3960.dromund_kaas) loses its inputs (no Malachor, no Rekkiad coordinates), so the Jedi Civil War (PP-18) does not happen as recorded.
  3. **No Mandalorian Wars as recorded** (step 1). The Neo-Crusaders keep raiding the fringe for decades; no Cathar, no Onslaught, no Revanchists. The Emperor's plan to bleed the Republic fails; the narrator directs any other tool he reaches for. O.crusaders_fringe (new).

### PP-18: The Jedi Civil War (3960–3956)
- **Hinge:** Darth Revan is taken alive and remade, and as the amnesiac Revan destroys the Star Forge and kills Malak at Rakata Prime (O.star_forge_destroyed). Revan's redemption and the Star Forge's destruction are canon outcomes the player can affect only through these steps.
- **Unbreakable:** Revan's own choices on the Star Forge: returning to the light, redeeming Bastila, the duel with Malak (as with Vader's turn in the 1000 BBY map). The player can't take Revan's part. Bastila, Carth, Mission, Zaalbar, Canderous, Jolee, Juhani and HK-47 are NPCs.
- **The steps:**
  1. **The capture** (E.3957.capture, three routes). Route 1: letting the wounded Revan die in Malak's barrage, or finishing him: high; needs a place on Bastila Shan's strike team, which takes Knight-level trust (prerequisite: met Bastila). Route 2: helping Revan escape the strike team: only through a Sith-side officer on Revan's flagship (high; prerequisite: serves on the flagship). Route 3: warning Revan of Malak's betrayal so he survives it: very high (prerequisites: senior rank in Revan's fleet, and evidence of Malak's plot found in play).
  2. **Taris** (E.3956.taris, medium). *Who:* a Sith officer in Malak's occupation, or a Tarisian bounty hunter or gang member. Taking Bastila, or the *Ebon Hawk* before it lifts, means the crew never leaves Taris together. A Tarisian PC likely dies in the bombardment unless off-world.
  3. **The Star Maps** (E.3956.star_maps, high; prerequisite: a lead that the maps matter). *Who:* a Sith agent with Malak's orders, a Czerka officer on Kashyyyk, or a Selkath official on Manaan. Taking or destroying one map before Revan's crew arrives is fine; doing it while they are there means facing the crew, Revan among them (duelist rule).
  The disruptor field on Lehon (E.3956.rakata_prime, medium) is a smaller step: if it holds, Dodonna's fleet fights blind and the Republic's losses are far higher, adjusted, but Revan's choices still decide the Star Forge.
- **What breaks the hinge:** step 1, 2 or 3.
- **Alternatives:**
  1. **Revan dies in 3957** (step 1, route 1). Malak rules alone; the Star Forge keeps producing; the Jedi are hunted to near extinction years early; the war's end comes only through play. No redeemed Revan means no Mask in 3954, no Dromund Kaas in 3950 and no prisoner to slow the Emperor: E.3954.mask, E.3954.revan_leaves and E.3950.attack lose their crucial role, and O.war_postponed is never produced (see PP-20 alternative 1 for what that does to 3681). O.malak_unchecked (new).
  2. **Darth Revan unbroken** (step 1, route 2 or 3). Revan keeps his empire. He is a far better strategist than Malak: the Sith take more of the Core. A later turn comes only through play. O.darth_revan_rules (new).
  3. **The Star Forge not found in time** (step 2 or 3). Revan and Bastila live, but the race for the Forge is lost: the war runs past 3956 (E.3956.rakata_prime moves later or diverges), and the Forge's fall becomes play-driven. O.star_forge_holds (new).

### PP-19: The First Jedi Purge and the Triumvirate (3954–3951)
- **Hinge:** the Triumvirate falls: Nihilus dies at Telos and Sion and Kreia die on Malachor V, which is destroyed (O.triumvirate_ended), and the Jedi survive to rebuild.
- **Unbreakable:** Nihilus's death at Telos from the boarding on (a closed system: Surik's team can't be stopped once they board, and Nihilus keeps no Force user aboard but Visas, so there is no Sith-side Force-user PC on the *Ravager*). Surik's duels with Sion and Kreia on Malachor V.
- **Nihilus's survival is not an authored alternative** (Jared). His death at Telos is fixed. He survives only if Surik is beaten before Telos, which is top tier (duelist rule); Telos is then narrator-directed.
- **Who the player can be:** a surviving Jedi, a Republic officer or Telos Security officer, a Khoonda militia member, one of the Preserver's Mandalorians, or a Sith of the Trayus Academy or Sion's assassins (no Force user serves on the *Ravager* but Visas). Surik, Kreia, Atton, Bao-Dur, Mira, Visas and the Preserver are NPCs; Nihilus, Sion and Kreia are top duelists.
- **The steps:**
  1. **Katarr** (E.3952.katarr, high; prerequisite: a Master in the conclave's circle, or Atris's confidant). Talking Atris out of the leak, or moving the conclave. Dorak, Zhar Lestin, Vandar Tokare and the hundred live: the Order rebuilds faster (adjusted). Doesn't break the hinge.
  2. **The Masters on Dantooine** (E.3951.masters, very high; prerequisites: already in the Enclave ruin, concealed from Kreia, and a lead on her true name). Kreia cut off all three Masters at once before they could react, so open confrontation fails; the only route is a PC hidden from her who acts before she does. Saves Vrook, Kavar and Zez-Kai Ell. Doesn't break the hinge.
  3. **Malachor** (E.3951.malachor, high; prerequisite: a Sith of the Trayus Academy who knows of the remote, or one of the *Ebon Hawk*'s allies at Malachor). A Sith-side player in the Trayus Academy finds and disables Bao-Dur's remote; or the Generator fails. The duels themselves stay unbreakable. Breaks the hinge (alternative 1).
  Telos (E.3951.telos) is no step: players in the battle (Carth's fleet, Telos Security, the Mandalorians) change its losses, not Nihilus's death.
- **What breaks the hinge:** step 3. (Surik beaten before Telos is outside the authored set: narrator-directed.)
- **Alternatives:**
  1. **Malachor endures** (step 3). The Trayus Academy stands; Sion or a successor regroups the warlords; the Republic does not reclaim Sith space in 3950 and Korriban gets no Republic guard yet. O.trayus_endures (new).

### PP-20: The attack on the Emperor (3954–3950)
- **Hinge:** Revan is taken alive in the attack on the Emperor and imprisoned in the Maelstrom; Surik dies; Scourge becomes the Emperor's Wrath (O.revan_imprisoned). Through Revan's mind the war is postponed by nearly three centuries (E.3950.revan_link).
- **Unbreakable:** the attack inside the Citadel is a closed system: the throne room (no PC would realistically be there), Scourge's betrayal, and the Emperor's survival in 3950 (killing him then is not possible). Scourge doesn't waver until his vision during the attack, which shows the attempt failing and an unknown future Jedi succeeding; no earlier lead on Scourge exists.
- **Who the player can be:** a character born in the hidden Empire (an Imperial officer or Guard, a servant or retainer in a Dark Councillor's household, a Sith in Scourge's service), or a Republic or Jedi character who came with Surik through a tie found in play (one of her Lost Jedi). Revan, Surik, Scourge and T3-M4 are NPCs; Revan is a top duelist, and the review puts beating Surik in the top tier too.
- **The steps:**
  1. **Nyriss's household** (E.3950.nyriss, two routes). Warning Nyriss of Sechel's deal: high (prerequisite: a trusted retainer with proof of the deal). Siding with Murtog in his ambush: Scourge wasn't in it and Surik won it, so it means beating Surik: top tier. Either route: Revan stays Nyriss's prisoner and dies with her when the Guard storms the stronghold (alternative 1).
  2. **The Purge** (E.3950.council_purge, high; prerequisite: a lead on the conspiracy). Not a hinge step: the Council is not wholly replaced, and O.prowar_council is never produced. The Empire's war plans are less unanimous (adjusted).
  3. **The turn back** (E.3950.attack, very high; prerequisites: a companion as close to Revan as Surik, through ties found in play, and with them on Dromund Kaas after Revan's rescue, before the dawn attack). The attack itself can't be broken, so the only way is before the Citadel: the companion talks Revan and Surik out of the attack.
  A Kaas City local in the right place can only carry what they saw: a rumour lead for PP-15, no break.
- **What breaks the hinge:** step 1 or step 3.
- **Alternatives:**
  1. **Revan dies on Dromund Kaas** (step 1). There is no prisoner. Nothing tempers the Emperor, so O.war_postponed is never produced and the invasion (E.3681.invasion) moves earlier: it comes 20–50 years after 3950 (ruled), while the Republic is still rebuilding; the narrator picks the year in that range. Revan is gone from the 3661 and 3640 maps (E.3642.maelstrom and the Revanite crisis are cancelled).
  2. **Revan and Surik turn back** (step 3). They never enter the Citadel and leave Dromund Kaas with proof of the hidden Empire: PP-15's hinge breaks (alternative 2, "Known for centuries"), and the belief check is met by Revan's standing. The Emperor, exposed, moves his timetable: the narrator directs. O.revan_returns (new).

### PP-21: The Great Galactic War's opening (3681)
- **Hinge:** the surprise invasion traps the first Republic fleet in the Tingel Arm and retakes Korriban (O.tingel_arm_lost and O.korriban_retaken).
- **No forewarning, no break** (Jared). The Republic has no way of knowing. Without O.republic_forewarned (a broken PP-15) or a Sith-side defector PC, nothing in PP-21 is breakable. No Jedi sensed anything; a premonition would be the narrator changing the story, which is not allowed.
- **Reads PP-15 and PP-20.** If O.republic_forewarned exists, step 3's first prerequisite is met and steps 1 and 2 open to Republic players as below. If PP-20 alternative 1 moved the invasion, the steps happen on the new date with the same roles (the people may be different).
- **The steps:**
  1. **The staging** (E.<3681.staging; also PP-15 step 5). Without forewarning, only through a Sith-side defector PC: very high (prerequisites: serves in the staging and defects, and proof carried out). With O.republic_forewarned: high (prerequisite: a lead in the Dalonbian sector). Exposing the puppets and the hidden ships before the first shot.
  2. **The Tingel Arm trap** (E.3681.tingel_arm). Without forewarning, only through a Sith-side defector PC: very high (prerequisites: knows the trap from inside the Imperial fleet, and reaches the convoy or first fleet with proof before it sails). With O.republic_forewarned: medium, and it needs command rank or a diplomat's authority in the convoy or fleet. Spotting the trap at Belkadan, Ruuria and Sernpidal saves the fleet.
  3. **Korriban** (E.3681.korriban, very high; prerequisites: forewarning, and reinforcement). Against over thirty dreadnaughts the station can only hold with warning and reinforcement. Without forewarning a PC can be there (a soldier on the vigil; another Jedi stationed there is very unlikely) but can't change the outcome. Saving Darach from Malgus is top tier.
  4. **The Senate** (E.3681.senate, medium). A senator or aide who carries Satele's report and ends the deadlock. Adjusts only: the Navy answers at once and the Jedi don't go to Minos alone.
- **What breaks the hinge:** step 3, or steps 1 and 2 together.
- **Alternatives:**
  1. **Korriban holds** (step 3). The Empire's first blow fails at the old Sith capital. Malgus doesn't rise there as recorded (O.malgus_lord diverges); the war is fought in the Tingel Arm and the Outer Rim; the 3661 map's E.3660.korriban becomes a fight for a Republic-held world.
