## How to read it

**IDs.** Events are `E.<year>.<name>`; outcomes are `O.<name>`. An event *consumes* outcomes (its inputs) and *produces* outcomes that later events consume. That is the network the ripple walks. No id here repeats one in the other three maps: where this map feeds an event of the 3661 or 3640 map it produces a new outcome and names the event that should add it to its Consumes (section 11, link 1).

An outcome is produced at the event's end unless the row says "(produced at its start)" (world-events.md §1); spans that later events need while they run are marked so. "(optional)" after a consumed outcome means the event goes ahead adjusted if it is broken.

**Roles.** `R` = replaceable: if the person is gone, someone of similar standing takes over and nothing downstream changes. `C` = crucial: if they are gone, the event diverges.

**Conditions.** Each has a crucial flag and a guard (world-events.md §2):
- **low:** one scene or a single good roll can break it;
- **medium:** a deliberate effort (a mission, an investigation, a won fight);
- **high:** foreknowledge or extraordinary action; needs **one prerequisite** first, named in the condition (a lead recorded in play);
- **very high:** several steps that must each succeed, or a much harder feat; needs **two distinct prerequisites**, both named.
A prerequisite is always a lead found in play (a figure met, a site found, a fact learned in a scene). Knowledge of the games, comics or novels never counts (D-468).

**Standing state.** Whatever happened before the campaign's start date is `standing`. This map has no "(start)" outcomes of its own: a campaign that starts inside it treats every earlier row as standing.

**Plot points.** Events marked **[PP-n]** feed a plot point tracker. Each plot point has a hinge, player-reachable steps (who the player has to be, where, and the guard) and 2–4 authored alternatives chosen from game state. Everything else diverges under the narrator's direction from the broken tags.

**Canon heroes are NPCs** (rulings of 23 Nov 2026, extended here). The KOTOR protagonists (Revan, Meetra Surik) are `NPC (protagonist)`. Book and comic heroes (Nomi Sunrider, Ulic Qel-Droma, Exar Kun, Zayne Carrick, Gav and Jori Daragon, Arca Jeth, Bastila Shan, Malak and the rest) are NPCs with ordinary roles. The player never takes their part. After a divergence each takes a role like their own.

**Unbreakable moments** (declared in place, with the reason): the Ritual of Nathema (E.4999.nathema), Marka Ragnos's spirit at the funeral and in the Iron Citadel (E.5000.ragnos_funeral, E.3997.iron_citadel), Revan's own choices on the Star Forge (E.3956.rakata_prime), and, from review: Nihilus's death at Telos from the boarding on and Surik's duels with Sion and Kreia (E.3951.telos, E.3951.malachor); the throne room on Dromund Kaas, Scourge's betrayal and the Emperor's survival in 3950 (E.3950.attack); and the opening of the Great Galactic War where the Republic has no forewarning and no Sith-side defector (E.<3681.staging, E.3681.tingel_arm, E.3681.korriban). The player can still change everything around them, earlier.

**Narrator colour.** Following the 3640 map's ruling 4 (local chains on Kesh are colour, not data), the Lost Tribe of Sith on Kesh (5000–3960) and a few purely local rows are listed as *narrator colour only, not shipped*. They produce nothing outside their world.

**Where this map joins the others.** It ends where the 3661 map begins (E.3661.blockade and the 3661 standing state) and feeds that map and the 3640 map through the outcomes listed in section 11, link 1. A campaign that starts in or after 3661 reads every row here as standing.

## Design rules from review (Jared, 24 Nov 2026)

These apply to every step in every tag map (D-489).

**Three tests for every step.**
1. *Is it realistic for a PC to be there?*
2. *What standing and power level does the PC need?* (rank, trust and fighting strength, not just presence)
3. *What kind of PC?* (which side, which role, which ties)

Jared's examples: the Death Star throne room is unbreakable except by the Emperor's or Vader's death earlier, because no PC would be there; the Ewoks on Endor break only if Leia is taken out on the speeder or earlier; Anakin's fall needs a High Council-level Jedi in good standing and in Anakin's trust.

**Top duelists (standing rule, all maps).** Any fight with a top duelist needs at least a Master. In this map the top duelists are Sadow, Kun, Ulic, Revan, Malak, Nihilus, Sion and Kreia (Vader and others in later eras; Yoda and Sidious are apex duelists, a tier above, which no one on this map is listed in: D-494); the review also puts beating Surik and saving Darach from Malgus in the top tier. Three tiers:
- **A regular Master** only holds one off briefly, like Kit Fisto against Sidious: enough to buy a moment, never to win.
- **Top tier:** beating one, or holding one off for long, needs a Mace-level character (combat 88, Force Control 85, Willpower 90: D-494). In this map "top tier" marks such a route. It is a power bar on top of the guard, not a guard level of its own: a PC below it fails however the rolls go.
- **Apex tier (Jared, 25 Nov 2026, D-494, D-495):** above the top duelists: combat 98, Force Control 110, Willpower 105. Yoda and Sidious; the Dark Times Vader, Mace and the freed Revan are just under it. Only another apex beats an apex duelist; a top-tier fighter only buys time; a Master does not even hold one off. No apex duelist on this map.

**No premonitions.** A vision, premonition or "sense in the Force" is never used to justify a step or counted as a lead. That would be the narrator changing the story. (Scourge's vision in the throne room is lore, not a lead a PC can have earlier.)

**Closed systems.** A scene no PC would realistically be in (a throne room, a Sith flagship with no other Force user aboard, a ritual of mind-dominated Lords) is unbreakable. It can only be changed earlier, by a step that changes who gets there or whether the scene happens. In this map: E.4999.nathema, E.3951.telos from the boarding on, Surik's duels on Malachor V, E.3950.attack inside the Citadel, and PP-21 without forewarning or a defector.
