# Ranks, Order requirements and milestones (v1)

All skill minimums are checked against the progression curve: an Average profile meets each
one at the typical age. Every promotion is a story event the player plays; code checks
requirements, rolls the checks, and applies the result. The AI can only propose
(promotionProposed) and narrate.

Era notes (decisions.md, 2026-11-16, L-03):
  Temple: starts before 3,653 BBY use the Jedi Temple on Coruscant under Grand Master Zym.
    Tython is used only for starts from 3,653 BBY (after the Sacking and the Treaty of
    Coruscant).
  The age and trial rules below (Initiate Trials, Padawan selection by 13, Council of
    Reassignment, the five Knight Trials) are a design choice based on prequel-era Legends
    practice (about 32 BBY). They are not attested for the Old Republic Order of 3,661 BBY.

## 1a. Order requirements (mandatory training per rank)
Added by D-42 (Fable review row 28, Padawan line amended by Jared). These hours are fixed
commitments in the routine (training-routines §1); the rest of the daily budget is filled
by the archetype shares (npc-generation §3). Activities: skills-and-activities.md §5.
| Rank | Mandatory training per training day |
|---|---|
| Initiate | 2 h lightsaber forms + 1 h Force (0.5 h Force Control, 0.5 h Force Sense) + 1 h study (instructors) |
| Padawan | 2 h with the master + 1 h solo (main form) + 1 h Force Control and 1 h Force Sense with the master (teacher factor applies). Scaled by age (D-431): the solo hour and the Force Control hour are 0.5 h to 16; Force Control is back to 1 h at 17 and the solo hour at 18 (4 h, 4.5 h, then 5 h), so a young Padawan's day ends under the standard tiredness limit with 0.5-1.5 h of free training |
| Knight and above | none mandatory (missions instead) |
| Sith acolyte (D-427) | the Initiate's hours with the Korriban content: 2 h Form I with the warblade, 0.5 h Force Control, 0.5 h centering, 1 h Sith Code and history (Overseers) |
| Sith apprentice (D-427, D-431) | the Padawan's hours and age scaling: 2 h with the master, the solo form hour, Force Control, and 1 h channelling (Force Output) with the master |
| Daily life (D-427, D-431) | outside the training budget, never trimmed: 0.5 h meditation every training day from Padawan or apprentice on (centered the next day, D-133); from Knight or Lord on, 1 h study (the Code or Force lore) on 3 days of 7 |

## 1. Jedi path
Initiate (youngling)
  From the training start age. Clan training, Order requirements (section 1a).
Initiate Trials (eligibility to be chosen)
  Eligible from age 10 with highest form >= 24 (Shii-Cho for an Initiate; D-118: every "main
  form" minimum reads the highest form) and Force Control >= 14 (amended by D-66,
  recomputed: the Average profile meets both at age 10.8, docs-v2/calc/progression-sim.mjs;
  the old 18 / 15 were met at 8.1 / 11.7). Re-run with D-67 decided (D-102): main form 24 at
  10.9 and Force Control 14 at 11.1, so the minimums stay. Played as a scene; checks at DC
  rating 20.
Padawan selection (playable window)
  From passing the Initiate Trials until age 13.
  Standing S (0-100) = 50% skill percentile for age + 30% notable deeds (engine-set deed
    flags, flag-registry.md) + 20% relationships with Knights and Masters.
    Deeds score (D-424, D-435): 20 a deed to 100 at five; deeds past the fifth still count, with
    diminishing returns toward 130 (100 + 30 x (1 - e^(-(n - 5) / 5)); invented). An average
    Padawan (15th-26th percentile among Padawans) stays under the bypass's 75 even with perfect
    relationships (at most 72); the 60th percentile with relationships +40 reaches it at about 13
    deeds. Less any standing lost to discovered covert learning (D-436, below).
    Skill percentile (amended by D-66): the character's composite (mean of main form, Force
    Control and Force Sense) ranked against a seeded population of 1,000 NPC Initiates of
    the same age, generated by the closed form (npc-generation §1-2: Jedi start ages 2-5,
    Force-trained talent distribution, variance). The population seed is fixed per campaign.
    For reference, the Average profile sits at about the 16th percentile (NPC talent only
    adds speed and NPC start ages average 3.5), computed by docs-v2/calc/padawan-selection.mjs
    (re-run with D-102: 15.7th percentile at 11.1).
  Each month: chance a Knight or Master chooses you = S / 940 (amended by D-66, recomputed by
    D-102 and again by D-120 with the same rule: the Initiate Trial minimums are now met at
    9.2 / 9.3, so the window opens at the Trials' minimum age 10 and runs 36 monthly rolls to 13;
    5.32% a month at S 50, 13.98% never chosen, seeded 14.11%; the Average sits at the 15th-17th
    percentile). Before D-120: S / 610 over 23 rolls. An average-standing Initiate (S 50) has 8.20% per month; over
    the real window (Initiate Trials at 11.1 to age 13 = 23 monthly rolls) 14.0% are never
    chosen (seeded 10,000 runs: 14.05%). D-66's divisor 685 assumed the window opened at 10.8
    (26 rolls); the old divisor 800 gives 22.7% over the new window. Tournaments, demonstrations and
    missions with Knights are story events that raise S through engine-set deed flags.
  Not chosen by 13: the Council of Reassignment sends the Initiate to the Service Corps
    (Agricultural, Medical, Educational or Exploration). Played as a story turning point: accept,
    leave the Order, or a rare later chance (a Knight on a mission takes interest: 2% per
    month while in the Corps, raised by deeds), as with Obi-Wan and Qui-Gon.
Padawan
  Missions with the master; the master is the main teacher (progression spec sec 15).
Knight Trials (five playable trials)
  D-426 (Jared, current rule): the Average is a Padawan from 12 and a Knight at 25. Eligible: at least
    10 years as a Padawan, age 18+, the master's approval, and highest form >= 47, Force Control >= 38,
    Force Sense >= 37, Willpower >= 58 (the Average's levels at 24 by the D-51 rule, re-read after the
    D-431 re-measurement; 47 / 40 / 36 / 56 under D-426). The history below
    is kept for reference.
  Eligible: at least 6 years as a Padawan, age 18+, the master's approval (engine flag
    masterApproval, flag-registry.md), and highest form >= 39, Force Control >= 34, Force Sense
    >= 31, Willpower >= 53 (Willpower amended by D-51: recomputed so the Average profile
    meets it at age 20.3; Force Sense amended by D-102, below; main form amended by D-111,
    below; Force Control, Force Sense and Willpower reset by D-120, below). Computed Average ages with
    D-67 decided (docs-v2/calc/progression-sim.mjs, K = 15.18): main form 38 at 18.3, Force
    Control 30 at 20.6, Willpower 48 at 19.6, Force Sense 26 at 19.6 (26.8 at 20.3).
    Force Sense (D-102, decides D-67): the Average reaches 30 only at age 26.9, so the
    minimum is recomputed by the D-51 / D-66 rule (a minimum stays while the Average meets it
    within +/-2 years of its stated age, test 1; otherwise it becomes the Average's level at
    that age, rounded down): 26. The other minimums stay by the same rule.
    Main form (D-111, Jared): 38 -> 39. Through the src engine (M3 lifetime test, which adds
    synergy spillover) the Average met 38 at 18.24-18.30 depending on the outcome-roll seed,
    2.0-2.06 years before 20.3, at the edge of test 1. It meets 39 at 18.9 (1.4 years early).
    The Sith Lord row mirrors it.
    D-120 (after D-118 / D-119, K = 11.683): the Average now meets Force Control 30 at 17.7, Force
    Sense 26 at 16.8 and Willpower 48 at 17.7, more than 2 years before 20.3. By the D-51 rule
    they become the Average's levels at 20.3, rounded down: Force Control 34 (34.45), Force Sense
    31 (31.21), Willpower 53 (53.94). Highest form 39 (met at about 19.0), the Initiate Trials 24 /
    14 (9.2 / 9.3) and Master 58 (35.2) stay.
  Each trial is a scene with its own checks (rolls spec, DC = 10 + rating/10):
    Skill: a duel or droid gauntlet; win a duel against a rating-45 opponent, or pass
      3 of 4 checks of main form at rating 45.
    Courage: a fear scenario; Willpower check at rating 40 + the scenario's fear level,
      under a fear state of mind if one is triggered.
    Flesh: pain and deprivation; Endurance and Willpower checks at rating 40 while fatigued
      (start the scene at f 0.8); Control Pain helps.
    Spirit: confront one's own darkness; Willpower check at DC 10 + dark pressure / 5 + 4;
      failure adds dark pressure (alignment spec).
    Insight: perceive a hidden truth; Force Sense check at rating 45.
  A failed trial can be retaken after 1 in-game year, at most twice. Failing a third time:
    Service Corps, remain a Padawan in service, or leave the Order (player choice).
  Wartime: the Council counts an engine-set heroicAct flag (flag-registry.md §2: a resolved
    lethal fight won against an opponent 10+ levels stronger, or a Sith Lord or higher) as
    passing Courage and Flesh. It may bypass the trials entirely for exceptional service:
    the Council bypass is an engine-run story event with written conditions
    (flag-registry.md §3: wartime, a heroicAct, standing >= 75, age 18+, Padawan, all Knight
    skill minimums met), never an AI flag (amended by D-33).
Knight
  Missions on their own; may take a Padawan once age 25+ and the Council approves.
Master
  Requires: a Padawan trained to Knighthood, OR exceptional deeds plus a Council decision.
  Skill minimum: main specialty >= 64, the Average's at 40 (63.9; D-437), plus the Knight minimums.
    (58 before, met by the Average at 33.7.)
High Council seat
  Requires: Master rank, a vacancy (story event), main specialty >= 70, and Council standing
    (deeds, relationships). Wartime appointments are possible as story events.
Grand Master: story only.

The Order's rules on Force powers (D-346, D-436; src/data/orderPowerRules.ts)
  Each power has an Order status: open, forbidden (the D-346 class: Electric Judgment, Deadly
    Sight, Morichro from 1000 BBY) or dark side (Force Lightning, Force Choke). Only Jedi-path
    characters are bound.
  A forbidden power needs a Master's permission (forbiddenPermissionGranted: the character's
    master or a Jedi Master; the narrator or the player proposes it, the engine checks it).
    A Master may study it under controlled conditions in its study years (Electric Judgment
    from 990 BBY). A permission opens it in any era (Electric Judgment: the Plo Koon precedent,
    D-449); Electric Judgment is open from 11 ABY (Luke's Praxeum, D-449). A dark-side power is
    never permitted, even to a Master (Jared, D-448).
  Order teachers (the Temple's instructors, the master, a Jedi teacher, Jedi records) refuse a
    power the rules close. Learning it any other way (alone, a Sith text, a non-Order teacher)
    is covert: each session is discovered with chance 3% a session hour (at most 25%), and a
    discovery costs 15 points of standing, fading by 5 a year (invented). The narrator sees
    the rules, the permissions and what the Order knows in the state block.
  Discoveries escalate (D-448; the code tracks, the narrator plays the consequence): the 1st
    discovery brings the Master's reprimand, the 2nd a summons before the Council, the 3rd and
    later the trials held back; any discovered dark-side power brings the Council's judgment
    (possible expulsion). Each discovery's note names the step it newly reaches.

## 2. Sith path (story-driven, harsher)
  Academy acolyte (start age 8-16) -> Apprentice (chosen by a Sith Lord; same standing
    formula, with deeds weighted 50%) -> Lord (recognized by a Darth or the Dark Council after
    a major deed) -> Darth (title granted by the Emperor or Dark Council) -> Dark Council
    (a vacancy, usually by someone's death).
  Academy trials can kill: failed lethal trials use the health spec.
  Skill minimums mirror the Jedi ones (acolyte trials ~ Initiate Trials, Lord ~ Knight).

## 3. Force-sensitive outsiders and non-Force careers
  Outsiders (Gray, independent, untrained): no rank ladder; reputation and teachers come
    through story. Being found by the Jedi or Sith later is a story event.
  Non-Force careers (soldier, smuggler, Mandalorian, bounty hunter...): faction ranks earned
    by years of service, skill minimums matched to the NPC archetype curve, and deeds.

## 4. Milestones (recalibrated)
  Built (D-430): checked monthly after 3 years of training; the Average reference is the closed form for
  the character's archetype, rank and start age with no talent; each milestone fires once as a beat.
  Triggers use pace for age instead of fixed thresholds:
    pace for age = the character's level / the Average profile's level at the same training
    years, per skill.
  "Noticed" (masters take interest): pace for age >= 1.5 in a main skill after 3+ years.
  "Prodigy talk" across the Temple: pace for age >= 2.0 after 3+ years.
  "Ahead of their time": meets the next rank's skill minimums 3+ years before the Average
    age for it.
  Each V170 milestone from legacy JEDI_MILESTONES / SITH_MILESTONES keeps its story hook and
    gets a pace-for-age or deed trigger instead of its old threshold.

## 5. Required tests
 1. The Average profile meets each rank's skill minimums within +/-2 years of the stated age.
 2. About 14% (+/-3) of average-standing Initiates (S 50) are not chosen by 13 (seeded,
    10,000 runs, divisor 940 over the 36-roll window; D-66, recomputed by D-102 and D-120).
 3. Not chosen by 13 always triggers the Council of Reassignment event.
 4. Each Knight Trial uses its stated checks; retakes wait 1 year; a third failure gives the
    three choices.
 5. Wartime: a heroic-act flag counts for Courage and Flesh only.
 6. No promotion is applied without its requirements met (or a Council bypass event whose
    flag-registry §3 conditions all hold).
 7. The AI cannot promote; promotionProposed with unmet requirements is rejected and logged.
 8. Rank-gating flags (masterApproval, heroicAct, trial results, deeds, redemption turning
    point, councilBypassGranted) are never set from the ai source (D-33).
 9. The skill percentile uses the seeded 1,000-NPC population of the same age (D-66).
