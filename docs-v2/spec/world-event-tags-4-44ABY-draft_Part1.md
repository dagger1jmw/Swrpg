## How to read it

**IDs.** Events are `E.<year>.<name>`; outcomes are `O.<name>`. An event *consumes* outcomes (its inputs) and *produces* outcomes that later events consume. That is the network the ripple walks. Year numbers in ids are ABY. Because the BBY maps also write plain years (E.19.order66 is 19 BBY), every id here uses a name no other map uses, so no full id clashes (checked by script against all four earlier maps); whether to mark ABY ids is still open (Part 3, §7).

An outcome is produced at the event's end unless the row says "(produced at its start)" (world-events.md §1); spans that later events need while they run are marked so. "(optional)" after a consumed outcome means the event goes ahead adjusted if it is broken.

**Roles.** `R` = replaceable: if the person is gone, someone of similar standing takes over and nothing downstream changes. `C` = crucial: if they are gone, the event diverges.

**Conditions.** Each has a crucial flag and a guard (world-events.md §2):
- **low:** one scene or a single good roll can break it;
- **medium:** a deliberate effort (a mission, an investigation, a won fight);
- **high:** foreknowledge or extraordinary action; needs **one prerequisite** first, named in the condition (a lead recorded in play);
- **very high:** several steps that must each succeed, or a much harder feat; needs **two distinct prerequisites**, both named.
A prerequisite is always a lead found in play (a figure met, a site found, a fact learned in a scene). Knowledge of the films, novels, comics or games never counts (D-468).

Most conditions also say **who** can reach them (Jared's three tests, below). A condition no player could reach is written **Unbreakable**, with the reason, and the earliest tag that can change it instead.

**Standing state.** Whatever happened before the campaign's start date is `standing`. A campaign that starts inside this map treats every earlier row as standing.

**Plot points.** Events marked **[PP-n]** feed a plot point tracker. Each plot point has a hinge, player-reachable steps scored against the three tests, its unbreakable moments, and 2–4 authored alternatives chosen from game state. Everything else diverges under the narrator's direction from the broken tags.

**Only the player breaks tags.** History otherwise plays out as recorded. A broken event is never re-staged. An investigation counts only from a lead recorded in play.

**Canon heroes are NPCs.** Luke, Leia, Han, Chewbacca, Lando, Mara Jade, Wedge, Corran Horn, Kyle Katarn, Jaden Korr, Talon Karrde, Kyp Durron, Jacen, Jaina and Anakin Solo, Tenel Ka, Ben Skywalker, Vestara Khai, Jagged Fel, Tahiri Veila, Zekk, Raynar Thul, Wynn Dorvan and the rest. A player can stand beside them, be trusted by them, warn them or fight them, never be them. After a divergence each takes a role like their own.

**Spirits and entities.** Exar Kun's spirit, Ragnos's spirit and Abeloth act beyond a player's reach. Steps against them work through the people and places they need (a student alone, a body, a prison, an office), never through out-fighting the entity.

**Unbreakable moments** (declared in place, with the reason; the full list is under "Closed systems" below). The player can still change everything around them, earlier.

**Where this map joins the others.** It starts where the 1000 BBY – 4 ABY map ends (E.4.endor, E.4.fragment, E.4.new_republic) and consumes its outcomes: O.emperor_dead, O.empire_fragmented, O.new_republic, O.alliance, O.han_free, O.luke_trained, O.twins_hidden. It also reads O.hapes_isolated (3640 map, E.3100.hapes) and O.kun_spirit_bound (5000 map, E.3996.yavin). If PP-8 diverged (the Emperor survives Endor, or no Endor), every event here is narrator-directed until a later ruling: this map assumes the recorded Endor. It ends at the close of 44 ABY; *Crucible* (45 ABY) and the Legacy era belong to the next map, which should consume O.one_sith_hidden, O.reige_head_of_state, O.fel_solo_marriage, O.dorvan_chief and O.abeloth_destroyed (Part 3, §1, link 14).

## Design rules from review (D-489; Jared, 24 Nov 2026, and 25 Nov 2026 for non-Force fighters)

These apply to every step in every tag map. Worded as in the reviewed 5000–3662 BBY map, with this map's duelist list.

**Three tests for every step.**
1. *Is it realistic for a PC to be there?*
2. *What standing and power level does the PC need?* (rank, trust and fighting strength, not just presence)
3. *What kind of PC?* (which side, which role, which ties: Jedi, pilot, intelligence agent, Imperial officer, smuggler, senator's aide, Yuuzhan Vong-side collaborator, Sith of the Lost Tribe)

Jared's examples: the Death Star throne room is unbreakable except by the Emperor's or Vader's death earlier, because no PC would be there; the Ewoks on Endor break only if Leia is taken out on the speeder or earlier; Anakin's fall needs a High Council-level Jedi in good standing and in Anakin's trust. A step that fails test 1 is not a step: the moment is a closed system.

**Top duelists (standing rule, all maps).** Any fight with a top duelist needs at least a Master. In this map the top duelists are **Luke Skywalker at his height** (from his return from Byss in 10 ABY; Ch14–Ch17 put him near-apex from then and at the mortal apex by 43 ABY), **Palpatine reborn**, **Joruus C'baoth** (raw power more than skill, but the review puts beating him in the top tier: Rev PP-24), **Exar Kun's spirit**, **Lumiya** (Rev PP-33), **Jacen Solo from 29 ABY and Darth Caedus** (Rev PP-32), **Lomi Plo**, **Darth Krayt**, and, beyond any duelist, **Abeloth** (no mortal fights her alone; it took Luke and Krayt together). The review also puts facing **Luke and Mara together** in the top tier (Mara near it: Rev PP-29). Three tiers:
- **A regular Master** only holds one off briefly, like Kit Fisto against Sidious: enough to buy a moment, never to win.
- **Top tier:** beating one, or holding one off for long, needs a Mace-level character (combat 88, Force Control 85, Willpower 90: D-494). In this map "top tier" marks such a route. It is a power bar on top of the guard, not a guard level of its own: a PC below it fails however the rolls go.
- **Apex tier (Jared, 25 Nov 2026, D-494, D-495):** above the top duelists: combat 98, Force Control 110, Willpower 105. Yoda and Sidious; the Dark Times Vader, Mace and the freed Revan are just under it. Only another apex beats an apex duelist; a top-tier fighter only buys time; a Master does not even hold one off. On this map: **Palpatine reborn** (Sidious). Whether Luke at his height and others on this map are is not ruled yet.

Below the list: **at least a Master** to fight Jerec and Desann (High Master tier) and Kyp Durron under Kun. **At least Knight-level to stop by force** (not top tier): **Tahiri Veila** in 41 ABY (Rev PP-34) and **Alema Rar** in 40 ABY (Rev PP-35).

**Non-Force fighters (Jared, 25 Nov 2026).** In an outright fight a non-Force fighter never reaches Master level against a top duelist. Only the most skilled bounty hunters and Mandalorians can reach the Master level, and only with planning recorded in play (an ambush, a trap, prepared ground or gear). Even then they buy time, never win.

**No premonitions.** A vision, premonition or "sense in the Force" is never used to justify a step or counted as a lead. That would be the narrator changing the story. Canon visions (Jacen's flow-walking and his Mind Walkers' vision, Luke's foresight) are part of the record and are NPCs' motives, never a player's lead. The Yuuzhan Vong are invisible to the Force (Ch15): no Jedi, player or not, senses the invasion coming or a Vong agent in a room.

**Closed systems.** A scene no PC would realistically be in is unbreakable. It can only be changed earlier, by a step that changes who gets there or whether the scene happens. In this map: Palpatine's spirit passing to Byss at Endor; Luke going to Byss (his duty as a Jedi: Rev PP-25) and his choice in the throne room there (10 ABY); Brand's sacrifice at Onderon (an instant in the Force: Rev PP-25); the *Lusankya* as a search target under Coruscant (Rev PP-22); Han's shot into the *Iron Fist*'s bridge at Dathomir (his act: Rev PP-23); the *Chimaera*'s bridge when Rukh strikes (9 ABY); Luke's fight with Kun's spirit around his own body; Anakin Solo's Force act at the Drall repulsor and the *Intruder*'s last act (Ossilege and Captison's choice) in 18 ABY (Rev PP-28); Luke and Mara on Nirauan (Rev PP-29); Chewbacca's death and the moon's fall at Sernpidal (Rev PP-30); Luke's fight with Shimrra and Onimi's attack in the citadel (Rev PP-31); Luke's duel with Lomi Plo; Mara hunting Lumiya's apprentice alone, and Jacen and Mara on Kavan (40 ABY; Rev PP-33); Jaina and Caedus aboard the *Anakin Solo* and Luke persuading the Remnant at Uroro (41 ABY; Rev PP-34); Wynn Dorvan killing the Kem body (Rev PP-35); the realm "beyond shadows" where Luke and Krayt fight Abeloth (44 ABY); and Abeloth's own acts at a distance (the psychosis).
