## 2. Major plot points (reviewed)

Numbered after the other maps (PP-0 to PP-35). Written to the reworked pattern and to Jared's review notes: every hinge breaks only through steps a player can reach; every step names who the player has to be, scored against the three tests (*There* / *Standing* / *Kind*); closed moments are named; nothing re-stages history; nothing rests on a premonition. Approved by Jared with the changes in the review notes of 25 Nov 2026 (docs-v2/reports/tagmap-review-notes-45-138ABY.md). Guards follow world-events.md §2; every count or threshold in a step is *invented*. Alternatives' outcomes, marked "(new)", appear in no table.

### PP-36: The Ossus Project's sabotage (122–127 ABY)
- **Hinge:** the Vongspawn sabotage stays hidden until it mutates the project worlds; the Vong are blamed, the Sith go unproven, and the Moffs invoke the Treaty of Anaxes: the Sith–Imperial War (O.vong_blamed, O.sith_imperial_war).
- **Closed:** Roan Fel's reluctant consent to the war (his own choice; he could not stop the Moff Council's declaration, so no persuasion of Fel is a step: E.127.war_declared is unbreakable that way); Maladi's laboratories on Korriban.
- **Who the player can be:** a Jedi escort, Shaper or Myneyrsh guide working on Wayland; a Jedi, Shaper or Alliance intelligence officer in Nei Rin's investigation; an Imperial Intelligence officer, Moff's aide or Imperial Knight who can put proof before the Moff Council's moderates. Kol, Nei Rin, Maladi, Zenoc Quah, Veed, Calixte and Roan Fel are NPCs. Quah is a renegade Shaper hiding in Wayland's wilds from the end of the Vong War until 127, working with Maladi; he is on no project team.
- **The steps:**
  1. **Quah's seeding** (E.122.vongspawn_sabotage, very high; prerequisites: a place among the project's workers on Wayland, and a lead on Zenoc Quah (his traces found in Wayland's wilds, or him met)). *There:* realistic for a Jedi escort, a Shaper or a Myneyrsh guide on Wayland. *Standing:* trusted by Kol or Nei Rin; the proof must reach them. *Kind:* Jedi, Vong Shaper caste, Myneyrsh. Breaks the hinge (alternative 1).
  2. **Wayland** (E.126.wayland_skirmish, medium). Keeping Nei Rin from Roax. Adjusts only.
  3. **The proof** (E.127.sabotage_found, high; prerequisite: a place in Nei Rin's investigation, through ties found in play). The route is Nei Rin's own investigation: she set out to learn how the sabotage was done and came back with proof the day after the Massacre at Ossus, too late. *There:* a player in her party, or a Jedi or Shaper working with her. *Standing:* believed by the High Council and the Triumvirs, or made public, or, the Imperial route, put before the Moff Council's moderates: Knight or officer standing or the physical proof. *Kind:* Jedi, Shaper, Alliance intelligence; for the Imperial route an Imperial Intelligence officer, a Moff's aide or an Imperial Knight. **Proof before the war** breaks the hinge (alternative 2): it removes the Moffs' pretext. **Proof later, 128–130,** does not stop the war: carried to Fel or the Imperial Knights it is a prerequisite for PP-37 step 2, and it can reach Ossus before the massacre (PP-38, step 1's other road).
- **What breaks the hinge:** step 1, or step 3 before the war. E.127.war_declared has no step of its own: its only break is step 3's proof.
- **Alternatives:**
  1. **The sabotage caught at its root** (step 1). Quah is taken before the mutations show; the project goes on and Wayland stays green. The trail leads toward the Sith, but no war has a pretext; the One Sith, exposed in part, must find another road to the throne (narrator-directed). O.project_saved (new).
  2. **The Sith named** (step 3, before the war). The Vong are not blamed; worlds do not secede on their account; the Alliance and the Jedi turn on an unseen Sith enemy. The Moffs lack their pretext, whether the proof reached the Alliance, the public or the Moff Council's moderates: E.127.war_declared loses its input, and the narrator directs whether they find another. O.sith_exposed (new).

### PP-37: The One Sith's war (128–130 ABY)
- **Hinge:** the One Sith join the war through Calixte, behind Fel's back, and turn it; the Alliance is broken at Caamas and surrenders, and Coruscant falls (O.sith_imperial_pact, O.alliance_surrendered).
- **Closed:** none of the steps is a closed system; the pact's making is reachable only from inside Calixte's or Veed's household. Coruscant's fall itself follows Caamas (no fleet battle on record).
- **Who the player can be:** an Imperial Intelligence officer or Moff's aide, an Imperial Knight, an Alliance intelligence officer, a Core Fleet captain, a Jedi at the Coruscant Temple, a Mandalorian in Chernan Ordo's service. Calixte, Veed, Krayt, Stazi, Nict and the Mandalores are NPCs.
- **The steps:**
  1. **Botajef** (E.127.botajef, high; prerequisite: a lead on Yaga Auchs's contact). *There:* a Mandalorian signals officer or clan warrior at Botajef. *Standing:* close enough to Ordo to be heeded. *Kind:* Mandalorian. Adjusts only: the Mandalorians stay in the war on the Alliance side.
  2. **The pact** (E.128.one_sith_pact, very high; prerequisites: a post on Calixte's or Veed's staff, and proof of the Sith meeting or Nei Rin's proof got sooner (PP-36 step 3)). Ruled: the pact was made behind Fel's back (W: Morrigan Corde; §4). *There:* realistic for an Imperial Intelligence officer or a Moff's aide. *Standing:* trusted inside the household, and believed by Fel or Draco's Knights (an officer's standing or the proof). *Kind:* Imperial. Breaks the hinge (alternative 1) and PP-39's.
  3. **The Jedi Temple** (E.130.coruscant_falls, medium, adjusts only). No fleet battle is on record: Coruscant falls because Caamas is lost, and the Sith attack the Jedi Temple. *There:* a Jedi Knight or Master at the Temple. *Standing:* Knight. *Kind:* Jedi. Changes who escapes (Rasi Tuum, Azlyn Rae and others), not the fall.
  4. **Caamas** (E.130.caamas, very high; prerequisites: command rank in Nict's or Stazi's fleet, and a lead on Veed's plan from intelligence work recorded in play (a captured order, an intercepted signal)). *There:* a Core Fleet captain. *Standing:* fleet command or the ear of whoever has it. *Kind:* Alliance naval. Breaks the hinge (alternative 2).
- **What breaks the hinge:** step 2 or 4; step 3 adjusts only.
- **Alternatives:**
  1. **Fel moves against the plotters** (step 2). Fel, shown the proof, moves against Calixte and Veed with his Imperial Knights (he later meant to arrest Veed anyway). Whether the Moffs still join the Sith is narrator-directed; the One Sith are now known to Fel, and E.130.krayt_coup loses its inputs. O.pact_refused (new).
  2. **The Alliance holds** (step 4, Caamas alone). No defeat at Caamas: Coruscant does not fall (E.130.coruscant_falls loses its input) and there is no surrender in 130: no Jedi summons, no "diplomatic meeting" on Ossus (E.130.jedi_summons loses its input) and no victory council for Krayt's coup. The war goes on under the narrator, with the Sith fighting openly beside the Moffs. O.alliance_holds (new).

### PP-38: The Massacre at Ossus (130 ABY)
- **Hinge:** the diplomatic meeting on Ossus becomes a massacre; Kol Skywalker dies; about half the Order survives scattered; Cade is left behind (O.ossus_massacre, O.kol_dead, O.cade_presumed_dead).
- **Closed:** Cade's dark transfer on Sazen and Krayt's sensing it (Cade's act; lore, never a lead); Kol's choice to stay and fight alone to cover the shuttles; Cade's choice to turn back alone after sensing Kol's death. No Jedi on Ossus can know from the Jedi side that the meeting is a trap, and no premonition counts.
- **Who the player can be:** a Jedi Knight, Master or Padawan at the academy, a top duelist among the Jedi, a pilot of the X-83 cover or a J-1 shuttle, a Yuuzhan Vong Shaper or warrior, an Imperial officer in Rulf Yage's force, an officer or Knight of Fel's household, a Sith in Nihl's force, a companion of Nei Rin. Kol, Cade, Sazen, Shado Vao, K'Kruhk, Nihl and Yage are NPCs; Nihl and Kol are top duelists; Sazen is a Master.
- **The steps:**
  1. **The trap** (E.130.ossus_massacre, very high; prerequisites: a post in Yage's force or Nihl's with a lead that the meeting is a trap, and a way to act before the attack). Fel had ordered no hostilities; Maladi passed Krayt's order to Veed, who countermanded Fel. Two ways to act: reach the Jedi before they gather (a Jedi contact met), or put proof of Veed's countermand before Fel, through his household, before the attack, which lets Fel stop Yage. *There:* realistic for an Imperial officer of the task force. *Standing:* close enough to the orders to know them. *Kind:* an Imperial officer who defects, a Sith acolyte who turns, an officer or Knight of Fel's household for the Fel route. Breaks the hinge (alternative 1). **Another road:** Nei Rin's proof came one day late; if a player gets her back sooner (PP-36 step 3), the proof reaches Ossus before the attack and gives the Jedi a reason to scatter: alternative 1 by another road.
  2. **Kol's last stand** (E.130.kol_dies, very high; prerequisites: a Jedi beside Kol in the stand, and top-duelist power). Sazen, a Master, already stood beside Kol and was overpowered by Nihl: a Master beside him changes nothing. Saving Kol needs a top duelist at his side (two top duelists against Nihl). *There:* realistic for an academy instructor or Council member. *Standing:* a top duelist. *Kind:* Jedi. Kol's choice to stay stays his. Kol reaches the shuttles (alternative 2).
  3. **Cade left behind** (E.130.cade_lost, medium). Cade fought at Kol's side, carried Sazen to the last shuttle, then turned back alone in an X-83 after sensing Kol's death (his choice), was shot down and ejected. The routes: a wingman who covers his dogfight, or a pilot who recovers him after he ejects. *There:* a Jedi pilot in the cover flight or a J-1 shuttle's crew. *Standing:* a pilot, Padawan to Knight. *Kind:* Jedi. Cade leaves with the Order (alternative 3).
- **What breaks the hinge:** step 1 (or Nei Rin's proof in time); steps 2 and 3 break its halves (Kol's death, Cade's loss).
- **Alternatives:**
  1. **The Jedi warned** (step 1, or Nei Rin's proof in time). The Jedi do not gather on Ossus, or Fel stops Yage; the Order scatters nearly whole and Kol leads it into hiding; O.third_jedi_purge is lost with the massacre, so the narrator starts the hunt from Krayt's bounties (§7, item 4). Krayt's coup still has its inputs. O.ossus_warned (new).
  2. **Kol lives** (step 2). The massacre happens; Kol escapes with the younglings and leads the hidden Order; Cade is not orphaned of his father, and his drift to Rav is narrator-directed. O.kol_lives (new).
  3. **Cade with the Order** (step 3). Cade is covered or recovered and leaves with Vao and Sazen; no Rav, no looting, no *Mynock*: E.130.rav_finds_cade loses its input and the *Mynock* chain (E.>130.mynock, E.137.hosk_captured and on) is cancelled or narrator-directed. Krayt's hunt in 137 finds him among the hidden Jedi. O.cade_with_jedi (new).

### PP-39: Krayt's coup (130 ABY)
- **Hinge:** at the victory council Krayt kills Fel's double and his Knights and takes the throne, while Fel, warned by Calixte, escapes to found the Empire-in-exile (O.krayt_emperor, O.fel_escapes).
- **Closed:** the Moff Council chamber (Krayt, an apex duelist, with three Sith Lords; no one on the player's side reaches apex and a top duelist only buys time; a player Imperial Knight on the guard dies with the four); Calixte's choice to warn Fel, which she made in person.
- **Who the player can be:** an Imperial Knight, an officer of Fel's household or Imperial Intelligence, a Moff's aide, a Sith in Maladi's intelligence service. Fel, Calixte, Draco, Krayt and his Lords are NPCs.
- **The steps:**
  1. **The pact exposed** (PP-37 step 2). Exposed before the surrender, it breaks this hinge too.
  2. **The warning stopped** (E.130.calixte_warns, very high; prerequisites: a post in Sith Intelligence watching Calixte, and a lead that she is going to Fel). Calixte warned Fel in person, in his quarters, while Veed celebrated; stopping her means a Sith-side agent intercepting her on the way. *There:* a Sith-side intelligence agent. *Standing:* Maladi's trust. *Kind:* One Sith. Fel goes to the council himself (alternative 1). Fel already suspected Krayt and Veed and meant to arrest Veed, so alternative 1 is weaker than drafted; it stays, very high.
  3. **Fel forewarned with proof** (E.130.calixte_warns, the same condition reached from the other side: very high; prerequisites: an Imperial Knight or Intelligence officer in Fel's trust, and proof of Krayt's intent (the pact's terms, a Sith order for the council)). *There:* realistic for a Knight of the household. *Standing:* Fel's trust. *Kind:* Imperial Knight, Imperial officer. Fel moves before the council (alternative 2). The warning Calixte gives stays hers; this step only matters if it comes earlier and with proof. He already suspected, so proof earlier makes alternative 2 likely.
- **What breaks the hinge:** step 1, 2 or 3.
- **Alternatives:**
  1. **Fel dies at the council** (step 2). Krayt kills Roan Fel himself; the loyalists rally to Marasiah, with Draco's Knights at her side (narrator-directed); the Empire-in-exile is weaker and led by a girl: E.130.empire_split fires adjusted. Weaker than drafted, since Fel already suspected. O.roan_fel_killed (new).
  2. **Fel strikes first** (step 3, or step 1). Fel arrests or flees Calixte's and Veed's faction before the council and holds Bastion and the loyal fleets; Krayt takes Coruscant by force rather than by the Moffs' oath; the civil war starts with a stronger Fel and a divided Moff Council. O.fel_forewarned (new).
