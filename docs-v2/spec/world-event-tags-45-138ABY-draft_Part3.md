## 2. Major plot points (proposed)

Numbered after the other maps (PP-0 to PP-35). Written to the reworked pattern and to Jared's review notes: every hinge breaks only through steps a player can reach; every step names who the player has to be, scored against the three tests (*There* / *Standing* / *Kind*); closed moments are named; nothing re-stages history; nothing rests on a premonition. All alternatives are drafts for Jared. Guards follow world-events.md §2; every count or threshold in a step is *invented*. Alternatives' outcomes, marked "(new)", appear in no table.

### PP-36: The Ossus Project's sabotage (122–127 ABY)
- **Hinge:** the Vongspawn sabotage stays hidden until it mutates the project worlds; the Vong are blamed, the Sith go unproven, and the Moffs invoke the Treaty of Anaxes: the Sith–Imperial War (O.vong_blamed, O.sith_imperial_war).
- **Closed:** Roan Fel's reluctant consent to the war (his own choice); Maladi's laboratories on Korriban.
- **Who the player can be:** a Jedi Knight escort on a project world, a Yuuzhan Vong Shaper or warrior working with Nei Rin, an official of a project world, an Alliance intelligence officer, an Imperial Intelligence officer or Moff's aide, an Imperial Knight. Kol, Nei Rin, Maladi, Zenoc Quah, Veed, Calixte and Roan Fel are NPCs.
- **The steps:**
  1. **Quah's seeding** (E.122.vongspawn_sabotage, very high; prerequisites: a place on a project team, and a lead on Zenoc Quah). *There:* realistic; a hundred worlds, each with Shapers and Jedi escorts. *Standing:* a Knight escort or a Shaper trusted by Nei Rin; the proof must reach Kol or Nei Rin. *Kind:* Jedi, Vong Shaper caste, project-world official. Breaks the hinge (alternative 1).
  2. **Wayland** (E.126.wayland_skirmish, medium). Keeping Nei Rin from Roax. Adjusts only.
  3. **The proof** (E.127.sabotage_found, high; prerequisite: the lead from step 1 or Quah met). *There:* a Jedi investigator or a Shaper. *Standing:* believed by the High Council and the Triumvirs: Knight or officer standing or the physical proof. *Kind:* Jedi, Shaper, Alliance intelligence. Breaks the hinge (alternative 2).
  4. **The Moffs' war** (E.127.war_declared, high; prerequisite: proof of the Sith hand, or of Maladi's meetings with Veed or Calixte). *There:* an Imperial Intelligence officer under Calixte, a Moff's aide, an Imperial Knight. *Standing:* a voice that reaches Fel and the Moff Council; Fel's consent itself is closed, so the step is the proof put before him. *Kind:* Imperial. Breaks the hinge (alternative 3).
- **What breaks the hinge:** step 1, 3 or 4.
- **Alternatives:**
  1. **The sabotage caught at its root** (step 1). Quah is taken before the mutations show; the project goes on and Wayland stays green. The trail leads toward the Sith, but no war has a pretext; the One Sith, exposed in part, must find another road to the throne (narrator-directed). O.project_saved (new).
  2. **The Sith named** (step 3). The Vong are not blamed; worlds do not secede on their account; the Alliance and the Jedi turn on an unseen Sith enemy. The Moffs lack their pretext: E.127.war_declared loses its input, and the narrator directs whether they find another. O.sith_exposed (new).
  3. **No war in 127** (step 4). The Moffs' frame and their tie to Maladi are put before Fel; the cold war goes on; Fel's Knights move against Veed's and Calixte's faction (narrator-directed). PP-37 does not happen as recorded. O.moffs_exposed (new).

### PP-37: The One Sith's war (128–130 ABY)
- **Hinge:** the One Sith join the war through Calixte, turn it, take Coruscant, and the Alliance surrenders after Caamas (O.sith_imperial_pact, O.alliance_surrendered).
- **Closed:** none of the steps is a closed system; the pact's making is reachable only from inside Calixte's or Veed's household.
- **Who the player can be:** an Imperial Intelligence officer or Moff's aide, an Imperial Knight, an Alliance Defense Force officer, a Core Fleet captain, a Jedi Master at the Coruscant Temple, a Mandalorian in Chernan Ordo's service. Calixte, Veed, Krayt, Stazi, Nict and the Mandalores are NPCs.
- **The steps:**
  1. **Botajef** (E.127.botajef, high; prerequisite: a lead on Yaga Auchs's contact). *There:* a Mandalorian signals officer or clan warrior at Botajef. *Standing:* close enough to Ordo to be heeded. *Kind:* Mandalorian. Adjusts only: the Mandalorians stay in the war on the Alliance side.
  2. **The pact** (E.128.one_sith_pact, very high; prerequisites: a post on Calixte's or Veed's staff, and proof of the Sith meeting). *There:* realistic for an Imperial Intelligence officer or a Moff's aide. *Standing:* trusted inside the household, and believed by Fel or Draco's Knights (an officer's standing or the proof). *Kind:* Imperial. Breaks the hinge (alternative 1) and PP-39's.
  3. **Coruscant** (E.130.coruscant_falls, high; prerequisite: a lead on the assault's plan). *There:* an Alliance officer in the defence, or a Jedi Master of the Temple. *Standing:* command in the defence; a trooper or Padawan cannot change it. *Kind:* Alliance military, Jedi. With step 4, alternative 2.
  4. **Caamas** (E.130.caamas, very high; prerequisites: command rank in Nict's or Stazi's fleet, and a lead on Veed's plan). *There:* a Core Fleet captain. *Standing:* fleet command or the ear of whoever has it. *Kind:* Alliance naval. Breaks the hinge (alternative 2).
- **What breaks the hinge:** step 2 or 4; step 3 alone changes where the Temple falls, not the surrender.
- **Alternatives:**
  1. **Fel refuses the Sith** (step 2). The war stays a conventional one, which the Alliance was winning; it ends in a negotiated peace (narrator-directed). Fel, warned, sets his Knights against the One Sith, now known to him; Krayt's road to the throne goes another way. E.130.krayt_coup loses its inputs. O.pact_refused (new).
  2. **The Alliance holds** (step 4, or steps 3 and 4). No surrender in 130: no Jedi summons, no "diplomatic meeting" on Ossus (E.130.jedi_summons loses its input) and no victory council for Krayt's coup. The war goes on under the narrator, with the Sith fighting openly beside the Moffs. O.alliance_holds (new).

### PP-38: The Massacre at Ossus (130 ABY)
- **Hinge:** the diplomatic meeting on Ossus becomes a massacre; Kol Skywalker dies; about half the Order survives scattered; Cade is left behind (O.ossus_massacre, O.kol_dead, O.cade_presumed_dead).
- **Closed:** Cade's dark transfer on Sazen and Krayt's sensing it (Cade's act; lore, never a lead); Kol's choice to make his last stand. No Jedi on Ossus can know the meeting is a trap: there is no lead on the Jedi side, and no premonition counts.
- **Who the player can be:** a Jedi Knight, Master or Padawan at the academy, a pilot of the X-83 cover or a J-1 shuttle, a Yuuzhan Vong Shaper or warrior, an Imperial officer in Rulf Yage's force, a Sith in Nihl's. Kol, Cade, Sazen, Shado Vao, K'Kruhk, Nihl and Yage are NPCs; Nihl and Kol are top duelists.
- **The steps:**
  1. **The trap** (E.130.ossus_massacre, very high; prerequisites: a post in Yage's force or Nihl's with a lead that the meeting is a trap, and a way to reach the Jedi before they gather). *There:* realistic for an Imperial officer of the task force. *Standing:* close enough to the orders to know them; the attack breaks Fel's own orders, so a warning can also go through Fel's household. *Kind:* an Imperial officer who defects, or a Sith acolyte who turns. Breaks the hinge (alternative 1).
  2. **Kol's last stand** (E.130.kol_dies, very high; prerequisites: a Jedi Knight or Master beside Kol in the stand, and Master-level power to hold Nihl). *There:* realistic for an academy instructor or Council member. *Standing:* Master-level; Nihl is a top duelist, so the player buys time, never wins. *Kind:* Jedi. Kol reaches the shuttles (alternative 2).
  3. **Cade left behind** (E.130.cade_lost, medium). *There:* a Jedi pilot in the cover flight or the shuttle's crew. *Standing:* a pilot, Padawan to Knight. *Kind:* Jedi. Cade leaves with Vao (alternative 3).
- **What breaks the hinge:** step 1; steps 2 and 3 break its halves (Kol's death, Cade's loss).
- **Alternatives:**
  1. **The Jedi warned** (step 1). The Jedi do not gather on Ossus; the Order scatters nearly whole and Kol leads it into hiding; O.third_jedi_purge is lost with the massacre, so the narrator starts the hunt from Krayt's bounties (§7, item 4). Krayt's coup still has its inputs. O.ossus_warned (new).
  2. **Kol lives** (step 2). The massacre happens; Kol escapes with the younglings and leads the hidden Order; Cade is not orphaned of his father, and his drift to Rav is narrator-directed. O.kol_lives (new).
  3. **Cade with the Order** (step 3). Cade leaves with Vao and Sazen; no Rav, no looting, no *Mynock*: E.130.rav_finds_cade loses its input and the *Mynock* chain (E.>130.mynock, E.137.hosk_captured and on) is cancelled or narrator-directed. Krayt's hunt in 137 finds him among the hidden Jedi. O.cade_with_jedi (new).

### PP-39: Krayt's coup (130 ABY)
- **Hinge:** at the victory council Krayt kills Fel's double and his Knights and takes the throne, while Fel, warned by Calixte, escapes to found the Empire-in-exile (O.krayt_emperor, O.fel_escapes).
- **Closed:** the Moff Council chamber (Krayt beyond any duelist, with three Sith Lords; a player Imperial Knight on the guard dies with the four); Calixte's choice to warn Fel.
- **Who the player can be:** an Imperial Knight, an officer of Fel's household or Imperial Intelligence, a Moff's aide, a Sith in Maladi's intelligence service. Fel, Calixte, Draco, Krayt and his Lords are NPCs.
- **The steps:**
  1. **The pact exposed** (PP-37 step 2). Exposed before the surrender, it breaks this hinge too.
  2. **The warning stopped** (E.130.calixte_warns, very high; prerequisites: a post in Sith Intelligence watching Calixte, and a lead on her channel to Fel's household). *There:* a Sith-side intelligence agent. *Standing:* Maladi's trust. *Kind:* One Sith. Fel goes to the council himself (alternative 1).
  3. **Fel forewarned with proof** (E.130.calixte_warns, the same condition reached from the other side: very high; prerequisites: an Imperial Knight or Intelligence officer in Fel's trust, and proof of Krayt's intent (the pact's terms, a Sith order for the council)). *There:* realistic for a Knight of the household. *Standing:* Fel's trust. *Kind:* Imperial Knight, Imperial officer. Fel moves before the council (alternative 2). The warning Calixte gives stays hers; this step only matters if it comes earlier and with proof.
- **What breaks the hinge:** step 1, 2 or 3.
- **Alternatives:**
  1. **Fel dies at the council** (step 2). Krayt kills Roan Fel himself; the loyalists rally to Marasiah, with Draco's Knights at her side (narrator-directed); the Empire-in-exile is weaker and led by a girl: E.130.empire_split fires adjusted. O.roan_fel_killed (new).
  2. **Fel strikes first** (step 3, or step 1). Fel arrests or flees Calixte's and Veed's faction before the council and holds Bastion and the loyal fleets; Krayt takes Coruscant by force rather than by the Moffs' oath; the civil war starts with a stronger Fel and a divided Moff Council. O.fel_forewarned (new).

Cade, Jariah, Deliah, Rav, Hosk, Corde, Talon, Nihl and Krayt are NPCs; the *Mynock*'s crew is the canon three.
- **The steps:**
  1. **Hosk** (E.137.hosk_captured, medium). *There:* on Lok or Socorro: realistic. *Standing:* a ship and a blaster; a Jedi contact needs a lead (Hosk met). *Kind:* bounty hunter, Jedi in hiding. Hosk is not sold (alternative 1).
  2. **The capture** (E.137.temple_capture, high; prerequisite: a place in Cade's party into the Temple). *There:* a guide or hireling. *Standing:* able to keep up; Nihl is a top duelist, so the step is the way out, never the fight. *Kind:* underworld guide, bounty hunter. Cade and Hosk get out (alternative 2).
  3. **Corde kept out** (E.137.cade_escapes, very high; prerequisites: a post in the Temple's security or Maladi's household, and a lead on Corde's double game). *There:* a Sith-side player. *Standing:* trusted in the Temple. *Kind:* One Sith, Imperial Intelligence.   1. **Hosk never sold** (step 1). Cade has no reason to go to Coruscant; Hosk lives; Krayt's hunt goes on, and Cade's road to Iego and the Hidden Temple comes later and another way (narrator-directed). O.hosk_free (new).

  3. **Cade kept** (step 3). Corde is unmasked; Krayt holds a Skywalker healer; whether Cade submits is narrator-directed (he is an NPC). Krayt's illness may be eased: Had Abbadon's lure (E.137.had_abbadon_lure) loses its input. The darkest branch in the map. O.cade_kept (new).

The assault fails (alternative 3). A player on the allied side only helps the record along.
