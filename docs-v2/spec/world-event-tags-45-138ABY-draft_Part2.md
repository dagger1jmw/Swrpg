## 6. *Legacy*, 137 ABY **[PP-40] [PP-41] [PP-42]**

Krayt sends his Sith after Princess Marasiah Fel and after Cade. Cade, a bounty hunter, sells the Jedi healer Hosk Trey'lis, is drawn to Coruscant to free him, is captured, tempted and escapes when his mother shoots Krayt. Fel retakes Bastion. The Remnant wins a prototype Star Destroyer at Dac; Krayt orders the Mon Calamari exterminated. On Had Abbadon Jedi and Imperial Knights trap Krayt; Wyyrlok kills him, hides the death and rules as Regent; his Final Protocol kills Dac, and 20% of its people are evacuated. Fel, the Jedi and Stazi ally on Agamar; Chalk betrays them; Cade, on Wayland, turns from the dark side (T45 P2; R-c 5, 7; W: Genocide on Dac, Final Protocol, Battle on Had Abbadon, Ambush on Daluuj).
