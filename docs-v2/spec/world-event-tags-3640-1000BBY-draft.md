# World event tag map, 3640 – 1000 BBY (draft for review)

The years between the two existing maps: the end of the Galactic War, the Eternal Empire, the third Galactic War, the long quiet after the Sith Empire vanishes, the New Sith Wars and the road to Ruusan. Same format and rules as world-event-tags-draft.md (3661–3640) and world-event-tags-1000BBY-4ABY-draft.md. Drafted by Claude, 23 Nov 2026. **Reviewed by Jared 23 Nov 2026; all rulings are recorded in place and in section 10. Brought to D-489's design rules on 24 Nov 2026 (D-490, D-492): section 11, and marked [D-490] in place.**

**Sources and accuracy**
- **W:** checked against the Legends Wookieepedia page named (fetched 23 Nov 2026 through the API).
- **LT:** taken from SW_Timeline_3700_3600BBY_Part2.md (lore batch 20, each row cited to Wookieepedia year or event pages). Not re-checked today, but already sourced.
- **LF:** taken from the old SW_Legends_Galaxy_Timeline.md (retired, D-484; its rows are in docs-v2/lore/superseded/, and the era sets SW_Timeline_* replace them) and **not re-checked**. Lower confidence.
- **T:** the overnight timeline drafts in Docs/SWRPG lore (SW_Timeline_3600_2000BBY, SW_Timeline_2000_1000BBY; 24 Nov 2026, drafts for review), each row cited to Wookieepedia.
- **W-TL:** the Legends Wookieepedia page *Timeline of galactic history/Legends* (one line, no event article checked).
- **Tags are design calls**, not lore: replaceable or crucial, the guards, and the plot point alternatives.
- **Lore is thin from 3621 to 2000 BBY.** Wookieepedia has no year pages for 3621–3600 and only scattered dated events until 2000 BBY. Where the lore is silent the map is silent (section 3); the gaps are listed in section 9 for docs-v2/lore/RESEARCH_QUEUE.md.
- **Corrections and conflicts** found while checking are in section 8.

**Notation** (as in the other maps). `E.<year>.<name>` = event; `O.<name>` = outcome. `R` = replaceable; `C` = crucial. Guards: low / medium / high / **very high**. Very high (ruled by Jared, 23 Nov 2026) means harder than high: it takes several steps that each have to succeed, or a feat that is much harder in general (for example, holding the strongest duellist alive in place until a thought bomb goes off). Used for a Sith player character uniting the clans (PP-12) and Bane caught in the bomb (PP-13). The level also needs adding to the 3661 map's "How to read it", the M8 design notes and Phase A's guard-to-check mapping. **[PP-n]** = feeds a major plot point tracker (section 6). Years are BBY.

**Protagonists and canon heroes are NPCs** (rulings 23 Nov 2026). SWTOR protagonists (the Outlander/Commander, Hero of Tython, Emperor's Wrath and others) and book or comic heroes of this era (Kerra Holt, Darth Bane's opponents, Lord Hoth) are NPCs. After a divergence each takes a role like their own.

**Where this map joins the others.** It starts after the last 3661-map row (E.3640.janarus_dies) and consumes that map's outcomes (O.galactic_war, O.dark_council_rules, O.emperor_trapped, O.malgus_defeated, O.revan_free, O.saresh_chancellor, O.dread_masters_free). It ends where the 1000 BBY map begins: its Ruusan outcomes feed E.1000.thought_bomb and E.1000.reformation.

## 5. Links across the eras

1. **Vitiate's end (3640 → 3626).** Valkorion, his main host, rules Zakuul throughout. His other presence, the Sith Emperor, is trapped on Voss and then driven out and left dormant when the Voice dies on Dromund Kaas (3661 map) → restored by feeding on the slaughter at Yavin 4 (3637) → Ziost (3636) → Valkorion's body struck down in the throne room and his spirit lodged in the Outlander (3636) → destroyed in the Commander's mind on Zakuul (3630) → Tenebrae destroyed (3626). Every link is crucial for the next. If Vitiate survives, PP-10 never resolves and nothing after it in this map can be assumed.
2. **The Eternal Fleet (3636 → 3628).** Arcann's control of the Throne → the conquest (O.zakuul_dominant) → the Alliance takes the Fleet (3630) → loses it to Zildrog (3629) → the opening for the third Galactic War. If the Alliance keeps the Fleet, the third war has much less reason to start.
3. **The Jedi homes (Tython → Coruscant).** Tython Temple finished 3647 (3661 map) → abandoned after 3636 → a new, unnamed homeworld chosen 3627 → Coruscant Temple rebuilt and expanded from c. 3500 → Tython ruined and dark → Darzu's fortress there (1250) → Bane and Zannah seek Darzu's holocron there in 990 (1000 BBY map's era).
4. **Korriban.** The Imperial Academy (3661 map) → attacked 3636 → under nominal Republic control by 1006 → taken by the Brotherhood and the Academy reopened (1006) → Bane trains there (from 1002).
5. **Revan to Bane (3637 → 1000).** Revan dies on Yavin 4 (3637); his holocron rests on Lehon, where Arkous also dies → Bane finds it (1000) and learns the thought bomb → Ruusan → the Rule of Two. (Darth Rivan takes his name from a corrupted manuscript that calls Revan "Rivan".)
6. **The roots of Ruusan's reforms (1400 → 1000).** Jedi command the military and hold the chancellery (1400) → the Dark Age makes Jedi Lords hereditary barons and pits the Grand Council against the High Council (1100) → the Army of Light (1010) → child recruits at Ruusan (1002–1000). PP-0's per-reform triggers read these: R2 (Jedi independence, Farfalla's side) depends on O.jedi_lords; R3 (infant training) follows from O.child_recruits.
7. **The Mandalorians.** Mandalore the Avenger dies (c. 3622) → mercenaries for the Jedi at Malrev IV (1750) → the Return and Mandalore the Uniter (1058–1051) → Mandalorian Space a regional power → on to Jaster Mereel and Jango (1000 BBY map).
8. **The Sith line.** Imperial Sith (to the unknown end of the Empire) → Sith clans survive in hiding → Darth Ruin unites them (2000) → the New Sith Empire → warlords and civil war (1100–1010) → Kaan's Brotherhood (1010) → Bane alone (1000) → the 1000 BBY map.
9. **The Hutts.** Karagga dies (3639) → the Cartel's Makeb gamble and its alliance with the Republic (3638) → Hutts on the Sith side in the New Sith Wars (W: New Sith Wars infobox).
10. **Isolated powers.** Hapes seals its borders c. 3100 (O.hapes_isolated holds through both later maps); the Lost Tribe on Kesh stays cut off entirely.

## 6. Major plot points (proposed)

### PP-9: The Eternal Empire (3637–3636)
- **Hinge:** Valkorion is struck down in the throne room with the Outlander present, so his spirit has a host; Arcann takes the Throne and turns the Eternal Fleet on the galaxy.
- **Arcann's betrayal is not breakable** (ruled by Jared, 23 Nov 2026). It's a quick, opportunistic strike the moment the room is cleared. No step prevents it, and there is no story reason to. The hinge breaks only around it: no vessel in the room, or the vessel gets away.
- **The sequence, step by step.** The player can't take the Outlander's part (protagonists are NPCs), so the hinge breaks through the events around the Outlander.
  1. **The Korriban raid** (E.3636.korriban) [D-490]. Three different feats, each with its own guard:
     - **the raid is repelled:** very high. Who: command rank in Korriban's defence. Prerequisites: (a) posted there with a force to lead, (b) a lead on the raiders' course (the worlds raided before it);
     - **Atroxa lives:** high. Who: on Atroxa's guard. Prerequisite: a lead that the raiders will come back the next day;
     - **Arcann is not maimed:** only a top-tier player who stops whoever does it, or never.
     The results: This doesn't touch the hinge; it changes Arcann's state (no mask, no cybernetic arm) and Korriban's fate, and the narrator carries that forward.
  2. **Thexan** (E.3636.thexan): **closed** [D-490]. The throne room and two princes: no PC is there. Only step 1 can change it, and it doesn't touch the hinge: Arcann still strikes when the moment comes.
  3. **Marr's expedition** (E.3636.expedition) [D-490: split by feat]. **This is the step most players can reach.** Any Republic or Imperial officer, soldier, Jedi or Sith can be posted to one of the seven ships. Ways to break it:
     - the fleet turns back or is ordered home before contact: high. Who: Marr's staff or the Republic commander's. Prerequisite: a lead on what waits in Wild Space (Ziost, or a survivor of the raids);
     - **the ship gets away with the Outlander aboard:** very high. Who: the captain or pilot of that ship. Prerequisites: (a) a lead on the Fleet's jamming and interdiction, (b) a course out laid before contact;
     - the Outlander is killed in the boarding: top tier (if killed, the "similar role" ruling moves the role to another survivor, who becomes Valkorion's chosen vessel instead);
     - Marr escapes: high. Who: aboard Marr's ship, in Marr's trust. Prerequisite: a way off it found before the boarding. Marr's escape doesn't break the hinge, but O.marr_dead is never produced, so the Empire keeps a Dark Lord through the conquest.
     The hinge breaks if no suitable survivor reaches the throne room.
  4. **The escape** (E.3636.eternal_empire): **closed** (Jared, 24 Nov 2026, D-490; it was very high). Arcann brought two survivors, and the ship is full of Knights of Zakuul with Arcann aboard. The alternative it fed comes only from step 3's ship that gets away.
- **What breaks the hinge:** step 3. Step 1 changes the people, not the outcome; steps 2 and 4 are closed.
- **Proposed alternatives** (the code picks the best fit from which step broke):
  1. **No vessel** (step 3 broken). With no prisoners, there's no throne-room scene and so no moment for Arcann's strike on the recorded day. Valkorion keeps the Throne with no host ready. The Eternal Empire stays in Wild Space and keeps raiding, and the Galactic War between Republic and Empire runs on. From here the narrator directs Zakuul from the new state. No event re-stages the throne-room scene: Arcann's resentment is still a fact the narrator can use, but any later move against his father happens only through play.
  2. **The Outlander gets away** (D-490; the timing ruled by Jared, 25 Nov 2026, D-493). It depends on when the Outlander's ship gets away, measured against Arcann's strike on Valkorion:
     - **Before the strike** (step 3's ship): the Outlander does **not** carry Valkorion's spirit. If another survivor reaches the throne room (the "similar role" ruling), Arcann strikes, holds the Throne and the Fleet and conquers as recorded, and the spirit goes to that survivor. If none does, alternative 1 applies.
     - **After the strike:** the Outlander **does** carry Valkorion's spirit, and is loose from 3636. Arcann holds the Throne and the Fleet and conquers as recorded. No step aboard the flagship gives this (step 4 is closed): it comes only from an earlier change that has the ship there in time.
     Either way PP-10 (the Alliance) can start five years early around the free Outlander, against an Eternal Empire that hasn't consolidated yet.
  3. **The Emperor's spirit is not restored on Yavin 4** (E.3637.yavin4's condition broken: no great slaughter on the moon, for example the coalition never forms, or the moon is evacuated). Very high [D-490]. Who: an adviser or envoy to Satele, Marr, Theron or Lana. Prerequisites: (a) a lead on the Order of Revan's site, (b) a lead on what the Emperor gains. Ending Revan's war by beating Revan is top tier. Vitiate is not gone: Valkorion was already his main host and ruling Zakuul (W: Tenebrae, host bodies 3950–3636; W: Valkorion). What stays weak is the part of him the Hero of Tython drove out on Dromund Kaas (3661 map, E.3640.voice). So Zakuul, the princes and their raids still come (a convergence anchor), but Ziost (E.3636.ziost) doesn't happen as recorded, and Marr's expedition has a different reason to go into Wild Space or doesn't go, which reaches step 3 above. If a player somehow lets Revan's ritual finish (it would consume all life on Yavin 4), the narrator directs what follows; there is no authored alternative (ruled by Jared, 23 Nov 2026).

### PP-12: The Fourth Great Schism (2000)
- **Hinge:** Phanius becomes Darth Ruin without being recognised and unites the surviving Sith clans.
- **What the lore gives** (W: New Sith Wars §Schism; W: Fourth Great Schism; W: Darth Ruin). Phanius resigns his commission on good terms, citing ideological objections to Jedi dogma, and is honoured as the first of the Lost Twenty. His real reason is to pursue forbidden teachings. He drops out of public view, infiltrates the surviving Sith clans and then unites them, and comes back as Darth Ruin. His former peers never realise Ruin was their friend. Fifty Jedi Knights desert to him soon after. No date is given for his resignation or disappearance, so the steps below run over an undated span before 2000.
- **The discovery, step by step.** A player character has to find him out. Each step is a different way in, and each produces a tag the tracker reads:
  1. **Before he leaves** (guard medium). A Jedi peer, Padawan, Archives worker or Council aide who notices that his objections go past philosophy into forbidden teachings. What he studied and where isn't on record, so the narrator supplies the clues (ruled by Jared, 23 Nov 2026). One clue is on record: a Sith holocron is missing from the Archives, because he secretly takes it with him (Ruled 24 Nov 2026). Breaks it if the player gets the Council to act before he resigns.
  2. **The disappearance** [D-490]: **finding** Phanius is medium; **stopping** Phanius takes at least a Master, or bringing the Council; alone it is very high (prerequisites: where Phanius hides; what Phanius carries). Someone who keeps track of a retired Master: a former student, an old friend, an investigator, or a smuggler who carries him. Following him into hiding links Phanius to the dark side. Breaks it if he is found before he reaches the clans.
  3. **Among the Sith clans** (guard high) [D-490]. Only through a clan lord, as the Ziost frame (PP-14): a servant's word changes nothing. Prerequisites: in a clan, with a lead on the missing Master. The clans' acceptance (once the row's own condition) is this step. A player who belongs to or spies on one of the surviving clans sees a newcomer take them over one by one, and connects the newcomer to the vanished Jedi Master. Breaks it if this is exposed before the clans unite.
  4. **After he is Darth Ruin** (guard high; prerequisite [D-490]: a lead from Ruin's side, such as the missing holocron or one of the fifty; the belief test applies). Proving that Ruin is Phanius. This no longer stops the clans uniting, but it changes what follows (alternative 4).
- **Discovering isn't enough on its own.** The player still has to be believed. Phanius leaves amicably and is revered, so accusing him takes evidence and standing with the Council. The check uses the player's standing and the strength of the evidence (ruled by Jared, 23 Nov 2026). Failing it leaves the hinge intact, and the player is seen as having slandered a revered Master.
- **Proposed alternatives:**
  1. **Stopped before he leaves** (step 1). Phanius is confronted inside the Order: redeemed, expelled or imprisoned. The surviving clans stay scattered. The Sith stay scattered unless a Sith player character unites them (alternative 3).
  2. **Stopped before the clans unite** (steps 2 or 3). Phanius dies or is captured in hiding, or his takeover falls apart. The Sith stay scattered; the New Sith Wars are delayed or much smaller.
  3. **A Sith player character unites the clans** (ruled by Jared, 23 Nov 2026: this happens only through a Sith player character, never as an NPC substitute). The New Sith were built on Ruin's personal charisma and ego, and the same ego got him killed by his own followers after a defeat, so uniting the clans is a long, hard arc, not a single check. The player has to win over or break each surviving clan in turn, with very high guards (two prerequisites for each clan [D-490]: its lord's measure taken, and something that lord wants) and a real risk of betrayal at every stage (ruled by Jared, 23 Nov 2026); Claude Code proposes the number of clans and the guards, tagged invented. If they succeed, the New Sith Wars begin under the player's leadership, on whatever date they manage it. If they fail or never try, the Sith stay scattered (alternative 2).
  4. **Ruin exposed as Phanius after the fact** (step 4) (ruled by Jared, 23 Nov 2026). The schism goes ahead, but the Order knows one of its revered Masters turned. If the exposure comes before the fifty Knights desert (the lore says they left soon after he re-emerged), fewer of them follow him. The Order's wider reaction is left to the narrator. The New Sith Wars still begin.
- **No schism is never picked as an alternative on its own.** It's the result of alternative 1 or 2 when no Sith player character unites the clans. How much changes depends on what happened and how early the Jedi stopped him:
  - stopped before he left the Order (step 1): no New Sith at all, a peaceful millennium continues, and the Ruusan chain and the 1000 BBY map change completely;
  - stopped in hiding or among the clans (steps 2–3): the clans stay scattered and weak. Any later Sith threat comes only through play, and the 1000 BBY map's Sith line has no New Sith to grow out of, so the narrator directs it.
