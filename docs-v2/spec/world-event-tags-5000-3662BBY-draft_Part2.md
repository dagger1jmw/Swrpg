## 8. The Dark Wars, the First Jedi Purge and Dromund Kaas, 3955–3950 **[PP-19] [PP-20]**

Revan's empire splinters into warlord kingdoms. Kreia, fallen on Malachor V as Darth Traya, trains Darth Sion and Darth Nihilus; they cast her out (3954) and hunt the last Jedi. Revan remembers the true Sith Empire and leaves (3954); Canderous becomes Mandalore the Preserver. In 3951 the Exile, Meetra Surik, returns, kills Nihilus at Telos and ends Sion and Kreia on Malachor V, which Bao-Dur's remote destroys. In 3950 the Sith Civil War ends, the Republic reclaims Sith space, and Surik leaves her Lost Jedi to rebuild the Order; on Dromund Kaas she frees Revan, and both fall in the attack on the Emperor (T3996 P3; T3950 P1; R-dec B2, B4, B5).

## 9. The recovery and the hidden Empire's build-up, 3949–3682 **[PP-15]**

The Republic rebuilds under Tol Cressa, then enjoys "two centuries of peace and prosperity" (SWTOR's own phrase). Under that peace the Emperor builds the Eternal Empire on Zakuul in Valkorion's body, keeps Revan in the Maelstrom (Revan's mind slows the war by nearly three centuries), plants spies in the Republic and the Jedi Order (the Ovair family), and stages the invasion. Wookieepedia has almost nothing dated between c. 3900 and 3760 BBY; where the lore is silent, so is the map (T3950 P1–P2; T3700 P1).

## 10. The Great Galactic War, 3681–3662 **[PP-21]**

The Sith Empire strikes from the Unknown Regions after centuries of preparation: the Tingel Arm trap, Korriban retaken, Sluis Van destroyed, the Senate deadlocked. The Jedi act ahead of the Senate in the Minos Cluster. The Empire takes nearly half the Outer Rim in a decade; Bothawui (3671), the Dread Masters' capture (3668) and Alderaan (3667) turn it back. Imperial Intelligence makes a Geonosian arena champion Mandalore the Lesser (3667). This section stops at 3662; the 3661 map takes over (T3700 P1; GGW P1).

## 12. Major plot points (proposed)

Numbered after the other maps (PP-0 to PP-13). Written to the reworked pattern of the 1000 BBY map (PP-0, PP-3 to PP-8): each hinge breaks only through steps a player can reach, each step names who the player has to be and where, and nothing re-stages history. All alternatives are drafts for Jared. Guards follow world-events.md §2; every count or threshold in a step is *invented*.

### PP-14: The Great Hyperspace War (5000)
- **Hinge:** Sadow wins the Dark Lordship, invades along the Daragon Trail, and the Republic, given Gav's coordinates, finds and breaks the Sith Empire at Korriban (E.5000.korriban_second, O.sith_empire_broken).
- **Unbreakable:** Ragnos's ghost at the funeral (a spirit's act; nothing reaches it).
- **Who the player can be:** a Sith-side character (a Massassi officer, a servant or apprentice in a Lord's household on Ziost, Khar Shian or the *Corsair*), or a Republic-side one (a Koros soldier or technician, one of Teta's officers, a Jedi at Kirrek or on Ossus, a Senate aide). Gav and Jori are NPCs.
- **The steps:**
  1. **The frame on Ziost** (E.5000.ziost_raid, high; prerequisite: in the raid or saw the Republic-issue blaster). Exposing that Sadow killed Simus himself turns the Council to Kressh. Breaks the hinge (alternative 1).
  2. **The beacon** (E.5000.khar_delba, medium; lead: meeting Jori on her return, or working on the *Starbreaker 12*). Finding Sadow's homing beacon means he cannot follow the Trail. Breaks the hinge (alternative 1).
  3. **The sphere** (E.5000.primus_sphere, high; prerequisite: met Gav after Koros Major, or on the sphere's guard). Stopping Gav's attack keeps the illusions up. Gav is not lured to the empty sphere and sends nothing. Breaks the hinge (alternative 2).
  4. **The coordinates** (E.5000.primus_goluud, high; prerequisite: on the *Corsair* or the sphere's guard with a lead on Gav's turn). Jamming or stopping the transmission. Breaks the hinge (alternative 2).
  5. **Kirrek** (E.5000.kirrek, medium). If Kirrek falls, the Sith hold part of the Koros system and the battle at Korriban is weaker. Doesn't break the hinge alone.
  6. **The pursuit** (E.5000.denarii, high; prerequisite: on Teta's or Jori's pursuit and saw Sadow's flagship leave Korriban). Catching or killing Sadow before the Denarii Nova. Doesn't break the hinge: alternative 3.
  The Senate warning (E.5000.warning, medium) only adjusts: a heeded warning makes Coruscant's battle shorter.
- **What breaks the hinge:** step 1, 2, 3 or 4.
- **Alternatives:**
  1. **No invasion** (step 1 or 2). Either Kressh rules a still-isolated Empire (step 1; the Daragons die on Ziost), or Sadow rules but cannot find the way back along the Trail (step 2). There is no counterinvasion. The Empire is not crumbling, so the reason for the Ritual of Nathema is gone: E.4999.nathema loses its crucial input and the narrator directs Vitiate from there (he is not removed; he waits on Nathema). The Republic learns of the Sith only through Jori's story; no Sith relics reach Ossus.
  2. **The Sith Empire survives in the Caldera** (step 3 or 4). Sadow's invasion is thrown back but never followed home. With no coordinates there is no second battle of Korriban and no counterinvasion. The Empire is a known, hostile power beyond the Daragon Trail under Sadow (or Kressh, if he wins the first battle of Korriban). Nathema's reason is weakened, so the narrator directs Vitiate; PP-15 starts from a Republic that knows the Sith live.
  3. **Sadow taken at the Denarii Nova** (step 6; the hinge holds). The war ends as recorded, but Sadow is captured or dead. O.sadow_on_yavin is never produced. Nadd (4400) finds no master on Yavin 4, the *Corsair* and the lab are not there for Kun (3997), and Ovair's mission (3756) has no spirit to hunt; each of those events diverges under the narrator.

### PP-15: The hidden Empire (4999–3681)
- **Hinge:** the Republic does not learn that a Sith Empire survives on Dromund Kaas until it strikes in 3681 (Kao Cen Darach's Empire "believed destroyed for centuries", W).
- **Unbreakable:** the Ritual of Nathema itself (E.4999.nathema). Every Lord there is under Vitiate's mind-domination and is consumed; no step outside the planet reaches it. A Sith player can only avoid answering the summons.
- **A long tracker.** It runs across fourteen centuries and many campaigns. Each step that succeeds produces an *exposure* fact. An exposure breaks the hinge only if it is believed (as in PP-12): the player carries evidence to someone with power to act (the Jedi Council, the Senate, the SIS), and the check uses the player's standing and the strength of the evidence. A failed check leaves the hinge intact and the fact on record as rumour, which a later player can find as a lead.
- **The steps:**
  1. **The exodus** (E.4999.exodus, very high; prerequisites: (a) a Republic scout, Jedi Shadow or soldier in Sith space who records the fleet leaving Korriban, and (b) a tracker aboard or a way to follow it through the random jumps, found in play). The hardest step: twenty years of jumps in the Unknown Regions.
  2. **The emissary** (E.3978.emissary, very high; the same prerequisites as PP-17 step 1). A Mandalorian who unmasks the Emperor's agent learns that a Sith power lies beyond the Unknown Regions border.

  4. **The spies in the Order** (E.<3756.ovair_spies and E.~3750.ovair_duel, high; prerequisite: a lead on Ovair, e.g. Gynt met, or the Korriban visit seen by the Republic guard). Proving a Jedi Master serves a Sith Emperor.
  5. **The staging** (E.<3681.staging, high; prerequisite: a lead in the Dalonbian sector). Exposing the puppet governments and the Belkadan–Ruuria–Sernpidal ships before 3681. This is also PP-21 step 1.
  PP-20 alternative 2 (Revan and Surik escape Dromund Kaas) also breaks this hinge.
- **Alternatives:**
  1. **Found in the cradle** (step 1, believed before c. 4900). The Republic finds Dromund Kaas while the Empire is young and few. The narrator directs the campaign that follows: the Emperor fights or flees deeper. O.sith_empire_kaas still stands if he survives there, but the Republic watches it. No surprise invasion is possible.
  2. **Known for centuries** (steps 2, 3 or 4, or PP-20 alternative 2). The Republic knows a Sith Empire lies beyond the Unknown Regions border. It keeps a border fleet and a stronger Korriban guard. The Emperor, whose plan relied on surprise, moves his timetable: the narrator sets whether he strikes sooner or later. E.3681.invasion and the opening battles fire adjusted, and PP-21 starts with O.republic_forewarned.
  3. **Exposed on the eve** (step 5, from 3700). The war still comes in 3681, but PP-21 starts with O.republic_forewarned and its steps get easier (see PP-21).
- **Effects:** every alternative produces O.republic_forewarned (new; not an outcome of the recorded timeline).

### PP-18: The Jedi Civil War (3960–3956)
- **Hinge:** Darth Revan is taken alive and remade, and as the amnesiac Revan destroys the Star Forge and kills Malak at Rakata Prime (O.star_forge_destroyed). Revan's redemption and the Star Forge's destruction are canon outcomes the player can affect only through these steps.
- **Unbreakable:** Revan's own choices on the Star Forge: returning to the light, redeeming Bastila, the duel with Malak (as with Vader's turn in the 1000 BBY map). The player can't take Revan's part. Bastila, Carth, Mission, Zaalbar, Canderous, Jolee, Juhani and HK-47 are NPCs.
- **The steps:**
  1. **The capture** (E.3957.capture, high). *Who:* a Jedi or trooper on Bastila Shan's strike team (prerequisite: met Bastila), or a Sith officer on Revan's flagship with a lead on Malak's intent. Three ways to break it: Revan dies in Malak's barrage (the bond fails); Revan escapes the strike team; or a Sith officer warns Revan and he survives Malak's betrayal.
  2. **Taris** (E.3956.taris, medium). *Who:* a Sith officer in Malak's occupation, or a Tarisian bounty hunter or gang member. Taking Bastila, or the *Ebon Hawk* before it lifts, means the crew never leaves Taris together.
  3. **The Star Maps** (E.3956.star_maps, high; prerequisite: a lead that the maps matter). *Who:* a Sith agent with Malak's orders, a Czerka officer on Kashyyyk, or a Selkath official on Manaan. Taking or destroying one map before the crew reaches it.
  The disruptor field on Lehon (E.3956.rakata_prime, medium) is a smaller step: if it holds, Dodonna's fleet fights blind and the Republic's losses are far higher, adjusted, but Revan's choices still decide the Star Forge.
- **What breaks the hinge:** step 1, 2 or 3.
- **Alternatives:**
  1. **Revan dies in 3957** (step 1, Revan killed). Malak rules alone; the Star Forge keeps producing; the Jedi are hunted to near extinction years early; the war's end comes only through play. No redeemed Revan means no Mask in 3954, no Dromund Kaas in 3950 and no prisoner to slow the Emperor: E.3954.mask, E.3954.revan_leaves and E.3950.attack lose their crucial role, and O.war_postponed is never produced (see PP-20 alternative 1 for what that does to 3681). O.malak_unchecked (new).
  2. **Darth Revan unbroken** (step 1, Revan escapes or is warned). Revan keeps his empire. He is a far better strategist than Malak: the Sith take more of the Core. A later turn comes only through play. O.darth_revan_rules (new).
  3. **The Star Forge not found in time** (step 2 or 3). Revan and Bastila live, but the race for the Forge is lost: the war runs past 3956 (E.3956.rakata_prime moves later or diverges), and the Forge's fall becomes play-driven. O.star_forge_holds (new).

### PP-19: The First Jedi Purge and the Triumvirate (3954–3951)
- **Hinge:** the Triumvirate falls: Nihilus dies at Telos and Sion and Kreia die on Malachor V, which is destroyed (O.triumvirate_ended), and the Jedi survive to rebuild.
- **Who the player can be:** a surviving Jedi, a Republic officer or Telos Security officer, a Khoonda militia member, one of the Preserver's Mandalorians, or a Sith of the Trayus Academy, Sion's assassins or the *Ravager*'s crew. Surik, Kreia, Atton, Bao-Dur, Mira, Visas and the Preserver are NPCs.
- **The steps:**
  1. **Katarr** (E.3952.katarr, medium). Talking Atris out of the leak, or moving the conclave. Dorak, Zhar Lestin, Vandar Tokare and the hundred live: the Order rebuilds faster (adjusted). Doesn't break the hinge.
  2. **The Masters on Dantooine** (E.3951.masters, high). Saving Vrook, Kavar and Zez-Kai Ell from Kreia. Doesn't break the hinge.
  3. **Telos** (E.3951.telos, high). A Sith-side player aboard the *Ravager* stops the boarding party; or a Republic-side player fails it. Breaks the hinge (alternative 1).
  4. **Malachor** (E.3951.malachor, high). A Sith-side player in the Trayus Academy finds and disables Bao-Dur's remote; or the Generator fails. Breaks the hinge (alternative 2).
- **What breaks the hinge:** step 3 or 4.
- **Alternatives:**
  1. **Nihilus lives** (step 3). The Lord of Hunger feeds on: Telos's restoration is lost and more worlds die; the Purge goes on; the Sith Civil War does not end in 3950 (E.3950.sith_space waits). The Exile, an NPC, takes a role like her own against him; the narrator directs. O.nihilus_feeds (new).
  2. **Malachor endures** (step 4). The Trayus Academy stands; Sion or a successor regroups the warlords; the Republic does not reclaim Sith space in 3950 and Korriban gets no Republic guard yet. O.trayus_endures (new). If both steps break, both effects apply.

### PP-20: The attack on the Emperor (3954–3950)
- **Hinge:** Revan is taken alive in the attack on the Emperor and imprisoned in the Maelstrom; Surik dies; Scourge becomes the Emperor's Wrath (O.revan_imprisoned). Through Revan's mind the war is postponed by nearly three centuries (E.3950.revan_link).
- **Who the player can be:** a character born in the hidden Empire (an Imperial officer or Guard, a servant or acolyte in a Dark Councillor's household, a Sith in Scourge's service), or a Republic or Jedi character who came with Surik through a tie found in play (one of her Lost Jedi). Revan, Surik, Scourge and T3-M4 are NPCs.
- **The steps:**
  1. **Nyriss's household** (E.3950.nyriss, medium). Warning Nyriss of Sechel's deal, or winning Murtog's ambush. Revan stays her prisoner and dies with her when the Guard storms the stronghold, or is taken by the Guard (narrator).
  2. **The Purge** (E.3950.council_purge, high). Not a hinge step: the Council is not wholly replaced, and O.prowar_council is never produced. The Empire's war plans are less unanimous (adjusted).
  3. **The throne room** (E.3950.attack, very high; prerequisites: in the Citadel that dawn, and a recorded lead on Scourge's intent). Stopping Scourge's stab. With Surik standing, Revan and Surik break off and escape the Citadel.
- **What breaks the hinge:** step 1 (with Revan's death) or step 3.
- **Alternatives:**
  1. **Revan dies on Dromund Kaas** (step 1). There is no prisoner. Nothing tempers the Emperor, so O.war_postponed is never produced and the invasion (E.3681.invasion) moves earlier: the narrator sets a date within decades of 3950, while the Republic is still rebuilding (how early is not ruled: section 16). Revan is gone from the 3661 and 3640 maps (E.3642.maelstrom and the Revanite crisis are cancelled).
  2. **Revan and Surik escape** (step 3). They bring proof of the hidden Empire home: PP-15's hinge breaks (alternative 2, "Known for centuries"), and the belief check is met automatically by Revan's standing. The Emperor, exposed, moves his timetable: the narrator directs. O.revan_returns (new).
- **Not authored:** a player killing the Emperor in 3950. Scourge's own vision says another Jedi will do it, and whether the body could die then is not on record (he learns essence transfer only after this attack). Listed in section 16.

### PP-21: The Great Galactic War's opening (3681)
- **Hinge:** the surprise invasion traps the first Republic fleet in the Tingel Arm and retakes Korriban (O.tingel_arm_lost and O.korriban_retaken).
- **Reads PP-15 and PP-20.** If O.republic_forewarned exists, step 3's second prerequisite is met and step 2 drops to low. If PP-20 alternative 1 moved the invasion, the steps happen on the new date with the same roles (the people may be different).
- **The steps:**
  1. **The staging** (E.<3681.staging, high; also PP-15 step 5). Exposing the puppets and the hidden ships before the first shot.
  2. **The Tingel Arm trap** (E.3681.tingel_arm, medium). *Who:* an officer or diplomat in the convoy or the first fleet. Spotting the trap at Belkadan, Ruuria and Sernpidal saves the fleet.
  3. **Korriban** (E.3681.korriban, very high; prerequisites: posted on the vigil or the orbital station (met Darach), and forewarning). Against over thirty dreadnaughts the station can only hold with warning and reinforcement.
  4. **The Senate** (E.3681.senate, medium). A senator or aide who carries Satele's report and ends the deadlock. Adjusts only: the Navy answers at once and the Jedi don't go to Minos alone.
- **What breaks the hinge:** step 3, or steps 1 and 2 together.
- **Alternatives:**
  1. **Korriban holds** (step 3). The Empire's first blow fails at the old Sith capital. Malgus doesn't rise there as recorded (O.malgus_lord diverges); the war is fought in the Tingel Arm and the Outer Rim; the 3661 map's E.3660.korriban becomes a fight for a Republic-held world. O.korriban_held_3681 (new).
  2. **The Republic is ready** (steps 1 and 2). No trapped fleet, Sluis Van defended. The war becomes a long stalemate on the Outer Rim edge: E.3671.seswenna diverges (O.outer_rim_half never produced), and the 3661 map's PP-1 starts from a stronger Republic (the narrator directs whether the Dark Council still makes its peace offer). O.war_stalemate (new).
