## 2. Major plot points (reviewed)

Numbered after the other maps (PP-0 to PP-21). Written to the reworked pattern: every hinge breaks only through steps a player can reach; every step names who the player has to be, scored against the three tests (*There* / *Standing* / *Kind*); closed systems are named; nothing re-stages history; nothing rests on a premonition. **Reviewed by Jared, 24–25 Nov 2026** (review-notes-4-44.md): all fourteen approved with the changes applied below and in the matching event rows; PP-28, PP-29, PP-34 and PP-35 were checked by Claude on Wookieepedia on his behalf and approved. Every step follows the design rules from review (Part 1). Guards follow world-events.md §2; every count or threshold in a step is *invented*.

### PP-22: Coruscant, Krytos and Isard's bacta (6–7 ABY)
- **Hinge:** the Republic takes Coruscant with Isard's Krytos virus already in it (O.coruscant_liberated, O.krytos_released), and her bacta gambit fails at Thyferra (O.thyferra_freed).
- **Unbreakable:** the *Lusankya* as a search target (step 6: NRI isn't searching Coruscant and nothing points to a ship buried there; Rev PP-22). Isard's own choice to give up the capital is reachable only through her intelligence staff (very high).
- **Who matters:** most of this plot point depends on a very capable NRI agent PC or a PC in Rogue Squadron (Jared).
- **Who the player can be:** a Republic pilot or commando (Rogue Squadron, a Wraith-type unit, Page's commandos), an NRI agent, a Black Sun or Alien Combine member on Coruscant, an Imperial Intelligence officer, a garrison officer or medic under Derricote, a Thyferran (Ashern or Xucphra), a smuggler under Booster or Karrde. Wedge, Corran, Tycho and Isard are NPCs.
- **The steps:**
  1. **Borleias** (E.6.borleias, medium). *There:* yes, two assaults and a garrison. *Standing:* changing the outcome needs command rank: an officer or squadron leader in either assault, or a senior officer in Derricote's garrison; a line pilot, commando or trooper can be there but can't change it. *Kind:* Republic or Imperial military. Holding Borleias for the Empire leaves the Core Campaign without its launch point (alternative 1).
  2. **The virus found** (E.6.krytos, high; prerequisite: a lead on Derricote's labs). *There:* a medic or scientist in his garrison, or a commando searching the taken base. *Standing:* lab access or an intelligence officer's clearance. *Kind:* Imperial medical staff who defect, or NRI. Exposing Krytos before the fall (alternative 2).
  3. **The Black Sun cell** (E.6.kessel, medium). *There:* the Kessel raid or an Imperial watcher. Adjusts only: without Black Sun the shields are harder (the condition in step 4 stays high).
  4. **The shields** (E.6.coruscant, high; prerequisite: in the infiltration team or the Combine, or an Imperial in the shield and power staff with a lead on the infiltrators). *There:* realistic for an infiltrator or Imperial technician. *Standing:* chosen for Wedge's team (an ace or a specialist), or a trusted shift officer. *Kind:* Republic infiltrator, Combine slicer, Imperial engineer. Keeping the shields up saves Coruscant for Isard (alternative 1).
  5. **The release** (E.6.krytos_release, high; prerequisite: a lead from step 2 and a place on Coruscant). *There:* an infiltrator or an Imperial water-works officer. *Standing:* access to the reservoirs. Stopping it (alternative 2).
  6. **The *Lusankya*** (E.7.lusankya): **unbreakable as a search** (Rev PP-22). Realistically it is never found: only a PC held prisoner aboard, as Corran was, would have a lead, and by then Isard launches it. No step.
  7. **Thyferra** (E.7.bacta_war, medium; Isard's escape high). *There:* with the resigned Rogues, the Ashern or the smugglers; or aboard the *Lusankya*. *Standing:* changing the battle's outcome needs command rank (a flight or ship commander, an Ashern cell leader, a smuggler captain; on the Imperial side a senior officer). Losing Thyferra leaves Isard the bacta (alternative 4). Stopping Isard's escape (prerequisite: aboard the *Lusankya* with a lead on her route) takes her: alternative 3, which is reached only this way.
- **What breaks the hinge:** step 1 or 4 (Coruscant stays Imperial); step 2 or 5 (no plague); Isard's capture in step 7; or Thyferra lost.
- **Alternatives:**
  1. **Coruscant stays Imperial** (step 1 or 4). The Republic stays a mobile government; Isard rules from the capital; Krytos is never released, so no plague and no Bacta War. Thrawn returns (8 ABY) to an Empire Isard still holds: the narrator directs whether she rules beside him or against him. O.isard_holds_coruscant (new).
  2. **A clean capital** (step 2 or 5). Coruscant falls without the plague; the Republic is not bankrupted by bacta and there is no Alien Combine grievance to exploit. Isard still flees in the *Lusankya*, but without the bacta crisis her coup on Thyferra has no lever: E.7.thyferra_coup loses its input and the narrator directs her. O.no_krytos (new).
  3. **Isard taken** (only through her capture at Thyferra, step 7). Isard is tried on Coruscant. The Bacta War ends with her in chains; no Isard clone, no *Lusankya* for the reborn Emperor; the Hegemony theater is Krennel's alone. O.isard_captured (new).
  4. **Isard keeps the bacta** (step 7). The Rogues are outlaws; the Republic must deal with Thyferra; Isard has a fleet and the galaxy's medicine when Thrawn returns. O.bacta_monopoly (new).

### PP-23: Zsinj (7–8 ABY)
- **Hinge:** Zsinj, master of a third of the known galaxy, dies over Dathomir (O.zsinj_dead) and his empire breaks up.
- **Unbreakable:** Han's shot into the *Iron Fist*'s bridge (his act; Rev PP-23).
- **Who the player can be:** a Wraith or Rogue, a crewman in Han's task force, a Hapan officer, a Dathomiri witch of the Singing Mountain Clan or a Nightsister, or Zsinj's own crewman or Raptor. Han, Wedge, Face Loran, Gara Petothel, Gethzerion and Teneniel Djo are NPCs.
- **The steps:**
  1. **The Wraiths' war** (E.7.ession, E.7.razors_kiss; no guard). Adjusts the hunt's pace only.
  2. **Selaggis** (E.7.selaggis, high; prerequisite: a lead that the explosion was faked). *There:* a Wraith, or a crewman of the *Iron Fist*. *Standing:* a trusted Wraith in good standing, who gets Han's task force to act on it. A lone *Iron Fist* crewman can only defect with what he knows; that alone doesn't break it. *Kind:* Republic commando or pilot. Catching him at Selaggis ends the hunt early (alternative 2).
  3. **Zsinj warned off Dathomir** (E.8.dathomir, high; prerequisite: a Nightsister trusted by Gethzerion who learns of the coming attack). *There:* realistic for a Nightsister player. *Standing:* Gethzerion's trust. *Kind:* Force-sensitive Dathomiri of the Nightsisters. She warns Zsinj off before the battle (alternative 1). Han's shot itself can't be stopped; Hapan or Republic pilots and gunners, and Singing Mountain witches, change the battle's losses only.
  4. **The coven's escape** (E.8.dathomir, medium). *There:* a Nightsister PC. In the lore Zsinj destroys their shuttle; the PC gets the coven out another way (alternative 3). Doesn't break the hinge.
- **What breaks the hinge:** step 3 (Zsinj lives), or step 2 (he dies earlier and elsewhere). Step 4 adds alternative 3 to whatever else happens.
- **Alternatives:**
  1. **Zsinj survives** (step 3). The strongest warlord keeps his empire; the post-Zsinj campaigns and the taking of Kuat do not happen; Thrawn (8 ABY) finds a rival or an ally, narrator-directed. O.zsinj_survives (new).
  2. **Zsinj falls at Selaggis** (step 2). The hunt ends early; there is no blockade of Dathomir to break, so the Hapan courtship plays without a battle and Luke's *Chu'unthor* records come only through play. O.zsinj_taken_early (new).
  3. **The Nightsisters loose** (step 4). Gethzerion's coven escapes Dathomir; a dark-side power is abroad in the galaxy; the narrator directs. O.nightsisters_loose (new).

### PP-24: The Thrawn campaign (9 ABY)
- **Hinge:** Thrawn's campaign ends with his death at Bilbringi and Mount Tantiss destroyed (O.thrawn_dead, O.tantiss_destroyed).
- **Thrawn's two biggest misses** were the Smugglers' Alliance and Rukh's betrayal (Jared): steps 5 and 2 decide the battle at Bilbringi together.
- **Unbreakable:** Rukh's strike on the *Chimaera*'s bridge (a closed system; changeable only earlier, through step 2).
- **Who the player can be:** an Imperial officer under Thrawn or Pellaeon, an Imperial Intelligence officer, an Emperor's Hand, a Noghri-side Imperial overseer, a smuggler under Karrde or in the Smugglers' Alliance, a Bel Iblis officer, an NRI agent, a Republic pilot. Thrawn, Pellaeon, C'baoth, Leia, Luke, Mara, Karrde and the Noghri leaders are NPCs; C'baoth is top tier.
- **The steps:**
  1. **Wayland** (E.9.wayland, very high; prerequisites: (a) court-level access to the lead in the Emperor's private records, and (b) a force able to deal with C'baoth (top tier) or to destroy the storehouse). *There:* only someone with that access: an Emperor's Hand, or Imperial Intelligence with it. *Standing:* a ship, a crew and that force. *Kind:* Imperial. Getting there first and holding or destroying it (alternative 2).
  2. **The Noghri** (E.9.noghri, high; prerequisite: a lead on the Empire's deceit, or on Khabarakh). *There:* with Leia's party, or an Imperial overseer on Honoghr. *Standing:* Leia's trust or Thrawn's. *Kind:* a Republic aide or bodyguard (helps the record along); an Imperial officer who exposes Khabarakh or wrecks the proof (breaks it). Breaking it keeps Rukh loyal (alternative 6, or alternative 1 with step 5).
  3. **The *Katana* fleet** (E.9.katana, high; prerequisite: a lead on the fleet's position). *There:* a smuggler or Bel Iblis officer at the site; a Republic insider who finds the leak. *Standing:* bringing it home needs command, or a captain who gets the Republic or Bel Iblis to act. Bringing it home (alternative 4).
  4. **Tantiss** (E.9.tantiss, high; prerequisite to join the strike team: Luke's, Mara's or Karrde's trust). *There:* the strike team or the garrison. *Standing:* holding Tantiss for the Empire, or beating C'baoth, is top tier: a Master only holds C'baoth off. *Kind:* Republic, smuggler, Imperial guard. Holding Tantiss (alternative 3).
  5. **The Smugglers' Alliance** (E.9.smugglers, high; prerequisite: a smuggler trusted among the captains, or an Imperial Intelligence officer with a lead on it and access to Thrawn's staff). *There:* among the captains, or on Thrawn's staff. *Standing:* the captains' trust; or access to Thrawn. *Kind:* a smuggler who keeps the Alliance from forming or arriving; an Imperial who warns Thrawn. Combined with step 2 (Rukh) at Bilbringi: both broken, alternative 1; only the smugglers, alternative 5; only Rukh, alternative 6.
- **What breaks the hinge:** step 2 (Thrawn lives: alternative 6, or alternative 1 with step 5), step 4 (Tantiss stands), step 1 (no storehouse). Step 5 alone keeps Thrawn's death but loses the battle (alternative 5); step 3 shortens the campaign.
- **Alternatives:**
  1. **Thrawn wins at Bilbringi** (steps 2 and 5 together). The shipyards fall; the Republic loses the Core worlds Thrawn threatens and is pushed back toward the Mid Rim; clone soldiers fill his ships. The Coalition never forms under others; the narrator directs Thrawn's Empire, and the reborn Emperor (10 ABY) meets a living Grand Admiral. O.thrawn_victorious (new).
  2. **No storehouse** (step 1). No clones, no ysalamiri, no C'baoth; Thrawn's campaign is short and conventional; the *Katana* fleet still matters. O.tantiss_denied (new).
  3. **Tantiss stands** (step 4). Thrawn still dies, but the clone vats serve his successors; and the Caamas Document is not found in Tantiss's ruins in 19 ABY (E.19.caamas_document loses its input). O.tantiss_survives (new).
  4. **The *Katana* fleet goes to the Republic** (step 3). Thrawn lacks the Dreadnaughts; Coruscant is never besieged (E.9.siege loses its input); the campaign ends earlier, adjusted. O.katana_republic (new).
  5. **Bilbringi falls, Thrawn dead** (step 5 only). Rukh still kills Thrawn, but without the smugglers the battle is won: Pellaeon inherits a won battle and the yards. The Imperial Coalition forms from a stronger remnant, narrator-adjusted. O.bilbringi_won_pellaeon (new).
  6. **Thrawn lives, beaten at Bilbringi** (step 2 only). Rukh stays loyal, but the smugglers still break the attack: Thrawn falls back with his fleet; narrator-directed, and the reborn Emperor (10 ABY) meets a living, beaten Grand Admiral. O.thrawn_beaten_alive (new).

### PP-25: Palpatine reborn (10–11 ABY)
- **Hinge:** the Emperor's last life ends: Byss is destroyed with the clone vats and his spirit is trapped at Onderon (O.byss_destroyed, O.palpatine_gone).
- **Unbreakable:** the transfer at Endor; Luke going to Byss (his duty as a Jedi) and his choice in the throne room; Brand's sacrifice at Onderon (an instant in the Force); the Emperor himself (top tier: Palpatine reborn).
- **Who the player can be:** a Republic soldier, pilot or Jedi trainee, a Smugglers' Alliance captain, a Dark Empire officer, crewman or Dark Side Adept on Byss. Luke, Leia, Han, Brand and the Emperor are NPCs.
- **The steps:**
  1. **Luke goes to Byss** (E.10.coruscant_lost): **unbreakable** (Rev PP-25). He goes no matter what; he sees it as his duty as a Jedi. No persuasion route. No step.
  2. **Leia reaches Byss** (E.10.luke_byss, high; prerequisite: in Leia's party). *There:* with Han and Chewbacca, or an Imperial on Byss. *Standing:* Leia's trust; or a Byss officer. *Kind:* a Republic bodyguard or pilot; an Imperial who stops her ship (breaks it: alternative 1).
  3. **Byss** (E.11.byss, very high; prerequisites: (a) on the *Eclipse II* or with the Gun's crew, and (b) a lead on where the clone bank lies). *There:* rare: a Republic boarding party or a Gun technician. *Standing:* a commando or trusted crewman. Keeping Byss whole (alternative 2) or, from the Imperial side, moving the vats.
  4. **Onderon** (E.11.onderon). Brand's sacrifice is **unbreakable** (an instant in the Force). The only route is removing Brand earlier: very high (prerequisites: (a) a Dark Empire PC near him, and (b) a lead on his part in the plan): alternative 3. Fighting the clone itself is top tier.
- **What breaks the hinge:** step 3 or 4; step 2 changes Luke.
- **Alternatives:**
  1. **Luke stays the Emperor's apprentice** (step 2). The Dark Empire has a Skywalker at its head; the Republic loses its Jedi hope; the narrator directs Luke (an NPC), and any return is only through play. O.luke_fallen (new).
  2. **Byss survives** (step 3). The vats endure; Onderon is not his last body; the Dark Empire goes on. O.clone_bank_intact (new).
  3. **The Emperor takes Anakin Solo** (step 4: Brand removed earlier). His spirit lives in the infant's body: the darkest branch in the map; the narrator directs from here (as PP-9's finished-ritual case). O.emperor_in_anakin (new).

### PP-26: Exar Kun and the Sun Crusher (11 ABY)
- **Hinge:** Kun's spirit corrupts Kyp, Carida is destroyed, and the students destroy Kun (O.carida_destroyed, O.kun_destroyed, O.kyp_redeemed).
- **A relatively closed loop** (Jared). Only a Jedi student at the Praxeum is there, and even then mostly part of the pack, unable to do much.
- **Unbreakable:** Kun's attack on Luke's body (a spirit's act); fighting Kun's spirit (top tier: only the students together, with Luke, end him).
- **Who the player can be:** a Praxeum student; for steps 1 and 3 also a Maw Installation scientist or trooper, a Kessel prisoner, or someone in Han's circle; an Imperial on Carida. Luke, Kyp, Han, Chewbacca, Qwi Xux and Daala are NPCs.
- **The steps:**
  1. **The Sun Crusher stolen** (E.11.maw, very high; prerequisites: (a) a place where it happens (on Qwi Xux's staff, a Maw trooper, or a Kessel prisoner escaping with Kyp), and (b) a lead on the escape as it forms). Stopping Han and Chewbacca is possible but highly unlikely, and for a fellow escapee an unlikely character choice. No Sun Crusher (alternative 2).
  2. **Kun and Kyp** (E.11.kun_wakes, very high, as drafted; prerequisites: a classmate who saw the night lessons, and the trust to be believed). *There:* yes, a fellow student. *Standing:* a trusted student; even so, mostly part of the pack. *Kind:* Force-sensitive trainee. Kyp never falls (alternative 1).
  3. **The weapon** (E.11.carida, high; prerequisite: a lead on where it was hidden, which comes only from Han's circle: Han, Chewbacca, Qwi, or someone they confided in). *There:* a student or guard on Yavin 4 who has that lead. Disabling it (alternative 2); evacuating Carida saves its people only.
  4. **The students unite** (E.11.kun_destroyed, medium). This one can definitely happen. Routes: a student who refuses the gathering, or one under Kun's influence who works against it. If they fail (alternative 3).
- **What breaks the hinge:** step 2 (no fall), step 1 or 3 (no weapon), step 4 (Kun wins).
- **Alternatives:**
  1. **Kyp never falls** (step 2). Carida stands; Kun's spirit still haunts the Praxeum, but no event re-stages a fall: the narrator directs what Kun does next. O.kyp_unfallen (new).
  2. **Carida stands** (step 1 or 3). Kyp may still fall, without the weapon; Daala keeps or loses the Sun Crusher in the Maw. O.carida_stands (new).
  3. **Kun wins** (step 4). Luke's body dies or stays empty; Kun's spirit, unbound, holds the Praxeum's students: the narrator directs; the New Jedi Order is stillborn. O.kun_unbound (new).

- **Unbreakable:** none.
- **Who the player can be:** a warlord's aide, guard or flag officer; Daala's or Pellaeon's staff; an Imperial officer with command in the Yavin assault. Praxeum students and Republic defenders are there but only change the losses. Daala and Pellaeon are NPCs.

  1. **Tsoss Beacon** (E.12.tsoss, very high; prerequisites: (a) a lead from inside Daala's staff on her intent, and (b) closeness to a warlord to be believed). *There:* a warlord's guard, aide or flag officer at Tsoss. *Standing:* close enough to be heeded. *Kind:* Imperial officer. A warned warlord leaves (alternative 1).
  2. **Yavin 4** (E.12.yavin_assault, high; prerequisite: command rank in the assault). Only the Imperial side breaks it: defending is the recorded outcome. If the Praxeum falls, Daala keeps command (alternative 2).

  2. **Daala's Remnant** (step 2). The Praxeum is destroyed; Daala keeps command and leads a militant Remnant; Pellaeon serves under her. O.daala_remnant (new).

### PP-28: The Corellian Crisis (18 ABY)
