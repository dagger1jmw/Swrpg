## 2. Major plot points (proposed)

Numbered after the other maps (PP-0 to PP-21). Written to the reworked pattern and to Jared's review notes: every hinge breaks only through steps a player can reach; every step names who the player has to be, scored against the three tests (*There* / *Standing* / *Kind*); closed systems are named; nothing re-stages history; nothing rests on a premonition. All alternatives are drafts for Jared. Guards follow world-events.md §2; every count or threshold in a step is *invented*.

### PP-22: Coruscant, Krytos and Isard's bacta (6–7 ABY)
- **Hinge:** the Republic takes Coruscant with Isard's Krytos virus already in it (O.coruscant_liberated, O.krytos_released), and her bacta gambit fails at Thyferra (O.thyferra_freed).
- **Unbreakable:** none of the steps is a closed system; Isard's own choice to give up the capital is reachable only through her intelligence staff (very high).
- **Who the player can be:** a Republic pilot or commando (Rogue or Wraith-type unit, Page's commandos), an NRI agent, a Black Sun or Alien Combine member on Coruscant, an Imperial Intelligence officer, a garrison officer or medic under Derricote, a Thyferran (Ashern or Xucphra), a smuggler under Booster or Karrde. Wedge, Corran, Tycho and Isard are NPCs.
- **The steps:**
  1. **Borleias** (E.6.borleias, medium). *There:* yes, two assaults and a garrison. *Standing:* a line pilot, commando or garrison officer. *Kind:* Republic or Imperial military. Holding Borleias for the Empire leaves the Core Campaign without its launch point (alternative 1).
  2. **The virus found** (E.6.krytos, high; prerequisite: a lead on Derricote's labs). *There:* a medic or scientist in his garrison, or a commando searching the taken base. *Standing:* lab access or an intelligence officer's clearance. *Kind:* Imperial medical staff who defect, or NRI. Exposing Krytos before the fall (alternative 2).
  3. **The Black Sun cell** (E.6.kessel, medium). *There:* the Kessel raid or an Imperial watcher. Adjusts only: without Black Sun the shields are harder (the condition in step 4 stays high).
  4. **The shields** (E.6.coruscant, high; prerequisite: in the infiltration team or the Combine, or an Imperial in the shield and power staff with a lead on the infiltrators). *There:* realistic for an infiltrator or Imperial technician. *Standing:* chosen for Wedge's team (an ace or a specialist), or a trusted shift officer. *Kind:* Republic infiltrator, Combine slicer, Imperial engineer. Keeping the shields up saves Coruscant for Isard (alternative 1).
  5. **The release** (E.6.krytos_release, high; prerequisite: a lead from step 2 and a place on Coruscant). *There:* an infiltrator or an Imperial water-works officer. *Standing:* access to the reservoirs. Stopping it (alternative 2).
  6. **The *Lusankya*** (E.7.lusankya, high; prerequisite: Corran's debrief, a guard or a prisoner met). *There:* an NRI investigator or a guard. Taking the ship in its berth takes Isard with it (alternative 3).
  7. **Thyferra** (E.7.bacta_war, medium; Isard's escape high). *There:* with the resigned Rogues, the Ashern or the smugglers; or aboard the *Lusankya*. *Standing:* a pilot, a smuggler captain, an officer. Losing Thyferra leaves Isard the bacta (alternative 4); taking Isard there is alternative 3.
- **What breaks the hinge:** step 1 or 4 (Coruscant stays Imperial); step 2 or 5 (no plague); step 6 or Isard's capture in step 7; or Thyferra lost.
- **Alternatives:**
  1. **Coruscant stays Imperial** (step 1 or 4). The Republic stays a mobile government; Isard rules from the capital; Krytos is never released, so no plague and no Bacta War. Thrawn returns (8 ABY) to an Empire Isard still holds: the narrator directs whether she rules beside him or against him. O.isard_holds_coruscant (new).
  2. **A clean capital** (step 2 or 5). Coruscant falls without the plague; the Republic is not bankrupted by bacta and there is no Alien Combine grievance to exploit. Isard still flees in the *Lusankya*, but without the bacta crisis her coup on Thyferra has no lever: E.7.thyferra_coup loses its input and the narrator directs her. O.no_krytos (new).
  3. **Isard taken** (step 6, or her capture at Thyferra). Isard is tried on Coruscant. No Thyferra warlord (or the Bacta War ends with her in chains), no Isard clone, no *Lusankya* for the reborn Emperor; the Hegemony theater is Krennel's alone. O.isard_captured (new).
  4. **Isard keeps the bacta** (step 7). The Rogues are outlaws; the Republic must deal with Thyferra; Isard has a fleet and the galaxy's medicine when Thrawn returns. O.bacta_monopoly (new).

### PP-23: Zsinj (7–8 ABY)
- **Hinge:** Zsinj, master of a third of the known galaxy, dies over Dathomir (O.zsinj_dead) and his empire breaks up.
- **Unbreakable:** none.
- **Who the player can be:** a Wraith or Rogue, a crewman in Han's task force, a Hapan officer, a Dathomiri witch of the Singing Mountain Clan or a Nightsister, or Zsinj's own crewman or Raptor. Han, Wedge, Face Loran, Gara Petothel and Teneniel Djo are NPCs.
- **The steps:**
  1. **The Wraiths' war** (E.7.ession, E.7.razors_kiss; no guard). Adjusts the hunt's pace only.
  2. **Selaggis** (E.7.selaggis, high; prerequisite: aboard the *Iron Fist* with a lead on the faked explosion). *There:* a crewman of the *Iron Fist*, or a Wraith beside Petothel: realistic. *Standing:* bridge or sensor crew; a trusted Wraith. *Kind:* Imperial naval rating or officer; Republic commando. Catching him at Selaggis ends the hunt early (alternative 2).
  3. **Dathomir** (E.8.dathomir, medium). *There:* a Hapan or Republic pilot or gunner, a Singing Mountain witch, or a Nightsister who warns Zsinj off. *Standing:* line crew; a clan sister. *Kind:* Force-sensitive Dathomiri (realistic for a witch player), military. If Zsinj escapes Dathomir (alternative 1); if the Nightsisters get off-world in the chaos (alternative 3).
- **What breaks the hinge:** step 3 (Zsinj lives), or step 2 (he dies earlier and elsewhere).
- **Alternatives:**
  1. **Zsinj survives** (step 3). The strongest warlord keeps his empire; the post-Zsinj campaigns and the taking of Kuat do not happen; Thrawn (8 ABY) finds a rival or an ally, narrator-directed. O.zsinj_survives (new).
  2. **Zsinj falls at Selaggis** (step 2). The hunt ends early; there is no blockade of Dathomir to break, so the Hapan courtship plays without a battle and Luke's *Chu'unthor* records come only through play. O.zsinj_taken_early (new).
  3. **The Nightsisters loose** (step 3, Nightsister side). Gethzerion's coven escapes Dathomir; a dark-side power is abroad in the galaxy; the narrator directs. O.nightsisters_loose (new).

### PP-24: The Thrawn campaign (9 ABY)
- **Hinge:** Thrawn's campaign ends with his death at Bilbringi and Mount Tantiss destroyed (O.thrawn_dead, O.tantiss_destroyed).
- **Unbreakable:** Rukh's strike on the *Chimaera*'s bridge (a closed system; changeable only earlier, through step 2).
- **Who the player can be:** an Imperial officer under Thrawn or Pellaeon, a Noghri-side Imperial overseer, a smuggler under Karrde or the Smugglers' Alliance, a Bel Iblis officer, an NRI agent, a Republic pilot. Thrawn, Pellaeon, Leia, Luke, Mara, Karrde and the Noghri leaders are NPCs.
- **The steps:**
  1. **Wayland** (E.9.wayland, high; prerequisite: a lead on Wayland from the Emperor's records). *There:* only someone with that lead: an ex-court or Imperial Intelligence player, a smuggler who carried for them. *Standing:* a ship and a crew. *Kind:* smuggler, Imperial, NRI. Getting there first (alternative 2).
  2. **The Noghri** (E.9.noghri, high; prerequisite: a lead on the Empire's deceit, or on Khabarakh). *There:* with Leia's party, or an Imperial overseer on Honoghr. *Standing:* Leia's trust or Thrawn's. *Kind:* a Republic aide or bodyguard (helps the record along); an Imperial officer who exposes Khabarakh or wrecks the proof (breaks it). Breaking it keeps Rukh loyal (alternative 1).
  3. **The *Katana* fleet** (E.9.katana, high; prerequisite: a lead on the fleet's position). *There:* a smuggler or Bel Iblis officer at the site; a Republic insider who finds the leak. *Standing:* a captain or intelligence officer. Bringing it home (alternative 4).
  4. **Tantiss** (E.9.tantiss, medium). *There:* the strike team or the garrison. *Standing:* a commando or Noghri-trusted fighter; facing C'baoth needs a Master. *Kind:* Republic, smuggler, Imperial guard. Holding Tantiss (alternative 3).
  5. **Bilbringi** (E.9.bilbringi, medium; adjusts). A Republic or smuggler pilot at the yards: a heavier Imperial defeat or a costlier Republic win.
- **What breaks the hinge:** step 2 (Thrawn lives), step 4 (Tantiss stands), or step 1 (no campaign as recorded).
- **Alternatives:**
  1. **Thrawn wins at Bilbringi** (step 2). The shipyards fall; the Republic loses the Core worlds Thrawn threatens and is pushed back toward the Mid Rim; clone soldiers fill his ships. The Coalition never forms under others; the narrator directs Thrawn's Empire, and the reborn Emperor (10 ABY) meets a living Grand Admiral. O.thrawn_victorious (new).
  2. **No storehouse** (step 1). No clones, no ysalamiri, no C'baoth; Thrawn's campaign is short and conventional; the *Katana* fleet still matters. O.tantiss_denied (new).
  3. **Tantiss stands** (step 4). Thrawn still dies, but the clone vats serve his successors; and the Caamas Document is not found in Tantiss's ruins in 19 ABY (E.19.caamas_document loses its input). O.tantiss_survives (new).
  4. **The *Katana* fleet goes to the Republic** (step 3). Thrawn lacks the Dreadnaughts; Coruscant is never besieged (E.9.siege loses its input); the campaign ends earlier, adjusted. O.katana_republic (new).

### PP-25: Palpatine reborn (10–11 ABY)
- **Hinge:** the Emperor's last life ends: Byss is destroyed with the clone vats and his spirit is trapped at Onderon (O.byss_destroyed, O.palpatine_gone).
- **Unbreakable:** the transfer at Endor; Luke's choice in the throne room on Byss; the Emperor himself (top duelist: Palpatine reborn; only a Yoda- or Mace-level character could hold him).
- **Who the player can be:** a Republic soldier, pilot or Jedi trainee, a Smugglers' Alliance captain, a Dark Empire officer, crewman or Dark Side Adept on Byss. Luke, Leia, Han, Brand and the Emperor are NPCs.
- **The steps:**
  1. **Luke taken to Byss** (E.10.coruscant_lost, medium). *There:* yes, in the fighting in Imperial City. *Standing:* a soldier or pilot. *Kind:* Republic. Getting him out (alternative 4).
  2. **Leia reaches Byss** (E.10.luke_byss, high; prerequisite: in Leia's party). *There:* with Han and Chewbacca, or an Imperial on Byss. *Standing:* Leia's trust; or a Byss officer. *Kind:* a Republic bodyguard or pilot; an Imperial who stops her ship (breaks it: alternative 1).
  3. **Byss** (E.11.byss, very high; prerequisites: (a) on the *Eclipse II* or with the Gun's crew, and (b) a lead on where the clone bank lies). *There:* rare: a Republic boarding party or a Gun technician. *Standing:* a commando or trusted crewman. Keeping Byss whole (alternative 2) or, from the Imperial side, moving the vats.
  4. **Onderon** (E.11.onderon, high; prerequisite: in the Solos' guard or among Brand's Jedi). *There:* yes, a bodyguard or Jedi. *Standing:* the Solos' trust. If Brand is gone or kept from the infant (alternative 3). Fighting the clone is top-duelist work.
- **What breaks the hinge:** step 3 or 4; step 2 changes Luke, step 1 changes the year's shape.
- **Alternatives:**
  1. **Luke stays the Emperor's apprentice** (step 2). The Dark Empire has a Skywalker at its head; the Republic loses its Jedi hope; the narrator directs Luke (an NPC), and any return is only through play. O.luke_fallen (new).
  2. **Byss survives** (step 3). The vats endure; Onderon is not his last body; the Dark Empire goes on. O.clone_bank_intact (new).
  3. **The Emperor takes Anakin Solo** (step 4). His spirit lives in the infant's body: the darkest branch in the map; the narrator directs from here (as PP-9's finished-ritual case). O.emperor_in_anakin (new).
  4. **Luke never kneels** (step 1). The first clone is not destroyed in 10 ABY; Shadow Hand is fought against a stronger Emperor, and the plot point is decided by steps 3 and 4. O.luke_free_10 (new).
