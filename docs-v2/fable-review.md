## Top 5 to fix before M1

1. Clock resolution and calendar (rows 3, 22). Store seconds, and define month lengths, the weekday anchor and year 0. M1 builds the clock first and everything hangs on it.
2. Resolve all dice before narration, with one entry point per action (rows 1, 2, 5). This sets the shape of the intent and narrative schemas and is the main V170 root cause still open.
3. One time-writer per turn and one day-loop definition (rows 4, 13). Reconcile §9, §10 and §11 before writing advanceTime and its hook-order tests.
4. Single source for the AI schemas (row 6). Make architecture §3 authoritative and retire or update ai-events.md, so M1 builds one contract.
5. applyEvents contract: same-turn refs, apply order and per-turn caps (rows 7, 15). These are part of the M1 core even though most events arrive later.
Row 12 (what checks do before M5) is a close sixth; it is cheap to settle in the M1 plan.
