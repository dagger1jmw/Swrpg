# m8-fix15: crime lords (2026-11-25)

Decision D-509.

## 1. The archetype

**Crime lord** (`crimeLord`): a career for generated and canon NPCs only, not on the creation form. It is the civilian's measured day with the hours moved from the trade to dealing and command. Paces (all invented): Charisma 1.1, Intelligence 1.0, Profession 0.9, Tactics 0.7, Willpower 0.651 (the civilian's), a little body and Blaster. No weapon or armour of their own (guards fight for them).

A Human crime lord of 50 against a civilian of 50: Charisma over 20 higher, Tactics over 30 (tested).
