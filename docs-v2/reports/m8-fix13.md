## 1. Training years (2a)

Years trained now run from the start of training or adulthood. A childhood counts at the Human's pace (its training age), so a Hutt's 150 years of it no longer use up the allowance. Adult years are real years, as before.

| Generated NPC (Soresu, Blaster) | fix12 | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Hutt Guardian Master, 300 / 500 / 800 | 73 | 133 |
| Hutt soldier | 88 | 97 |
| Wookiee and Kel Dor, every path | 86 to 88 | the same |
| Humans | as before to 80 | the same |

**Worth a look:** the Hutt Jedi is now far above a Human Master (133 against 88). D-474 has a child train in real years, and a Hutt is a child for about 150 of them. Its childhood no longer uses up the allowance, but it still trains through all of it. No Hutt Jedi is on the roster.

## 2. Long life counts for the mind (2b)

Past the stop, a long-lived NPC without a sheet (lifespan over 150) keeps gaining experience by real years in Intelligence, Charisma, Profession, Investigation, Tactics and Medicine. Combat, physical, Force and Willpower stay stopped.

- **Pace and cap (all invented):** each stat drifts toward **75**, the middle of Master. Over **150 years** it covers about 63% of the way. It gains **at most +30** in all, never goes down, and only for a stat the NPC has at **20** or more, so a skill never practised does not appear.
- **Why not your +15 over the Human-85 line:** a civilian's Charisma line at 85 is 35, so +15 stops a Hutt crime lord at 50, short of Master. His Intelligence is already 79, so any flat gain takes it past 80. Drifting toward a target meets both.
- Force Knowledge is left out: it is a Force discipline, not life experience.

## 3. Species strength

**The edge already reaches growth.** A species' Strength ceiling sets the natural baseline at half of it: a Wookiee is 60 at its peak, a Human 40. Training then adds the same on top. Same training, same age (40), Strength:

| Path | Human | Wookiee | Trandoshan |
|---|---|---|---|
| Civilian | 48 | 60 | 51 |
| Melee warrior | 56 | 68 | 58 |
| Soldier | 50 | 62 | 53 |
| Jedi Guardian Master | 48 | 60 | 53 |

A Wookiee ends 10 to 15 stronger on every path (tested). Nothing changed, so no species stat moves.

**Why Bowdaar is at 73:** he is a *civilian* on the roster, a profession archetype that hardly trains the body, though his lore is a century as a gladiator with over 10,000 bouts. At 177 a Wookiee is near his prime: 60 natural, 13 trained. As a melee warrior (D-422) he would be Strength 87, Agility 74, Endurance 81, Melee 88; as a soldier, 76 / 83 / 72. Before fix12 his 98 came from banking more than a century of training at a slow Human-equivalent age.
