## 2. Planning against a top duelist

**As proposed and built**
- **Who:** a bounty hunter or Mandalorian whose strongest fighting skill is at least 90.
- **How it is recorded:** the player says they lay an ambush, set a trap or prepare the ground for a named foe. The engine rolls it and writes one line: foe, kind, place, day, laid or not. No event of the narrator's can write one.
- **The roll:** DC 17, the better of two skills (ambush: stealth or tracking; trap: tactics or investigation; ground: tactics or tracking), once a day for each kind.
- **A plan:** two different kinds laid against that foe, at that place, in the last 3 days. It is lost if the foe strikes first, and spent by the fight.
- **What it gives:** a hold counts as a Master's (a short delay). Never a win, never the top tier, and it does not open a gate that asks for a Master.
- **Gear** suited to the foe waits for equipment; nothing records it yet.
