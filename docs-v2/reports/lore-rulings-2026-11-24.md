## Clone Wars and the Republic's fall
1. **Sifo-Dyas's Pyke mission is accepted** (TCW adds to the lore here). PP-4 step 2 stands as written.
2. **Ahsoka Tano exists, as Plo Koon's apprentice**, not Anakin's (pre-2008 Anakin is a Padawan until c. 20 BBY). She dies in Order 66. The only way she survives is PP-5 alternative 2 (Sidious escapes, exposed), where the warning race decides who lives; how that is decided is still to design. Her year-one events with Anakin (Christophsis, Teth, Ryloth, the *Malevolence*) play out without her as his Padawan.
3. **Rank dates, pre-2008 version:** Obi-Wan made a Master and given a Council seat after Haruun Kal (22 BBY); Anakin knighted after Praesitlyn and Vjun (c. 20 BBY).
4. **Maul's return keeps Savage Opress, Mother Talzin and Satine** as part of that kept event ([S-canon]).
5. **Maul's origin follows *Darth Plagueis* (Luceno, 2012):** born 54 BBY on Dathomir to Kycina, a Nightsister of Talzin's coven; Savage Opress and Feral are his blood brothers. Kycina wanted one son free of Talzin and begged Sidious to take him from the life of a Nightbrother. Trained on Mustafar as an assassin, not a full apprentice; Plagueis knew and supported it because an assassin was expendable. Sidious trained him as a Sith beyond that and gave him the title of Darth. (W: Darth Maul/Legends confirms all of this except that the title was given behind Plagueis's back, which is Jared's reading of the novel: check against the novel and add to RESEARCH_QUEUE.md.)
6. **Maul's death:** 16 BBY on Tatooine; in *Old Wounds* Kenobi hesitates and Owen Lars shoots him.
7. **Anakin's birth: 41 BBY.** (Legends had it at 41.9 BBY before later sources rounded it; the difference is small.)

## Before 1000 BBY
8. **The Ruusan blockade: both versions.** Bane orders Admiral Nyras to engage the Jedi fleet (*Path of Destruction*), which opens the blockade, and Farfalla's Knights storm through the gap (*Jedi vs. Sith*). PP-13 already reads this way.
9. **The Sith Empire's end: defeated, then vanished.** The Republic defeats it before 3600 BBY (*The Essential Guide to Warfare*); it then declines until it vanishes, at no recorded date. This fits PP-11 (the narrator runs the decline).
10. **Phanius: both versions.** He resigns on good terms and secretly takes a Sith holocron with him. The missing holocron is a clue a player can find in PP-12 step 1.
