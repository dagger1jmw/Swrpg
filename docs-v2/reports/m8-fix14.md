## 3. A long childhood

A childhood now counts its own years, but never more than a Human child's (the Chiss's 10 stay 10; a Hutt's 200 count as 18). Only the Hutt has a childhood longer than a Human's among the 55 species. Players are under the same rule.

**One more fix found.** Willpower's training stop (D-500) counted real years from the start of training, so a Hutt who began as a child stopped training Willpower at training age 8. It now counts years as the other skills do (D-507). For Humans nothing changes.
