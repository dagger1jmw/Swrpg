## Martial cultures (`SW_Martial_Cultures_AllEras`)

11. **Bunduki** was a Human world that the Epicanthix conquered.
12. **Clone assassins** stay; they're on a Legends page.
13. **Powers that come from game rules** are not lore. That covers Matukai poison immunity, temperature control, Soft to Solid and the kouhun sting; Teräs Käsi meditation healing and strike power; and any other ability sourced only to tabletop RPG or MMO rules. Mark them "game-derived" and keep them away from the narrator. Item 12 (skills and archetypes) uses them as ideas for its mechanics.
14. **The Royal Guard** is a few hundred strong, never more than about a thousand: a working figure, Ruled.
15. **Echani training:** the full art stays within the Echani castes. Outsiders (special forces, the Royal Guard, Imperial Intelligence) learn military versions.
16. **The Mistryl** are founded 22–19 BBY and reorganised as mercenaries 19–16 BBY. Their long history is part of the founding story they were told.
17. **Ilena Xan** specialises in joint locks.
18. **The Cuy'val Dar** start c. 30 BBY, from their own page and *Triple Zero*. Fix the 32 BBY in `SW_Mandalorians_preTCW_vs_TCW.md`.
19. **ARCs may train regular clones.**
20. **Stava spreads to outsiders after 9 ABY.**
