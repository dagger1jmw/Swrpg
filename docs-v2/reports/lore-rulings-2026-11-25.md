# Rulings, 25 Nov 2026 (Jared)

Four open calls from the M8 handoff, ruled on Claude's recommendation.

1. **Dac (45–138 ABY map):** the spores spread and most of the population died within a week (the Final Protocol page). The lore file's "over 80% within three days" is not used. Apply in the 45–138 ABY tag-map review and the lore file.
2. **Krayt's duel tier:** apex duelist, not "beyond any duelist" (Cade kills him). His death scenes are closed systems. Apply in the 45–138 ABY tag-map review.
3. **Non-Force fighters against Master-level foes:** the best bounty hunters and Mandalorians can beat a Master-level foe (e.g. Grievous, Baras) with planning recorded in play (ambush, trap, prepared ground). They never beat a top duelist; against one they only buy time.
4. **Karrid's sheet:** the invented values from fix8 to fix10 (Willpower 87, Niman 75, and the rest of the drafted sheet) are kept as ruled. Remove any "for review" marks.
