## 8. First battle between Jedi and Sith / did any Exiles survive on Kesh

**Recommendation:** Treat these as **two separate expeditions**. (a) c. 6,900 BBY, within a year of the conquest of Korriban, a group of ambitious new Sith Lords defies Ajunta Pall, raids Republic space with Massassi and alchemy, and is wiped out; the Jedi learn the Exiles hold a region of space (*Jedi vs. Sith*). (b) Separately, c. 6,900 BBY, Baron **Remulus Dreypa** (one of the twelve Exiles) leaves the Stygian Caldera with a band of lesser Dark Jedi and stolen Leviathan larvae; a Jedi patrol of Corbos veterans chases them to **Kesh**, where both crash and fight the **Great Calamity**. Dreypa's own followers seal him in his oubliette, make peace with the Jedi and become **the Doomed** on Eshkrene. So yes: one Exile (Dreypa, in stasis) and the descendants of his followers and the Jedi survive on Kesh, hidden from the Lost Tribe until 2,974 BBY, when Dreypa is freed and the Doomed, Dreypa and the hidden Jedi starship *Last Hope* are all destroyed (*Spiral*).

**Why:** Wookieepedia itself keeps them apart: the Great Calamity article calls the *JvS* battle "another conflict" in which the Jedi won and reported to the Council, and the Exiles article dates the *JvS* raid "within a year" of Korriban and gives the Kesh chase as a separate episode of "less important Dark Jedi" following Dreypa. Nothing contradicts that reading, and *Lost Tribe of the Sith: Spiral* (2012) is the newest and most specific source. What the Lost Tribe era needs to know: the Keshiri "Protectors and Destructors" / Great Calamity legends that Yaru Korsin's crew co-opt in 5,000 BBY are memories of this fight; the Doomed (a mixed-species, Force-sensitive, pacifist "true neutrality" community; bows, no lightsabers) watch the Tribe by Force "ice images" from 5,000 to 2,974 BBY and never intervene (they considered it c. 3,000 BBY but held off when Varner Hilts took power); a working Jedi starship lies hidden in the burial cairns under the Circle Eternal in Tahv; Dreypa's Leviathans hibernate in Sessal Spire. Note also that the Exile Remulus Dreypa is not dead in 6,900 BBY: he dies in 2,974 BBY.

**Sources:** [First battle between Jedi and Sith](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/First_battle_between_Jedi_and_Sith); [Great Calamity](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Calamity) (§Legacy); [Exiles](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Exiles) (lead, §Jen'ari); [Remulus Dreypa](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Remulus_Dreypa); [The Doomed](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/The_Doomed); [Dark Jedi faction (Doomed)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Jedi_faction_(Doomed)); [Jedi faction (Doomed)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_faction_(Doomed))

**Confidence:** Clear

## 1. Force Wars: Rakatan invasion or Ashla/Bogan war only

**Recommendation:** Use the combined version: the Force Wars run **c. 25,793–25,783 BBY** on Tython; they begin as the Rakatan Infinite Empire's invasion of the Tython system (*Dawn of the Jedi: Force War*), and once the Rakata are beaten the Je'daii split into Ashla and Bogan followers; the Ashla side founds the Jedi Order, Rajivari defects and his followers die at Kaleth, and the 25,783 BBY cataclysm drives the Jedi to Ossus.

**Why:** This is exactly how the current Wookieepedia article reads it, with the infobox putting the Je'daii Order vs. the Infinite Empire "initially" and the Ashla vs. Bogan followers after. The "conflicting sources" banner (its note: dating, weaponry, and whether the Rakata are involved) is answered by putting the Rakatan phase first, which is what *Dawn of the Jedi: Force War* (2013–14, the newest source) shows; the older account (*New Essential Chronology*, 2005) is the second phase. Both sides fight with Force-imbued metal swords, not lightsabers, which fits both.

**Sources:** [Force Wars](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_Wars); [Invasion of the Tython system](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Invasion_of_the_Tython_system); [Rajivari's purges](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rajivari%27s_purges)

**Confidence:** Clear

## 2. Why the Jedi left Tython

**Recommendation:** The Jedi leave Tython c. 25,783 BBY because a **cataclysm at the end of the Force Wars wrecks Tython's biosphere** and makes it uninhabitable; the survivors go to the Rim and settle Ossus. Arden Lyn's line (that the Order left "to distance itself from the machinations of the growing Republic") stays only as her in-character opinion.

**Why:** The cataclysm is the Wookieepedia narrative on both the Force Wars and Ossus pages (*Star Wars: The Old Republic Encyclopedia*, 2012, with *The Essential Atlas* for the flight to Ossus). Lyn's statement is a quote from a partisan enemy of the Jedi, not narration, and it can't be literal: the Republic is founded in 25,053 BBY, some 730 years after the move. The two still sit together: by Lyn's day (c. 24,500 BBY) the Jedi had indeed kept apart from the Republic on Ossus for centuries, and Tython itself was absorbed into the Republic after the Jedi left (*Essential Guide to Warfare*).

**Sources:** [Force Wars](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_Wars) (§Conclusion); [Ossus/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ossus/Legends) (§Early history, Lyn quote); [Tython/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tython/Legends) (§Abandonment); [Arden Lyn](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Arden_Lyn)

**Confidence:** Clear

## 3. Did the Jedi fight in the Unification Wars and join at the founding, or join from Ossus in 24,953 BBY?

**Recommendation:** The Jedi Order **formally joins the Republic c. 24,953 BBY**, when Duros scouts on the new Perlemian reach Ossus and Master Haune Tiar, after touring the Republic, leads the Order to pledge itself as its protector. Keep the Unification Wars (Core Worlds wars before 25,053 BBY, Zakrinand Minus) as background, and tag the claim that "the Jedi" fought and won them, with Bendu monks, as a later historians' account [unclear]; if Jedi took part, they were individual Jedi, not the Order joining at the founding.

**Why:** Wookieepedia's own Unification Wars article already reconciles the two: it keeps the Jedi victory (from the *Complete Star Wars Encyclopedia*) but says the Jedi, "initially wary of the Republic" (*New Essential Chronology*), joined it "a century after the wars' end" (SWTOR, 24,953 BBY). Its Behind the scenes notes that the Unification Wars (first named in *Revenge of the Sith Incredible Cross-Sections*, 2005) "have been ignored by most subsequent sources", which say the Republic was founded peacefully. The SWTOR codex account is newer, more specific (named Master, the Perlemian contact) and fits the Ossus and Tython pages.

**Sources:** [Unification Wars (Galactic Republic)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unification_Wars_(Galactic_Republic)) (incl. §Aftermath, §Behind the scenes); [24,953 BBY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/24,953_BBY); [Haune Tiar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Haune_Tiar); [Ossus/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ossus/Legends) (§Early history)

**Confidence:** Likely

## 5. Who reverse-engineered the hyperdrive

**Recommendation:** Around 25,053 BBY, **Corellian and Duros** engineers work around the Force-attuned parts of Rakatan drives and build the first true hyperdrives (the Duros independently as well); within twenty years the technology is in general use and the Corellians sell hyperdrives to nearby systems. Use "Corellians and Duros", not "Humans and Duros".

**Why:** The Hyperdrive/Legends history section states it this way, and the Unification Wars article cites *The New Essential Chronology* for "the Duros and Corellians" reverse-engineering the Rakatan hyperdrive. The SWTOR Encyclopedia's "Corellian scientists" and the Republic article's uncited "Humans and Duros" are both partial versions of the same fact (the Corellians are Human), so nothing is dropped.

**Sources:** [Hyperdrive/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hyperdrive/Legends) (§History); [Unification Wars (Galactic Republic)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unification_Wars_(Galactic_Republic)) (§Prelude); [Galactic Republic/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_Republic/Legends) (§Formation)

**Confidence:** Clear

## 6. Desevro after the Tionese War

**Recommendation:** c. 23,900 BBY Desevro, already pressure-bombed and devastated, offers unconditional surrender; the Republic ignores it and means to sterilise the planet as an object lesson, but the Jedi object, and in tense talks in orbit a compromise is reached: the planet is spared and the Jedi become the Watchmen of the Tion. The Union dissolves and its worlds join the Republic as a sector by c. 23,900 BBY; Desevro survives as an inhabited but decaying backwater (3 billion people in later eras). Drop both "sterilised" and "stayed independent".

**Why:** *The Essential Guide to Warfare* (2012, the newest and most detailed account, carried in the Tionese War article's History section) has the sterilisation planned and then headed off by the Jedi compromise. The Desevro article confirms the planet stayed populated, with cities that "fell into a precipitous decay", and later sides with the Sith Empire's Reaver Station, the CIS and the Empire, which a sterilised world could not do. The Tionese War article's Aftermath line ("Horrified by the sterilization of Desevro…") carries no reference of its own and contradicts the article's History section and the planet page; the uncited "independent" claim contradicts the sourced "joined the Republic by 23,900 BBY". The Jedi still pull back from the Republic in disgust while guarding its Tion frontier, which fits either way.

**Sources:** [Tionese War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tionese_War) (§History, §Aftermath); [Desevro/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Desevro/Legends) (infobox, §History); [Galactic Republic/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_Republic/Legends) (§Early history)

**Confidence:** Likely

## 7. Who started the First Great Schism

**Recommendation:** Use the sequence that fits both accounts: Xendor leaves Ossus against the Council's refusal and founds the Legionnaire Academy on Lettow; the Jedi declare war on the Legions and raise an army first (*Jedi vs. Sith*; Arden Lyn in *The Essential Guide to Warfare*); the first major battle is Xendor's attack on Ossus, which fails (*Essential Guide to Warfare*; the SWTOR codex's "Xendor attacked"). In the game, each side's records blame the other: Jedi histories say Xendor attacked, Legion survivors such as Lyn say the Jedi declared war.

**Why:** Wookieepedia states outright that "sources are divided" and gives both. Neither is neutral narration: the SWTOR codex is a Jedi-era history (Galactic History 13), and the *Warfare* version is Lyn's first-person account (she calls the Jedi "doctrinaire" and claims Xendor became general "to his dismay"). But they don't really clash on events: *Warfare* itself has the Jedi raise armies first and then Xendor strike Ossus first, so "Jedi declared war; Xendor struck first" covers every source. (The same Lyn-sourced section has the Jedi leaving Tython to step outside the Republic's influence; see item 2 for why that is her view.)

**Sources:** [First Great Schism](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/First_Great_Schism) (§Open war, §Behind the scenes); [Xendor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Xendor); [Arden Lyn](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Arden_Lyn); [Legions of Lettow](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Legions_of_Lettow)

**Confidence:** Likely

## 10. Gree–Kwa conflict dates

**Recommendation:** Date it **from before 30,000 BBY to c. 27,000 BBY**: a territorial war of millennia between the Gree Empire and the Kwa holdings, sparked by their hypergates and Infinity Gates, in which Abeloth is first freed a few centuries in; the Infinite Empire's war on the Celestial client species (its peak, c. 30,000 BBY) drives the Gree back to their home cluster and nearly exterminates the Kwa, and the conflict has petered out by c. 27,000 BBY. Fix the Part 1 row at c. 30,000 BBY that says the war "ends" then: make it "the Rakata push both back".

**Why:** Both dates come from *The Essential Atlas* (2009) and describe different moments, not rival dates: the long timeline gives the start ("prior to 30,000 BBY") and places the Rakatan push at c. 30,000 BBY; the conflict article gives the end "around the time" the Devaronians and Gossam try the tumble hyperdrive, c. 27,000 BBY. Read together, the Rakatan campaign starts the end of the war and the end is complete by c. 27,000 BBY. Low stakes: nothing else in the era depends on the exact year.

**Sources:** [Gree-Kwa conflict](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gree-Kwa_conflict); [War against Celestial client species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/War_against_Celestial_client_species); [Defeat of the Gree Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Defeat_of_the_Gree_Empire); [Annexation of Kwa holdings](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Annexation_of_Kwa_holdings); [Timeline of galactic history/Legends §Pre-Republic era](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Timeline_of_galactic_history/Legends#Pre-Republic_era)

**Confidence:** Clear

## 11. King Adas's reach

**Recommendation:** c. 27,700 BBY Adas leads the defence of Korriban, drives the Rakata off and captures their ships; with those ships his forces push on and reach **Ziost, Malachor V and Tund**; Adas dies in the course of this war against the Rakata (the sources don't say at which world or in which battle). His death sets off the Korriban civil war, and the capital later moves to Ziost.

**Why:** It is one story told at two levels of detail, and Wookieepedia's Adas article and long timeline already combine them: *Evil Never Dies* (2006) says Adas perished "in securing… a future of freedom", *The Essential Atlas* (2009) and *Jedi vs. Sith* (2007) add that his forces reached Ziost, Malachor V and Tund. Nothing in *Evil Never Dies* says he died on Korriban before the push, so the newer, more detailed version adds to it without contradicting it. The Ziost conquest also explains why Ziost is ready to become the capital after the civil war.

**Sources:** [Adas](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Adas) (§King, §Behind the scenes); [Invasion of Korriban](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Invasion_of_Korriban); [Timeline of galactic history/Legends §Pre-Republic era](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Timeline_of_galactic_history/Legends#Pre-Republic_era); [Korriban civil war](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Korriban_civil_war)

**Confidence:** Clear

## 15. Quermia: discovered c. 7,000 BBY or joins in 6,900 BBY

**Recommendation:** Keep both as two steps: Republic scouts reach the Quermian system c. 7,000 BBY (*Alien Anthology*), and Quermia joins the Republic in 6,900 BBY [weak: the 6900 BBY year page gives no source for this line]. Add, from the Quermia article, that Troiken (the Xexto homeworld) and Quermia were found by the Republic at about the same time and joined together, which is when the Xexto learned of their link to the Quermians.

**Why:** The two claims don't contradict: discovery then membership a century later is the normal pattern, and *The New Essential Guide to Alien Species* says the two worlds were discovered "at about the same time" and joined "at the same time", without dates. The joining date is the weaker fact (uncited on its year page), so tag it, but there's nothing to replace it with. It also bears on item 17: Quermia is not a Republic member before c. 7,000 BBY.

**Sources:** [Quermia/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Quermia/Legends) (§History); [Quermian/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Quermian/Legends) (§History); [6900 BBY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/6900_BBY); [Timeline of galactic history/Legends §Manderon Period](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Timeline_of_galactic_history/Legends#Manderon_Period_(7000_BBY-5000_BBY))

## 16. GenoHaradan: Xim's secret police or guardians of the Republic's founders

**Recommendation:** One continuous organisation: Xim creates the GenoHaradan c. 25,130 BBY as his secret police; after his fall (25,100 BBY) it goes underground as an assassins' guild for hire, and over the following centuries it comes to favour the young Republic and quietly protects it, working "almost exclusively" for it after the Tionese War (c. 23,900 BBY), unknown to all but a few leaders and Jedi; later the single Guildmaster is replaced by four Overseers. Drop the wording that the GenoHaradan "shielded the founders" at the 25,053 BBY founding: say it begins serving the Republic in the centuries after.

**Why:** The GenoHaradan article (SWTOR codex, *Essential Atlas* Xim material, *KOTOR*) gives exactly this sequence, so the Republic article's uncited line is the later stage of the same group, not a rival account. Its Behind the scenes notes that *KOTOR*'s in-game dates for the founding (Hulas's "five thousand years before the Republic", the quest log's "twenty thousand years") are inconsistent and unreliable, so the Xim origin (*Atlas*, SWTOR) is the one to keep.

**Sources:** [GenoHaradan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/GenoHaradan) (infobox, §Early history, §Behind the scenes); [Xim/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Xim/Legends); [Galactic Republic/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_Republic/Legends) (§Formation)

**Confidence:** Clear

## 17. Sith raids on "Republic" Draethos, Quermia, Felucia, Gand, Florn

**Recommendation:** Keep the raids but **don't date them to the 27,700 BBY expansion**: file them as "undated, between the Republic's founding (25,053 BBY) and the Exiles' arrival (6,900 BBY)", with Sorzus Syn's own note that "the time frame remains murky". The early expansion row (c. 27,700 BBY onward: Massassi conquer the Caldera worlds and Malachor V, Tund, Thule, Arorua) stays where it is; only the Draethos attack and the four raids move.

**Why:** All five come from one in-universe passage in *Book of Sith* (2012): Sorzus Syn, studying the Holocron of King Nakgru, confirms the attack on "the Republic world of Draethos" but says its timing is murky. Wookieepedia's battle pages give no date and list the Republic as the enemy, and the Draethos planet page says only "at some point after the Sith colonized the Stygian Caldera". Since the source calls Draethos a Republic world, the attack must come after 25,053 BBY; it fits the "murky" wording, contradicts nothing, and needs no loose reading of "Republic". (If Quermia was already a Republic member when raided, that would mean after c. 7,000 BBY, per item 15, but the source only calls Draethos a Republic world, so don't push the Quermia raid that late.)

**Sources:** [Sith Empire expansion](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_expansion); [Attack on Draethos](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Attack_on_Draethos); [Raid on Quermia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Raid_on_Quermia); [Draethos (planet)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Draethos_(planet))

**Confidence:** Likely

## 18. Krayiss II "Sith" library-temple c. 14,000 BBY

**Recommendation:** **Keep it**, as the Sith *species*: before 14,000 BBY, Sith colonists using Rakatan ships settle Krayiss II as part of the ancient Sith Empire's expansion and found a library-temple where pure-blooded Sith sorcerers meditate and record their experiments; c. 14,000 BBY the temple is abandoned, and its sorcerers' spirits haunt it. It is buried over time but for its obelisk; Jedi who come seeking it are killed (Jev Sunrider c. 4,008 BBY, Vara Nreem 4,000 BBY). Krayiss II is one of the five sacred Sith worlds on the Nache Bhelfia, mapped by the Exiles in 6,900 BBY.

**Why:** Wookieepedia now reads "Sith" here as the species: the planet article, drawing on *Book of Sith* (2012), says "surviving members of the Sith species" colonised it with Rakatan vessels, and calls the occupants "sorcerers of pure Sith blood". That removes the apparent anachronism (no Sith Order before 6,900 BBY) without dropping a well-attested site that matters in later eras (*Tales of the Jedi Companion*, *Book of Sith*, SWTOR, *Fate of the Jedi*). Ignore the FFG *Force and Destiny* (2015) and *Build the Millennium Falcon* (2016) material flagged on the page, per the source rules.

**Sources:** [Krayiss II](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Krayiss_II) (§Early history, §Great Sith War, §Behind the scenes); [Sith Empire expansion](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire_expansion); [Timeline of galactic history/Legends §Kymoodon Era](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Timeline_of_galactic_history/Legends#Kymoodon_Era_(15,000_BBY-11,987_BBY))

**Confidence:** Clear
