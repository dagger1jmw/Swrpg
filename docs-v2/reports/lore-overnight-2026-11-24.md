## Decisions that touch rulings or plot points already written

1. **PP-4 step 2 (Sifo-Dyas and the Pykes)** comes only from TCW season 6 (2014). Pre-2008 sources say only that Dooku killed him, place unknown. Reframe the step.
2. **Rank dates (pre-2008 primary):** Obi-Wan made Master and given a Council seat after Haruun Kal (22 BBY); Anakin knighted after Praesitlyn and Vjun (c. 20 BBY).
3. **Ahsoka doesn't exist** under rule 4a: her year-one events (Christophsis, Teth, Ryloth, the *Malevolence*) need someone else or go.
4. **Maul:** his return rests on Savage Opress, Talzin and Satine (TCW-only). In *Old Wounds* Owen Lars shoots him, not Kenobi: the 1000 BBY map's E.16.maul_dies row says Obi-Wan kills him and needs fixing. His origin is Iridonia (pre-2008) or Dathomir (2012).
5. **The Ruusan blockade:** broken by Bane's order to Nyras (*Path of Destruction*) or by Farfalla's Knights (*Jedi vs. Sith*). PP-13 uses both.
6. **How the Sith Empire ended:** the Republic won outright, it "vanished", or the Republic absorbed it. The third Galactic War ended before 3600 BBY (*Essential Guide to Warfare*), not just "before 3522".
7. **How Phanius left the Order:** an amicable resignation, or with a stolen Sith holocron (PP-12).

- SW_Legends_Galaxy_Timeline: Han Solo's early years out of order (Chewbacca saved in 5 BBY, not ~10); about fifteen 999–33 BBY dates differ from the year pages; Muunilinst came about four months after Geonosis, not four weeks; not every Temple occupant died in 19 BBY; Bane did not survive Ruusan alone.
