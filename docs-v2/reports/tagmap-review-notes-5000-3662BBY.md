# Review notes: 5000-3662 BBY tag map (Jared, 24 Nov 2026)

## Jared's three tests for every step
1. Is it realistic for a PC to be there?
2. What standing and power level does the PC need?
3. What kind of PC?
Examples: the Death Star throne room is unbreakable except by the Emperor's or Vader's death earlier (no PC would be there); the Ewoks on Endor break only if Leia is taken out on the speeder or earlier; Anakin's fall needs a High Council-level Jedi in good standing and in Anakin's trust.

## Standing rule: top duelists (all maps)
Any fight with a top duelist (Sadow, Kun, Ulic, Revan, Malak, Nihilus, Sion, Kreia; Sidious, Vader, etc. in other eras) needs at least a Master. A regular Master only holds them off briefly (Kit Fisto vs Sidious). Beating one, or holding them off long, needs a Yoda- or Mace-level character.
