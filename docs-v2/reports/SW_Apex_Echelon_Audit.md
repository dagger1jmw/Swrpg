# SWRPG Apex Echelon Audit: the top of the power scale

Legends lore reference for SWRPG. Researched 25 Nov 2026. Sources: Wookieepedia (Legends) via the API (cited per point, with section names); for Clone Wars subjects, pre-August-2008 revisions where cited by `oldid`; ruled lore files in `Docs/SWRPG lore/` where noted. FFG books from 2015 on are not used. Draft for review. Work in progress: sections are written as research proceeds (see _status_ line).

_status: complete (25 Nov 2026)_

**Method** (same as `SW_Stat_Calibration_Evidence.md`): sheet numbers copied as written; lore evidence beside them; "should be above X / below Y" only where the evidence puts a figure out of order; any number offered is marked *suggested* and is only a starting point.

**Scale** (sheet headers; game engine): Knight 41-60 · Master 61-80 · Grandmaster / High Master 81-100 · Legendary 101-150 · Transcendent 151+. Stat keys: FS = ForceSense, FC = ForceControl, FO = ForceOutput, WP = Willpower, FK = ForceKnowledge, Best = best lightsaber form or combat rating.

**Rulings by Jared supplied with this task (not yet written into the sheets):** freed Revan Niman 94 / FC 94 / WP 106; Yoda FC 104; Sidious (Chancellor) Juyo 98 / FC 104 / FO 108; Mace Vaapad 100; Malgus Djem So 92 / FC 88 / FO 90 / WP 94. Where the sheets still show older numbers, both are given.

---

## 1. Overview (headline findings)

- **The order at the very top holds; two of its justifications do not.** Abeloth on top and peak Luke as the strongest mortal are supported ("widely recognized as the most powerful Force user in the galaxy"). But Abeloth's "a dozen times Luke" is a physical-strength line, and Luke beat her manifestations one-on-one; and the Mortis anchor ("near-full-potential Anakin overcame the Son") is true only of one scene: in the final fight the Son overpowered Anakin, Obi-Wan and Ahsoka with ease (O-1, O-2).
- **Most of the apex cluster's internal order (reborn Palpatine 135, Vitiate 134, Krayt 132, Emperor 130) is a design call**: no source compares these figures. Krayt's headline (132) is higher than every number in his own block (I-4).
- **Clear out-of-order pairs**: rising Luke vs Vader II at Endor (O-3); Vader vs Yoda (O-4); Karness Muur's spirit vs his only sheet (O-8); protagonists (Outlander, Hero of Tython) vs the beings they beat (O-9). Smaller: Yoda's WP vs freed Revan's ruled WP (O-6); Zannah's WP vs Bane's (O-7).
- **The rest of the roster scales sensibly below the apex**: Malgus, Mace, Yoda, Sidious (with Jared's ruled numbers) sit in the right order; Caedus < Luke, Krayt < Luke, Jaina < Caedus, Cade < Krayt.
- **Seventeen internal inconsistencies** between blocks, quick tables and era notes (Section 5), the largest being Abeloth 185 vs 145, peak Luke FC 138 vs 130, and the Vader blocks vs their own quick table.
- **Potential vs realized** is applied only to the Skywalker line, rests on the misread Mortis feat, and uses the weaker-fit version of Vader's loss (Section 5, end).

## 2. Figure sections

### 2.1 Abeloth (Era 17 sheet, 43-44 ABY)

**Sheet** (`SW_Characters_17_FateOfTheJedi.md`): FS 175 · Med 168 · FK 180 · FC 172 · FO 185 · WP 170. No saber. Headline "≈ 185, TRANSCENDENT". Sheet claims: "stated to be on the order of a dozen times stronger than Luke in the Force"; "Luke at his absolute apex could only barely keep her from crushing his throat"; each diluted avatar "pushed apex Luke to his utter limit"; "No single being could defeat her"; Era 12 Mortis note calls her "~145" (see Inconsistencies).

**Lore**
- The "dozen times" figure is about **physical augmentation strength**, not Force power: "Abeloth's strength physical augmentation was a dozen times that of Luke Skywalker, who was widely recognized as the most powerful Force user in the galaxy. However, Skywalker was able to defeat in a duel her despite this by raining debris on her" (*Vortex*) [Abeloth §Unlimited power](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Abeloth).
- General power statements: "astonishingly powerful", "impossible for mortals to understand her dark power" (*Abyss*); Jaina likened her to a nova (*Vortex*); Tahiri called her "a living Force volcano" (*Apocalypse*) [same].
- Feats: Force waves on Tahv imploded nearby beings and melted buildings (*Ascension*); moved people "from across star systems" (*Abyss*); galaxy-range psychosis on Jedi who had been in the Maw (*Abyss*); fleet-visible Force-flash "something that Luke Skywalker was not expected to be able to do" (*Apocalypse*); feeding on Coruscant's suffering let her manifest three times at once, and it "was presumed she would become effectively all-powerful" given long enough (*Apocalypse*) [same].
- Limits: wounded by thrown objects, blades and blasters; a thermal detonator destroyed one manifestation; Ben's Oneness Force blast (frigate-scale) moved her only centimetres, but a thrown pillar cost her "blood, an eye, some skull, and some brain" [same; §At the same time]. "Nearly overcome by Vol" in a mental duel (Vol used Mind Shards and insults) (*Ascension*) [§Unlimited power]. Cannot be truly slain except by the Mortis Dagger [same].
- **Duels with Luke:** (a) Vortex, Fallanassi village: she had Luke "in a deadly embrace"; he drew on the Force to the utmost, the light side harmed her, he brought a ceiling beam down and "seemingly killed her with his lightsaber" [Luke Skywalker/Legends §Jedi Grandmaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Luke_Skywalker/Legends). (b) Apocalypse, beyond shadows: Luke and Krayt fought her together; Luke constricted *her* larynx and struck her throat; Krayt drained her and also drained Luke; Luke's elbow went through her head; Luke and Krayt each ended with fist-sized holes in the chest [Abeloth §All she did was smile, §At the same time](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Abeloth). Luke's page: "Using his bare hands and offensive Force exertions, Luke was able to temporarily destroy the dark side entity, with help from Darth Krayt" [Luke Skywalker/Legends §Jedi Grandmaster]. In the same hour Ben and Vestara killed her body at the Font (shikkar through the heart, pillar to the chest) and Saba Sebatyne beheaded a manifestation in the Temple computer core [Abeloth §Checkmate, §Pierced through the heart](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Abeloth). Her "dependency on avatars" was her weakness, "which Luke ultimately utilized to deliver the finishing blow on Abeloth in their duel" [§Unlimited power].
- No "throat-crush" of Luke by Abeloth was found in the sections read; the throat action found runs the other way (Luke on her). [unclear: a throat attack may exist in a section not read]

**Assessment.** Abeloth at the top of everything is supported (no single mortal finished her; it took several simultaneous fights). But the sheet's justification is wrong in two places: (1) "a dozen times Luke **in the Force**" misreads a physical-strength line, and the same sentence says Luke then beat her; (2) Luke repeatedly **won** single fights against her manifestations (Vortex) and delivered the finishing blow beyond shadows. That fits a figure well above peak Luke but not "beyond measurement". FO 185 vs Luke 138 (+47) is a design call, not something the lore states; the lore gives no multiplier for Force power. Order (Abeloth > Luke): **in order**. Gap size: not supported as written; see Out-of-order item O-1.

### 2.3 Darth Krayt / A'Sharad Hett (Era 18 sheet, 130-138 ABY; Era 17 pointer)

**Sheet** (`SW_Characters_18_LegacyEra.md`): Best Djem So 102 · FS 110 · FC 128 · FO 126 · WP 110 · FK 116. Headline "Tier A apex (~132)", "near-equal of peak Luke (138), below him". Quick table: "FOut 132 / FCtrl 130". APEX CEILING: Krayt ≈ 132.

**Lore**
- Statements: "the strongest Sith Lord of his time" and "one of the most capable that ever lived" (*Legacy* 16); in 40 ABY Luke's visions showed a figure "at least as ruthless and strong in the Force as the Chosen One" (*Betrayal*, *Apocalypse*); "Krayt was Luke Skywalker's dark counterpart in the contest for the Throne of Balance"; Luke "was wary of dueling Krayt" beyond shadows (*Apocalypse*) [Darth Krayt §Force Powers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Krayt).
- Forms: Niman / Jar'Kai ("master practitioner of Niman", *Legacy Era Campaign Guide*) [Darth Krayt §Lightsaber and Martial Arts Skills](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Krayt). **The sheet's primary Djem So 102 is not the named form.**
- Duels: as Hett, beat Aurra Sing (Padawan; again "barely" in 30 BBY); beat an enraged Anakin while armed only with a gaderffii (*Enemy Lines*, Clone Wars); c. 17 BBY on Tatooine held his own against Obi-Wan, then was defeated and maimed by him (*Legacy* 16); as Krayt quickly beat four Imperial Knights; nearly beat Cade (shot in the back by Cade's mother) (*Legacy* 19); killed Wyyrlok III in a saber duel; final duel 138 ABY, Cade could only kill him "when he was not prepared to defend himself" (*War* 6) [same].
- Had Abbadon, 137 ABY: overwhelming Celeste Morne's lightning, even with Karness Muur's spirit, until Azlyn Rae stabbed him; then pushed off a cliff by Morne/Muur, survived, and was electrocuted by Wyyrlok [§Force Powers; §Betrayer of the dream](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Krayt).
- Power feats: mind-controlled armies of Sith troopers ("command millions of soldiers with his will"); a galaxy-wide call that empowered all Sith after his resurrection; resurrection by dark transfer; Korriban weather control; drained Abeloth beyond shadows (and Luke with her) [§Force Powers].
- Abeloth: fought her with Luke beyond shadows; drained her (crippling her shadow-ghouls on Coruscant); "Ultimately, Krayt was able to 'kill' her along with Luke" (*Apocalypse*) [§Lightsaber and Martial Arts Skills].

**Assessment.** Krayt just below Luke is supported by the *Apocalypse* framing (counterpart; Luke wary of him; neither alone; no duel between them). Order Krayt (132) < Luke (138): in order. Krayt vs Vitiate (134) and reborn Palpatine (135): **no source compares them**; the order is a design call. Two internal issues: block FC 128 / FO 126 vs quick table "FOut 132 / FCtrl 130"; primary form Djem So vs lore Niman/Jar'Kai.

### 2.4 Jacen Solo / Darth Caedus (Era 15 NJO sheet, 25-29 ABY; Era 16 Caedus sheet, 40-41 ABY)

**Sheets**: Jacen (Era 15): Best Djem So 94 · FS 96 · FC 104 · FO 112 · WP 96 · FK 92, "Legendary". Caedus (Era 16): Best Djem So 96 · FS 96 · FC 110 · FO 120 · WP 100 · FK 94, "near-apex; surpasses Vader; below near-apex Luke".

Caedus vs Mara: Mara nearly killed him in a duel he entered wounded by her ambush; the sheet gap (Mara NJO best Ataru 88 vs Caedus 96) is wide for someone "on his level" at the blade, but there is no Era 16 Mara sheet to compare. Minor. Jacen's Onimi moment ("most powerful manifestation of the Force on record") is a momentary Oneness feat; the sheet treats it as an ability note, which is fair, but note it if any number is meant to cap "single strongest manifestation".

Era 03 origin (WP 105, FC 102) sits above Ragnos (WP 105 equal, FC 100) at a time when Ragnos was Dark Lord and recognized him; that is a fair reading of "Ragnos recognized Tenebrae's power" but not a stated ranking.

### 2.6 The Mortis Ones: Father, Son, Daughter (Era 12 sheets, c. 21 BBY) [S-canon]

**Sheets** (`SW_Characters_12_CloneWars.md`): Father FS 145 · FC 142 · FO 144 · WP 145 · FK 142; Son FS 138 · FC 136 · FO 140 · WP 140; Daughter FS 138 · FC 136 · FO 136 · WP 138. All "Ω (Mortis-bound)". Ruling text: "a near-full-potential Anakin **overcame the Son**", so "realized-peak Luke ≥ the Ones"; the Son's tier line: "Overcome by a near-full-potential Anakin on Mortis — the anchor for 'realized-peak Luke ≥ the Ones'".

**Lore** (all TCW 2008, [S-canon]; kept under rule 4a because *Fate of the Jedi* refers to Mortis)
- *Overlords*: in the Father's test Anakin "telekinetically freed Kenobi and Tano before forcing the Son and the Daughter to their knees in submission" [Son/Legends §Arrival of the Chosen One](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Son/Legends).
- *Altar of Mortis*: Son and Daughter "evenly matched", deadlocked even in beast forms; the Son then broke the weakened Father's lightning defence; he killed the Daughter by accident with the Dagger [Son/Legends §The Son's machinations](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Son/Legends).
- *Ghosts of Mortis*: the Son "fought and overpowered all three Jedi [Anakin, Obi-Wan, Ahsoka] with ease"; only after the Father stabbed himself did the Son lose "his powers and immortality", letting Anakin strike him down [Son/Legends §Death](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Son/Legends).
- Only "the Father, the Daughter, Abeloth, and Anakin Skywalker were known to have faced him with success in battle"; his connection to the Force was "on a level beyond the domain of most Sith" [Son/Legends §Powers and abilities](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Son/Legends).

**Assessment.** **The anchor ruling misstates the lore.** Anakin's success over the Son was in the Father's test (*Overlords*), with the Daughter subdued at the same time; in the final fight the Son overpowered Anakin, Obi-Wan and Ahsoka together "with ease", and Anakin killed him only after the Son lost his powers. So "near-full-potential Anakin overcame the Son" holds only for *Overlords*. The conclusion "realized-peak Luke ≥ the Ones" therefore rests on one feat (the kneeling) against two contrary ones. Son = Daughter (evenly matched) is consistent with the sheets (140/136 FO is close). Father > both: consistent. Abeloth faced the Son with success (per *Apocalypse*): consistent with Abeloth above the Ones. Numbers: no change forced, but the ruling text should be corrected, and Luke ≥ Ones should be marked a design call (see O-2).

### Potential vs realized: is the framework applied consistently?

- **Applied to the Skywalker line only.** Anakin (Knightfall 120 observed, ~138 potential), Luke (138 realized = Anakin's potential), Cade ("apex potential unrealized", FO 104). Galen Marek's "potential rivaled Sidious" is noted but not split. Other prodigies (Jacen, Kyp, Anakin Solo) carry no potential figure.
- **The "glimpse" rule is used once and misread.** Anakin's ~138 is anchored on a Mortis "glimpse" that the lore does not show as a sustained win (2.6). Jacen's Onimi moment, described as "the most powerful manifestation of the Force on record", is the closer analogue of a glimpse, but sets no ceiling. Treat both the same way or neither.
- **Vader's loss uses the version the lore file calls the weaker fit.** The Era 13 sheets assume the suit capped his output (Version A). `SW_Midichlorians_Force_Potential.md` §6 calls Version B (potential intact, limits psychological) the best fit for actual potential, with A as "how it looked to others". The sheets should say which version they follow.
- **Luke's realized = Anakin's potential** depends on the "equal" reading of the [BTS] commentary (I-17). If the "exceed" paraphrase is taken, the ceiling logic inverts (Luke's realized could exceed Anakin's potential), which does not break any order but changes the justification.
- **Entities are exempt.** Abeloth and the Ones have no potential/realized split; their numbers are "nominal floor" (Abeloth) or "Mortis-bound" (Ones). Consistent with each other, but the Ones' sheets are higher than peak Luke while the ruling says Luke ≥ them; one of the two should change (O-2).
