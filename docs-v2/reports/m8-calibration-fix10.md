## 1. The cap

Past the last sheet a stat gains at most 5: 1.4 in 3 years, 3.8 in 10, 5 at 20, then it holds. Slower growth is left alone and the body still ages. A stat the sheet leaves to the game is held to 5 over what the figure had then. In a campaign the rule is the same.

**Training years:** read as D-500's stop (46 years for a Jedi, 42 for a Sith), so about 50. A figure sheeted before that grows as ever until then, and the 5 counts from its value there.

## 3. Dark powers before a fall

Force Choke, Force Lightning and Deadly Sight, the dark powers in the skill list. A fall is the dated one, else the first dark rank after a Jedi rank.

| Figure | Fall | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| Karrid | 3,645 | Force Choke, 6 to 34 in 3,668 to 3,646, now 0 (lightning was already 0) |
| Jaesa Willsaam | 3,642 | Force Choke, 6 to 24 in 3,654 to 3,643, now 0 |
| Bengel Morr, Ako Domi, Parkanas Tark | 3,645, 3,649, 3,663 | nothing: Jedi archetypes, no dark powers at any date, after the fall either |

- **In a campaign begun before the fall,** they come with it, unless the figure has left its canon course first. Before this, Karrid in a campaign from 3,661 would never have had Force Lightning.
- **Not fallen Jedi:** Praven and Sajar (Sith first). Figures of other eras have no roster entry.

## 4. Found on the way (not changed)

- **A bug in campaigns, older than this run.** A figure whose training starts after the campaign does is calculated at almost no training, and everything, body included, is multiplied up from there. Voidhound, in a campaign from 3,661: 26 in 3,656, **764 in 3,651, 1,543 in 3,641**. From 3,661 also Meteor, Aric Jorgan, Kaliyo Djannis, Pierce, Andronikos Revel, Felix Iresso, Skadge, Tanno Vik, Guss Tuno, Tharan Cedrax and Jebevel Madon (about 2,200 to 2,600 by 3,631); from 3,681 Garza, Var Suthra, Broysc, Kilran, Jicoln Cadera, Gault Rennow and Nok Drayen. Milder (about 150 to 220): Zenith, Quinn, Harron Tavus, Rusk, Shara Jenn, Braden, Tarrick. **This is in play in a 3,661 campaign** and I would fix it next. It is careers that start at 14 to 17: the Order's children start earlier and are not hit.
- **The young heroes still run away,** because the rule keeps normal growth until about 50. By 3,620: the Barsenthor 132 form and 163 Willpower at 48, Nox 118 / 135, the Hero 122 / 125, the Wrath 125 / 125, Kira Carsen 109 / 111, Cipher Nine 140 / 147. All over the apex bar's form and Willpower long before the cap begins.
