Form ratings: Shien/Djem So 92 (primary) · Shii-Cho 82 · Juyo 80 · Ataru 76. Force abilities: Telekinesis 88 (Push 85%, Choke 82%) · Lightning 85 (Chain 80%) · Force Body 86 (Enhance 88%) · Barrier 78.

**Combat (dueling record)**
- 3681 BBY, Korriban station: nearly killed Padawan Satele (Darach saved her with a saber throw); after Darach mortally wounded Vindican, Malgus took Vindican's saber and killed Darach with "a brutal, rage-filled assault" [Kao Cen Darach §Outbreak of War, §Sacrifice](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kao_Cen_Darach). Used Jar'Kai to kill Darach "despite the latter's proficiency in the same fighting style"; "strength, rage, and clear focus" let him swiftly disarm Darach of one saber [Darth Malgus §Lightsaber training](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Malgus). The Encyclopedia notes Darach's defeat "could only be circumstantial" since Malgus was already one of the Empire's most seasoned fighters [Kao Cen Darach §Powers and abilities](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kao_Cen_Darach).
- c. 3667 BBY, Alderaan: dueled Knight Satele, drove her to her knees, cut her saberstaff in half (she blocked his blade bare-handed by tutaminis); then Malcom's grenade, and Satele's Force blasts threw him into a cliff and brought the cliff down on him [Darth Malgus §The Battle of Alderaan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Malgus). Satele's page: "Malgus proved to be the superior lightsaber duelist… before being caught off guard by Malcom and overwhelmed by Shan's astonishing Force powers" [Satele Shan §Powers and abilities](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satele_Shan). **So: won the blade exchange, lost the encounter.** (Sheet says "beat Satele one-on-one; only a grenade saved her", which leaves out that her Force powers then defeated him.)
- 3653 BBY, Jedi Temple: killed Master Ven Zallow "in a short but ferocious duel"; Zallow landed a hilt strike to his face but "was no match for Malgus" [Ven Zallow §Biography, §Powers and abilities](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ven_Zallow). Zallow is called his "most famous kill" [Darth Malgus §Lightsaber training](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Malgus).
- 3653 BBY, Coruscant: duel 1 with Knight Aryn Leneer was a draw (Korr interrupted after "a lengthy display of athleticism"); duel 2, she injured him, then he overwhelmed her with Force lightning and spared her [Aryn Leneer §A quest for vengeance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aryn_Leneer). Their speed was too great for an observer to follow [Darth Malgus §Force abilities](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Malgus).
- Crossed blades with Lord Adraas (stopped by Angral) [Darth Malgus §Disillusionment](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Malgus); strode through Adraas's lightning and redirected it [Darth Malgus §Force abilities](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Malgus).
- Style: "exceptionally talented duelist" using rage and Form V "brute strength", plus Form IV acrobatics; Jar'Kai on occasion [Darth Malgus §Lightsaber training](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Malgus). Djem So by name is implied by the Form V link, not stated as "Djem So".
- Later: defeated at Ilum (c. 3640 BBY) by class protagonists (narrative in SW_Great_Galactic_War files; not re-read here); "it took various Force-users to immobilize him on Elom" [Darth Malgus §Force abilities](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Malgus).

**Force Control (precision)**
- Can hide his presence in the Force; sensed a Jedi who suppressed his own signature [Darth Malgus §Force abilities](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Malgus).
- Tutaminis: absorbed blasterfire one-handed at Ilum [same].
- Redirected Adraas's lightning through saber and fingers [same].
- Under the Alderaan rubble he "controlled his anger and sharpened his power" before blasting it away [same].
- Most of his named feats are power and rage feats, not finesse; the page names no fine-control feat (healing, mind-reading, shielding others).

**Force Output (raw power)**
- Held up two collapsed buildings of duracrete and steel, then blew the rubble into nearby buildings [Darth Malgus §Force abilities](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Malgus).
- Lightning blast killed three Jedi at once; perfected lightning to a Force Maelstrom (Book of Sith) [same].
- Force wave tore the doors off a medical facility and threw a crowd [same]; Force scream shattered a column and destroyed fighters in range [same].
- "Handle multiple opponents simultaneously without much effort" (Deceived trailer) [same].

**Willpower / endurance**
- Survived rockets and a point-blank grenade; "seriously injured but he survived and felt no pain" at Alderaan [Darth Malgus §Force abilities](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Malgus); jaw wounds left him on a respirator [Darth Malgus §Revenge and the third lesson](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Malgus).
- Tolerated Force lightning; walked through Adraas's barrage [same, §Force abilities].
- Broke with Emperor and Empire to found his own (ideological independence) [Darth Malgus §A New Emperor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Malgus) (section title; content summarized in SW_Characters_09_GGW_ColdWar_Part2).

**Relative note.** Combat: above Darach, Zallow, Aryn Leneer and Knight-era Satele at the blade (each lost or drew); his peers in power are the other Dark Council-tier warriors. FC: his record is raw power plus rage; Aryn (a Knight) and Satele beat him on *control-type* tools (Aryn snatched his thrown saber out of his telekinetic grip; Satele's tutaminis caught his blade). Evidence supports Jared's note: **FC should sit below his combat (92) and below his FO**, since every big feat is output. FO: high (building-lifting, crowd waves, Maelstrom), but Knight-Satele's telekinesis out-muscled him at Alderaan. WP: the "felt no pain" survival supports a high WP; 94 fits.

## A4. Aryn Leneer (NO SHEET)

**Combat:** "a powerful Jedi and a remarkably skilled duelist" [Aryn Leneer §A skilled combatant](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aryn_Leneer). Later admitted she was "no longer the fighter she once was" [Aryn Leneer §A skilled combatant](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aryn_Leneer).
**FC:** seized Malgus's thrown saber out of his "mental grasp" mid-flight; telekinetic disarms of a Sith adept and Eleena Daru; split a Sith's Force wave in two with a gesture; slowed a 50-km fall from orbit with tutaminis protecting herself and Korr; blunted every impact when Malgus threw her [same]. **FO:** hurled six cargo-tram cars "with a gesture but barely an effort"; threw two Sith adepts into statues hard enough to shatter them [same]. But "Malgus was able to overwhelm Leneer with his telekinetic abilities" [same].
**WP:** gave up revenge and spared Daru at the point of killing her [same §A quest for vengeance].

**Relative note.** Combat: close below Malgus (a draw, then a loss in which she wounded him); plausibly at or above Zallow, her master, who lasted a "short" duel [inferred]. FC: her feats are finesse-heavy; she out-controlled Malgus in one exchange (stole his saber) → her FC should be **at or above Malgus's**, even though her output and combat are lower. FO: below Malgus (he overwhelmed her telekinetically). **If a sheet is made, she is the clearest evidence that Malgus's FC 95 is too high**: a Knight who beat him on control while losing on power.
