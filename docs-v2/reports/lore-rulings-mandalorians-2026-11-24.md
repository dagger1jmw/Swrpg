# Lore rulings: Mandalorians, c. 60–19 BBY (Jared, 24 Nov 2026)

Source file: `Docs/SWRPG lore/SW_Mandalorians_preTCW_vs_TCW.md`. Jared chose option A, "Clan Mandalore with a New Mandalorian enclave". Mark the rows that go beyond the sources "Ruled (Jared, 24 Nov 2026)".

1. **The framework is clan Mandalore.** Every pre-TCW event and date stays. Mandalore is a clan world with its capital at Keldabe. The title of *Mand'alor* is vacant after Jango's death until Spar claims it.
2. **The Mandalorian Protectors are the main Mandalorian force of the Clone Wars.** Spar raises them, fights for the Confederacy, and holds MandalMotors. New Holstice and Zaadja stay in 22 BBY. Sidious sacrifices them at Norval II. Fenn Shysa becomes *Mand'alor* in 19 BBY. Spar holds the title before him. *Order 66*'s "first since Jango" line for Shysa is the clans' own view.
3. **Death Watch is a small remnant cell under Pre Vizsla.** The order was effectively wiped out when Jango killed Tor Vizsla on Corellia, c. 42 BBY, two years after Galidraan. At Galidraan (44 BBY) the Jedi, tricked by Death Watch, wiped out the True Mandalorians. Pre's cell works from Concordia. It is never a planetary army.
4. **The New Mandalorians are an enclave.** Duchess Satine leads a pacifist faction centred on Sundari, in the desert region. The Republic recognises it; the clans do not. Almec and Bo-Katan stay, inside that setting. Drop "pacifist rule over Mandalore for centuries" and the planet-wide Great Clan Wars. Keep the 738 BBY Excision only as the history of that region and movement.
5. **Maul's takeover in 20 BBY covers Sundari only.** The Shadow Collective is defeated in 20 BBY. Spar's Protectors take Sundari and drive Maul out, and Maul goes on to his death on Tatooine in 16 BBY. The Protectors taking Sundari is Ruled, not lore.
6. **The Cuy'val Dar are Mandalorians on the Republic side.** Jango summoned 100 trainers to Kamino, the Cuy'val Dar, and 75 of them were Mandalorians he recruited. They trained the clones. Traviss's *Republic Commando* novels cover this. So Mandalorians fight on both sides of the war: the Protectors for the Confederacy, and the Cuy'val Dar and the clones they trained for the Republic.
7. **Jango Fett is a Mandalorian.** Drop the TCW line that he wasn't.
8. Tag every TCW-sourced row [S-canon], as the TCW rule says.
