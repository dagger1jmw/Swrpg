## 2. One clock in holds

**Why a worth per exchange could not stay.** A regular Master lasts about 25 exchanges against Malgus: some three minutes at 5 to 10 s each. At that rate nearly every hold would buy any place's time. So what is tuned now is when the duelist gets past.

**The rule.** Each exchange takes the clock's 5 to 10 s. After each, the duelist may get past, by a chance that rises with the ability gap: 1.2% at 2 or less, 5.5% at 12, 10% at 20, 21% at 24, 28% at 31, 53% at 38, 75% at 45, 95% at 70. Getting past is a blow (margin 8 to 20, plus 0.3 a point of gap) that strikes the holder down or aside. The duel's own dice are unchanged, and the holder can still be beaten first.

**The player's own hold:** the fight's clock until the same chance says the way past was open. The fight itself goes on as it would have.

**Shares** that buy a corridor's 20 s / a hangar's 45 s (400 holds each; fix3's in brackets):

| Holder | Malgus | Marr | Satele | Revan | Sidious |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Knight, 30 | 23 / 1 (10 / 1) | 22 / 1 (24 / 2) | 19 / 1 (25 / 2) | 8 / 0 (4 / 0) | 1 / 0 (0 / 0) |
| **Master, 45** | **60 / 25 (54 / 19)** | **60 / 23 (58 / 25)** | **59 / 22 (59 / 29)** | **51 / 14 (51 / 18)** | **6 / 0 (15 / 0)** |
| High Master, 60 | 82 / 57 (83 / 57) | 82 / 56 (81 / 60) | 81 / 55 (81 / 61) | 73 / 42 (77 / 53) | **13 / 1 (57 / 22)** |
| Near Malgus's level | 97 / 90 (96 / 88) | 98 / 90 (97 / 90) | 98 / 90 (97 / 90) | 93 / 80 (96 / 88) | 61 / 26 (76 / 53) |

- **Apex:** the gap counts 1.15 times against an apex duelist or one beyond.
- **What became of regular Masters** against Malgus: 87% dead, 7% taken or left, 6% standing (fix3: 81, 12, 7). The median hold is 3 exchanges and 25 s.
- **High Masters beaten more often:** fewer break a top duelist off them (standing 7 to 10%, from 16 to 44%), because the duelist is usually past before that.
- **Narration:** the pace lines now say a *burst* within the exchange, and the narrator is told an exchange is 5 to 10 s whoever fights.

## 3. Force Speed

- **Who:** a canon Force user whose sheet lists neither Force Speed nor a Force Body. A named Force Body already counts in a chase, as before.
- **From:** the mean of Force Control and Force Output. 40 gives 20, 60: 36, 80: 53, 100: 70, 120: 86, 150: 110. The sheets that list it run at 65 to 86% of that mean, so this stays under most of them.
- **Values:** Vitiate 94, Jadus 68, Nox 62, the Dread Masters 61, Angral and the Barsenthor 58, Acina and Thanaton 57, Baras 56, Zash and Mekhis 55, Vindican and Darnala 54, Zym 52, Ngani Zho 49, Ragate 42.
- **In fights:** it is in the Force boost that comes off the sheet's body, so Strength, Agility and Endurance in combat stay as written. Maximum Vitality rises with it (0.2 a point).
- **Sidious** has no sheet in the game yet. As generated, he takes it from his generated levels; from the lore sheet (118 and 125) he would have 87.
