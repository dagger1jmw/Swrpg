## Rulings on the 14 decisions (Part 3)
1. **Lightsaber vs vonduun armour:** the armour stops slashes and blaster bolts. Thrusts are resisted but can get through with a strong hit. Called shots at the armpit gills, a bare face or the throat bypass it (as with Mara at Belkadan and Jaina at Ebaq 9). Force lightning destroys it. Crushgaunts crack it through continuous pressure. Bafforr pollen kills armour and wearer, but it is unobtainable after Ithor. The Ando shellcrusher carbine is the one blaster that works against it.
2. **Amphistaff vs lightsaber:** it can't be severed. A saber can wound it but not cut through it, which covers Luke and the Scepter of Power. It dies from repeated blunt blows to the head or one blow to the centre of the head crest.
3. **Jacen on Helska IV:** cloaker plus gnullith.
4. **Vacuum:** the cloaker is the vacuum suit. The *Trickster* masquer case is a one-off exception.
5. **Jacen's slave seed:** follow his own page (Vergere stabbed it in and it tied him to a dhuryam). The hook-of-bone failure was an earlier, separate attempt [inferred].
6. **Embrace of Pain after 40 ABY:** Embraces exist in the Legacy era (Krayt, the One Sith, Maladi). "Last known" meant known to the Jedi.
7. **Yammosks:** they sense the Force and recognise Jedi. Voxyn are the Jedi-hunting creation. A PC hiding from a yammosk needs a Vergere-style "make yourself small" check.
8. **Krayt's armour:** an exoskeleton grown from his implants, not vonduun crab armour.
9. **Dark Transfer:** works on Vong. Maladi's bioform was a specially engineered exception (One Sith, Legacy era only).
10. **Surge coral:** both are true. Slaves normally vanish in the Force. A trained Force user with a Vong-biotech link can, as a rare narrative event, turn it into Vongsense. It is never a choice made off a menu.
11. **Whipsting and finger-spear:** two variants of one shaper-hand weapon. Shaper caste only.
12. **Fighting claw:** keep it, tagged [last resort]. Warrior implant; NPC gear.
13. **Tokhlor tallow 1.6 kg:** drop the figure. Keep the item; any weight is design-assigned.
14. **Mind tricks:** apply the general rule. Powers aimed at a Vong's body or mind fail, including mind tricks, sensing, choke and a push on the body. Things that work:
    - telekinesis on objects and the surroundings, including air pressure;
    - telekinesis on gear (masquer, senalak), case by case;
    - Force lightning and Emerald lightning, the dark-side Force net, Dark Transfer;
    - Shatterpoint, partly.
    Jedi notice Vong as "holes" in the Force. Mara sensing masquered infiltrators stays [unclear]; the narrator may allow it only as noticing a hole.

## Design rules from the gaps (tagged design, not lore)
- **Bonding:** a living weapon (amphistaff, tsaisi) is bonded to its captor. A stranger needs a bonding event: fluency in the Vong language (Jariah Syn), Vongsense (Jacen), or capture at the polyp. Without one, the weapon is inert or hostile. Villips, cloakers, gnulliths and healing swatches work for anyone. A qahsa needs its genetic key.
- **Castes:** there are no weapon bans by caste. Rank items (tsaisi, command cloak, grafted armour, Steng's Talons, Cloak of Nuun) are socially locked; a non-entitled Vong carrying one is a serious offence. Shaper-hand weapons need the graft.
- **Stun immunity:** Vong ignore stun settings and spray-stick shocks, and snap bugs barely affect them.
- **Pain cost:** most biots that go on the body or into it (masquer, cloaker, fighting claw, implants) hurt the wearer. The gnullith is the exception. Implants risk rejection (Shamed status), Steng's Talons rarely survive implantation, and Yorik-Kul slowly kills.
- **Availability by era:**
  - pre-war: Praetorite agents only;
  - war (25–29 ABY): outsiders get gear by capture only (live armour is rare);
  - post-war: thud and razor bugs are contraband, used by the underworld, and the Jedi train against modified vonduun armour;
  - Legacy era: rare, through Vong exiles, Nei Rin's shapers, feral biots on Wayland and One Sith labs.
  Prices and training times have no source; invent them at M8, tagged `invented`.
- **Embrace of Pain:** a character held in it can't use the Force. Long exposure pushes toward the dark side through the existing fall-path mechanics; no new system.
- **Vongsense:** add to the Force catalog as a special sense, marked review, built at M8. It needs a link to Vong biotech (lambent, slave seed, shaping); works only on Vong and their biots; some users must set aside their Force connection to use it. Era: 26 ABY onward.
- **Lambent crystal:** add to the lightsaber crystal list (batch 3). It is harvested under planetary or dovin-basal gravity and gives an attuned user a vague sense of Vong. Users: Anakin Solo (26 ABY), Cade Skywalker (137 ABY).
