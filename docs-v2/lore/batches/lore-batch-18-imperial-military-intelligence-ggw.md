## Rulings on the 10 decisions
1. **Commanders in 3661:** no Moff, admiral or general is named for that year; don't invent one as "the" commander. Grand Moff Zellos and Admiral Layek Davos can appear as serving officers, tagged [inferred]. The ministers are on firmer ground:
   - Shareis, Minister of War, in post [inferred from 3660];
   - Shullis Khamarr, Minister of Logistics. Her own page says she held the post during the war, so she may be in post in 3661, tagged [unclear].
2. **Navy ranks:** use the Imperial Navy page's list (the longest), with its sub-grades.
3. **Grand Moff:** a real wartime rank (Zellos). It could also be conferred as an honour, as with Kilran after the war. Both uses stand.
4. **Armour colour:** gray is the standard. The sourced variants are unit markings: black and white for commandos, green for grenadiers, with black and red also attested. The Imperial Guard wear white with red.
5. **Rim Campaign and Ord Radama:** c. 3660 BBY, tagged [gray]. The 3655 BBY author's intent stays [BTS]. The blockade (3661–3660) and the Rim Campaign overlap at the start of 3660.
6. **Operation: Undertow and Fixer Seven:** c. 3664 BBY. Watcher X's forced retirement to Shadow Town comes later, undated, and Shadow Town is out of 3661 (batch 16), so in 3661 Watcher X is in post or under review [unclear].
7. **Soldiers' training:** the sourced route is conscription at adulthood, then live-fire exercises in the Dromund Kaas jungles within weeks, then 25–30 years of service. A naval officer's first command is often a Gage-class transport, and demotion back to one is a known punishment. An officer academy may be invented if a story needs one, tagged `invented`. Don't use the Canon or Galactic Empire "Imperial Academy".
8. **Terminus-class destroyer:** allowed as the Harrower's escort, tagged [unclear] (undated on the general pages; its own page says "by the Cold War"). The Harrower and the Gage remain the attested core.
9. **Keeper and Minister of Intelligence in 3661:** unnamed. The narrator refers to them by title only.
10. **Aliens in the military:** none in the regular forces in 3661; formal admission comes after Ilum in 3640. Routes for an alien Imperial PC:
    - a Chiss agent or liaison (the Ascendancy is the Empire's only true ally; Chiss Imperial Agents are attested);
    - a Force-sensitive slave sent to Korriban (batch 12);
    - an informer or asset outside the formal ranks.
    Field hospitals treated only favoured species (Humans, Chiss, Duros, Kaleesh, Twi'leks, Aqualish, Zabraks, Purebloods), a detail the narrator can use.
