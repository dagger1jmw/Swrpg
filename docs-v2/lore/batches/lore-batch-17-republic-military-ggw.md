## Rulings on the 11 decisions
1. **Supreme Commander in 3661:** Rans, tagged [inferred] (the only holder named for the war; consistent with batch 6's order: Rans, then Stantorrs late in the Cold War, Rans again briefly, Malcom from 3640).
2. **Havoc Squad:** a Great War unit (Infantry Squad 326, named "Havoc" under Malcom). The post-Tython origin on the Havoc page's History section is the minority version. Its commander in 3661 is Jek Kardan [inferred], with Tavus a member since 3667.
3. **Special Forces:** founded during the Great War (Garza a founder; Omas, Kaedan's team, Tavus). The trooper page's post-Treaty origin is the minority version.
4. **Malcom's rank:** both pages fit if read as a sequence. He was a colonel after Alderaan (3667) and a general by Hoth (3665) [inferred]. In 3661 he is General Malcom.
5. **Training:** 18 weeks of basic training, then up to 12 months of specialist training, used for this window, tagged [conflict].
6. **Garza in 3661:** not placed by the sources. She commanded Special Forces during the war; put her where a story needs her, within Special Forces or the Minos Cluster, tagged.
7. **Korr's discharge:** after the deaths and injury in his family, sometime before 3653. Default for 3661: still serving [inferred].
8. **Numbered fleets:** leave Republic fleets unnumbered. If a story needs a number, invent one tagged `invented` (e.g. "Third Fleet"); never "Seventh".
9. **Officer schools:** Navy officers train at the Coruscant Naval Academy. The Army officer school is unnamed; if a name is needed, invent it, tagged `invented`. Don't use Carida in this window.
10. **First Battle of Bothawui:** 3671 BBY, matching the earlier batches (Allusis killed 3671). Greik's c. 3670 is the minority version.
11. **Blockade route:** the Hydian Way. Record the SIS page's "Rimma Trade Route" as an error or variant, not a second route.

## Design defaults for the gaps (tagged `invented`)

- **Ground vehicles:** none are attested. Generic transports, speeders and armour may be narrated, unnamed, tagged `invented`.

## Who is where in 3661 BBY (military)
- **Command:** Supreme Commander Rans [inferred]; General Jace Malcom [inferred].
- **Havoc Squad:** Jek Kardan commanding [inferred]; Harron Tavus a member. As the war nears its third decade, Tavus and Orgus Din lead forces in the Minos Cluster (the Karideph win).
- **Special Forces:** Elin Garza (placement open, ruling 6).
- **Jedi with the military:** Ven Zallow (Rim Campaign, c. 3660); Jaric Kaedan.
- **SIS:** Marcus Trant, a field agent. This year the SIS abducts the Cathar prince Shange and holds him in stasis on Belsavis.
- **Possibly still serving:** Zeerid Korr [inferred].
- **Dead:** Laren Omas (Hoth, 3665).
- **The Navy:** still reeling from Hoth; the *Intercessor* is one of the few ships that escaped intact.
