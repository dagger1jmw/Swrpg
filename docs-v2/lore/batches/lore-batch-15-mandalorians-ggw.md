## Rulings on the 10 decisions
1. **The Lesser's title:** 3667 BBY. The Empire courted the Mandalorians that year and he claimed the title. The clans rallied on Geonosis over 3667–3665, and the army left a year after that.
2. **Gap before the Lesser:** centuries. No Mand'alor between the Preserver (c. 3951 BBY) and the Lesser.
3. **Battle on the Hydian Way:** 3660 BBY. The blockade runs 3661–3660, matching batch 14.
4. **The Vindicated's accession:** c. 3660 BBY, right after the blockade's defeat and the Great Hunt. The *Encyclopedia*'s "after the Treaty" stays [BTS].
5. **Crusader's schism:** under the Vindicated, c. 3660–3652 BBY [inferred from Cadera's "nearly a decade" in hiding before c. 3642]. In 3661 it hasn't happened; Clans Ordo, Itera, Kelborn and Cadera are dissenters in waiting, still loyal in form.
6. **Great Hunt:** keep both. The Defenestrator won a Hunt between 3678 and 3668. The Lesser's c. 3660 Hunt "revived" the contest as a Mandalorian rallying event; read "revived" loosely.
7. **Culture from other eras:** allowed as flavour, tagged `[other era]`: Resol'nare, Mando'a vocabulary, adoption, nomad life, food, verd'goten. Note that the old Neo-Crusader zealotry (outsiders inducted under threat of death) had faded by this era.
8. **Beskar:** rare in this window (design default, tagged `invented`). Most warriors wear ordinary Mandalorian armour; full beskar is a mark of wealth or standing. The state of Mandalore and its mines is unrecorded; don't describe it beyond what the file gives.
9. **Republic-side Mandalorian PCs:** allowed as exceptions. Most clans refused the Sith in 3667 and stayed independent nomads, so a nomad mercenary who takes Republic contracts is plausible. They have no institutional backing, and the Lesser's followers see them as traitors. After 3660 the schism clans are the natural route, at the cost of the stigma of arue'tal.
10. **Kyramud and Davaab craft:** excluded from this war. Blockade ships are Mandalorian cruisers and support ships.

## Who is where in 3661 BBY
- **Mandalore the Lesser:** commanding the blockade (the stretch between Devaron and the Minos Cluster). His real name is unknown; the narrator never gives one.
- **Artus Lok:** rising under the Lesser; exact whereabouts unknown.
- **Shae Vizla:** an Imperial contractor for heavy-risk work, hating Jedi since her brother's death (c. 3664). Place her where a story needs her, consistent with her recorded deeds.
- **Jicoln Cadera:** Clan Cadera chief, not yet in revolt; whereabouts unknown.
- **The warship *Beskad*:** lost this year.
- **Mandalorian Jedi hunters:** active (the Bakarn pattern).
