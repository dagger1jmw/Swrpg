## Rulings on the 10 decisions
1. **Supreme Mogul:** Karagga the Unyielding, ruling from Nal Hutta, tagged [inferred] for the window (from "nearly two centuries by the Cold War").
2. **Barrga's Rendili deal:** during the war, before the blockade run, following the order of Visz's career on her page (the *Crimson Fleece* lost on Corellia, then the *Vanguard*, then the blockade).
3. **The Exchange's defeat by the Shadow Syndicate:** c. 3643 BBY, after the window.
4. **The Lesser's Great Hunt:** stays c. 3660 BBY, per batch 15. The sources for c. 3660 (the Lesser, Great Hunt c. 3660 and Vindicated biography pages, plus the game, the Holonet site and *The Journal of Master Gnost-Dural*) outnumber those for "after the Treaty" (the Great Hunt competition page, the Vindicated intro and the *Encyclopedia*). Record "after the Treaty" as the minority version. The three wartime Hunts on the competition page (the Defenestrator, Bloodworthy, a third) stand. Either way, the Lesser is Mandalore in 3661.
5. **Quesh breach:** c. 3643 BBY, after the window. The treaty is unbroken throughout the war.
6. **Operation: Undertow:** c. 3664 BBY. Watcher X's move to Shadow Town is later and undated; Shadow Town doesn't appear in 3661 (ruling 7).
7. **Nar Shaddaa districts:** Nar Shaddaa was never sacked, so batch 9's looser Kaas City rule applies, not Coruscant's.
   - Districts attested in the Cold War may appear in 3661, tagged "unconfirmed in window": the Corellian, Nikto and Duros Sectors, the Promenade, the Red Light Sector, Network Access. They come without their Cold War-specific occupants and events.
   - These stay out, because they depend on post-Treaty developments: Shadow Town (Imperial prison slum), the Bounty Brokers Association, the Republic Embassy (the treaty forbids the Hutts to further Republic interests), the Shadow Syndicate.
   - The Refugee Sector and the Jekk'Jekk Tarr belong to the 3951 BBY refugee wave; keep them out unless a story needs an old ruin, tagged `invented`.
   - The Undercity, the Cartel docks and shipyards, and an unnamed cantina are on record and always available.
8. **Tatooine:** not Hutt-ruled in this window (Hutt control comes in 65 BBY). The Exchange holds Mos Ila, which was founded during the war, so in 3661 it may be newly founded or not yet taken [unclear].
9. **Hutt currency:** allowed in Hutt Space as flavour (peggats, truguts, wupiupi), tagged `[other era]`. Credits remain the main money.
10. **Visz's hiding:** c. 3659 BBY [inferred].

## Who is where in 3661 BBY
- **Nal Hutta:** Karagga [inferred]; Suudaa Nem'ro (Jiguuna).
- **Nar Shaddaa:** Barrga, based there, employing Visz before the Corellia debacle. Hylo Visz, a young Cartel smuggler working toward independence, on the moon or around Ord Mantell. Tyresius Lokai, Zale Barrows and Quan are freelancing, before the 3660 run.
- **Rising:** Nok Drayen, from small-time smuggler to syndicate lord; his massacre of half the Cartel is undated, so don't place it before 3661 without a reason.
- **Bounty hunters:** Braden is active.
- **On Coruscant:** Jewl'a Nightbringer, a child in the lower levels during the food riots (a future Hunt champion).
- **Not yet:** the SIS bureau under Marcus Trant (from 3654); the Shadow Syndicate; the Bounty Brokers Association.
