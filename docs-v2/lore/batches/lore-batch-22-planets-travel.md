## Step 2: Rulings

**Dates**

1. **Battle on the Hydian Way: 3660 BBY.** This confirms batch 20 (ruling 9). The blockade runs 3661–3660, and "3661" on the battle page is recorded as a conflict.
2. **Serenno falls c. 3660 BBY**, in the Rim Campaign. Record "about 3661" (the Serenno page) as a conflict. In 3661 Serenno is Republic-held, with Ven Zallow's base.

**Travel**

3. **Base travel-time table: approved as a design item, tagged `invented`.** Legends gives no point-to-point trip times for the era, so the table is ours. Its shape:
   - Travel follows lanes. A trip is a sum of hops between stops on a route, each with a base time at Class 1.
   - Hyperdrive class multiplies the base time (Class 2 = twice as long, Class 0.5 = half), as Legends states.
   - Leaving the lanes costs more time and risk. Plotting a jump to a new destination takes hours (lore).
   - Ships climb out of gravity wells before jumping, which adds sublight time at both ends (lore).
   - Hazards: the Deep Core is slow; the Maelstrom forces sublight; Ruhnuk's nebula has cut it off since 3663; asteroid fields lengthen trips.
   - Blockades close segments: the Hydian Way south of Devaron is closed at the 3661 start. Smugglers can run it at high risk (the Visz storyline).

   Claude Code drafts the numbers: base hop times, the off-lane multiplier and the climb-out time. It labels them invented and lists them for my review. The only real anchor is the Imperial-era Coruscant–Alderaan trip of about 16 hours, which is a guide from another era, not era data. **Don't wire the numbers into gameplay until I approve them.**
4. **Hyperdrive classes:** the *Ascendant Spear* at Class 0.5 is lore. The KOTOR Campaign Guide classes (Class 2 for the *Hammerhead*, *Aurek*, *Foray* and *Kandosii*) may be used for those ship types in 3661, tagged `game stat`. Ships with no rating (the *Valor*, *Harrower*, *Terminus*, *Thranta*, *Fury*, *Defender* and common freighters) get a `house default` class from the draft table.

**What each side knows**

5. **Dromund Kaas in 3661:** the Republic knows the name (from 3668) but has no usable coordinates. The world is "safe from Republic reprisal" through the war, and the first Republic assault is in 3640. Republic characters can't travel there at the 3661 start, and a story that puts one there needs a reason, such as capture or a stolen Imperial navicomputer. Imperial characters travel there normally.
6. **Quesh:** it isn't on public charts in 3661. The Republic found it at some point in the war and dismissed it, but the year isn't stated. At the 3661 start it may sit in Republic survey records only, with no settlement and no Hutt interest. It becomes important c. 3643.
7. **Gated at the 3661 start** (no known route, secret, or not yet existing), each with the year it opens:
   - **Tython:** lanes erased. It opens after 3653 via Satele Shan's route. This fits batch 20: the Order is relocating 3653–3650 and the Temple is finished in 3647.
   - **Voss and Denova:** found after the Treaty.
   - **Belsavis:** Republic-secret. Imperial characters can't know it until the Galactic War. Republic characters know it only with a story reason (SIS, prison staff).
   - **The Maelstrom station:** Imperial-secret until 3642.
   - **Sebaddon:** secret, and only from 3658.
   - **Rishi:** hidden in the Rishi Maze.
   - **Nathema:** routes erased.
   - **Zakuul:** 3636.
   - **Odessen and Iokath:** c. 3631.
   - **Obus VI:** uncharted.
   - **Mek-Sha:** after 3638.
   - **Port Nowhere:** Cold War or later.
   - **Ruhnuk:** cut off from 3663.
   - **Yavin 4:** remote and unheard of, so not a destination without a story reason.
8. **Ilum** is a Republic and Jedi staging world reached by difficult lanes. It's on the Republic's charts only, not the Empire's.
9. **Odacer-Faustin:** don't place an Imperial academy there at the 3661 start. The first dated student is in 3655. Before that, the world can appear as Imperial space with no academy named.
10. **Kaleesh (amends batch 21, ruling 21):** Kalee is in Wild Space, and the Kaleesh were "only recently contacted" by the Galactic War. Kaleesh are NPC-only at the 3661 start, with the same gate as Voss. First check the Kalee page wording in Part 2. If it puts contact before 3661, drop this gate and say so in the report.

**Places and data**

11. **SWTOR-era places on worlds that are open in 3661:** they may be used, tagged "unconfirmed in window", unless they exist only because of a later event. Each entry names those, for example the Taris resettlement (3648), the Tython Temple, Mek-Sha and Vaiken Spacedock. This is the looser Kaas City and Nar Shaddaa rule from batches 12 and 16, now applied to every world. **Coruscant keeps its strict rule** (batch 14).
12. **Population figures** from infoboxes often belong to other eras. Keep them as background, tagged with their era. The narrator may give orders of magnitude ("billions") but never an exact figure as a 3661 fact.
13. **Mention-only worlds** (the Part A3 index) go on the map as background nodes with control blank. Travel there is allowed. Local colour may be invented, tagged `invented`, as long as it doesn't contradict galactic control (batch 21, ruling 18). Worlds whose only grid square is Canon-only stay off the map until a Legends location turns up. They can still be mentioned.
14. **Control 3661 "not on record":** leave it blank. Don't guess a side. Where Part A tags a role [inferred], keep the tag.
15. **Routes:** the B1 routes are attested in the window and are the trunk network. The B2 routes (infobox-only) may be drawn on the map, flagged "unattested in window". Treat each as existing in 3661 only if its founding date is earlier. Leave out the Pabol Hutta (Canon-sourced).
16. **Devaron and the Hydian Way:** place the blockaded stretch as the blockade page describes it, "between Devaron and the Minos Cluster". That is a stretch of space, not a stop list. Devaron itself stays on the Corellian Trade Spine.
