## Rulings on the 12 decisions
1. **3680 BBY riots:** use "the Coruscant riots of 3680 BBY" as a named event. Note in the file that the name is Wookieepedia's own title; the events are on record.
2. **Surrender talk in 3661:** it came from individual senators, many of them, including war hawks. The Senate as a body never formally debated surrender.
3. **Peace offer:** the offer came in 3654 BBY; the conference and the Sacking were in 3653 BBY.
4. **Blockade relief (3660):** both accounts fit together. The SIS sent Republic Navy elements and the Jedi starfighter fleet, and starfighters and corvettes from nearby systems joined.
5. **Visz's medal:** the Senate organised a ceremony; she didn't attend.
6. **Blockade dates:** 3661–3660 BBY. The riots begin in 3661 and end when the blockade breaks in 3660.
7. **Senate security:** the CSF plus the military's Senate Security Officers (the CSF page is the most specific about the Old Republic). The narrator may call them "Senate guards" informally. The formal Senate Guard (blue robes, Senate Commandos) is later-era and stays out.
8. **Underworld Police:** allowed in this window, tagged undated. The lower levels need police, and the division fits.
9. **Garza at Bomodon:** a ten-month campaign spanning 3674–3673 BBY.
10. **Pre-Sacking underworld:** this departs from the bot's best fit.
    - The named cast stays the Migrant Merchants' Guild (laundering, smuggling), the Exchange (offworld engspice) and the Hutts (money behind blockade runs).
    - Small local gangs are allowed, tagged `invented`, because a lower-level start needs street-level crime.
    - No invented organisation may be galaxy-wide or fill Black Sun's later role; that space stays empty until after the Sacking.
11. **Rationing, curfews and conscription:**
    - Show shortages, soaring prices and volunteer enlistment, as the record does.
    - No formal rationing: the riots demanded food precisely because the Republic wasn't supplying it.
    - No conscription: the record says volunteers.
    - No standing curfew (same rule as Kaas City). Short district lockdowns during riots may be narrated, tagged `invented`.
12. **Market kiosks and the market air-taxi station:** keep both named places out of 3661. Generic public transport (air taxis, turbolifts, airspeeders) may be narrated as era-neutral city function, without naming a specific station, consistent with batch 8's rule that living spaces exist in function.
