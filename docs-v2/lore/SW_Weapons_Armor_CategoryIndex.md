# Weapons and Armour: Legends category index

Research index for SWRPG, not a lore file. Built 23 Nov 2026 by Claude from the Wookieepedia API, using the same category ∩ Legends_articles intersection as the CategoryIntersection page.

**How it was built**
- Walked the whole **Category:Weapons** tree (389 subcategories) and the **Category:Armor** tree (53).
- Kept only articles also in Category:Legends_articles: 5,114 weapon articles and 600 armour articles.
- Left out branches that aren't personal gear or are already covered: starship, vehicle and emplacement weapons, superweapons, lightsaber duels and colours, lightsaber crystals, Sithspawn, plagues, poisonous plants.
- Tagged each article **OR-era** when its Appearances or Sources list includes an Old Republic–era work: KOTOR I/II, SWTOR and its expansions, Tales of the Jedi, *Revan*, *Deceived*, *Fatal Alliance*, *Red Harvest*, *Annihilation*, the Threat of Peace and Blood of the Empire comics, *The Lost Suns*, Dawn of the Jedi, *Into the Void*, *Lost Tribe of the Sith*, the KOTOR Campaign Guide, or *Jedi vs. Sith: The Essential Guide*.
- Each article is listed once, under its most specific category.
- Each section gives the intersection URL, the category's Legends count, and the OR-era articles. Articles that aren't OR-era are counted but not named.
- "OR-era" only means the article appears in an Old Republic–era work. It doesn't prove the item exists at 3661 BBY; check the page.
- Many OR-era rows are SWTOR or KOTOR gear items. They're game items, so treat them as (game stat) when they're pulled.
- Categories Jared had already pulled are marked "(already pulled)".
