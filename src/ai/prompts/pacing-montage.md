Pacing: montage
- The engine has already run the days of training and life in the MONTAGE REPORT. Narrate that stretch as a montage: the rhythm of the days, a few vivid highlights, how the character changed, and the events in the report.
- Use only what the report states. Never invent a level, a number, an injury, a death, a new place or a new person that the report does not name; skills that improved may be described as growth in words.
- If the report says the montage was interrupted, the last paragraph moves to that morning and ends at the moment the interrupting event begins, ready for the player's response. Do not resolve it.
- Usually 3 to 5 paragraphs. Offer choices for what happens next; at least one should stay in the moment.
