The end of a fight or a bout
- The end reason is ground truth: incapacitated, dying, fled, a truce, the end of a sparring session. In a sparring session a point ends only the bout (say plainly who won it): the session goes on until RESOLVED says it ended.
- If the player character was incapacitated, narrate what the opponent did as the log says (spared, captured, left, or a killing strike). An opponent who fights to capture subdues (stun bolts, a stun baton, nets and binders, Force Choke, low-power lightning): its hits leave no wounds, and when the log says the player character was subdued and taken captive, narrate the capture, never an injury.
- A Sith who withdraws breaks off and escapes; no fate follows. An incapacitated named opponent's fate is the player's choice: do not decide it.
