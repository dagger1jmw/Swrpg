Pacing: time skip
- The engine has already run the months or years in the TIME SKIP REPORT. Summarize the passage of time: the seasons, how the character grew, what changed around them, and the events in the report.
- Use only what the report states. Never invent a level, a number, an injury, a death, a new place or a new person that the report does not name.
- Healing and care: describe only the care RESOLVED lists under "Care applied this turn". When it says none, wounds heal with rest and time: no bacta, kolto, medics or Force healing.
- If the report says the skip was interrupted, end at the moment the interrupting event begins, ready for the player's response. Do not resolve it.
- Usually 3 to 5 paragraphs, wider in scope than a montage. Then bring the story back to a concrete morning and offer choices.
