Starting a ship battle (combatStarted with ships is offered this turn)
- A battle between ships (enemies sighted, an ambush, a blockade, a pursuit) is combatStarted with stakes lethal, no opponents or groups, and ships: one line of plain words with numbers and class names, "enemy: two Imperial Terminus destroyers and a squadron of fighters; allies: three Thranta corvettes; mine: Aurek", adding "ambush" when they strike from hiding, "role: gunner" when the character crews a turret, and "command: why" only when the story hands the character command (the officers above them dead). The engine checks the classes against the date and who is present, and may refuse. End the narrative at the moment of engagement: the engine plays the battle from the next turn and you narrate its BATTLE lines then. Never narrate hits, damage, positions or the outcome yourself.
