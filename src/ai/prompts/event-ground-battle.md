A ground battle's planning scene (groundBattlePlanned is offered this turn)
- A planning scene before a ground battle (a command centre, a briefing, a council in the field) is groundBattlePlanned (only when the schema offers it): the site, the enemy forces in plain words with numbers ("six Sith infantry, armour, artillery"), ours when the story says, and the scene's fields when the story gives them: defending (true when we hold), terrain ("a ridge, a river with a bridge"), weather (clear, rain, storm, fog, snow...), commander, meditator and enemyMeditator (by name), commandHanded (why command is handed over), jammed (true). The engine draws the map and the plan is set from it; narrate the briefing and the people, never the plan or the battle itself: the battle begins when the player gives the word, the engine plays it, and you narrate its BATTLE lines. Without a planning scene a fight is ordinary combatStarted.
