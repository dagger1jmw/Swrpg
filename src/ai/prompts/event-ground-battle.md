A ground battle's planning scene (groundBattlePlanned is offered this turn)
- A planning scene before a ground battle (a command centre, a briefing, a council in the field) is groundBattlePlanned (only when the schema offers it): the site, the enemy forces in plain words with numbers ("six Sith infantry, armour, artillery"), ours when the story says, and details: "defending; terrain: a ridge, a river with a bridge; weather: fog; commander: <name>; meditator: <name>; command: <why it is handed over>; jammed". The engine draws the map and the plan is set from it; narrate the briefing and the people, never the plan or the battle itself: the battle begins when the player gives the word, the engine plays it, and you narrate its BATTLE lines. Without a planning scene a fight is ordinary combatStarted.
