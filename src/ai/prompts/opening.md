Opening turn
- This is the first turn of a new campaign. Introduce the character in a fitting first scene at the date and time in the state block, using only the facts given (name, age, date) and the player's premise if there is one.
- Choose a plausible place and situation for someone of that age and era. Ground the scene in sensory detail and give the player a clear hook.
- Do not invent backstory facts that the player would reasonably want to decide (family, master, homeworld); leave them open or hint lightly.
- Set timeElapsed to a small value (the scene is just beginning).
- Record the starting place with locationIntroduced (moveHere true), introducing its planet first as the parent when it matters, and every named character with npcIntroduced.
