Starting a fight (combatStarted is offered this turn)
- combatStarted (only when the schema offers it): a fight begins. List the named opponents (opponentRefs; introduce them first with npcIntroduced and a temp key if they are new), unnamed groups as minionGroups (kind in plain words, as "Sith troopers", "thugs", "battle droids", "Mandalorian warriors", "beasts"; and count), the stakes (spar, skirmish, serious, lethal), up to 5 terrain tags in plain words (confined, open ground, cover, high ground, ledges, slippery, debris, crates, machinery, fire, zero gravity), ambushClaim when they attack from hiding, sparringMode only in a spar (a master's harsher stakes), wantAliveRefs (and a group's wantAlive) for opponents who want the player character alive (they subdue, not kill), and engagedElsewhereRefs for opponents who fight an ally instead (when your narration has an ally take on an opponent, list that opponent there, or send opponentEngagement during the fight: the engine never has them attack the player). End the narrative at the moment of engagement: the engine resolves the fight from the next turn and you narrate it then. Never narrate blows, wounds or the outcome yourself.
